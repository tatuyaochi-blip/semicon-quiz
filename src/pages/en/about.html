About this site

{{siteName}} is an unofficial study site for learning semiconductor technology through multiple-choice questions. It follows the scope of Japan's Semiconductor Engineer Certification (半導体技術者検定).

Unofficial site

This site is run by an individual and is not affiliated with the organizer of the Semiconductor Engineer Certification or any related organization. For official information such as registration and exam scope, please refer to the organizer.

About the questions

All questions and explanations are original to this site. No official past exam questions are used. The questions are divided into {{categoryTotal}} topics, from semiconductor fundamentals to devices, fabrication processes, packaging and testing, and design and industry.

The English questions are translations of the original Japanese questions. We take care to keep them accurate, but they may contain errors. If you find one, please let us know via the contact page.

How to use

  • By topic: pick a topic on the home page and answer its questions.
  • 10 random questions: ten questions drawn from all topics.
  • Weak questions: retry only the questions you last got wrong.
  • Mock exam: 30 questions from all topics, with explanations shown at the end.

Your answers are stored only in your browser and can be checked on the progress page. No account is needed.
