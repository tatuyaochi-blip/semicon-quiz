Privacy policy

Storage of your answers

This site stores your answer records (correct or incorrect, time answered, and session history) in your browser's localStorage. This data stays on your device and is never sent to our servers. You can delete it with the reset button on the progress page or by clearing site data in your browser.

Personal information

This site has no user registration and does not collect personal information such as names or email addresses for browsing or answering questions.

Contact form

We use an external service (Google Forms) for inquiries. Information you enter is used only to reply and to improve this site, and is not provided to third parties except as required by law.
