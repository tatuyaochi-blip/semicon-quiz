Logic circuits: key points

Digital circuits are built from basic gates such as AND, OR and NOT, and their combinations such as NAND, NOR and XOR. Truth tables, Boolean algebra and De Morgan's laws let you simplify logic expressions and replace gates with others.

Also understand the difference between combinational circuits, whose outputs depend only on their inputs, and sequential circuits, which hold state in elements such as flip-flops.
