Environment, health and safety: key points

Semiconductor fabs use many chemicals such as hydrofluoric acid, as well as toxic or flammable specialty gases. Understanding the hazards of chemicals and handling them correctly according to their SDS (safety data sheet) is fundamental.

Exhaust gases are treated by abatement systems before release. Reducing emissions of greenhouse gases such as PFCs, used for etching and chamber cleaning, is also an important issue.
