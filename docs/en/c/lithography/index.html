Lithography: key points

Lithography transfers the circuit pattern on a mask (reticle) onto photoresist on the wafer using light. It is the key step that sets the limit of scaling, and exposure tools are among the most advanced semiconductor manufacturing equipment.

Resolution improves with a shorter wavelength λ and a larger numerical aperture NA. ArF excimer laser (193 nm) immersion lithography and EUV lithography with a 13.5 nm wavelength are used for leading-edge products.
