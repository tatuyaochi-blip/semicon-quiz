MEMS and sensors: key points

New to this topic? Start with: How Do Touchscreens Work? Capacitive Touch Explained / What Chips Are Inside a Smartphone? The Main Chips Explained

MEMS (micro-electro-mechanical systems) are devices that use semiconductor microfabrication to build tiny mechanical structures together with electronic circuits. They are widely used as accelerometers, gyroscopes, microphones and pressure sensors in smartphones.

Understand sacrificial-layer etching, which frees movable structures, and the principles that turn displacement, stress or magnetic fields into electrical signals (capacitance, the piezoresistive effect and the Hall effect).
