Power Management: key points

Both smartphone battery life and data-center electricity bills depend heavily on how chips use power. Power in CMOS circuits consists of dynamic power, consumed each time signals switch, and static power, consumed through leakage even when idle, and each calls for different countermeasures.

This topic covers how operating systems and firmware make use of hardware mechanisms such as DVFS, which lowers voltage and frequency to match the load; clock gating, which stops the clock to unused circuits; and power gating, which cuts off their supply entirely. It also covers CPU sleep and performance states and the idea behind thermal throttling when temperatures climb too high.
