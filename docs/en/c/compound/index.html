Compound semiconductors: key points

Compound semiconductors are made of more than one element, such as GaAs (gallium arsenide) and GaN (gallium nitride). Combinations of group III and group V elements (III-V) are typical.

Some have higher electron mobility than silicon, some have direct band gaps that emit light efficiently, and some have large band gaps. They are used in high-frequency devices, LEDs and lasers, and power devices.
