Silicon photonics: key points

Silicon photonics integrates waveguides that guide light, modulators, photodetectors and other components on a single chip using the same silicon fabrication technology as CMOS. Silicon barely absorbs light in the 1.31–1.55 µm bands used for communications, and its large refractive index contrast with the surrounding oxide confines light tightly in small circuits.

Data center traffic is surging with AI training and other workloads, and electrical wiring keeps incurring more loss and consuming more power as speeds rise. Optical interconnects such as optical transceivers and CPO, which mounts optical components right next to the switch IC, are drawing attention as the key technology for solving these problems.
