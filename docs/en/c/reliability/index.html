Reliability: key points

Reliability is the ability of a product to keep functioning under specified conditions for a specified time. How the failure rate changes over time is described by the bathtub curve, made up of early-failure, random-failure and wear-out periods.

Failure mechanisms specific to semiconductors include electromigration in interconnects, time-dependent dielectric breakdown (TDDB) of gate insulators, and degradation of transistor characteristics. Accelerated tests estimate lifetimes in a short time.
