Analog and electronic circuits: key points

Analog circuits handle voltages and currents that change continuously. The operational amplifier, a typical component, is ideally treated as having infinite input impedance, zero output impedance and infinite gain.

The gain of inverting and non-inverting amplifiers with negative feedback, and the time constant and cutoff frequency of RC circuits, are common subjects for calculation questions.
