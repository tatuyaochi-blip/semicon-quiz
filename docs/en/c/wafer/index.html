Silicon wafers: key points

Most semiconductor devices are made on wafers sliced from high-purity single-crystal silicon. Single crystals are grown either by the CZ method, which pulls a crystal from silicon melted in a crucible, or by the FZ method, which uses no crucible.

The grown crystal becomes wafers through slicing, polishing and cleaning. Wafer diameters have kept increasing, and 300 mm wafers are now standard in leading-edge production lines.
