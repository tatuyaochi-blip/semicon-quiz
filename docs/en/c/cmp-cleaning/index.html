CMP and cleaning: key points

New to this topic? Start with: How Are Chips Made? The Semiconductor Manufacturing Process

CMP (chemical mechanical polishing) planarizes the wafer surface by combining the chemical action of a slurry with mechanical polishing by a pad. It is essential for multilevel interconnects and for forming copper wiring by the damascene process.

Cleaning removes particles, organics, metal contamination and native oxide, and is repeated many times during manufacturing. Learn how the chemicals derived from RCA cleaning are used for different purposes.
