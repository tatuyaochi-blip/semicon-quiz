Power Electronics Circuits: key points

New to this topic? Start with: What Is a Power Semiconductor? SiC, GaN and IGBTs Explained / How Do Solar Cells Work? Light to Electricity and Its Limits

Power semiconductor devices cannot convert power on their own. Diodes, thyristors, MOSFETs and IGBTs are combined with inductors, capacitors and transformers into circuits and switched at the right moments to turn AC into DC, DC into DC at another voltage, and DC into AC. This topic covers the basics of these power conversion circuits and of the motors they drive.

It starts with single-phase and three-phase rectifiers, the average value of full-wave rectification, estimating the ripple with a smoothing capacitor, thyristor phase control and the firing angle, line commutation and the power factor of distorted currents. It then derives the conversion ratios of buck, boost and buck-boost converters from the volt-second balance of the inductor and works through the inductor ripple current, the boundary between continuous and discontinuous conduction modes, output voltage ripple, the current path during dead time with synchronous rectification, switching loss and light-load efficiency, with calculations. Isolated converters are introduced only at the level of how flyback and forward converters operate. The second half covers half-bridge and full-bridge inverters, sinusoidal PWM and the modulation index, the line voltage of three-phase inverters, overmodulation, choosing the carrier frequency, the voltage error caused by dead time, sizing the bootstrap capacitor and boost-type power factor correction, and ends with the synchronous speed and slip of induction motors, V/f control, the back EMF and torque of DC motors, brushless DC motors, regeneration during deceleration and the current ratio of an ideal transformer. It can serve as an introduction to the power electronics and electric machines parts of electrical licensing exams such as Japan's Electric Chief Engineer exam (third class) and Korea's Electrical Engineer exam. The values in the calculation questions, including those of the wrong choices, have been checked by computing them in Python (NumPy). The structure and characteristics of devices such as MOSFETs, IGBTs, SiC and GaN, gate drivers, snubber circuits and soft switching are covered in "Power semiconductors"; the comparison of LDOs and switching regulators in "Analog and electronic circuits"; AC circuits, three-phase AC, transformer voltage ratios and impedance transformation in "Circuit and electromagnetism basics"; the control loops of DC-DC converters and cascade control of motors in "Control Engineering Basics"; PWM and duty cycle in "Embedded Systems & Firmware"; and electric-vehicle inverters in "Automotive Semiconductors and Functional Safety".
