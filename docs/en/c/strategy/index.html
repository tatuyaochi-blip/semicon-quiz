Business Strategy and Accounting Basics: key points

The results of a semiconductor company depend not only on its technology but also on management decisions: which businesses to invest in and how much, at what price to sell, and how much must be made to earn a profit. The shape of the business, such as owning fabs that require huge capital investment or specializing in design as a fabless company, also affects the break-even point and resilience to economic cycles through the size of fixed costs and depreciation.

This topic covers accounting calculations such as reading the income statement, balance sheet and cash flow statement, break-even and target-profit sales, depreciation, ROE and the equity ratio, and inventory valuation; business strategy such as SWOT analysis, PPM, five forces analysis, the value chain, Porter's generic strategies and the experience curve; and marketing such as the 4Ps, STP, the product life cycle and pricing strategies. It also covers, mainly through calculation questions, ABC analysis, regression analysis, decision trees, the maximin principle, linear programming, investment appraisal by NPV, BPR and cloud services, copyright and patents, non-disclosure agreements and the idea of personal information. It also helps with the strategy sections of IT certification exams such as Japan's IT Passport, Fundamental and Applied Information Technology Engineer Examinations.
