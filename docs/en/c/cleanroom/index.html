Cleanrooms and equipment: key points

In semiconductor manufacturing even tiny particles can cause defects, so work is done in cleanrooms where airborne particles are kept extremely low. Cleanliness is expressed as the number of particles in a given volume of air.

In 300 mm fabs, wafers are carried automatically in sealed containers called FOUPs, and the usual approach is to keep only the inside of equipment at very high cleanliness.
