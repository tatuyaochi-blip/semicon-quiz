Emerging memories: key points

To overcome the limits of DRAM and flash memory, memories based on many new principles are being developed. Most are non-volatile and store information not as charge but as differences in resistance or polarization direction.

MRAM uses the magnetization direction of a magnetic tunnel junction, PCM the resistance difference between crystalline and amorphous phases, ReRAM conductive paths in an oxide, and FeRAM the polarization of a ferroelectric. Learn to tell these principles and features apart.
