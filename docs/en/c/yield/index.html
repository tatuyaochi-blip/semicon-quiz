Yield and quality control: key points

Yield is the proportion of good products among those started, and it greatly affects a fab's profitability. With the same defect density, larger chips are more likely to contain a defect, so their yield is lower.

To keep processes stable, data is monitored with statistical methods such as control charts (SPC), so that abnormalities are found and fixed quickly.
