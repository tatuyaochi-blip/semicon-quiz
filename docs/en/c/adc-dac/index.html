AD/DA conversion: key points

An analog-to-digital converter (ADC) turns analog signals into digital values, and a digital-to-analog converter (DAC) does the reverse. They are used wherever real-world signals meet digital processing, such as sensors, communications and audio.

The basic performance measures are resolution (number of bits) and conversion speed. Understand the sampling theorem, quantization error, and the characteristics of each architecture, such as flash, successive approximation and delta-sigma.
