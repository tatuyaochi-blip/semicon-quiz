Advanced transistors (FinFET and GAA): key points

As transistors shrink, short-channel effects make it harder for the gate to control the channel. FinFETs, whose fin-shaped channel is surrounded by the gate on three sides, solved this, and the design has evolved further into gate-all-around (GAA) transistors that surround the channel completely.

High-k/metal gates, which suppress gate leakage, and strain techniques, which raise mobility, are also important technologies behind advanced transistors.
