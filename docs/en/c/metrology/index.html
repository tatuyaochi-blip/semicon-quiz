Metrology and analysis: key points

In semiconductor manufacturing, line widths, film thicknesses, impurity concentrations and defects are measured along the way to confirm that each process is working correctly. The finer the features, the more accurate nanometer-scale measurement must be.

Different methods are used for different purposes: electron microscopes (SEM and TEM) for shape, ellipsometry for film thickness, secondary ion mass spectrometry (SIMS) for impurity profiles, and the four-point probe for sheet resistance.
