Image sensors and optical devices: key points

An image sensor converts light into charge with a photodiode in each pixel and reads it out as an electrical signal. CMOS image sensors are now mainstream and widely used in smartphones and automotive cameras.

Backside illumination, which lets light enter from the side opposite the wiring layers, and stacked sensors, which put pixels and circuits on separate chips, have improved performance. Optical devices that convert electricity into light, such as LEDs and lasers, are also part of this topic.
