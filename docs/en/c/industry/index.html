Semiconductor industry and market: key points

In addition to IDMs, which handle everything from design to manufacturing and sales in-house, the semiconductor industry has seen a growing division of labor between fabless companies, which own no fabs and focus on design, and foundries, which specialize in contract manufacturing.

Moore's law, which says that the number of transistors on an integrated circuit doubles at regular intervals, has long served as a benchmark for the industry's technology development.
