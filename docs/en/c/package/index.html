Packaging and assembly: key points

A package protects the chip from its environment, connects it electrically to the board and carries away heat. Chips cut from the wafer become products through die bonding, interconnection, encapsulation and other steps.

Chip-to-package connections include wire bonding and flip chip, and package shapes have evolved from types with leads to types like BGA with solder balls on the underside to support higher pin counts.
