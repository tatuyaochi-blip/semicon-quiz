Interconnect processes: key points

In an IC, transistors are connected by many layers of metal wiring. As scaling progressed, delay caused by wire resistance and capacitance (RC delay) came to limit performance, and wiring materials have been improved accordingly.

Copper wiring, which has lower resistance than aluminum, is hard to dry-etch, so it is made by the damascene process: filling trenches and removing the excess by CMP. Low-k materials with low dielectric constants are used for the interlayer insulators.
