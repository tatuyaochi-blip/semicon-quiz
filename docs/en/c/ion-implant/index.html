Ion implantation: key points

Ion implantation ionizes impurities, accelerates them with an electric field and drives them into the wafer. Because the dose and depth can be controlled precisely, it is now the main method for introducing dopants.

The implanted ions damage the crystal, so after implantation the wafer is annealed to restore the crystal and electrically activate the dopants. Also understand what determines implant depth and how channeling is prevented.
