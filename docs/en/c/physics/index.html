Semiconductor physics: key points

The properties of a semiconductor are largely determined by its band gap, the energy difference between the valence band and the conduction band. Silicon's band gap is about 1.12 eV at room temperature: not as large as an insulator's, and not zero like a metal's.

Adding impurities produces n-type semiconductors with many electrons or p-type semiconductors with many holes. The temperature dependence of carrier density, mobility, and the difference between drift and diffusion currents form the foundation for understanding the devices you will study later.
