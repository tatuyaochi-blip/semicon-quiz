Circuit and electromagnetism basics: key points

Circuit analysis rests on Ohm's law and Kirchhoff's current and voltage laws. With series and parallel combinations, voltage and current dividers, Thevenin's theorem and superposition, even complex circuits can be reduced to simple equivalents for calculation.

In AC circuits, the reactances of inductors and capacitors change with frequency and create phase differences between voltage and current. Impedance, resonance, power factor, transient time constants and the underlying laws of electric fields, magnetic fields and induction are the foundation of every field that deals with electronic circuits, not just semiconductors.
