Process materials (gases and chemicals): key points

Semiconductor manufacturing uses many materials: high-purity silicon, specialty gases for deposition and etching, chemicals and ultrapure water for cleaning, sputtering targets, photoresists, CMP slurries and more.

Even tiny amounts of impurities can cause defects, so these materials must be extremely pure. Many of the gases are also pyrophoric or toxic, so safe handling is essential.
