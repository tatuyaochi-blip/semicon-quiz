Power semiconductors: key points

Power semiconductors convert and control electric power and are used in inverters, power supplies, electric vehicles and more. They need high breakdown voltage, low on-resistance and fast switching.

In addition to silicon MOSFETs and IGBTs, devices made from SiC and GaN, which have large band gaps and high breakdown fields, are becoming widespread. It is important to understand how material properties relate to device structure.
