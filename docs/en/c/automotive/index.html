Automotive Semiconductors and Functional Safety: key points

Automotive semiconductors are used for around 15 years at temperatures ranging from −40 °C to as high as 150 °C, and because their failures can endanger lives, they must meet stricter quality and reliability requirements than consumer parts. The foundation is qualification testing such as AEC-Q100, quality control aiming for zero defects, change notification and traceability.

Beyond that, ISO 26262 functional safety determines an ASIL from the magnitude of the hazard and uses safety mechanisms such as lockstep CPUs, ECC, and voltage and clock monitoring to detect faults and move to a safe state. You will learn the basics of automotive electronic design, from CAN and automotive Ethernet networks and 48 V and 800 V power systems to ADAS sensors and SOTIF.
