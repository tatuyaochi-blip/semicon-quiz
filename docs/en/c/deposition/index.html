Deposition (CVD and PVD): key points

Deposition creates thin films such as insulators, semiconductors and metals on the wafer. It is broadly divided into CVD, which deposits films through chemical reactions of gases, and PVD, which deposits them physically, as in sputtering and evaporation.

As devices shrink and become three-dimensional, ALD, which controls film thickness one atomic layer at a time and coats deep trenches uniformly, is becoming more important.
