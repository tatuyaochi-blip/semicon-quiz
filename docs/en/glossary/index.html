Semiconductor Glossary

Basic terms that come up often when studying semiconductors. Type a keyword to filter.

Acceptor
An impurity that accepts electrons from a semiconductor and creates holes. For silicon, group III elements such as boron (B) act as acceptors and make p-type semiconductors. Topic: Semiconductor physics →
ALD
Atomic layer deposition. A deposition method that repeats self-limiting surface reactions of precursor gases to control film thickness one atomic layer at a time. Topic: Deposition (CVD and PVD) →
Anisotropic etching
Etching that proceeds only in a particular direction (mainly vertically). Needed to pattern fine features. Topic: Etching →
Annealing
Heat treatment. Used to repair crystal damage after ion implantation and to activate dopants, among other purposes. Topic: Ion implantation →
Band gap
The energy difference between the top of the valence band and the bottom of the conduction band. About 1.12 eV for silicon at room temperature. A larger band gap means better tolerance to high temperatures and voltages. Topic: Semiconductor physics →
Bathtub curve
A curve showing how the failure rate changes over time. It consists of the early-failure, random-failure and wear-out periods and is shaped like a bathtub. Topic: Reliability →
BGA
Ball grid array. A high-pin-count package with solder balls arranged in a grid on its underside. Topic: Packaging and assembly →
Burn-in
A test that operates products under stress such as high temperature and high voltage to remove units prone to early failure before shipment. Topic: Testing and inspection →
Cache memory
Fast, small memory placed close to the CPU. It holds frequently used data to bridge the speed gap with main memory. Mostly built from SRAM. Topic: CPUs and architecture →
CD-SEM
A scanning electron microscope for measuring pattern line widths (critical dimensions). Used for dimensional control after lithography and etching. Topic: Metrology and analysis →
Chiplet
A small chip made for a specific function. Several chiplets are connected inside one package to build a larger system. Topic: Advanced packaging (3D and chiplets) →
CMOS
A circuit style that combines nMOS and pMOS transistors in a complementary way. Almost no current flows through it in steady state, so power consumption is low. Topic: MOSFET →
CMP
Chemical mechanical polishing. Planarizes the wafer surface by combining the chemical action of a slurry with mechanical polishing by a pad. Topic: CMP and cleaning →
Current gain (hFE)
The ratio of collector current to base current (IC/IB) in a bipolar transistor. Topic: Bipolar transistors →
CVD
Chemical vapor deposition. A method of depositing thin films on a wafer through chemical reactions of source gases. Topic: Deposition (CVD and PVD) →
Czochralski (CZ) method
A method of making single-crystal silicon by dipping a seed crystal into molten silicon in a crucible and slowly pulling it up while rotating. Topic: Silicon wafers →
Damascene process
A method of forming interconnects by etching trenches in an insulating film, filling them with metal, and removing the excess metal by CMP. Used for copper wiring. Topic: Interconnect processes →
Depletion layer
A region near a PN junction boundary that contains almost no carriers. It widens under reverse bias. Topic: PN junctions and diodes →
DFT (design for testability)
A design approach that builds test features such as scan chains and BIST into the circuit in advance. Topic: Testing and inspection →
Direct band gap
A band structure in which the bottom of the conduction band and the top of the valence band are at the same wave number. Electron-hole recombination emits light efficiently. Example: GaAs. Topic: Compound semiconductors →
Donor
An impurity that supplies electrons to a semiconductor. For silicon, group V elements such as phosphorus (P) and arsenic (As) act as donors and make n-type semiconductors. Topic: Semiconductor physics →
Electromigration
The movement of metal atoms in interconnects caused by electric current. It creates voids and hillocks that lead to open or short circuits. Topic: Reliability →
Epitaxial growth
A technique for growing a single-crystal thin film on a single-crystal substrate with the same crystal orientation as the substrate. Topic: Epitaxial growth →
EUV lithography
Lithography using extreme ultraviolet light with a wavelength of 13.5 nm. It uses reflective optics and a vacuum environment. Topic: Lithography →
Fabless
A company model that owns no fabrication plant and focuses on designing and selling semiconductors. Topic: Semiconductor industry and market →
FinFET
A 3D transistor whose channel stands up like a fin and is surrounded by the gate on three sides. It suppresses short-channel effects well. Topic: Advanced transistors (FinFET and GAA) →
Flip chip
A mounting method in which the chip is placed face down and connected directly to the substrate with bumps. Short connections make it suitable for high pin counts. Topic: Packaging and assembly →
Flip-flop
The basic element of sequential circuits that stores one bit. A D flip-flop captures its input at a clock edge. Topic: Logic circuits →
Foundry
A company or business model that specializes in manufacturing semiconductors designed by other companies. Topic: Semiconductor industry and market →
FOUP
A sealed container for transporting and storing 300 mm wafers. Topic: Cleanrooms and equipment →
GaN (gallium nitride)
A III-V compound semiconductor with a band gap of about 3.4 eV. Used in blue LEDs and in high-frequency and power devices. Topic: Compound semiconductors →
HBM
High Bandwidth Memory. Multiple DRAM dies stacked with TSVs, achieving very high bandwidth through an extremely wide bus. Topic: Advanced packaging (3D and chiplets) →
High-k / metal gate
The combination of a high-dielectric-constant gate insulator and a metal gate electrode. It keeps gate capacitance high while suppressing gate leakage. Topic: Advanced transistors (FinFET and GAA) →
IGBT
Insulated-gate bipolar transistor. Driven by a MOS gate, it achieves a low on-state voltage even at high voltage ratings through conductivity modulation. Topic: Power semiconductors →
Ion implantation
A technique that ionizes impurities, accelerates them and implants them into the wafer. The dose and depth can be controlled precisely. Topic: Ion implantation →
Logic synthesis
The design step that converts a circuit described in RTL into a netlist made of gates from a cell library. Topic: LSI design flow and EDA →
Low-k material
An interlayer dielectric with a low dielectric constant. It lowers the capacitance between wires, reducing signal delay and power consumption. Topic: Interconnect processes →
MEMS
Micro-electro-mechanical systems. Devices that integrate tiny mechanical structures and electronic circuits using semiconductor microfabrication. Topic: MEMS and sensors →
Mobility
How easily carriers move when an electric field is applied. In silicon, electron mobility is higher than hole mobility. Topic: Semiconductor physics →
MRAM
Non-volatile memory that stores data as the difference in resistance caused by the magnetization direction of a magnetic tunnel junction (MTJ). Topic: Emerging memories →
NAND flash memory
Non-volatile memory in which memory cells are connected in series. It is dense and easy to scale to large capacities, and is used in SSDs and memory cards. Topic: SRAM and flash memory →
Numerical aperture (NA)
A value that expresses the range of angles of light a lens can collect. A larger NA gives higher resolution. Topic: Lithography →
Operational amplifier (op-amp)
An amplifier with differential inputs and very high gain. Used with negative feedback in amplifiers, filters and more. Topic: Analog and electronic circuits →
Photodiode
A diode that generates charge (current) when it receives light. Used as the light-receiving element in image sensor pixels. Topic: Image sensors and optical devices →
Photoresist
A light-sensitive material whose solubility in developer changes when exposed. In positive resist the exposed areas dissolve; in negative resist they remain. Topic: Lithography →
RCA clean
A cleaning method that removes particles and organics with SC-1 (ammonia and hydrogen peroxide) and metal contamination with SC-2 (hydrochloric acid and hydrogen peroxide). Topic: CMP and cleaning →
Refresh
The periodic reading and rewriting of DRAM data before the charge in its capacitors is lost through leakage. Topic: DRAM →
Sampling theorem
The theorem stating that a signal can be reconstructed if it is sampled at a frequency higher than twice its highest frequency component. Also called the Nyquist-Shannon theorem. Topic: AD/DA conversion →
SDS (safety data sheet)
A document describing the hazards of a chemical, how to handle it, first-aid measures and more. Topic: Environment, health and safety →
Selectivity
In etching, the ratio of the etch rate of the target film to that of the mask or underlying film. Higher selectivity means less damage to the underlayer. Topic: Etching →
Sense amplifier
A circuit that amplifies the tiny voltage difference read from a memory cell and decides whether it is 0 or 1. Topic: DRAM →
SiC (silicon carbide)
A wide-band-gap semiconductor with a band gap of about 3.3 eV. Its high breakdown field makes it suitable for high-voltage, low-loss power devices. Topic: Power semiconductors →
SIMS (secondary ion mass spectrometry)
A method that sputters a sample with primary ions and mass-analyzes the secondary ions to measure impurity depth profiles with high sensitivity. Topic: Metrology and analysis →
Sputtering
A type of PVD in which ions in a plasma bombard a target and the ejected atoms are deposited on the wafer. Topic: Deposition (CVD and PVD) →
STA (static timing analysis)
A method that calculates the delay of every path in a circuit without test patterns and checks that timing constraints are met. Topic: LSI design flow and EDA →
Thermal oxidation
A method of forming an SiO2 film on silicon by heating it in oxygen or water vapor. There are dry and wet oxidation. Topic: Oxidation and diffusion →
Threshold voltage
The gate voltage at which a channel (inversion layer) forms in a MOSFET and current starts to flow. Written as Vth. Topic: MOSFET →
TSV (through-silicon via)
An electrode that passes through the silicon substrate. It connects stacked chips vertically over a short distance. Topic: Advanced packaging (3D and chiplets) →
Ultrapure water
Water from which ions, particles and organic matter have been removed to the extreme. Used in large amounts for wafer cleaning, with purity controlled by resistivity and other measures. Topic: Process materials (gases and chemicals) →
Wire bonding
A method of connecting the chip's bond pads to a lead frame or substrate with thin wires of gold, copper or similar metals. Topic: Packaging and assembly →
Yield
The proportion of good products to the total produced. A key factor in semiconductor manufacturing cost. Topic: Yield and quality control →
Zener diode
A diode that provides a constant voltage by using the nearly fixed reverse breakdown voltage. Topic: PN junctions and diodes →
