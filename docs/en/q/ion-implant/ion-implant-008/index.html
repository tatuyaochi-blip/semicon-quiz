Ion implantation

What is the main advantage of ion implantation over thermal diffusion?

  1. AIt can be done at low temperature, with precise control of dose and depth
  2. BIt allows long processing at high temperature
  3. CIt causes no crystal damage at all
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. It can be done at low temperature, with precise control of dose and depth

Ion implantation runs near room temperature, so photoresist can serve as the mask, and dose is controlled to about ±1% by integrating beam current. Depth is set independently through energy, and doses spanning 10¹¹ to 10¹⁶ cm⁻² are accessible. Thermal diffusion ties surface concentration to depth and, being hot, required oxide masks. Implantation does damage the crystal, however, so a post-implant anneal is required.
