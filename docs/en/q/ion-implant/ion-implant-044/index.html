Ion implantation

What is the technique of implanting n-type impurities into an already p-type region to lower the net doping or invert the conductivity type called?

  1. ACounter-doping (compensation)
  2. BGettering
  3. CPassivation
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. Counter-doping (compensation)

Implanting impurities of the opposite type causes donors and acceptors to cancel each other (compensation), lowering the net carrier concentration; with enough, the conductivity type inverts. It is used for threshold-voltage adjustment and similar, but the total impurity content rises, which tends to lower mobility.

  • A. Correct. Impurities of the opposite type cancel the existing doping.
  • B. Gettering captures harmful impurities.
  • C. Passivation protects or deactivates surfaces.
