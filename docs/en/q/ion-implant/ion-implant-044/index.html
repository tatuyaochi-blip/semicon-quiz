Ion implantation

What is the technique of implanting n-type impurities into an already p-type region to lower the net doping or invert the conductivity type called?

  1. AGettering
  2. BPassivation
  3. CCounter-doping (compensation)
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. Counter-doping (compensation)

Implanting dopant of the opposite type over an existing region makes donors and acceptors cancel (compensation), lowering net carrier concentration and, with enough dose, inverting the conductivity type. It is used to lower surface channel doping for threshold adjustment or to form buried channels. Because total impurity content rises, ionized-impurity scattering increases, lowering mobility and adding variability.

  • A. Gettering captures harmful impurities.
  • B. Passivation protects or deactivates surfaces.
  • C. Correct. Impurities of the opposite type cancel the existing doping.
