Ion implantation

In ion implantation, how is the dose made uniform across the wafer?

  1. AThe beam is held fixed at one point
  2. BThe beam or the wafer is scanned so the whole wafer is implanted uniformly
  3. CThe wafer is kept heated
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. The beam or the wafer is scanned so the whole wafer is implanted uniformly

Ion beams are smaller than wafers, so implanters cover the surface by sweeping the beam electrostatically or magnetically, moving the wafer mechanically, or spreading the beam into a ribbon and moving the wafer in one direction. Scan speed is corrected by position, and beam profiles measured with Faraday cups are fed back, holding in-wafer dose variation to about 0.5% or less.
