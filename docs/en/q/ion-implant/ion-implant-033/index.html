Ion implantation

Which correctly pairs dopants implanted into silicon with the conductivity type they produce?

  1. ABoron: n-type; phosphorus: p-type
  2. BBoron: p-type; phosphorus and arsenic: n-type
  3. CPhosphorus: p-type; arsenic: p-type
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. Boron: p-type; phosphorus and arsenic: n-type

Group III boron acts as an acceptor and produces p-type silicon, while group V phosphorus and arsenic act as donors and produce n-type. Ion implantation places the required amount of these elements where they are needed.

  • A. The types for boron and phosphorus are reversed.
  • B. Correct. Boron gives p-type; phosphorus and arsenic give n-type.
  • C. Both phosphorus and arsenic produce n-type.
