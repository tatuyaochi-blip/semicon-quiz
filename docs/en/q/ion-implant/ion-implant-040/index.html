Ion implantation

What is the pair of a vacancy and an interstitial atom called, created in the crystal when an implanted ion knocks a silicon atom out of place?

  1. AFrenkel pair
  2. BCooper pair
  3. CElectron-hole pair
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. Frenkel pair

When an implanted ion strikes a silicon atom, it knocks it off its site into an interstitial position, leaving a vacancy behind; the pair is a Frenkel pair, and a single ion can create hundreds to thousands. At high doses the damage overlaps into an amorphous layer. Most pairs recombine during annealing, but leftover interstitials drive transient enhanced diffusion and end-of-range defects.

  • A. Correct. It is a vacancy-interstitial pair.
  • B. A Cooper pair is a pair of electrons in superconductivity.
  • C. An electron-hole pair is a pair of carriers created by light or similar.
