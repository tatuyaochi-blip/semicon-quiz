Ion implantation

What method has commonly been used to introduce impurities into a MOSFET's polysilicon gate electrode to lower its resistance?

  1. AShining light on the gate
  2. BIon implantation (often done together with the source/drain implant)
  3. CSoaking the gate in water for a long time
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. Ion implantation (often done together with the source/drain implant)

Polysilicon gates are made conductive by doping them heavily, to about 10²⁰ cm⁻³ or more. nMOS gates receive n-type and pMOS gates p-type dopants, usually implanted together with the sources/drains (dual-gate), which also allows symmetric threshold voltages. Annealing activates the dopants, but insufficient activation at the bottom of the gate leads to poly depletion.

  • A. Shining light does not introduce impurities.
  • B. Correct. Impurities are introduced by ion implantation.
  • C. Soaking in water does not introduce impurities.
