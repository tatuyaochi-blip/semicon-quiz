Ion implantation

What method has commonly been used to introduce impurities into a MOSFET's polysilicon gate electrode to lower its resistance?

  1. AIon implantation (often done together with the source/drain implant)
  2. BSoaking the gate in water for a long time
  3. CShining light on the gate
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. Ion implantation (often done together with the source/drain implant)

Polysilicon gates are made conductive by heavy doping. n-type dopants are implanted into nMOS gates and p-type into pMOS gates, often together with the source/drain implant. The dopants are then activated by annealing.

  • A. Correct. Impurities are introduced by ion implantation.
  • B. Soaking in water does not introduce impurities.
  • C. Shining light does not introduce impurities.
