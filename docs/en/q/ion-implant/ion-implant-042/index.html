Ion implantation

What is the typical distribution of crystal damage created by ion implantation?

  1. AIt is concentrated only at the surface
  2. BIt is concentrated at the back of the wafer
  3. CIt forms mostly around the depth where ions stop (the range) and slightly shallower
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. It forms mostly around the depth where ions stop (the range) and slightly shallower

Fast ions lose energy mainly to electrons (electronic stopping) and displace few atoms; as they slow, nuclear collisions (nuclear stopping) dominate and knock out atoms in cascades. Lattice damage therefore peaks near and slightly before the end of the range—around 0.7–0.8 Rp—while the region just below the surface is relatively undamaged, so the damage profile lies a bit shallower than the dopant profile.

  • A. Damage extends down to around the range, not just the surface.
  • B. Implantation is done from the front, so it does not reach the back.
  • C. Correct. It forms mostly around and slightly before the range.
