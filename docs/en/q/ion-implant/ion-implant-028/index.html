Ion implantation

If dopants with a dose of 1×10¹⁵ cm⁻² are distributed uniformly over a depth of 100 nm, what is the approximate average concentration?

  1. A1×10¹⁸ cm⁻³
  2. B1×10²² cm⁻³
  3. C1×10²⁰ cm⁻³
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. 1×10²⁰ cm⁻³

Concentration is dose ÷ depth: 100 nm = 1×10⁻⁵ cm, so 1×10¹⁵ ÷ 1×10⁻⁵ = 1×10²⁰ cm⁻³—one dopant per 500 silicon atoms (atomic density 5×10²² cm⁻³). Source/drain regions sit at about this level, close to solid solubility, so maximizing activation is key to low resistance. Real profiles are Gaussian-like, so the peak concentration exceeds this average.
