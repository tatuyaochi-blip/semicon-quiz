Ion implantation

Which gas is typically used in the ion source of an implanter to produce boron ions?

  1. ABoron trifluoride (BF3)
  2. BArgon
  3. COxygen
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. Boron trifluoride (BF3)

In the ion source, gases such as BF3 are decomposed and ionized by a discharge to produce ions such as B⁺ and BF2⁺. Phosphine (PH3) is used for phosphorus and arsine (AsH3) for arsenic. The desired ions are selected in the mass analyzer.
