Ion implantation

Which ion implantation is used for wafer splitting (Smart Cut) and for lifetime control in power devices?

  1. AHigh-dose arsenic implantation
  2. BImplantation of light ions such as hydrogen and helium
  3. CLow-energy antimony implantation
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. Implantation of light ions such as hydrogen and helium

Implanting hydrogen at about 5×10¹⁶ cm⁻² forms a plane of microcavities at the implanted depth, and annealing at about 400–500 °C splits the wafer there (Smart Cut). Defects created by hydrogen or helium implants also act as recombination centers, so they locally shorten carrier lifetime at chosen depths in IGBTs and diodes, speeding up tail current and reverse recovery.
