Ion implantation

Which type of ion implanter is used to implant large doses in a short time, as for source/drain formation?

  1. AHigh-energy implanter
  2. BHigh-current implanter
  3. CMedium-current implanter
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. High-current implanter

Implanters fall into high-current, medium-current and high-energy classes by beam current and energy. High-dose implants around 10¹⁵ ions/cm², such as sources/drains and polysilicon gates, use high-current tools delivering several to tens of mA, where transporting low-energy, high-current beams without blow-up is the key technology. Medium-current tools handle threshold-adjust and halo implants, and high-energy tools deep wells.
