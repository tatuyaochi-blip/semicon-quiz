Ion implantation

In CMOS well formation, what is a well called in which high-energy implantation makes the dopant concentration higher deep below the surface?

  1. ARetrograde well
  2. BLDD
  3. CSilicide
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. Retrograde well

A retrograde well has its concentration peak placed deep by high-energy implantation. The surface concentration is kept low to suit transistor characteristics, while the high concentration deeper down suppresses punch-through and latch-up. It also eliminates the need for long thermal diffusion.
