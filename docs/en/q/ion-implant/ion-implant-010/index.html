Ion implantation

What is used to suppress wafer charging (charge-up) during ion implantation?

  1. AHeating the wafer to high temperature
  2. BMaximizing the beam current
  3. CDevices that supply electrons to neutralize charge (such as a plasma flood gun)
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. Devices that supply electrons to neutralize charge (such as a plasma flood gun)

Continuously implanting positive ions charges insulated areas on the wafer, which can destroy gate oxides. Plasma flood guns and electron showers supply low-energy electrons near the wafer to neutralize the positive charge.
