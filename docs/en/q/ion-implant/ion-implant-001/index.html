Ion implantation

In ion implantation, which parameter mainly determines how deep the dopants are implanted (the range)?

  1. AAcceleration energy
  2. BDose
  3. CPost-implant cleaning time
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. Acceleration energy

Implant depth is determined mainly by the ions' acceleration energy: the higher the energy, the deeper they go. Dose is the number of ions implanted per unit area and determines dopant concentration. The ion species (mass) also affects depth; at the same energy, lighter ions go deeper.
