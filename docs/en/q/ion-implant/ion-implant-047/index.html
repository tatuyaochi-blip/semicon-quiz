Ion implantation

Which pair correctly names the two mechanisms by which implanted ions lose energy in a material?

  1. ACollisions with nuclei (nuclear stopping) and interaction with electrons (electronic stopping)
  2. BHeat conduction and radiation
  3. CElectrical resistance and magnetoresistance
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. Collisions with nuclei (nuclear stopping) and interaction with electrons (electronic stopping)

An ion loses energy through nuclear stopping (elastic collisions with nuclei) and electronic stopping (exciting and ionizing electrons). Nuclear stopping dominates for heavy ions and low energies, electronic stopping for light ions and high energies—for boron in silicon, electronic stopping prevails above roughly ten-odd keV. Nuclear stopping causes most lattice damage, and both are calculated with LSS theory or simulations such as SRIM.

  • A. Correct. Nuclear and electronic stopping.
  • B. Heat conduction and radiation are not the main ways ions stop.
  • C. These are not kinds of resistance.
