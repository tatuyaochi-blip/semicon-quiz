Ion implantation

In an ion implanter, what is the energy of a doubly charged ion (twice the charge) accelerated through 50 kV?

  1. A100 keV
  2. B25 keV
  3. C50 keV
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. 100 keV

The energy an ion gains is charge × accelerating voltage: 50 keV singly charged, 100 keV doubly charged and 150 keV triply charged. Multiply charged ions thus reach energies beyond a tool's maximum voltage and are used for deep wells. They are produced sparingly in the source, however, giving small beam currents unsuited to high doses. Molecular ions, conversely, are used to achieve lower effective energies.

  • A. Correct. 2 × 50 kV = 100 keV.
  • B. 25 keV would correspond to half the charge.
  • C. 50 keV is for a singly charged ion.
