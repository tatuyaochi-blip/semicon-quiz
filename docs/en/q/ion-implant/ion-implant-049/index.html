Ion implantation

Which element, heavier than boron, has been considered and used as a p-type dopant to create steep retrograde profiles in nMOS channels?

  1. AIndium
  2. BArsenic
  3. CPhosphorus
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. Indium

Indium is a group III acceptor that is much heavier than boron and diffuses slowly, so it stops at shallow depths and keeps a steep profile after heat treatment. It was therefore used to create retrograde channel profiles, low at the surface and high slightly deeper. Its drawback is that it is hard to activate.

  • A. Correct. It is a heavy, slowly diffusing p-type dopant.
  • B. Arsenic is an n-type dopant.
  • C. Phosphorus is an n-type dopant.
