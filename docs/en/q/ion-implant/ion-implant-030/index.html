Ion implantation

When an amorphized layer is recrystallized, what are the defects called that remain slightly deeper than the original boundary between the amorphous layer and the crystal?

  1. AEOR (end-of-range) defects
  2. BCOP
  3. COSF
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. EOR (end-of-range) defects

Just below an amorphized layer, excess silicon interstitials knocked out by the implant accumulate in the crystal. Even after the amorphous layer recrystallizes, these interstitials can coalesce into dislocation loops and other defects—end-of-range (EOR) defects. If they fall within a junction's depletion region they cause leakage, so implant and anneal conditions are tuned to position the amorphous depth relative to the junction.
