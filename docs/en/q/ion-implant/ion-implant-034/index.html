Ion implantation

Which is closest to the range of ion implantation doses used in IC manufacturing?

  1. AAbout 10³⁰ cm⁻² or more
  2. BAbout 10¹¹ to 10¹⁶ cm⁻²
  3. CAbout 10¹ to 10³ cm⁻²
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. About 10¹¹ to 10¹⁶ cm⁻²

Doses span more than five orders of magnitude: about 10¹¹–10¹³ cm⁻² for threshold adjustment, around 10¹⁵ cm⁻² for low-resistance source/drains and gates, and above 10¹⁶ cm⁻² for hydrogen in Smart Cut. A 10¹² cm⁻² implant places a trillion ions per square centimeter, yet that is only about a thousandth of the surface atoms. Controlling this range to about ±1% is a hallmark of ion implantation.

  • A. 10³⁰ cm⁻² vastly exceeds the number of surface atoms on silicon (about 10¹⁵ cm⁻²) and is impossible.
  • B. Correct. About 10¹¹ to 10¹⁶ cm⁻² is used depending on the application.
  • C. 10¹ to 10³ cm⁻² is far too little to change electrical characteristics.
