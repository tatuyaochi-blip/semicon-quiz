Ion implantation

Which is closest to the range of ion implantation doses used in LSI manufacturing?

  1. AAbout 10³⁰ cm⁻² or more
  2. BAbout 10¹¹ to 10¹⁶ cm⁻²
  3. CAbout 10¹ to 10³ cm⁻²
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. About 10¹¹ to 10¹⁶ cm⁻²

Threshold-voltage adjustment uses doses of about 10¹¹ to 10¹³ cm⁻², while lowering the resistance of sources, drains and gates uses large doses around 10¹⁵ cm⁻², so overall the range spans roughly 10¹¹ to 10¹⁶ cm⁻². Being able to control this wide range accurately is a strength of ion implantation.

  • A. 10³⁰ cm⁻² vastly exceeds the number of surface atoms on silicon (about 10¹⁵ cm⁻²) and is impossible.
  • B. Correct. About 10¹¹ to 10¹⁶ cm⁻² is used depending on the application.
  • C. 10¹ to 10³ cm⁻² is far too little to change electrical characteristics.
