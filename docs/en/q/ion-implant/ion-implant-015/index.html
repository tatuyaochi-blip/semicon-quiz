Ion implantation

What is the phenomenon called in which diffusion of boron and other dopants is temporarily accelerated early in annealing because of interstitial silicon created by implantation?

  1. ATransient enhanced diffusion (TED)
  2. BGettering
  3. CSegregation
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. Transient enhanced diffusion (TED)

Excess interstitial silicon atoms created by ion implantation assist the diffusion of boron and other dopants, so diffusion is greatly enhanced for a time early in annealing. This is called transient enhanced diffusion (TED). Because it hinders shallow junctions, it is suppressed with short anneals and other techniques.
