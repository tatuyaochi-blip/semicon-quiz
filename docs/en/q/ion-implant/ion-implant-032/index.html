Ion implantation

Which unit is commonly used to express the acceleration energy in ion implantation?

  1. AΩ (ohms)
  2. BkeV (kilo-electron-volts)
  3. CW (watts)
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. keV (kilo-electron-volts)

Implant energy is expressed in electron volts, where 1 eV is the energy a singly charged ion gains across 1 V. Typical energies range from hundreds of eV to hundreds of keV, and high-energy implants reach several MeV. 1 keV is about 1.6×10⁻¹⁶ J—enormous for a single ion and tens to thousands of times the roughly 15 eV needed to displace a silicon atom—hence the lattice damage.

  • A. Ω is a unit of resistance.
  • B. Correct. It is expressed in electron-volts such as keV.
  • C. W is a unit of power.
