Ion implantation

Which unit is commonly used to express the acceleration energy in ion implantation?

  1. AΩ (ohms)
  2. BkeV (kilo-electron-volts)
  3. CW (watts)
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. keV (kilo-electron-volts)

Implant energy is expressed in electron-volts, where 1 eV is the energy of a singly charged ion accelerated through 1 V. Typical ranges are a few keV to several hundred keV, and several MeV for high-energy implants.

  • A. Ω is a unit of resistance.
  • B. Correct. It is expressed in electron-volts such as keV.
  • C. W is a unit of power.
