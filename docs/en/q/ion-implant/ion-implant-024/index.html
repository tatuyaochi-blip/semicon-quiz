Ion implantation

In an ion implanter, which component measures the beam current to determine the number of implanted ions (dose)?

  1. AFaraday cup
  2. BMicrolens
  3. CPellicle
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. Faraday cup

A Faraday cup is a cup-shaped electrode that captures the ion beam and measures its charge as current. With the charge per ion known, integrating current over time gives the number of ions implanted (the dose), and implantation stops when the target is reached. Secondary electrons knocked out by the beam would distort the reading if they escaped, so magnetic fields or electrodes keep them confined.
