Ion implantation

In low-energy implants using decel mode (transporting at high energy and decelerating just before the wafer), what causes the "energy contamination" that requires care?

  1. AIons neutralized before deceleration reaching the wafer at high energy because they are not decelerated
  2. BThe wafer temperature being too low
  3. CThe mass-analysis magnet being too strong
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. Ions neutralized before deceleration reaching the wafer at high energy because they are not decelerated

In deceleration mode, the beam travels at tens of keV and is slowed just before the wafer to implant at hundreds of eV to a few keV—a way to get high current, since low-energy beams blow up from space charge. Particles neutralized by collisions with residual gas en route are not decelerated and arrive at high energy, implanting too deep. Even a few percent ruins shallow junctions, so better vacuum and deflection filters remove them.

  • A. Correct. Neutralized particles arrive without being decelerated.
  • B. Wafer temperature is not the cause of energy contamination.
  • C. The strength of mass analysis is not the cause.
