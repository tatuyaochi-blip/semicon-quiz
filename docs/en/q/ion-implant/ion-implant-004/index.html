Ion implantation

Which unit is used for the dose in ion implantation?

  1. AeV
  2. Bions/cm² (number of ions per unit area)
  3. CΩ/□
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. ions/cm² (number of ions per unit area)

Dose is the number of ions implanted per unit wafer area, expressed in ions/cm² (or atoms/cm²); 10¹⁵ cm⁻², for example, is comparable to the number of atoms on a silicon surface (about 7×10¹⁴ cm⁻²). eV is the unit of acceleration energy and Ω/□ that of sheet resistance, which is commonly used after annealing to verify dose and activation.
