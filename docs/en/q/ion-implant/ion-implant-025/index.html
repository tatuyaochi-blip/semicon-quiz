Ion implantation

When forming shallow p-type junctions, what is the main purpose of implanting carbon or similar species together with boron (co-implantation)?

  1. ATo change p-type into n-type
  2. BTo make the wafer emit light
  3. CTo suppress boron diffusion and keep the junction shallow
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. To suppress boron diffusion and keep the junction shallow

Boron diffusion is enhanced with the help of interstitial silicon (transient enhanced diffusion). Co-implanted carbon traps interstitial silicon, suppressing boron diffusion so the junction stays shallow after annealing.
