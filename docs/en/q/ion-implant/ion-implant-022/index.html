Ion implantation

Which is a characteristic of laser annealing?

  1. AIt heats the whole wafer uniformly for a long time
  2. BIt heats slowly at low temperature
  3. CIt heats only near the wafer surface for an extremely short time, achieving high activation while limiting diffusion
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. It heats only near the wafer surface for an extremely short time, achieving high activation while limiting diffusion

Laser annealing raises only the near-surface region to high temperature for an extremely short time, on the order of microseconds to nanoseconds. Dopants are activated before they can diffuse appreciably, giving shallow, low-resistance junctions. Because it heats underlying layers little, it is also used for back-side processes.
