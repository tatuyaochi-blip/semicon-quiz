Ion implantation

Which correctly pairs the distribution that approximates the depth profile of implanted dopants with the term for the depth of its peak?

  1. AUniform distribution; the peak is always at the surface
  2. BGaussian distribution; the peak depth is called the projected range (Rp)
  3. CExponential distribution; the peak depth is called the diffusion length
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. Gaussian distribution; the peak depth is called the projected range (Rp)

Implanted profiles approximate a Gaussian whose peak depth is the projected range Rp and whose width is the straggle ΔRp; peak concentration is roughly dose ÷ (2.5 × ΔRp). Rp and ΔRp depend on energy and the ion and substrate species and are tabulated. Real profiles are often asymmetric, with channeling tails extending deeper, so more elaborate distribution functions and simulations are used.
