Environment, health and safety

What is appropriate first aid when a chemical gets in the eyes?

  1. AImmediately rinse thoroughly with plenty of running water, such as at an eyewash station, and seek medical attention
  2. BRub the eyes hard to get the chemical out
  3. CWait a while and rinse if the pain continues
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. Immediately rinse thoroughly with plenty of running water, such as at an eyewash station, and seek medical attention

If a chemical gets in the eye, flush immediately with copious running water at an eyewash station, holding the eyelids open, for at least about 15 minutes, then seek medical care promptly. Rubbing can spread corneal damage, and injury progresses while waiting. Alkalis are especially dangerous because they penetrate deep into tissue. First aid can vary by chemical, so check the SDS in advance.
