Environment, health and safety

Which correctly describes a hazard of hydrogen gas, used in epitaxial growth and annealing?

  1. AIts range of concentrations in air that can burn or explode is very wide, about 4 to 75%, and the energy needed to ignite it is small
  2. BIt is heavier than air and tends to pool on the floor
  3. CIt dissolves in water to form a strong acid
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. Its range of concentrations in air that can burn or explode is very wide, about 4 to 75%, and the energy needed to ignite it is small

Hydrogen's flammable range in air is extremely wide at about 4–75 vol%, and its minimum ignition energy, about 0.02 mJ, is less than a tenth of gasoline vapor's, so even slight static can ignite it. Its small molecules leak easily, and its flame is nearly invisible. Being much lighter than air, it collects near ceilings, so detection and ventilation there and leak control in piping are important. It does not dissolve in water to form an acid.

  • A. Correct. It is a gas with a wide explosive range that ignites easily.
  • B. Hydrogen is the lightest gas and collects high up.
  • C. Hydrogen does not dissolve in water to form an acid.
