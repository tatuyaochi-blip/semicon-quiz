Environment, health and safety

Which is an appropriate environmental initiative for the large amounts of water used in semiconductor fabs?

  1. ADischarge all used water into the sea untreated
  2. BTreat and recover wastewater and reuse it, for example as feed water for ultrapure water
  3. CDo not record water usage
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. Treat and recover wastewater and reuse it, for example as feed water for ultrapure water

Fabs use vast amounts of ultrapure water for wafer cleaning, reaching tens of thousands of tonnes a day at large sites. Relatively clean rinse water is therefore reclaimed and treated for reuse as UPW feed or cooling water, raising recovery rates. In water-scarce regions, water availability also affects site selection. Discharged water is treated to meet legal standards, and usage is recorded and managed.
