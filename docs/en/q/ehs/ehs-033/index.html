Environment, health and safety

What is the purpose of reporting near misses, incidents that caused no injury but gave someone a scare?

  1. ATo punish the person who reported
  2. BTo time the work
  3. CTo find small hazards behind serious accidents early and prevent accidents by addressing them
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. To find small hazards behind serious accidents early and prevent accidents by addressing them

Heinrich's law holds that behind every serious accident lie 29 minor accidents and 300 near misses. Collecting and analyzing near misses and improving equipment and procedures prevents serious accidents before they happen. Not blaming reporters is essential to fostering an atmosphere where people report. It is not for timing work or punishing anyone.

  • A. Blaming reporters means reports stop coming in.
  • B. Timing work is not the purpose.
  • C. Correct. It is an activity for addressing hazards while they are still small.
