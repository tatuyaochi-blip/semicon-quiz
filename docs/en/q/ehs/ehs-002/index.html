Environment, health and safety

Which correctly describes an SDS (safety data sheet)?

  1. AA maintenance manual for manufacturing equipment
  2. BA document describing a chemical's hazards, handling methods, first aid and more
  3. CA report of outgoing inspection results for a product
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. A document describing a chemical's hazards, handling methods, first aid and more

An SDS is a document that a supplier gives the recipient when transferring or providing a chemical or a product containing one. It describes components, hazards, handling and storage, exposure controls, first-aid measures, disposal methods and more. Checking its contents before handling a chemical is fundamental.
