Environment, health and safety

Which Japanese system requires businesses to determine and report to the government their releases of certain chemicals to the environment and their transfers as waste?

  1. AGHS
  2. BRoHS
  3. CPRTR (Pollutant Release and Transfer Register)
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. PRTR (Pollutant Release and Transfer Register)

Japan's PRTR (pollutant release and transfer register) system, under the Chemical Substances Control Law, requires businesses handling listed chemicals above set amounts to report annually their releases to air, water and soil and transfers as waste or to sewers. Aggregated results are published, and residents can view data by facility. Rather than banning use, it promotes voluntary management and reduction through disclosure. GHS concerns labeling and RoHS is an EU regulation.

  • A. GHS is a system for labeling chemical hazards.
  • B. RoHS is the EU restriction on specific hazardous substances.
  • C. Correct. It is a system for reporting releases and transfers.
