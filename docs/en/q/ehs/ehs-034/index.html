Environment, health and safety

Which set correctly gives the "5S" widely practiced as a basis of workplace safety and quality?

  1. ASpeed, accuracy, savings, quality and trust
  2. BSort, set in order, shine, standardize and sustain (seiri, seiton, seiso, seiketsu, shitsuke)
  3. CDesign, manufacturing, testing, shipping and maintenance
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. Sort, set in order, shine, standardize and sustain (seiri, seiton, seiso, seiketsu, shitsuke)

The 5S are seiri (sort out the unnecessary), seiton (set in order for quick retrieval), seiso (shine, i.e., clean), seiketsu (standardize to maintain the state) and shitsuke (sustain the discipline), all beginning with S in romanized Japanese. Clear aisles and fixed locations for chemicals help prevent accidents and mix-ups, and in cleanrooms 5S also reduces particle generation.

  • A. These words are not the 5S.
  • B. Correct. Sort, set in order, shine, standardize and sustain.
  • C. That is a different matter from product life-cycle stages.
