Environment, health and safety

In workplaces handling hazardous chemicals, what is it called to regularly measure concentrations of hazardous substances in the air and evaluate the work environment?

  1. AProduct inspection
  2. BWork environment measurement
  3. CStatistical process control
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. Work environment measurement

Workplace environment measurement samples airborne concentrations of hazardous substances to evaluate whether conditions protect worker health. In Japan, the Industrial Safety and Health Act specifies which workplaces must be measured and how often, and certified measurement specialists perform it. Results are rated into control classes, and poor results prompt measures such as improving local exhaust. Product inspection and SPC are quality-control methods.
