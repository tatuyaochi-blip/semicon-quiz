Environment, health and safety

In workplaces handling hazardous chemicals, what is it called to regularly measure concentrations of hazardous substances in the air and evaluate the work environment?

  1. AProduct inspection
  2. BWork environment measurement
  3. CStatistical process control
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. Work environment measurement

Work environment measurement measures concentrations of hazardous substances and the like in workplace air to evaluate whether an environment protecting workers' health is maintained. Laws specify which workplaces are covered and how often to measure, and countermeasures such as equipment improvements are taken based on the results.
