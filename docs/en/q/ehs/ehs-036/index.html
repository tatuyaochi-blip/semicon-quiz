Environment, health and safety

In calculating a company's greenhouse gas emissions, which category covers indirect emissions from the use of purchased electricity and heat?

  1. AScope 1
  2. BScope 3
  3. CScope 2
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. Scope 2

The GHG Protocol classifies direct emissions such as fuel combustion and process gases as Scope 1, emissions from generating purchased electricity and heat as Scope 2, and all other indirect supply-chain emissions as Scope 3. Because fabs consume huge amounts of electricity, Scope 2 accounts for a large share, making procurement of renewable electricity important.

  • A. Scope 1 is the company's direct emissions.
  • B. Scope 3 is the other indirect emissions in the supply chain.
  • C. Correct. It is indirect emissions from purchased electricity and heat.
