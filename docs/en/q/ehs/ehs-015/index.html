Environment, health and safety

Which correctly describes the facilities that account for a large share of energy consumption in semiconductor fabs?

  1. ALighting only
  2. BIn addition to manufacturing equipment, utility facilities such as cleanroom air conditioning, ultrapure water production and exhaust treatment
  3. COffice PCs
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. In addition to manufacturing equipment, utility facilities such as cleanroom air conditioning, ultrapure water production and exhaust treatment

In semiconductor fabs, besides the manufacturing equipment itself, utility facilities use much electricity: cleanroom air conditioning that circulates huge volumes of air, ultrapure water production, exhaust and wastewater treatment, cooling water and more. Improving their efficiency is an important theme for fab energy saving and greenhouse gas reduction.
