Environment, health and safety

Which device absorbs and removes acidic gases and the like from exhaust gas by bringing it into contact with water or chemical solutions?

  1. AULPA filter
  2. BOHT
  3. CWet scrubber
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. Wet scrubber

A wet scrubber brings exhaust gas into contact with showers of water or neutralizing solutions, absorbing and removing water-soluble acidic and basic gases. It is used for fab-wide exhaust treatment and downstream of tool-level abatement systems.
