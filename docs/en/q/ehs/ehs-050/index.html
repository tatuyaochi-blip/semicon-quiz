Environment, health and safety

In managing chemical exposure, which limit is set for short exposures such as 15 minutes, separately from the 8-hour average value?

  1. ALower explosive limit (LEL)
  2. BShort-term exposure limit (STEL)
  3. CFlash point
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. Short-term exposure limit (STEL)

Even if the 8-hour time-weighted average is below the limit, brief exposure to high concentrations can cause irritation or acute poisoning. The short-term exposure limit (STEL) caps the average over a short period such as 15 minutes, and some strongly irritating substances also have ceiling values that must never be exceeded even momentarily. These matter especially for short tasks such as chemical changeouts or leak response. LEL and flash point relate to combustion.

  • A. The LEL is the lowest concentration that can burn or explode.
  • B. Correct. It is the upper limit for short-term exposure.
  • C. The flash point is the lowest temperature at which a liquid's vapor can ignite.
