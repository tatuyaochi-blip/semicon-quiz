Environment, health and safety

For chamber cleaning of CVD equipment, which method has been adopted to reduce PFC emissions?

  1. AIncrease cleaning with C2F6
  2. BRemote plasma, which decomposes NF3 outside the chamber and cleans efficiently with the resulting fluorine radicals
  3. CDo no cleaning at all
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. Remote plasma, which decomposes NF3 outside the chamber and cleans efficiently with the resulting fluorine radicals

Chamber cleaning once used PFCs such as C2F6 activated by plasma inside the chamber, and much of the gas left undecomposed. Remote plasma sources decompose NF3 outside the tool with over 95% efficiency and send only fluorine radicals into the chamber, greatly cutting greenhouse gas emissions and reducing damage to chamber parts. Neither increasing C2F6 use nor skipping cleaning is an option.
