Environment, health and safety

Which combination of protective equipment is appropriate for work handling acid and alkali chemicals?

  1. ACotton work gloves and ordinary glasses
  2. BChemical-resistant gloves, safety goggles (face shield) and a chemical-resistant apron
  3. CA dust mask only
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. Chemical-resistant gloves, safety goggles (face shield) and a chemical-resistant apron

Chemical splashes on skin or eyes can cause severe chemical burns, so workers wear chemical-resistant gloves suited to the chemical, safety goggles or face shields, and aprons. Glove material is chosen for each chemical. Cotton work gloves soak up liquid and hold it against the skin, and ordinary glasses do not stop splashes from the side. Workers also confirm the locations of emergency showers and eyewash stations in advance.
