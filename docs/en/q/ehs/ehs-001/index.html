Environment, health and safety

Which correctly describes the handling of hydrofluoric acid?

  1. AIf it contacts skin, it can penetrate deep and cause serious injury
  2. BIt does not volatilize, so ventilation is unnecessary
  3. COnce diluted with water, it may be handled with bare hands
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. If it contacts skin, it can penetrate deep and cause serious injury

Hydrofluoric acid penetrates deep through skin, and fluoride ions bind calcium and magnesium, destroying tissue; large exposures can lower blood calcium and cause arrhythmia. At low concentrations pain may appear only hours later, delaying recognition. It is volatile and requires ventilation, and even diluted it must never be handled bare-handed. On contact, rinse immediately with copious water and seek medical care.
