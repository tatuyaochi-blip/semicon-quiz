Environment, health and safety

Which EU regulation requires businesses that manufacture or import chemicals to register substances and submit safety information, and manages the use of substances of very high concern?

  1. ARoHS Directive
  2. BREACH Regulation
  3. CISO 9001
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. REACH Regulation

REACH, in force since 2007, is the EU regulation covering registration, evaluation, authorisation and restriction of chemicals. It requires companies manufacturing or importing a chemical at one tonne or more per year to register it and requires communication when substances of very high concern (SVHCs) exceed 0.1 wt% in articles. It differs in scope and mechanism from the RoHS directive, which restricts specific hazardous substances in products.

  • A. The RoHS Directive restricts specific hazardous substances in electrical and electronic equipment.
  • B. Correct. It is the regulation on registration, evaluation, authorisation and restriction of chemicals.
  • C. ISO 9001 is the standard for quality management systems.
