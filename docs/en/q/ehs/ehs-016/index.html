Environment, health and safety

In storing chemicals, why are substances that may react violently when mixed kept apart?

  1. ATo prevent heat generation, toxic gas release and fires from mixing
  2. BTo make them look uniform
  3. CTo lower storage costs
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. To prevent heat generation, toxic gas release and fires from mixing

Some combinations, such as acids and bases, oxidizers and combustibles, or HF and glass, react violently when mixed, generating heat, toxic gas or fire. Mixing hypochlorite with acid, for example, releases toxic chlorine gas. Incompatible chemicals are stored in separate areas, each with its own containment dike, so they cannot mix even if they leak. It is not for appearance or cost.
