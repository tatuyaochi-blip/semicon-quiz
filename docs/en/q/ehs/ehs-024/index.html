Environment, health and safety

Which is the correct combination for the '3Rs', a philosophy for reducing waste?

  1. ARepair, Reform, Return
  2. BReset, Reload, Restart
  3. CReduce, Reuse, Recycle
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. Reduce, Reuse, Recycle

The 3Rs are Reduce (generate less waste), Reuse (use items repeatedly) and Recycle (reprocess into resources), prioritized in that order. Fabs reduce chemical and gas usage, reuse items such as monitor wafers reclaimed by polishing and cleaned FOUPs, and recycle waste liquids. The other options are unrelated words.
