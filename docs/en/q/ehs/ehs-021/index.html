Environment, health and safety

Which is the correct basic action when a toxic gas leak alarm sounds?

  1. AApproach to look for the leak yourself
  2. BSilence the alarm and continue working
  3. CEvacuate promptly following the established procedure, while designated personnel carry out responses such as shutting off the gas
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. Evacuate promptly following the established procedure, while designated personnel carry out responses such as shutting off the gas

Approaching a toxic gas leak carelessly risks inhaling the gas and suffering serious harm. When the alarm sounds, evacuate promptly by designated routes to a safe upwind location, while trained responders wearing protective equipment and breathing apparatus isolate the gas according to procedure. Silencing the alarm and continuing work is unthinkable. Regular drills and headcounts after evacuation are essential.
