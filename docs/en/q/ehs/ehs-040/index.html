Environment, health and safety

Which group of substances, which hardly break down in the environment and are called "forever chemicals", poses regulatory compliance and substitution challenges for the semiconductor industry because they are used in resist materials and the like?

  1. APFAS (per- and polyfluoroalkyl substances)
  2. BGlucose
  3. CSodium chloride
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. PFAS (per- and polyfluoroalkyl substances)

PFAS is the umbrella term for thousands of organofluorine compounds with strong carbon–fluorine bonds; their persistence in the environment and the body has prompted tightening regulation in Europe and the US. Semiconductors use them in resist photoacid generators, antireflective coatings, surfactants, and fluoropolymer piping and parts. Few materials can match their performance, so substitute development and emission control proceed in parallel.

  • A. Correct. It is the umbrella term for persistent organofluorine compounds.
  • B. Glucose is a sugar that breaks down easily.
  • C. Sodium chloride is table salt, not a PFAS.
