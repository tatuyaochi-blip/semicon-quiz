Environment, health and safety

What is the safety advantage of storing and supplying toxic gases such as arsine and phosphine adsorbed on a sorbent in containers below atmospheric pressure?

  1. AEven if the container leaks, the gas is unlikely to gush out
  2. BThe gas loses its toxicity
  3. CGas detectors become unnecessary
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. Even if the container leaks, the gas is unlikely to gush out

With high-pressure cylinders, a broken valve or line can release a large amount of gas at once. In containers that store gas adsorbed on a sorbent below atmospheric pressure (sub-atmospheric delivery), a leak tends instead to draw air in, greatly reducing the risk of a massive release of toxic gas. Since the gas itself remains toxic, detection and exhaust measures are still required.

  • A. Correct. The low pressure reduces the risk of a massive release.
  • B. The toxicity of the gas itself does not change.
  • C. Measures such as detectors and exhaust are still required.
