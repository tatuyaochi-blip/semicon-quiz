CPUs and architecture

If 90% of a program can be parallelized, what is the theoretical maximum speedup when it runs on an unlimited number of processors?

  1. A10 times
  2. B90 times
  3. CInfinite
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. 10 times

Amdahl's law gives speedup as 1/((1 − p) + p/N) for parallel fraction p and N processors. With p = 0.9 the limit as N grows is 1/0.1 = 10×, and even N = 10 yields only about 5.3×. Even a small serial portion caps the speedup, so reducing it is crucial. Gustafson's law takes the complementary view of scaling the problem size to grow the parallel part.
