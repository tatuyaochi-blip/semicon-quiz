CPUs and architecture

If 90% of a program can be parallelized, what is the theoretical maximum speedup when it runs on an unlimited number of processors?

  1. A10 times
  2. B90 times
  3. CInfinite
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. 10 times

According to Amdahl's law, the part that cannot be parallelized (10%) does not get shorter no matter how many processors are added, so the total execution time cannot fall below 10% of the original. The maximum speedup is therefore 1 ÷ 0.1 = 10 times. This shows the importance of reducing the non-parallelizable part.
