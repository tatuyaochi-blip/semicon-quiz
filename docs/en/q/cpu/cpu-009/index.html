CPUs and architecture

If the cache access time is 10 ns, the main memory access time is 100 ns, and the cache hit rate is 90%, what is the average access time?

  1. A19 ns
  2. B55 ns
  3. C91 ns
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. 19 ns

Average access time = 0.9 × 10 + 0.1 × 100 = 19 ns (assuming a miss costs only the main-memory time). At a 99% hit rate it becomes 0.99 × 10 + 0.01 × 100 = 10.9 ns—about 1.7× faster from a 9-point change. Because miss rate dominates performance this way, caches use multiple levels and tuned capacity and associativity to maximize hit rate.
