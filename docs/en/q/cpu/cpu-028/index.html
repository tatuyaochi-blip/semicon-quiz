CPUs and architecture

Which metric indicates the amount of heat generated under a standard heavy load, used as a guide for CPU cooling design?

  1. ACPI
  2. BIPC
  3. CTDP (thermal design power)
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. TDP (thermal design power)

TDP is the heat output used as the target for cooling design, expressed in watts; coolers are designed to dissipate it. Modern CPUs boost above TDP for short periods when thermal and power headroom allows, so actual peak power can exceed TDP. Some vendors therefore specify sustained power and short-term maximum power separately.
