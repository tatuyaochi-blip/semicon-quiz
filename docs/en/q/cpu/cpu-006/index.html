CPUs and architecture

What is the period of a 1 GHz clock?

  1. A1 ms
  2. B1 µs
  3. C1 ns
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. 1 ns

Period is the inverse of frequency: 1 GHz = 10⁹ Hz, so the period is 1/10⁹ s = 1 ns. In that time light travels only about 30 cm, and signals on on-chip wires travel far less. In CPUs running at several GHz, a signal may not cross the whole chip within one clock, so wire delay becomes a factor that limits clock frequency.
