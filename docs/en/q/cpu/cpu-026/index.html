CPUs and architecture

Which technique reduces CPU power consumption by dynamically changing operating frequency and supply voltage according to the load?

  1. ADVFS (dynamic voltage and frequency scaling)
  2. BOverclocking
  3. CSIMD
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. DVFS (dynamic voltage and frequency scaling)

DVFS lowers frequency and supply voltage when the load is light and raises them when it is heavy. Dynamic power is proportional to the square of the voltage and to frequency, so lowering both together greatly reduces power. It is widely used in smartphones and laptops.
