Emerging memories

Which effect, being studied to reduce MRAM write energy, changes the magnetic anisotropy of a magnetic layer with a voltage?

  1. AThe Seebeck effect
  2. BVCMA (voltage-controlled magnetic anisotropy)
  3. CThe piezoresistive effect
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. VCMA (voltage-controlled magnetic anisotropy)

VCMA (voltage-controlled magnetic anisotropy) is the effect whereby applying voltage across a thin magnetic layer and its insulating interface (such as MgO) alters the interfacial electronic states and thus the magnetic anisotropy. Because it assists switching with almost no current, it could cut write energy by one to two orders of magnitude compared with current-driven STT. Precisely timing the pulse to stop the reversal is the challenge.

  • A. The Seebeck effect is a thermoelectric effect in which a temperature difference produces a voltage.
  • B. Correct. It changes magnetic anisotropy with voltage.
  • C. The piezoresistive effect is a change in resistance with stress, used in pressure sensors and the like.
