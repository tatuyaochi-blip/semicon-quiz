Emerging memories

Which phenomenon becomes a problem when phase-change memory is scaled, in which heat from writing reaches a neighboring cell and changes its state?

  1. ARow hammer
  2. BElectrostatic discharge
  3. CThermal disturb (thermal crosstalk)
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. Thermal disturb (thermal crosstalk)

Writing PCM heats the material to hundreds of degrees (over 600 °C for reset). With cell spacing below tens of nm, heat can spread to neighbors and unintentionally crystallize amorphous cells—thermal disturb. It is countered with structures that confine heating to a narrow electrode region (such as the mushroom cell) and with thermally insulating dielectrics around cells.
