Emerging memories

Which correctly describes the tunnel magnetoresistance (TMR) ratio of an MTJ?

  1. AThe difference between the antiparallel and parallel resistances divided by the parallel resistance
  2. BThe ratio of insulator thickness to magnetic layer thickness
  3. CThe maximum current that can flow through the MTJ
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. The difference between the antiparallel and parallel resistances divided by the parallel resistance

The TMR ratio is (R_antiparallel − R_parallel)/R_parallel, expressing how different the two resistance states are; with R_parallel = 2 kΩ and R_antiparallel = 5 kΩ it is 150%. A higher ratio makes 0 and 1 easier to distinguish and reads faster. Values above 600% at room temperature have been reported with MgO barriers, but production MRAM runs around 100–200% given small cell size and thermal-budget constraints.

  • A. Correct. It is the ratio of the resistance difference between the two states.
  • B. It is not a ratio of film thicknesses.
  • C. The maximum current is a separate property from the TMR ratio.
