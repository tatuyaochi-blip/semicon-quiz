Emerging memories

What kind of material is GST, the typical recording material for phase-change memory?

  1. AAn alloy of gallium, silicon and titanium
  2. BAn alloy of gold, silver and tin
  3. CA compound of germanium, antimony and tellurium (such as Ge2Sb2Te5)
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. A compound of germanium, antimony and tellurium (such as Ge2Sb2Te5)

GST is a chalcogenide of germanium (Ge), antimony (Sb) and tellurium (Te), typically Ge2Sb2Te5. It combines fast crystallization within tens of nanoseconds with a stable amorphous phase at room temperature, and its resistance and optical reflectivity differ greatly between phases. Its crystallization temperature of about 150 °C is relatively low, so Ge-rich compositions are used for high-temperature applications such as automotive.
