Emerging memories

What is the structure called that stores one bit in MRAM, consisting of two magnetic layers sandwiching a thin insulating layer?

  1. APN junction
  2. BSchottky junction
  3. CMagnetic tunnel junction (MTJ)
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. Magnetic tunnel junction (MTJ)

An MRAM storage element is a magnetic tunnel junction (MTJ): two magnetic layers (such as CoFeB) separated by an insulating tunnel barrier (such as MgO) about 1 nm thick. Electrons cross the barrier by quantum tunneling, and how easily they do so depends on the relative magnetization of the two sides. Real MTJs are stacks of more than ten layers, including many that stabilize the reference layer.

  • A. A PN junction is a junction of p-type and n-type semiconductors.
  • B. A Schottky junction is a metal-semiconductor junction.
  • C. Correct. It is an MTJ with an insulator between magnetic layers.
