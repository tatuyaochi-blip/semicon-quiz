Emerging memories

In an MTJ, how do the resistances compare when the magnetizations of the two magnetic layers are parallel versus antiparallel?

  1. AThe resistance is the same in both cases
  2. BThe resistance is higher when parallel
  3. CThe resistance is lower when parallel
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. The resistance is lower when parallel

When the magnetizations are parallel, electrons with matching spin tunnel through the insulator easily, so the resistance is low. When antiparallel, tunneling is harder and the resistance is high. This resistance difference distinguishes 0 from 1.

  • A. If the resistances were equal, the information could not be read.
  • B. The resistance is higher in the antiparallel state.
  • C. Correct. The resistance is lower when parallel.
