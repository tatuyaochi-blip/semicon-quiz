Emerging memories

Why have MTJs with magnetization perpendicular to the film plane (perpendicular MTJs) become mainstream in recent STT-MRAM?

  1. ABecause no magnetic materials are needed to manufacture them
  2. BBecause they maintain thermal stability more easily when scaled and allow lower write currents
  3. CBecause light can be used for reading
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. Because they maintain thermal stability more easily when scaled and allow lower write currents

In-plane MTJs rely on cell shape, such as an ellipse, to stabilize magnetization, so stability is lost as they shrink. Perpendicular MTJs use perpendicular magnetic anisotropy arising at interfaces such as CoFeB/MgO, keeping even small round cells thermally stable while lowering write current. This materials advance made volume production of STT-MRAM possible in the late 2010s.
