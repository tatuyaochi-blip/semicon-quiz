Emerging memories

Which is an example of a material researched and used as the switching layer of ReRAM?

  1. APure copper
  2. BMetal oxides such as hafnium oxide and tantalum oxide
  3. CSingle-crystal silicon
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. Metal oxides such as hafnium oxide and tantalum oxide

ReRAM often uses metal oxides such as hafnium oxide, tantalum oxide and titanium oxide. Voltage forms or dissolves conductive paths made of clustered oxygen vacancies in the film, changing its resistance. Compatibility with materials already used in CMOS processes is another advantage.
