Emerging memories

Which is an example of a material researched and used as the switching layer of ReRAM?

  1. APure copper
  2. BMetal oxides such as hafnium oxide and tantalum oxide
  3. CSingle-crystal silicon
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. Metal oxides such as hafnium oxide and tantalum oxide

ReRAM commonly uses metal oxides such as hafnium oxide, tantalum oxide and titanium oxide. Voltage moves oxygen ions, and conductive paths of clustered oxygen vacancies form or disappear, changing resistance. Hafnium and tantalum oxides are already used in CMOS gate dielectrics and capacitors, so existing equipment and processes can be readily reused.
