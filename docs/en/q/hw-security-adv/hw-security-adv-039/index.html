Hardware Security: Advanced Topics and Trends

A fault injection attempt succeeds with a probability of 0.1% each time, and the results of attempts are independent. Which is closest to the probability of at least one success in 1,000 attempts?

  1. Aabout 0.1%
  2. Babout 63%
  3. C100%
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. about 63%

The probability of no success at all is (1 − 0.001)¹⁰⁰⁰ ≈ e⁻¹ ≈ 0.368, so the probability of at least one success is 1 − 0.368 ≈ 0.632, about 63%. Even with a low success rate per attempt, an attack that can be tried thousands of times automatically is quite likely to succeed. Countermeasures therefore combine lowering the per-attempt success rate with mechanisms such as erasing keys after a few detected anomalies or counting and limiting attempts. Simply computing 0.1% × 1,000 = 100% is an error that double-counts cases with more than one success.

  • A. This is the probability for a single attempt.
  • B. Correct. 1 − 0.999¹⁰⁰⁰ ≈ 63%.
  • C. This simply multiplies 0.1% × 1,000, which is wrong as a probability calculation.
