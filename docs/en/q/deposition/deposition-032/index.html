Deposition (CVD and PVD)

Which correctly describes the basic way an LSI is built in manufacturing?

  1. AParts are glued together
  2. BSteps such as deposition, lithography and etching are repeated dozens of times to build up layers
  3. CThe wafer is processed just once to finish it
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. Steps such as deposition, lithography and etching are repeated dozens of times to build up layers

An LSI is built by repeating deposition, lithography (pattern transfer), etching, doping, planarization and cleaning to stack transistors and more than ten layers of wiring. Advanced logic exceeds 1000 process steps and takes around three months from wafer start to completion, with inspection and metrology at every stage.

  • A. The interior of an LSI is not glued together but built by repeated steps.
  • B. Correct. Steps are repeated to build up layers.
  • C. It cannot be made in a single processing step.
