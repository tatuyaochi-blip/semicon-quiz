Deposition (CVD and PVD)

What is generally done to increase the deposition rate in sputtering?

  1. AIncrease the power applied to the target
  2. BMake the gas pressure extremely high
  3. CReduce the power to the target to zero
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. Increase the power applied to the target

Raising target power increases the number and energy of ions striking the target, ejecting more atoms, so deposition rate rises roughly in proportion to power; 300 mm targets may run at tens of kilowatts. Most of that power becomes heat, so targets are water-cooled to prevent overheating, cracking or melting of the bonding layer, and effects on film quality must be watched.
