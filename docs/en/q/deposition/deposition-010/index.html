Deposition (CVD and PVD)

What is the method called in which liquid material is dropped onto the center of a wafer and spun at high speed to form a uniform film?

  1. ASputtering
  2. BSpin coating
  3. CIon implantation
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. Spin coating

Spin coating dispenses liquid at the wafer center and spins it at thousands of rpm, spreading it by centrifugal force and flinging off the excess to form a uniform film. Thickness scales roughly inversely with the square root of spin speed and is tuned by viscosity, achieving within-wafer variation of a few nm. It applies photoresists, anti-reflective coatings, spin-on glass (SOG) and polyimide passivation, though most of the liquid is spun off, so material efficiency is low.
