Deposition (CVD and PVD)

What is the method called in which liquid material is dropped onto the center of a wafer and spun at high speed to form a uniform film?

  1. ASputtering
  2. BSpin coating
  3. CIon implantation
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. Spin coating

In spin coating, centrifugal force spreads the liquid and throws off the excess, forming a uniform film. Thickness is adjusted by spin speed and liquid viscosity. It is used to apply photoresist and to form spin-on insulating films (SOG).
