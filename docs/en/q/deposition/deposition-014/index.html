Deposition (CVD and PVD)

Which correctly describes uses of silicon nitride (Si3N4) films?

  1. AAs the main material for gate electrodes and wiring
  2. BAs the oxidation mask for LOCOS, the CMP stop layer, and the surface protective film (passivation)
  3. CAs a substitute for photoresist
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. As the oxidation mask for LOCOS, the CMP stop layer, and the surface protective film (passivation)

Silicon nitride resists the passage of oxygen and moisture and is hard, so it is used as the mask for local oxidation (LOCOS), the CMP stop layer in STI formation, and passivation that protects the chip surface from moisture and mobile ions. It is also used as an etch stop and hard mask.
