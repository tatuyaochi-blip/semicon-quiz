Deposition (CVD and PVD)

Which source gas is commonly used to deposit polysilicon by LPCVD?

  1. AArgon
  2. BSilane (SiH4)
  3. CCarbon dioxide
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. Silane (SiH4)

Polysilicon is deposited by thermally decomposing silane at around 600 °C. Grain size changes with deposition temperature, and at lower temperatures the film becomes amorphous silicon. It is used for gate electrodes, resistors, capacitor electrodes and more.
