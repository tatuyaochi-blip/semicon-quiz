Deposition (CVD and PVD)

What is the main advantage of plasma CVD (PECVD)?

  1. AIt requires higher temperatures than thermal CVD
  2. BThe film contains no hydrogen at all
  3. CIt can deposit films at lower temperatures than thermal CVD
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. It can deposit films at lower temperatures than thermal CVD

Plasma CVD uses plasma energy to decompose and activate source gases, so films can be deposited at lower temperatures than thermal CVD, which relies on heat alone. It is used in steps that cannot tolerate high temperatures, such as after wiring is formed. On the other hand, hydrogen from the source gases tends to be incorporated into the film.
