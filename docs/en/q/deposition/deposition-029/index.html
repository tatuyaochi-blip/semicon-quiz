Deposition (CVD and PVD)

What is an advantage of vacuum evaporation that heats material with an electron beam?

  1. AIt can evaporate even high-melting-point materials
  2. BIts step coverage is very high
  3. CIt forms films through chemical reactions of source gases
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. It can evaporate even high-melting-point materials

Electron-beam evaporation focuses an electron beam accelerated through several kV onto the source material, vaporizing refractory metals such as tungsten and molybdenum and oxides that resistive heating cannot, with little crucible contamination. Atoms travel in straight lines, giving poor step coverage—which is exploited in lift-off, where film deposited over patterned resist is removed along with the resist to define electrodes.
