Deposition (CVD and PVD)

In deposition and etching equipment, which mechanism holds the wafer on the stage by electrostatic force?

  1. AVacuum tweezers
  2. BElectrostatic chuck (ESC)
  3. CFOUP
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. Electrostatic chuck (ESC)

An electrostatic chuck applies voltage to electrodes inside the stage, and the electrostatic force between them and the wafer holds the wafer in place. It works in vacuum and holds the entire wafer uniformly, which also makes wafer temperature easier to control.
