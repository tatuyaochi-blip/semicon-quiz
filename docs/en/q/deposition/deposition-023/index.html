Deposition (CVD and PVD)

What is an advantage of plasma-enhanced ALD (PEALD)?

  1. AHigh temperatures are required for deposition
  2. BThick films are obtained quickly in a single step
  3. CUsing highly reactive radicals allows deposition at lower temperatures than thermal ALD and broadens the range of usable materials
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. Using highly reactive radicals allows deposition at lower temperatures than thermal ALD and broadens the range of usable materials

PEALD uses plasma in the reaction step to produce highly reactive radicals, so films can be deposited at lower temperatures than thermal ALD, which relies on heat alone. It can also be used with material combinations that do not react thermally. On the other hand, radicals have difficulty reaching the bottom of deep structures, so step coverage can be inferior to thermal ALD.
