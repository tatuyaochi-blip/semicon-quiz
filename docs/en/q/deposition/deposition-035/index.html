Deposition (CVD and PVD)

What does "sputter yield" mean in sputtering?

  1. AThe time required for deposition
  2. BThe fraction of good chips per wafer
  3. CThe number of atoms ejected per ion striking the target
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. The number of atoms ejected per ion striking the target

Sputter yield expresses how many atoms are ejected for each ion that strikes the target. It depends on ion energy and mass, angle of incidence and target material, and is one factor that sets the deposition rate.

  • A. It is not a time.
  • B. The fraction of good chips is the yield in the manufacturing sense.
  • C. Correct. It is the number of atoms ejected per ion.
