Deposition (CVD and PVD)

Which correctly describes silicon nitride deposited by plasma CVD?

  1. AIt contains relatively much hydrogen and tends to be less dense than thermal CVD film
  2. BIt is perfectly stoichiometric (Si3N4) and contains no hydrogen
  3. CIt conducts electricity like a metal
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. It contains relatively much hydrogen and tends to be less dense than thermal CVD film

Plasma CVD silicon nitride is formed by reacting silane, ammonia and the like at low temperatures of about 300 to 400 °C, so it contains much hydrogen in the form of Si-H and N-H. It therefore tends to be less dense and etch faster than thermal CVD film. It is used, for example, as a protective (passivation) layer.

  • A. Correct. It contains much hydrogen and is less dense.
  • B. Being deposited at low temperature, it contains hydrogen and its composition deviates easily.
  • C. Silicon nitride is an insulator.
