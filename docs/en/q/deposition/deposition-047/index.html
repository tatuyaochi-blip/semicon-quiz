Deposition (CVD and PVD)

Which model organizes, by temperature and gas pressure, the tendency of sputtered metal films to form columnar grains with many voids at low deposition temperatures?

  1. AThornton's structure zone model
  2. BThe Poisson model
  3. CThe Deal-Grove model
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. Thornton's structure zone model

Thornton's structure zone model organizes how film microstructure changes with substrate temperature (relative to the material's melting point) and sputtering gas pressure, from porous columnar structure to dense fibrous structure, large columnar grains and recrystallized equiaxed grains. It serves as a guide to film density, stress and resistivity.

  • A. Correct. It organizes film structure by temperature and gas pressure.
  • B. The Poisson model is used for yield calculations and similar.
  • C. The Deal-Grove model describes thermal oxide growth.
