Deposition (CVD and PVD)

Which is an advantage of sputtering over vacuum evaporation?

  1. AThe energy of the depositing atoms is low
  2. BThe deposition rate is always higher
  3. CFilms can be deposited while largely preserving the composition of alloy or compound targets, with good adhesion
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. Films can be deposited while largely preserving the composition of alloy or compound targets, with good adhesion

In evaporation, each component of an alloy evaporates with different ease, so the film composition tends to shift. Sputtering physically ejects atoms from the target surface, so composition is largely preserved. The arriving atoms also have higher energy, giving better film adhesion.

  • A. Sputtered atoms have higher energy than evaporated ones, which improves adhesion.
  • B. Depending on material and conditions, evaporation can be faster.
  • C. Correct. Preserving composition and good adhesion are its advantages.
