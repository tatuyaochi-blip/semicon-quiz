Deposition (CVD and PVD)

Which technique uses differences in surface materials to grow films only in specific places?

  1. ASputtering
  2. BBlanket deposition
  3. CArea-selective deposition
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. Area-selective deposition

Area-selective deposition uses differences in how readily the underlying material reacts to deposit films only where needed, for example on metal but not on insulator. It can eliminate lithography and etching steps and has no alignment errors, so it is being researched as a means of further scaling.
