Deposition (CVD and PVD)

Which is an example of the precursors used to deposit HfO2 gate dielectrics by ALD?

  1. ASilane and ammonia
  2. BTungsten hexafluoride and hydrogen
  3. CHafnium tetrachloride (HfCl4) or organohafnium compounds, with an oxidant such as water or ozone
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. Hafnium tetrachloride (HfCl4) or organohafnium compounds, with an oxidant such as water or ozone

HfO2 ALD alternately supplies a hafnium source such as HfCl4 or TEMAHf and an oxidant such as H2O or O3, depositing one layer at a time by surface reactions. The choice of source changes the deposition temperature and film impurities (chlorine or carbon).

  • A. Silane and ammonia are sources for silicon nitride.
  • B. Tungsten hexafluoride and hydrogen are sources for tungsten CVD.
  • C. Correct. A hafnium source and an oxidant are supplied alternately.
