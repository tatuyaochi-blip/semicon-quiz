Deposition (CVD and PVD)

Which liquid organosilicon compound is widely used as a source for CVD of silicon oxide?

  1. ATungsten hexafluoride (WF6)
  2. BTEOS (tetraethoxysilane)
  3. CTrimethylgallium
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. TEOS (tetraethoxysilane)

TEOS (Si(OC2H5)4) is an organosilicon compound that is liquid at room temperature and is vaporized as a CVD precursor. It is safer to handle than pyrophoric silane, and its surface-driven decomposition gives conformal oxide with good step coverage. It is used in thermal CVD (about 700 °C), plasma CVD (about 400 °C) and atmospheric-pressure CVD with ozone, among others, for interlayer dielectrics, sidewall spacers and more.
