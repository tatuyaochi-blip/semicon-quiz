Deposition (CVD and PVD)

What is it called when a gas containing a dopant such as phosphorus is flowed during LPCVD polysilicon deposition to make a conductive film?

  1. AIn-situ doping
  2. BIon implantation
  3. CThermal oxidation
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. In-situ doping

In-situ doping flows dopant gases such as phosphine or diborane during deposition so impurities are incorporated as the film grows. No later implant or diffusion is needed, and films lining deep trenches or holes—such as DRAM capacitor electrodes—can be doped uniformly. Dopant gases alter deposition rate and uniformity, however, so conditions must be retuned.

  • A. Correct. The impurity is incorporated during deposition.
  • B. Ion implantation introduces impurities after deposition.
  • C. Thermal oxidation forms an oxide film.
