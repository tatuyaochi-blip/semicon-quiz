Deposition (CVD and PVD)

Which deposition method fills very narrow, deep trenches from the bottom with a flowable film and then solidifies it?

  1. AVacuum evaporation
  2. BFlowable CVD (FCVD) or spin-on dielectric (SOD)
  3. CMagnetron sputtering
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. Flowable CVD (FCVD) or spin-on dielectric (SOD)

Flowable CVD reacts silicon- and nitrogen-containing precursors at low temperature (below about 100 °C) to deposit a film that flows like a liquid and fills trenches from the bottom; subsequent anneals in ozone or steam solidify and convert it to oxide. Spin-on dielectrics (SOD) likewise exploit liquid flow. These methods fill narrow, high-aspect-ratio gaps—STI trenches around 10 nm wide and spaces between FinFET fins—where ordinary CVD would leave voids.
