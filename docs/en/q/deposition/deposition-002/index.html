Deposition (CVD and PVD)

Which is a characteristic of ALD (atomic layer deposition)?

  1. AIt can deposit films several µm thick quickly in a single step
  2. BIt repeats self-limiting surface reactions of precursors, controlling thickness at the atomic-layer level
  3. CIt is a printing technique that needs no vacuum
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. It repeats self-limiting surface reactions of precursors, controlling thickness at the atomic-layer level

ALD repeats a cycle: dose a precursor gas that adsorbs only one layer on the surface, purge the excess, then supply a reactant gas to complete the reaction. Because reaction stops once surface sites are filled (self-limiting), thickness is controlled precisely at about 0.1 nm per cycle, and films coat trenches with aspect ratios of several tens uniformly. The trade-off is a slow deposition rate.
