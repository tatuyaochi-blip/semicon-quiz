Deposition (CVD and PVD)

Which gas is used as the source for tungsten CVD?

  1. ATEOS
  2. BSilane (SiH4)
  3. CTungsten hexafluoride (WF6)
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. Tungsten hexafluoride (WF6)

Tungsten CVD deposits tungsten films by reducing WF6 with hydrogen or silane. With good step coverage, it can fill narrow holes such as contact holes, so it has been used to form contact plugs.
