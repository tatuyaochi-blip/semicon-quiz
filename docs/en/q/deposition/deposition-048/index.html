Deposition (CVD and PVD)

In CVD, what is the phenomenon called in which more source gas is consumed and the deposition rate drops as the area of wafers or patterns being coated increases?

  1. AElectromigration
  2. BLoading effect
  3. CChanneling
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. Loading effect

In CVD, larger reactive surface areas consume more precursor, and deposition slows where supply cannot keep up. This loading effect makes rate depend on the number of wafers and on exposed pattern area (pattern density): in batch furnaces thickness varies from gas inlet to far end, and within a chip dense and sparse regions differ. It is compensated through gas-flow and temperature profiles.

  • A. Electromigration is the movement of wiring metal atoms caused by current.
  • B. Correct. It is the drop in rate from source consumption.
  • C. Channeling is ions traveling deep along crystal channels during implantation.
