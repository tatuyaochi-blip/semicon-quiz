Deposition (CVD and PVD)

Which deposition method has mainly been used to form aluminum wiring films?

  1. AIon implantation
  2. BSputtering
  3. CThermal oxidation
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. Sputtering

Aluminum wiring has been deposited by sputtering, in which a target of aluminum (an alloy with a little copper or similar) is bombarded with argon ions and the ejected atoms deposit on the wafer. The film is then patterned and etched into wiring.

  • A. Ion implantation introduces impurities.
  • B. Correct. Aluminum wiring has been deposited by sputtering.
  • C. Thermal oxidation forms silicon oxide films.
