Deposition (CVD and PVD)

Into which category does deposition by sputtering fall?

  1. APVD (physical vapor deposition)
  2. BCVD (chemical vapor deposition)
  3. CThermal oxidation
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. PVD (physical vapor deposition)

Sputtering accelerates ions from an argon or similar plasma at several hundred volts into a target; atoms knocked out by momentum transfer deposit on the wafer. Since no chemical reaction is involved, it is classified as PVD (physical vapor deposition). It deposits metal wiring, barrier metals (Ta, TiN), Cu seed layers and electrodes. CVD relies on chemical reactions of precursor gases, and thermal oxidation oxidizes the silicon itself.
