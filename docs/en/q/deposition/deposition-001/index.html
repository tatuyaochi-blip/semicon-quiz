Deposition (CVD and PVD)

Into which category does deposition by sputtering fall?

  1. APVD (physical vapor deposition)
  2. BCVD (chemical vapor deposition)
  3. CThermal oxidation
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. PVD (physical vapor deposition)

Sputtering bombards a target with ions from a plasma and deposits the ejected atoms on the wafer; because it relies on a physical phenomenon, it is classified as PVD. It is used to deposit metal wiring, barrier metals and more.
