Deposition (CVD and PVD)

For films of the same material, in what way are high-temperature thermal CVD films generally better than plasma CVD films?

  1. ALower deposition temperature
  2. BAlways faster deposition
  3. CDenser films with fewer impurities such as hydrogen
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. Denser films with fewer impurities such as hydrogen

High-temperature thermal CVD reacts precursors fully, producing dense, near-stoichiometric films with few impurities such as hydrogen—LPCVD nitride contains only a few percent hydrogen, whereas PECVD nitride may contain around 20%. PECVD deposits at low temperature but yields less dense films that etch faster, so the choice depends on thermal constraints and required film quality.
