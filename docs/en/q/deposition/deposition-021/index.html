Deposition (CVD and PVD)

Which pair of source gases is used to deposit silicon nitride (Si3N4) by thermal CVD?

  1. ADichlorosilane and ammonia
  2. BSilane and oxygen
  3. CTungsten hexafluoride and hydrogen
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. Dichlorosilane and ammonia

LPCVD silicon nitride is typically formed by reacting dichlorosilane (SiH2Cl2) with ammonia (NH3) at about 700–800 °C (3SiH2Cl2 + 4NH3 → Si3N4 + 6HCl + 6H2), giving dense, near-stoichiometric films; excess ammonia keeps the composition on target. Plasma CVD or ALD is used when lower temperatures are required. Silane with oxygen deposits oxide, and WF6 with hydrogen deposits tungsten.
