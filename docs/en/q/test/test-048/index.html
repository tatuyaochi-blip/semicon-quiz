Testing and inspection

In ADC testing, what is the main purpose of inputting a slowly varying sine wave or similar and counting how often each output code appears (a histogram)?

  1. ATo weigh the chip
  2. BTo determine linearity such as DNL and INL
  3. CTo measure power consumption
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. To determine linearity such as DNL and INL

Feeding an ideal ADC a signal with known distribution yields a predictable code-occurrence histogram. Deviations of the actual counts give each code's width, the DNL, and accumulating DNL gives INL; this is the histogram method. A sine wave dwells near its peaks, so counts are corrected against its theoretical distribution, while a triangle or ramp gives a nearly uniform one. Power is found from supply current.

  • A. It has nothing to do with weight.
  • B. Correct. Linearity is obtained from code counts.
  • C. Power consumption is found by measuring supply current.
