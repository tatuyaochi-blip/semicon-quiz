Testing and inspection

What is the two-dimensional chart called that summarizes pass/fail results while varying conditions such as supply voltage and operating frequency?

  1. APareto chart
  2. BWafer map
  3. CShmoo plot
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. Shmoo plot

A shmoo plot shows pass/fail results on a grid, for example with operating frequency on the horizontal axis and supply voltage on the vertical. It reveals at a glance the range of conditions over which the IC works (operating margin), and the shape of the pass boundary hints whether failures are speed- or voltage-limited. A Pareto chart ranks defect causes, and a wafer map shows results by die position.
