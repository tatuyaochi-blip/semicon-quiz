Testing and inspection

In wafer test, what is it called to push the probe needles slightly further after they touch the bond pads to ensure contact?

  1. AUnderfill
  2. BGuard band
  3. COverdrive
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. Overdrive

Overdrive is pushing the probe needle a further tens of micrometers after it first touches the pad. The tip scrubs slightly across the pad, breaking through the aluminum oxide to make stable contact. Probe marks that are too large can cause poor wire bonds later, and excessive overdrive can damage structures beneath the pad.
