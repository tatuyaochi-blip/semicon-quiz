Testing and inspection

For a chip that failed scan testing, what is the process called that logically narrows down candidate fault locations from which outputs mismatched on which patterns?

  1. AFault diagnosis
  2. BGettering
  3. CLithography
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. Fault diagnosis

Fault diagnosis matches the failing chip's test results against simulations assuming faults, narrowing down the nets or cells likely to be faulty. It narrows where to do physical failure analysis and helps identify manufacturing problems and improve yield.

  • A. Correct. It narrows candidate fault locations from test results.
  • B. Gettering is a technique for capturing harmful impurities.
  • C. Lithography is the pattern transfer step.
