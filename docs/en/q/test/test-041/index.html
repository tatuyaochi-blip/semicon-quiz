Testing and inspection

What is the purpose of enabling a prober's wafer stage (chuck) to be controlled to high or low temperature?

  1. ATo dry the wafer
  2. BTo confirm at the wafer stage whether chips meet specifications across the actual operating temperature range
  3. CTo bond the chips
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. To confirm at the wafer stage whether chips meet specifications across the actual operating temperature range

Chip characteristics change with temperature: leakage rises when hot and timing shifts when cold. Controlling chuck temperature over roughly −40 to 150 °C during wafer test finds temperature-dependent failures before packaging, avoiding wasted assembly cost. For cold testing, dry air surrounds the wafer to prevent condensation.

  • A. It is not for drying.
  • B. Correct. It confirms performance over the operating temperature range at the wafer stage.
  • C. It is not for bonding.
