Testing and inspection

In test fixtures connecting the tester and IC, which component uses thin spring-loaded pins to make reliable contact with terminals?

  1. ABonding wires
  2. BSolder balls
  3. CPogo pins (spring probes)
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. Pogo pins (spring probes)

A pogo pin is a slender contact with a spring inside a barrel; pressed against an IC terminal or board pad, it makes reliable contact with moderate force and absorbs height variation. Many are used in test sockets and between test heads and load boards. After hundreds of thousands of touchdowns the tips wear and collect contamination, so they are cleaned and replaced periodically. Bonding wires and solder balls are parts of the product.

  • A. Bonding wires are permanent connections between chip and package.
  • B. Solder balls are terminals for joining packages to boards.
  • C. Correct. They are spring-loaded contact pins.
