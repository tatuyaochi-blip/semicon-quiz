Testing and inspection

Which items are mainly checked in a semiconductor DC test?

  1. ALeakage current and voltage levels of I/O pins, and supply current
  2. BOnly the logic function of the circuit
  3. CThe appearance of the package
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. Leakage current and voltage levels of I/O pins, and supply current

DC test measures whether static electrical characteristics meet spec: I/O leakage, output high and low voltage levels, input thresholds, and standby and operating supply current. It usually begins with a contact test that drives current through each pin's protection diode to confirm probe contact and detect opens or shorts. Logic function is checked by functional test and appearance by visual inspection.
