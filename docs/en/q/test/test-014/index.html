Testing and inspection

In final test after packaging, which machine works with the tester to feed ICs automatically and sort them according to test results?

  1. AProber
  2. BHandler
  3. CStepper
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. Handler

A handler automatically carries packaged ICs to the tester socket, presses them into contact, and after testing sorts them into good, bad or performance grades according to the results. Many include temperature control for hot and cold testing. A prober is used for testing at the wafer stage, and a stepper is lithography equipment.
