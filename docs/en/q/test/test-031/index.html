Testing and inspection

What is the main purpose of testing in semiconductor manufacturing?

  1. ATo find and remove defective parts made during manufacturing and ship only products that meet specifications
  2. BTo check the color of the chips
  3. CTo weigh the chips
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. To find and remove defective parts made during manufacturing and ship only products that meet specifications

However well controlled, manufacturing produces a certain fraction of chips with defects. Testing checks electrical characteristics and functions and removes defective parts so only products that meet specifications are shipped. Test results are also used to improve the manufacturing process.

  • A. Correct. Defective parts are removed so only good ones ship.
  • B. Checking color is not the purpose.
  • C. Weighing chips is not the purpose.
