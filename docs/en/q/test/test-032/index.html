Testing and inspection

In wafer testing, where on the chip do the probe card needles make contact?

  1. AOn top of transistor gates
  2. BOn the chip's electrode pads
  3. COnly at the center of the wafer's back
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. On the chip's electrode pads

In wafer test, the many needles of a probe card contact pads arranged around each chip, and the tester applies power and signals and measures the responses. Pads, tens of micrometers square, also serve later for wire bonding or bumps. Probe-mark size and position are inspected so they do not impair bonding. Transistors lie beneath the wiring layers and cannot be touched directly.

  • A. Transistor gates lie beneath the wiring layers and cannot be probed directly.
  • B. Correct. The needles contact the electrode pads.
  • C. The wafer back alone cannot connect to each circuit terminal.
