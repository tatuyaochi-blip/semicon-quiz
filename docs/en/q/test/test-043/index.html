Testing and inspection

What is it called when good chips are judged defective and discarded because test limits are too strict or measurements are unstable?

  1. AGuard band
  2. BOverkill (excessive rejection)
  3. CTest escape
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. Overkill (excessive rejection)

Overkill means judging good parts as defective, unnecessarily lowering yield and raising cost. Conversely, passing a defective part as good is a test escape, which leads to field failures. Test conditions are set by balancing the two.

  • A. A guard band is the margin of limits tightened beyond specification to allow for error.
  • B. Correct. It is judging good parts as defective.
  • C. A test escape is passing a defective part as good.
