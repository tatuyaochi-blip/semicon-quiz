Testing and inspection

For automotive semiconductors, what is the main reason for testing not only at room temperature but also at high and low temperatures?

  1. ATo guarantee correct operation over the wide temperature range in which they are used
  2. BTo shorten test time
  3. CTo lower test cost
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. To guarantee correct operation over the wide temperature range in which they are used

Cars are used over a very wide temperature range, from cold regions to engine compartments under a blazing sun. Semiconductor characteristics change with temperature, so tests at high and low temperatures confirm that specifications are met across the entire guaranteed range.
