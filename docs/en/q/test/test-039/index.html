Testing and inspection

What is the main advantage of vertical probe cards, which stand the needles perpendicular to the pads?

  1. AThe wafer cannot be heated
  2. BThey readily support high pin counts at fine pitch, simultaneous testing of many chips and pads arrayed over an area
  3. COnly one needle can be used
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. They readily support high pin counts at fine pitch, simultaneous testing of many chips and pads arrayed over an area

Traditional cantilever probes touch pads with angled needles extending from the side, suiting pads around the chip perimeter. Vertical and MEMS probes can be packed densely in a grid, suiting high-pin-count products with pads across the whole die and simultaneous testing of many dies. Because the needles move vertically, probe marks are also smaller. Whether the wafer can be heated depends on the prober's chuck.

  • A. Whether the wafer can be heated depends on the prober's chuck.
  • B. Correct. They readily support high pin counts, fine pitch and area arrays.
  • C. Vertical cards can carry many needles.
