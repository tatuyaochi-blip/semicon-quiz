Testing and inspection

What are the dedicated patterns called, placed between chips on the wafer (in scribe lines) and elsewhere, used to measure characteristics of transistors, resistors and the like to evaluate the process?

  1. ADummy gate
  2. BAlignment mark
  3. CTEG (test element group)
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. TEG (test element group)

A TEG is a collection of simple structures such as transistors, resistors, capacitors and wiring chains for evaluating how well the process turned out. Placed in scribe lines and elsewhere, their electrical characteristics are measured to confirm that the steps were performed correctly.

  • A. Dummy gates are non-functional gates placed for processing uniformity and the like.
  • B. Alignment marks are markers for exposure alignment.
  • C. Correct. They are test patterns for process evaluation.
