Testing and inspection

What is the main use of boundary scan, specified in IEEE 1149.1 (JTAG)?

  1. AMeasuring film thickness on wafers
  2. BEvaluating electromigration in wiring
  3. CTesting connections between the pins of LSIs mounted on a printed circuit board, without applying probes
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. Testing connections between the pins of LSIs mounted on a printed circuit board, without applying probes

In boundary scan, scan cells are placed just inside each LSI pin and chained serially so values can be set and observed from outside. Even for BGAs and other parts whose pins cannot be probed on the board, defects such as bad solder joints can be detected. It is also used for debugging and programming.
