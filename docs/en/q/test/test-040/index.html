Testing and inspection

As wafer testing continues, aluminum debris from the pads and the like accumulates on the probe tips. What is done periodically in response?

  1. AThe probe tips are cleaned, for example with an abrasive sheet
  2. BThe needles are oiled
  3. CThe needles are bent
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. The probe tips are cleaned, for example with an abrasive sheet

Aluminum debris and oxide on probe tips raise contact resistance, corrupting measurements and causing good chips to fail. After a set number of touchdowns, the tips are therefore rubbed on abrasive sheets to clean them and stabilize contact. Excessive cleaning wears and shortens the needles, so cleaning frequency is also managed. Oil or bending would worsen contact.

  • A. Correct. The tips are cleaned periodically.
  • B. Oil would cause poor contact and contamination.
  • C. Bending the needles would prevent proper contact.
