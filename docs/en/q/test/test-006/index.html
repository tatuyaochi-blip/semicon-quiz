Testing and inspection

Which correctly describes a probe card?

  1. AA mold for encapsulating chips
  2. BA container for transporting wafers
  3. CA fixture that contacts needles to the chip electrodes on a wafer and connects them electrically to the tester
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. A fixture that contacts needles to the chip electrodes on a wafer and connects them electrically to the tester

A probe card is a fixture used in wafer test, with many needles (probes) arranged to match the layout of the chip's bond pads. A machine called a prober moves the wafer to align the needles with the electrodes, electrically connecting the tester and the chip.
