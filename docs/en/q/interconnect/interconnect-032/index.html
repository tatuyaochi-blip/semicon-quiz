Interconnect processes

Which correctly describes "multilevel wiring" in an IC?

  1. AWiring arranged in a single flat layer
  2. BMany layers of wiring stacked with insulating films between them and connected between layers by vias
  3. CSending signals wirelessly without wiring
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. Many layers of wiring stacked with insulating films between them and connected between layers by vias

Multilevel interconnects alternate wiring layers with dielectric layers and link adjacent levels through vias, routing far more connections than a single plane could hold. Routing direction typically alternates between horizontal and vertical from level to level to reduce congestion. Integrated circuits of the 1960s had a single wiring level; CMP planarization made stacking easier, and advanced logic now uses about 15.

  • A. A single layer cannot connect a large number of devices.
  • B. Correct. Many layers are stacked with insulators between and connected by vias.
  • C. Wiring inside an IC is wired metal, not wireless.
