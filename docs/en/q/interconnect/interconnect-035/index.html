Interconnect processes

What is the basic role of the interlayer dielectric that fills the space between wires?

  1. ATo lower wire resistance
  2. BTo set the transistor threshold voltage
  3. CTo electrically insulate wires from each other and prevent shorts
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. To electrically insulate wires from each other and prevent shorts

Interlayer dielectrics electrically isolate neighboring wires on the same level and between levels, preventing shorts. They also set inter-wire capacitance, which is why low-k materials were adopted. They must simultaneously provide mechanical strength to survive CMP, resistance to copper diffusion, heat conduction and long-term insulation reliability, making them a demanding materials challenge.

  • A. The insulator does not lower wire resistance.
  • B. The threshold voltage is set by the transistor's gate structure and similar.
  • C. Correct. It insulates wires from each other.
