Interconnect processes

What is the phenomenon called in which current flowing through the resistance of power wiring lowers the supply voltage that reaches circuits?

  1. AIR drop
  2. BCrosstalk
  3. CDIBL
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. IR drop

Power wiring has resistance, so when current I flows, an I × R voltage drop occurs and the voltage reaching circuits falls. This is called IR drop, which slows circuits and can cause malfunctions. It is reduced by widening power wires and arranging them in a mesh.
