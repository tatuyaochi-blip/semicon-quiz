Interconnect processes

What is the main purpose of barrier metals such as Ta and TaN in copper wiring?

  1. ATo reduce wire resistance
  2. BTo prevent copper from diffusing into insulators and silicon
  3. CTo oxidize the copper surface
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. To prevent copper from diffusing into insulators and silicon

Copper diffuses readily into dielectrics and silicon; in silicon it forms deep levels that cause transistor leakage and degradation, and in dielectrics it compromises insulation between wires. Copper lines are therefore lined on sides and bottom with a few nm of barrier metal such as Ta/TaN and capped on top with a dielectric film. The barrier also improves adhesion to the dielectric and helps texture the copper grains.
