Interconnect processes

What is the method called that fills wiring trenches and vias with copper at the same time and finishes them with a single CMP step?

  1. ASingle damascene process
  2. BEtch-back process
  3. CDual damascene process
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. Dual damascene process

Dual damascene patterns both via holes and wiring trenches into the dielectric, then fills them together with barrier, seed and electroplated copper and finishes with a single CMP. Compared with single damascene, which builds vias and lines separately, it saves one round each of deposition, plating and CMP, and avoids a barrier interface between via and line, benefiting resistance and reliability. Variants differ in whether vias or trenches are opened first.
