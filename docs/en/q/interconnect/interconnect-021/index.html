Interconnect processes

What is the method called that fills wiring trenches and vias with copper at the same time and finishes them with a single CMP step?

  1. ASingle damascene process
  2. BEtch-back process
  3. CDual damascene process
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. Dual damascene process

In the dual damascene process, both via holes and wiring trenches are formed in the insulator, then filled together with a barrier layer, a seed layer and copper plating, and finished with one CMP step. It needs fewer steps than single damascene, which forms vias and wiring separately, and eliminates the interface between via and wire.
