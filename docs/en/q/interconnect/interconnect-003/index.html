Interconnect processes

What is the main purpose of using low-k (low dielectric constant) materials for interlayer dielectrics?

  1. ATo reduce wire resistance
  2. BTo increase the mechanical strength of the interlayer dielectric
  3. CTo reduce parasitic capacitance between wires and lower RC delay
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. To reduce parasitic capacitance between wires and lower RC delay

As wiring spacing shrinks, inter-wire capacitance grows, increasing RC delay, switching power and crosstalk. Dielectrics with permittivity below SiO2's (about 3.9) reduce capacitance: carbon-doped SiOC (about 2.7–3.0) and porous films (about 2.2–2.5) are used. Lower permittivity comes with softer, weaker films, so cracking and delamination during CMP and packaging are challenges.
