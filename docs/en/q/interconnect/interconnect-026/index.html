Interconnect processes

For wiring with a resistivity of 2 µΩ·cm, 1 mm long, with width and thickness of 100 nm each, approximately what is the resistance?

  1. AAbout 2 Ω
  2. BAbout 2 kΩ
  3. CAbout 2 MΩ
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. About 2 kΩ

Resistance R = ρL/(W × T); with ρ = 2×10⁻⁸ Ω·m, L = 1×10⁻³ m and W × T = 1×10⁻¹⁴ m², R = 2000 Ω, about 2 kΩ. Even a 1 mm run has this much resistance at a 100 nm square cross-section, and with wire capacitance (about 0.2 pF per mm) the RC delay reaches hundreds of ps. Long routes therefore use thick upper-level wires or repeaters.
