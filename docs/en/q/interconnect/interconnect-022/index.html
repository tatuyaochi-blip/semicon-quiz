Interconnect processes

What is the role of the thin copper film sputtered before filling copper by electroplating?

  1. AA barrier against copper diffusion
  2. BA conductive layer that carries plating current (seed layer)
  3. CInsulation between layers
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. A conductive layer that carries plating current (seed layer)

Electroplating deposits metal by passing current through the surface, so a conductive layer is needed all the way down the inside of the trench. A thin copper seed layer is therefore sputtered over the barrier before copper is filled by plating.
