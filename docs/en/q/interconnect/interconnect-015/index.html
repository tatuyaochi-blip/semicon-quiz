Interconnect processes

What is the main purpose of placing a cap layer such as cobalt on top of copper wiring?

  1. ATo deliberately increase wire resistance
  2. BTo make the wiring transparent
  3. CTo suppress atom migration along the copper surface and improve electromigration resistance
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. To suppress atom migration along the copper surface and improve electromigration resistance

Electromigration in copper wiring proceeds mainly by atom migration along the top surface (the interface with the dielectric). Covering the top with a metal cap such as cobalt strengthens interface bonding and makes atoms harder to move, improving electromigration resistance.
