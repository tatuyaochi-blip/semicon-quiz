Interconnect processes

What is the main purpose of placing a cap layer such as cobalt on top of copper wiring?

  1. ATo deliberately increase wire resistance
  2. BTo make the wiring transparent
  3. CTo suppress atom migration along the copper surface and improve electromigration resistance
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. To suppress atom migration along the copper surface and improve electromigration resistance

Electromigration in copper lines proceeds mainly by atom transport along the top interface with the dielectric cap. Selectively capping the top with a metal such as cobalt strengthens interfacial bonding so atoms move less freely, sometimes extending lifetime by an order of magnitude or more. The cap is grown selectively only on copper; any deposition on the dielectric would cause leakage between lines, so selectivity is critical.
