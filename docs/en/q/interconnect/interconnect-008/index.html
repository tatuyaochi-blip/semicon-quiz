Interconnect processes

Which metal has been widely used to fill contact holes connecting transistors to the lowest wiring layer?

  1. ATungsten (W)
  2. BGold (Au)
  3. CLead (Pb)
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. Tungsten (W)

Contact holes have widely been filled with tungsten, which CVD can deposit deep into narrow holes (tungsten plugs). It is used with Ti or TiN barrier layers to prevent diffusion into silicon.
