Interconnect processes

Which metal has been widely used to fill contact holes connecting transistors to the lowest wiring layer?

  1. ATungsten (W)
  2. BGold (Au)
  3. CLead (Pb)
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. Tungsten (W)

Contact holes have long been filled with tungsten, whose CVD deposition fills holes tens of nm across (tungsten plugs). Its high melting point gives strong electromigration resistance and stability close to hot transistors. Ti and TiN barrier layers prevent diffusion into silicon and attack by fluorine from WF6. At advanced nodes, lower-resistance cobalt or ruthenium is replacing tungsten in some contacts.
