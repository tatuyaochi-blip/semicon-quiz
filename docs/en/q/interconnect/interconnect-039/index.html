Interconnect processes

Which term refers to the contacts and local interconnects that connect the transistors to the multilevel wiring?

  1. ADicing
  2. BMOL (middle of line)
  3. CPackage
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. MOL (middle of line)

The steps and structures between transistor fabrication and multilevel wiring fabrication, such as contacts to the source, drain and gate and local connections, are called MOL (middle of line). With scaling, resistance in this region strongly affects performance, driving improvements in materials and structures.

  • A. Dicing cuts chips out of the wafer.
  • B. Correct. It is the region between transistors and wiring.
  • C. The package is what encloses the finished chip.
