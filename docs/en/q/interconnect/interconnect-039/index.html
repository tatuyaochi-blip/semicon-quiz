Interconnect processes

Which term refers to the contacts and local interconnects that connect the transistors to the multilevel wiring?

  1. ADicing
  2. BMOL (middle of line)
  3. CPackage
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. MOL (middle of line)

Middle-of-line (MOL) refers to the processes and structures between transistor fabrication (FEOL) and the multilevel interconnect (BEOL): contacts to sources, drains and gates and local interconnects. Resistance of contacts only ten-odd nm across has risen sharply with scaling and now strongly affects transistor performance, driving the adoption of cobalt and ruthenium and innovations such as contacts over active gates.

  • A. Dicing cuts chips out of the wafer.
  • B. Correct. It is the region between transistors and wiring.
  • C. The package is what encloses the finished chip.
