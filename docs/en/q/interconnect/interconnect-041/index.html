Interconnect processes

Which statement about the capacitance of a wire is correct?

  1. AOnly the capacitance to adjacent wires in the same layer needs to be considered
  2. BIn addition to capacitance to adjacent wires in the same layer, there is capacitance to wires in layers above and below and to the substrate
  3. CWires have no capacitance
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. In addition to capacitance to adjacent wires in the same layer, there is capacitance to wires in layers above and below and to the substrate

A wire's capacitance combines the lateral capacitance to adjacent wires in the same layer and the vertical capacitance to wires in layers above and below and to the substrate. As wiring pitch tightens, the lateral share grows.

  • A. Capacitance to layers above and below and to the substrate cannot be ignored.
  • B. Correct. There are both lateral and vertical components.
  • C. Wires always have capacitance to surrounding conductors.
