Interconnect processes

What is the main purpose of the technique that makes part of the insulator between wires a cavity (air gap)?

  1. ATo increase the mechanical strength of the wiring
  2. BTo reduce capacitance between wires (air has a relative permittivity of about 1)
  3. CTo reduce wire resistance
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. To reduce capacitance between wires (air has a relative permittivity of about 1)

Air (or vacuum) has a relative permittivity of about 1, lower than any insulating material, so making the space between wires a cavity greatly reduces capacitance between them. Because of challenges in mechanical strength and heat dissipation, it is used only in layers where capacitance reduction matters most.
