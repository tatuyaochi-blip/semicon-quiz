Interconnect processes

Which correctly describes backside power delivery, which is being introduced in advanced logic?

  1. AA method that supplies no power from outside the chip
  2. BA method that replaces all wiring with optical fibers
  3. CA method that places power wiring on the back side of the wafer, freeing the front-side wiring for signals and reducing voltage drop and routing congestion
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. A method that places power wiring on the back side of the wafer, freeing the front-side wiring for signals and reducing voltage drop and routing congestion

Conventionally, signal and power wiring were all built in the wiring layers above (on the front side of) the transistors. In backside power delivery, the wafer is thinned and power wiring is placed on the back, supplying power to transistors from below. Power wires can be thick and short, and front-side wiring can be used for signals.
