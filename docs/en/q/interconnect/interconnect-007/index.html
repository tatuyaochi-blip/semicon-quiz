Interconnect processes

What is the purpose of forming silicides such as NiSi on the surfaces of a transistor's source, drain and gate?

  1. ATo insulate the electrodes
  2. BTo make the surface shine for easier inspection
  3. CTo reduce the resistance and contact resistance of the electrodes
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. To reduce the resistance and contact resistance of the electrodes

Silicides are metal-silicon compounds with sheet resistance over an order of magnitude below doped silicon—NiSi, for instance, is about 15 µΩ·cm. Silicidizing source/drain and gate surfaces lowers sheet and contact resistance. Metal is deposited everywhere, annealed so it reacts only where it touches silicon, and the unreacted metal is stripped, forming silicide self-aligned without a mask—hence "salicide." Materials have progressed from TiSi2 to CoSi2 to NiSi over generations.
