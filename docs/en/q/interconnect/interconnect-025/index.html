Interconnect processes

Which technique forms vias self-aligned to wiring trenches to tolerate misalignment between upper and lower wiring layers?

  1. ATSV
  2. BSelf-aligned via
  3. CLDD
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. Self-aligned via

At wiring pitches in the 20 nm range, a via misplaced by just a few nm can short to the adjacent line or narrow the spacing enough to cause dielectric breakdown. Self-aligned vias retain the trench-patterning hard mask or use materially distinct layers atop the lines so the via etch is confined within the target line's width, preventing connection to neighbors despite misalignment.
