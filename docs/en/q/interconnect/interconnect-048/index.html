Interconnect processes

What is the design practice called in which active devices and wiring are placed directly beneath the bond pads used for wire bonding and the like?

  1. AScribe line
  2. BCUP (circuit under pad)
  3. CDummy fill
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. CUP (circuit under pad)

Circuits were traditionally kept out from under pads so that bonding impact would not damage the layers below. CUP reinforces the structure beneath the pad and places circuits there, reducing chip area. With weak materials such as low-k films, ensuring strength is a challenge.

  • A. A scribe line is the region for separating chips.
  • B. Correct. It places circuits under the pads.
  • C. Dummy fill is non-functional patterning added for CMP planarity.
