Interconnect processes

In multilevel interconnects, what is the main reason upper-level wires are made wider and thicker?

  1. ABecause the upper levels must transmit light
  2. BBecause fine patterning is harder in upper levels
  3. CTo reduce the resistance of long power and signal wires
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. To reduce the resistance of long power and signal wires

Lower-level wires connect nearby transistors densely and are thin, while upper-level wires distribute power across the chip and connect distant blocks, so they are made wide and thick. Lowering the resistance of long wires limits voltage drop and signal delay.
