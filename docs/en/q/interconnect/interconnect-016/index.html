Interconnect processes

In Black's equation for wire lifetime under electromigration, which factors shorten the lifetime?

  1. AMaking the wire wider
  2. BHigh current density and high temperature
  3. CLowering the temperature
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. High current density and high temperature

Black's equation states that median time to failure is inversely proportional to current density to the power n and proportional to exp(Ea/kT). The higher the current density and the temperature, the shorter the lifetime. In design, an upper limit on current density is set for each wire.
