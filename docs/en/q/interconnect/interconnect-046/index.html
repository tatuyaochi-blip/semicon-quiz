Interconnect processes

When a wire becomes narrower than its grains so that grain boundaries line up across the wire, what is this structure called, and how does it affect electromigration?

  1. AA porous structure, which lowers resistance
  2. BAn amorphous structure, which lowers resistance
  3. CA bamboo structure, which reduces grain-boundary diffusion paths along the wire and improves resistance
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. A bamboo structure, which reduces grain-boundary diffusion paths along the wire and improves resistance

When the wire is narrower than the grain size, grain boundaries cross the wire like the nodes of bamboo. This structure removes most of the grain-boundary paths along the wire where atoms move fast, so electromigration by grain-boundary diffusion is suppressed. In Cu, however, interface diffusion becomes the dominant path.

  • A. A porous structure describes low-k films and the like.
  • B. Wiring metals are crystalline.
  • C. Correct. Fewer grain-boundary paths improve resistance.
