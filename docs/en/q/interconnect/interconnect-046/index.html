Interconnect processes

When a wire becomes narrower than its grains so that grain boundaries line up across the wire, what is this structure called, and how does it affect electromigration?

  1. AA porous structure, which reduces electromigration resistance
  2. BAn amorphous structure, which reduces electromigration resistance
  3. CA bamboo structure, which reduces grain-boundary diffusion paths along the wire and improves electromigration resistance
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. A bamboo structure, which reduces grain-boundary diffusion paths along the wire and improves electromigration resistance

When a wire is narrower than its grain size, grain boundaries cross it like the nodes of bamboo (a bamboo structure). Fast diffusion paths along the wire's length disappear, suppressing grain-boundary electromigration—a major lifetime boost for aluminum lines. In copper, however, diffusion along the top interface dominates, so a bamboo structure alone is insufficient and cap improvements are critical.

  • A. A porous structure describes low-k films and the like.
  • B. Wiring metals are crystalline.
  • C. Correct. Fewer grain-boundary paths improve resistance.
