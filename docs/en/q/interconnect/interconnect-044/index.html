Interconnect processes

What film is commonly placed on top of Cu wiring to act both as a diffusion barrier toward the dielectric and as an etch stop?

  1. APhotoresist
  2. BPolysilicon
  3. CA dielectric cap such as SiCN
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. A dielectric cap such as SiCN

The top of Cu wiring exposed by CMP is covered with a dielectric cap such as SiCN or SiN that prevents Cu from diffusing into the dielectric above and stops the via etch of the next level. Because its permittivity is relatively high, it is kept thin.

  • A. Photoresist is a temporary film for patterning.
  • B. Polysilicon is conductive and is not used as an insulating cap over wiring.
  • C. Correct. A dielectric cap such as SiCN is used.
