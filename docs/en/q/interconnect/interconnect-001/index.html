Interconnect processes

What is the main reason the wiring material of advanced ICs was changed from aluminum (Al) to copper (Cu)?

  1. ALower resistivity and higher electromigration resistance
  2. BIt is easier to process by dry etching
  3. CIts adhesion to oxide is so high that no barrier layer is needed
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. Lower resistivity and higher electromigration resistance

Copper's resistivity (about 1.7 µΩ·cm) is roughly 60% of aluminum's (about 2.7 µΩ·cm), cutting wiring delay and power, and its higher melting point makes atoms less mobile, improving electromigration resistance by an order of magnitude or more. IBM announced production in 1997, and copper became mainstream around the 0.13 µm generation. Because copper forms no volatile compounds for dry etching and diffuses readily into dielectrics, it is formed by damascene and encased in Ta/TaN barriers.
