Interconnect processes

Where are voids due to electromigration likely to form?

  1. AWhere the flow of metal atoms is discontinuous, such as at the boundary between a via and a wire
  2. BIn the middle of a wire carrying no current
  3. CIn the package substrate outside the chip
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. Where the flow of metal atoms is discontinuous, such as at the boundary between a via and a wire

In electromigration, atoms are pushed along by the electron flow (the electron wind). Where atomic flux is interrupted—at via-to-line interfaces or the barrier beneath a via—atoms leave the upstream side without replacement, forming voids, while they accumulate downstream as hillocks. Design countermeasures include redundant vias, wider metal around vias and attention to current direction.
