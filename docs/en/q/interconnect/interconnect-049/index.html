Interconnect processes

Which technique deposits a Cu alloy with a small addition of manganese or similar and, through annealing, lets a thin barrier form spontaneously at the interface with the dielectric?

  1. ASelf-forming barrier
  2. BLOCOS
  3. CIon implantation
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. Self-forming barrier

With a Cu-Mn alloy seed, annealing drives Mn to the interface, where it reacts with the dielectric to form a very thin barrier such as MnSiOx. Because less separate barrier metal needs to be deposited, it is expected to increase the share of low-resistance Cu in narrow wires and vias.

  • A. Correct. A barrier forms spontaneously at the interface.
  • B. LOCOS is isolation by selective oxidation.
  • C. Ion implantation introduces impurities.
