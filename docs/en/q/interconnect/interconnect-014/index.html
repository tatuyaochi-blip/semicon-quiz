Interconnect processes

What is the phenomenon called in which, through capacitive coupling between adjacent wires, a signal change on one wire appears as noise on the other?

  1. AIR drop
  2. BCrosstalk
  3. CElectromigration
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. Crosstalk

When wires run close together, a transition on one (the aggressor) couples through inter-wire capacitance into its neighbor (the victim), producing noise glitches and delay variation when both switch together—crosstalk. It worsens as scaling increases the share of coupling capacitance. Remedies include wider spacing, power or ground shield wires, low-k dielectrics and stronger drivers, and design tools analyze it.
