Which is closest to the resistivity of copper (Cu) at room temperature? (Bulk value)

  1. AAbout 17 Ω·cm
  2. BAbout 1.7 mΩ·cm
  3. CAbout 1.7 µΩ·cm
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. About 1.7 µΩ·cm

Bulk copper resistivity at room temperature is about 1.7 µΩ·cm (1.7×10⁻⁸ Ω·m), second only to silver (about 1.6) and below aluminum (about 2.7) and tungsten (about 5.3); being inexpensive and workable, copper is also the standard for electric cable. In very narrow lines, however, surface and grain-boundary scattering raise effective resistivity to about 2–5 times the bulk value.

  • A. About 17 Ω·cm is a semiconductor-level resistivity, far too large for a metal.
  • B. About 1.7 mΩ·cm is about 1,000 times copper's resistivity.
  • C. Correct. Copper is about 1.7 µΩ·cm.
