Which is closest to the resistivity of copper (Cu) at room temperature? (Bulk value)

  1. AAbout 17 Ω·cm
  2. BAbout 1.7 mΩ·cm
  3. CAbout 1.7 µΩ·cm
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. About 1.7 µΩ·cm

Bulk copper has a resistivity of about 1.7 µΩ·cm at room temperature, lower than aluminum (about 2.7 µΩ·cm). In very narrow wires, however, electron scattering at surfaces and grain boundaries makes the effective resistivity higher.

  • A. About 17 Ω·cm is a semiconductor-level resistivity, far too large for a metal.
  • B. About 1.7 mΩ·cm is about 1,000 times copper's resistivity.
  • C. Correct. Copper is about 1.7 µΩ·cm.
