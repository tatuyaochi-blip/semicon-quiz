Interconnect processes

What is the phenomenon called in which electroplated Cu films, simply left at room temperature, grow larger grains over time and drop in resistivity?

  1. AElectromigration
  2. BStress migration
  3. CSelf-annealing
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. Self-annealing

As-plated copper has small, unstable grains tens of nm in size—partly due to incorporated additives—and at room temperature it recrystallizes over hours to days into grains around 1 µm, lowering resistivity by about 20%. This is self-annealing. Because properties and polish rates would vary with wait time, wafers are typically annealed at about 100–400 °C after plating to complete grain growth before CMP.

  • A. Electromigration is current-driven atomic migration.
  • B. Stress migration is stress-driven atomic migration that forms voids.
  • C. Correct. It is recrystallization proceeding at room temperature.
