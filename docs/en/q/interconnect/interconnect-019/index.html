Interconnect processes

What is the film called that is formed on the top of a chip to protect the internal circuits from moisture, contamination and mechanical damage?

  1. AGate oxide
  2. BPassivation film
  3. CBuried oxide
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. Passivation film

Passivation covers the chip surface: silicon nitride (a few hundred nm to about 1 µm) that blocks moisture and ions such as sodium, often with oxide and a stress-relieving polyimide layer. It protects circuits from moisture, ionic contamination and assembly damage and is essential for surviving high-temperature, high-humidity reliability tests. Openings are made only over the bond pads where wires or bumps attach.
