Interconnect processes

Which metal has been used in many LSIs for the top-level bond pads where wire bonding is done?

  1. ATungsten
  2. BTitanium
  3. CAluminum
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. Aluminum

Even after internal wiring switched to copper, aluminum has been widely used for top-level bond pads. Aluminum forms a thin, stable surface oxide that resists corrosion and has a long track record of bonding with gold and copper wire.
