Interconnect processes

Which is closest to the number of wiring layers in advanced logic ICs?

  1. A1 layer
  2. B3 layers
  3. C10 or more layers
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. 10 or more layers

Advanced logic ICs stack about 15 wiring levels above the transistors: fine lower levels with minimum pitches in the 20 nm range, intermediate levels for mid-range routing and thick upper levels for power and global signals. Back-end-of-line (BEOL) processing involves as many steps as transistor fabrication and a large share of manufacturing cost. Backside power delivery is set to reshape the stack further.
