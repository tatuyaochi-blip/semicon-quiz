Interconnect processes

In chips using mechanically weak low-k films, what is the problem called in which stress during packaging cracks or delaminates the films?

  1. AQuantization error
  2. BChip-package interaction (CPI) problems
  3. CShort-channel effects
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. Chip-package interaction (CPI) problems

Low-k films, being porous or otherwise weak, can crack or delaminate in the wiring layers when stress from solder bump bonding, resin encapsulation or temperature changes is applied to the chip. This is called a chip-package interaction (CPI) problem and is addressed through structural design and material selection.

  • A. Quantization error is an AD conversion error.
  • B. Correct. It is wiring-layer failure from packaging stress.
  • C. Short-channel effects are a transistor characteristic problem.
