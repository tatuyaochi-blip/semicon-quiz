Interconnect processes

If a wire's length is doubled without changing its cross-section or spacing, approximately how many times larger does its RC delay become?

  1. A2 times
  2. B4 times
  3. C8 times
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. 4 times

Both the wire's resistance R and capacitance C are proportional to its length, so RC delay is proportional to the square of the length. Doubling the length makes the delay about four times larger. This is why buffers (repeaters) are inserted along long wires to limit delay.
