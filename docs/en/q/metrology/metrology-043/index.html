Metrology and analysis

When measuring very narrow trenches or steep sidewalls with AFM, what tends to cause errors?

  1. AAFM can only be used in vacuum
  2. BThe tip has a finite size and shape, so its shape gets superimposed on the measured profile
  3. CAFM cannot measure surface topography
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. The tip has a finite size and shape, so its shape gets superimposed on the measured profile

Because an AFM traces the surface with a tip, it cannot reach the bottom of trenches narrower than the tip, and on steep sidewalls the side of the tip makes contact first. The measured profile is thus a convolution of the real shape with the tip shape, making trenches look shallower and narrower and lines wider. Sharper tips or flared tips for sidewall measurement are used, along with analysis that estimates and removes the tip shape.

  • A. AFM can also be used in air.
  • B. Correct. The tip's shape is superimposed on the result.
  • C. AFM is an instrument for measuring surface topography.
