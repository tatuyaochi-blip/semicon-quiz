Metrology and analysis

Which correctly describes the sample preparation needed to observe a sample with a transmission electron microscope (TEM)?

  1. AStack samples to a thickness of several cm
  2. BThin the sample to about 100 nm or less so electrons can pass through it
  3. CDissolve the sample in liquid
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. Thin the sample to about 100 nm or less so electrons can pass through it

A TEM forms images from electrons transmitted through the sample, so the specimen must be thinned to about 100 nm or less for the beam to pass. For semiconductor devices, the lift-out method is common: an FIB mills around the region of interest, extracts a thin lamella and finishes it. Aberration-corrected TEMs resolve below 0.1 nm, showing individual atomic columns. Thick stacks or dissolved samples cannot transmit electrons.
