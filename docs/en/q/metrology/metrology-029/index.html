Metrology and analysis

Which method measures wafer carrier lifetime without contact to evaluate metal contamination and the like?

  1. AMicrowave photoconductance decay (μ-PCD)
  2. BSIMS
  3. CXRD
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. Microwave photoconductance decay (μ-PCD)

In μ-PCD, a light pulse generates carriers in the wafer, and their decay through recombination is tracked by changes in microwave reflectivity. Metal contaminants such as iron act as recombination centers that shorten lifetime, so it is used to monitor furnace contamination and assess wafer quality. Requiring no electrodes, it can map the whole wafer. SIMS and XRD cannot measure lifetime.
