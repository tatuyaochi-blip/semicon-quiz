Metrology and analysis

Which method measures wafer carrier lifetime without contact to evaluate metal contamination and the like?

  1. AMicrowave photoconductance decay (μ-PCD)
  2. BSIMS
  3. CXRD
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. Microwave photoconductance decay (μ-PCD)

In μ-PCD, a light pulse generates carriers in the wafer, and their decay through recombination is measured from changes in microwave reflection. Metal contamination shortens lifetime, so it is used to monitor contamination in furnaces and similar equipment.
