Metrology and analysis

Which is a typical principle used in defect inspection tools that detect particles and pattern defects on wafers?

  1. ADetecting light scattered when light is shone on the wafer, and comparing images of adjacent dies
  2. BChanges in wafer weight
  3. CThe sound when the wafer is tapped
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. Detecting light scattered when light is shone on the wafer, and comparing images of adjacent dies

Particles scatter incident light, so they are found by detecting scattered light. Pattern defects are found by comparing images of neighboring dies that share the same pattern and flagging differences. Comparing two dies cannot tell which one is defective, so three dies are compared and a majority vote decides. Detected defects are then imaged and classified on a review SEM. Weight or sound reveals nothing about defects.
