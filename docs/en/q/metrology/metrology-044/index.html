Metrology and analysis

In TEM, which technique scans a finely focused electron beam and measures the energy lost by transmitted electrons to map elements and chemical states?

  1. AFour-point probe
  2. BHall measurement
  3. CSTEM-EELS (electron energy loss spectroscopy)
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. STEM-EELS (electron energy loss spectroscopy)

In STEM (scanning transmission electron microscopy), a fine electron probe is scanned across the sample. Combined with EELS, which measures how much energy transmitted electrons have lost, it maps elements and chemical bonding states with near-atomic spatial resolution. It is strong at detecting light elements and is used to evaluate ultrathin gate dielectrics and interfaces. Hall measurement determines carrier type and mobility.

  • A. The four-point probe measures resistivity.
  • B. Hall measurement determines carrier type, concentration and mobility.
  • C. Correct. Elements and states are probed by the energy loss of transmitted electrons.
