Metrology and analysis

What is the technique called that uses statistical models to predict film thickness or dimensions of wafers that were not actually measured, from equipment sensor data and the like?

  1. ADestructive analysis
  2. BVisual inspection
  3. CVirtual metrology
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. Virtual metrology

Virtual metrology models the relationship between tool sensor data during processing, such as temperature, pressure and power, and past measurement results, often by machine learning, to predict results for unmeasured wafers. It estimates process status without measuring every wafer, aiding early excursion detection and wafer-by-wafer control. Since models drift as tool conditions change, they are checked periodically against real measurements.

  • A. Destructive analysis examines samples by breaking them.
  • B. Visual inspection looks for abnormalities in appearance.
  • C. Correct. It predicts measurements from sensor data.
