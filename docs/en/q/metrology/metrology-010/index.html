Metrology and analysis

Which can be evaluated from C-V (capacitance-voltage) measurements of a MOS capacitor?

  1. APattern linewidth
  2. BOxide thickness, interface states, substrate doping concentration and more
  3. CThe number of particles on the wafer surface
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. Oxide thickness, interface states, substrate doping concentration and more

Measuring MOS capacitor capacitance while sweeping voltage shows changes through accumulation, depletion and inversion. The maximum accumulation capacitance Cox gives oxide thickness via tox = εox·A/Cox; the curve's position gives flat-band voltage and oxide charge, its stretch-out gives interface trap density, and the depletion slope gives substrate doping. It reveals neither linewidth nor particle counts.
