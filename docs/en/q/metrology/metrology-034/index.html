Metrology and analysis

Which correctly describes observing a cross-section in detail after breaking or milling the wafer?

  1. AOnly surface contamination can be seen
  2. BIt is destructive, so the observed wafer or chip cannot be used as product
  3. CThe product can be shipped as is
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. It is destructive, so the observed wafer or chip cannot be used as product

Cross-sectional SEM and TEM require cleaving the wafer or milling it with an FIB, so the examined area can no longer be sold (destructive analysis). Product wafers are therefore pulled for sampling, or dedicated test wafers are used. In return, internal structure that top-down non-destructive metrology cannot see, such as layer stacking, fill defects and sidewall profiles, can be checked directly.

  • A. Cross-sectional structure can be examined in detail.
  • B. Correct. It is destructive and cannot become product.
  • C. Broken or milled wafers cannot be shipped.
