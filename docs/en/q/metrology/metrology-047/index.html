Metrology and analysis

Which technique locates faults by detecting tiny heat generation in a powered chip with high sensitivity, periodically modulating the applied power and accumulating synchronized thermal images?

  1. ALock-in thermography
  2. BEllipsometry
  3. CSIMS
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. Lock-in thermography

Lock-in thermography switches the chip's bias on and off at a fixed frequency and accumulates only infrared images synchronized with that frequency, locating tiny heating from shorts at the microwatt level. Noise unrelated to the frequency averages out, raising sensitivity by orders of magnitude over ordinary thermal imaging. Because it can localize faults even through the package, it is used early in failure analysis.

  • A. Correct. Synchronized thermal images detect tiny heating.
  • B. Ellipsometry measures film thickness.
  • C. SIMS profiles elements in depth.
