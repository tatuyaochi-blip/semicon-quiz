Metrology and analysis

Which method is used to measure wafer bow, warp and flatness?

  1. ANoncontact shape measurement with optical interferometers or capacitance sensors
  2. BMass analysis by SIMS
  3. CElemental analysis by EDX
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. Noncontact shape measurement with optical interferometers or capacitance sensors

Wafer shape is measured without contact using optical interferometers or capacitive sensors that gauge the distance to both surfaces. Bow and warp affect handling and chucking, while local flatness determines whether the surface stays within the scanner's depth of focus, so both are controlled at incoming inspection and after deposition. SIMS is mass spectrometry and EDX is elemental analysis.
