Metrology and analysis

Which method is used to measure wafer bow, warp and flatness?

  1. ANoncontact shape measurement with optical interferometers or capacitance sensors
  2. BMass analysis by SIMS
  3. CElemental analysis by EDX
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. Noncontact shape measurement with optical interferometers or capacitance sensors

Wafer shape is measured without contact, using instruments based on optical interference or sensors that measure the distance to both sides of the wafer by capacitance. Bow and flatness affect exposure focus and wafer handling and chucking, so they are controlled in incoming wafer inspection and after deposition.
