Metrology and analysis

What is placed on the wafer to measure overlay error?

  1. ABond pads
  2. BNotch
  3. CDedicated overlay measurement marks
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. Dedicated overlay measurement marks

To measure overlay accuracy, dedicated marks (targets) combining patterns from the previous and current layers are made in places such as the scribe lines around chips. Optical metrology tools measure their relative positions to determine the misalignment, which is also used to correct the exposure tool.
