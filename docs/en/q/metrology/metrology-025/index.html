Metrology and analysis

Which inspection detects defects such as contact open failures using differences in brightness caused by differences in charging under an electron beam?

  1. AX-ray diffraction (XRD)
  2. BVoltage contrast inspection
  3. CX-ray reflectometry (XRR)
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. Voltage contrast inspection

Under an electron beam, contacts electrically connected to the substrate drain their charge, while floating ones cut off by an open charge up. The difference in charging changes the secondary-electron yield, appearing as a brightness difference. This voltage contrast reveals unopened contacts and broken wires invisible from the surface, and e-beam inspection tools use it in production.
