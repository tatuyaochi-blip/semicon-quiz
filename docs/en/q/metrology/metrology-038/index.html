Metrology and analysis

Which tool is widely used to find delamination at interfaces inside a package, such as between molding compound and chip, non-destructively?

  1. AScanning acoustic microscope (SAT)
  2. BElectron-beam writer
  3. CIon implanter
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. Scanning acoustic microscope (SAT)

A scanning acoustic microscope (SAT) applies ultrasound to the package in water and images the waves reflected from internal interfaces. At delaminated areas, the air gap reflects sound strongly, so delamination between resin and chip or leadframe, cracks and voids can be found non-destructively.

  • A. Correct. Delamination is found from ultrasonic reflections.
  • B. An electron-beam writer draws patterns.
  • C. An ion implanter introduces impurities.
