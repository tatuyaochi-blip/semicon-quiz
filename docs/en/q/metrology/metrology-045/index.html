Metrology and analysis

Which analysis method field-evaporates atoms one by one from the tip of a needle-shaped specimen and detects their positions and identities to map the 3D distribution of atoms such as dopants?

  1. AAtom probe tomography
  2. BOptical microscopy
  3. CScanning acoustic microscopy
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. Atom probe tomography

In atom probe tomography, a sample milled by FIB into a needle with a tip about 100 nm across is subjected to high voltage and pulses that evaporate surface atoms one by one as ions. Their impact positions on the detector give original positions and their time of flight gives mass, hence element, reconstructing the 3D atomic distribution. It reveals dopant distributions inside transistors at the atomic level, though detection efficiency is below 100%.

  • A. Correct. Atoms are removed one by one to map their 3D distribution.
  • B. Optical microscopes cannot show atomic distributions.
  • C. Acoustic microscopes examine delamination inside packages and the like.
