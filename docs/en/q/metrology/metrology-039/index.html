Metrology and analysis

Which method evaluates the quality of compound semiconductor wafers and epi layers without contact by shining laser light and measuring the spectrum and intensity distribution of the emitted light?

  1. AAFM
  2. BPhotoluminescence (PL) measurement
  3. CFour-point probe
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. Photoluminescence (PL) measurement

Photoluminescence creates carriers with laser light above the band gap and measures the light emitted when they recombine. The emission wavelength reveals composition and band gap, and the intensity distribution reveals defects and non-uniformity, so it is widely used to evaluate epi layers for LEDs and lasers.

  • A. AFM measures surface topography.
  • B. Correct. It measures light emitted under illumination.
  • C. The four-point probe is a contact method for resistivity.
