Metrology and analysis

What is the main purpose of performing metrology and inspection partway through the process on a production line?

  1. ATo deliberately slow down the process
  2. BTo catch process abnormalities early and stop them, preventing large volumes of defective product
  3. CTo increase product weight
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. To catch process abnormalities early and stop them, preventing large volumes of defective product

LSI manufacturing takes months, so discovering a problem only at the end would leave large volumes of defective product. Measuring and inspecting mid-process catches abnormalities early, so the responsible tool or step can be stopped and corrected, limiting losses.

  • A. Slowing the process is not the purpose.
  • B. Correct. It catches abnormalities early to limit losses.
  • C. It is not to increase weight.
