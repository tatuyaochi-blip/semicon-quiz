Metrology and analysis

Which spectroscopic method is often used to evaluate stress (strain) in silicon locally and non-destructively?

  1. ARaman spectroscopy
  2. BFour-point probe
  3. CC-V measurement
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. Raman spectroscopy

Raman spectroscopy measures light scattered with a slight wavelength shift through interaction with lattice vibrations. Unstressed silicon peaks at about 520 cm⁻¹; compressive stress shifts it up and tensile stress down, by about 1 cm⁻¹ per 500 MPa for uniaxial stress and per 250 MPa for biaxial stress. With the laser focused to about 1 µm, it evaluates channel strain and package stress locally and non-destructively.

  • A. Correct. Stress is evaluated from the Raman shift.
  • B. The four-point probe measures resistivity.
  • C. C-V measurement measures the capacitance of MOS structures.
