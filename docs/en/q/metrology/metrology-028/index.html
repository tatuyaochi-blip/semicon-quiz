Metrology and analysis

Which method is used to measure interstitial oxygen concentration in silicon?

  1. AFour-point probe method
  2. BEDX
  3. CInfrared absorption (FTIR)
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. Infrared absorption (FTIR)

Interstitial oxygen in silicon absorbs infrared light at a specific wavenumber. Measuring the strength of that absorption by FTIR gives the oxygen concentration nondestructively. It is widely used to control the oxygen concentration of CZ wafers and also to measure carbon and other concentrations.
