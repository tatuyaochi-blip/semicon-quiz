Metrology and analysis

Which equation is used to determine film stress from the change in wafer bow before and after deposition?

  1. ABlack's equation
  2. BStoney's equation
  3. CPreston's equation
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. Stoney's equation

Stoney's equation, σ = Es·hs²/(6(1−νs)hf)×(1/R − 1/R0), gives film stress from the substrate's Young's modulus Es, Poisson's ratio νs and thickness hs, the film thickness hf, and the change in wafer curvature before and after deposition. Conveniently, the film's elastic constants are not needed; curvature is measured by laser reflection. Black's equation concerns electromigration, and Preston's equation the CMP removal rate.
