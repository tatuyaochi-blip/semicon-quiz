Metrology and analysis

Which equation is used to determine film stress from the change in wafer bow before and after deposition?

  1. ABlack's equation
  2. BStoney's equation
  3. CPreston's equation
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. Stoney's equation

Stoney's equation gives film stress from the substrate thickness and elastic constants, the film thickness, and the change in wafer curvature before and after deposition. Stress is evaluated by measuring wafer bow with a laser or similar means. Black's equation concerns electromigration, and Preston's equation concerns CMP removal rate.
