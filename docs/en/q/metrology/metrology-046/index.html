Metrology and analysis

Which method, without forming electrodes, deposits charge on the wafer surface by corona discharge and measures changes in surface potential to evaluate oxide and interface electrical properties?

  1. AFour-point probe
  2. BX-ray diffraction
  3. CContactless electrical evaluation using corona charging (such as COCOS)
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. Contactless electrical evaluation using corona charging (such as COCOS)

Depositing a known charge on the wafer surface by corona discharge and measuring surface potential with a Kelvin probe allows oxide thickness, oxide charge, interface traps and leakage to be evaluated without fabricating MOS structures. No gate-electrode processing is required and measured wafers can continue processing, so it is used for inline monitoring. Four-point probe and X-ray diffraction cannot reveal these electrical properties.

  • A. The four-point probe is a contact method for resistivity.
  • B. X-ray diffraction examines crystal structure.
  • C. Correct. Corona charging and surface potential measurement are used.
