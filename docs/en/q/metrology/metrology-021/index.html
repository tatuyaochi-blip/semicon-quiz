Metrology and analysis

What is the main reason fine patterns a few tens of nm wide cannot be observed directly with an optical microscope?

  1. ABecause diffraction of light limits resolution (to roughly the wavelength of light)
  2. BBecause optical microscopes show no color
  3. CBecause optical microscopes are too dark
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. Because diffraction of light limits resolution (to roughly the wavelength of light)

Because of diffraction, the resolution of an optical microscope is limited to about half the wavelength of the light used. Visible light has wavelengths of about 400–700 nm, so patterns a few tens of nm wide cannot be resolved. SEMs and TEMs, which use electron beams with shorter wavelengths, are therefore used.
