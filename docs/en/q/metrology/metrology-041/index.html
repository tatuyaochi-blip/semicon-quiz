Metrology and analysis

Which instrument traces a sample surface with a fine stylus and measures step heights and surface shape from the stylus's vertical motion?

  1. AMass spectrometer
  2. BStylus profiler
  3. CEllipsometer
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. Stylus profiler

A stylus profiler drags a fine stylus across the surface with a small constant force and measures step heights and waviness from its vertical displacement. It is used to check etch depth, film steps and long-range planarity after CMP. Its resolution is coarser than an AFM's, but it can scan distances of millimeters. A mass spectrometer identifies composition and an ellipsometer measures film thickness.

  • A. A mass spectrometer identifies components from ion masses.
  • B. Correct. It traces the surface with a stylus to measure steps.
  • C. An ellipsometer measures film thickness from changes in polarization.
