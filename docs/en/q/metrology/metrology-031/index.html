Metrology and analysis

Which correctly describes the general difference between "metrology" and "inspection" on a semiconductor production line?

  1. AMetrology measures values such as dimensions and film thickness, while inspection searches for abnormalities such as particles and pattern defects
  2. BMetrology and inspection mean exactly the same thing
  3. CMetrology is done only by eye and inspection only by tools
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. Metrology measures values such as dimensions and film thickness, while inspection searches for abnormalities such as particles and pattern defects

Fabs distinguish metrology, which quantitatively measures values such as linewidth, film thickness and overlay at defined sites, from inspection, which searches wide areas of the wafer for anomalies such as particles and missing or bridged patterns. Locations found by inspection are then examined in detail by review SEMs or metrology tools. Both serve to reveal process status early and reduce defects.

  • A. Correct. Metrology measures values; inspection searches for abnormalities.
  • B. Their purposes and tools differ.
  • C. Both are mainly done with dedicated tools.
