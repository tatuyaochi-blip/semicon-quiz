Metrology and analysis

What is the measurement called that monitors a process by measuring product wafers partway through processing on the production line?

  1. AInline metrology
  2. BOffline metrology
  3. CDestructive analysis
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. Inline metrology

Inline metrology uses measurement tools built into the production line to measure film thickness, dimensions, overlay and defects on product wafers mid-process. It catches excursions early, keeping bad wafers from flowing downstream, and results may also feed back to adjust tool settings. Only non-destructive methods are used. It is distinguished from offline metrology, where samples are taken out of the line for detailed study, and from destructive analysis.
