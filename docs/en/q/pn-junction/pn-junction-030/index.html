PN junctions and diodes

What can be evaluated by measuring the capacitance of a reverse-biased PN junction while varying the voltage (C-V measurement)?

  1. AThe color of emitted light
  2. BOnly the carrier mobility
  3. CThe doping concentration on the lightly doped side and its depth profile
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. The doping concentration on the lightly doped side and its depth profile

As reverse bias increases, the depletion region widens and capacitance falls, and how it falls is set by the doping at the depletion edge. The slope of 1/C² versus voltage gives the doping, and W = εA/C gives the depth, so the doping profile of the lightly doped side can be measured nondestructively. The method is used to monitor epitaxial-layer doping, and with a mercury probe it can be done without forming electrodes.
