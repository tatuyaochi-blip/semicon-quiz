PN junctions and diodes

Which correctly describes the relationship between forward current and forward voltage of a PN junction diode?

  1. ACurrent increases linearly in proportion to voltage
  2. BCurrent increases exponentially with voltage
  3. CCurrent is constant regardless of voltage
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. Current increases exponentially with voltage

Diode current follows I = Is{exp(qV/nkT) − 1}, rising exponentially with forward voltage. At room temperature it grows about tenfold for each ~60 mV, so it seems to switch on abruptly above a certain voltage. It therefore does not obey Ohm's law like a resistor, although at high current series resistance makes the curve more linear. This steep turn-on is the basis of rectification and switching.
