PN junctions and diodes

What is the typical open-circuit voltage of a single crystalline silicon solar cell?

  1. AAbout 6 V
  2. BAbout 0.6–0.7 V
  3. CAbout 60 V
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. About 0.6–0.7 V

A single crystalline-silicon solar cell has an open-circuit voltage of about 0.6–0.7 V, limited by the band gap and by how little recombination occurs. Practical modules therefore connect about 60–72 cells in series to reach roughly 30–50 V. Open-circuit voltage falls with temperature (about −0.3%/°C), so output drops in summer heat. Making a cell larger increases its current, not its voltage.
