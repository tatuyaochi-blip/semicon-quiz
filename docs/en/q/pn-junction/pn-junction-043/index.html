PN junctions and diodes

Roughly what is the small-signal (dynamic) resistance of an ideal silicon diode carrying a forward current of 1 mA at room temperature? (kT/q ≈ 26 mV)

  1. AAbout 260 Ω
  2. BAbout 26 Ω
  3. CAbout 2.6 Ω
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. About 26 Ω

A diode's dynamic resistance is the inverse slope of its I-V curve, rd = nkT/(qI). With n = 1 and I = 1 mA, rd = 26 mV ÷ 1 mA = 26 Ω. It is inversely proportional to current: 2.6 Ω at 10 mA and 260 Ω at 0.1 mA. In small-signal AC analysis the diode is replaced by this resistance, and the bipolar transistor's emitter resistance re = 26 mV/IE follows the same idea.

  • A. About 260 Ω is the value at a current of 0.1 mA.
  • B. Correct. 26 mV ÷ 1 mA = 26 Ω.
  • C. About 2.6 Ω is the value at a current of 10 mA.
