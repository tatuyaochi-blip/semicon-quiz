PN junctions and diodes

Which value is closest to the theoretical maximum efficiency of a single-junction solar cell (the Shockley-Queisser limit)?

  1. AAbout 100%
  2. BAbout 80%
  3. CAbout 33%
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. About 33%

A single-junction solar cell cannot absorb photons below its band gap, and the excess energy of higher-energy photons is lost as heat. So even at the optimal band gap (about 1.34 eV), the theoretical efficiency limit is about 33% (the Shockley-Queisser limit). For silicon (1.12 eV) the practical limit is about 29–30%, and production cells have reached around 25%. Multi-junction cells, stacking different bandgaps, are used to exceed the limit.
