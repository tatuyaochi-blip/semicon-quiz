PN junctions and diodes

Which value is closest to the theoretical maximum efficiency of a single-junction solar cell (the Shockley-Queisser limit)?

  1. AAbout 100%
  2. BAbout 80%
  3. CAbout 33%
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. About 33%

A single PN junction solar cell cannot absorb light with energy below the band gap, and the excess energy of higher-energy light is lost as heat. As a result, even with the optimal band gap, the theoretical efficiency limit is about 33%. Multi-junction solar cells and other approaches are being studied to exceed it.
