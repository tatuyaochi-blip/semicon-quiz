PN junctions and diodes

What is the property of a diode that lets current flow easily in one direction but hardly at all in the other called?

  1. AThe photoelectric effect
  2. BRectification
  3. CAmplification
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. Rectification

The property of conducting well in the forward direction and hardly at all in the reverse direction is called rectification. Rectifier circuits that convert AC to DC are built using this property.

  • A. The photoelectric effect is the emission of electrons or generation of carriers by light.
  • B. Correct. Conducting in only one direction is rectification.
  • C. Amplification makes small signals larger and is done by devices such as transistors.
