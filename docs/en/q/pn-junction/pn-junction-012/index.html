PN junctions and diodes

Which correctly describes the mechanism of avalanche breakdown?

  1. ACarriers accelerated by a high field collide with the lattice and create new electron-hole pairs, which multiply in a chain reaction
  2. BElectrons tunnel quantum-mechanically through a thin barrier
  3. CThe junction melts as the temperature rises
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. Carriers accelerated by a high field collide with the lattice and create new electron-hole pairs, which multiply in a chain reaction

In avalanche breakdown, carriers accelerated by the strong depletion-region field strike atoms and create electron-hole pairs (impact ionization); these are accelerated in turn, and the current multiplies like an avalanche. Silicon's critical field is about 3×10⁵ V/cm, and lightly doped junctions with wide depletion regions break down at higher voltages. SiC's critical field is about ten times higher, so it achieves the same voltage rating with a thinner drift layer, giving lower-loss power devices.
