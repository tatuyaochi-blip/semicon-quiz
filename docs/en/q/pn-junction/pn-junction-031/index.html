PN junctions and diodes

Of the two terminals of a PN junction diode, what is the terminal on the p-type side called?

  1. AAnode
  2. BGate
  3. CCathode
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. Anode

The p-side terminal of a diode is the anode and the n-side terminal the cathode; forward current flows when the anode is at a higher potential. In the circuit symbol the triangle points in the direction of current, with the bar on the cathode side, and physical parts mark the cathode with a band. The words come from Greek—anode "way up" and cathode "way down"—describing where current enters and leaves.

  • A. Correct. The p-type side terminal is the anode.
  • B. A gate is the control terminal of a MOSFET or thyristor; a diode has none.
  • C. The cathode is the n-type side terminal.
