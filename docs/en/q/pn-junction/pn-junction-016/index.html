PN junctions and diodes

How does an LED emit light?

  1. AElectrons and holes injected under forward bias emit light when they recombine
  2. BIt is heated by reverse breakdown current and glows by thermal radiation
  3. CCurrent flows when light falls on it
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. Electrons and holes injected under forward bias emit light when they recombine

When forward voltage is applied to an LED, electrons are injected from the n side and holes from the p side into the junction, and when they recombine they release energy corresponding to the band gap as light. Current flowing when light falls on the device is how a photodiode works.
