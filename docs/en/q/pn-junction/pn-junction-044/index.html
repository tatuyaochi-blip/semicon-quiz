PN junctions and diodes

How does the breakdown voltage of a PN junction generally change when the impurity concentration on one side is lowered?

  1. AIt does not depend on impurity concentration
  2. BIt becomes lower
  3. CIt becomes higher
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. It becomes higher

With a lower impurity concentration, the depletion layer extends further at the same voltage and the field in the junction is weaker. A higher voltage is then needed to reach the critical field for avalanche breakdown, so the breakdown voltage rises. This is why high-voltage power devices use lightly doped drift layers.

  • A. The breakdown voltage depends strongly on impurity concentration.
  • B. Heavier doping lowers the breakdown voltage; lighter doping raises it.
  • C. Correct. The depletion layer widens and the field weakens, so the breakdown voltage rises.
