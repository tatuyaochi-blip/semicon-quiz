PN junctions and diodes

How does the breakdown voltage of a PN junction generally change when the impurity concentration on one side is lowered?

  1. AIt does not depend on impurity concentration
  2. BIt becomes lower
  3. CIt becomes higher
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. It becomes higher

With lower doping the depletion region extends further at a given voltage and the peak field is weaker, so a higher voltage is needed to reach the critical field (about 3×10⁵ V/cm in silicon), raising the breakdown voltage. Breakdown voltage scales roughly as doping to the −3/4 power; 600 V devices use drift layers doped in the 10¹⁴ cm⁻³ range. Lighter, thicker layers raise on-resistance, creating a trade-off with breakdown voltage.

  • A. The breakdown voltage depends strongly on impurity concentration.
  • B. Heavier doping lowers the breakdown voltage; lighter doping raises it.
  • C. Correct. The depletion layer widens and the field weakens, so the breakdown voltage rises.
