PN junctions and diodes

What is the phenomenon in which forward current through a PIN diode injects many carriers into the i-layer and greatly reduces its resistance?

  1. AEarly effect
  2. BConductivity modulation
  3. CHall effect
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. Conductivity modulation

The lightly doped i-layer normally has high resistance, but under forward current, large numbers of electrons and holes are injected from the p and n layers and its resistance drops sharply. This is conductivity modulation, the mechanism that lets high-voltage diodes and IGBTs combine high breakdown voltage with low on-state voltage.
