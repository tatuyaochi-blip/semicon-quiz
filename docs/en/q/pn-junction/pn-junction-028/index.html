PN junctions and diodes

How does the avalanche breakdown voltage generally change as temperature rises?

  1. AIt increases
  2. BIt decreases
  3. CIt does not change
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. It increases

As temperature rises, scattering by lattice vibrations increases, making it harder for carriers to gain enough energy for impact ionization, so the avalanche breakdown voltage increases (a positive temperature coefficient). Zener breakdown, by contrast, occurs at a lower voltage as temperature rises.
