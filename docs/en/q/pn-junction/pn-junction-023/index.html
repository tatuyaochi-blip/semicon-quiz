PN junctions and diodes

Why are termination structures such as guard rings placed at the edge (periphery) of a high-voltage PN junction?

  1. ATo make the junction emit light
  2. BTo prevent the electric field from concentrating at the junction edge and causing breakdown at a lower voltage than designed
  3. CTo increase the junction capacitance
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. To prevent the electric field from concentrating at the junction edge and causing breakdown at a lower voltage than designed

Real junctions are finite, and their curved edges concentrate the electric field, so they break down below the voltage of the planar region. Termination structures—guard rings, field plates, and junction termination extension (JTE), which tapers doping toward the outside—spread the edge field to approach the planar breakdown voltage. In high-voltage power devices, the termination around the chip edge takes up a significant share of the area.
