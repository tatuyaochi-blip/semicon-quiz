PN junctions and diodes

How does the reverse saturation current of a PN junction diode depend on temperature?

  1. AIt increases as temperature rises
  2. BIt is constant regardless of temperature
  3. CIt becomes zero as temperature rises
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. It increases as temperature rises

The reverse saturation current is carried by thermally generated minority carriers, so it rises sharply with temperature. In silicon it is said to roughly double for every 10 °C rise. This is one reason leakage current increases at high temperature.
