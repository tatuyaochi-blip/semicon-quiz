PN junctions and diodes

When a reverse bias is applied to an abrupt PN junction, where is the electric field strongest?

  1. AAt the junction interface where p-type meets n-type
  2. BAt the edges of the depletion layer
  3. CAt the contacts with the electrodes
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. At the junction interface where p-type meets n-type

The field in the depletion region is the integral of the ionized-impurity charge, so it is zero at the depletion edges and maximum at the metallurgical junction where p and n meet. In an abrupt junction the field profile is a triangle whose area equals the built-in potential plus the reverse bias. Avalanche breakdown occurs when this peak field reaches the critical field, so breakdown design focuses on reducing the field at the junction.

  • A. Correct. The field peaks at the junction interface.
  • B. The field is zero at the edges of the depletion layer.
  • C. There is almost no field in the neutral regions near the electrodes.
