PN junctions and diodes

Which property does a variable-capacitance (varactor) diode use?

  1. AEmitting light with forward current
  2. BResistance changing with temperature
  3. CJunction capacitance changing with reverse bias voltage
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. Junction capacitance changing with reverse bias voltage

When reverse bias is applied to a PN junction, the depletion width changes with voltage and so does the junction capacitance. A varactor diode uses this to control capacitance with voltage. It is used in radio and TV tuners and in voltage-controlled oscillators (VCOs).
