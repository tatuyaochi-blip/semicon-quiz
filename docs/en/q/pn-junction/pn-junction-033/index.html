PN junctions and diodes

Which statement correctly describes the depletion layer of a PN junction in thermal equilibrium?

  1. AMany majority carriers are gathered there
  2. BElectrons and holes are both present at high, equal densities
  3. CThere are almost no carriers, and the charges of ionized impurities remain
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. There are almost no carriers, and the charges of ionized impurities remain

Near the junction, electrons and holes diffuse and recombine, leaving a region with almost no carriers. This is the depletion layer: negatively charged acceptor ions remain on the p side and positively charged donor ions on the n side, creating the built-in electric field.

  • A. The depletion layer is a region from which majority carriers have been swept out.
  • B. There are almost no carriers in the depletion layer.
  • C. Correct. Carriers are depleted, and the charges of fixed ions remain.
