PN junctions and diodes

Which is a characteristic of an avalanche photodiode (APD)?

  1. AIt emits strong light when current flows
  2. BIt uses avalanche multiplication to amplify the photocurrent internally and can detect very weak light
  3. CIt converts AC to DC
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. It uses avalanche multiplication to amplify the photocurrent internally and can detect very weak light

An APD is a photodiode biased with a large reverse voltage close to breakdown, so carriers generated by light are multiplied by avalanche multiplication and collected as a large current. Because it can detect very weak light with high sensitivity, it is used in optical communication receivers and LiDAR.
