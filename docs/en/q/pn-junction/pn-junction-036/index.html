PN junctions and diodes

When an LED is connected to a power supply to light it, what is the main purpose of a resistor in series with the LED?

  1. ATo limit the current through the LED to a suitable value
  2. BTo change the LED's emission color
  3. CTo raise the LED's reverse breakdown voltage
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. To limit the current through the LED to a suitable value

Above its forward voltage an LED's current rises steeply, so connecting it directly to a supply would destroy it with excessive current. A series resistor limits the current; its value is (supply voltage − LED forward voltage) ÷ desired current. For example, driving a red LED (about 2 V) at 10 mA from 5 V needs (5 − 2) ÷ 0.01 = 300 Ω. High-current lighting LEDs use constant-current drivers instead to avoid resistor losses.

  • A. Correct. It limits the current to protect the LED.
  • B. The color is set by the semiconductor material, not the resistor.
  • C. A series resistor does not raise the reverse breakdown voltage.
