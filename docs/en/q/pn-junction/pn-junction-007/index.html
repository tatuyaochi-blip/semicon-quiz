PN junctions and diodes

Which is a characteristic of a Schottky barrier diode?

  1. AIt is a diode made by joining two p-type semiconductors
  2. BIt uses a metal-semiconductor junction, with a low forward voltage and fast switching
  3. CIt emits strong light when forward current flows
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. It uses a metal-semiconductor junction, with a low forward voltage and fast switching

A Schottky barrier diode uses a metal-semiconductor junction (Schottky junction). It operates with majority carriers only and has no minority-carrier storage, so its reverse recovery time is short and it is fast. Its forward voltage is also lower than that of a PN junction diode, so it is used in switching power supplies and similar circuits.
