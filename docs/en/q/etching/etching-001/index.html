Etching

Which correctly describes anisotropic etching?

  1. AEtching proceeds mainly vertically and hardly at all laterally
  2. BEtching proceeds at the same rate in all directions
  3. COnly the mask material is selectively removed
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. Etching proceeds mainly vertically and hardly at all laterally

In anisotropic etching, vertical etching proceeds preferentially, for example due to ion bombardment, and there is almost no lateral etching undercutting the mask. The mask pattern is therefore transferred accurately, allowing fine, vertical features to be formed.
