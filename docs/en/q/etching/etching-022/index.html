Etching

Which solution, made by buffering hydrofluoric acid with ammonium fluoride, is used for wet etching of SiO2?

  1. ASPM
  2. BHot phosphoric acid
  3. CBuffered hydrofluoric acid (BHF)
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. Buffered hydrofluoric acid (BHF)

Buffered hydrofluoric acid adds ammonium fluoride to hydrofluoric acid to stabilize its pH, so the oxide etch rate changes little as the solution is consumed and resist is less likely to peel. It has been used for oxide patterning and similar steps.
