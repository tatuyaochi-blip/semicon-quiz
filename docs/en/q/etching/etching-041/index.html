Etching

Which metric expresses how far the sidewall of an etched hole or trench tilts from vertical?

  1. AAspect ratio
  2. BTaper angle
  3. CSelectivity
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. Taper angle

Taper angle describes sidewall slope, with angles near 90° indicating highly anisotropic etching; heavy passivation produces tapered profiles with smaller angles. Features that will later be filled may benefit from a slight taper (around 85°) for void-free filling, whereas gates and fins are made nearly vertical to minimize top-to-bottom dimension differences, so taper is controlled per application.

  • A. Aspect ratio is the ratio of depth to width.
  • B. Correct. It is the angle of the sidewall slope.
  • C. Selectivity is the ratio of etch rates, such as film to mask.
