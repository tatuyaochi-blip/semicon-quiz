Etching

Which metric expresses how far the sidewall of an etched hole or trench tilts from vertical?

  1. AAspect ratio
  2. BTaper angle
  3. CSelectivity
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. Taper angle

The taper angle expresses the inclination of an etched sidewall. The closer to vertical (90°), the more anisotropic the etch. Because a slight slope can make later filling easier, it is controlled according to the application.

  • A. Aspect ratio is the ratio of depth to width.
  • B. Correct. It is the angle of the sidewall slope.
  • C. Selectivity is the ratio of etch rates, such as film to mask.
