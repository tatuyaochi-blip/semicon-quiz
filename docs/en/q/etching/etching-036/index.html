Etching

What is the masking scheme called that stacks three layers, an organic (carbon) film at the bottom, a silicon-containing middle layer and resist on top, to etch fine patterns deeply?

  1. ATrilayer resist process
  2. BDamascene process
  3. CLOCOS process
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. Trilayer resist process

In the trilayer process, the thin resist pattern is first transferred into the silicon-containing middle layer, which then serves as the mask for etching the thick organic film with oxygen-based plasma. Chaining the high selectivities of different materials in sequence produces a thick, robust mask from thin resist.

  • A. Correct. Three layers are stacked and the pattern is transferred in sequence.
  • B. Damascene forms wiring by filling trenches with metal.
  • C. LOCOS is isolation by selective oxidation.
