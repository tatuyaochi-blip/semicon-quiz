Etching

What is a mask of nitride, carbon or similar material called, used when photoresist alone cannot survive as a mask, such as when etching deep holes?

  1. APellicle
  2. BPhase-shift mask
  3. CHard mask
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. Hard mask

Deep etches would consume resist only about 100 nm thick before finishing, so the resist pattern is first transferred into a nitride, oxide or amorphous-carbon layer, which then masks the main etch—a hard mask. For 3D NAND's deep holes, amorphous-carbon hard masks several µm thick are used, making their deposition and removal important steps in their own right.
