Etching

Which metric indicates how small the variation in etch rate is across the wafer?

  1. ASelectivity
  2. BUniformity
  3. CAspect ratio
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. Uniformity

Uniformity indicates how consistent the etch rate and profile are between, for example, the center and edge of the wafer. Poor uniformity causes under-etching in some places and over-etching in others, so it is improved by adjusting plasma distribution, gas flow, wafer temperature and more.
