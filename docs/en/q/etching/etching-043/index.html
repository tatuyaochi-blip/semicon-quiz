Etching

In a typical etching plasma, how do the numbers of ions and radicals compare?

  1. AThey are always equal
  2. BRadicals far outnumber ions
  3. CIons far outnumber radicals
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. Radicals far outnumber ions

In a plasma, molecules dissociate into radicals more readily than they ionize, and radicals, having no charge, also live longer. Radical densities are therefore typically orders of magnitude higher than ion densities. Radicals drive the chemical reactions, while ion bombardment gives them direction.

  • A. They differ in how easily they form and how long they live, so their numbers differ.
  • B. Correct. Radicals typically outnumber ions by orders of magnitude.
  • C. Under typical conditions, ions are fewer.
