Etching

In a typical etching plasma, how do the numbers of ions and radicals compare?

  1. AThey are always equal
  2. BRadicals far outnumber ions
  3. CIons far outnumber radicals
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. Radicals far outnumber ions

In a plasma, dissociation into radicals requires less energy than ionization and occurs more readily, and radicals, being uncharged, are not drawn to the walls and live longer. Radical densities therefore typically exceed ion densities by two to three orders of magnitude or more. Radicals carry out most of the chemistry, while the scarcer ions promote reactions through bombardment and set the etch direction.

  • A. They differ in how easily they form and how long they live, so their numbers differ.
  • B. Correct. Radicals typically outnumber ions by orders of magnitude.
  • C. Under typical conditions, ions are fewer.
