Etching

Which gas can etch silicon isotropically without a plasma, for example to remove sacrificial layers in MEMS?

  1. AO2 (oxygen)
  2. BN2 (nitrogen)
  3. CXeF2 (xenon difluoride)
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. XeF2 (xenon difluoride)

XeF2 sublimes near room temperature and reacts spontaneously with silicon to form SiF4 (2XeF2 + Si → 2Xe + SiF4), etching silicon isotropically without plasma. Its selectivity to oxide, nitride, resist and many metals exceeds 1000, and because no liquid is involved stiction is avoided, so it is used to release MEMS structures from silicon sacrificial layers.

  • A. Oxygen is used to remove organic materials such as resist.
  • B. Nitrogen does not etch silicon.
  • C. Correct. It is a gas that etches silicon without plasma.
