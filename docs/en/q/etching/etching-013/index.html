Etching

During plasma etching, what is the phenomenon called in which conductors acting as antennas, such as long wires, collect charge and damage the gate oxide they connect to?

  1. AARDE
  2. BDIBL
  3. CAntenna effect (plasma charging damage)
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. Antenna effect (plasma charging damage)

Conductors exposed to plasma collect charge, and when it flows as current through the gate oxide they are connected to, the oxide degrades or breaks down. The larger the conductor area, the greater the effect, so layouts have antenna rules limiting the ratio of wire area to gate area.
