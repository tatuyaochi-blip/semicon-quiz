Etching

Why is it difficult to pattern copper wiring directly by dry etching?

  1. ABecause copper does not conduct electricity
  2. BBecause copper is too soft
  3. CBecause copper halides are not volatile, so the reaction products cannot be removed
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. Because copper halides are not volatile, so the reaction products cannot be removed

Aluminum reacts with chlorine to form volatile aluminum chloride and can be dry-etched, but copper chlorides do not volatilize below several hundred °C and remain on the surface. Copper wiring is therefore made without etching the metal: trenches in the dielectric are filled with copper and the excess removed by CMP (damascene). For very fine wires, etchable metals such as ruthenium are being explored.
