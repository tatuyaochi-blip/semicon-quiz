Etching

Which are examples of profile defects seen in high-aspect-ratio etching?

  1. ABowing, where the hole widens partway down, and twisting, where the hole bends
  2. BDishing, where wiring becomes dish-shaped in CMP
  3. CShadowing, where tilted implants create shadows
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. Bowing, where the hole widens partway down, and twisting, where the hole bends

Deep-hole etching is prone to profile defects: bowing, where ions scattered from mask edges widen the hole partway down; twisting, where charging at the bottom deflects ions so holes bend or spiral; and tapering that fails to open the bottom. In 3D NAND, keeping memory holes straight with uniform diameter through hundreds of layers is a major challenge, addressed with cryogenic etching and other techniques.
