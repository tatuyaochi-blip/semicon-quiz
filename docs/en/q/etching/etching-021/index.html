Etching

If the oxide etch rate is 100 nm/min and the mask is consumed at 20 nm/min, what is the selectivity to the mask?

  1. A0.2
  2. B5
  3. C80
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. 5

Selectivity = etch rate of the target film ÷ etch rate of the mask (or underlayer) = 100 ÷ 20 = 5. For example, etching 500 nm of oxide would consume 100 nm of mask.
