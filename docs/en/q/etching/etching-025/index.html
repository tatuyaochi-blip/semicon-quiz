Etching

What is an advantage of pulsed plasma, which etches while switching the RF power on and off?

  1. AEtching equipment becomes unnecessary
  2. BThe wafer is kept heated
  3. CElectron temperature and ion energy are easier to control, making charging and damage easier to suppress
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. Electron temperature and ion energy are easier to control, making charging and damage easier to suppress

In pulsed plasmas, electron temperature drops sharply during off-periods of tens of µs and negative ions form, helping neutralize wafer charging. Duty cycle and pulse frequency also tune ion energy and the mix of radical species, so pulsing is used at advanced nodes to suppress notching and electron-shading profile defects and to limit damage to gate dielectrics.
