Etching

When a 1 µm film is etched just down to the underlayer by perfectly isotropic wet etching, roughly how much is etched laterally from the mask edge (undercut)?

  1. AAbout 1 µm
  2. BAbout 10 µm
  3. CAlmost 0
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. About 1 µm

In isotropic etching, material is removed at the same rate vertically and laterally, so by the time 1 µm has been removed and the underlayer reached, about 1 µm has also been removed sideways from the mask edge. This produces dimensional changes comparable to the film thickness, making it unsuitable for fine patterns.

  • A. Correct. About 1 µm is etched laterally, the same as vertically.
  • B. In isotropic etching, the lateral amount is about the same as the vertical, not ten times.
  • C. No lateral etching is the case for ideal anisotropic etching.
