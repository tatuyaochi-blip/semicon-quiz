Etching

Which is a general characteristic of wet etching?

  1. AIt is highly anisotropic because it uses plasma
  2. BIt generally proceeds isotropically and readily undercuts the mask
  3. CIt requires vacuum equipment
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. It generally proceeds isotropically and readily undercuts the mask

Wet etching dissolves films by chemical reaction in liquid, so it generally proceeds equally in all directions (isotropic) and undercuts the mask by about the film thickness. It needs simple equipment, processes many wafers at once and easily achieves high selectivity. Dry etching is used for fine patterning, but wet etching remains important for blanket film removal and cleaning.
