Etching

Which correctly describes the "plasma" used in dry etching?

  1. AA very cold liquid
  2. BA state of gas that is ionized, containing a mixture of electrons, ions, radicals and so on
  3. CA solid metal
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. A state of gas that is ionized, containing a mixture of electrons, ions, radicals and so on

Plasma is a state in which power applied to a gas accelerates electrons and ionizes atoms and molecules, giving a mixture of electrons, ions, highly reactive radicals and neutral molecules. Dry etching uses the ions and radicals to remove film.

  • A. Plasma is not a liquid.
  • B. Correct. It is an ionized state of gas.
  • C. Plasma is not a solid.
