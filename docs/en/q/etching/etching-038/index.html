Etching

Which technique gradually thins an exposed resist pattern with plasma to create lines narrower than the exposure limit?

  1. AResist trimming (slimming)
  2. BReflow
  3. CEdge bead removal
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. Resist trimming (slimming)

Resist trimming isotropically erodes resist lines a little at a time with oxygen-based plasma to narrow them—printing 50 nm lines and trimming them to 30 nm, for example. It has been used to form gates narrower than lithography can resolve. It does not shrink pitch, however, and variations in trim amount and LER translate directly into dimensional variation, so control is critical.

  • A. Correct. It thins the resist to make narrow lines.
  • B. Reflow heats a film to make it flow.
  • C. Edge bead removal clears resist from the wafer edge.
