Etching

What is the process called that removes photoresist by oxidizing it to ash with oxygen plasma or similar?

  1. AAshing
  2. BAnnealing
  3. CDicing
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. Ashing

Ashing oxidizes resist organics in an oxygen plasma or similar, removing them as CO2 and H2O—hence the name, from turning the resist to ash. It strips resist after it has served as an etch or implant mask, followed by wet cleaning to remove residue. Downstream (radical-only) ashers are common to avoid damage, and hydrogen-based plasmas replace oxygen over low-k films.
