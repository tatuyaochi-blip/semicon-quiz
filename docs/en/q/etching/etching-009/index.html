Etching

What is the process called that removes photoresist by oxidizing it to ash with oxygen plasma or similar?

  1. AAshing
  2. BAnnealing
  3. CDicing
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. Ashing

Ashing oxidizes the organic resist with oxygen plasma or similar, removing it as CO2 and H2O. It is done to remove resist after it has served as a mask for etching or ion implantation, followed by wet cleaning to remove residues.
