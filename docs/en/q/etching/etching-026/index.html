Etching

Which gases are commonly used for dry etching of GaN?

  1. AChlorine-based gases (Cl2, BCl3, etc.)
  2. BOxygen only
  3. CWater vapor
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. Chlorine-based gases (Cl2, BCl3, etc.)

GaN's strong bonds make it chemically stable and nearly immune to wet etching, but chlorine-based plasmas remove it as volatile GaCl3, with BCl3 also helping strip surface oxide. High-density ICP tools etch mesas for LEDs and lasers and gate recesses for HEMTs, using low ion energies to keep etch damage from degrading performance.
