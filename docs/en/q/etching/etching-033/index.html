Etching

If etching proceeds at 100 nm/min for 3 minutes, how much film is removed?

  1. AAbout 3,000 nm
  2. BAbout 33 nm
  3. CAbout 300 nm
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. About 300 nm

Material removed equals etch rate × time: 100 nm/min × 3 min = 300 nm. In practice, variations in thickness and rate mean a short overetch follows endpoint detection. Working backward, a 500 nm film needs 5 minutes, or about 6 minutes with 20% overetch. Tools manage this by combining timed steps with endpoint detection.

  • A. About 3,000 nm is an order of magnitude too large.
  • B. About 33 nm is 100 divided by 3.
  • C. Correct. 100 × 3 = 300 nm.
