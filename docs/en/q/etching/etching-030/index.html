Etching

What is needed to etch oxide and stop on the nitride underneath?

  1. ALow selectivity
  2. BIsotropic etching
  3. CHigh etch selectivity of oxide to nitride
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. High etch selectivity of oxide to nitride

Stopping on nitride requires conditions that etch oxide quickly while barely touching nitride. Carbon-rich fluorocarbons such as C4F6 or C4F8 deposit polymer on nitride that halts the reaction, whereas oxygen released from oxide consumes the polymer so oxide keeps etching, yielding selectivity of several tens or more. This is crucial for forming self-aligned contacts.
