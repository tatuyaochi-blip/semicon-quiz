Etching

What is needed to etch oxide and stop on the nitride underneath?

  1. ALow selectivity
  2. BIsotropic etching
  3. CHigh etch selectivity of oxide to nitride
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. High etch selectivity of oxide to nitride

To stop on nitride, conditions are needed that etch oxide quickly while barely etching nitride. Selectivity of oxide to nitride is raised, for example, by using carbon-rich fluorocarbon gas conditions. This is important in forming self-aligned contacts.
