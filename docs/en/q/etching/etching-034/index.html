Etching

In wet etching, how does the etch rate generally change when the solution temperature is raised?

  1. AIt does not change
  2. BIt increases
  3. CIt decreases
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. It increases

Wet etching proceeds by chemical reaction, so higher temperature generally speeds it up—often by 1.5–2× per 10 °C. Stable removal therefore requires bath temperature control to about ±0.5 °C. Some chemistries, such as hot phosphoric acid for nitride (about 160 °C), are intentionally used at high temperature.

  • A. The etch rate depends strongly on temperature.
  • B. Correct. The reaction speeds up and the etch rate rises.
  • C. Raising the temperature generally speeds up reactions.
