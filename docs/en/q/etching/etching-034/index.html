Etching

In wet etching, how does the etch rate generally change when the solution temperature is raised?

  1. AIt does not change
  2. BIt increases
  3. CIt decreases
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. It increases

Wet etching proceeds by chemical reaction, so raising the temperature generally speeds up the reaction and the etch rate. Solution temperature must therefore be tightly controlled to keep the amount removed stable. Some solutions, such as hot phosphoric acid for nitride, are used at high temperature.

  • A. The etch rate depends strongly on temperature.
  • B. Correct. The reaction speeds up and the etch rate rises.
  • C. Raising the temperature generally speeds up reactions.
