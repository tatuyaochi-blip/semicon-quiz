Etching

Which method is commonly used to detect the endpoint of dry etching?

  1. AWeighing the wafer
  2. BMonitoring changes in the plasma's emission spectrum
  3. CChecking visually after etching
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. Monitoring changes in the plasma's emission spectrum

When the film being etched is gone and the underlayer is exposed, the reaction products in the plasma change, and the intensity of emission at specific wavelengths changes. Monitoring these changes in the emission spectrum detects the etch endpoint in real time (optical emission spectroscopy, OES).
