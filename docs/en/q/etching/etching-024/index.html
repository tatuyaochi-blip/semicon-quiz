Etching

Because changes in the condition of an etch chamber's walls alter results, what is it called to process dummy wafers before product to stabilize the chamber?

  1. ASeasoning
  2. BAshing
  3. CDeposition
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. Seasoning

Reaction products deposit on the walls of an etch chamber, and their condition changes, for example, the amount of radicals in the plasma. Processing dummy wafers after cleaning or at other times to condition the walls and stabilize results is called seasoning.
