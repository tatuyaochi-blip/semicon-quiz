Etching

Which is a characteristic of inductively coupled plasma (ICP) etching equipment?

  1. AIt does not use plasma
  2. BIt generates high-density plasma with a coil, and plasma density and ion energy are easy to control separately
  3. CIt uses liquid chemicals
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. It generates high-density plasma with a coil, and plasma density and ion energy are easy to control separately

In ICP, high-density plasma is generated by an induced electric field from RF current in a coil, while a separate RF supply biases the wafer. Plasma density and the energy of ions striking the wafer are easy to adjust independently, enabling fast, highly anisotropic etching at low pressure.
