Etching

Which is a characteristic of atomic layer etching (ALE)?

  1. AIt etches several µm quickly in one step
  2. BIt alternates surface modification and removal, controlling the etched amount at the atomic-layer level
  3. CIt is isotropic wet etching using chemical solutions
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. It alternates surface modification and removal, controlling the etched amount at the atomic-layer level

ALE repeats a self-limiting cycle: first only the top layer of the surface is reacted and modified, then only that layer is removed. Because the amount removed per cycle is fixed, etching can be controlled precisely at the atomic-layer level with little surface damage. It is essentially the etching counterpart of ALD.
