Etching

What is the role of etching in semiconductor manufacturing?

  1. ARemoving film from areas not covered by a mask such as resist to form patterns
  2. BDiffusing impurities
  3. CDepositing films on the wafer
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. Removing film from areas not covered by a mask such as resist to form patterns

Etching uses a resist pattern made by lithography, or a similar mask, to remove film or substrate from uncovered areas by chemical reaction or ion bombardment, shaping the circuit pattern.

  • A. Correct. Uncovered areas are removed to form patterns.
  • B. Diffusing impurities is a heat-treatment step.
  • C. Adding films is the deposition step.
