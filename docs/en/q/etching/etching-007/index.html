Etching

What is the phenomenon called in which the etch rate decreases as patterns become narrower and deeper?

  1. AAutodoping
  2. BAspect-ratio-dependent etching (ARDE, microloading)
  3. CDishing
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. Aspect-ratio-dependent etching (ARDE, microloading)

Ions and radicals have difficulty reaching the bottom of narrow, deep holes, and reaction products are hard to remove, so the higher the aspect ratio, the slower the etch rate. This is called ARDE or the microloading effect, and it causes depths to vary with pattern dimensions.
