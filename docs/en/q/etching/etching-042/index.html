Etching

What is the general effect of adding a little oxygen to fluorine-based etching gas?

  1. AEtching stops completely
  2. BSelectivity to the mask always increases
  3. CPolymer deposition on sidewalls and elsewhere decreases, and the resist erodes faster
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. Polymer deposition on sidewalls and elsewhere decreases, and the resist erodes faster

Oxygen burns off carbon-containing polymer as CO and CO2, reducing deposition on sidewalls and surfaces, and by scavenging carbon from fluorocarbons it frees more fluorine radicals, speeding etching. Resist is also eroded more readily by oxygen, so mask selectivity tends to fall. Additions are tuned in percent-level steps to balance profile against selectivity.

  • A. A small amount of oxygen does not stop etching.
  • B. Because resist erodes more easily, selectivity actually tends to drop.
  • C. Correct. Polymer decreases and the resist erodes faster.
