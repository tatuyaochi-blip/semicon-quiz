Etching

Why is cryogenic etching, which cools the wafer to very low temperatures, attracting attention for high-aspect-ratio processing?

  1. ABecause the etch rate becomes zero
  2. BBecause reactions on the sidewalls are suppressed, making fast, vertical deep etching easier
  3. CBecause plasma is no longer needed
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. Because reactions on the sidewalls are suppressed, making fast, vertical deep etching easier

When the wafer is cooled cryogenically, spontaneous chemical reactions by radicals on the sidewalls are suppressed or passivation forms more easily, while etching proceeds at the bottom where ions strike. Deep, vertical holes can therefore be etched quickly, attracting attention for 3D NAND memory-hole etching and similar uses.
