Etching

Why are coatings such as yttrium oxide (Y2O3) used on the inner walls and parts of plasma etch tools?

  1. AThey resist plasma, especially fluorine-based plasma, and erode little, reducing particles and contamination
  2. BThey conduct electricity well
  3. CThey make the tool look attractive
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. They resist plasma, especially fluorine-based plasma, and erode little, reducing particles and contamination

Plasma erosion of chamber walls releases metal contamination such as aluminum and particles, and changing wall conditions shift the process. Yttrium oxide forms nonvolatile yttrium fluoride when attacked by fluorine, which stays on the surface, making it chemically stable and erosion-resistant in fluorine plasmas; it is used to coat walls and parts. Denser coating methods than thermal spraying are also being developed.

  • A. Correct. They resist plasma and reduce particles and contamination.
  • B. Yttrium oxide is an insulating ceramic.
  • C. It is not for appearance.
