Etching

Which method inserts a small electrode into a plasma and measures the current as the voltage is varied to determine the plasma's electron temperature and density?

  1. AFour-point probe
  2. BX-ray diffraction
  3. CLangmuir probe
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. Langmuir probe

A Langmuir probe places a small electrode (probe) in the plasma and measures the current while sweeping the applied voltage. From the current-voltage characteristic, electron temperature, electron density, plasma potential and so on can be obtained, so it is used to evaluate the plasma state.

  • A. The four-point probe measures film or wafer resistivity.
  • B. X-ray diffraction examines crystal structure.
  • C. Correct. The plasma is evaluated from the probe's I-V characteristic.
