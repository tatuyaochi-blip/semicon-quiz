Yield and quality control

Which is the general explanation of the difference between a product's "quality" and its "reliability"?

  1. AQuality refers only to appearance and reliability to price
  2. BQuality mainly indicates whether it meets specifications at shipment; reliability indicates whether it keeps functioning over its period of use
  3. CThe two mean exactly the same thing
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. Quality mainly indicates whether it meets specifications at shipment; reliability indicates whether it keeps functioning over its period of use

Quality (initial quality) indicates whether a product performs to specification at the time of shipment. Reliability is the time-related property of continuing to function without failure under specified conditions for a specified period. Both are essential for semiconductor products.

  • A. They do not refer to appearance or price.
  • B. Correct. It is the difference between conformance at shipment and persistence over use.
  • C. They differ in whether time is involved.
