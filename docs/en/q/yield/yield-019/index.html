Yield and quality control

What is the purpose of evaluating manufacturing variation separately by factor, such as lot-to-lot, wafer-to-wafer and within-wafer?

  1. ATo find which level of variation is largest and narrow down the responsible process or equipment
  2. BTo set product selling prices
  3. CTo make wafer colors uniform
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. To find which level of variation is largest and narrow down the responsible process or equipment

Different levels of variation point to different causes: large lot-to-lot variation suggests drifting materials or tool condition, wafer-to-wafer variation suggests chamber differences or processing order, and within-wafer variation suggests tool uniformity. Quantifying each component, for example by analysis of variance, lets engineers tackle the most effective fix first. It has nothing to do with pricing or color.
