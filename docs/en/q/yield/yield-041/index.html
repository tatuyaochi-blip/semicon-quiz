Yield and quality control

Which system manages which lot is at which step and tool, under what conditions it was processed and so on, directing and recording the production flow in a factory?

  1. ABIST
  2. BMES (manufacturing execution system)
  3. CEDA
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. MES (manufacturing execution system)

An MES (manufacturing execution system) manages lot progress, equipment status, processing conditions (recipes) and work records, and directs which tool processes what next. It also forms the foundation for tracing manufacturing history (traceability).

  • A. BIST is built-in self-test circuitry on a chip.
  • B. Correct. It manages the factory's production flow.
  • C. EDA refers to circuit design tools.
