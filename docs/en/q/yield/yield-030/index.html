Yield and quality control

Which correctly describes the difference between the control limits of a control chart and a product's specification limits?

  1. AControl limits are set from the process's actual variation, while specification limits are set from the requirements placed on the product
  2. BControl limits and specification limits always have the same values
  3. CControl limits are set by the customer, while specification limits are calculated from process data
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. Control limits are set from the process's actual variation, while specification limits are set from the requirements placed on the product

Control limits are calculated from the mean and variation of data when the process is stable, and they indicate whether something abnormal has happened. Specification limits define the acceptable product range based on customer requirements or design. A process can be in control yet out of spec, and conversely in spec but out of control, which signals a process change. Cp and Cpk quantify the relationship between the two.
