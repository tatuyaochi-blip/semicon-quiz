Yield and quality control

In a semiconductor fab, what is the time from wafer start to completion called?

  1. ARefresh period
  2. BCycle time (manufacturing lead time)
  3. CClock period
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. Cycle time (manufacturing lead time)

Cycle time is the time from starting a wafer in the fab until it completes all steps, reaching several months for advanced logic. Shorter cycle times speed response to orders and accelerate the learning loop from problem discovery to confirming fixes, so yield improves faster. Actual processing is only a fraction of the total; much is spent waiting for tools, so managing WIP can shorten it.

  • A. A refresh period is the DRAM data retention cycle.
  • B. Correct. It is the time from start to completion.
  • C. A clock period is the cycle of circuit operation.
