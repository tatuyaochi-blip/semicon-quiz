Yield and quality control

In a semiconductor fab, what is the time from wafer start to completion called?

  1. ARefresh period
  2. BCycle time (manufacturing lead time)
  3. CClock period
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. Cycle time (manufacturing lead time)

Cycle time is the time from starting a wafer in the fab until it completes all steps, reaching several months for advanced logic. The shorter it is, the faster the response to orders and the faster the learning loop from finding a problem to confirming a fix, speeding yield improvement.

  • A. A refresh period is the DRAM data retention cycle.
  • B. Correct. It is the time from start to completion.
  • C. A clock period is the cycle of circuit operation.
