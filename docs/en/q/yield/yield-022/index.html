Yield and quality control

Which control chart is used to control the fraction defective?

  1. AX̄-R chart
  2. Bp chart
  3. CScatter diagram
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. p chart

A p chart is an attribute control chart that plots and controls the fraction defective for each sample such as a lot. To control the mean and spread of variables such as film thickness and dimensions, an X̄-R chart is used.
