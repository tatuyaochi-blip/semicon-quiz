Yield and quality control

In sampling inspection, which judges an entire lot as accepted or rejected by inspecting a sample, which metric expresses the quality level considered acceptable?

  1. AMTBF
  2. BSNR
  3. CAQL (acceptable quality level)
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. AQL (acceptable quality level)

In acceptance sampling, the AQL (acceptable quality level) determines, according to lot size, how many units to sample and how many defects are allowed for acceptance. Lot quality can be assured with a known probability without 100% inspection. Being a sample, however, a lot at the AQL is occasionally rejected and a poor lot occasionally accepted. MTBF is a reliability metric and SNR a signal-quality metric.

  • A. MTBF is mean time between failures, a reliability metric.
  • B. SNR is the signal-to-noise ratio.
  • C. Correct. It is the quality level accepted in sampling inspection.
