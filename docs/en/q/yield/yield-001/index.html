Yield and quality control

A wafer yields 500 chips, of which 450 are good. What is the yield of this wafer?

  1. A80%
  2. B90%
  3. C95%
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. 90%

Yield is calculated as good units ÷ total units. 450 ÷ 500 = 0.9, so the yield is 90%.
