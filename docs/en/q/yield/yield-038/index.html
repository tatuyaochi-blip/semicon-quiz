Yield and quality control

Which metric evaluates equipment productivity by multiplying the fraction of time running, the performance (speed) and the good-product rate?

  1. AOEE (overall equipment effectiveness)
  2. BTOC
  3. CCMRR
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. OEE (overall equipment effectiveness)

OEE (overall equipment effectiveness) multiplies availability (the fraction of time the tool runs), performance (actual versus ideal speed) and quality (fraction good). For example, 90% × 95% × 99% gives about 85%. It shows whether downtime from breakdowns and changeovers, speed losses or defects is the largest loss, guiding equipment improvement. Since semiconductor tools are expensive, raising OEE is especially important.

  • A. Correct. It multiplies availability, performance and quality.
  • B. TOC stands for theory of constraints or total organic carbon in ultrapure water, neither of which fits here.
  • C. CMRR is the common-mode rejection ratio of a differential amplifier.
