Yield and quality control

Among the causes of yield loss, what are defects called that recur in specific patterns due to, for example, interactions between design and manufacturing processes?

  1. ASystematic defects
  2. BRandom defects
  3. CSoft errors
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. Systematic defects

Yield loss divides into random defects, which occur probabilistically from particles and the like, and systematic defects, which recur reproducibly with particular layouts or process conditions. Systematic failures appear repeatedly at specific locations, such as pattern shapes prone to thinning during lithography. Their share grows with scaling, making design for manufacturability (DFM) essential. A soft error is a transient upset caused by radiation.
