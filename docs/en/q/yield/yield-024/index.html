Yield and quality control

Which concept expresses, from particle size and wiring layout, the area in which a falling particle could cause a failure?

  1. AActive area
  2. BDie area
  3. CCritical area
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. Critical area

Critical area is the area of locations where a particle of a given size would cause a short or open. It is nearly zero for particles smaller than the wiring spacing and grows with particle size and wiring density. Averaging it over the particle-size distribution predicts random-defect yield, and it guides layout improvements such as widening spacing.
