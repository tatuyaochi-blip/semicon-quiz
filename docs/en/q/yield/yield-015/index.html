Yield and quality control

Which type of control automatically adjusts equipment processing conditions for the next lot or wafer based on, for example, measurement results from earlier steps?

  1. AFMEA
  2. BPareto analysis
  3. CAPC (run-to-run control, etc.)
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. APC (run-to-run control, etc.)

APC (Advanced Process Control) uses measurement data to adjust equipment conditions automatically, reducing process variation. For example, etch or CMP time is corrected based on film thickness measured in an earlier step. It is used as a method of actively correcting the process, in addition to SPC monitoring with control charts.
