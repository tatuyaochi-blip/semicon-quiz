Yield and quality control

Which control chart monitors shifts in a process's mean and changes in its spread at the same time by plotting each sample group's mean and range (max − min) over time?

  1. AX̄-R chart
  2. Bp chart
  3. CPareto chart
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. X̄-R chart

An X̄-R chart computes the mean (X̄) and range (R = max − min) of each subgroup, for example five samples, and plots them on separate charts. The mean chart detects shifts in process center and the range chart detects changes in spread, catching increased variation that the mean alone would miss. For larger subgroups, the X̄-s chart uses standard deviation instead of range. A p chart tracks fraction defective.

  • A. Correct. It monitors the mean and range together.
  • B. A p chart controls the defective fraction.
  • C. A Pareto chart is not a control chart but a chart sorting items by frequency.
