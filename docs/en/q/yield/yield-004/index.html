Yield and quality control

Which correctly describes the purpose of a Pareto chart used in quality control?

  1. ATo examine the correlation between two variables
  2. BTo monitor process abnormalities over time
  3. CTo arrange defect categories in descending order of count and find the items to address first
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. To arrange defect categories in descending order of count and find the items to address first

A Pareto chart arranges defect categories or causes as bars in descending order of count (or monetary loss), overlaid with a line showing the cumulative percentage. A few items often account for most of the total, so the items to tackle first are clear at a glance. Correlation is examined with scatter diagrams and abnormalities over time with control charts.
