Yield and quality control

With the same defect density, what generally happens to yield when chip area is increased?

  1. AIt decreases
  2. BIt increases
  3. CIt does not change
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. It decreases

In the Poisson model, yield is Y = exp(−D × A) (D: defect density, A: chip area). The larger the area, the more likely a chip contains a defect, so yield falls. This is one reason chiplets, which split a large chip into several smaller ones, are attracting attention.
