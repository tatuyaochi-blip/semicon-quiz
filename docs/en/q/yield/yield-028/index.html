Yield and quality control

What is the system called that tracks each product's manufacturing history, needed to identify products that may have been affected when an abnormality is found?

  1. ABinning
  2. BGuard band
  3. CTraceability
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. Traceability

Traceability records which wafer and lot each product came from and which tools and conditions processed it, so its history can be traced back. When a tool problem is found, the affected products can be identified quickly, minimizing shipment holds and recalls. Unique IDs may also be written into each chip so that a field failure can be traced back to its position on the wafer.
