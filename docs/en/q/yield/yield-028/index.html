Yield and quality control

What is the system called that tracks each product's manufacturing history, needed to identify products that may have been affected when an abnormality is found?

  1. ABinning
  2. BGuard band
  3. CTraceability
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. Traceability

Traceability records which wafer and lot each product came from and which equipment and conditions processed it, so its history can be traced back. When an equipment abnormality or similar issue is found, the range of affected products can be identified quickly, minimizing shipment holds and recalls.
