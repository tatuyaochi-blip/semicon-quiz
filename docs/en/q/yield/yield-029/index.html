Yield and quality control

Which technique rejects chips that are within specification in test but show values far outside the distribution of the same lot?

  1. AATPG
  2. BPAT (part average testing)
  3. CAPC
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. PAT (part average testing)

PAT (part average testing) computes the distribution of measurements per lot or wafer and rejects outlier chips, for example beyond ±6σ from the mean, even if within spec. Outliers are likely to harbor latent defects and fail early. It is used to improve automotive semiconductor quality and is defined in AEC (Automotive Electronics Council) guidelines. ATPG generates patterns and APC controls processes.
