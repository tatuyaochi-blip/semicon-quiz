Yield and quality control

Which technique rejects chips that are within specification in test but show values far outside the distribution of the same lot?

  1. AATPG
  2. BPAT (part average testing)
  3. CAPC
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. PAT (part average testing)

PAT (Part Average Testing) determines the distribution of measured values for each lot or wafer and excludes chips that deviate greatly from it even if they are within specification. Such chips may have latent defects and be prone to future failure, so PAT is used to improve the quality of automotive semiconductors.
