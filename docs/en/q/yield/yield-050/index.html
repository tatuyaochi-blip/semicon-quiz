Yield and quality control

When there are several tools of the same type, what is it called to adjust and manage them so that processing results differ little between tools and chambers?

  1. ALithography
  2. BChamber matching
  3. CGettering
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. Chamber matching

In volume fabs, the same step runs in parallel on many tools and chambers, so differences in their results make product characteristics depend on which tool was used. Chamber matching adjusts and monitors parts condition, sensor readings and process results to make them consistent. It must be rechecked after part replacements and maintenance, and it is essential for reducing variation and for flexible tool allocation.

  • A. Lithography is the pattern transfer step.
  • B. Correct. It is adjustment and management to minimize tool-to-tool differences.
  • C. Gettering is a technique for capturing harmful impurities.
