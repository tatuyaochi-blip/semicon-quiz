Yield and quality control

When there are several tools of the same type, what is it called to adjust and manage them so that processing results differ little between tools and chambers?

  1. ALithography
  2. BChamber matching
  3. CGettering
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. Chamber matching

Volume fabs run the same step in parallel on many tools and chambers, so differences in their results spread product characteristics. Adjusting and monitoring to align part conditions, sensor values and process results is called chamber matching and is essential for reducing variation.

  • A. Lithography is the pattern transfer step.
  • B. Correct. It is adjustment and management to minimize tool-to-tool differences.
  • C. Gettering is a technique for capturing harmful impurities.
