Yield and quality control

Which chart divides data into intervals and shows the frequencies as bars to see the shape of the distribution of variation?

  1. AScatter diagram
  2. BCause-and-effect diagram
  3. CHistogram
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. Histogram

A histogram divides measured values into intervals and shows the number of data points in each interval as the height of a bar. It reveals the center, spread and skew of the distribution and whether there are two peaks, and is used to judge the state of a process by comparing it with the specification range.
