Yield and quality control

What is it called that shows yield improving over time as defect causes are analyzed and addressed after a new process is launched?

  1. ABathtub curve
  2. BYield learning curve
  3. CS-curve
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. Yield learning curve

Yield is low right after a new process launches and improves gradually through repeated failure analysis and corrective action; this trajectory is the yield learning curve. Prices are high early in production, so the faster yield climbs, the greater the profit, directly affecting competitiveness. The bathtub curve, by contrast, is a reliability curve showing how product failure rate changes over time.
