Yield and quality control

What is it called that shows yield improving over time as defect causes are analyzed and addressed after a new process is launched?

  1. ABathtub curve
  2. BYield learning curve
  3. CS-curve
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. Yield learning curve

Right after a new process is launched, yield is low, and it gradually improves through repeated failure analysis and countermeasures. The yield learning curve shows this progression. Raising yield to a high level quickly is directly tied to a semiconductor maker's competitiveness.
