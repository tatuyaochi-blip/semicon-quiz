Yield and quality control

Among defects on a wafer, what are those called that actually cause electrical failure of a chip?

  1. AKiller defects
  2. BFalse defects
  3. CNuisance defects
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. Killer defects

Not every defect found in inspection leads to failure. Defects that cause electrical failure, such as a large particle positioned to bridge wires, are called killer defects. The probability of failure is estimated from defect size and location to predict the impact on yield.
