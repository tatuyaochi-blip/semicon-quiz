Yield and quality control

What is the approach called that emphasizes, when a problem occurs, going to the actual place, looking at the actual thing and confirming the actual situation?

  1. AFormalism
  2. BPerformance-based pay
  3. CSangen-shugi (actual place, actual thing, actual situation)
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. Sangen-shugi (actual place, actual thing, actual situation)

The three-gen principle (genba, genbutsu, genjitsu) insists on going to the actual place, seeing the actual thing (defective parts or tools) and confirming the actual facts (conditions and data) rather than judging from reports or guesswork. It is a fundamental problem-solving stance in Japanese manufacturing, because causes imagined in a meeting room tend toward assumptions, while seeing the real thing often reveals clues missing from reports. Adding principles and rules makes it the five-gen principle.

  • A. Formalism values form over substance.
  • B. Performance-based pay is a human-resources concept.
  • C. Correct. It emphasizes the actual place, item and situation.
