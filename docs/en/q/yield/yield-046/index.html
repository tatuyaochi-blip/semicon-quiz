Yield and quality control

What is the quantity called that, for each type of defect found by inspection, gives the fraction of chips containing that defect that actually fail electrically?

  1. AAspect ratio
  2. BSelectivity
  3. CKill ratio
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. Kill ratio

Kill ratio is the fraction of chips with a given defect type that fail testing. A particle of the same size may be fatal on a densely wired layer but harmless where there is no wiring, so outcomes depend on defect type, size and layer. Knowing kill ratios shows which defects to prioritize and allows final yield to be predicted from inline inspection data.

  • A. Aspect ratio is the ratio of depth to width.
  • B. Selectivity is a ratio of etch rates.
  • C. Correct. It is the fraction of defects that actually cause failures.
