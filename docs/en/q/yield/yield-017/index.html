Yield and quality control

Which chart plots pairs of two characteristic values to examine their correlation?

  1. AScatter diagram
  2. BPareto chart
  3. CControl chart
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. Scatter diagram

A scatter diagram plots each data point with, for example, film thickness on the horizontal axis and transistor current on the vertical axis, to examine the relationship between two quantities. Points rising to the right show positive correlation, and points falling to the right show negative correlation. It is one of the seven QC tools, used for tasks such as inferring defect causes.
