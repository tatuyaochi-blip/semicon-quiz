Yield and quality control

What is the analysis called that statistically identifies, from the manufacturing histories of many lots, the tools, chambers, materials and the like commonly used by low-yield lots?

  1. AEquivalent circuit analysis
  2. BCommonality analysis
  3. CRaman spectroscopy
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. Commonality analysis

Commonality analysis compares the processing histories of low-yield and normal lots and statistically searches for tools, chambers, materials or time periods shared by the bad lots. If 90% of bad lots passed through the same etch chamber, for example, that chamber becomes the prime suspect. It quickly narrows candidate causes among hundreds of steps and many tools.

  • A. Equivalent circuit analysis builds models representing circuit behavior.
  • B. Correct. It identifies factors common to bad lots.
  • C. Raman spectroscopy is a spectroscopic method for measuring stress and the like.
