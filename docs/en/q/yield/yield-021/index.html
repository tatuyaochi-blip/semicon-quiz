Yield and quality control

On the same wafer, why does making chip area smaller greatly increase the number of good chips per wafer?

  1. ABecause chips become heavier
  2. BBecause the wafer color changes
  3. CBecause the number of chips increases and the probability of a defect per chip also falls
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. Because the number of chips increases and the probability of a defect per chip also falls

Smaller chips mean more chips per wafer. In addition, at the same defect density, smaller chips are less likely to contain a defect, so yield rises. Because the two effects combine, the number of good chips increases more than the area ratio alone.
