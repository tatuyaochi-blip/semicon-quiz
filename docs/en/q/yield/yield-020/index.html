Yield and quality control

Which correctly describes the idea behind the quality management activity called 'Six Sigma'?

  1. AInspecting each product six times
  2. BDividing the process into six parts
  3. CReducing process variation so the margin to the specification limits is about six standard deviations, making defects extremely rare
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. Reducing process variation so the margin to the specification limits is about six standard deviations, making defects extremely rare

Six Sigma aims to reduce process variation (σ) so that the distance from the mean to the specification limits is about 6σ. Allowing for mean shifts, the goal is to keep defects to a few per million. It is also widely used as a method of data-driven improvement.
