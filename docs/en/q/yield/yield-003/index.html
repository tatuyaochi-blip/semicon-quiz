Yield and quality control

In control charts (Shewhart control charts) used for process control, how are the control limits generally set?

  1. ACenter line ± 1σ
  2. BCenter line ± 2σ
  3. CCenter line ± 3σ
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. Center line ± 3σ

Shewhart charts typically place upper and lower control limits at ±3σ (σ: standard deviation) from the centerline. Under a normal distribution, a point falls outside by chance only about 0.27% of the time, so a point outside signals an abnormality. With ±1σ, about 32% would fall outside by chance, and even with ±2σ about 5%, producing far too many false alarms.
