Yield and quality control

In control charts (Shewhart control charts) used for process control, how are the control limits generally set?

  1. ACenter line ± 1σ
  2. BCenter line ± 2σ
  3. CCenter line ± 3σ
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. Center line ± 3σ

In Shewhart control charts, upper and lower control limits are generally drawn at ± 3σ (σ: standard deviation) from the center line. With a normal distribution, the probability that a point falls outside this range from chance variation alone is small, about 0.3%, so a point outside is taken as a sign of some abnormality in the process.
