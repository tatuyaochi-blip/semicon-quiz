Yield and quality control

Which diagram organizes the relationship between a problem (effect) and the factors that influence it in a shape like a fish skeleton?

  1. APareto chart
  2. BHistogram
  3. CCause-and-effect diagram
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. Cause-and-effect diagram

A cause-and-effect diagram places the problem (effect) at the right and branches influencing factors into major bones such as man, machine, material and method (the 4Ms), with finer causes as smaller bones; its shape gives it the name fishbone diagram. It is also called an Ishikawa diagram after its originator. One of the seven basic QC tools, it helps list candidate causes exhaustively. Pareto charts rank counts and histograms show distributions.
