Reliability

When electromigration in flip-chip solder bumps becomes a problem due to large currents, where are failures known to be especially likely?

  1. ANear the bump-to-wiring connection, where current crowds into one point
  2. BAt the center of the bump where no current flows
  3. CIn the resin outside the package
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. Near the bump-to-wiring connection, where current crowds into one point

In solder bumps, current density becomes locally very high at the corner of the connection where current enters the wide bump from a narrow trace (current crowding), so voids readily form there. Temperature rise from Joule heating and growth of intermetallic compounds between solder and metal also accelerate degradation.

  • A. Correct. Failures tend to occur near the connection where current crowds.
  • B. Electromigration does not occur where no current flows.
  • C. The resin does not carry current.
