Reliability

Which correctly describes latch-up in CMOS circuits?

  1. ANormal operation for holding data
  2. BA phenomenon in which a parasitic thyristor structure turns on and a large current keeps flowing between power and ground
  3. CA phenomenon in which the clock stops
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. A phenomenon in which a parasitic thyristor structure turns on and a large current keeps flowing between power and ground

CMOS structures contain a parasitic PNPN thyristor formed by PNP and NPN transistors in the wells and substrate. If noise or input overvoltage injects substrate current and turns it on, the two transistors amplify each other's current, sustaining a large supply-to-ground current that persists until power is removed and can burn out the device. Strong well contacts, guard rings and SOI prevent it.
