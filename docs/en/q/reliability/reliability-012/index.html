Reliability

Which failures are mainly evaluated by temperature-humidity-bias tests (THB and HAST)?

  1. ASoft errors caused by cosmic rays
  2. BClock delay
  3. CElectrode corrosion and increased leakage current due to moisture ingress
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. Electrode corrosion and increased leakage current due to moisture ingress

When voltage is applied in a hot, humid environment, moisture that has entered the package causes corrosion of aluminum pads and ion migration, leading to increased leakage current and open circuits. HAST applies high pressure to accelerate moisture ingress, evaluating moisture resistance in a short time.
