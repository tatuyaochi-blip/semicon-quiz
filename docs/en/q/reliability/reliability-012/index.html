Reliability

Which failures are mainly evaluated by temperature-humidity-bias tests (THB and HAST)?

  1. ASoft errors caused by cosmic rays
  2. BClock delay
  3. CElectrode corrosion and increased leakage current due to moisture ingress
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. Electrode corrosion and increased leakage current due to moisture ingress

Applying bias in hot, humid conditions lets moisture and ionic impurities that penetrate the package corrode aluminum pads and drive ion migration, increasing leakage or causing opens. THB runs at 85 °C/85% RH for about 1,000 hours, while HAST uses pressurized steam around 130 °C to accelerate moisture ingress, evaluating humidity resistance in roughly 100 hours. Soft errors and clock delays are targets of other tests.
