Reliability

When extrapolating TDDB test results to operating voltage, there is the E model, which expresses lifetime as an exponential of field, and the 1/E model, which uses the reciprocal of field. Which statement about their difference is correct?

  1. AThe 1/E model always gives the shorter lifetime
  2. BExtrapolated to low fields, their predicted lifetimes differ greatly, and the E model generally gives shorter (more conservative) estimates
  3. CThe two models always predict the same lifetime
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. Extrapolated to low fields, their predicted lifetimes differ greatly, and the E model generally gives shorter (more conservative) estimates

Depending on how high-field results are extrapolated to the use field, predicted lifetimes can differ by orders of magnitude. The E model (lifetime ∝ exp(−γE)) predicts shorter low-field lifetimes and the 1/E model (lifetime ∝ exp(G/E)) longer ones, so the E model is often used for conservative estimates. For ultrathin films, power-law voltage models have also been proposed, and which model fits best has been debated depending on dielectric type and field range.

  • A. Generally the E model gives the shorter lifetime.
  • B. Correct. They diverge at low field, with the E model being conservative.
  • C. Predictions change greatly with the extrapolation method.
