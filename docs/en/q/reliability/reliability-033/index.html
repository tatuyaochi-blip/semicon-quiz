Reliability

For the same kind of semiconductor, which correctly describes the reliability levels required for consumer and automotive use?

  1. AThey are exactly the same for any application
  2. BAutomotive use is more lenient
  3. CAutomotive use requires very low failure rates over wide temperature ranges and long periods, making it stricter
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. Automotive use requires very low failure rates over wide temperature ranges and long periods, making it stricter

Automotive semiconductors operate for well over a decade amid engine-compartment heat, arctic cold and vibration, and failures directly affect safety. They therefore require operation over wider temperature ranges than consumer parts and extremely low defect rates aiming at ppb levels, along with AEC-Q100 qualification and compliance with the ISO 26262 functional safety standard. Requirements are neither the same as nor looser than consumer ones.

  • A. Required levels differ by application.
  • B. Because safety is involved, automotive requirements are stricter.
  • C. Correct. Automotive use requires stricter reliability.
