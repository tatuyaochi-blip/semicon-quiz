Reliability

On printed circuit boards and similar, what is the phenomenon called in which, under humid conditions, metal (such as silver or copper) dissolves as ions between biased electrodes, migrates and deposits in tree-like forms, causing a short?

  1. AElectromigration
  2. BStress migration
  3. CIon migration
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. Ion migration

Ion migration occurs when, in the presence of moisture, electrode metal dissolves as ions, migrates under the electric field to the opposite electrode and deposits as tree-like dendrites, eventually shorting them. Silver is most prone, and copper also migrates. Narrow spacing, high humidity and contaminants such as halides accelerate it, and cleaning and conformal coating prevent it. It differs from electromigration, the movement of atoms within a metal.

  • A. Electromigration is atoms moving within a metal due to current.
  • B. Stress migration is atomic movement driven by stress.
  • C. Correct. Metal ions migrate with moisture and field, causing shorts.
