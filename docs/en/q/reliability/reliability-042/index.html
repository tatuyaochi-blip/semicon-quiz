Reliability

On printed circuit boards and similar, what is the phenomenon called in which, under humid conditions, metal (such as silver or copper) dissolves as ions between biased electrodes, migrates and deposits in tree-like forms, causing a short?

  1. AElectromigration
  2. BStress migration
  3. CIon migration
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. Ion migration

In ion migration, electrode metal dissolves into ions in the presence of moisture, migrates in the electric field to the opposite electrode and deposits there in branching (dendritic) form, eventually shorting the electrodes. It differs from electromigration, in which atoms move within a metal driven by electron flow.

  • A. Electromigration is atoms moving within a metal due to current.
  • B. Stress migration is atomic movement driven by stress.
  • C. Correct. Metal ions migrate with moisture and field, causing shorts.
