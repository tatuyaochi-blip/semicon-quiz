Reliability

Which correctly describes electromigration?

  1. AA phenomenon in which wiring corrodes due to moisture
  2. BA phenomenon in which current moves metal atoms in wiring, creating voids and hillocks
  3. CA phenomenon in which electrostatic discharge destroys devices
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. A phenomenon in which current moves metal atoms in wiring, creating voids and hillocks

Electromigration is a phenomenon in which electrons flowing through wiring collide with metal atoms, transferring momentum and gradually moving them. Voids form where atoms leave, causing open circuits, and hillocks form where atoms accumulate, causing shorts to adjacent wires. It occurs more readily at higher current densities and temperatures.
