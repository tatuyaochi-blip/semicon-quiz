Reliability

Which correctly describes electromigration?

  1. AA phenomenon in which wiring corrodes due to moisture
  2. BA phenomenon in which current moves metal atoms in wiring, creating voids and hillocks
  3. CA phenomenon in which electrostatic discharge destroys devices
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. A phenomenon in which current moves metal atoms in wiring, creating voids and hillocks

Electromigration occurs when electrons flowing through a wire collide with metal atoms, transferring momentum and gradually moving atoms in the direction of electron flow. Voids form where atoms leave, causing opens, and hillocks form where they pile up, causing shorts to neighboring lines. Black's equation gives lifetime inversely proportional to roughly the square of current density and shorter at higher temperatures. Moisture corrosion and electrostatic damage are different failures.
