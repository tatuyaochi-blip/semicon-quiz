Reliability

What is provided at an IC's I/O pins as a countermeasure against ESD (electrostatic discharge)?

  1. ALight-emitting diodes
  2. BCooling fans
  3. CESD protection circuits (such as protection diodes)
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. ESD protection circuits (such as protection diodes)

Static charge on people or equipment can reach thousands of volts, and discharging into an IC pin can instantly destroy thin gate oxides and junctions. I/O pins therefore have ESD protection circuits built from protection diodes and power-to-ground clamps that shunt the discharge current to supply or ground. Factories also use wrist straps, conductive floors and containers, and ionizers.
