Reliability

Which thermodynamics-based model expresses lifetime acceleration including the effects of multiple stresses such as humidity and voltage in addition to temperature?

  1. AThe Poisson model
  2. BOhm's law
  3. CThe Eyring model
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. The Eyring model

The Eyring model multiplies the Arrhenius temperature term by terms for stresses such as humidity, voltage and current, expressing lifetime acceleration under multiple stresses. It is used to estimate lifetime under real usage from results such as temperature-humidity-bias tests.

  • A. The Poisson model is used for yield calculations and similar.
  • B. Ohm's law relates voltage, current and resistance.
  • C. Correct. It is an acceleration model including multiple stresses.
