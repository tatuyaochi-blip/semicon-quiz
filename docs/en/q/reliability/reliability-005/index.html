Reliability

Which correctly describes TDDB (time-dependent dielectric breakdown)?

  1. AA phenomenon in which current moves metal atoms in wiring
  2. BA phenomenon in which solder joints fatigue under repeated temperature changes
  3. CA phenomenon in which a gate dielectric under a continuously applied electric field breaks down after a certain time
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. A phenomenon in which a gate dielectric under a continuously applied electric field breaks down after a certain time

TDDB (Time Dependent Dielectric Breakdown) is a phenomenon in which even an electric field too weak to cause immediate breakdown, applied for a long time, gradually creates defects in the dielectric, eventually leading to breakdown. It becomes a more important reliability item as gate dielectrics become thinner.
