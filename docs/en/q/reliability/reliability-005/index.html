Reliability

Which correctly describes TDDB (time-dependent dielectric breakdown)?

  1. AA phenomenon in which current moves metal atoms in wiring
  2. BA phenomenon in which solder joints fatigue under repeated temperature changes
  3. CA phenomenon in which a gate dielectric under a continuously applied electric field breaks down after a certain time
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. A phenomenon in which a gate dielectric under a continuously applied electric field breaks down after a certain time

TDDB (time-dependent dielectric breakdown) occurs when a field too weak to cause immediate breakdown, applied for a long time, gradually creates defects in the dielectric until they link up and bridge the electrodes, causing breakdown. Thinner gate dielectrics see higher fields, making it a key reliability concern; it also affects low-k dielectrics between wires. Atom migration in wires is electromigration, and solder fatigue is a temperature-cycling failure.
