Reliability

Which correctly describes MTBF (mean time between failures)?

  1. AThe average time from a failure until repair is completed
  2. BThe shortest time until the first failure occurs
  3. CFor a repairable system, the average operating time from one failure to the next
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. For a repairable system, the average operating time from one failure to the next

MTBF (mean time between failures) is the average operating time between failures of a system that is repaired and kept in service; higher means more reliable. Mean time from failure to completed repair is MTTR, and mean time to failure for non-repaired parts is MTTF. Because MTBF is an average, it does not mean nothing fails before then, nor is it the shortest time to first failure.
