Reliability

What is the main purpose of collecting and analyzing information on failures of products in use in the market (field failures)?

  1. ATo raise product prices
  2. BTo eliminate testing altogether
  3. CTo understand failure trends under real usage and feed them back into improving design, manufacturing and test conditions
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. To understand failure trends under real usage and feed them back into improving design, manufacturing and test conditions

Reliability tests only simulate field conditions, and in the market products may fail under unanticipated usage or environments. Analyzing returned parts and tracking failure rates and modes informs improvements to design, manufacturing and test conditions. It also prompts checks of other products from the same lot or tools, and decisions to halt shipments if needed.

  • A. Raising prices is not the purpose.
  • B. Pre-shipment testing is still needed even with field data.
  • C. Correct. Understanding real failure trends feeds improvement.
