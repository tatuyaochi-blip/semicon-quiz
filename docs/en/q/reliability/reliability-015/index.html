Reliability

What is the phenomenon called in which needle-like metal crystals grow from the surface of tin plating and short to an adjacent terminal?

  1. AHillock
  2. BVoid
  3. CWhisker
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. Whisker

Whiskers are needle-like crystals a few micrometers in diameter that grow from tin plating, driven by internal compressive stress, sometimes reaching millimeters in length. They can touch adjacent leads and cause shorts. Tin–lead plating suppressed them, so the move to lead-free brought the problem back. Annealing after plating, nickel underlayers and plating chemistry help suppress them. Hillocks are bumps on wires and voids are cavities.
