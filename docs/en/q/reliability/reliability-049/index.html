Reliability

To evaluate susceptibility to soft errors caused by cosmic-ray neutrons, which method measures quickly by applying neutrons far more intense than in the real environment?

  1. AAccelerated testing by irradiating with a neutron beam from an accelerator or similar
  2. BPutting the chip in a freezer
  3. CShining strong light on the chip
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. Accelerated testing by irradiating with a neutron beam from an accelerator or similar

Terrestrial neutron flux is low, so observing enough soft errors in the real environment would take a long time. Instead, an intense neutron beam at an accelerator facility is applied, and the soft error rate (FIT) in the real environment is estimated from the number of errors that occur in a short time.

  • A. Correct. Accelerated testing irradiates with an intense neutron beam.
  • B. Cooling cannot evaluate neutron-induced errors.
  • C. Light cannot reproduce neutron-induced phenomena.
