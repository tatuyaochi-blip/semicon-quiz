Reliability

Which typical reliability test evaluates lifetime and failure rate by operating products at high temperature for a long time?

  1. ATemperature cycling test
  2. BHigh temperature operating life test (HTOL)
  3. CDrop test
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. High temperature operating life test (HTOL)

HTOL (high temperature operating life) testing runs products at around 125 °C with at least nominal voltage for long periods to observe failures. Automotive AEC-Q100 requires 77 units from each of three lots for 1,000 hours with zero failures. Temperature and voltage acceleration allow field lifetime and failure rates to be estimated from short tests. Temperature cycling evaluates joint fatigue and drop testing evaluates shock resistance.
