Reliability

In automotive and similar applications, what is the summary called of which temperature, voltage and operating-time conditions a product will be exposed to over its lifetime?

  1. AMission profile
  2. BData sheet
  3. CNetlist
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. Mission profile

A mission profile describes the use conditions over a product's life: how many hours at which temperatures and voltages, and how many thermal and power cycles. For automotive parts, for example, temperature distributions while driving and parked are accumulated over the years of service. It determines required test durations and conditions and design margins, so it must be realistic.

  • A. Correct. It is the assumed usage conditions over the lifetime.
  • B. A data sheet summarizes a product's specifications.
  • C. A netlist represents circuit connectivity.
