Reliability

In automotive and similar applications, what is the summary called of which temperature, voltage and operating-time conditions a product will be exposed to over its lifetime?

  1. AMission profile
  2. BData sheet
  3. CNetlist
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. Mission profile

A mission profile is the assumed set of usage conditions over a product's life: how many hours at which temperatures, at which voltages, and how many temperature cycles. It is used to decide the required reliability test conditions and design margins.

  • A. Correct. It is the assumed usage conditions over the lifetime.
  • B. A data sheet summarizes a product's specifications.
  • C. A netlist represents circuit connectivity.
