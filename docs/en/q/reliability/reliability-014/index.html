Reliability

Which phenomenon creates voids as metal atoms move due to internal stress in wiring, even without current flowing?

  1. AElectromigration
  2. BStress migration
  3. CLatch-up
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. Stress migration

Because wires and surrounding dielectrics expand differently, tensile stress remains in wires after thermal processing. Metal atoms migrate to relieve this stress, forming voids: stress migration. It peaks at intermediate temperatures (around 150–250 °C) where atoms are mobile yet stress remains high, and it is a concern at vias under wide lines. It is distinguished from current-driven electromigration.
