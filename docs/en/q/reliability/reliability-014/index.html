Reliability

Which phenomenon creates voids as metal atoms move due to internal stress in wiring, even without current flowing?

  1. AElectromigration
  2. BStress migration
  3. CLatch-up
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. Stress migration

Because wiring and the surrounding insulator have different coefficients of thermal expansion, tensile stress remains inside the wiring after heat treatments during manufacturing. Stress migration is the phenomenon in which metal atoms move to relieve this stress, forming voids. Its cause differs from that of current-driven electromigration.
