Reliability

If 10,000 parts with a failure rate of 100 FIT are used for one year (about 8,760 hours), which is closest to the expected number of failures? (1 FIT = 1 failure per 10⁹ hours)

  1. AAbout 900
  2. BAbout 9
  3. CAbout 0.009
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. About 9

100 FIT is 100 failures per 10⁹ hours, or 10⁻⁷ per hour. 10,000 units × 8,760 hours = 8.76 × 10⁷ unit-hours, so the expected number of failures is 8.76 × 10⁷ × 10⁻⁷ ≈ 8.8, about 9. About 900 mistakes the rate for 10⁻⁵ per hour, and about 0.009 for 10⁻¹⁰ per hour. In systems with many parts, even small FIT values produce non-negligible failures.

  • A. About 900 is 100 times too large.
  • B. Correct. 8.76 × 10⁷ × 10⁻⁷ ≈ 9.
  • C. About 0.009 results from an order-of-magnitude error.
