Reliability

For gate insulator breakdown lifetime, why must the difference between the area of test devices and the total gate area on an actual chip be considered?

  1. ABecause area does not affect lifetime
  2. BBecause larger areas always last longer
  3. CBecause breakdown starts at the weakest spot, larger areas are more likely to fail early, requiring area scaling based on the Weibull distribution
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. Because breakdown starts at the weakest spot, larger areas are more likely to fail early, requiring area scaling based on the Weibull distribution

Dielectric breakdown is thought to start at the weakest spot in the film (weakest link). Larger areas are more likely to contain a weak spot, so lifetime shortens. Under Weibull statistics lifetime scales as area to the −1/m power; with slope m = 1.5, a 1,000-fold area increase cuts lifetime to 1/100. This relation scales results from small test structures to the total gate area of a chip to estimate product lifetime.

  • A. Area strongly affects lifetime.
  • B. Larger areas tend to shorten lifetime.
  • C. Correct. Larger areas fail earlier, so scaling is needed.
