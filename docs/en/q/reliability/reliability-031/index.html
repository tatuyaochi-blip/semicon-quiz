Reliability

Which pair correctly names "failures in which function is suddenly and completely lost" and "failures in which characteristics gradually drift out of specification"?

  1. ACatastrophic (sudden) failures and degradation failures
  2. BEarly failures and random failures
  3. CDesign failures and manufacturing failures
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. Catastrophic (sudden) failures and degradation failures

Catastrophic (sudden) failures abruptly lose function, for example through broken wires or insulator breakdown. Degradation failures gradually change characteristics, such as threshold voltage shifts or rising leakage, until they fall out of specification. Because countermeasures and evaluation methods differ, they are treated separately.

  • A. Correct. Sudden loss of function versus gradual degradation.
  • B. Early and random failures are classified by when they occur.
  • C. Design and manufacturing are classifications by cause.
