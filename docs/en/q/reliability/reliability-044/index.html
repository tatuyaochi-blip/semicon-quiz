Reliability

In leading-edge IC design, what is the practice called of anticipating transistor degradation from BTI and HCI over the usage period and checking that timing is met even with degraded characteristics?

  1. APhotolithography
  2. BWafer reclaim
  3. CAging-aware reliability design
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. Aging-aware reliability design

Transistor characteristics degrade gradually in use, slowing circuits. Aging-aware reliability design predicts degradation from each transistor's voltage, temperature and activity using models, then runs circuit simulation and timing analysis with aged characteristics to ensure margins hold until end of life. Another approach monitors degradation in operation and gradually raises supply voltage to compensate for the slowdown.

  • A. Photolithography is the pattern transfer step.
  • B. Wafer reclaim reuses spent monitor wafers.
  • C. Correct. Circuits are designed to work even after degradation.
