Reliability

Which correctly describes the general approach to failure analysis?

  1. AFirst break open the package and observe the inside
  2. BDiscard failed parts immediately and examine new ones
  3. CFirst perform nondestructive analysis such as visual inspection, X-ray imaging and electrical measurement, then destructive analysis such as decapsulation and cross-sectioning
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. First perform nondestructive analysis such as visual inspection, X-ray imaging and electrical measurement, then destructive analysis such as decapsulation and cross-sectioning

Starting with destructive analysis risks erasing the evidence and losing the cause. Non-destructive techniques, such as visual inspection, X-ray, acoustic microscopy and electrical measurements, are therefore used first to narrow down the failure mode and location, followed by destructive steps such as decapsulation, emission analysis and cross-sectioning. Failed parts are the only clue to the cause, so they should be kept as received and protected from static and moisture.
