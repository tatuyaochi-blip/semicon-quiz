Reliability

In high-k/metal gate nMOS transistors, what is the degradation called in which the threshold voltage shifts when positive gate voltage and high temperature are applied continuously?

  1. ALatch-up
  2. BPBTI (positive bias temperature instability)
  3. CElectromigration
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. PBTI (positive bias temperature instability)

PBTI is degradation in which positive gate voltage and high temperature on an nMOS cause electrons to be trapped at defects in the high-k film, raising the threshold voltage. It was minor with SiO2 gate insulators but became an issue with the introduction of high-k materials. It is the counterpart of NBTI, which occurs in pMOS.

  • A. Latch-up is a high current caused by a parasitic thyristor.
  • B. Correct. It is positive-bias temperature instability in nMOS.
  • C. Electromigration is the migration of wiring metal atoms.
