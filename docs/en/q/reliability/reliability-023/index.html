Reliability

What is the mean time to failure (MTTF) of a part with a constant failure rate of 1×10⁻⁵ per hour?

  1. A1×10³ hours
  2. B1×10⁵ hours
  3. C1×10⁷ hours
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. 1×10⁵ hours

When the failure rate is constant, mean time to failure is its reciprocal: 1 ÷ (1×10⁻⁵) = 1×10⁵ hours, about 11 years. However, this value applies to the random failure period and does not indicate when wear-out begins.
