Reliability

For typical consumer semiconductor products, which is closest to the usage period commonly assumed in reliability design?

  1. AAbout one week
  2. BAbout 10 years
  3. CAbout 1,000 years
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. About 10 years

Many semiconductor products are designed and evaluated to keep failure rates sufficiently low for about 10 years under specified usage conditions. Automotive and similar products require longer periods and harsher conditions. Degradation over this period is estimated from short test times using accelerated tests and models.

  • A. About a week is far too short for a product.
  • B. Correct. About 10 years is commonly assumed.
  • C. Designing to guarantee 1,000 years is not realistic.
