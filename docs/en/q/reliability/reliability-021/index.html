Reliability

Which standard is widely used as the reliability qualification standard for automotive integrated circuits?

  1. AAEC-Q100
  2. BISO 14001
  3. CISO 9001
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. AEC-Q100

AEC-Q100 is the reliability qualification standard for automotive ICs set by an industry body for automotive electronic components. It specifies many test items and conditions, such as high temperature operating life, temperature cycling and ESD, and automotive semiconductors are generally tested in compliance with it.
