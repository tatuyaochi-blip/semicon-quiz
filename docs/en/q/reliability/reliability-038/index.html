Reliability

Which model expresses the fatigue life of solder joints under temperature cycling as a relationship between the temperature swing (strain magnitude) and the number of cycles?

  1. AThe Coffin-Manson relationship
  2. BBlack's equation
  3. CMoore's law
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. The Coffin-Manson relationship

The Coffin–Manson law relates the magnitude of cyclic strain to the number of cycles to fatigue failure; for temperature cycling it is written N ∝ (ΔT)^(−m). For solder, m is around 2, so halving the temperature swing roughly quadruples cycle life. It is used to estimate life in milder field conditions from harsh temperature-cycling tests.

  • A. Correct. It relates temperature swing to fatigue life.
  • B. Black's equation is the electromigration lifetime equation.
  • C. Moore's law is an empirical rule about integration density.
