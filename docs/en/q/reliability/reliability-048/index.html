Reliability

Regarding BTI degradation, part of the threshold voltage shift recovers once the stress is removed. What does this imply for evaluation?

  1. AMeasurement timing does not affect the results
  2. BResults depend on the time between removing stress and measuring, so measurements must be fast or conditions standardized
  3. CBecause it recovers, the degradation can be ignored
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. Results depend on the time between removing stress and measuring, so measurements must be fast or conditions standardized

When BTI stress is removed, some trapped charge is released and the threshold shift partially recovers over microseconds to seconds. A delay between ending stress and measuring therefore underestimates degradation. Evaluation uses on-the-fly or microsecond-scale fast measurements and accounts for recovery during the on/off duty cycles of real circuits. Recovery is only partial; degradation remains.

  • A. Because of recovery, measurement timing affects the results.
  • B. Correct. Recovery makes measurement timing important.
  • C. Only part recovers; degradation remains.
