Reliability

Which failure can occur when electromigration creates a hillock on a wire?

  1. AOnly open circuits
  2. BA short to an adjacent wire
  3. CAn increase in threshold voltage
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. A short to an adjacent wire

In electromigration, voids form where atoms leave, causing open circuits, and hillocks form where atoms accumulate, which can touch adjacent wires or wires above and below and cause shorts.
