Reliability

Which failure can occur when electromigration creates a hillock on a wire?

  1. AOnly open circuits
  2. BA short to an adjacent wire
  3. CAn increase in threshold voltage
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. A short to an adjacent wire

In electromigration, atoms depleted upstream in the electron flow form voids that cause opens, while atoms accumulating downstream form hillocks that can rupture the dielectric and short to neighboring or overlying wires. Failures are not limited to opens. Threshold voltage increase comes from transistor degradation such as BTI, not from wiring phenomena.
