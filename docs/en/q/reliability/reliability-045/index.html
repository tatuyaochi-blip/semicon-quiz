Reliability

When estimating field lifetime from temperature-humidity test results, which model is commonly used to express the effect of humidity as a power of relative humidity?

  1. APeck's model
  2. BDennard scaling
  3. CThe Shockley equation
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. Peck's model

Peck's model states that lifetime is the product of a power of relative humidity, RH^(−n) with n around 3, and an Arrhenius temperature term. It converts results from accelerated tests such as 85 °C/85% RH or HAST into lifetime under real temperature and humidity. With n = 3, for example, lowering humidity from 85% to 40% alone extends life about tenfold. Dennard scaling and the Shockley equation belong to other fields.

  • A. Correct. It is an acceleration model expressing humidity as a power.
  • B. Dennard scaling is a guideline for scaling.
  • C. The Shockley equation is the diode current equation.
