Reliability

Which statistical distribution is commonly used to describe the times to failure of many wires in electromigration life tests?

  1. ABinomial distribution
  2. BLognormal distribution
  3. CUniform distribution
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. Lognormal distribution

Electromigration failure times usually follow a near-normal distribution in log time and are analyzed with the lognormal distribution. A chip has hundreds of millions of wires, so what matters is not the median but the time at which a tiny fraction, such as 0.1 ppm, fail early. That time is estimated from the distribution's median and spread, and design rules for allowable current density are set accordingly.

  • A. The binomial distribution counts successes and failures, not failure times.
  • B. Correct. The lognormal distribution is commonly used.
  • C. A uniform distribution cannot describe failure-time scatter.
