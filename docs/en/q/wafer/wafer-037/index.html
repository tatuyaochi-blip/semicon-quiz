Silicon wafers

Which is closest to the mass of one silicon wafer 300 mm in diameter and 775 µm thick? (Silicon density is about 2.33 g/cm³.)

  1. AAbout 13 g
  2. BAbout 1,280 g
  3. CAbout 128 g
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. About 128 g

The volume is π × (15 cm)² × 0.0775 cm ≈ 706.9 cm² × 0.0775 cm ≈ 54.8 cm³. Multiplying by the density of 2.33 g/cm³ gives about 128 g.

  • A. About 13 g is an order of magnitude too small.
  • B. About 1,280 g is an order of magnitude too large.
  • C. Correct. About 54.8 cm³ × 2.33 g/cm³ ≈ 128 g.
