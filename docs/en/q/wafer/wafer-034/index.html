Silicon wafers

Which pair of Japanese companies holds a large share of the global silicon wafer market?

  1. AKioxia and Renesas
  2. BShin-Etsu Chemical and SUMCO
  3. CTokyo Electron and Advantest
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. Shin-Etsu Chemical and SUMCO

The silicon wafer market is led by Japan's Shin-Etsu Chemical and SUMCO, together holding over half of the world market, followed by Taiwan's GlobalWafers, Germany's Siltronic and Korea's SK siltron. Japanese companies have long excelled at growing high-purity, highly flat crystals. Kioxia and Renesas are device makers, while Tokyo Electron and Advantest make manufacturing and test equipment.

  • A. Kioxia makes memory and Renesas makes microcontrollers and other devices.
  • B. Correct. These two are the leading wafer makers.
  • C. Tokyo Electron makes fabrication equipment and Advantest makes testers.
