Silicon wafers

When finishing a CZ pull, why is the ingot diameter gradually reduced (a tail cone is formed) before separating it from the melt?

  1. ATo raise the oxygen concentration
  2. BTo prevent slip dislocations caused by thermal shock from sudden separation from spreading into the crystal
  3. CTo make the ingot lighter
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. To prevent slip dislocations caused by thermal shock from sudden separation from spreading into the crystal

Separating a full-diameter crystal abruptly from the melt causes thermal stress that generates dislocations, which can propagate upward about one diameter's length into the product body. Tapering the diameter into a cone before separation prevents their formation and spread. Since the shoulder and tail are not product, keeping them short is also a cost concern.

  • A. It is not meant to raise the oxygen concentration.
  • B. Correct. It prevents dislocations from thermal shock.
  • C. Lightening the ingot is not the purpose.
