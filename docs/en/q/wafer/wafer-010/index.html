Silicon wafers

Which correctly describes the relationship between the resistivity of a silicon wafer and its doping concentration?

  1. AThe higher the doping, the higher the resistivity
  2. BDoping and resistivity are unrelated
  3. CThe higher the doping, the lower the resistivity
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. The higher the doping, the lower the resistivity

More dopant means more carriers and lower resistivity, so wafer resistivity is specified as a proxy for doping concentration: 1–10 Ω·cm p-type corresponds to boron in the 10¹⁵ cm⁻³ range, and 0.01 Ω·cm to about 10¹⁹ cm⁻³. At high concentrations, ionized-impurity scattering lowers mobility, so the relationship is not simply inverse; standard conversion tables such as ASTM's are used.
