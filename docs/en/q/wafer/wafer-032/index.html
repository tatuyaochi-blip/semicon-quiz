Silicon wafers

What is the cylindrical block of single-crystal silicon grown by the CZ method or similar, before it is cut into wafers, called?

  1. ATarget
  2. BIngot
  3. CPellet
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. Ingot

The cylindrical block obtained by single-crystal pulling is called an ingot. After its outer surface is ground to a uniform diameter, it is sliced thinly into wafers.

  • A. A target is a plate of source material used in sputtering.
  • B. Correct. A cylindrical single-crystal block is an ingot.
  • C. A pellet is material formed into small granules.
