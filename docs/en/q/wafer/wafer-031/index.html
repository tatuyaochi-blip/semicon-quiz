Silicon wafers

Roughly what size in inches is a silicon wafer 300 mm in diameter commonly called?

  1. A8-inch
  2. B12-inch
  3. C6-inch
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. 12-inch

One inch is about 25.4 mm, so 300 mm is about 11.8 inches, and it is commonly called a "12-inch wafer". 200 mm wafers are called 8-inch and 150 mm wafers 6-inch.

  • A. 8-inch refers to 200 mm wafers.
  • B. Correct. 300 mm is called 12-inch.
  • C. 6-inch refers to 150 mm wafers.
