Silicon wafers

Roughly what size in inches is a silicon wafer 300 mm in diameter commonly called?

  1. A12-inch
  2. B6-inch
  3. C8-inch
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. 12-inch

One inch is 25.4 mm, so 300 mm is about 11.8 inches but is commonly called a "12-inch wafer"; 200 mm is "8-inch" and 150 mm "6-inch." Early wafers were specified in inches, but from 150 mm onward the metric diameter is the formal standard and inch names are colloquial. Fabs are often described the same way, as "8-inch" or "12-inch" lines.

  • A. Correct. 300 mm is called 12-inch.
  • B. 6-inch refers to 150 mm wafers.
  • C. 8-inch refers to 200 mm wafers.
