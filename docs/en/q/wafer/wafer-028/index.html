Silicon wafers

Which method applies a magnetic field to the crucible in the CZ method to suppress melt convection and control oxygen concentration and other properties?

  1. AFZ method
  2. BMCZ method (magnetic-field-applied CZ)
  3. CBridgman method
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. MCZ method (magnetic-field-applied CZ)

Molten silicon conducts electricity, so an applied magnetic field exerts a Lorentz force opposing melt motion and damps convection. Magnetic CZ (MCZ) thereby reduces oxygen transport from the crucible into the crystal and suppresses temperature fluctuations and growth striations, improving uniformity. With melts of hundreds of kg for 300 mm crystals, convection is vigorous, so transverse-field MCZ is standard.
