Silicon wafers

Which conditions are mainly adjusted during pulling to keep the diameter of a silicon single-crystal ingot constant?

  1. AThe pull rate and melt temperature
  2. BThe color of the ingot
  3. CThe brightness of the fab lighting
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. The pull rate and melt temperature

In CZ growth, a camera watches the bright meniscus ring at the crystal-melt boundary to measure diameter, and pull rate and heater power (melt temperature) are adjusted automatically to hold it constant—pulling faster makes the crystal thinner, slower makes it thicker. Because pull rate also affects which point defects are incorporated via V/G, control must balance diameter against defect quality.
