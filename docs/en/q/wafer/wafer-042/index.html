Silicon wafers

What is the main use of low-resistivity n⁺ silicon wafers heavily doped with arsenic or antimony?

  1. AAs the light-absorbing layer of an image sensor itself
  2. BAs the anti-reflection coating of a solar cell
  3. CAs substrates for growing lightly doped epitaxial layers, and as the drain-side substrate of vertical power devices
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. As substrates for growing lightly doped epitaxial layers, and as the drain-side substrate of vertical power devices

Heavily doped, low-resistivity n⁺ wafers (about 0.001–0.01 Ω·cm) serve as substrates for epitaxial wafers with lightly doped epi layers on top. In vertical power MOSFETs and diodes, current flows down through the substrate, so low substrate resistance reduces on-resistance. Arsenic, antimony and phosphorus are chosen based on segregation and evaporation behavior, and suppressing out-diffusion into the epi layer (autodoping) is a challenge.

  • A. Lightly doped layers are used for light absorption.
  • B. Anti-reflection coatings are films such as nitride, not the substrate's role.
  • C. Correct. They serve as epi substrates and substrates for vertical power devices.
