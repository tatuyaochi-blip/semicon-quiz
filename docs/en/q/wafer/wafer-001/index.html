Silicon wafers

Which method is most widely used to produce single-crystal silicon for LSIs?

  1. ACzochralski (CZ) method
  2. BSol-gel method
  3. CElectrolytic refining
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. Czochralski (CZ) method

In the CZ method, a seed crystal is dipped into silicon melted in a quartz crucible and slowly pulled up while rotating, producing a large single-crystal ingot. It is well suited to large diameters, and most LSI wafers are made this way.
