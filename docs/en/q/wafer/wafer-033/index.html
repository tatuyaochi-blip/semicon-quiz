Silicon wafers

What is the main reason silicon wafers are round?

  1. ABecause square chips are easy to arrange on them
  2. BBecause exposure is impossible unless the wafer is round
  3. CBecause the single crystal is pulled while rotating, so the ingot is cylindrical
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. Because the single crystal is pulled while rotating, so the ingot is cylindrical

In CZ growth, the seed and crucible counter-rotate as the crystal is pulled, evening out the temperature around the axis, so the ingot grows as a cylinder and slices into round wafers. Placing rectangular chips wastes a few percent at the edge, but the crystal-growth method—and processes suited to round substrates such as spin coating and CMP—make this the practical shape.

  • A. For square chips, a square substrate would waste less.
  • B. Exposure tools do not require round substrates.
  • C. Correct. The growth method makes the ingot cylindrical.
