Silicon wafers

What is the main role of the notch (small cutout) on the edge of a silicon wafer?

  1. ATo indicate the reference crystal orientation
  2. BTo make the wafer lighter
  3. CTo drain cleaning solutions
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. To indicate the reference crystal orientation

The notch marks the crystal orientation of the wafer. Equipment detects the notch to align the wafer so that patterns are formed in the correct direction relative to the crystal. Smaller wafers used an orientation flat, a straight edge ground along part of the circumference.
