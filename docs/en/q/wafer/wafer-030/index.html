Silicon wafers

Which is one reason SiC wafers are difficult and expensive to manufacture?

  1. ABecause crystals can be grown very quickly by the CZ method
  2. BBecause the raw material is more abundant than silicon
  3. CBecause SiC does not melt at normal pressure, crystals must be grown at high temperature by methods such as sublimation, so growth is slow and defects form easily
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. Because SiC does not melt at normal pressure, crystals must be grown at high temperature by methods such as sublimation, so growth is slow and defects form easily

SiC sublimes rather than melting at normal pressure, so crystals cannot be pulled from a melt as with silicon. They are grown by sublimation methods, in which the source sublimes at temperatures above 2000 °C and recrystallizes on a seed, so growth is slow and defects such as dislocations form easily.
