Silicon wafers

Along which crystal planes does single-crystal silicon cleave most easily?

  1. A{100} planes
  2. B{111} planes
  3. C{110} planes
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. {111} planes

Silicon cleaves most easily along {111} planes. A (100) wafer tends to break along {111} planes inclined to the surface or {110} planes perpendicular to it, and the straight fracture lines can even be used to infer the wafer's crystal orientation.

  • A. {100} is the surface plane of a (100) wafer, not the easiest cleavage plane.
  • B. Correct. Silicon cleaves most easily along {111}.
  • C. Silicon also cleaves along {110}, but {111} is the easiest.
