Silicon wafers

Which method irradiates FZ silicon with neutrons to convert some silicon into phosphorus, making it uniformly n-type?

  1. ANeutron transmutation doping (NTD)
  2. BIon implantation
  3. CThermal diffusion
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. Neutron transmutation doping (NTD)

Neutron irradiation converts Si-30, about 3% of natural silicon, into Si-31 by neutron capture, which beta-decays with a half-life of about 2.6 hours into phosphorus (P-31). Because neutrons penetrate uniformly, the result is n-type silicon with resistivity variation of only a few percent—far more uniform than conventional doping with segregation. Requiring a nuclear reactor, supply is limited, and NTD silicon serves high-voltage power devices such as thyristors and IGBTs.
