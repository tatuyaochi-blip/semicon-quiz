Silicon wafers

What is the purpose of using bulk microdefects (BMDs), formed by oxygen precipitation inside CZ wafers?

  1. ATo use them as gettering sites that capture metal impurities
  2. BTo make the wafer emit light
  3. CTo weaken the wafer
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. To use them as gettering sites that capture metal impurities

Oxygen in CZ wafers forms precipitates inside the wafer during heat treatment (BMDs, bulk microdefects). These precipitates and the strain around them capture metal impurities, so they are used for intrinsic gettering, keeping metal contamination away from the device region at the surface.
