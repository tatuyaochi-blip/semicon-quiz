Silicon wafers

Which impurity is typically added to the melt to make p-type silicon crystals by the CZ method?

  1. APhosphorus (P)
  2. BArsenic (As)
  3. CBoron (B)
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. Boron (B)

Boron, a group III element, is added to make p-type silicon. For n-type, group V elements such as phosphorus, arsenic and antimony are used. The crystal's resistivity is set by the amount of dopant added.
