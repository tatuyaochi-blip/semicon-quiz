Silicon wafers

In CZ crystals, what are the periodic, striped variations in dopant or oxygen concentration caused by fluctuations such as temperature at the growth interface called?

  1. AStriations (growth bands)
  2. BNotch
  3. CFacets
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. Striations (growth bands)

As the crystal rotates during pulling, melt convection and temperature fluctuations periodically vary the growth rate and impurity incorporation at the interface. The result is striations—banded variations in dopant and oxygen concentration—that cause small-scale resistivity variation. In a wafer they appear as concentric rings like tree rings, and suppressing convection with MCZ reduces them.

  • A. Correct. They are periodic, striped concentration variations.
  • B. A notch is a cut in the wafer edge.
  • C. Facets are flat crystal faces.
