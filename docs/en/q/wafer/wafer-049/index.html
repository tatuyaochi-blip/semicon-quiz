Silicon wafers

In CZ crystals, what are the periodic, striped variations in dopant or oxygen concentration caused by fluctuations such as temperature at the growth interface called?

  1. AStriations (growth bands)
  2. BNotch
  3. CFacets
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. Striations (growth bands)

When the crystal is pulled while rotating, convection and temperature fluctuations in the melt periodically change how much impurity is incorporated at the growth interface. The result is striations, striped variations in dopant or oxygen concentration, which cause small variations in resistivity.

  • A. Correct. They are periodic, striped concentration variations.
  • B. A notch is a cut in the wafer edge.
  • C. Facets are flat crystal faces.
