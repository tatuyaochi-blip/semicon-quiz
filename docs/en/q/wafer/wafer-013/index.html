Silicon wafers

Which is the typical method for producing high-purity polycrystalline silicon?

  1. ABosch process
  2. BSiemens process (deposition by hydrogen reduction of trichlorosilane)
  3. CDamascene process
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. Siemens process (deposition by hydrogen reduction of trichlorosilane)

In the Siemens process, metallurgical silicon is reacted with hydrogen chloride to make trichlorosilane, which is purified by distillation and then reacted with hydrogen on heated silicon rods to deposit high-purity silicon. The polysilicon made this way is the raw material for the CZ method and others.
