Silicon wafers

Which method has been used to make multicrystalline silicon ingots for solar cells cheaply and in large volumes?

  1. ACasting, in which the melt is solidified directionally in a mold
  2. BThe FZ method
  3. CMBE
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. Casting, in which the melt is solidified directionally in a mold

In casting (directional solidification), molten silicon is solidified gradually from the bottom of a square crucible, forming large multicrystalline ingots of hundreds of kg to a ton. The square shape wastes little area when cells are tiled, and it is cheaper than single-crystal growth, so it dominated solar cells into the early 2010s. Grain boundaries reduce efficiency, however, and higher-efficiency monocrystalline (CZ) silicon now exceeds 90% of production.

  • A. Correct. The melt is solidified directionally in a mold.
  • B. The FZ method makes high-purity single crystals.
  • C. MBE grows thin films epitaxially.
