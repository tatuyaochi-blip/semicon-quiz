Silicon wafers

Which method has been used to make multicrystalline silicon ingots for solar cells cheaply and in large volumes?

  1. ACasting, in which the melt is solidified directionally in a mold
  2. BThe FZ method
  3. CMBE
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. Casting, in which the melt is solidified directionally in a mold

In casting (directional solidification), molten silicon is solidified gradually from the bottom in a square mold to make a large multicrystalline ingot. Its quality is lower than single crystal, but it can be mass-produced cheaply, so it has been widely used for solar cells. Recently the share of single-crystal solar cells has grown.

  • A. Correct. The melt is solidified directionally in a mold.
  • B. The FZ method makes high-purity single crystals.
  • C. MBE grows thin films epitaxially.
