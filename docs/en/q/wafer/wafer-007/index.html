Silicon wafers

What is the most appropriate order of steps for making wafers from a single-crystal ingot?

  1. ASlicing → lapping (grinding) → etching → mirror polishing
  2. BMirror polishing → slicing → etching → lapping
  3. CEtching → mirror polishing → slicing → lapping
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. Slicing → lapping (grinding) → etching → mirror polishing

The ingot is cut into thin slices with a wire saw or similar (slicing), and lapping or grinding sets thickness and flatness. Next, the damaged layer from machining is removed by etching, and finally mirror polishing gives a mirror-like surface. The wafers are then cleaned and inspected before shipment.
