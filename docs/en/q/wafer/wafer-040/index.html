Silicon wafers

Which is closest to the typical concentration of interstitial oxygen in silicon crystals grown by the CZ method?

  1. AAbout 10¹⁸ cm⁻³
  2. BAbout 10²³ cm⁻³
  3. CAbout 10¹⁰ cm⁻³
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. About 10¹⁸ cm⁻³

CZ crystals contain about 10¹⁷–10¹⁸ cm⁻³ (a few to over ten ppma) of oxygen from the quartz crucible, mostly at interstitial sites. It precipitates during anneals to aid gettering and pins dislocations to strengthen wafers, but too much causes defects and thermal donors. Concentration is measured by infrared absorption (near 9 µm) and held within narrow ranges per application.

  • A. Correct. About 10¹⁷ to 10¹⁸ cm⁻³ is typical.
  • B. About 10²³ cm⁻³ exceeds silicon's atomic density (about 5×10²² cm⁻³), which is impossible.
  • C. About 10¹⁰ cm⁻³ is on the order of the intrinsic carrier density, far too low for oxygen.
