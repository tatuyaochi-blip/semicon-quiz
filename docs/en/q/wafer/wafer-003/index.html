Silicon wafers

Which is a characteristic of single-crystal silicon made by the FZ (floating zone) method?

  1. AIt is the easiest to scale to large diameters and is the mainstream method for 300 mm wafers
  2. BBecause no quartz crucible is used, it contains little oxygen and other impurities, making high-resistivity crystals easy to obtain
  3. CIt turns polycrystalline silicon into a single crystal without melting it
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. Because no quartz crucible is used, it contains little oxygen and other impurities, making high-resistivity crystals easy to obtain

The float-zone (FZ) method melts a narrow band of a polysilicon rod with RF induction heating and moves the molten zone along the rod to convert it to a single crystal. With no crucible, little oxygen enters, and impurities are swept away in the molten zone, yielding high-purity crystals with resistivities above 1000 Ω·cm. FZ silicon serves high-voltage power devices such as IGBTs and radiation detectors, but CZ scales more easily, with FZ limited to about 200 mm.
