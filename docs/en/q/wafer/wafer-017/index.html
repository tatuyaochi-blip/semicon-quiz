Silicon wafers

What is the main purpose of chamfering (beveling) the edge of a wafer?

  1. ATo prevent chipping and cracking during handling and processing, and to suppress film buildup at the edge
  2. BTo make the wafer lighter
  3. CTo change the color of the wafer
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. To prevent chipping and cracking during handling and processing, and to suppress film buildup at the edge

If the edge were left sharp, it would chip easily on contact during handling, and the fragments would become particles. Rounding the edge prevents chipping and cracking and also suppresses buildup of resist and epitaxial layers at the edge.
