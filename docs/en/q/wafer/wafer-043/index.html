Silicon wafers

When CZ silicon is heat-treated around 450 °C, oxygen atoms can cluster and act as donors, changing the wafer's resistivity. What is this called?

  1. AGettering sites
  2. BThermal donors
  3. CDeep acceptors
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. Thermal donors

Annealing oxygen-bearing CZ silicon at 350–500 °C forms small clusters of a few oxygen atoms that release electrons—thermal donors. They form fastest near 450 °C and can reach about 10¹⁶ cm⁻³, substantially changing the resistivity of lightly doped p-type wafers or even inverting them to n-type. A brief anneal above about 650 °C eliminates them and is applied before wafers ship.

  • A. Gettering sites are places that capture impurities.
  • B. Correct. Oxygen clusters act as donors.
  • C. They act as donors, not acceptors.
