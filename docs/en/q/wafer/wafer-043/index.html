Silicon wafers

When CZ silicon is heat-treated around 450 °C, oxygen atoms can cluster and act as donors, changing the wafer's resistivity. What is this called?

  1. AThermal donors
  2. BDeep acceptors
  3. CGettering sites
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. Thermal donors

Heat-treating CZ silicon containing interstitial oxygen at about 350 to 500 °C forms small oxygen clusters that release electrons and act as donors. These are called thermal donors, and especially in lightly doped p-type wafers they can change the resistivity greatly or even convert it to n-type. They can be eliminated by heat treatment at about 650 °C or above.

  • A. Correct. Oxygen clusters act as donors.
  • B. They act as donors, not acceptors.
  • C. Gettering sites are places that capture impurities.
