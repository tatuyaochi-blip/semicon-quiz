Silicon wafers

In the CZ method, what is the main purpose of controlling the ratio of pull rate to temperature gradient in the crystal (V/G)?

  1. ATo control the type and amount of point defects (vacancies and interstitial silicon) incorporated, reducing crystal defects
  2. BTo change the color of the crystal
  3. CTo extend the life of the crucible
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. To control the type and amount of point defects (vacancies and interstitial silicon) incorporated, reducing crystal defects

The ratio of pulling speed V to the temperature gradient G near the interface determines whether excess point defects incorporated into the crystal are vacancies or self-interstitials. High V/G leaves surplus vacancies that cluster into voids (COPs); low V/G leaves interstitials that form dislocation loops. Growing within the narrow window where they balance yields nearly defect-free ("perfect") silicon.
