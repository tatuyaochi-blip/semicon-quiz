Silicon wafers

What are the very gentle undulations on a wafer surface, with lateral wavelengths of a few millimeters and heights of tens of nanometers, called?

  1. ANanotopography
  2. BNotch
  3. CSurface roughness
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. Nanotopography

Nanotopography refers to gentle undulations with lateral extents of about 0.2 to 20 mm and heights of a few to tens of nanometers. When films are planarized by CMP, these cause film thickness to vary along the undulations, so they are tightly controlled in wafers for advanced devices.

  • A. Correct. It is gentle undulation of tens of nanometers with millimeter-scale periods.
  • B. A notch is a cut in the wafer edge.
  • C. Surface roughness is finer unevenness with shorter periods (nm to µm).
