Silicon wafers

Which crystal orientation is commonly used for silicon wafers for CMOS LSIs?

  1. A(111)
  2. B(100)
  3. C(211)
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. (100)

CMOS LSIs commonly use (100) wafers, because the (100) surface has a low density of interface states at the SiO2 interface, giving good MOS transistor characteristics. (111) wafers were once used for bipolar devices and similar applications.
