Silicon wafers

Which wafer diameter is currently mainstream in leading-edge logic and memory production lines?

  1. A150 mm
  2. B200 mm
  3. C300 mm
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. 300 mm

Leading-edge production lines mainly use 300 mm wafers. A larger diameter yields more chips per wafer, lowering cost per chip. Wafers of 200 mm and smaller are still used to make power semiconductors, analog chips, sensors and more.
