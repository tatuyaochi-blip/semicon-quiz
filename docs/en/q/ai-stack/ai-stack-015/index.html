AI Hardware & Software Stack

A model with about 14 GB of FP16 weights decodes at batch size 1 on a GPU with 2 TB/s memory bandwidth. What is closest to the lower bound on time per generated token?

  1. AAbout 7 µs
  2. BAbout 7 s
  3. CAbout 7 ms
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. About 7 ms

Decode must read all weights for every token, so each token takes at least model size ÷ memory bandwidth: 14 GB ÷ 2 TB/s = 0.007 s = 7 ms, a maximum of about 140 tokens per second. Quantizing weights to 4 bits, about 3.5 GB, lowers this bound to about 1.75 ms. This is why weight quantization is especially effective for decode.

  • A. That is off by a factor of 1000.
  • B. That is off by a factor of 1000.
  • C. Correct. 14 GB ÷ 2 TB/s = 7 ms.
