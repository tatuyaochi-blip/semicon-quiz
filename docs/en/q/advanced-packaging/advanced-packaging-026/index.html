Advanced packaging (3D and chiplets)

To stack chips with TSVs, approximately how thin are wafers ground from the back?

  1. AA few tens of µm
  2. BA few mm
  3. CA few cm
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. A few tens of µm

Dies stacked with TSVs are thinned to a few tens of micrometers to keep via depth manageable and the stack thin. Starting wafers are about 775 µm thick, so this is less than a tenth. Thin wafers crack and warp easily, so they are processed while temporarily bonded to carriers. Millimeters or centimeters would be thicker than the original wafer and defeat the purpose of stacking.
