Advanced packaging (3D and chiplets)

In 2.5D packaging, what is the main advantage of using an interposer made of redistribution layers (RDL) of organic dielectric and copper wiring instead of a silicon interposer?

  1. AIt can always form finer wiring than silicon
  2. BTSVs become unnecessary and no heat dissipation is needed at all
  3. CIt keeps cost down more easily and makes large-area interposers easier to build
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. It keeps cost down more easily and makes large-area interposers easier to build

Organic RDL interposers avoid silicon wafer processing, so they are cheaper, more compliant and easier to scale up in size. Their thermal expansion is closer to the package substrate's, which also reduces joint stress. Their wiring pitch and density usually fall short of silicon interposers, however, so they are chosen per application or combined with silicon bridges only where high density is needed.

  • A. Silicon interposers often have the finer wiring.
  • B. Heat dissipation does not become unnecessary.
  • C. Correct. Low cost and easy scaling up are the advantages.
