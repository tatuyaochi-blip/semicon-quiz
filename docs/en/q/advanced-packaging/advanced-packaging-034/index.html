Advanced packaging (3D and chiplets)

In contrast to chiplets, what is the conventional approach called in which all the functions a system needs are built into one large chip?

  1. ADiscrete
  2. BMonolithic
  3. CHybrid
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. Monolithic

Monolithic means "a single block of stone": all functions are built on one silicon chip. On-chip wiring connects everything, an advantage for performance and power, but yield falls as chips grow larger, and there is also a limit on the maximum area that can be exposed. Chiplets that split functions apart came into use for these reasons.

  • A. Discrete refers to products such as single transistors and diodes.
  • B. Correct. It builds everything into one chip.
  • C. Hybrid is a general word for combining different things.
