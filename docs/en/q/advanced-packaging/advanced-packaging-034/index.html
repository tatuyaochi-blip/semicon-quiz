Advanced packaging (3D and chiplets)

In contrast to chiplets, what is the conventional approach called in which all the functions a system needs are built into one large chip?

  1. ADiscrete
  2. BMonolithic
  3. CHybrid
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. Monolithic

Monolithic, meaning "single stone," refers to building all functions on one silicon chip. On-chip wiring gives advantages in performance and power, but yield falls as dies grow, and die size is capped by the lithography field of about 858 mm². Chiplets that split functions were adopted for these reasons. Discretes are single-device products.

  • A. Discrete refers to products such as single transistors and diodes.
  • B. Correct. It builds everything into one chip.
  • C. Hybrid is a general word for combining different things.
