Advanced packaging (3D and chiplets)

Which is a challenge of moving to chiplets?

  1. AThe area and power of interface circuits connecting chips, and the effort of assembly and testing, increase
  2. BChip yield always falls
  3. CChips from different processes cannot be combined
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. The area and power of interface circuits connecting chips, and the effort of assembly and testing, increase

With chiplets, connections that on-chip wiring handled in a monolithic design must pass through die-to-die interface circuits (PHYs), adding area, power and latency. Advanced packaging assembly, screening of good dies and post-stacking tests are also required. Adoption is therefore advancing in large chips where the benefits outweigh these costs.

  • A. Correct. The burden of interfaces and assembly increases.
  • B. Splitting into smaller chips actually tends to raise yield.
  • C. Being able to combine chips from different processes is one of the advantages.
