Advanced packaging (3D and chiplets)

Which approach, instead of a large silicon interposer, embeds small pieces of silicon only at the chip-to-chip connection areas to achieve high-density wiring?

  1. AWire bonding approach
  2. BSilicon bridge approach
  3. CLead frame approach
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. Silicon bridge approach

In the silicon bridge approach, small pieces of silicon covering only the connection areas between adjacent chips are embedded in the package substrate, and fine wiring on them connects the chips at high density. Because no large interposer is needed, large-area packaging becomes possible while keeping costs down.
