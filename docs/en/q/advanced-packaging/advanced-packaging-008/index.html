Advanced packaging (3D and chiplets)

Which is especially likely to be a challenge in 3D packaging, which stacks chips vertically?

  1. AWiring becomes too long
  2. BChips are too light to secure
  3. CTemperature rise from heat generated by stacked chips, and removing that heat
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. Temperature rise from heat generated by stacked chips, and removing that heat

3D packaging has the advantage of shorter wiring, but because heat-generating chips are stacked, heat tends to build up and is hard to remove from the inner chips. Higher temperatures degrade performance and reliability, so thermal design is an important challenge.
