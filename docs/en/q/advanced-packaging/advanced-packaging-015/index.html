Advanced packaging (3D and chiplets)

Among hybrid bonding methods, which allows only chips judged good in test to be selected and bonded to the target wafer?

  1. AWafer-to-wafer (W2W) bonding
  2. BChip-to-wafer (C2W) bonding
  3. CWire bonding
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. Chip-to-wafer (C2W) bonding

W2W bonding bonds entire wafers at once and is efficient, but defective chips get bonded to each other, and good chips to defective ones. C2W bonding can select and bond only good singulated chips, giving a yield advantage, and chips of different sizes can be combined.
