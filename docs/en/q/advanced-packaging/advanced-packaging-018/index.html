Advanced packaging (3D and chiplets)

In 3D NAND, which is a correct advantage of making the memory cell array and peripheral circuits on separate wafers and bonding them together?

  1. AThe manufacturing process always takes just one step
  2. BMemory cells become unnecessary
  3. CEach can be made with a process suited to it, and chip area can be reduced
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. Each can be made with a process suited to it, and chip area can be reduced

Fabricating the memory array and peripheral CMOS circuits on separate wafers, each with an optimal process, then bonding them places the periphery under the array and shrinks the die. Since array formation involves high-temperature steps, separating them keeps peripheral transistors from degrading thermally, allowing higher performance. The two wafers can also be processed in parallel, shortening manufacturing time.
