Advanced packaging (3D and chiplets)

In 3D NAND, which is a correct advantage of making the memory cell array and peripheral circuits on separate wafers and bonding them together?

  1. AThe manufacturing process always takes just one step
  2. BMemory cells become unnecessary
  3. CEach can be made with a process suited to it, and chip area can be reduced
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. Each can be made with a process suited to it, and chip area can be reduced

Making the memory cell array and peripheral (CMOS) circuits on separate wafers with optimal processes and bonding them together puts the peripheral circuits under the memory cells, reducing chip area. Another advantage is that the peripheral circuits are not affected by the cell array's high-temperature steps.
