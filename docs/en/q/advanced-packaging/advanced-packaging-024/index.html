Advanced packaging (3D and chiplets)

Which correctly describes the typical TSV formation process?

  1. AConnect by threading wires through
  2. BForm it simply by pouring in solder
  3. CEtch a hole by deep etching, form an insulating film and barrier layer, then fill with copper or similar
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. Etch a hole by deep etching, form an insulating film and barrier layer, then fill with copper or similar

A TSV is made by etching a hole in silicon by deep etching (such as the Bosch process), forming an insulating film, barrier layer and seed layer on the inside of the hole, then filling it with copper or similar by plating. The wafer is then thinned from the back to expose the TSV tips.
