Advanced packaging (3D and chiplets)

In HBM, what is the typical number of DRAM dies stacked in one stack?

  1. A1
  2. B2
  3. CAbout 8–16
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. About 8–16

In HBM, DRAM dies connected with TSVs are stacked 8, 12 or even 16 high and combined with a base die (logic die) at the bottom to form one stack. More layers increase capacity, but techniques for thinning dies and countermeasures for heat become necessary.
