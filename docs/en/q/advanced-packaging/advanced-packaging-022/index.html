Advanced packaging (3D and chiplets)

In HBM, what is the typical number of DRAM dies stacked in one stack?

  1. A1
  2. B2
  3. CAbout 8–16
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. About 8–16

HBM stacks 8, 12 or even 16 TSV-connected DRAM dies on a base (logic) die to form one stack. Since stack height is fixed by the standard, adding layers requires thinning dies to around 30 µm and thinning the bond layers, and thermal countermeasures are also essential. One or two dies would forfeit the benefits of stacking.
