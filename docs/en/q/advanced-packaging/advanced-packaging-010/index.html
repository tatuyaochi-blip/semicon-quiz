Advanced packaging (3D and chiplets)

In flip-chip connections, what is the main advantage of using copper pillars (Cu pillars) instead of solder balls?

  1. ABumps can be made larger to increase contact area
  2. BBump pitch can be narrowed, and electrical and thermal conduction are also excellent
  3. CSoldering becomes entirely unnecessary
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. Bump pitch can be narrowed, and electrical and thermal conduction are also excellent

A copper-pillar bump is a copper post tipped with a small amount of solder. Because it does not melt and spread sideways like a solder ball, bump pitch can be tighter, allowing more terminals. It also maintains standoff height between chip and substrate, easing underfill flow. Copper conducts electricity and heat well, benefiting current capacity and electromigration resistance. Soldering is still required.
