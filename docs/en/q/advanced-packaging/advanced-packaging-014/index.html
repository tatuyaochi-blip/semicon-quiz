Advanced packaging (3D and chiplets)

What is the technology called that mounts optical transceiver components close to switch ICs and the like in the same package, shortening electrical wiring to reduce power?

  1. ACo-packaged optics (CPO)
  2. BBGA
  3. CQFP
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. Co-packaged optics (CPO)

As data-center traffic grows, the power spent driving electrical signals from switch ICs to front-panel optical modules has become a problem. Co-packaged optics place optical transceivers in the same package as the switch IC, shortening the electrical path to a few millimeters and reducing power and signal degradation. It is one form of optoelectronic integration. BGA and QFP are common package types.
