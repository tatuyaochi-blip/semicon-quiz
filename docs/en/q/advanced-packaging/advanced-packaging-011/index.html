Advanced packaging (3D and chiplets)

What is the wiring layer called that is formed on a chip or wafer surface to route electrode positions to the positions of external terminals?

  1. ARedistribution layer (RDL)
  2. BSTI
  3. CBuried oxide (BOX layer)
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. Redistribution layer (RDL)

A redistribution layer (RDL) routes wiring from a chip's pads to wherever external terminals such as solder balls should go, built from alternating dielectric layers such as polyimide and copper traces. It is used in wafer-level packages, fan-out packages and interposers, with line widths shrinking from several micrometers toward about 1 µm. STI is device isolation and the BOX layer is the oxide in SOI.
