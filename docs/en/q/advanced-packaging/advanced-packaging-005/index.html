Advanced packaging (3D and chiplets)

Which correctly describes hybrid bonding?

  1. AA technology that enlarges solder bumps to improve connection reliability
  2. BA technology that connects using two wires at a time
  3. CA technology that directly bonds surfaces of copper electrodes and dielectric without bumps, enabling extremely dense connections
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. A technology that directly bonds surfaces of copper electrodes and dielectric without bumps, enabling extremely dense connections

Hybrid bonding planarizes chip or wafer surfaces and directly bonds copper electrodes to each other and dielectric to dielectric at the same time. Because no solder bumps are used, connection pitch can be reduced to a few µm, and it is used in stacked image sensors and advanced chip stacking.
