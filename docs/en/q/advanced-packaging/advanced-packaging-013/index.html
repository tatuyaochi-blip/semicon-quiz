Advanced packaging (3D and chiplets)

Which is a correct advantage of glass substrates, being considered in place of organic materials for next-generation large package substrates?

  1. AThey conduct electricity well
  2. BThey are soft and easy to bend
  3. CThey have high flatness and dimensional stability, making fine wiring easier to form
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. They have high flatness and dimensional stability, making fine wiring easier to form

Glass has a flat surface, little dimensional change or warpage with temperature, and a thermal expansion coefficient that can be tuned close to silicon's, so it is expected to support fine wiring and dense through-glass vias (TGVs) even in large package substrates. Since organic substrates warp more as they grow, glass is being considered for large AI packages. Glass is an insulator and is rigid, not flexible.
