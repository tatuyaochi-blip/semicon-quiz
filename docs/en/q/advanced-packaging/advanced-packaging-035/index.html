Advanced packaging (3D and chiplets)

Advanced packaging steps that use front-end-like fine processing at the wafer level, such as forming TSVs and fine redistribution layers, sit between the front end and back end. What are they called?

  1. AMiddle end (mid-end)
  2. BUpstream process
  3. CFinal process
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. Middle end (mid-end)

Advanced packaging performs TSV etching and filling, fine redistribution, bumping and thinning at the wafer level using equipment and techniques close to front-end processing. Because these steps fit neither the traditional front end (circuit formation) nor back end (assembly and test), they are sometimes called the middle end. Both foundries and OSATs are investing in this area, and their division of roles is shifting.

  • A. Correct. These are the steps between the front end and back end.
  • B. Upstream process is a term used in software development and the like for requirements definition and design.
  • C. Final process is not a common category.
