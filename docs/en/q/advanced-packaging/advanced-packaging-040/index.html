Advanced packaging (3D and chiplets)

Through the HBM3 generation, which is the data bus width per HBM stack?

  1. A1024 bits
  2. B8 bits
  3. C64 bits
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. 1024 bits

Whereas a typical DDR DRAM module has a 64-bit data bus, HBM has an extremely wide 1,024-bit bus per stack, achieving high bandwidth even with per-pin speeds lower than DDR. Gaining bandwidth through width rather than speed also lowers energy per bit. So many wires cannot be routed on an ordinary substrate, requiring dense wiring such as a silicon interposer. The next generation, HBM4, widens the bus to 2,048 bits.

  • A. Correct. A very wide bus yields high bandwidth.
  • B. 8 bits is a very narrow bus, the opposite of HBM's character.
  • C. 64 bits is the bus width of a typical DDR module.
