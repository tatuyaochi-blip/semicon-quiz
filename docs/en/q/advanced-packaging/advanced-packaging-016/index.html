Advanced packaging (3D and chiplets)

What are the bumps called, far smaller than ordinary solder balls, used to connect chips stacked with TSVs and to connect chips to interposers?

  1. AMicrobumps
  2. BBGA solder balls
  3. CLeads
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. Microbumps

Microbumps are tiny bumps 10 to several tens of micrometers in diameter at pitches of tens of micrometers, used for the many connections between dies. They join HBM DRAM dies in stacks and chips to interposers, often by thermocompression bonding. BGA solder balls, by contrast, have pitches of about 0.4–1 mm. When even finer pitch is needed, hybrid bonding is used.
