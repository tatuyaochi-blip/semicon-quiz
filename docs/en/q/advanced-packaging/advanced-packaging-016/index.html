Advanced packaging (3D and chiplets)

What are the bumps called, far smaller than ordinary solder balls, used to connect chips stacked with TSVs and to connect chips to interposers?

  1. AMicrobumps
  2. BBGA solder balls
  3. CLeads
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. Microbumps

Microbumps are very small bumps arranged at pitches of a few tens of µm, used for the many connections between chips. They are used to stack HBM DRAM dies and to connect chips on interposers. When an even finer pitch is needed, hybrid bonding is used.
