Advanced packaging (3D and chiplets)

Which TSMC fan-out wafer-level packaging technology became widely known when it was adopted for a smartphone processor in 2016?

  1. AInFO (Integrated Fan-Out)
  2. BCoWoS
  3. CHBM
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. InFO (Integrated Fan-Out)

InFO is a fan-out packaging technology that forms redistribution layers on a reconstituted wafer with dies embedded in mold compound, making packages thin and small without a substrate. Its adoption in 2016 for Apple's smartphone processors, in a PoP configuration with memory stacked on top, triggered the widespread adoption of fan-out advanced packaging. CoWoS is 2.5D packaging with an interposer, and HBM is stacked memory.

  • A. Correct. It is TSMC's fan-out packaging technology.
  • B. CoWoS is a 2.5D packaging technology using a silicon interposer.
  • C. HBM is stacked memory.
