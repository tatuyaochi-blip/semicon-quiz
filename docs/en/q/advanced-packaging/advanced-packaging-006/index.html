Advanced packaging (3D and chiplets)

Which correctly describes a feature of fan-out wafer-level packaging (FOWLP)?

  1. ARedistribution layers extend beyond the chip edges, so external terminals can be placed over an area larger than the chip
  2. BConnections are made only by wire bonding
  3. CThe package is always smaller than the chip
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. Redistribution layers extend beyond the chip edges, so external terminals can be placed over an area larger than the chip

FOWLP embeds singulated dies in mold compound to form a reconstituted wafer, then builds redistribution layers that extend beyond the die edges. Terminals can spread over an area larger than the die, so even small dies get many terminals, and without a package substrate the result is thin. It is used for smartphone processors and similar chips. Connections use redistribution layers rather than wires, and the package is larger than the die.
