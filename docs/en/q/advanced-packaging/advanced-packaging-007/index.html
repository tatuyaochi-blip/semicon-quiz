Advanced packaging (3D and chiplets)

Which industry-standard die-to-die interface aims to let chiplets from different companies connect to each other?

  1. AHDMI
  2. BUCIe
  3. CBluetooth
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. UCIe

UCIe (Universal Chiplet Interconnect Express) is a standard for connecting chiplets within a package. A common standard makes it easier to combine chiplets made by different companies and on different processes, and is expected to broaden the use of chiplets.
