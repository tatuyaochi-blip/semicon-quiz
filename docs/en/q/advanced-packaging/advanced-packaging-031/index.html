Advanced packaging (3D and chiplets)

Which is a basic advantage of 3D packaging, which stacks chips vertically?

  1. AInterconnects between chips become shorter, enabling higher speed, lower power and a smaller footprint
  2. BChips cool more easily
  3. CThe manufacturing process becomes simpler and cost always falls
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. Interconnects between chips become shorter, enabling higher speed, lower power and a smaller footprint

Compared with placing chips side by side and connecting them through package substrate wiring, stacking them vertically makes the distance between chips very short. Signal delay and the power of charging and discharging wires shrink, many connections become easier to provide, and the footprint becomes smaller. On the other hand, heat tends to build up and processing becomes more complex.

  • A. Correct. Shorter interconnects are the biggest advantage.
  • B. Stacking makes heat build up more easily.
  • C. Processing becomes more complex, and cost does not necessarily fall.
