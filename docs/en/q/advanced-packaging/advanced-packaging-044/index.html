Advanced packaging (3D and chiplets)

What is the main aim of products that stack a cache SRAM chip on top of a processor chip with hybrid bonding?

  1. ATo change the chip's color
  2. BTo boost performance by placing a large cache close by without enlarging the processor
  3. CTo raise the processor's supply voltage
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. To boost performance by placing a large cache close by without enlarging the processor

A larger cache reduces memory accesses and boosts performance, but SRAM consumes a lot of area and no longer shrinks well with scaling. Stacking an SRAM die directly atop the processor adds several times the cache capacity over very short distances without enlarging the processor die. It improves performance in cache-sensitive workloads such as gaming. It has nothing to do with color or supply voltage.

  • A. It is not for changing color.
  • B. Correct. Stacking puts a large cache close by.
  • C. It is not for raising the supply voltage.
