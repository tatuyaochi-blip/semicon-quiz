Advanced packaging (3D and chiplets)

What is the concept called that integrates chips made with different manufacturing processes and materials into one package?

  1. AMonolithic integration
  2. BHeterogeneous integration
  3. CDiscrete mounting
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. Heterogeneous integration

Heterogeneous integration combines chips made with different processes and materials, such as leading-edge logic, DRAM, analog, RF and photonic devices, into a single system using advanced packaging. Combinations that cannot be built optimally on the same wafer, such as DRAM and logic, can be brought together. It is attracting attention as a way to overcome the limits of monolithic integration, which builds everything on one chip.
