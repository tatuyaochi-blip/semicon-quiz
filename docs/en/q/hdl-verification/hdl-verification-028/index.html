HDL & Verification

Which set of values makes up the four-state logic used in Verilog simulation?

  1. A0, 1, X (unknown) and Z (high impedance)
  2. B0, 1, 2 and 3
  3. CTrue, false, up and down
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. 0, 1, X (unknown) and Z (high impedance)

Besides 0 and 1, Verilog signals can be X (unknown) and Z (high impedance, undriven). Uninitialized registers and signals with conflicting drivers become X, while unconnected wires and tri-state lines whose drivers are disabled become Z. Spotting X or Z in simulation is a major clue to finding bugs.

  • A. Correct. These represent real circuit conditions.
  • B. They are not the numbers 0 to 3.
  • C. Those are not logic values.
