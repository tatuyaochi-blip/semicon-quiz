Process materials (gases and chemicals)

Which gas is used as the germanium source in SiGe epitaxial growth?

  1. AAmmonia (NH3)
  2. BGermane (GeH4)
  3. CArsine (AsH3)
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. Germane (GeH4)

SiGe growth flows germane (GeH4) as the germanium source together with a silicon source such as silane or dichlorosilane. The ratio of their flows controls the Ge fraction. Germane is also a hydride and is handled carefully because it is toxic and flammable.

  • A. Ammonia is a nitrogen source used for nitride films and the like.
  • B. Correct. Germane is the germanium source.
  • C. Arsine is an arsenic source used for n-type doping and the like.
