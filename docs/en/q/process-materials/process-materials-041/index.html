Process materials (gases and chemicals)

Which gas is used as the germanium source in SiGe epitaxial growth?

  1. AAmmonia (NH3)
  2. BGermane (GeH4)
  3. CArsine (AsH3)
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. Germane (GeH4)

SiGe is grown by flowing germane (GeH4) as the germanium source together with a silicon source such as silane or dichlorosilane, with the flow ratio controlling Ge content. For pMOS source/drain, diborane is added to grow heavily boron-doped SiGe that compresses the channel. Ammonia is a nitrogen source and arsine an arsenic source.

  • A. Ammonia is a nitrogen source used for nitride films and the like.
  • B. Correct. Germane is the germanium source.
  • C. Arsine is an arsenic source used for n-type doping and the like.
