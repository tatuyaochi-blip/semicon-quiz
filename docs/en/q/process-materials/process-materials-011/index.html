Process materials (gases and chemicals)

Which source gases are used to introduce the p-type dopant boron?

  1. ADiborane (B2H6) and boron trifluoride (BF3)
  2. BPhosphine (PH3)
  3. CArsine (AsH3)
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. Diborane (B2H6) and boron trifluoride (BF3)

Boron is introduced using diborane (B2H6) or boron trifluoride (BF3). BF3 is used as an ion-source gas for implantation, yielding B⁺ as well as heavier BF2⁺ for shallow implants. Diborane is commonly used to dope films in situ during CVD or epitaxial growth. Phosphine and arsine are sources of the n-type dopants phosphorus and arsenic.
