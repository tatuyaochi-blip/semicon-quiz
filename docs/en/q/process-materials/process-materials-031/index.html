Process materials (gases and chemicals)

Which set correctly lists the main kinds of process materials used in semiconductor manufacturing?

  1. AWafers, gases, wet chemicals, photoresist, sputtering targets, CMP slurries and so on
  2. BWood, paper and cloth
  3. CGasoline, diesel and kerosene
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. Wafers, gases, wet chemicals, photoresist, sputtering targets, CMP slurries and so on

Semiconductor manufacturing uses hundreds of high-purity materials: wafers as substrates, gases for deposition and etching, chemicals for cleaning and etching, photoresists for exposure, sputtering targets, and CMP slurries and pads. Back-end processes add lead frames, bonding wires, molding compounds and package substrates. A shortage of any one can halt production, so stable material supply is an industry-wide concern.

  • A. Correct. Wafers, gases, chemicals, resist, targets, slurries and so on.
  • B. Wood, paper and cloth are not semiconductor process materials.
  • C. Fuels are not process materials.
