Process materials (gases and chemicals)

Which high-purity, heat-resistant material is widely used for the process tubes of furnaces and the fixtures that hold wafers?

  1. APolyethylene
  2. BQuartz glass
  3. CCopper
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. Quartz glass

Fused quartz is high in purity, softens only around 1,600 °C, and has a very small thermal expansion coefficient (about 0.5×10⁻⁶ /K), so it resists cracking under rapid temperature changes and withstands most chemicals. It is used for furnace tubes, wafer boats and CZ crucibles. SiC is used where higher-temperature strength or longer life is needed. Polyethylene lacks heat resistance, and copper causes metal contamination.
