Process materials (gases and chemicals)

Which combination of gases is used to make an ArF excimer laser oscillate?

  1. AOxygen and nitrogen
  2. BOnly hydrogen and helium
  3. CArgon and fluorine (with a buffer gas such as neon)
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. Argon and fluorine (with a buffer gas such as neon)

An ArF excimer laser excites a gas mixture containing argon and fluorine with an electrical discharge, emitting light at 193 nm. Most of the gas is a buffer gas such as neon. A KrF excimer laser (248 nm) uses krypton and fluorine.
