Process materials (gases and chemicals)

Which correctly describes the target used in sputtering?

  1. AA plate made of the material to be deposited, from which atoms are ejected by ion bombardment
  2. BAn electrostatic chuck for holding the wafer
  3. CA photomask used for exposure
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. A plate made of the material to be deposited, from which atoms are ejected by ion bombardment

A target is a plate of the material to be deposited, such as Ti, Ta, Cu or Al. Argon ions from the plasma strike it and knock out surface atoms, which deposit on the wafer as a film. Magnets behind the target confine the plasma (magnetron sputtering) to raise the deposition rate. Impurities and uneven grains show up directly in the film, so targets need high purity and uniform microstructure. It is unrelated to electrostatic chucks or photomasks.
