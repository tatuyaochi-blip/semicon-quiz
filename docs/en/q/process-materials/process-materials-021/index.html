Process materials (gases and chemicals)

Which aqueous solution is commonly used to develop photoresist?

  1. ATMAH (tetramethylammonium hydroxide) solution
  2. BHydrochloric acid
  3. CHydrofluoric acid
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. TMAH (tetramethylammonium hydroxide) solution

Positive resists are developed in 2.38% aqueous TMAH (about 0.26 N), an industry standard. Areas made alkali-soluble by exposure dissolve, revealing the pattern. A common concentration makes development rates reproducible across fabs. Hydrochloric and hydrofluoric acids are acids and do not develop resist. In negative-tone development, an organic solvent dissolves the unexposed areas instead.
