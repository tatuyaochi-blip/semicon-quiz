Process materials (gases and chemicals)

Which measure is commonly used to express trace moisture in high-purity gases?

  1. ApH
  2. BSpecific gravity
  3. CDew point (the temperature at which moisture begins to condense)
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. Dew point (the temperature at which moisture begins to condense)

Moisture in gas is often expressed as the dew point, the temperature at which water begins to condense on cooling. Lower dew point means less water: about −80 °C corresponds to roughly 0.5 ppm and −100 °C to about 14 ppb. Moisture causes line corrosion and film degradation, so purifiers hold it to ppb levels or below. pH measures acidity of liquids and specific gravity is a density ratio; neither indicates moisture.

  • A. pH measures the acidity or alkalinity of liquids.
  • B. Specific gravity is a density ratio, not a moisture measure.
  • C. Correct. Moisture is expressed by the dew point.
