Process materials (gases and chemicals)

Which source gas can deposit silicon films at lower temperatures than monosilane (SiH4)?

  1. ADisilane (Si2H6)
  2. BCarbon tetrafluoride (CF4)
  3. CNitrogen (N2)
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. Disilane (Si2H6)

Disilane (Si2H6) has a Si–Si bond that breaks more easily than silane's bonds, so it decomposes readily and deposits silicon films at lower temperatures. In low-temperature epitaxy it limits dopant diffusion, enabling abrupt profiles. Trisilane (Si3H8), which decomposes even more easily, is also used. CF4 is an etch gas and nitrogen is inert; neither can supply silicon.

  • A. Correct. It decomposes more readily than monosilane and deposits at low temperature.
  • B. CF4 is a gas for etching and the like, not a silicon source.
  • C. Nitrogen contains no silicon and cannot be a source.
