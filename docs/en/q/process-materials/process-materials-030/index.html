Process materials (gases and chemicals)

What is the chemical name of IPA, used for drying after cleaning and similar purposes?

  1. AMethanol
  2. BEthanol
  3. CIsopropyl alcohol (2-propanol)
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. Isopropyl alcohol (2-propanol)

IPA stands for isopropyl alcohol (2-propanol). It mixes readily with water and has low surface tension, so displacing rinse water with IPA before drying prevents pattern collapse and water marks. IPA vapor is also used in Marangoni drying, where a surface-tension gradient pulls water off the wafer. It is distinct from methanol and ethanol, and because it is flammable, static control and ventilation are required.
