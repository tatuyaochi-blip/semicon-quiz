Process materials (gases and chemicals)

Which device in deposition and etch tools precisely measures gas flow and controls it to stay constant?

  1. AMass flow controller (MFC)
  2. BA pressure gauge alone
  3. CThe gas cylinder valve
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. Mass flow controller (MFC)

A mass flow controller measures the mass flow of a gas with a sensor and adjusts an internal valve to hold the setpoint. The common thermal type derives flow from the heat carried by gas through a small tube. Flow is often expressed in sccm (standard cubic centimeters per minute). Deposition rate, film composition and etch selectivity depend strongly on flow, so each gas line has its own MFC.

  • A. Correct. It measures and controls flow.
  • B. A pressure gauge only measures pressure and does not control flow.
  • C. A cylinder valve alone cannot control flow precisely.
