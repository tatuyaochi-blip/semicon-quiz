Process materials (gases and chemicals)

What is the main reason extremely high purity is required of the gases and chemicals used in semiconductor manufacturing?

  1. ATo remove odors
  2. BBecause even trace impurities such as metals and particles degrade device characteristics and yield
  3. CTo raise prices
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. Because even trace impurities such as metals and particles degrade device characteristics and yield

Trace metals in gases and chemicals that enter silicon increase leakage current and lower carrier lifetime, and particles become pattern defects. High-purity materials with impurities controlled at the ppb or ppt level are therefore used.

  • A. Removing odors is not the purpose.
  • B. Correct. Even trace impurities degrade characteristics and yield.
  • C. Raising prices is not the purpose.
