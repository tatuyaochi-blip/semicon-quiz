Process materials (gases and chemicals)

Which gas is used as a carrier gas and reducing atmosphere in epitaxial growth and heat treatment?

  1. AOzone
  2. BHydrogen (H2)
  3. CChlorine
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. Hydrogen (H2)

Hydrogen serves as the carrier gas for epitaxial precursors and as a reducing ambient for removing native oxide at high temperature before growth. Annealing in forming gas, a few percent hydrogen in nitrogen, terminates dangling bonds at the Si/SiO2 interface and reduces interface traps. Above about 4% in air it becomes explosive, so it is used under leak monitoring. Ozone and chlorine are oxidizing gases.
