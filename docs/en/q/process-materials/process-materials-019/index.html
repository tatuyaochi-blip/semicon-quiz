Process materials (gases and chemicals)

Which is a typical material used for low-k interlayer dielectrics?

  1. ACarbon-containing silicon oxide (SiOC) films
  2. BCopper
  3. CTitanium nitride
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. Carbon-containing silicon oxide (SiOC) films

SiOC films, in which carbon or methyl groups are incorporated into silicon oxide to reduce polarizability, are widely used as low-k dielectrics, with permittivity around 2.7–3.0 versus about 3.9 for SiO2. Most are deposited by PECVD. Porous films with fine voids reach 2.5 or below but lose mechanical strength and are more easily damaged by plasma. Copper is a wiring metal, and titanium nitride is a barrier or hard-mask material.
