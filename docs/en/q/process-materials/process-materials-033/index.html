Process materials (gases and chemicals)

For what purpose is ultrapure water used in the largest quantities in semiconductor fabs?

  1. ADrinking water for workers
  2. BOnly humidification for air conditioning
  3. CWafer cleaning and rinsing
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. Wafer cleaning and rinsing

Ultrapure water is used in vast quantities to clean wafers at every stage, especially for rinsing after chemical cleans. Large fabs can use tens of thousands of tonnes a day, so reclaiming rinse water and reprocessing it into UPW is important. To remove ions, organics, particles and dissolved gases to the limit, UPW is made by combining reverse osmosis, ion exchange, UV oxidation and ultrafiltration.

  • A. Drinking water is managed separately.
  • B. It is not used only for humidification.
  • C. Correct. It is used in large quantities for cleaning and rinsing.
