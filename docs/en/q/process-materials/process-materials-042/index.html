Process materials (gases and chemicals)

Which gas is sometimes used as the oxidant with silane to deposit silicon oxide or oxynitride films by CVD?

  1. AArgon (Ar)
  2. BHelium (He)
  3. CNitrous oxide (N2O)
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. Nitrous oxide (N2O)

Nitrous oxide (N2O) serves as the oxidant reacting with silane to form silicon oxide in PECVD, allowing deposition at around 300–400 °C. It reacts more gently than oxygen, reducing gas-phase particle formation, and under some conditions it incorporates nitrogen to form oxynitride (SiON). Because SiON's refractive index can be tuned, it is also used as an antireflective layer for lithography. Argon and helium are inert.

  • A. Argon is inert and is not an oxidant.
  • B. Helium is inert and is not an oxidant.
  • C. Correct. It is used as an oxidant with silane.
