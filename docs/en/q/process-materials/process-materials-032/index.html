Process materials (gases and chemicals)

What is the appropriate general way to store photoresist?

  1. AKeep it in a bright, sunny place
  2. BKeep it in a light-tight container at the specified temperature (such as refrigerated) and use it before its expiration date
  3. CStore it open at room temperature
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. Keep it in a light-tight container at the specified temperature (such as refrigerated) and use it before its expiration date

Photoresist is photosensitive and reacts to light and heat, so it is stored in light-tight containers at the specified temperature, often refrigerated, and used before expiry. A refrigerated bottle must warm to room temperature before opening, or condensed moisture will alter its performance. It is filtered through fine filters right before use. Fabs use yellow-lit rooms for the same reason: to avoid the short wavelengths resists respond to.

  • A. Light reacts with the resist and degrades it.
  • B. Correct. It is kept dark at the specified temperature.
  • C. Leaving it open lets solvent evaporate and contaminants enter.
