Process materials (gases and chemicals)

Which correctly describes chlorine trifluoride (ClF3), used for chamber cleaning and similar purposes?

  1. AIt is inert and reacts with almost nothing
  2. BIt is extremely reactive, reacting violently with many substances, and requires strict handling
  3. CIt is harmless and needs no ventilation
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. It is extremely reactive, reacting violently with many substances, and requires strict handling

ClF3 is so reactive that it etches many materials without plasma, so it is used for in-situ cleaning of deposits inside chambers such as epitaxial reactors. On the other hand, it can react violently and ignite with organics and many metals, and it is highly toxic, so materials selection and leak prevention are strict. It is neither inert nor harmless; its reactivity is the reason for both its usefulness and its danger.
