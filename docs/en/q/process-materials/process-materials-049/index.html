Process materials (gases and chemicals)

What is the operation called in which a gas line is repeatedly pressurized with inert gas and evacuated before it is opened or switched to another gas?

  1. ACycle purging
  2. BDry etching
  3. CAnnealing
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. Cycle purging

Cycle purging repeatedly pressurizes a line with inert gas such as nitrogen and then evacuates or vents it, diluting and expelling residual gas and moisture. Each cycle cuts the concentration by a fixed fraction; if one cycle gives a tenfold reduction, five cycles reach one part in 100,000. It is performed, for example, when changing toxic-gas cylinders, to ensure both safety and purity.

  • A. Correct. Repeated pressurizing and venting drives out residual gas.
  • B. Dry etching removes films.
  • C. Annealing is a heat-treatment step.
