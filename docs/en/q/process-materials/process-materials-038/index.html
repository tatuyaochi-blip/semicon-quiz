Process materials (gases and chemicals)

Which material is widely used for piping and containers of highly corrosive chemicals such as hydrofluoric acid?

  1. AFluoropolymers such as PFA
  2. BAluminum
  3. COrdinary glass
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. Fluoropolymers such as PFA

Fluoropolymers such as PFA and PTFE, thanks to the strong carbon–fluorine bond, resist nearly all chemicals including hydrofluoric acid and leach extremely few impurities. They are therefore widely used for high-purity chemical piping, fittings, containers and valves. PFA can be melt-processed, making it suitable for complex parts. Glass dissolves in HF, and aluminum is attacked by acids and alkalis.

  • A. Correct. Fluoropolymers resist chemicals and release few impurities.
  • B. Aluminum is attacked by acids and alkalis.
  • C. Glass dissolves in hydrofluoric acid.
