Process materials (gases and chemicals)

In ArF immersion lithography, which film is applied over the photoresist to keep the immersion water from contacting it directly?

  1. ABARC (bottom anti-reflective coating)
  2. BPellicle
  3. CTop coat
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. Top coat

In immersion lithography the water between lens and wafer touches the resist, so components such as the photoacid generator can leach out and contaminate the lens, or water can soak in and cause defects. A topcoat is a protective film over the resist that prevents this and keeps the surface hydrophobic so no water is left behind during fast stage motion. BARC is an antireflective layer under the resist, and a pellicle is a thin membrane protecting the mask.
