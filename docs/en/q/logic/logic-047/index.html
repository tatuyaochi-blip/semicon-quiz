Logic circuits

When adding two's complement numbers, which condition indicates that an overflow has occurred?

  1. AWhen the carry into the most significant bit differs from the carry out of the most significant bit
  2. BWhen the result is 0
  3. CWhen the sum of the least significant bits is 1
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. When the carry into the most significant bit differs from the carry out of the most significant bit

In two's-complement addition, overflow occurs when two positives yield a negative or two negatives yield a positive, detected when the carry into the top bit differs from the carry out of it (their XOR is 1). For example, in 4 bits 0111 (7) + 0001 (1) = 1000 (−8). Adding numbers of opposite sign can never overflow. CPUs report this as the V flag, distinct from the C flag for unsigned carry-out.

  • A. Correct. Overflow is when the carries into and out of the MSB differ.
  • B. A result of 0 does not indicate overflow.
  • C. The least significant bit is irrelevant to overflow detection.
