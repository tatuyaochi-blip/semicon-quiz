Logic circuits

In a synchronous circuit, what determines the maximum clock frequency at which it can operate?

  1. AThe delay of the fastest path
  2. BOnly the supply voltage
  3. CThe delay of the slowest path between flip-flops (the critical path)
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. The delay of the slowest path between flip-flops (the critical path)

The clock period must be at least the clock-to-Q delay plus the path's logic delay plus setup time plus margins such as skew, so the slowest path (the critical path) sets the maximum frequency; a 1 ns total allows at most 1 GHz. Speed-up techniques include pipelining to split paths, reducing logic depth, and using stronger or low-Vth cells on slow paths.
