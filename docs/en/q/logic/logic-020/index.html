Logic circuits

When is the output of a three-input majority circuit 1?

  1. AOnly when all three inputs are 1
  2. BWhen two or more of the three inputs are 1
  3. CWhen any input is 1
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. When two or more of the three inputs are 1

A majority circuit outputs 1 when more than half of its inputs are 1. With three inputs, that means two or more. Its logic expression is AB + BC + CA, the same as the carry output of a full adder. It is also used in redundant systems to mask the effect of a failure by majority vote.
