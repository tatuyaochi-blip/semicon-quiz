Logic circuits

When is the output of a three-input majority circuit 1?

  1. AOnly when all three inputs are 1
  2. BWhen two or more of the three inputs are 1
  3. CWhen any input is 1
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. When two or more of the three inputs are 1

A majority circuit outputs 1 when more than half its inputs are 1—for three inputs, when two or more are 1. Its expression AB + BC + CA is the same as a full adder's carry output. Triple modular redundancy (TMR), which votes among three copies of a circuit, keeps the output correct even if one copy fails or suffers a radiation-induced bit flip, and is used in aerospace equipment and high-reliability servers.
