Which correctly describes clock skew in a synchronous circuit?

  1. AVariation of the clock frequency over time
  2. BThe difference in the time the clock signal arrives at different flip-flops
  3. CThe clock signal voltage exceeding the supply voltage
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. The difference in the time the clock signal arrives at different flip-flops

Clock skew is the difference in arrival time of the same clock at different flip-flops, caused by differences in wire length and load. Large skew can cause setup and hold violations, so clock trees are designed to equalize arrival times.

  • A. Time-varying fluctuation of the clock period is called jitter.
  • B. Correct. It is the difference in clock arrival time between locations.
  • C. Clock skew is not a voltage problem.
