What is the drawback of a ripple-carry adder made by chaining full adders?

  1. AIt cannot handle negative numbers
  2. BBecause the carry propagates from the lower bits in sequence, the delay grows in proportion to the number of bits
  3. CThe circuit size does not change even as the number of bits increases
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. Because the carry propagates from the lower bits in sequence, the delay grows in proportion to the number of bits

In a ripple-carry adder, each full adder waits for the carry from below, so the carry ripples from the least to the most significant bit and delay grows linearly with width. At 64 bits that is 64 full-adder delays, too slow for GHz CPUs. Being small and simple, it is used where speed does not matter. Faster designs use carry-lookahead, carry-select and parallel-prefix (e.g., Kogge-Stone) adders.

  • A. With two's complement, negative numbers can be handled by the same circuit.
  • B. Correct. Carry propagation makes the delay proportional to the number of bits.
  • C. More bits means more full adders, so the circuit grows.
