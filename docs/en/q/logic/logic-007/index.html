Logic circuits

In a 1-bit half adder, which logic produces the sum (S) and carry (C)?

  1. ASum is AND, carry is OR
  2. BSum is NOR, carry is NAND
  3. CSum is XOR, carry is AND
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. Sum is XOR, carry is AND

Adding one-bit A and B gives sum = A XOR B and carry = A AND B; for example, 1 + 1 = binary 10, so the sum is 0 and the carry is 1. A full adder, which also adds a carry-in, can be built from two half adders and an OR gate, with sum = A⊕B⊕Cin and carry = AB + Cin(A⊕B). CPU arithmetic units (ALUs) are built on full adders combined with fast carry schemes.
