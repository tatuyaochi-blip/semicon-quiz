Logic circuits

A T flip-flop whose output toggles on every clock edge produces an output at what multiple of the clock frequency?

  1. A2 times
  2. B1/2
  3. C1/4
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. 1/2

A T flip-flop (with T = 1) toggles on every rising clock edge, so its output completes one cycle every two clocks, giving half the clock frequency. Chaining them gives dividers of 1/4, 1/8 and so on, forming a binary counter.

  • A. A flip-flop cannot double the frequency.
  • B. Correct. One period takes two clocks, so the frequency is halved.
  • C. Dividing by 4 requires two T flip-flops in series.
