Logic circuits

What is an advantage of a synchronous circuit, in which all flip-flops operate on a common clock?

  1. APower consumption is always zero
  2. BTiming is easy to design and verify
  3. CNo clock is needed
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. Timing is easy to design and verify

In a synchronous circuit, data is updated together at clock edges, so correct operation is ensured by checking that the delay between flip-flops fits within the clock period. This is easy to verify with tools such as static timing analysis, and almost all large digital circuits are designed synchronously.
