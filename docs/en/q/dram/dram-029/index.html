DRAM

Which function, adopted in DDR5, corrects errors inside the DRAM chip?

  1. AOn-die ECC
  2. BWear leveling
  3. CTRIM
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. On-die ECC

To cope with more cell defects and errors due to scaling, DDR5 builds an error-correction circuit into the DRAM chip itself (on-die ECC). Separate from system-level ECC, it corrects single-bit errors and similar errors inside the chip.
