DRAM

As DRAM capacity grows, what tends to become a problem with respect to refresh?

  1. AMore rows must be refreshed, so a larger share of time and power goes to refresh
  2. BThe refresh period must be lengthened
  3. CRefresh becomes unnecessary
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. More rows must be refreshed, so a larger share of time and power goes to refresh

As capacity increases, more cells must be refreshed within the same time. This increases both the time during which normal access is unavailable and the power spent on refresh, affecting performance and power consumption. Techniques such as per-bank refresh have been introduced to mitigate this.

  • A. Correct. The refresh burden increases.
  • B. Retention time does not get longer with capacity, so the period does not need to lengthen.
  • C. As long as it is DRAM, refresh is required.
