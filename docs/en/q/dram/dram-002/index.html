DRAM

Why does DRAM need periodic refresh operations?

  1. ATo increase read speed
  2. BBecause the number of writes is limited
  3. CBecause the charge stored in the capacitor is gradually lost through leakage current
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. Because the charge stored in the capacitor is gradually lost through leakage current

The charge stored in a DRAM capacitor is gradually lost through leakage currents in the transistor and junctions. Refresh reads and rewrites the data at regular intervals (generally within tens of milliseconds) before it is lost. A limited number of writes is a characteristic of flash memory.
