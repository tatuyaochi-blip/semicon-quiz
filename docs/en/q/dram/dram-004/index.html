DRAM

Which pair of three-dimensional structures has been used in DRAM cells to secure capacitor capacitance?

  1. AFin and planar
  2. BTrench and stacked
  3. CFloating gate and charge trap
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. Trench and stacked

To secure enough capacitance as cells shrink, DRAM capacitors have been made three-dimensional. There are trench capacitors, formed by etching deep holes into the substrate, and stacked capacitors, built as tall structures above the transistor; stacked capacitors are now mainstream. Floating gate and charge trap are flash memory structures.
