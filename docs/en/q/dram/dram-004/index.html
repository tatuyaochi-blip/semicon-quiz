DRAM

Which pair of three-dimensional structures has been used in DRAM cells to secure capacitor capacitance?

  1. AFin and planar
  2. BTrench and stacked
  3. CFloating gate and charge trap
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. Trench and stacked

To keep around 10 fF of capacitance as cells shrink, DRAM has made its capacitors three-dimensional: trench capacitors dug deep into the substrate, and stacked capacitors built as tall cylinders above the transistor. Stacked capacitors now dominate. In advanced parts they are tens of nm wide yet over 1 µm tall—aspect ratios of several tens—and supporting films keep them from toppling. Floating gates and charge traps are flash structures.
