DRAM

How does the data retention time of DRAM cells generally change as temperature rises?

  1. AIt gets longer
  2. BIt does not change
  3. CIt gets shorter
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. It gets shorter

Higher temperature increases junction and subthreshold leakage, so charge drains faster and retention time shrinks—roughly halving for every 10 °C rise as a rule of thumb. DDR standards therefore halve the refresh interval above 85 °C, and some devices adjust refresh frequency using their on-die temperature sensors. Memory cooling is important in servers.
