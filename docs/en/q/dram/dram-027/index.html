What does F mean in '6F²', used to express DRAM cell area?

  1. AFarad (the unit of capacitance)
  2. BThe number of fins
  3. CThe minimum feature size
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. The minimum feature size

F denotes the minimum feature size, and cell area is expressed as a multiple of F². If word lines and bit lines each have a 2F pitch, the smallest cell at their crossing is 4F². DRAM cells have improved from 8F² to 6F², and 4F² is being pursued with vertical transistors. This notation lets cell-design efficiency be compared across generations and manufacturers regardless of feature size.
