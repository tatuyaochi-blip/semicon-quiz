DRAM

In DDR-family DRAM, which feature places termination resistors inside the DRAM chip, with controllable on/off, to suppress signal reflections?

  1. ASelf-refresh
  2. BODT (on-die termination)
  3. CECC
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. ODT (on-die termination)

At high speeds, impedance mismatch at the end of a line causes reflections that distort the waveform. ODT places termination resistors inside the DRAM die and lets them be switched on or off, or changed in value, depending on whether reads or writes are taking place. Introduced with DDR2, it removes board-level termination parts while keeping signals clean even when several modules or ranks share a bus.

  • A. Self-refresh lets the DRAM refresh itself while idle.
  • B. Correct. It is on-chip termination.
  • C. ECC corrects errors and is unrelated to termination.
