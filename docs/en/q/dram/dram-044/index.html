DRAM

What is commonly required by DRAM standards when the chip temperature is in the high range (for example, above 85 °C)?

  1. AStop refreshing
  2. BDouble the clock frequency
  3. CRefresh more often (shorten the refresh interval)
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. Refresh more often (shorten the refresh interval)

At high temperatures, junction leakage in the cells increases and the data retention time shortens. Many DRAM standards therefore require more frequent refresh in the high-temperature range, for example by halving the refresh interval.

  • A. Stopping refresh would lose data.
  • B. Raising the clock frequency does not solve the retention problem.
  • C. Correct. Retention time is shorter at high temperatures, so refresh is done more often.
