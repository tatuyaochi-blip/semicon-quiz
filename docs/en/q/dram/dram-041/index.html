DRAM

Which correctly describes a "rank" on a DRAM module?

  1. AThe number of banks inside one chip
  2. BA group of DRAM chips accessed together to make up the full data bus width (for example, 64 bits)
  3. CA performance grade of DRAM chips
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. A group of DRAM chips accessed together to make up the full data bus width (for example, 64 bits)

A rank is a group of DRAM chips selected together by a chip-select signal to form the full data-bus width; eight x8 chips make one 64-bit rank. Multiple ranks per module increase capacity, and interleaving ranks can hide latency, but the extra bus load can lower the maximum speed. Divisions within a chip are banks.

  • A. The internal divisions of a chip are called banks.
  • B. Correct. It is the group of chips forming the full data bus width.
  • C. Rank does not denote a performance grade.
