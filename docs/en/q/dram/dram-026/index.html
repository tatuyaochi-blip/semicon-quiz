DRAM

Which correctly describes the main leakage paths that determine the data retention time of DRAM cells?

  1. ASubthreshold leakage of the cell transistor and junction leakage
  2. BCharge generated by light
  3. CCharge moved by magnetic fields
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. Subthreshold leakage of the cell transistor and junction leakage

The charge on a DRAM capacitor is lost mainly through subthreshold leakage when the cell transistor is off and through leakage at the storage-node junction (including GIDL). Reducing these extends retention time and lowers refresh frequency and power.
