DRAM

What does 'DDR (double data rate)' in DDR SDRAM mean?

  1. AData is transferred on both the rising and falling edges of the clock
  2. BTwo DRAM chips are stacked
  3. CIt operates at twice the supply voltage
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. Data is transferred on both the rising and falling edges of the clock

DDR transfers data on both the rising and falling clock edges, doubling throughput at the same clock frequency. Data rates are therefore given in MT/s (transfers per second) rather than MHz: DDR4-3200 runs a 1600 MHz clock at 3200 MT/s. Each generation has raised speed and lowered voltage—DDR4 at 1.2 V, DDR5 at 1.1 V—and DDR5 parts exceed 6400 MT/s.
