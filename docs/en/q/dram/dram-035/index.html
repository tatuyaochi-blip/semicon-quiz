DRAM

Which trio correctly names the three major manufacturers holding large shares of the global DRAM market?

  1. AIntel, AMD, NVIDIA
  2. BTSMC, GlobalFoundries, UMC
  3. CSamsung, SK hynix, Micron
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. Samsung, SK hynix, Micron

The DRAM market is an oligopoly in which Korea's Samsung Electronics and SK hynix and America's Micron Technology hold over 90%. In the 1980s Japanese companies dominated, but price wars and huge investment races led to exits and consolidation; Elpida Memory was acquired by Micron in 2013. China's CXMT has been catching up recently, and SK hynix leads in HBM.

  • A. Intel, AMD and NVIDIA are mainly processor and GPU companies.
  • B. TSMC, GlobalFoundries and UMC are contract-manufacturing foundries.
  • C. Correct. These three hold most of the DRAM market.
