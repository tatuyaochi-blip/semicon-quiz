DRAM

Some DRAM cells have a data retention time that irregularly switches between longer and shorter values over time. What is this phenomenon called?

  1. AVRT (variable retention time)
  2. BSoft error
  3. CRow hammer
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. VRT (variable retention time)

VRT (variable retention time) occurs when a cell's leakage current switches as a defect changes state, so its retention time hops irregularly between two or more values. A cell that passes at shipping test can later become weak in the field, so a single test cannot catch every marginal cell. It is handled with refresh-period margin and ECC.

  • A. Correct. It is the irregular fluctuation of retention time.
  • B. A soft error is a temporary data flip caused by radiation or similar.
  • C. Row hammer is data flipping caused by frequently activating an adjacent row.
