Lithography

Which is the correct general order of lithography steps?

  1. AExposure → resist coating → development → pre-bake
  2. BResist coating → pre-bake → exposure → post-exposure bake → development
  3. CDevelopment → exposure → resist coating → etching
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. Resist coating → pre-bake → exposure → post-exposure bake → development

The wafer is first primed with HMDS for adhesion, then coated with an anti-reflective layer and resist and soft-baked (about 100 °C) to drive off solvent. It is exposed, given a post-exposure bake (PEB) for chemically amplified resists, and developed in alkaline developer, with an optional hard bake. Coating through development runs in a coater/developer track linked to the scanner, holding temperatures to about ±0.1 °C.
