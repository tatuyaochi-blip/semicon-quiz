Lithography

Which correctly describes masks used in EUV lithography?

  1. AReflective masks with an absorber pattern on a multilayer mirror that reflects light
  2. BTransmissive masks through which light passes glass
  3. CNo mask is used
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. Reflective masks with an absorber pattern on a multilayer mirror that reflects light

Because almost all materials absorb EUV light, transmissive masks cannot be used. EUV masks are reflective, with a pattern of light-absorbing material on a multilayer mirror of alternating molybdenum and silicon layers.
