Lithography

Based on the resolution formula R = k1 × λ / NA, how can resolution be improved (R made smaller)?

  1. AShorten the exposure wavelength and increase the numerical aperture (NA)
  2. BLengthen the exposure wavelength and decrease NA
  3. CLengthen the exposure wavelength and increase NA
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. Shorten the exposure wavelength and increase the numerical aperture (NA)

From the Rayleigh equation R = k1·λ/NA, resolution improves with shorter wavelength λ, larger numerical aperture NA and lower k1. Sources have shrunk from g-line (436 nm) through i-line (365 nm), KrF (248 nm) and ArF (193 nm) to EUV (13.5 nm), while NA grew from about 0.3 to 1.35 with immersion. ArF immersion (NA 1.35, k1 0.27) gives R ≈ 39 nm; finer pitches require multiple patterning or EUV.
