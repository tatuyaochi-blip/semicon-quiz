Lithography

What is the technique called that transfers a pattern by pressing a mold with fine patterns into resist?

  1. AElectron-beam lithography
  2. BEUV lithography
  3. CNanoimprint lithography
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. Nanoimprint lithography

Nanoimprint lithography presses a mold with a relief pattern into resist and cures it with UV light or heat to copy the shape. It needs no expensive optics and may lower costs, and it is being put into practical use in some memory manufacturing.
