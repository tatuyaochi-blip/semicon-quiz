Lithography

Which correctly describes photoresist?

  1. AAn adhesive for holding wafers in place
  2. BA metal used for wiring
  3. CA photosensitive material whose properties change when exposed to light, becoming more or less soluble in developer
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. A photosensitive material whose properties change when exposed to light, becoming more or less soluble in developer

Photoresist is a photosensitive polymer, coated tens of nm to several µm thick, whose chemistry changes on exposure: in positive resists exposed areas dissolve in developer, and in negative resists they remain. Beyond capturing the pattern, it masks the substrate during subsequent etching or implantation, so formulations must balance resolution, sensitivity and etch resistance—a field in which Japanese companies hold a large share.

  • A. It is not an adhesive.
  • B. Resist is a resin, not a wiring metal.
  • C. Correct. It is a photosensitive material whose properties change with light.
