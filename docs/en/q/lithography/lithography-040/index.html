Lithography

What is the material called, used to make photomasks, consisting of a quartz glass substrate pre-coated with a light-blocking film and similar?

  1. AMask blanks
  2. BWafer
  3. CPellet
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. Mask blanks

A mask blank is a low-defect, highly flat quartz substrate (6 inches square, about 6 mm thick) coated with chromium or MoSi absorber/phase-shift films—or, for EUV, a multilayer plus absorber—and resist. Patterns are written by electron beam and etched to make the photomask. EUV blanks in particular must be nearly defect-free, and Japanese companies such as HOYA hold large market shares.

  • A. Correct. It is the starting material for masks.
  • B. A wafer is the silicon disk on which devices are made.
  • C. A pellet is material formed into granules.
