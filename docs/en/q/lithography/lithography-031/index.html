Lithography

What is the role of lithography in semiconductor manufacturing?

  1. ATransferring circuit patterns onto resist on the wafer using light or similar
  2. BImplanting impurities into the wafer
  3. CGrinding wafers thin
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. Transferring circuit patterns onto resist on the wafer using light or similar

Lithography transfers circuit patterns from a mask onto resist coated on the wafer by exposure to light. The resulting resist pattern then masks etching or ion implantation. Advanced logic requires dozens of lithography steps, and the tools account for a large share of manufacturing cost, making lithography one of the steps that most determines the pace of scaling.

  • A. Correct. It transfers patterns onto resist.
  • B. Implanting impurities is ion implantation.
  • C. Thinning wafers is done by grinding or CMP.
