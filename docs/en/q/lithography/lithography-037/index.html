What does "half-pitch", used as a measure of scaling, mean?

  1. AHalf the side length of a chip
  2. BHalf the wafer diameter
  3. CHalf the period (pitch) of a repeating line-and-space pattern
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. Half the period (pitch) of a repeating line-and-space pattern

In a repeating line-and-space pattern, the combined length of one line and one space is the pitch, and half of that is the half-pitch. For patterns with equal lines and spaces, the half-pitch equals the line width. It has been used as a scaling metric, for memory among others.

  • A. It is unrelated to chip size.
  • B. It is unrelated to wafer size.
  • C. Correct. It is half the period of a repeating pattern.
