Lithography

How is 13.5 nm light generated in the light source of an EUV lithography tool?

  1. ABy discharge in a mercury lamp
  2. BBy wavelength-converting ArF excimer laser light
  3. CBy hitting tiny droplets of tin with a powerful laser to create plasma
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. By hitting tiny droplets of tin with a powerful laser to create plasma

The EUV source fires a high-power CO2 laser at tens of thousands of tiny tin droplets per second, turning them into hot plasma, and collects the 13.5 nm light emitted with multilayer mirrors (the LPP method). Obtaining enough brightness stably was a major hurdle in making EUV practical.

  • A. Mercury lamps are sources of i-line and similar light.
  • B. 13.5 nm is not produced by converting ArF light.
  • C. Correct. Tin droplets are turned into plasma by a laser.
