Lithography

In immersion lithography with very high numerical aperture (NA), why does light polarization need to be considered?

  1. ABecause light arrives at large angles, the strength of interference (image contrast) depends on the polarization direction
  2. BBecause polarization changes the wavelength of light
  3. CBecause polarization changes resist thickness
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. Because light arrives at large angles, the strength of interference (image contrast) depends on the polarization direction

At high NA, light hits the wafer at large angles. Whether the electric fields of interfering beams line up then depends on the polarization direction, causing differences in image contrast. Illumination with polarization controlled to suit the pattern is therefore used.

  • A. Correct. At large angles, polarization changes image contrast.
  • B. Polarization does not change wavelength.
  • C. Polarization does not change resist thickness.
