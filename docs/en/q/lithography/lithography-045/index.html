Lithography

Which technique uses computation to jointly optimize the illumination shape of the exposure tool and the mask pattern to improve resolution?

  1. ASMO (source mask optimization)
  2. BCMP
  3. CALD
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. SMO (source mask optimization)

SMO (source-mask optimization) jointly optimizes, by computation, the illumination shape (source distribution in the pupil) and the mask pattern including OPC to widen the process window. Inverse lithography technology (ILT), which works backward from the desired wafer image to derive freeform curvilinear mask shapes, is entering use now that multi-beam writers can draw curves. The enormous computation is accelerated with GPUs.

  • A. Correct. Illumination and mask are optimized together.
  • B. CMP is a planarizing polishing step.
  • C. ALD is a deposition technique that adds one atomic layer at a time.
