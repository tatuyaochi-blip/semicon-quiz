Lithography

What are the weak spots in a layout called, which lithography or process simulation predicts are prone to patterns bridging or breaking?

  1. AHotspots
  2. BNotch
  3. CDicing lanes
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. Hotspots

Hotspots are locations in a layout where patterns are prone to bridging or pinching under dose and focus variation. They can arise even when design rules are satisfied, from particular combinations with neighboring patterns. They are found beforehand by lithography simulation and pattern matching, then addressed through layout fixes, OPC tuning and focused wafer inspection.

  • A. Correct. They are weak spots prone to failure.
  • B. A notch is a cut in the wafer edge.
  • C. Dicing lanes are the lines along which chips are cut apart.
