Lithography

What is the process called that removes, with solvent, the resist that builds up around the wafer edge after spin coating?

  1. AAshing
  2. BEdge bead removal
  3. CPost-exposure bake
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. Edge bead removal

Spin coating leaves a thick bead of resist at the wafer edge from surface tension. Left in place, it flakes off as particles during handling, contaminates chucks and scanner stages, and disturbs focus at the periphery, so solvent is applied to the outer few mm to remove it (edge bead removal, EBR). A backside rinse also removes resist that has wrapped onto the back of the wafer.

  • A. Ashing removes all resist with plasma or similar.
  • B. Correct. It removes the built-up resist at the edge.
  • C. A post-exposure bake is a heat treatment after exposure.
