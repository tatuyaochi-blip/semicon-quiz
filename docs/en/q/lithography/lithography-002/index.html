Lithography

What wavelength of light is used in EUV lithography?

  1. A193 nm
  2. B248 nm
  3. C13.5 nm
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. 13.5 nm

EUV (extreme ultraviolet) lithography uses 13.5 nm light—about 1/14 of ArF's wavelength—to print fine patterns in a single exposure. Since nearly all materials, including air and glass, absorb it, EUV uses reflective optics of Mo/Si multilayer mirrors instead of lenses and operates in vacuum. Each mirror reflects only about 70%, so after nearly ten mirrors most light is lost and a very powerful source is needed.
