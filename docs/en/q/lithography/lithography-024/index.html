Lithography

What is the range of exposure dose and focus over which pattern dimensions stay within tolerance called?

  1. AProcess window
  2. BOverlay
  3. CLine edge roughness
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. Process window

The process window shows how much variation in dose and focus can be tolerated. In production, dose and focus vary because of tool variation and wafer topography, so a wider process window means more stable manufacturing. OPC and illumination optimization are done to widen it.
