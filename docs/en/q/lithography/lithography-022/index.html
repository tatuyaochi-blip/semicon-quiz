Lithography

What is the main cause of the stochastic fluctuations that are a problem in EUV lithography?

  1. AEUV light has a longer wavelength than ArF
  2. BEUV lithography uses lenses
  3. CEach EUV photon carries much energy, so there are fewer photons for the same dose
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. Each EUV photon carries much energy, so there are fewer photons for the same dose

Photon energy is inversely proportional to wavelength: EUV (13.5 nm, about 92 eV) carries about 14 times the energy of ArF (193 nm, about 6.4 eV). At equal dose, only 1/14 as many photons arrive—perhaps a few hundred to a few thousand in a 20 nm square—fluctuating statistically by about √N. These stochastic effects cause missing or bridging features, LER and unopened contacts. Raising dose helps but lowers throughput.
