Lithography

In current scanner exposure tools, what is the typical maximum area on the wafer that can be exposed in one exposure (one shot)?

  1. AThe whole wafer (300 mm diameter)
  2. BAbout 1 mm × 1 mm
  3. CAbout 26 mm × 33 mm
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. About 26 mm × 33 mm

A typical scanner exposes about 26 mm × 33 mm (roughly 858 mm²) per shot and steps across the wafer to cover it. This sets the practical maximum die size (the reticle limit), which large GPUs approach. Larger systems are built from chiplets connected in the package. High-NA EUV fields are half this size.

  • A. The whole wafer is not exposed at once.
  • B. About 1 mm square is too small.
  • C. Correct. About 26 mm × 33 mm is typical.
