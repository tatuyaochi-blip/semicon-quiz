Lithography

Which is a characteristic of chemically amplified resists?

  1. AThey dissolve during development even without exposure
  2. BThey react only to heat, not to light
  3. CAcid generated by exposure acts as a catalyst during the post-exposure bake (PEB), driving the reaction with a small exposure dose
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. Acid generated by exposure acts as a catalyst during the post-exposure bake (PEB), driving the reaction with a small exposure dose

In a chemically amplified resist, exposure generates acid from a photoacid generator, and during the post-exposure bake (PEB) the acid catalyzes repeated reactions. Because one photon drives many reactions, sensitivity is high, and these resists are widely used for KrF, ArF and EUV.
