Lithography

Which company is effectively the sole supplier of EUV lithography tools?

  1. AIntel (United States)
  2. BASML (Netherlands)
  3. CTSMC (Taiwan)
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. ASML (Netherlands)

EUV lithography tools are developed and supplied by effectively a single company, ASML of the Netherlands. They combine advanced technologies such as the light source, reflective optics and stages, and each costs on the order of hundreds of millions of dollars. TSMC and Intel are chipmakers that buy and use the tools.

  • A. Intel is a chipmaker that uses EUV tools.
  • B. Correct. ASML is effectively the only supplier.
  • C. TSMC is a foundry that uses EUV tools.
