Lithography

Which company is effectively the sole supplier of EUV lithography tools?

  1. AIntel (United States)
  2. BASML (Netherlands)
  3. CTSMC (Taiwan)
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. ASML (Netherlands)

The Dutch company ASML is effectively the sole developer and supplier of EUV scanners. They integrate technologies from several countries—Zeiss multilayer optics from Germany and Cymer's tin-based light source from the United States—contain over 100,000 parts and reportedly cost well over $100 million each (High-NA several hundred million). Because they gate leading-edge capacity, they are subject to export controls. TSMC and Intel are customers that buy and operate them.

  • A. Intel is a chipmaker that uses EUV tools.
  • B. Correct. ASML is effectively the only supplier.
  • C. TSMC is a foundry that uses EUV tools.
