Lithography

Which patterning technique uses the tendency of block copolymers to form regular fine patterns spontaneously?

  1. AOPC
  2. BDirected self-assembly (DSA)
  3. CAtomic layer etching
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. Directed self-assembly (DSA)

Block copolymers join two dissimilar polymer chains (for example, polystyrene and PMMA), which try to separate and spontaneously form regular stripes or dots a few to tens of nm in size, set by molecular length. Directed self-assembly (DSA) uses lithographically defined guide patterns to control their position and orientation, creating features finer than the lithographic limit. It promises low-cost scaling, but achieving low defectivity is the barrier to production.
