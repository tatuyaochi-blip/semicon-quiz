Lithography

Which correctly describes 'High-NA EUV', developed as the next-generation EUV exposure tool?

  1. AThe numerical aperture of the projection optics was increased from 0.33 to 0.55 to improve resolution
  2. BThe exposure wavelength was lengthened to 193 nm
  3. CIt exposes without a mask
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. The numerical aperture of the projection optics was increased from 0.33 to 0.55 to improve resolution

Standard EUV scanners have an NA of 0.33; High-NA EUV raises it to 0.55, improving resolution about 1.7× and enabling single-exposure patterning of pitches in the low 20 nm range at the same 13.5 nm wavelength. Depth of focus shrinks, and because reduction differs by direction, the exposure field is halved. Developed by ASML, each tool is reported to cost on the order of hundreds of millions of dollars.
