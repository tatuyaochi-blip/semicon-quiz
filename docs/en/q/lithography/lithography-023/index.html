Lithography

Why are defects buried inside the multilayer of an EUV mask a problem?

  1. ABecause they make the mask heavier
  2. BBecause they disturb the phase or intensity of reflected light and are printed as pattern defects on the wafer
  3. CBecause they change the mask's color
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. Because they disturb the phase or intensity of reflected light and are printed as pattern defects on the wafer

A bump or particle only a few nm tall in or beneath an EUV mask's multilayer disturbs the dozens of layers above it, altering the phase and intensity of reflected light and printing as a wafer defect. Such defects are hard to see from the surface, so dedicated inspection tools using EUV light (actinic inspection) have been developed, and absorber patterns are sometimes shifted to cover defect locations.
