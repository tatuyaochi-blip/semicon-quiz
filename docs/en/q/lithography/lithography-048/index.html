Lithography

In EUV masks, what is the phenomenon called in which the absorber pattern's thickness is not negligible compared with the wavelength, so obliquely incident light casts shadows and otherwise affects the image?

  1. ABlooming
  2. BMask 3D effects
  3. CLoading effect
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. Mask 3D effects

EUV masks are reflective, illuminated at about 6° incidence. The absorber, 50–70 nm thick—several times the 13.5 nm wavelength—casts shadows and shifts phase at its sidewalls, so dimensions and placement vary with pattern orientation and best focus varies by pattern (mask 3D effects). Responses include thinner high-absorption absorber materials, phase-shift EUV masks and OPC corrections.

  • A. Blooming is charge overflow in image sensors.
  • B. Correct. They are effects of the mask's three-dimensional structure.
  • C. The loading effect is a change in process rate with processed area.
