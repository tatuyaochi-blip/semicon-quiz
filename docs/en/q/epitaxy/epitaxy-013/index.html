Epitaxial growth

In epitaxial growth by CVD, the growth rate depends strongly on temperature in the relatively low-temperature regime. Why?

  1. ABecause the growth rate is determined only by the source gas supply
  2. BBecause the rate of the chemical reaction at the surface determines the growth rate (surface-reaction limited)
  3. CBecause the growth rate does not depend on temperature
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. Because the rate of the chemical reaction at the surface determines the growth rate (surface-reaction limited)

At low temperature, surface reactions are slow and limit the growth rate (reaction-limited). Reaction rates follow the Arrhenius law, depending exponentially on temperature, so growth rate does too, and temperature uniformity across the wafer translates directly into thickness uniformity. At high temperature reactions are fast and the arrival rate of precursor gas limits growth (mass-transport-limited), making gas-flow uniformity critical.
