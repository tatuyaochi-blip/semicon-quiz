Epitaxial growth

In the X-ray diffraction rocking curve used to evaluate the crystal quality of an epitaxial layer, what does a narrow peak width (FWHM) indicate?

  1. AThat there is little tilt, mosaicity or defects, so the crystal quality is good
  2. BThat the impurity concentration is high
  3. CThat the film is thick
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. That there is little tilt, mosaicity or defects, so the crystal quality is good

A rocking curve measures the intensity of a particular diffraction peak while slightly varying the sample angle. Variation in crystal orientation (mosaicity) and dislocations broaden the peak, so a narrower FWHM indicates better crystal quality. It is widely used to evaluate epi layers such as GaN.

  • A. Correct. It indicates well-aligned crystal with few defects.
  • B. It is not a direct measure of impurity concentration.
  • C. The FWHM is not a direct measure of thickness.
