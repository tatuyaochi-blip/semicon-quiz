Epitaxial growth

Which polytype is mainly used for SiC power devices?

  1. A4H-SiC
  2. B3C-SiC
  3. C15R-SiC
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. 4H-SiC

SiC has more than 200 polytypes, but power devices mainly use 4H-SiC, which has a large band gap of about 3.3 eV and little anisotropy in electron mobility. The established technology for high-quality substrates and epitaxial growth is another reason.
