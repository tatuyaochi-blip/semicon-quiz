Epitaxial growth

Which is closest to the typical growth temperature for silicon epitaxy using trichlorosilane?

  1. AAbout 100 °C
  2. BAbout 1100 °C
  3. CAbout 2000 °C
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. About 1100 °C

Silicon epitaxy with trichlorosilane is typically done at high temperatures around 1100 °C. Dichlorosilane or silane allow growth at lower temperatures. 2000 °C exceeds silicon's melting point (about 1414 °C).
