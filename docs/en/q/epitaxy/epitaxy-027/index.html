Epitaxial growth

Compared with thermal diffusion, what is an advantage of adding dopants during epitaxial growth?

  1. AIt is always cheaper
  2. BIt is easier to create abrupt changes in concentration with depth
  3. CUniform concentration cannot be achieved
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. It is easier to create abrupt changes in concentration with depth

Thermal diffusion spreads dopants inward from the surface, so it can only produce profiles that peak at the surface and fall off gradually. Epitaxy changes doping layer by layer by switching gas flows, enabling transitions within a few nm, buried heavily doped layers, and even inverted profiles with light doping at the surface. It is indispensable for HBT bases and the multilayer structures of power devices.
