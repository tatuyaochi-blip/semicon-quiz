Epitaxial growth

Which technique, developed by Isamu Akasaki, Hiroshi Amano and colleagues to grow high-quality GaN on sapphire, first deposits a thin layer at low temperature and then grows GaN at high temperature?

  1. AChemical mechanical polishing
  2. BLow-temperature buffer layer technique
  3. CFloating zone method
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. Low-temperature buffer layer technique

Growing GaN directly on sapphire gives sparse nucleation and rough hexagonal columns. In 1986 Akasaki and Amano first deposited a thin (tens of nm) AlN buffer at a low temperature of about 500 °C, then grew GaN at about 1000 °C, obtaining dense nucleation and a smooth, high-quality crystal. Together with p-type GaN, this led to the blue LED; low-temperature GaN buffers were later adopted as well.

  • A. Chemical mechanical polishing is a technique for planarizing surfaces.
  • B. Correct. It uses a buffer layer deposited at low temperature.
  • C. The floating zone method makes silicon single crystals.
