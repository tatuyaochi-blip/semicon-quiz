Epitaxial growth

What is the phenomenon called in which impurities from the substrate or elsewhere are incorporated into the growing layer through the gas phase during epitaxial growth?

  1. AAutodoping
  2. BGettering
  3. CElectromigration
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. Autodoping

Autodoping occurs when impurities evaporate from a heavily doped substrate or its back side and are incorporated through the gas into the growing layer. Because it causes the epitaxial layer's doping to deviate from the design, countermeasures such as covering the back of the substrate with oxide are used.
