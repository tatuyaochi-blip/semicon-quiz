Epitaxial growth

In a lattice-mismatched system, which growth mode grows layer by layer for the first few atomic layers and switches to island growth as strain accumulates? It is also used to make quantum dots.

  1. AFrank–van der Merwe
  2. BVolmer–Weber
  3. CStranski–Krastanov
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. Stranski–Krastanov

In Stranski-Krastanov (SK) growth, a wetting layer one or two monolayers thick grows layer by layer; once strain energy builds up, forming islands that relieve strain becomes favorable despite the added surface area, and growth turns three-dimensional. These self-assembled islands, tens of nm across, serve as quantum dots; InAs/GaAs quantum-dot lasers have been commercialized as temperature-robust light sources for optical communications.

  • A. Frank–van der Merwe growth proceeds layer by layer throughout.
  • B. Volmer–Weber growth forms islands from the start.
  • C. Correct. It switches from layer growth to island growth.
