Epitaxial growth

When a lightly doped epi layer is grown at high temperature on a heavily doped substrate, what is the phenomenon called in which substrate dopants seep into the epi layer by solid-state diffusion, smoothing the concentration change at the interface?

  1. AElectromigration
  2. BSputtering
  3. COut-diffusion
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. Out-diffusion

High temperatures during epitaxy and later anneals let dopants from a heavily doped substrate diffuse through the solid into the epi layer, grading the transition at the interface. This out-diffusion thins the effective lightly doped layer and can reduce breakdown voltage in power devices. It is limited by using slow-diffusing arsenic or antimony in the substrate and minimizing thermal budget. It is distinct from autodoping, which occurs through the gas phase.

  • A. Electromigration is the movement of wiring metal atoms caused by current.
  • B. Sputtering is a deposition method that ejects atoms by ion bombardment.
  • C. Correct. Substrate dopants diffuse through the solid into the epi layer.
