Epitaxial growth

When a lightly doped epi layer is grown at high temperature on a heavily doped substrate, what is the phenomenon called in which substrate dopants seep into the epi layer by solid-state diffusion, smoothing the concentration change at the interface?

  1. AElectromigration
  2. BSputtering
  3. COut-diffusion
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. Out-diffusion

The high temperatures of epitaxy and later heat treatments make dopants from the heavily doped substrate diffuse through the solid into the epi layer, smoothing the concentration transition at the boundary. This is called out-diffusion, and it reduces the effective epi thickness. It is distinguished from autodoping, in which impurities are incorporated through the gas phase.

  • A. Electromigration is the movement of wiring metal atoms caused by current.
  • B. Sputtering is a deposition method that ejects atoms by ion bombardment.
  • C. Correct. Substrate dopants diffuse through the solid into the epi layer.
