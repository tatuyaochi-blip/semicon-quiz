Epitaxial growth

In GaN and similar materials, what is the technique called that reduces dislocations by growing crystal from mask openings and then laterally over the mask?

  1. AEpitaxial lateral overgrowth (ELO)
  2. BALD
  3. CCMP
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. Epitaxial lateral overgrowth (ELO)

In ELO, crystals grow up through stripe openings in a mask such as SiO2 and then spread laterally over the mask until they coalesce. Dislocations rising from the substrate are blocked by the mask, so regions over it have dislocation densities several orders of magnitude lower. In the late 1990s ELO greatly extended the lifetime of GaN blue-violet lasers, and maskless variants have since been developed.
