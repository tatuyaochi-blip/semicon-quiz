Epitaxial growth

When a thin silicon layer is grown epitaxially on a relaxed SiGe layer with a larger lattice constant, what strain develops in the silicon?

  1. ACompressive strain
  2. BNo strain
  3. CTensile strain
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. Tensile strain

Silicon's lattice constant is smaller than SiGe's, so when grown on SiGe, the spacing of silicon atoms is stretched to match the SiGe, producing tensile strain. Tensile-strained silicon has higher electron mobility and has been used to improve nMOS performance.
