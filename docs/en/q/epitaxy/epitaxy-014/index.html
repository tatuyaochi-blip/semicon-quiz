Epitaxial growth

Which is a typical crystal defect in epitaxial layers in which the stacking sequence of atomic planes is disrupted?

  1. AVoid
  2. BHillock
  3. CStacking fault
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. Stacking fault

A stacking fault is a defect in which the order in which atomic planes stack is locally disrupted. In epitaxial growth it can originate from contamination or scratches on the substrate surface. Because it causes leakage current and other problems, surface preparation before growth is important.
