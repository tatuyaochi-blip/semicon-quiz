Epitaxial growth

What is it called when doping gas is flowed during epitaxial growth to add dopants at the same time as growth?

  1. AIn-situ doping
  2. BIon implantation
  3. CGettering
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. In-situ doping

In-situ doping introduces dopant gases (phosphine, diborane, etc.) during growth, avoiding the crystal damage of ion implantation and the high-temperature anneal needed to activate it. Switching gas flows creates precise depth profiles with high activation. Source/drain epitaxy in advanced transistors—SiGe:B and Si:P—reaches concentrations on the order of 10²¹ cm⁻³ this way.
