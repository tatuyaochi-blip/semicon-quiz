Epitaxial growth

What is it called when doping gas is flowed during epitaxial growth to add dopants at the same time as growth?

  1. AIn-situ doping
  2. BIon implantation
  3. CGettering
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. In-situ doping

In-situ doping incorporates dopants during growth, so there is no crystal damage as with ion implantation, and precise depth profiles can be created. It is used in the source/drain epitaxial layers of advanced transistors.
