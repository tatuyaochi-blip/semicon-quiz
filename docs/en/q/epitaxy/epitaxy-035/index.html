Epitaxial growth

Which is closest to the density of threading dislocations, which run up to the film surface, in GaN grown on sapphire?

  1. AAbout 10²⁰ cm⁻²
  2. BAbout 10¹ to 10² cm⁻²
  3. CAbout 10⁸ to 10⁹ cm⁻²
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. About 10⁸ to 10⁹ cm⁻²

Sapphire and GaN differ in lattice constant by about 16%, so GaN on sapphire contains threading dislocations at about 10⁸–10⁹ cm⁻²—fatal levels for silicon ICs. Yet InGaN LEDs shine efficiently, thought to be because compositional fluctuations in InGaN localize carriers, which recombine before reaching dislocations. For lasers and power devices, however, reducing dislocations is essential.

  • A. About 10²⁰ cm⁻² approaches atomic density and could not form a crystal.
  • B. About 10¹ to 10² cm⁻² is nearly dislocation-free, very high-quality crystal.
  • C. Correct. A high density of about 10⁸ to 10⁹ cm⁻².
