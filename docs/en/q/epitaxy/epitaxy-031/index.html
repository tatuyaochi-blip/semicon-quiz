Epitaxial growth

What is the origin of the word "epitaxy"?

  1. AGreek "epi" (upon) and "taxis" (arrangement)
  2. BLatin "epi" (heat) and "taxis" (melt)
  3. CThe inventor's name
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. Greek "epi" (upon) and "taxis" (arrangement)

Epitaxy combines the Greek epi ("upon") and taxis ("arrangement"), describing crystals grown on a substrate in alignment with its arrangement. The French mineralogist Royer coined the term in 1928. The same root appears in biology's "taxis" (directed movement toward light or chemicals) and in "taxonomy" (classification).

  • A. Correct. It means "arranged upon".
  • B. It does not mean heat or melting.
  • C. It does not come from a person's name.
