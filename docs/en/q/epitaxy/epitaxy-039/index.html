Epitaxial growth

In heteroepitaxy of materials with very different lattice constants, what is the purpose of inserting a "graded buffer layer" whose composition changes gradually?

  1. ATo make the wafer thinner
  2. BTo change the lattice constant gradually, relaxing strain step by step and reducing dislocations threading into the upper layer
  3. CTo increase the growth rate
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. To change the lattice constant gradually, relaxing strain step by step and reducing dislocations threading into the upper layer

Growing a material with a large lattice mismatch directly causes many dislocations to thread up into the top layer. Inserting a buffer with gradually changing composition relaxes the strain step by step, keeping misfit dislocations within the buffer and reducing dislocations in the upper layer. It is used in growing SiGe, III-Vs and others.

  • A. It is not intended to thin the wafer.
  • B. Correct. Strain is relaxed gradually to reduce dislocations in the upper layer.
  • C. It is not intended to increase the growth rate.
