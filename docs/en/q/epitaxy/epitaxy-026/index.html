Epitaxial growth

Which technology is used to form the base layer of a SiGe HBT?

  1. AEpitaxial growth (sometimes grading the Ge concentration with depth)
  2. BCMP
  3. CSputtering
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. Epitaxial growth (sometimes grading the Ge concentration with depth)

The base of a SiGe HBT is made by epitaxially growing a SiGe layer on silicon. Increasing the Ge concentration from the emitter side toward the collector side creates a built-in field in the base from the band-gap gradient, accelerating electrons, which favors high-speed operation.
