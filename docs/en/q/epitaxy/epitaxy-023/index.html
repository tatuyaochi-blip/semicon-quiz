Epitaxial growth

What is the main challenge in growing GaN on Si substrates?

  1. AThe substrate is very expensive
  2. BLarge differences in lattice constant and thermal expansion tend to cause dislocations, cracks and wafer bow
  3. CGaN does not grow at all
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. Large differences in lattice constant and thermal expansion tend to cause dislocations, cracks and wafer bow

The large lattice mismatch between GaN and silicon generates many dislocations, and the large difference in thermal expansion makes cracks and wafer bow likely when cooling after growth. Engineering buffer layers such as AlN and AlGaN has made GaN growth on large-diameter Si substrates practical.
