Epitaxial growth

Which research-stage technique grows a crystal on a substrate covered with a 2D material such as graphene so that the grown film can be peeled off and the substrate reused?

  1. ARemote epitaxy
  2. BThermal diffusion
  3. CAnodic bonding
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. Remote epitaxy

In remote epitaxy, the substrate is covered with one or two layers of graphene or a similar 2D material before growth. Graphene is thin enough that the substrate's atomic potential penetrates it, transferring crystal information so a single crystal grows, yet the film does not chemically bond to the substrate and can be peeled off—even with tape—allowing substrate reuse. Applications include reusing expensive GaAs or SiC substrates and flexible devices.

  • A. Correct. Growth through a 2D material allows the film to be peeled off.
  • B. Thermal diffusion is a process that spreads dopants by heat.
  • C. Anodic bonding joins silicon and glass with voltage and heat.
