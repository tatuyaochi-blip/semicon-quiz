Epitaxial growth

What is the typical thickness range of the epitaxial layer on silicon epi wafers for ICs and power devices?

  1. AA few nanometers
  2. BA few micrometers to several tens of micrometers
  3. CA few millimeters
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. A few micrometers to several tens of micrometers

Epi layers for logic and image sensors are a few µm thick, while power-device layers scale with voltage rating: tens of µm for 600 V silicon and over 100 µm for 10 kV SiC—still thin compared with a 775 µm wafer. Compound-semiconductor epi such as HBT bases and quantum wells, by contrast, is only nm to tens of nm thick, so epi thicknesses span more than four orders of magnitude.

  • A. A few nanometers is the thickness of ultra-thin layers such as quantum wells.
  • B. Correct. A few to several tens of micrometers is typical.
  • C. A few millimeters is thicker than the wafer itself, not an epi layer thickness.
