Epitaxial growth

Which correctly describes the critical thickness in heteroepitaxial growth?

  1. AThe minimum thickness needed for growth to start
  2. BThe maximum thickness that can be removed by etching
  3. CThe maximum thickness that can be grown while keeping its strain without generating dislocations
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. The maximum thickness that can be grown while keeping its strain without generating dislocations

A film with a different lattice constant grows strained to match the substrate while it is thin (coherent growth). Once its thickness exceeds the critical thickness, misfit dislocations form to release the strain energy. This value is important when designing strained SiGe layers and similar structures.
