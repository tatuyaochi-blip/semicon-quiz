Epitaxial growth

Which is closest to the typical growth temperature for SiC epitaxy (CVD)?

  1. AAbout 600 °C
  2. BAbout 1,000 °C
  3. CAbout 1,500 to 1,650 °C
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. About 1,500 to 1,650 °C

SiC has strong bonds and its atoms move with difficulty, so good epitaxial layers require temperatures of about 1,500 to 1,650 °C, higher than for silicon. Silane-type gases and hydrocarbons such as propane are used as sources, and reactor parts must withstand the high temperature.

  • A. Good single-crystal SiC does not grow at about 600 °C.
  • B. About 1,000 °C is close to the temperature for silicon epitaxy.
  • C. Correct. It is grown at about 1,500 to 1,650 °C.
