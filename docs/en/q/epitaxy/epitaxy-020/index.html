Epitaxial growth

In SiC epitaxial growth, what is the main reason for using substrates tilted a few degrees from the crystal axis (with an off-angle)?

  1. ATo grow laterally from atomic steps on the surface (step-flow growth) and inherit the same polytype as the substrate
  2. BTo make the substrate cheaper
  3. CTo make the growth rate zero
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. To grow laterally from atomic steps on the surface (step-flow growth) and inherit the same polytype as the substrate

SiC has many polytypes with different stacking sequences, and growth on a flat (on-axis) surface tends to nucleate unwanted ones such as 3C-SiC. Substrates cut about 4° off-axis present many atomic steps; adatoms incorporate at the steps and growth proceeds laterally (step-flow growth), so the crystal inherits the substrate's 4H-SiC stacking sequence.
