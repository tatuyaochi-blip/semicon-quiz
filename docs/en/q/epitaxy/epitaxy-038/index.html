Epitaxial growth

Which type of reactor is mainstream for silicon epitaxial growth on 300 mm wafers?

  1. ASingle-wafer reactors processing one wafer at a time
  2. BDip systems that immerse wafers in liquid
  3. CBatch diffusion furnaces processing hundreds of wafers at once
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. Single-wafer reactors processing one wafer at a time

Epitaxy on 300 mm wafers is dominated by single-wafer reactors that heat each wafer rapidly with halogen lamps through quartz windows and precisely control gas flow and temperature. Rotating the wafer keeps thickness and resistivity variation to about ±1–2%. Conditions are easy to tune, and fast heating and cooling keep thermal budgets small. Leading suppliers include Applied Materials and ASM.

  • A. Correct. Single-wafer reactors are mainstream.
  • B. Immersion is liquid-phase epitaxy, not used for silicon IC epi.
  • C. Batch furnaces are used for oxidation and diffusion, but single-wafer reactors dominate 300 mm epitaxy.
