Epitaxial growth

Which type of reactor is mainstream for silicon epitaxial growth on 300 mm wafers?

  1. ASingle-wafer reactors processing one wafer at a time
  2. BDip systems that immerse wafers in liquid
  3. CBatch diffusion furnaces processing hundreds of wafers at once
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. Single-wafer reactors processing one wafer at a time

For 300 mm epitaxy, single-wafer reactors that rapidly heat one wafer at a time with lamps and precisely control gas flow and temperature are mainstream. They make it easier to achieve uniform thickness and resistivity and to adjust conditions for each run.

  • A. Correct. Single-wafer reactors are mainstream.
  • B. Immersion is liquid-phase epitaxy, not used for silicon LSI epi.
  • C. Batch furnaces are used for oxidation and diffusion, but single-wafer reactors dominate 300 mm epitaxy.
