Epitaxial growth

In MOCVD growth of GaN, what does the controlled condition "V/III ratio" mean?

  1. AThe ratio of film thickness to growth time
  2. BThe molar ratio of the group V source (such as ammonia) to the group III source (such as organogallium) supplied
  3. CThe ratio of growth temperature to pressure
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. The molar ratio of the group V source (such as ammonia) to the group III source (such as organogallium) supplied

The V/III ratio is the molar ratio of group-V to group-III precursors fed to the reactor. For GaN, ammonia cracks inefficiently and nitrogen tends to desorb at high temperature, so ratios of hundreds to thousands are used. The ratio affects surface smoothness, the balance of lateral versus vertical growth, incorporation of impurities such as carbon, and point-defect levels.

  • A. Thickness divided by time is the growth rate.
  • B. Correct. It is the supply ratio of group V to group III sources.
  • C. It is not the ratio of temperature to pressure.
