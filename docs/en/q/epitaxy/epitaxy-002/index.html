Epitaxial growth

Which is an example of heteroepitaxial growth?

  1. AGrowing silicon on a silicon substrate
  2. BGrowing SiGe on a silicon substrate
  3. CThermally oxidizing a silicon substrate
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. Growing SiGe on a silicon substrate

Growing the same material as the substrate is called homoepitaxy, and growing a different material is called heteroepitaxy. SiGe on silicon and GaN on sapphire or SiC are examples of heteroepitaxy. SiO2 formed by thermal oxidation is amorphous and is not epitaxial growth.
