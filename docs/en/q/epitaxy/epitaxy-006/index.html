Epitaxial growth

Which gases are used as sources for silicon epitaxial growth?

  1. AArgon only
  2. BTrichlorosilane (SiHCl3) and dichlorosilane (SiH2Cl2)
  3. COxygen
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. Trichlorosilane (SiHCl3) and dichlorosilane (SiH2Cl2)

Silicon epitaxy uses silicon-bearing gases—trichlorosilane, dichlorosilane or silane—together with hydrogen. At high temperature they decompose or are reduced, depositing silicon, as in SiHCl3 + H2 → Si + 3HCl. Chlorinated precursors need higher temperatures, but the HCl they produce suppresses deposition on oxide, favoring selective growth. Phosphine or diborane is added for doping.
