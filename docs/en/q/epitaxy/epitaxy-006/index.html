Epitaxial growth

Which gases are used as sources for silicon epitaxial growth?

  1. AArgon only
  2. BTrichlorosilane (SiHCl3) and dichlorosilane (SiH2Cl2)
  3. COxygen
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. Trichlorosilane (SiHCl3) and dichlorosilane (SiH2Cl2)

Silicon epitaxy uses silicon-containing gases such as trichlorosilane, dichlorosilane and silane together with hydrogen. They decompose and are reduced at high temperature, depositing silicon on the substrate. Phosphine, diborane and similar gases are added for doping.
