Epitaxial growth

Which method grows crystal on a substrate by precipitating it from a solution such as a molten metal?

  1. ALiquid phase epitaxy (LPE)
  2. BMolecular beam epitaxy (MBE)
  3. CMetal-organic chemical vapor deposition (MOCVD)
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. Liquid phase epitaxy (LPE)

Liquid-phase epitaxy contacts the substrate with a metal solution (gallium, for GaAs) saturated with the material to be grown, then cools it so the excess precipitates as crystal. Growth near equilibrium gives excellent crystallinity, and the equipment is simple and fast, so LPE once dominated red and infrared LEDs and early laser diodes. MOCVD and MBE are better at thin layers and abrupt interfaces.
