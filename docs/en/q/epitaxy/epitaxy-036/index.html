Epitaxial growth

In bipolar transistors and similar devices, what is the main purpose of a heavily doped layer (buried layer) beneath the epitaxial layer?

  1. ATo lower the collector series resistance
  2. BTo make the base thicker
  3. CTo make the epi layer highly resistive
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. To lower the collector series resistance

In a vertical bipolar transistor, collector current flows laterally along the bottom of the epi layer to a surface collector contact. Forming a heavily doped n⁺ buried layer with arsenic or antimony before epitaxy lowers this path's resistance, improving saturation voltage and high-frequency performance. Slow-diffusing arsenic and antimony are used to limit upward out-diffusion during epi growth.

  • A. Correct. It is a heavily doped layer that lowers collector resistance.
  • B. It is unrelated to base thickness.
  • C. The epi layer's resistivity is set by doping during growth.
