Epitaxial growth

What is MOCVD (metal-organic chemical vapor deposition) mainly used for?

  1. AThermal oxidation of silicon
  2. BPlating copper wiring
  3. CEpitaxial growth of compound semiconductors such as GaN
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. Epitaxial growth of compound semiconductors such as GaN

MOCVD grows compound semiconductor crystals by reacting metal-organic sources such as trimethylgallium with source gases such as ammonia. It is well suited to mass production and widely used to make GaN-based blue and white LEDs, semiconductor lasers and RF devices.
