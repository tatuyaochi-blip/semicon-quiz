Epitaxial growth

What is the periodic structure called in which layers of different materials, each only a few nm thick, are stacked alternately?

  1. APolycrystal
  2. BAmorphous
  3. CSuperlattice
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. Superlattice

A superlattice periodically stacks layers a few nm thick of different materials, such as GaAs and AlGaAs; Leo Esaki and colleagues proposed it in 1970. The imposed periodicity creates new energy minibands and electronic and optical properties absent from the constituent materials. Superlattices are used in quantum cascade lasers and infrared detectors, as buffers whose strain bends and halts dislocations, and in DBR mirrors.
