Epitaxial growth

Why are epitaxial layers important in the fabrication of SiC power devices?

  1. ATo form the voltage-blocking drift layer precisely, with low doping and the required thickness
  2. BTo make the wafer transparent
  3. CTo reduce wiring resistance
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. To form the voltage-blocking drift layer precisely, with low doping and the required thickness

In SiC, dopants hardly diffuse thermally, so the device's main layers are formed by epitaxial growth. The doping and thickness of the drift layer, which determine breakdown voltage, must be controlled precisely, and reducing crystal defects in the epitaxial layer is also important for yield and reliability.
