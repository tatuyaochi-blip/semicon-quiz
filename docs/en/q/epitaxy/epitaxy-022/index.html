Epitaxial growth

Which method measures the thickness of a lightly doped epitaxial layer on a heavily doped substrate using infrared interference?

  1. ASIMS
  2. BCross-sectional SEM observation
  3. CFourier transform infrared spectroscopy (FTIR)
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. Fourier transform infrared spectroscopy (FTIR)

A heavily doped substrate and the epitaxial layer have slightly different refractive indices for infrared light, so reflection occurs at the interface. Measuring the interference between reflections from the surface and the interface with FTIR gives the epitaxial layer thickness non-destructively. It is widely used to control epitaxial wafer thickness.
