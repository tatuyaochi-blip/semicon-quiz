Epitaxial growth

In advanced CMOS processes, why is it necessary to lower the temperature of silicon and SiGe epitaxial growth?

  1. ATo speed up epitaxial growth
  2. BTo make equipment larger
  3. CTo limit diffusion of dopants and deformation of structures already formed (thermal budget constraints)
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. To limit diffusion of dopants and deformation of structures already formed (thermal budget constraints)

The fine structures and abrupt dopant profiles of transistors are degraded when exposed to high temperatures. Epitaxial growth performed later in the process must therefore be done at as low a temperature as possible. The total amount of heat the process can tolerate is called the thermal budget.
