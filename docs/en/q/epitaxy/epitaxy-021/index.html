Epitaxial growth

Which is an advantage of performing silicon epitaxial growth at reduced pressure?

  1. AAutodoping is easier to suppress
  2. BThe growth rate always increases
  3. CThe growth temperature can exceed 2000 °C
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. Autodoping is easier to suppress

At reduced pressure, gas diffusion is faster, so impurities evaporated from the substrate are less likely to linger near the growth surface, suppressing autodoping. Thickness uniformity also tends to improve.
