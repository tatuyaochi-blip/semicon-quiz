Epitaxial growth

What is the main purpose of heating the wafer in high-temperature hydrogen just before silicon epitaxial growth?

  1. ATo oxidize the surface thickly
  2. BTo dope heavily
  3. CTo remove the native oxide and expose a clean crystal surface
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. To remove the native oxide and expose a clean crystal surface

If native oxide or contamination remains on the surface, single crystal does not grow properly on it and defects result. Before growth, the wafer is heated in hydrogen (hydrogen bake) to remove the native oxide and expose a clean silicon crystal surface.
