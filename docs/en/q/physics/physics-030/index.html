Semiconductor physics

Which correctly describes a hole?

  1. AA vacancy left in the valence band when an electron leaves, which behaves like a positively charged particle
  2. BA proton in the atomic nucleus
  3. CAn electron in the conduction band
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. A vacancy left in the valence band when an electron leaves, which behaves like a positively charged particle

When an electron leaves the valence band, a neighboring electron moves into the vacancy, so the vacancy appears to move in the opposite direction. Treating this vacancy as a particle with positive charge and positive effective mass gives the hole, the majority carrier in p-type material. It is really the collective motion of valence electrons, but the fact that the Hall voltage actually has a positive sign confirms that treating it as a positive particle is valid.
