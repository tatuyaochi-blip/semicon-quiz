Semiconductor physics

Which is a typical factor that shortens the minority carrier lifetime in silicon?

  1. AImpurities that create deep levels, such as gold, and crystal defects
  2. BCooling the wafer to cryogenic temperatures
  3. CPlacing the wafer in a vacuum
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. Impurities that create deep levels, such as gold, and crystal defects

Heavy-metal impurities such as gold and iron, and crystal defects, create deep levels near the middle of the band gap that act as recombination centers, promoting electron-hole recombination. This shortens the minority carrier lifetime. In fast-switching diodes, heavy metals are sometimes added deliberately, or electron irradiation is used, to shorten the lifetime.
