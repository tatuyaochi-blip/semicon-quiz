Semiconductor physics

Where is the Fermi level located in an n-type semiconductor?

  1. ACloser to the valence band than the intrinsic Fermi level
  2. BCloser to the conduction band than the intrinsic Fermi level
  3. CAlways inside the valence band
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. Closer to the conduction band than the intrinsic Fermi level

In an n-type semiconductor doped with donors, there are many electrons in the conduction band, so the Fermi level shifts from the intrinsic Fermi level near mid-gap toward the conduction band. The higher the donor concentration, the closer it gets. In p-type material it shifts toward the valence band.
