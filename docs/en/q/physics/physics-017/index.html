Semiconductor physics

What can be learned from a Hall effect measurement?

  1. AThe type of carrier (electron or hole) and the carrier density
  2. BThe size of the band gap
  3. CThe melting point of the crystal
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. The type of carrier (electron or hole) and the carrier density

When a magnetic field is applied perpendicular to a sample carrying current, the carriers are deflected and a Hall voltage appears. Its sign tells whether the carriers are electrons or holes, and its magnitude gives the carrier density. Combined with a resistivity measurement, the mobility can also be found.
