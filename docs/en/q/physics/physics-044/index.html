Semiconductor physics

Which is the correct definition of the work function of a material?

  1. AThe energy difference between the vacuum level and the bottom of the conduction band
  2. BThe energy difference between the bottom of the conduction band and the top of the valence band
  3. CThe energy difference between the vacuum level and the Fermi level
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. The energy difference between the vacuum level and the Fermi level

The work function is the energy needed to remove an electron at the Fermi level into vacuum, i.e. the difference between the vacuum level and the Fermi level. In a semiconductor the Fermi level moves with doping, so the work function changes too (about 4.05–5.17 eV in silicon). The work-function difference between metal and semiconductor affects Schottky barriers and MOS threshold voltage, and metal-gate materials (TiN, TiAl, etc.) are chosen for the work function that gives the target threshold.

  • A. The difference between the vacuum level and the conduction band bottom is the electron affinity.
  • B. The difference between the conduction band bottom and valence band top is the band gap.
  • C. Correct. The work function is the difference between the vacuum level and the Fermi level.
