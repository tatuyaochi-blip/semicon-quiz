Which value is closest to the atomic density of crystalline silicon?

  1. AAbout 5×10¹⁰ cm⁻³
  2. BAbout 5×10¹⁶ cm⁻³
  3. CAbout 5×10²² cm⁻³
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. About 5×10²² cm⁻³

Silicon's atomic density is about 5×10²² cm⁻³, which follows from eight atoms per unit cell 0.543 nm on a side (8 ÷ (0.543×10⁻⁷ cm)³). A donor concentration of 1×10¹⁶ cm⁻³ is only one atom in five million, yet it lowers resistivity from about 2×10⁵ Ω·cm (intrinsic) to about 0.5 Ω·cm. This sensitivity is why raw silicon must be purified to about 11N (99.999999999%).
