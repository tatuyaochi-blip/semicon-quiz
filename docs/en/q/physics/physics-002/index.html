Semiconductor physics

Which statement is correct when phosphorus (P) is added to silicon as an impurity?

  1. AIt becomes a p-type semiconductor with holes as majority carriers
  2. BIt becomes an n-type semiconductor with electrons as majority carriers
  3. CCarriers do not increase, and it remains an intrinsic semiconductor
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. It becomes an n-type semiconductor with electrons as majority carriers

Phosphorus is a group V element with five valence electrons. When it enters the silicon (group IV) crystal, one electron is left over and becomes a conduction electron. Such an impurity is called a donor and produces an n-type semiconductor. To make p-type silicon, a group III element (acceptor) such as boron (B) is added.
