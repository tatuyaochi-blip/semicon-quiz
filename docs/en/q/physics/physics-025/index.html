Semiconductor physics

What is the approximate resistivity of n-type silicon with an electron density of 1×10¹⁶ cm⁻³ and an electron mobility of 1000 cm²/V·s? (Ignore holes; elementary charge 1.6×10⁻¹⁹ C)

  1. AAbout 0.06 Ω·cm
  2. BAbout 0.6 Ω·cm
  3. CAbout 60 Ω·cm
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. About 0.6 Ω·cm

Conductivity is σ = qnμ = 1.6×10⁻¹⁹ × 1×10¹⁶ × 1000 = 1.6 S/cm, and resistivity is its inverse, ρ ≈ 0.63 Ω·cm. ρ ≈ 1/(qnμ) is the basic formula for estimating dopant concentration from wafer resistivity. In practice, conversion tables (such as ASTM standards) that include the doping dependence of mobility are used; for example, a 1–10 Ω·cm p-type wafer corresponds to boron in the 10¹⁵ cm⁻³ range.
