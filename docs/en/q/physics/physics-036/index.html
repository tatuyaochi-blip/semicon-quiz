Semiconductor physics

Which is the correct unit of carrier mobility?

  1. Acm²/(V·s)
  2. BΩ·cm
  3. Ccm/s
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. cm²/(V·s)

Mobility is drift velocity per unit electric field, so its unit is (cm/s) ÷ (V/cm) = cm²/(V·s). The SI unit is m²/(V·s), but the semiconductor field conventionally uses cm, with 1 m²/(V·s) = 10⁴ cm²/(V·s). Typical values are about 1400 for electrons and 450 for holes in silicon, and about 8500 cm²/(V·s) for electrons in GaAs, figures that directly guide material choice for high-frequency transistors.

  • A. Correct. Velocity divided by electric field gives cm²/(V·s).
  • B. Ω·cm is the unit of resistivity.
  • C. cm/s is a unit of velocity, such as drift velocity.
