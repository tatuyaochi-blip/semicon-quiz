Semiconductor physics

Which is the correct unit of carrier mobility?

  1. Acm/s
  2. Bcm²/(V·s)
  3. CΩ·cm
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. cm²/(V·s)

Mobility is the drift velocity (cm/s) per unit electric field (V/cm), so its unit is (cm/s) ÷ (V/cm) = cm²/(V·s). cm/s is a unit of velocity, and Ω·cm is the unit of resistivity.

  • A. cm/s is a unit of velocity, such as drift velocity.
  • B. Correct. Velocity divided by electric field gives cm²/(V·s).
  • C. Ω·cm is the unit of resistivity.
