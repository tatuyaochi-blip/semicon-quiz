Semiconductor physics

What is the process called in which electrons and holes recombine through deep levels (traps) in the band gap?

  1. ASRH (Shockley-Read-Hall) recombination
  2. BRadiative recombination
  3. CAuger recombination
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. SRH (Shockley-Read-Hall) recombination

Recombination of electrons and holes in steps through deep levels created by metal impurities or crystal defects is called SRH recombination. In indirect-gap semiconductors such as silicon, it is the dominant recombination process and a major factor determining minority carrier lifetime.

  • A. Correct. Recombination through traps is SRH recombination.
  • B. Radiative recombination is the direct recombination of an electron and a hole with light emission, common in direct-gap semiconductors.
  • C. Auger recombination passes the released energy to another carrier and becomes significant at high carrier densities.
