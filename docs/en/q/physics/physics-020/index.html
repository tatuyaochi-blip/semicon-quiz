Which value is closest to the thermal voltage kT/q at room temperature (300 K)?

  1. AAbout 26 mV
  2. BAbout 0.26 mV
  3. CAbout 2.6 V
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. About 26 mV

With the Boltzmann constant k ≈ 1.38×10⁻²³ J/K, T = 300 K and the elementary charge q ≈ 1.6×10⁻¹⁹ C, kT/q ≈ 0.0259 V, or about 26 mV. This important value appears in many equations, such as the diode current equation and MOSFET subthreshold characteristics.
