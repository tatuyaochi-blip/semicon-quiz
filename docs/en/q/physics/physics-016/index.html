Semiconductor physics

What type of band structure does silicon have?

  1. ADirect band gap
  2. BIndirect band gap
  3. CMetallic, with no band gap
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. Indirect band gap

Silicon is an indirect-band-gap semiconductor: the bottom of the conduction band and the top of the valence band are at different wave numbers. Because radiative recombination requires the involvement of lattice vibrations (phonons), light emission is very inefficient, so direct-gap materials such as GaAs are used for LEDs and lasers.
