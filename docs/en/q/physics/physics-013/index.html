What does the Fermi level mean?

  1. AThe energy at the top of the valence band
  2. BThe energy at the bottom of the conduction band
  3. CThe energy at which the probability of occupation by an electron is exactly 1/2
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. The energy at which the probability of occupation by an electron is exactly 1/2

The Fermi level is the energy at which the occupation probability in the Fermi-Dirac distribution is 1/2. In an intrinsic semiconductor it lies near mid-gap; it moves toward the conduction band in n-type material and toward the valence band in p-type material. In thermal equilibrium, the Fermi levels of materials in contact line up.
