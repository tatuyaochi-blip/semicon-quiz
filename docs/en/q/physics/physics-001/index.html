Semiconductor physics

Which value is closest to the band gap of silicon (Si) at room temperature?

  1. AAbout 0.66 eV
  2. BAbout 1.12 eV
  3. CAbout 3.4 eV
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. About 1.12 eV

The band gap of silicon is about 1.12 eV at room temperature. About 0.66 eV is close to germanium (Ge), and about 3.4 eV is close to gallium nitride (GaN), which is classified as a wide-band-gap semiconductor.
