Semiconductor physics

Which of the following materials has the largest band gap?

  1. ASilicon dioxide (SiO2)
  2. BSilicon (Si)
  3. CGermanium (Ge)
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. Silicon dioxide (SiO2)

Silicon dioxide has a very large band gap of about 9 eV and behaves as an insulator. Silicon is about 1.12 eV and germanium about 0.66 eV. Because of its large band gap, SiO2 has been used for gate insulators and interlayer dielectrics.

  • A. Correct. SiO2 is an insulator with a large band gap of about 9 eV.
  • B. Silicon is about 1.12 eV, much smaller than SiO2.
  • C. Germanium is about 0.66 eV, the smallest of the three.
