Semiconductor physics

What happens to the intrinsic carrier density when the temperature of an intrinsic semiconductor is raised?

  1. AIt increases
  2. BIt decreases
  3. CIt does not change
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. It increases

As temperature rises, thermal energy makes it easier for electrons in the valence band to be excited across the band gap into the conduction band. More electron-hole pairs are created, so the intrinsic carrier density increases roughly exponentially.
