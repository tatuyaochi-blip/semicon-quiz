Semiconductor physics

What are the majority carriers in a p-type semiconductor?

  1. AIonized acceptors
  2. BHoles
  3. CElectrons
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. Holes

In p-type material acceptors take electrons from the valence band, creating holes, so holes are the majority carriers and electrons the minority carriers. Ionized acceptors are negative charges fixed in the lattice and cannot move. In a depletion region, however, these fixed charges are what create the electric field and built-in potential. A MOSFET forms an n-channel by gathering minority-carrier electrons at the surface of a p-type substrate (inversion).

  • A. Ionized acceptors are fixed charges that cannot move, so they are not carriers.
  • B. Correct. The majority carriers in p-type material are holes.
  • C. Electrons are the minority carriers in a p-type semiconductor.
