Semiconductor physics

Silicon contains both donors at 5×10¹⁶ cm⁻³ and acceptors at 2×10¹⁶ cm⁻³ (room temperature, all ionized). Which describes it correctly?

  1. Ap-type, with a hole concentration of about 2×10¹⁶ cm⁻³
  2. Bn-type, with an electron concentration of about 3×10¹⁶ cm⁻³
  3. Cn-type, with an electron concentration of about 7×10¹⁶ cm⁻³
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. n-type, with an electron concentration of about 3×10¹⁶ cm⁻³

When both donors and acceptors are present, they cancel each other out (compensation). Donors outnumber acceptors, so the material is n-type, and the electron concentration is the difference: 5×10¹⁶ − 2×10¹⁶ = 3×10¹⁶ cm⁻³. The conductivity type and concentration of a source or drain formed by implantation inside a well are determined the same way.
