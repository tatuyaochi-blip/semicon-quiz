Semiconductor physics

Silicon contains both donors at 5×10¹⁶ cm⁻³ and acceptors at 2×10¹⁶ cm⁻³ (room temperature, all ionized). Which describes it correctly?

  1. Ap-type, with a hole concentration of about 2×10¹⁶ cm⁻³
  2. Bn-type, with an electron concentration of about 3×10¹⁶ cm⁻³
  3. Cn-type, with an electron concentration of about 7×10¹⁶ cm⁻³
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. n-type, with an electron concentration of about 3×10¹⁶ cm⁻³

When both donors and acceptors are present they cancel each other (compensation), and the majority type wins. Donors dominate here, so the material is n-type with an electron density equal to the difference, 5×10¹⁶ − 2×10¹⁶ = 3×10¹⁶ cm⁻³. Mobility, however, is limited by scattering from all 7×10¹⁶ cm⁻³ ionized impurities, so it is lower than in uncompensated material with the same electron density. Forming sources and drains inside a well relies on this counter-doping to invert the conductivity type.
