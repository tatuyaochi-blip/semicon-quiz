Semiconductor physics

Which correctly compares electron and hole mobility in silicon?

  1. AElectron mobility is higher
  2. BHole mobility is higher
  3. CThey are always equal
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. Electron mobility is higher

In silicon at room temperature and low doping, electron mobility is about 1400 cm²/V·s and hole mobility about 450 cm²/V·s, so electrons are about three times faster, mainly because holes have a larger effective mass. An nMOS of the same size therefore carries more current, and in CMOS the pMOS is typically made 2–3 times wider than the nMOS to balance drive strength. Advanced processes boost pMOS mobility with compressive strain from SiGe.
