Cleanrooms and equipment

Which correctly describes a FOUP?

  1. AThe light source of an exposure tool
  2. BA machine that polishes wafers
  3. CA container that transports and stores 300 mm wafers sealed inside
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. A container that transports and stores 300 mm wafers sealed inside

A FOUP (front opening unified pod) is a front-opening container that seals and carries up to 25 300 mm wafers, weighing about 8 kg when loaded. Keeping wafers clean only inside FOUPs and tools prevents contamination without making the whole fab ultra-clean. Standardized dimensions and door mechanisms let it fit any vendor's tool, and it is moved automatically by overhead transport.
