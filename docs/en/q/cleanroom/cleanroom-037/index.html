Cleanrooms and equipment

Which type of cleanroom blows clean air from parts of the ceiling and maintains cleanliness by mixing with and diluting the room air? Its cleanliness is lower than with unidirectional flow, but its equipment cost is lower.

  1. ANon-unidirectional (turbulent) flow
  2. BDownflow (vertical unidirectional flow)
  3. CMini-environment
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. Non-unidirectional (turbulent) flow

Non-unidirectional (turbulent) flow introduces clean air through ceiling diffusers and dilutes particles by mixing before exhausting the air. It needs less airflow and costs less to build and run, but reaches lower cleanliness than unidirectional flow, typically ISO Class 6–8. In semiconductor plants it is used for back-end areas, anterooms and spaces outside minienvironments.

  • A. Correct. It works by mixing and diluting air.
  • B. Downflow sends air in one direction from the whole ceiling to the floor.
  • C. A mini-environment cleans only the small space around the wafers.
