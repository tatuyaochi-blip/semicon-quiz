Cleanrooms and equipment

What is the main reason wafers must not be touched with bare hands?

  1. ASodium and other substances in skin oil and sweat contaminate the wafer and degrade device characteristics and reliability
  2. BBody heat melts the wafer
  3. CFingerprints change the wafer's crystal orientation
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. Sodium and other substances in skin oil and sweat contaminate the wafer and degrade device characteristics and reliability

Skin oils and sweat contain organics and alkali metals such as sodium. Sodium becomes a mobile ion in oxide that shifts transistor threshold voltage, and organics cause deposition and bonding defects. Wafers are therefore handled with dedicated gloves, tweezers or robots. Even with gloves, only the edges are touched, never the surface.

  • A. Correct. Components of skin oil and sweat are contamination sources.
  • B. Body heat does not melt silicon.
  • C. Fingerprints do not change the crystal orientation.
