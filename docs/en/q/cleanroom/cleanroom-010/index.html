Cleanrooms and equipment

In 300 mm wafer fabs, which system automatically transports FOUPs between tools along rails on the ceiling?

  1. AManual cart transport
  2. BTransport by conveyor belts only
  3. COHT (overhead hoist transport)
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. OHT (overhead hoist transport)

A FOUP holding 25 300 mm wafers weighs about 8 kg, so manual handling is strenuous and risks particles and drops. An OHT is a vehicle running on ceiling rails that suspends FOUPs and delivers them automatically to tool load ports, freeing floor space for people and equipment. It is the core of the automated material handling system (AMHS), and large fabs run hundreds of vehicles or more.
