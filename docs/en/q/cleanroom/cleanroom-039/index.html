Cleanrooms and equipment

In cleanroom air conditioning, which unit filters outside air and adjusts its temperature and humidity before supplying it to the recirculating room air?

  1. ALoad port
  2. BScrubber
  3. CMakeup air unit (MAU)
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. Makeup air unit (MAU)

Cleanrooms take in outside air to maintain positive pressure and replace exhaust, but outside air varies greatly in temperature and humidity with the seasons and carries contaminants. A makeup air unit filters it and brings temperature and humidity to fixed values, while recirculated room air is cleaned by FFUs and fine-tuned for temperature. Concentrating humidity control in the MAU keeps room control stable.

  • A. A load port is where a FOUP is placed and handed to the tool.
  • B. A scrubber cleans exhaust gas.
  • C. Correct. It pretreats outside air centrally.
