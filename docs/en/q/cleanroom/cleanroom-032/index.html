Cleanrooms and equipment

Which item, placed on the floor at cleanroom entrances and elsewhere, removes dust stuck to shoe soles?

  1. AGrating
  2. BSticky mat (tacky mat)
  3. CFFU
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. Sticky mat (tacky mat)

A tacky mat has an adhesive surface that removes dust from shoe soles and cart wheels. When soiled, the top sheet is peeled off to expose a fresh one. Placed in anterooms and at cleanroom entrances, it reduces dirt carried in from outside. Workers also change into dedicated cleanroom shoes to reduce particles kicked up from the floor.

  • A. A grating is a perforated floor panel that lets air pass under the floor.
  • B. Correct. It removes dust from shoe soles with an adhesive surface.
  • C. An FFU is a unit that blows clean air from the ceiling.
