Cleanrooms and equipment

Which item, placed on the floor at cleanroom entrances and elsewhere, removes dust stuck to shoe soles?

  1. AGrating
  2. BSticky mat (tacky mat)
  3. CFFU
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. Sticky mat (tacky mat)

A sticky mat has an adhesive surface that removes dust from the shoe soles and cart wheels that step on it. When it gets dirty, the top sheet is peeled off to expose a fresh surface. Placed in anterooms and at cleanroom doorways, it reduces dirt carried in from outside.

  • A. A grating is a perforated floor panel that lets air pass under the floor.
  • B. Correct. It removes dust from shoe soles with an adhesive surface.
  • C. An FFU is a unit that blows clean air from the ceiling.
