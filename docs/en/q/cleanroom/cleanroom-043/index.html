Cleanrooms and equipment

In a high-cleanliness downflow cleanroom, which is closest to the typical number of times the room's air is replaced per hour (air changes per hour)?

  1. ASeveral hundred
  2. BOne
  3. CFive to ten
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. Several hundred

Downflow at about 0.4 m/s from the entire ceiling moves air about 1,440 m per hour, so a room 3–4 m high exchanges its air several hundred times an hour. Ordinary offices get only a few changes per hour, and 1 or 5–10 changes would be far from enough for high cleanliness. Cleanrooms consume large amounts of electricity to move air, a major share of fab power use.

  • A. Correct. It is far more than in ordinary buildings.
  • B. Once is not enough to remove particles.
  • C. That is close to ordinary buildings and too few for high cleanliness.
