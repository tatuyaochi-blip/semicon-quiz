Cleanrooms and equipment

Which items are strictly restricted from being brought into a cleanroom?

  1. ACleanroom paper (lint-free paper)
  2. BFOUPs
  3. CItems prone to shedding particles, such as ordinary paper and pencils
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. Items prone to shedding particles, such as ordinary paper and pencils

Ordinary paper sheds fibers and pencils produce shavings and graphite dust, so neither can be used in a cleanroom. Low-lint cleanroom paper and dedicated pens are used instead. Cosmetics and hair products may also be restricted because they generate particles and molecular contamination. Items brought in are wiped down or rebagged and passed through pass boxes. Cleanroom paper and FOUPs are permitted.
