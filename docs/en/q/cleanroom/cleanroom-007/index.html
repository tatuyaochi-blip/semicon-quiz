Cleanrooms and equipment

What is the main reason the air pressure inside a cleanroom is kept higher than its surroundings (positive pressure)?

  1. ATo raise the oxygen concentration in the room
  2. BTo reduce noise
  3. CTo keep dirty air from entering from outside
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. To keep dirty air from entering from outside

Keeping a cleanroom at positive pressure makes air flow outward through doorways and gaps, so dirty outside air has trouble getting in. Cleaner rooms are kept at higher pressure, with a cascade of pressure steps so air flows toward less clean zones. Areas handling toxic chemicals, conversely, are kept at lower pressure than their surroundings so nothing leaks out. It has nothing to do with oxygen concentration or noise.
