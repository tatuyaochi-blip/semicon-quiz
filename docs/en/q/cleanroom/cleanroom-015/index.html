Cleanrooms and equipment

Which unit at the front of process equipment opens FOUP doors and transfers wafers into the equipment while keeping them in a clean environment?

  1. AOHT
  2. BULPA filter
  3. CEFEM (equipment front-end module, including load ports and transfer robots)
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. EFEM (equipment front-end module, including load ports and transfer robots)

An EFEM (equipment front end module) houses the load ports for FOUPs, the door-opening mechanism and the robot that transfers wafers into the tool, all in a space kept clean by an FFU. Wafers move between FOUP and tool without exposure to room air, making it the heart of the minienvironment. OHTs move FOUPs between tools, and ULPA filters clean the air.
