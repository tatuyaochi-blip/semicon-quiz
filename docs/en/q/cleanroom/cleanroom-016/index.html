Cleanrooms and equipment

Which correctly describes the properties required of cleanroom suits?

  1. AVery high heat retention
  2. BA conspicuous color
  3. CLow particle shedding, such as lint, and keeping particles from the body from escaping
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. Low particle shedding, such as lint, and keeping particles from the body from escaping

Cleanroom suits are made of low-shedding fabrics woven from long filament fibers such as polyester and cover the whole body to keep skin flakes, hair and clothing fibers inside. Neck, wrists and ankles are snug, and many include conductive threads to prevent static. They are laundered in dedicated facilities. Warmth and conspicuous colors are not required properties.
