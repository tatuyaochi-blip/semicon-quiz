Cleanrooms and equipment

Which device supplies ions to the air in a cleanroom to neutralize charge on wafers and equipment?

  1. AULPA filter
  2. BIonizer (static eliminator)
  3. CScrubber
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. Ionizer (static eliminator)

Dry cleanroom air readily generates static, and charged wafers or masks attract particles or suffer discharge damage to devices and mask patterns. An ionizer creates positive and negative ions, for example by corona discharge, and supplies them to the air to neutralize charge on objects. It is especially effective for insulators that cannot be grounded. ULPA filters remove particles and scrubbers clean exhaust gas.
