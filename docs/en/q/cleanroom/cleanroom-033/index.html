Cleanrooms and equipment

Which statement about the amount of particles shed by cleanroom workers is correct?

  1. AWith a cleanroom suit it becomes zero regardless of how they move
  2. BIt is greater when sitting than when walking
  3. CIt increases with larger body movements, so workers avoid running and sudden motions
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. It increases with larger body movements, so workers avoid running and sudden motions

People constantly shed skin flakes and fibers, and the amount rises several- to more than tenfold from standing still to walking to vigorous motion. Cleanroom suits reduce what escapes but cannot stop it entirely, so cleanrooms also have behavioral rules: no running, no sudden movements, and no leaning over wafers.

  • A. Even with a cleanroom suit it does not become zero.
  • B. More is shed when moving.
  • C. Correct. Shedding increases with larger movements.
