Cleanrooms and equipment

Which is a major source of particles in cleanrooms, addressed by wearing cleanroom suits?

  1. AWorkers (people)
  2. BWafers
  3. CCeiling filters
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. Workers (people)

People constantly shed skin flakes, hair and clothing fibers, and walking alone can release millions of particles per minute, making them a major particle source. Workers therefore wear full-body cleanroom suits, masks and gloves and pass through air showers before entering. Automation that keeps people away from wafers is also a key way to reduce particles. Ceiling filters remove particles rather than generate them.
