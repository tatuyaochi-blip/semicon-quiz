Cleanrooms and equipment

Which is a major source of particles in cleanrooms, addressed by wearing cleanroom suits?

  1. AWorkers (people)
  2. BWafers
  3. CCeiling filters
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. Workers (people)

People constantly shed skin flakes, hair and clothing fibers, making them a major source of particles in cleanrooms. Workers therefore wear full-body cleanroom suits, masks and gloves, and pass through an air shower before entering. Automation is also an important way to reduce particle generation.
