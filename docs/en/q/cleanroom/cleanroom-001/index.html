Cleanrooms and equipment

How is the cleanliness of a cleanroom generally expressed?

  1. ABy the number of particles above a certain size in a given volume of air
  2. BBy the room's temperature and humidity
  3. CBy the brightness of the lighting
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. By the number of particles above a certain size in a given volume of air

Cleanroom cleanliness is expressed by the number of particles above a certain size in a given volume of air. The international standard ISO 14644-1 classifies cleanrooms into Classes 1 to 9 based on particles per m³; the smaller the number, the cleaner. Temperature and humidity are also controlled, but they are not measures of cleanliness.
