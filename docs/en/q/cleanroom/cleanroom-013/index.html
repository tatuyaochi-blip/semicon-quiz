Cleanrooms and equipment

What is used with precision equipment such as exposure tools to suppress the effects of vibration transmitted from the floor?

  1. AVibration isolation tables (such as active vibration isolation systems)
  2. BHumidifiers
  3. CFOUPs
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. Vibration isolation tables (such as active vibration isolation systems)

Precision tools such as scanners and electron microscopes lose accuracy from even slight vibration. They are mounted on isolation tables using springs, air springs, or active isolation systems that sense vibration and apply counteracting forces. Fab buildings themselves are designed for low vibration with thick floors and heavy columns, and vibration sources such as pumps are placed on separate floors. Humidifiers and FOUPs are not vibration countermeasures.
