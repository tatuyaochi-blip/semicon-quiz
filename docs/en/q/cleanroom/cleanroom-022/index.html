Cleanrooms and equipment

Which mini-environment method, widespread in the 200 mm era, carries cassettes in sealed pods and delivers them to equipment?

  1. AFOUP
  2. BSMIF
  3. CEFEM
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. SMIF

SMIF (standard mechanical interface) carries a wafer cassette in a sealed pod; on the tool, the pod's bottom opens downward to bring the cassette inside. Devised in the 1980s, it popularized the minienvironment concept. For 300 mm it evolved into the FOUP, which integrates cassette and pod and opens from the front. An EFEM is the tool front-end module that receives FOUPs.
