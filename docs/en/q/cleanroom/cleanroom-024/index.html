Cleanrooms and equipment

For process equipment using vacuum, which device treats exhaust gas containing harmful substances produced by reactions, tool by tool?

  1. AAbatement system (local scrubber)
  2. BFFU
  3. CEFEM
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. Abatement system (local scrubber)

Exhaust gas from deposition and etching equipment contains unreacted specialty gases and harmful by-products. An abatement system near the equipment renders them harmless by combustion, thermal decomposition, adsorption, water scrubbing and the like before sending them to the fab exhaust system.
