Cleanrooms and equipment

What is the main reason conductive (static-dissipative) materials are used for cleanroom floors and workbenches?

  1. ATo make the floor shine brightly
  2. BTo prevent devices from being destroyed by electrostatic discharge and particles from being attracted and stuck by static electricity
  3. CTo heat the floor by passing electricity through it
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. To prevent devices from being destroyed by electrostatic discharge and particles from being attracted and stuck by static electricity

Cleanrooms are full of plastic containers and garments and are dry, so static readily builds up. Discharge (ESD) from charged objects or people can destroy devices, and charged wafers attract particles. Conductive floors and work surfaces, grounded wrist straps and conductive shoes are combined to drain charge to ground. Appearance or floor heating is not the reason.

  • A. It is not for appearance.
  • B. Correct. It keeps static charge from building up.
  • C. It is not for floor heating.
