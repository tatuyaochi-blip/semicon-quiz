Cleanrooms and equipment

What is the approach called that keeps only the small space around wafers at high cleanliness?

  1. ABallroom method
  2. BMini-environment method
  3. CDownflow stop method
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. Mini-environment method

The minienvironment approach keeps only the spaces wafers touch, such as FOUPs and tool interiors, ultra-clean while relaxing cleanliness where people work. Since the volume to be kept clean shrinks dramatically, construction and operating costs drop compared with making an entire room ultra-clean. Separating people from wafers also reduces human contamination.
