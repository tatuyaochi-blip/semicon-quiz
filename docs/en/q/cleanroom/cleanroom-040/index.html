Cleanrooms and equipment

In a fab's automated material handling system, which automated storage holds FOUPs temporarily until their next process step?

  1. AStocker
  2. BEFEM
  3. CAir shower
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. Stocker

A stocker is an automated warehouse with shelves that a robot loads and unloads, temporarily storing FOUPs waiting for tools. Kept in clean air or nitrogen, it is linked to transport systems such as OHTs and releases FOUPs as directed by the manufacturing execution system. Recently, small storage racks beside the ceiling rails have also come into use.

  • A. Correct. It is an automated warehouse for temporarily storing FOUPs.
  • B. An EFEM is the module at the front of a tool that transfers wafers.
  • C. An air shower removes dust from clothing on entry.
