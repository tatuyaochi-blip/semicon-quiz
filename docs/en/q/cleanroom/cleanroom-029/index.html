Cleanrooms and equipment

Which element can be released from glass-fiber filters and similar sources and cause unintended doping (contamination) of wafers?

  1. ABoron
  2. BGold
  3. CIron
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. Boron

HEPA filters using glass fiber and similar sources can release boron-containing gases, which may adhere to wafer surfaces and cause unintended doping as a p-type impurity. Low-boron filters and chemical filters are therefore used.
