Cleanrooms and equipment

Which instrument is used to measure the number and size of fine particles in cleanroom air?

  1. ALight-scattering particle counter
  2. BFour-point probe
  3. CEllipsometer
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. Light-scattering particle counter

An optical particle counter draws in air and shines a laser through it; the intensity of light scattered by each particle gives its size, and the number of light pulses gives the count. It counts particles down to about 0.1 µm and is used to classify cleanrooms by measuring at specified locations and sample volumes, as well as for continuous monitoring. Liquid particle counters are used for ultrapure water and chemicals.

  • A. Correct. It counts particles by scattered light.
  • B. A four-point probe measures the resistivity of films and wafers.
  • C. An ellipsometer measures thin-film thickness and refractive index.
