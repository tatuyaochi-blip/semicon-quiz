Cleanrooms and equipment

What is the main purpose of replacing (purging) the inside of a FOUP with nitrogen?

  1. ATo suppress oxidation of wafer surfaces and adhesion of moisture and molecular contamination
  2. BTo make the FOUP heavier
  3. CTo cool the wafers
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. To suppress oxidation of wafer surfaces and adhesion of moisture and molecular contamination

Air inside a FOUP lets wafer surfaces oxidize and pick up moisture and molecular contamination while waiting between steps. Purging with nitrogen reduces oxygen and moisture and preserves the surface. It also keeps gases outgassing from freshly etched wafers from building up and contaminating other wafers, which matters increasingly in advanced processes. Weight and cooling are not the purpose.
