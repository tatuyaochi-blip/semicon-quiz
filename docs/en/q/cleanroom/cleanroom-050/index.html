Cleanrooms and equipment

When installing electron-beam lithography systems, electron microscopes and the like, which environmental factor needs special attention besides vibration?

  1. AOutside noise
  2. BMagnetic field fluctuations from nearby power cables, elevators and the like
  3. CThe color of the lighting
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. Magnetic field fluctuations from nearby power cables, elevators and the like

Being charged, an electron beam is deflected by the Lorentz force from even slight magnetic field fluctuations, causing placement errors in writing and blur or jitter in images. Variations thousands of times smaller than Earth's field can matter. Tools are therefore sited away from high-current cables, transformers and elevators, and magnetic shielding or active field-cancellation systems are used. Lighting color has no effect.

  • A. Noise can also cause vibration, but it is not a factor specific to electron beams.
  • B. Correct. Magnetic field fluctuations deflect the electron beam.
  • C. Lighting color does not affect the electron beam.
