Cleanrooms and equipment

Which statement about ISO 14644-1 cleanliness classes is correct?

  1. AThe larger the number, the cleaner
  2. BThey are defined by particle weight rather than particle count
  3. CEach step down in the class number N cuts the allowed particle count by about a factor of 10
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. Each step down in the class number N cuts the allowed particle count by about a factor of 10

In ISO 14644-1, ISO Class N limits particles of 0.1 µm and larger to 10 to the power N per cubic meter (limits for other sizes follow from a formula). For example, Class 5 allows 100,000 and Class 4 allows 10,000, so the smaller the number, the cleaner the room.

  • A. The smaller the number, the cleaner.
  • B. They are defined by particle number concentration.
  • C. Correct. The particle limit changes tenfold per class.
