Cleanrooms and equipment

Which facility at a cleanroom entrance blows strong jets of clean air at workers to remove dust from the surface of their clothing?

  1. APass box
  2. BFume hood
  3. CAir shower
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. Air shower

An air shower is a small room workers pass through when entering a cleanroom, blowing high-velocity clean air from all sides to remove dust from the surface of cleanroom suits. A small box-shaped facility used to pass items in and out is called a pass box.
