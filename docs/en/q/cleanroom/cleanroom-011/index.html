Cleanrooms and equipment

Which facility at a cleanroom entrance blows strong jets of clean air at workers to remove dust from the surface of their clothing?

  1. APass box
  2. BFume hood
  3. CAir shower
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. Air shower

An air shower is a small room workers pass through when entering a cleanroom; nozzles on all sides blast clean air at over 20 m/s to remove dust from cleanroom suits. Interlocked doors that never open simultaneously also block outside air. A small box for passing materials is a pass box, and a fume hood is a bench that exhausts chemical vapors.
