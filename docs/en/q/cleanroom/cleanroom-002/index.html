Cleanrooms and equipment

What is the main reason yellow lighting is used in lithography areas?

  1. ATo prevent eye strain for workers
  2. BTo exclude the short-wavelength light to which photoresist is sensitive
  3. CTo prevent static electricity
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. To exclude the short-wavelength light to which photoresist is sensitive

Photoresists respond to ultraviolet and blue light, roughly wavelengths shorter than about 500 nm. Ordinary white lighting contains such light, so yellow lighting with short wavelengths filtered out is used to prevent unintended exposure between coating and development. Such areas are called yellow rooms. The purpose is not to reduce eye strain or static.
