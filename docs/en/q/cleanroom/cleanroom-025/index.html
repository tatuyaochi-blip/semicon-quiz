Cleanrooms and equipment

Which equipment is installed in semiconductor fabs to prevent loss of wafers in process due to voltage sags or power outages?

  1. AULPA filters
  2. BUninterruptible power supplies (UPS) and voltage-sag protection equipment
  3. CFOUPs
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. Uninterruptible power supplies (UPS) and voltage-sag protection equipment

Fab tools can stop on a voltage sag lasting only about 0.1 seconds, scrapping in-process wafers and requiring long restarts to restore vacuum and temperature. Sags from lightning and similar causes are not rare, so critical tools and utilities are protected by uninterruptible power supplies or sag-compensation equipment. Filters and FOUPs are not power countermeasures.
