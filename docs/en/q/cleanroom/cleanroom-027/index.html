Cleanrooms and equipment

What is a typical guideline for the air velocity blown from the ceiling in downflow cleanrooms?

  1. AAbout 30 m/s
  2. BAbout 0.3–0.5 m/s
  3. CAbout 0.001 m/s
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. About 0.3–0.5 m/s

In downflow cleanrooms, a slow unidirectional airflow of about 0.3–0.5 m/s flows from the ceiling. Too fast causes turbulence that stirs up particles, and too slow cannot carry particles away adequately, so an appropriate velocity is maintained.
