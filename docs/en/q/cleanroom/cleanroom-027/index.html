Cleanrooms and equipment

What is a typical guideline for the air velocity blown from the ceiling in downflow cleanrooms?

  1. AAbout 30 m/s
  2. BAbout 0.3–0.5 m/s
  3. CAbout 0.001 m/s
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. About 0.3–0.5 m/s

Downflow cleanrooms supply a gentle unidirectional flow of about 0.3–0.5 m/s from the ceiling. Too fast, and turbulence around people and tools stirs up particles; too slow, and thermal updrafts overcome the flow so particles are not carried away. 30 m/s would be typhoon-force, and at 0.001 m/s the air barely moves.
