Advanced transistors (FinFET and GAA)

In highly scaled transistors, which factors are becoming increasingly important limits on performance?

  1. AParasitic resistance of source, drain and contacts, and parasitic capacitance around the gate
  2. BWafer diameter
  3. CPackage color
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. Parasitic resistance of source, drain and contacts, and parasitic capacitance around the gate

As transistors shrink, the resistance of the source, drain and contacts grows as a share compared with the channel resistance itself. Narrower spacing between gate and contacts also increases parasitic capacitance. Because these limit performance, materials and structures continue to be improved.
