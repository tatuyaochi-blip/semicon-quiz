Advanced transistors (FinFET and GAA)

What is the main purpose of growing raised crystal on the fins in the source and drain of a FinFET by epitaxial growth?

  1. ATo make the transistor emit light
  2. BTo lengthen the gate
  3. CTo reduce parasitic resistance and apply strain to the channel
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. To reduce parasitic resistance and apply strain to the channel

Fins are only a few nm wide, so source/drain resistance would be high and contact area small. Epitaxially growing diamond-shaped raised crystals enlarges the cross-section, lowering resistance and easing contact formation. pMOS uses Ge-rich SiGe and nMOS uses heavily phosphorus-doped SiP; their lattice mismatch also strains the channel to boost mobility.
