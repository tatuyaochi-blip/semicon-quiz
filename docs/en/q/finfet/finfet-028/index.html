Advanced transistors (FinFET and GAA)

Which technique covers the gate with insulator so that a slightly misaligned contact does not short to the gate?

  1. ALDD
  2. BTSV
  3. CSelf-aligned contact (SAC)
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. Self-aligned contact (SAC)

Capping the gate's top and sides with nitride means that if a contact etch lands over the gate due to misalignment, it stops on the nitride—etched far more slowly than oxide—and never reaches the gate. Self-aligned contacts remove the margin otherwise reserved for overlay error, tightening gate-to-contact spacing, and lead to techniques such as contact-over-active-gate (COAG) that further shrink cells.
