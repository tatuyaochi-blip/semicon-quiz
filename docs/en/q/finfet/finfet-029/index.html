Advanced transistors (FinFET and GAA)

Why are SiGe and germanium considered or adopted instead of silicon as pMOS channel materials?

  1. ABecause their hole mobility is high
  2. BBecause their electron mobility is very low
  3. CBecause the materials are cheap
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. Because their hole mobility is high

Germanium and SiGe have higher hole mobility than silicon, so they increase pMOS drive current. SiGe-channel pMOS also helps adjust the threshold voltage and has been adopted in leading-edge processes.
