Advanced transistors (FinFET and GAA)

In nanosheet GAA transistors now in production, how many nanosheets are typically stacked in one transistor?

  1. A1
  2. BAbout 3
  3. CAbout 100
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. About 3

Current nanosheet GAA transistors typically stack about three sheets. More sheets increase current in the same footprint, but lower sheets suffer more from distance to the source/drain and parasitic capacitance, and fabrication gets harder, so the count balances benefit against difficulty.

  • A. A single sheet would forgo the current gain from stacking.
  • B. Correct. About three is typical.
  • C. Stacking 100 sheets is not realistic for logic transistors.
