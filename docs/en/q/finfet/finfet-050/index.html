Advanced transistors (FinFET and GAA)

In a FinFET made in the usual orientation on a (100) wafer, the fin sidewalls that form the channel are mainly (110) planes. What is the effect on mobility?

  1. ANeither electron nor hole mobility changes
  2. BCompared with the (100) plane, hole mobility tends to rise and electron mobility tends to fall
  3. CBoth electron and hole mobility increase greatly
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. Compared with the (100) plane, hole mobility tends to rise and electron mobility tends to fall

Silicon mobility depends on crystal plane and current direction: on (110) surfaces hole mobility is higher (about twice) and electron mobility lower than on (100). In FinFETs most current flows along the sidewalls, an orientation that favors pMOS and penalizes nMOS; the nMOS deficit is offset with strain engineering. As a result, the nMOS-pMOS performance gap is smaller in FinFETs than in planar devices.

  • A. Mobility does change with the crystal plane.
  • B. Correct. Holes tend to benefit and electrons to lose.
  • C. Electron mobility actually tends to drop on (110) planes.
