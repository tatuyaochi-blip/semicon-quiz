Advanced transistors (FinFET and GAA)

What is the state called in which the channel is so short that carriers travel from source to drain almost without scattering?

  1. ADiffusive transport
  2. BHopping transport
  3. CBallistic transport
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. Ballistic transport

When the channel length becomes comparable to or shorter than the carriers' mean free path, carriers move from source to drain with almost no scattering by lattice vibrations or impurities. This is called ballistic transport, and the current is then set not by mobility but by factors such as the injection velocity from the source.

  • A. Diffusive transport is ordinary conduction with repeated scattering.
  • B. Hopping transport is carriers jumping between localized states, seen in amorphous materials and the like.
  • C. Correct. Carriers pass through without scattering.
