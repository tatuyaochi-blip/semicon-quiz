Advanced transistors (FinFET and GAA)

What is an effect of quantum confinement when fins or nanosheets are thinned to a few nanometers?

  1. AThe effective band gap increases and the threshold voltage shifts (thickness variation becomes threshold voltage variation)
  2. BThe charge of the electron increases
  3. CSilicon turns into a metal
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. The effective band gap increases and the threshold voltage shifts (thickness variation becomes threshold voltage variation)

When the channel is only a few nanometers thick, electron energies are quantized into discrete levels and the lowest level rises, enlarging the effective band gap. The threshold voltage shifts as a result, and small variations in thickness become variations in threshold voltage.

  • A. Correct. The effective band gap grows and the threshold voltage changes.
  • B. The charge of a single electron does not change.
  • C. Silicon does not become a metal.
