Advanced transistors (FinFET and GAA)

Which company and generation first put FinFETs (called Tri-Gate by Intel) into volume-production logic products?

  1. AIntel's 22 nm generation (around 2011–2012)
  2. BTSMC's 90 nm generation (around 2004)
  3. CSamsung's 2 nm generation (around 2025)
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. Intel's 22 nm generation (around 2011–2012)

Intel adopted FinFETs (which it called Tri-Gate) for volume production at the 22 nm generation, shipping products from around 2012. TSMC and Samsung later adopted FinFETs at the 16/14 nm generation, completing the move away from planar transistors.

  • A. Correct. Intel was first to adopt them in volume at 22 nm.
  • B. The 90 nm generation was the era of planar transistors.
  • C. At the 2 nm generation, the industry is moving to GAA.
