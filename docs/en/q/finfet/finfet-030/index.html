Advanced transistors (FinFET and GAA)

Which 3D integration method builds transistor layers one after another on the wafer, without bonding chips?

  1. A2.5D integration
  2. BMonolithic 3D integration (sequential 3D integration)
  3. CSiP
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. Monolithic 3D integration (sequential 3D integration)

Monolithic (sequential) 3D integration builds a second transistor layer on top of the first by forming or transferring a thin semiconductor layer. Tiers connect through vias tens of nm in size—over ten thousand times denser than TSV-based die stacking. Processing the upper tier must stay below about 500 °C to avoid damaging the lower devices and wiring. CFETs built sequentially are one form of this approach.
