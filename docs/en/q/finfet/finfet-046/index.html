Advanced transistors (FinFET and GAA)

Which transistor has a source, channel and drain of the same conductivity type and doping, with no PN junctions?

  1. ABipolar transistor
  2. BIGBT
  3. CJunctionless transistor
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. Junctionless transistor

A junctionless transistor dopes a thin silicon nanowire uniformly and heavily from source to drain and turns off by depleting the channel from the surrounding gate—essentially a gate-pinched resistor. With no need for abrupt PN junctions over nanometer distances, it scales easily; it was reported in 2010. Heavy channel doping lowers mobility, however, and variations in cross-section cause large threshold variability.

  • A. A bipolar transistor has two PN junctions.
  • B. An IGBT has a structure with multiple PN junctions.
  • C. Correct. It is a transistor without PN junctions.
