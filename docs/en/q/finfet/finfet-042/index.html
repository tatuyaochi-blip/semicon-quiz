Advanced transistors (FinFET and GAA)

As physical scaling becomes harder, what is the practice of considering design and process technology together to optimize area and performance called?

  1. AMoore's law
  2. BDTCO (design-technology co-optimization)
  3. CWafer-level test
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. DTCO (design-technology co-optimization)

DTCO (design-technology co-optimization) considers transistor and interconnect process technology together with design rules for standard cells, SRAM and so on, improving chip area, performance and power through measures such as reducing cell height and smarter routing. It has grown in importance at leading-edge generations, where dimensional shrinking alone is no longer enough.

  • A. Moore's law is an empirical rule about integration density, not the name of a practice.
  • B. Correct. Design and process are optimized together.
  • C. Wafer-level test means testing chips while still on the wafer.
