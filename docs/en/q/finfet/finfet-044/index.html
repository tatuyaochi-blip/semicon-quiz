Advanced transistors (FinFET and GAA)

Which structure places nMOS and pMOS nanosheets close together on either side of a thin insulating wall to reduce cell area?

  1. AFD-SOI
  2. BPlanar MOSFET
  3. CForksheet FET
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. Forksheet FET

A forksheet FET places nMOS and pMOS nanosheets on both sides of a dielectric wall, giving a shape like the tines of a fork. The n-to-p spacing can be greatly reduced, shrinking standard cell and SRAM area. It is being studied as a candidate generation between GAA and CFET.

  • A. FD-SOI is a planar structure with a thin silicon layer on an insulator.
  • B. A planar device is the conventional structure with a flat channel.
  • C. Correct. n and p sheets sit on either side of a dielectric wall.
