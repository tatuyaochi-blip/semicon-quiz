Advanced transistors (FinFET and GAA)

Which is considered to show the real degree of logic scaling better than the process generation (node) name?

  1. AThe wafer diameter
  2. BActual dimensions such as gate pitch (CPP) and minimum metal pitch
  3. CThe chip's selling price
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. Actual dimensions such as gate pitch (CPP) and minimum metal pitch

Through the 1990s node names roughly matched physical dimensions such as gate length, but since FinFETs names like "3 nm" merely denote a generation of performance and density; actual gate lengths exceed ten-odd nm. Real density is compared using contacted poly pitch (CPP, just under 50 nm at 3 nm), minimum metal pitch (in the 20 nm range), SRAM cell area and transistor density per mm².
