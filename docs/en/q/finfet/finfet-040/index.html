Advanced transistors (FinFET and GAA)

Which correctly describes the transistor performance metric "Ion/Ioff ratio"?

  1. AThe ratio of on-state current to off-state leakage current; the larger, the better the switch
  2. BThe ratio of gate length to gate width
  3. CThe ratio of supply voltage to threshold voltage
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. The ratio of on-state current to off-state leakage current; the larger, the better the switch

The Ion/Ioff ratio compares on-state current to off-state leakage, about 10⁴–10⁶ in advanced logic; higher means a better switch that is fast yet leaks little in standby. Lowering supply voltage forces a trade-off: keep Vth and lose Ion, or lower Vth and raise Ioff. FinFETs and GAA, with steeper subthreshold swings, deliver more Ion at the same Ioff.

  • A. Correct. It is the ratio of on-current to off-current.
  • B. It is not a ratio of dimensions.
  • C. It is not a ratio of voltages.
