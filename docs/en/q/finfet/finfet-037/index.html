Advanced transistors (FinFET and GAA)

To adequately suppress short-channel effects in a FinFET, what is generally required of the fin width?

  1. AIt can be chosen independently of the gate length
  2. BMake it much wider than the gate length
  3. CMake it thinner than the gate length
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. Make it thinner than the gate length

The thinner the fin, the better the field from the gates on both sides reaches its center, making it easier for the gate to control the whole channel. As a guideline, the fin width must be about half the gate length or less, and in leading-edge generations it has shrunk to a few nanometers.

  • A. Fin width strongly affects suppression of short-channel effects.
  • B. Too thick a fin leaves the center uncontrolled by the gate, increasing leakage.
  • C. Correct. A thin fin strengthens gate control.
