Advanced transistors (FinFET and GAA)

In very small transistors, what is the phenomenon called in which the threshold voltage varies because of statistical variation in the number and positions of dopant atoms in the channel?

  1. ARandom dopant fluctuation (RDF)
  2. BElectromigration
  3. CTime-dependent dielectric breakdown (TDDB)
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. Random dopant fluctuation (RDF)

As transistors shrink, the channel contains only tens to hundreds of dopant atoms, and random variation in their number and placement strongly affects the threshold voltage. FinFETs and FD-SOI, which allow lightly doped channels, have an advantage in this respect too.
