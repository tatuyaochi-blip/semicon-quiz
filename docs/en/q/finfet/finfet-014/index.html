Advanced transistors (FinFET and GAA)

In very small transistors, what is the phenomenon called in which the threshold voltage varies because of statistical variation in the number and positions of dopant atoms in the channel?

  1. ARandom dopant fluctuation (RDF)
  2. BElectromigration
  3. CTime-dependent dielectric breakdown (TDDB)
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. Random dopant fluctuation (RDF)

As transistors shrink, their channels contain only tens to hundreds of dopant atoms, and random variation in their number and placement scatters threshold voltage by tens of mV. Variation scales roughly as 1/√(gate area) (Pelgrom's law) and is especially troublesome in circuits like SRAM that use many minimum-size devices. FinFETs and FD-SOI, which allow undoped channels, are advantageous here.
