Advanced transistors (FinFET and GAA)

In a CFET (complementary FET), which statement about connecting to the lower of the two stacked transistors is correct?

  1. AThe lower transistor has no gate, so only its source and drain need connections
  2. BBecause the upper transistor sits directly above it, front-side connections must be routed around the upper device, and connecting to the lower device directly from the backside is being studied
  3. CThe upper and lower transistors share all their sources and drains, so the lower device needs no separate connection
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. Because the upper transistor sits directly above it, front-side connections must be routed around the upper device, and connecting to the lower device directly from the backside is being studied

A CFET (complementary FET) stacks nMOS and pMOS vertically to shrink cell area. The gates and output can be shared, but each source must connect to a different supply (VDD and VSS). Reaching the lower device from the front side requires deep contacts routed around the upper transistor, crowding wiring in a small cell. Connecting directly to the lower device from the wafer backside, combined with backside power delivery, is therefore being studied; at IEDM 2023 Intel reported a working CFET inverter with direct backside contacts to the lower device.

  • A. The lower transistor also has a gate, often shared with the upper one.
  • B. Correct. Access to the lower device becomes the wiring bottleneck.
  • C. Even in an inverter, the nMOS and pMOS sources connect to different supplies.
