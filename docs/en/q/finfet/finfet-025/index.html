Advanced transistors (FinFET and GAA)

In nanosheet GAA, what is the main role of the insulator (inner spacer) placed between the gate and the source/drain between the sheets?

  1. ATo reduce parasitic capacitance between the gate and source/drain and prevent shorts
  2. BTo make the channel emit light
  3. CTo amplify current
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. To reduce parasitic capacitance between the gate and source/drain and prevent shorts

In nanosheet GAA, the gate fills the gaps between sheets and faces the source/drain only a few nm away. Inner spacers are formed by recessing the ends of the SiGe layers and filling the cavities with a dielectric such as nitride, reducing gate-to-source/drain capacitance and preventing shorts. They also protect the source/drain as an etch stop when the SiGe is removed.
