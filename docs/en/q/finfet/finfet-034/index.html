Advanced transistors (FinFET and GAA)

What does "nanosheet" refer to in a nanosheet GAA transistor?

  1. AA sheet-like film that protects the chip
  2. BA thin, plate-shaped silicon channel a few nanometers thick, stacked vertically
  3. CA thin metal plate used as the gate insulator
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. A thin, plate-shaped silicon channel a few nanometers thick, stacked vertically

A nanosheet is a thin, plate-shaped silicon channel a few nanometers thick and tens of nanometers wide. Several are stacked vertically, each surrounded on all sides by the gate. It is like turning FinFET fins on their side and stacking them.

  • A. It is not a protective film.
  • B. Correct. It is a stack of thin, plate-shaped channels.
  • C. The gate insulator is an insulator such as high-k, not a metal plate.
