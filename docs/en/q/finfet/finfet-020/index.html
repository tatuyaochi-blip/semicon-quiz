Advanced transistors (FinFET and GAA)

Which correctly describes materials such as molybdenum disulfide (MoS2), researched as channel materials for future ultra-small transistors?

  1. AThey are metals a few mm thick
  2. BThey are two-dimensional materials that act as semiconductors even a few atomic layers thick
  3. CThey are insulators that do not conduct electricity
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. They are two-dimensional materials that act as semiconductors even a few atomic layers thick

Transition metal dichalcogenides such as MoS2 keep their semiconductor properties even as 2D materials a few atomic layers thick. Silicon's mobility drops greatly when it is made extremely thin, so these materials are being studied as candidates for future ultra-thin channels.
