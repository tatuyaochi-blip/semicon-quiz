Advanced transistors (FinFET and GAA)

In high-k/metal gate transistors, which is one of the main ways to adjust the threshold voltage?

  1. AChoosing the work function of the metal used for the gate electrode
  2. BChanging the wafer diameter
  3. CChanging the package material
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. Choosing the work function of the metal used for the gate electrode

With a metal gate, the threshold voltage depends on the work function of the gate metal. Metals with suitable work functions are used for nMOS and pMOS, and thin metal layers are stacked to tune the work function, achieving the target threshold voltages.
