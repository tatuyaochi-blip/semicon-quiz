Advanced transistors (FinFET and GAA)

Which technique, used to make the thin fins of FinFETs, uses sidewall spacers to create patterns finer than the lithography limit?

  1. ASADP/SAQP (self-aligned double/quadruple patterning)
  2. BOPC
  3. CCMP
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. SADP/SAQP (self-aligned double/quadruple patterning)

In SADP, a film is deposited on the sidewalls of a core pattern to form spacers, and the core is then removed, producing thin lines at twice the density of the original pattern. Repeating this twice (SAQP) gives four times the density. It is used to form regular fine patterns such as fins and wiring.
