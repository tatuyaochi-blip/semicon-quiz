Advanced transistors (FinFET and GAA)

Which correctly describes the structure of a FinFET?

  1. AThe gate wraps around a fin-shaped channel on three sides
  2. BA planar gate covers only the top of the channel
  3. CMetal contacts the channel directly with no gate insulator
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. The gate wraps around a fin-shaped channel on three sides

A FinFET is a 3D transistor in which silicon is formed into a thin, upright fin, with the gate surrounding both sides and the top. Because the gate controls the channel from three sides, short-channel effects are easier to suppress than in planar transistors, and off-state leakage is reduced.
