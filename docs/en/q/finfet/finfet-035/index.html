Advanced transistors (FinFET and GAA)

What does the "k" in "high-k gate dielectric" represent?

  1. ACrystal hardness
  2. BThermal conductivity
  3. CRelative permittivity (dielectric constant)
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. Relative permittivity (dielectric constant)

The k in high-k denotes the relative permittivity, and high-k materials are insulators with a higher relative permittivity than SiO2 (about 3.9). A typical example is hafnium-based oxide (relative permittivity around 20), which gives a large gate capacitance even with a physically thick film.

  • A. It does not stand for hardness.
  • B. It does not stand for thermal conductivity.
  • C. Correct. k represents the relative permittivity.
