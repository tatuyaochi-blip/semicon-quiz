Automotive Semiconductors and Functional Safety

Which correctly describes the ASIL in ISO 26262?

  1. AIt ranges from A to D, with ASIL A representing the most stringent requirements. QM represents even more stringent requirements than ASIL D
  2. BIt ranges from A to D, with ASIL D representing the most stringent requirements. When the risk is sufficiently low, the classification is QM and it is handled by ordinary quality management
  3. CIt ranges from A to D, with ASIL D representing the components with the highest failure rate. QM represents components with no quality issues, for which all testing may be skipped
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. It ranges from A to D, with ASIL D representing the most stringent requirements. When the risk is sufficiently low, the classification is QM and it is handled by ordinary quality management

ASIL (Automotive Safety Integrity Level) has four levels, A, B, C and D, with D the most stringent, imposing high requirements on development processes, safety mechanisms, failure rate targets and so on. For example, parts of airbag, brake and steering control are typical cases that often require ASIL D. When the risk is low and no ASIL needs to be assigned, the classification is QM (Quality Management) and development is carried out under ordinary quality management. Quality still has to be assured under QM, and testing cannot be skipped.
