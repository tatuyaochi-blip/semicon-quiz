IC design flow and EDA

Which set correctly gives the three metrics represented by "PPA", often used to evaluate IC designs?

  1. APressure, power and angle
  2. BPerformance, power and area
  3. CPrice, profit and advertising
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. Performance, power and area

PPA stands for performance (such as speed), power and area, the basic metrics for comparing designs and processes. They usually trade off: faster cells raise performance but add power and area. The balance depends on the application, prioritizing power for smartphones and performance for servers. Area translates directly into cost through the number of dies per wafer.

  • A. It is not pressure or angle.
  • B. Correct. Performance, power and area.
  • C. Price and profit are not the design metric PPA.
