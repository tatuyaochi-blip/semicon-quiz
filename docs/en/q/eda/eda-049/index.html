LSI design flow and EDA

In analog circuit design, which technique repeatedly simulates with randomly applied transistor variations from manufacturing to estimate performance distributions and yield?

  1. AMonte Carlo simulation
  2. BLogic synthesis
  3. CDicing
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. Monte Carlo simulation

Monte Carlo simulation repeats circuit simulation hundreds or thousands of times while randomly varying parameters such as threshold voltage according to the PDK's variation models. The resulting distributions of offset voltage, gain and so on give the fraction meeting spec (parametric yield). For circuits like SRAM cells, where not one in millions may fail, techniques for efficiently estimating rare failures are also used.

  • A. Correct. It repeats simulations with random variations applied.
  • B. Logic synthesis generates gate-level circuits from RTL.
  • C. Dicing separates the wafer into chips.
