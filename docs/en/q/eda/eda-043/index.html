IC design flow and EDA

Which set correctly names cells with no logic function (or auxiliary ones) placed among standard cells to fill gaps, tie well potentials and suppress supply noise?

  1. ASRAM, DRAM and flash
  2. BFiller cells, tap cells and decoupling capacitor cells
  3. CFlip-flops, adders and multipliers
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. Filler cells, tap cells and decoupling capacitor cells

Filler cells fill gaps in cell rows to keep wells and power rails continuous, tap cells tie wells and substrate to fixed potentials to prevent latch-up, and decap cells suppress supply noise with capacitance between power and ground. None provides logic function, but all are required in the layout. Separating taps out lets the logic cells themselves be smaller. Memories and arithmetic units are functional circuits.

  • A. These are types of memory.
  • B. Correct. They are auxiliary cells with no logic function.
  • C. These are cells and circuits with logic functions.
