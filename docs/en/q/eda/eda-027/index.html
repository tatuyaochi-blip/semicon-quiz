IC design flow and EDA

In logic synthesis and static timing analysis, what is the description of timing conditions such as clock period and I/O delays called?

  1. APDK
  2. BTiming constraints (SDC, etc.)
  3. CGDSII
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. Timing constraints (SDC, etc.)

Timing constraints specify clock periods and waveforms, I/O port delays, and paths to exclude (false paths) or allow multiple cycles, typically written in SDC format. Tools optimize and verify the design against them. Wrong constraints can yield a chip that passes every tool check yet fails in silicon. A PDK is a design kit and GDSII a layout format.
