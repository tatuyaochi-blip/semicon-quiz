LSI design flow and EDA

Which approach prefabricates a common base (master) covered with transistors and designs and manufactures only the wiring layers for each product?

  1. AGate array
  2. BFull custom
  3. CPDK
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. Gate array

In a gate array, layers up through the transistors are made in advance as a common master, and only the wiring masks are designed per product. Fewer masks and shorter development made it popular for low-volume, high-mix LSIs. Today it has largely given way to FPGAs, structured ASICs and the like.

  • A. Correct. Only the wiring layers are designed per product.
  • B. Full custom designs every layer.
  • C. A PDK is the set of design data provided by a foundry.
