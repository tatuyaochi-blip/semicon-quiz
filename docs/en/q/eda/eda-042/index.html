IC design flow and EDA

What is it called to reflect small fixes found late in design or during manufacturing by partially modifying the existing layout, without redoing the whole design?

  1. ATape-out
  2. BFloorplan
  3. CECO (engineering change order)
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. ECO (engineering change order)

An ECO incorporates small logic bug fixes or timing adjustments through local changes without redoing all of place-and-route. If spare cells were placed in advance, a fix can sometimes be made by changing only the metal masks while leaving transistor layers untouched, saving time and mask cost. Formal equivalence checking then confirms the change is as intended.

  • A. Tape-out is handing off manufacturing data.
  • B. Floorplanning decides the rough placement.
  • C. Correct. Fixes are reflected through local changes.
