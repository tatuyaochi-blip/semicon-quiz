LSI design flow and EDA

What is the step called that extracts wire resistance and capacitance from the layout after place and route?

  1. ALogic synthesis
  2. BATPG
  3. CParasitic extraction (RC extraction)
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. Parasitic extraction (RC extraction)

Parasitic extraction calculates and extracts wire resistance and capacitance from the shapes and spacing of wires in the layout. These values are used to perform static timing analysis and power analysis accurately. It is a step growing in importance as wiring effects increase with scaling.
