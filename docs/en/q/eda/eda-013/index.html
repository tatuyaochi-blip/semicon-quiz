IC design flow and EDA

Which is the representative circuit simulator that calculates voltages and currents of transistor-level circuits in detail using device models?

  1. AVerilog
  2. BSQL
  3. CSPICE
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. SPICE

SPICE is a circuit simulator that computes time-domain and frequency responses of voltages and currents in circuits of transistors, resistors and capacitors based on device models. Developed at UC Berkeley in the 1970s, it lives on in many derivatives. Accurate but computationally heavy, it is used for analog design and standard-cell characterization. Verilog is a logic description language.
