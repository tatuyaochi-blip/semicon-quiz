IC design flow and EDA

Which technique describes properties a design must satisfy (for example, "respond within 3 cycles of a request") and has them checked automatically during simulation or by formal verification?

  1. AStealth dicing
  2. BAssertion-based verification
  3. CDummy fill
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. Assertion-based verification

Assertions describe properties a circuit must always satisfy and temporal relationships between signals. They detect violations automatically during simulation or can be proven mathematically by formal verification, catching bugs early and close to their source. They miss less than people inspecting waveforms. They are written in languages such as SystemVerilog Assertions (SVA). Stealth dicing and dummy fill are manufacturing techniques.

  • A. Stealth dicing is a method of separating chips.
  • B. Correct. Properties are described and checked automatically.
  • C. Dummy fill is non-functional patterning added for CMP.
