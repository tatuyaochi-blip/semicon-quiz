LSI design flow and EDA

What is the main purpose of placing dummy patterns that do not function as circuits in empty areas of the layout?

  1. ATo decorate the chip
  2. BTo even out pattern density and suppress variation in CMP and etch results
  3. CTo increase capacitance between wires
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. To even out pattern density and suppress variation in CMP and etch results

If pattern density varies greatly from place to place, CMP removal and etch rates vary, making film thickness and dimensions nonuniform. Placing dummy patterns (dummy fill) in empty areas to even out density suppresses this variation.
