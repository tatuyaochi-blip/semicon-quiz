IC design flow and EDA

Which design approach has people design each transistor's dimensions and placement to push performance and area to the limit?

  1. AAutomated place and route with standard cells
  2. BProgramming an FPGA
  3. CFull-custom design
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. Full-custom design

In full-custom design, designers craft transistor sizes and layouts directly to push speed and area to the limit. Because it is labor-intensive, it is used for critical processor datapaths, memory cells such as SRAM, analog circuits and the standard cells themselves, while automated place-and-route handles the rest. The standard-cell approach places ready-made cells automatically, and FPGAs are programmed after manufacture.

  • A. The standard-cell approach automatically places and routes prepared cells.
  • B. An FPGA is a device programmed with circuits after manufacture.
  • C. Correct. People design the details by hand.
