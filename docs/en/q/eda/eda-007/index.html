LSI design flow and EDA

Which correctly describes standard-cell design?

  1. AEvery transistor is placed one by one by hand
  2. BCells such as gates, designed and verified in advance, are placed and wired to build the circuit
  3. CThe circuit is rewritten after manufacturing before use
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. Cells such as gates, designed and verified in advance, are placed and wired to build the circuit

In standard-cell design, basic circuits such as NANDs and flip-flops are designed and verified in advance as cells of uniform height, and logic synthesis and place-and-route tools arrange and connect them. Most large digital LSIs are designed this way.
