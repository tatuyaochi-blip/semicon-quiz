IC design flow and EDA

In static timing analysis, what is the technique called that multiplies delays by factors to add margin for transistor and wire delays varying by location even within the same chip?

  1. AOCV (on-chip variation) derating
  2. BClock gating
  3. CAntenna rules
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. OCV (on-chip variation) derating

Even within one chip, threshold voltage, wire dimensions, supply voltage and temperature vary slightly by location, so delays vary. OCV applies separate derating factors to data and clock paths, for example making data slower and the capture clock faster in setup analysis, to cover the worst combination. Refined methods such as AOCV and POCV account for path depth and distance. Antenna rules prevent gate damage during processing.

  • A. Correct. It accounts for on-chip variation with factors.
  • B. Clock gating is a technique for reducing power.
  • C. Antenna rules are layout rules preventing gate damage during plasma processing.
