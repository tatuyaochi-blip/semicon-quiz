LSI design flow and EDA

In verifying large SoCs, which technique runs the designed circuit on dedicated hardware or FPGAs and verifies it quickly together with software?

  1. AEmulation and FPGA prototyping
  2. BDRC
  3. CCMP
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. Emulation and FPGA prototyping

Software logic simulation becomes very slow for large circuits. Implementing and running the circuit on an emulator or FPGA allows verification orders of magnitude faster, and software development and verification can proceed before the chip exists, while running a real OS and applications.
