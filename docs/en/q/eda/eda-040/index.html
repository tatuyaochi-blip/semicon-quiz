LSI design flow and EDA

In low-power design, which standard format describes the "power intent", such as power domains, power shutoff and level shifters, separately from the circuit description?

  1. AUPF (Unified Power Format)
  2. BGDSII
  3. CSPICE netlist
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. UPF (Unified Power Format)

UPF is a standard format describing power design intent: which blocks run on which supplies, when supplies are shut off, and what isolation cells or level shifters sit between domains. Combined with RTL, it lets synthesis, verification, place-and-route and other tools handle the power structure consistently.

  • A. Correct. It is the standard format for describing power intent.
  • B. GDSII is a layout data format.
  • C. A SPICE netlist describes circuits at the transistor level.
