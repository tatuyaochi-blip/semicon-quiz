Memory Hierarchy

If the cache hit rate is 95%, what is the miss rate?

  1. A95%
  2. B0.95%
  3. C5%
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. 5%

Every access is either a hit or a miss, so miss rate = 1 − hit rate = 100% − 95% = 5%. A small-looking miss rate still matters a lot when the miss penalty is large: with a 1-cycle hit time and a 100-cycle penalty, the average is 1 + 0.05 × 100 = 6 cycles, shrinking to 2 cycles at a 99% hit rate. 95% is the hit rate itself, and 0.95% confuses a fraction with a percentage.

  • A. 95% is the hit rate itself.
  • B. 0.95% confuses a fraction with a percentage.
  • C. Correct. 100% − 95% = 5%.
