Performance Analysis

You want to make an entire task 2× faster by speeding up only the part that takes 60% of the time. How many times faster must that part become?

  1. A2×
  2. B3.3×
  3. C6×
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. 6×

Doubling overall speed requires the new time to be 0.5. The untouched 40% still takes 0.4, leaving 0.5 − 0.4 = 0.1 for the 60% part. Shrinking 0.6 to 0.1 requires a 6× speedup. As the unimprovable share approaches half the total, the required factor grows rapidly, and beyond 50% a 2× overall speedup becomes impossible.

  • A. Doubling only that part does not double the whole.
  • B. That computes 0.6 ÷ 0.3 but forgets the remaining 0.4.
  • C. Correct. 0.4 + 0.6 ÷ 6 = 0.5.
