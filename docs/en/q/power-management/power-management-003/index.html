Power Management

With the supply voltage unchanged, only the clock frequency is halved. What happens to the dynamic energy needed to finish a fixed amount of computation?

  1. AIt stays about the same (power halves but time doubles)
  2. BIt halves
  3. CIt falls to a quarter
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. It stays about the same (power halves but time doubles)

Dynamic power is proportional to f, so halving the frequency halves the power. But the same computation takes twice as long, so energy, power times time, is unchanged, because the energy per switching event is set by CV². Reducing energy requires lowering voltage; lowering frequency is what allows the circuit to work correctly at that lower voltage.

  • A. Correct. Energy = power × time.
  • B. Power halves, but time doubles.
  • C. Without lowering voltage it does not fall that far.
