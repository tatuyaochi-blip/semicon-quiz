Power Management

What is the role of a level shifter inserted when passing signals between two blocks running at different supply voltages?

  1. ATo double the signal frequency
  2. BTo encrypt the signal
  3. CTo convert the signal's voltage to 0 and 1 levels matching the receiving side's supply
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. To convert the signal's voltage to 0 and 1 levels matching the receiving side's supply

If a block running at 0.6 V sends a 1 (0.6 V) to a block running at 1.0 V, the receiver sees an intermediate voltage it cannot reliably read as 1, causing malfunction or shoot-through current. A level shifter converts the signal to voltages suited to the receiving side's supply. Designs that save power by running blocks at different voltages need them on every signal crossing a voltage boundary.

  • A. It does not change frequency.
  • B. It does not encrypt.
  • C. Correct. It is especially needed going from low to high voltage.
