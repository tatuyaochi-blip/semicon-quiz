Power Management

A device drawing 300 mA on average runs from a 3000 mAh battery. Roughly how many hours does it run (ignoring voltage drop and similar)?

  1. AAbout 1 hour
  2. BAbout 100 hours
  3. CAbout 10 hours
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. About 10 hours

mAh (milliampere-hours) is a battery capacity unit indicating how many milliamps can flow for how many hours. Drawing an average of 300 mA from a 3000 mAh battery depletes it in 3000 ÷ 300 = 10 hours. Halving the average current doubles the runtime. In practice, battery voltage changes, temperature and reduced efficiency at high current make this an estimate.

  • A. The order of magnitude is wrong.
  • B. The order of magnitude is wrong.
  • C. Correct. 3000 ÷ 300 = 10.
