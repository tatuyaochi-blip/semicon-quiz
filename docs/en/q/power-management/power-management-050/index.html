Power Management

If only the supply voltage is lowered by 10% (all else equal), by roughly what percentage does dynamic power fall?

  1. AAbout 19%
  2. BAbout 10%
  3. CAbout 50%
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. About 19%

Dynamic power is proportional to the square of voltage, so scaling voltage by 0.9 scales power by 0.9² = 0.81, a reduction of about 19%. Even small voltage changes have a large effect on power, making voltage reduction one of the most effective power-saving levers. Lower voltage slows circuits, though, so it must stay within the range that keeps required speed, and doing this according to load is what DVFS does.

  • A. Correct. 0.9² = 0.81, about a 19% reduction.
  • B. Power is proportional to the square of voltage.
  • C. A 10% change does not cut it that much.
