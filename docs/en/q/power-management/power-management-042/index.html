Power Management

Why are temperature sensors often placed at many locations within a large chip?

  1. AMore sensors cool the chip
  2. BTemperature sensors can be used only once
  3. CAreas such as dense arithmetic units heat up locally (hot spots), so a single sensor cannot reveal the hottest point
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. Areas such as dense arithmetic units heat up locally (hot spots), so a single sensor cannot reveal the hottest point

Power usage varies greatly across a chip, and active cores or arithmetic units can run tens of degrees hotter than cache regions. Temperature limits are set by the hottest spot, so a single sensor might miss a hot spot reaching a dangerous temperature. Sensors are placed at many locations, and frequency control or throttling is based on the highest reading.

  • A. Sensors do not cool anything.
  • B. Sensors work repeatedly.
  • C. Correct. Control is based on the hottest spot.
