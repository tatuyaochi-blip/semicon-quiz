Power Management

What is the dynamic power of a circuit with activity factor α = 0.1, switched capacitance C = 1 nF, supply voltage V = 1 V and frequency f = 1 GHz?

  1. A1 W
  2. B0.1 W
  3. C10 W
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. 0.1 W

Substituting into P = αCV²f gives 0.1 × 1 × 10⁻⁹ F × (1 V)² × 1 × 10⁹ Hz = 0.1 W. The nano (10⁻⁹) of the capacitance and the giga (10⁹) of the frequency cancel, which keeps the arithmetic simple. In real chips the switched capacitance grows with circuit size, and total dynamic power can reach tens to hundreds of watts.

  • A. That forgets to multiply by α.
  • B. Correct. 0.1 × 10⁻⁹ × 1² × 10⁹ = 0.1.
  • C. The order of magnitude is wrong.
