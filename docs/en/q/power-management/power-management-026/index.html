Power Management

Which is NOT a reason chips need margin in their supply voltage?

  1. AWhen circuits switch together, supply wiring resistance and inductance cause momentary voltage drops (supply noise)
  2. BHigher voltage always reduces power consumption
  3. CManufacturing variation, temperature and aging change circuit speed
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. Higher voltage always reduces power consumption

Circuits slow down with even a slight voltage drop and can miss timing. When many circuits switch at once, resistive drop in the supply wiring (IR drop) and inductive voltage from rapid current changes momentarily lower the voltage. Speed also varies with process, temperature and aging, so some margin is needed. But margin also wastes power, so it is reduced through stronger power delivery networks, AVS and similar techniques.

  • A. This is a reason margin is needed.
  • B. Correct. Raising voltage increases power.
  • C. This is a reason margin is needed.
