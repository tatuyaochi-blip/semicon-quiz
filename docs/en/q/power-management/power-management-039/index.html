Power Management

What characterizes near-threshold operation, running with supply voltage lowered close to the transistor threshold?

  1. ASpeed rises and energy falls
  2. BLowering voltage does not change power
  3. CIt greatly reduces energy per operation, but speed falls sharply and variation has a much larger effect
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. It greatly reduces energy per operation, but speed falls sharply and variation has a much larger effect

Energy per switching event is proportional to CV², so lowering voltage close to threshold cuts energy to a fraction. But transistor current shrinks, so circuits slow dramatically, and even small threshold variations change speed greatly. It is being researched and used for devices such as sensor nodes, where modest speed suffices and long battery life is the goal.

  • A. Lower voltage makes circuits slower.
  • B. Lower voltage reduces power substantially.
  • C. Correct. It suits uses where battery life matters more than speed.
