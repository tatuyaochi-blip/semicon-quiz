Power Management

Assume DVFS can lower the supply voltage in proportion to frequency. When frequency is halved, roughly what happens to dynamic power and to the dynamic energy for a fixed amount of computation?

  1. APower falls to one-eighth and energy to one-quarter
  2. BBoth power and energy halve
  3. CPower falls to a quarter and energy is unchanged
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. Power falls to one-eighth and energy to one-quarter

Power is proportional to V²f, so halving both V and f gives (1/2)² × 1/2 = 1/8. The computation takes twice as long, so energy is 1/8 × 2 = 1/4. Equivalently, since energy per switching event is proportional to CV², energy scales with the square of voltage. In practice the voltage range is limited and static power exists, so the reduction is not this ideal.

  • A. Correct. Power scales with V²f and energy with V².
  • B. Voltage is also lowered, so the reduction is larger.
  • C. Lowering voltage also reduces energy.
