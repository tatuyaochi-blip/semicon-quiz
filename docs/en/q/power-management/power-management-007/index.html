Power Management

Which describes power gating?

  1. AStopping only the clock supply to circuit blocks
  2. BSwitching off the power supply to unused circuit blocks, eliminating nearly all their leakage power too
  3. CDoubling the supply voltage to speed up processing
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. Switching off the power supply to unused circuit blocks, eliminating nearly all their leakage power too

Clock gating stops switching and so cuts dynamic power, but leakage continues as long as power is connected. Power gating turns off a large transistor switch placed between the block and the supply, cutting power itself, so leakage nearly disappears too. Smartphone SoCs aggressively power off idle CPU cores, GPUs and accelerators.

  • A. That is clock gating.
  • B. Correct. Its key feature is cutting static power too.
  • C. It is a power-saving technique.
