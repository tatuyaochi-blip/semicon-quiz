MEMS and sensors

What is the main reason for sealing the moving parts of MEMS gyroscopes and similar devices in vacuum?

  1. ATo reduce air resistance (damping), keeping the vibration large and increasing sensitivity
  2. BTo make the moving parts heavier
  3. CTo change the sensor's color
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. To reduce air resistance (damping), keeping the vibration large and increasing sensitivity

In a vibratory MEMS gyroscope, the larger and more stable the vibration of the moving part, the higher the sensitivity. Air resistance weakens the vibration, so the inside is sealed in vacuum by wafer-level bonding or similar methods to raise the sharpness of resonance (Q factor).
