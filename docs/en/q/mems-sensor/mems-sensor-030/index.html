MEMS and sensors

What is an advantage of RF MEMS switches, which switch RF signals by mechanically opening and closing contacts?

  1. AThey are always faster than semiconductor switches
  2. BThey have no contacts, so there is no wear at all
  3. CThey switch with low insertion loss and little signal distortion
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. They switch with low insertion loss and little signal distortion

RF MEMS switches move a tiny electrode electrostatically to open and close metal contacts, giving low on-resistance, off-capacitance of only a few fF, insertion loss around 0.1–0.5 dB and almost none of the nonlinear distortion of semiconductor switches. Drawbacks are switching times of microseconds to tens of microseconds, lifetime and reliability limited by contact wear and stiction, and drive voltages of tens of volts.
