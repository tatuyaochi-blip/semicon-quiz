MEMS and sensors

Which force does a vibratory MEMS gyroscope use to detect angular velocity?

  1. ACoriolis force
  2. BOnly the Coulomb force
  3. CBuoyancy
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. Coriolis force

When a vibrating object rotates, a Coriolis force acts perpendicular to both the vibration direction and the rotation axis. A MEMS gyroscope detects the tiny displacement caused by this force, for example capacitively, to determine angular velocity. It is used for image stabilization and smartphone orientation sensing.
