MEMS and sensors

Which is the most appropriate scale for the movable structures made by MEMS?

  1. ASeveral meters
  2. BSeveral micrometers to several millimeters
  3. CSeveral picometers
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. Several micrometers to several millimeters

MEMS uses semiconductor microfabrication to build mechanical structures (springs, masses, membranes, mirrors and so on) ranging from micrometers to millimeters in size, together with electronic circuits. They are far larger than atoms (tens to hundreds of picometers) but smaller than parts visible to the naked eye.

  • A. Several meters is the size of ordinary machines.
  • B. Correct. They range from micrometers to millimeters.
  • C. Several picometers is smaller than an atom; no structure can be made at that size.
