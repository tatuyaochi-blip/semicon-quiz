MEMS and sensors

What is the general principle of the sensor a smartphone uses to turn off the screen when your face approaches during a call, preventing accidental touches?

  1. AMeasuring the strength of the Earth's magnetic field
  2. BEmitting infrared light and detecting with a photodetector the strength of light reflected back from an object
  3. CMeasuring sound loudness
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. Emitting infrared light and detecting with a photodetector the strength of light reflected back from an object

A proximity sensor emits light from an infrared LED or similar and detects with a photodetector the light reflected back from a nearby object such as a face. If the reflection is strong, it judges the object to be close and, for example, turns off the screen.

  • A. Measuring the Earth's magnetic field is the principle of an electronic compass.
  • B. Correct. It detects proximity from reflected infrared light.
  • C. It does not judge the approach of a face by sound level.
