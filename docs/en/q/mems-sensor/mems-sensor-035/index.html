MEMS and sensors

What is the general principle of the sensor a smartphone uses to turn off the screen when your face approaches during a call, preventing accidental touches?

  1. AMeasuring sound loudness
  2. BMeasuring the strength of the Earth's magnetic field
  3. CEmitting infrared light and detecting with a photodetector the strength of light reflected back from an object
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. Emitting infrared light and detecting with a photodetector the strength of light reflected back from an object

A proximity sensor emits invisible light around 940 nm from an infrared LED and detects light reflected back by a nearby object such as a face. If the reflection exceeds a threshold, the object is judged close and the screen is turned off during calls to prevent accidental touches. Pulsing the LED and subtracting off-state readings removes ambient light. Some newer sensors measure distance directly with time-of-flight.

  • A. It does not judge the approach of a face by sound level.
  • B. Measuring the Earth's magnetic field is the principle of an electronic compass.
  • C. Correct. It detects proximity from reflected infrared light.
