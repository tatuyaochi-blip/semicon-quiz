MEMS and sensors

Which sensor, widely used in motion sensors that turn on lights when a person approaches, uses a material that generates charge when its temperature changes?

  1. APyroelectric infrared sensor
  2. BHall element
  3. CStrain gauge
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. Pyroelectric infrared sensor

A pyroelectric infrared sensor uses a pyroelectric material (such as PZT or lithium tantalate) that develops surface charge when its temperature changes, detecting changes in the roughly 10 µm infrared emitted by people. Responding only to change, it signals when a person moves and struggles to detect someone standing still. A Fresnel lens divides the field of view to capture motion; such sensors control lighting, toilets, air conditioners and security systems.

  • A. Correct. It detects changes in infrared via the pyroelectric effect.
  • B. A Hall element detects magnetic fields.
  • C. A strain gauge measures the resistance change caused by deformation.
