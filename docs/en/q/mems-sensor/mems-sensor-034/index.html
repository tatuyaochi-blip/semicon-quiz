MEMS and sensors

Which sensor does a smartphone use to automatically adjust screen brightness to its surroundings?

  1. ABarometric pressure sensor
  2. BIlluminance (ambient light) sensor
  3. CGyroscope
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. Illuminance (ambient light) sensor

An ambient light sensor measures surrounding brightness with a photodiode or similar. Filtered to resemble the sensitivity of the human eye, it lets the phone adjust screen brightness to the measured light, balancing visibility and power saving.

  • A. A pressure sensor estimates altitude changes and similar from air pressure.
  • B. Correct. It measures the surrounding brightness.
  • C. A gyroscope measures rotation rate (angular velocity).
