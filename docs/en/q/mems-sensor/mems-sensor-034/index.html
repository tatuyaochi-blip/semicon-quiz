MEMS and sensors

Which sensor does a smartphone use to automatically adjust screen brightness to its surroundings?

  1. ABarometric pressure sensor
  2. BIlluminance (ambient light) sensor
  3. CGyroscope
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. Illuminance (ambient light) sensor

An ambient light sensor measures surrounding illuminance (in lux) with photodiodes. Since silicon also responds to infrared, filters or multiple photodiodes shape the response to match the eye's sensitivity, which peaks near 555 nm green. Screen brightness is adjusted accordingly for readability and power savings, and newer sensors also measure color temperature to tune the display's white balance.

  • A. A pressure sensor estimates altitude changes and similar from air pressure.
  • B. Correct. It measures the surrounding brightness.
  • C. A gyroscope measures rotation rate (angular velocity).
