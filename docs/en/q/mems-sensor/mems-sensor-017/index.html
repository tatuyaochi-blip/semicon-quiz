MEMS and sensors

What can be estimated from changes in air pressure using a smartphone's MEMS pressure sensor?

  1. AAmbient brightness
  2. BThe direction of the Earth's magnetic field
  3. CChanges in altitude (such as the floor of a building)
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. Changes in altitude (such as the floor of a building)

Air pressure falls with altitude, by about 1.2 hPa per 10 m near the ground. Precise MEMS barometers resolve changes of about ±0.05 hPa—roughly 50 cm of height—so devices can determine the floor in a building for navigation, count stairs climbed, or report altitude in emergency calls. To separate altitude from weather-driven pressure changes, processing focuses on short-term variations.
