MEMS and sensors

Which method is widely used to evaluate the bias stability (slow drift of the output) of MEMS gyroscopes?

  1. AAllan variance (Allan deviation) analysis
  2. BC-V measurement
  3. CFour-point probe method
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. Allan variance (Allan deviation) analysis

Allan variance divides hours of static output into intervals of various averaging times τ and examines the scatter of differences between adjacent interval averages. On a log-log plot, slopes separate components such as angle random walk (slope −1/2), dominant at short times, from bias instability (in °/h), read at the curve's minimum. It is the standard way to compare gyroscopes, accelerometers and oscillators.

  • A. Correct. Allan variance evaluates noise and bias drift.
  • B. C-V measurement evaluates the capacitance of MOS structures and the like.
  • C. The four-point probe measures the resistivity of films and wafers.
