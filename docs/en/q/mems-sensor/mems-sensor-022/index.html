MEMS and sensors

Which MEMS sensor has been used to decide when to deploy car airbags?

  1. AAccelerometer
  2. BMEMS microphone
  3. CDMD
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. Accelerometer

An accelerometer detects the sudden deceleration of a crash (tens of g), and from its magnitude and time profile the system decides within a few tens of milliseconds whether to deploy the airbag. Analog Devices' mass-produced automotive MEMS accelerometer (ADXL50) in the early 1990s, small, cheap and reliable, launched the widespread adoption of MEMS. Modern systems combine sensors at several locations on the vehicle to avoid false deployment.
