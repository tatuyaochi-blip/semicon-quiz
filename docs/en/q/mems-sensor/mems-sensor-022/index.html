MEMS and sensors

Which MEMS sensor has been used to decide when to deploy car airbags?

  1. AAccelerometer
  2. BMEMS microphone
  3. CDMD
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. Accelerometer

An accelerometer detects the sudden deceleration of a collision, and the decision to deploy the airbag is based on its magnitude and time profile. MEMS accelerometers are small, reliable and cheap to mass-produce, and their adoption in cars helped them spread.
