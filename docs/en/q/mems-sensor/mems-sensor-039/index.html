MEMS and sensors

Which is an example of MEMS "energy harvesting"?

  1. AConnecting batteries in parallel to increase capacity
  2. BDrawing power from a wall outlet
  3. CConverting ambient vibration into electricity with a piezoelectric element to power a sensor
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. Converting ambient vibration into electricity with a piezoelectric element to power a sensor

Energy harvesting converts small amounts of ambient energy, such as vibration, heat, light or radio waves, into electricity for use. A MEMS piezoelectric element that turns machine vibration into electricity can power sensors placed where battery replacement is difficult.

  • A. Adding batteries is not energy harvesting.
  • B. Drawing power from an outlet is not energy harvesting.
  • C. Correct. Ambient vibration is converted into electricity.
