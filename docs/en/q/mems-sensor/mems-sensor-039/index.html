MEMS and sensors

Which is an example of MEMS "energy harvesting"?

  1. ADrawing power from a wall outlet
  2. BConverting ambient vibration into electricity with a piezoelectric element to power a sensor
  3. CConnecting batteries in parallel to increase capacity
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. Converting ambient vibration into electricity with a piezoelectric element to power a sensor

Energy harvesting converts small amounts of ambient energy, such as vibration, heat, light or radio waves, into electricity for use. A MEMS piezoelectric element that turns machine vibration into electricity can power sensors placed where battery replacement is difficult.

  • A. Drawing power from an outlet is not energy harvesting.
  • B. Correct. Ambient vibration is converted into electricity.
  • C. Adding batteries is not energy harvesting.
