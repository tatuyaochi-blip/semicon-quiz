MEMS and sensors

What is the principle of the optical sensor that measures heart rate at the wrist in smartwatches and similar devices?

  1. AIt measures changes in skin temperature
  2. BIt calculates from the acceleration of arm movement alone
  3. CIt shines LED light and detects with a photodiode the changes in reflected light caused by changes in blood flow
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. It shines LED light and detects with a photodiode the changes in reflected light caused by changes in blood flow

An optical heart-rate sensor shines light, often green, from an LED onto the skin and uses a photodiode to detect changes in the reflected light caused by the blood volume in the vessels changing with each heartbeat. This method is called photoplethysmography (PPG). Noise from body movement is corrected using accelerometer data.

  • A. Skin temperature changes are hard to use for detecting the pulse.
  • B. The accelerometer is used to correct for motion, but the heartbeat itself is detected optically.
  • C. Correct. A photodiode detects changes in reflected light.
