MEMS and sensors

Which phenomenon do BAW filters, used as RF filters in smartphones and similar devices, use?

  1. AHall effect
  2. BMechanical resonance of a piezoelectric thin film
  3. CSeebeck effect
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. Mechanical resonance of a piezoelectric thin film

A BAW (bulk acoustic wave) filter uses resonators made of piezoelectric films such as aluminum nitride (or ScAlN), about 1 µm thick, sandwiched between electrodes. The film vibrates through its thickness and resonates at a frequency set by that thickness, passing only the desired band. With low loss at roughly 1.5–6 GHz, dozens of BAW filters appear in 5G smartphones handling many frequency bands.
