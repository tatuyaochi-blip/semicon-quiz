MEMS and sensors

In very small MEMS accelerometers and gyroscopes, which noise, caused by the proof mass jiggling randomly through thermal interaction with surrounding gas molecules and the like, is one limit on resolution?

  1. AThermomechanical noise (Brownian noise)
  2. BClock noise
  3. CQuantization noise
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. Thermomechanical noise (Brownian noise)

At any finite temperature, the proof mass jiggles randomly from gas-molecule collisions and similar effects. This thermomechanical (Brownian) noise, expressed as an equivalent acceleration of √(4kTω₀/(mQ)), grows as the mass gets lighter and damping increases (lower Q). Miniaturization therefore hurts, and precision sensors use heavier (larger) proof masses and vacuum sealing to raise Q and lower noise.

  • A. Correct. It is noise from thermally driven mechanical motion.
  • B. Clock noise is not a fundamental limit of the sensor's mechanical part.
  • C. Quantization noise arises in AD conversion.
