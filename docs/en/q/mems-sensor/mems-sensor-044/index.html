MEMS and sensors

In a MEMS electrostatic actuator, if the voltage between two parallel electrodes is doubled, by roughly what factor does the electrostatic attraction between them change? (Assume the gap is unchanged.)

  1. A1/2
  2. B2 times
  3. C4 times
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. 4 times

The electrostatic attraction between parallel-plate electrodes is F = εAV² / (2d²), proportional to the square of the voltage V. Doubling the voltage therefore quadruples the force. This is why electrostatic actuators need high voltages to produce large forces.

  • A. Raising the voltage increases the force.
  • B. The force is proportional to the square of the voltage, not the voltage itself.
  • C. Correct. 2² = 4 times.
