MEMS and sensors

What is an advantage of MEMS oscillators, sometimes used instead of quartz crystals as clock sources?

  1. ATheir frequency stability is always better than quartz
  2. BBecause they are made with semiconductor processes, they are small, shock-resistant and easy to integrate with ICs
  3. CThey are large and easy to handle
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. Because they are made with semiconductor processes, they are small, shock-resistant and easy to integrate with ICs

MEMS oscillators pair a tiny silicon resonator with drive, temperature-compensation and PLL circuits, all mass-producible in semiconductor processes. Silicon's resonance shifts strongly with temperature, so circuits correct it, and modern parts achieve stability of a few ppm. Small, shock- and vibration-resistant, freely programmable in frequency and reliably supplied, they replace quartz in automotive and wearable devices.
