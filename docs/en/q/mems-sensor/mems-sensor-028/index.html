MEMS and sensors

What is an advantage of MEMS oscillators, sometimes used instead of quartz crystals as clock sources?

  1. ATheir frequency stability is always better than quartz
  2. BBecause they are made with semiconductor processes, they are small, shock-resistant and easy to integrate with ICs
  3. CThey are large and easy to handle
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. Because they are made with semiconductor processes, they are small, shock-resistant and easy to integrate with ICs

A MEMS oscillator combines a tiny silicon resonator with drive and temperature-compensation circuits and can be mass-produced with semiconductor processes. It is small, resistant to shock and vibration and easy to package, so it is used instead of quartz crystals in some applications.
