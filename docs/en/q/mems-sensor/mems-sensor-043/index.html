MEMS and sensors

In surface micromachining, what problem occurs if a structural thin film such as polysilicon has large internal residual stress?

  1. AThe film's resistance becomes zero
  2. BWhen the sacrificial layer is removed to release the structure, it warps or buckles
  3. CThe film becomes transparent
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. When the sacrificial layer is removed to release the structure, it warps or buckles

High residual stress from deposition causes trouble once structures are released: compressively stressed doubly clamped beams buckle, and stress gradients through the thickness curl cantilevers. Shapes and resonant frequencies then deviate from design, and parts may touch electrodes and stick. Polysilicon stress is tuned to tens of MPa or less via deposition temperature and annealing, and silicon nitride is adjusted to tensile stress through its composition.

  • A. The resistance does not become zero.
  • B. Correct. Warping or buckling occurs.
  • C. Stress does not make the film transparent.
