MEMS and sensors

What is the general ink ejection method of inkjet printer heads made with MEMS technology?

  1. ALetting ink fall naturally by gravity
  2. BCharging the ink and attracting it with magnets
  3. CPushing out ink with the deformation of a piezoelectric element or the pressure of a bubble formed by a heater
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. Pushing out ink with the deformation of a piezoelectric element or the pressure of a bubble formed by a heater

Inkjet heads use either the piezoelectric method (Epson and others), which deforms a piezo element with voltage to push out ink, or the thermal method (Canon, HP and others), which heats ink above 300 °C in microseconds to form a vapor bubble that ejects the drop. Thousands of nozzles tens of µm across, plus their channels, are made by MEMS techniques and fire droplets of a few picoliters. Beyond printing, the technology is used in display manufacturing.
