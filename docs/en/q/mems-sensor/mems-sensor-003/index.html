MEMS and sensors

What is the purpose of sacrificial-layer etching in MEMS fabrication?

  1. ATo thin and lighten the wafer
  2. BTo reduce wiring resistance
  3. CTo remove the layer under a structure so the movable part floats free of the substrate
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. To remove the layer under a structure so the movable part floats free of the substrate

Sacrificial-layer etching deposits the structural film (e.g., polysilicon) on top of a layer intended for later removal (e.g., SiO2), then selectively etches away the sacrificial layer, leaving beams or membranes suspended a few µm above the substrate. The sacrificial thickness sets the gap, and release holes are usually patterned into the structure so the etchant can reach underneath.
