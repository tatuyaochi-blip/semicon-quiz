MEMS and sensors

Which technology builds tiny channels on a chip to perform chemical analysis or testing with small amounts of liquid?

  1. AMicrofluidic devices (lab-on-a-chip)
  2. BPhotomask
  3. CBonding wire
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. Microfluidic devices (lab-on-a-chip)

Microfluidic devices use MEMS fabrication to create channels, mixers and reaction chambers tens to hundreds of µm wide on a chip, analyzing samples of a microliter or less. Also called lab-on-a-chip, they use little sample and reagent, react quickly and enable portable instruments, serving rapid COVID-19 tests, blood analysis, genetic analysis and cell sorting. Besides glass and silicon, inexpensive polymers such as PDMS are used.

  • A. Correct. Analysis is done in tiny channels on a chip.
  • B. A photomask is the master for transferring circuit patterns by exposure.
  • C. A bonding wire is a thin metal wire connecting a chip to its terminals.
