MEMS and sensors

Which correctly describes the piezoresistive effect used in semiconductor pressure sensors?

  1. AThe electrical resistivity of a material changes when mechanical stress is applied
  2. BA voltage appears when light shines on it
  3. CThe direction of current is bent by a magnetic field
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. The electrical resistivity of a material changes when mechanical stress is applied

Stress alters silicon's band structure, changing its resistivity tens of times more than a metal strain gauge (the piezoresistive effect). Four resistors diffused at the edges of a thin diaphragm that flexes under pressure form a Wheatstone bridge, converting pressure into an electrical signal. Such sensors measure engine and tire pressure, blood pressure and barometric pressure, and temperature compensation is key to their design.
