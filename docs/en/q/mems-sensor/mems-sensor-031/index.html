MEMS and sensors

Which temperature sensor uses the property that its electrical resistance changes greatly with temperature?

  1. AThermistor
  2. BHall element
  3. CPhotodiode
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. Thermistor

A thermistor is a sintered ceramic of metal oxides such as manganese, nickel and cobalt whose resistance changes strongly with temperature. NTC types, whose resistance falls as temperature rises, are most common, changing about −3 to −5% per °C—over ten times a platinum RTD—making them cheap and sensitive. They monitor battery temperature and control air conditioners and refrigerators. PTC types, whose resistance jumps sharply, provide overcurrent protection.

  • A. Correct. It is a temperature sensor using the temperature dependence of resistance.
  • B. A Hall element detects magnetic fields.
  • C. A photodiode detects light.
