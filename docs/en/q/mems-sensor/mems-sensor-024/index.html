MEMS and sensors

Which MEMS fabrication method builds movable structures by processing thin films deposited on the substrate?

  1. ABulk micromachining
  2. BSurface micromachining
  3. CDamascene process
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. Surface micromachining

Surface micromachining stacks sacrificial and structural thin films on the substrate, patterns them, and finally removes the sacrificial layer to free the structure. Making structures by deeply etching the substrate itself is called bulk micromachining.
