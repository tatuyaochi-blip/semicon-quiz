MEMS and sensors

Which MEMS-fabricated ultrasonic elements are sometimes used in under-display fingerprint sensors in smartphones and in medical ultrasound?

  1. AVCSELs
  2. BCCDs
  3. CPMUTs and CMUTs (micromachined ultrasonic transducers)
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. PMUTs and CMUTs (micromachined ultrasonic transducers)

PMUTs (piezoelectric) and CMUTs (capacitive) are ultrasonic transmit/receive elements made with MEMS technology. They vibrate a thin membrane to emit ultrasound and detect the reflected waves. Because many elements can be arrayed in a semiconductor process, they are used to read fingerprint ridges and acquire ultrasound images.

  • A. A VCSEL is a vertical-cavity surface-emitting laser.
  • B. A CCD is an image sensor that detects light.
  • C. Correct. They are MEMS-fabricated ultrasonic transducers.
