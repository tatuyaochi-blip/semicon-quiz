MEMS and sensors

Which MEMS-fabricated ultrasonic elements are sometimes used in under-display fingerprint sensors in smartphones and in medical ultrasound?

  1. AVCSELs
  2. BCCDs
  3. CPMUTs and CMUTs (micromachined ultrasonic transducers)
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. PMUTs and CMUTs (micromachined ultrasonic transducers)

PMUTs (piezoelectric) and CMUTs (capacitive) are MEMS ultrasonic transducers that vibrate thin membranes to emit ultrasound and detect its echoes. Unlike bulky piezoceramic elements, they can be arrayed at fine pitch in large numbers by semiconductor processes and integrated with electronics. Uses include in-display ultrasonic fingerprint sensors, handheld ultrasound imagers and range sensors.

  • A. A VCSEL is a vertical-cavity surface-emitting laser.
  • B. A CCD is an image sensor that detects light.
  • C. Correct. They are MEMS-fabricated ultrasonic transducers.
