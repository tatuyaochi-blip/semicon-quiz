MEMS and sensors

What is the principle of the NDIR gas sensor often used to measure indoor carbon dioxide (CO2) concentration?

  1. AIt uses the fact that CO2 absorbs infrared light at a specific wavelength and finds the concentration from the amount of transmitted infrared
  2. BIt uses the fact that CO2 conducts electricity
  3. CIt weighs the CO2 directly
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. It uses the fact that CO2 absorbs infrared light at a specific wavelength and finds the concentration from the amount of transmitted infrared

The NDIR (non-dispersive infrared) method uses the strong absorption of infrared light at about 4.3 µm by CO2. A detector with a filter passing only that wavelength measures how much the infrared from the source weakens as it passes through the gas, giving the concentration. It is highly selective and used for ventilation control and similar purposes.

  • A. Correct. CO2 concentration is found from infrared absorption.
  • B. It does not use electrical conduction by CO2.
  • C. It does not weigh the gas directly.
