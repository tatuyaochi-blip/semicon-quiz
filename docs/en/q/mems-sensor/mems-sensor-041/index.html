MEMS and sensors

What is the principle of the NDIR gas sensor often used to measure indoor carbon dioxide (CO2) concentration?

  1. AIt weighs the CO2 directly
  2. BIt uses the fact that CO2 absorbs infrared light at a specific wavelength and finds the concentration from the amount of transmitted infrared
  3. CIt uses the fact that CO2 conducts electricity
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. It uses the fact that CO2 absorbs infrared light at a specific wavelength and finds the concentration from the amount of transmitted infrared

NDIR (non-dispersive infrared) sensing exploits CO2's strong absorption at about 4.3 µm. A detector fitted with a filter passing only that wavelength measures how much infrared from the source is attenuated through the gas, and the Beer-Lambert law gives the concentration. Selective and stable over long periods, NDIR sensors manage ventilation (keeping below about 1000 ppm), greenhouses and HVAC control.

  • A. It does not weigh the gas directly.
  • B. Correct. CO2 concentration is found from infrared absorption.
  • C. It does not use electrical conduction by CO2.
