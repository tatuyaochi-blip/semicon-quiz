MEMS and sensors

Which device uses acoustic waves traveling along the surface of a piezoelectric substrate and serves as a high-frequency filter in smartphones and similar?

  1. ALCD panel
  2. BDRAM cell
  3. CSAW (surface acoustic wave) filter
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. SAW (surface acoustic wave) filter

A SAW filter patterns interdigital transducers (IDTs) on a piezoelectric substrate such as lithium tantalate, converting electrical signals into acoustic waves traveling along the surface and passing only frequencies set by the electrode period. Acoustic waves travel about 100,000 times slower than electromagnetic waves, so wavelengths are short and tiny chips handle RF. SAW filters are used in huge numbers for phone bands from hundreds of MHz to about 2 GHz, with BAW filters covering higher frequencies.

  • A. An LCD panel is a display.
  • B. A DRAM cell is a memory element.
  • C. Correct. It is a filter using surface acoustic waves.
