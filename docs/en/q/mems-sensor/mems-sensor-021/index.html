MEMS and sensors

How can the resonant frequency of a MEMS structure consisting of a spring and a mass be raised?

  1. AMake the spring softer and the mass heavier
  2. BMake the spring stiffer or the mass lighter
  3. CChange the color of the structure
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. Make the spring stiffer or the mass lighter

A spring-mass structure resonates at f = (1/2π)√(k/m), so a stiffer spring or lighter mass raises it. In an accelerometer, proof-mass displacement is acceleration ÷ (2πf)², so higher resonance lowers sensitivity, while bandwidth and shock tolerance improve. The resonant frequency (typically a few kHz) is chosen to balance sensitivity against bandwidth.
