MEMS and sensors

Which MEMS device is used to scan laser light in automotive LiDAR and similar systems?

  1. AMEMS microphone
  2. BAccelerometer
  3. CMEMS mirror (a tiny movable mirror that scans light)
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. MEMS mirror (a tiny movable mirror that scans light)

A MEMS mirror tilts a tiny mirror at high speed with electrostatic or electromagnetic force to change the direction of reflected laser light. Because it is small and needs no mechanical rotating parts, it is being used in LiDAR and laser projection displays.
