MEMS and sensors

Which MEMS device is used to scan laser light in automotive LiDAR and similar systems?

  1. AMEMS microphone
  2. BAccelerometer
  3. CMEMS mirror (a tiny movable mirror that scans light)
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. MEMS mirror (a tiny movable mirror that scans light)

A MEMS mirror is a tiny mirror about 1 mm across that is resonated or tilted at several to tens of kHz by electrostatic or electromagnetic force, steering a reflected laser beam. Compared with motor-driven polygon mirrors it is small, light and free of rotating parts, making it robust to vibration; uses include LiDAR scanning, laser projection in head-up displays and AR glasses, and optical switches.
