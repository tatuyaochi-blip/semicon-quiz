MEMS and sensors

Which MEMS sensor measures flow rate using the fact that gas flow carries away heat from a heater?

  1. AThermal flow sensor
  2. BHall element
  3. CDMD
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. Thermal flow sensor

A thermal flow sensor places a heater with upstream and downstream temperature sensors on a thermally isolated membrane about 1 µm thick. Gas flow cools the upstream sensor and warms the downstream one, and their difference gives both flow rate and direction. The thin membrane's low heat capacity yields fast response at low power. Applications include engine mass-airflow sensors, gas meters and medical devices such as ventilators.
