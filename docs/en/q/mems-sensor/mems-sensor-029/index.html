MEMS and sensors

Which MEMS sensor measures flow rate using the fact that gas flow carries away heat from a heater?

  1. AThermal flow sensor
  2. BHall element
  3. CDMD
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. Thermal flow sensor

A thermal flow sensor places a heater and upstream and downstream temperature sensors on a thin membrane and determines flow rate from the change in temperature distribution caused by gas flow. It is small and responds quickly, and is used in gas meters, medical equipment and engine intake measurement.
