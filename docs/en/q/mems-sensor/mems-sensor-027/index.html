MEMS and sensors

What is the processing called that combines information from accelerometers, gyroscopes, magnetometers and other sensors to determine orientation and motion more accurately?

  1. ABinning
  2. BATPG
  3. CSensor fusion
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. Sensor fusion

Each sensor has weaknesses: gyroscope errors accumulate over time, accelerometers have trouble separating motion from gravity, and magnetometers are affected by nearby magnetic materials. Sensor fusion combines them so that each compensates for the others' weaknesses, determining orientation and motion accurately.
