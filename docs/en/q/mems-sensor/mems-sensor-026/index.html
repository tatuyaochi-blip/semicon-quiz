MEMS and sensors

Why does a MEMS accelerometer detect about 1 G of acceleration even when stationary?

  1. ABecause it detects gravitational acceleration
  2. BBecause the sensor is broken
  3. CBecause temperature alone produces an output
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. Because it detects gravitational acceleration

An accelerometer detects the displacement of a mass due to inertial forces, so even at rest it detects the force of gravity as acceleration. This property is used to determine a smartphone's tilt and screen orientation. To find acceleration due to motion, the gravity component must be separated out.
