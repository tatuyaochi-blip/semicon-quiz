I/O Interfaces & Buses

Which statement about the structure of PCI Express is correct?

  1. AAll devices share one bus, and only one device can use it at a time
  2. BDevices connect to each other wirelessly
  3. CFrom the root complex on the CPU side, devices (endpoints) connect through switches in a tree of point-to-point links
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. From the root complex on the CPU side, devices (endpoints) connect through switches in a tree of point-to-point links

Old PCI had several devices share one bus and compete for access. In PCI Express, the root complex, connected to the CPU and memory, is the root; switches branch out, and every link is a point-to-point connection between exactly two devices. Each link has its bandwidth to itself, so adding devices causes less contention, and signal quality is easier to maintain.

  • A. That is a shared-bus scheme like old PCI.
  • B. The connections are wired.
  • C. Correct. Each link is dedicated to two devices.
