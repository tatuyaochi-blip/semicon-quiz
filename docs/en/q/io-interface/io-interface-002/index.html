I/O Interfaces & Buses

What is the role of a SerDes (serializer/deserializer) in high-speed serial links?

  1. AIt encrypts data to prevent eavesdropping
  2. BIt converts parallel on-chip data into one fast bit stream on the transmit side and restores the parallel data on the receive side
  3. CIt converts supply voltages to suit other circuits
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. It converts parallel on-chip data into one fast bit stream on the transmit side and restores the parallel data on the receive side

Inside a chip, data moves slowly over buses dozens of bits wide. The SerDes transmitter reorders it into a single very fast bit stream, and the receiver recovers a clock from the stream, decides each bit and restores data of the original width. The physical layers of many high-speed interfaces, such as PCI Express, USB, Ethernet and storage links, are built from SerDes.

  • A. It is not an encryption circuit.
  • B. Correct. It is the core circuit of serial transmit and receive.
  • C. It is unrelated to power conversion.
