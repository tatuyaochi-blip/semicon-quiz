Analog and electronic circuits

What increases the thermal noise (Johnson noise) voltage generated in a resistor?

  1. AHigher resistance, higher temperature and wider bandwidth
  2. BLower resistance
  3. CLower temperature
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. Higher resistance, higher temperature and wider bandwidth

The thermal noise voltage is √(4kTRB) (k: Boltzmann constant, T: absolute temperature, R: resistance, B: bandwidth). It grows with resistance, temperature and bandwidth. Circuits handling tiny signals keep resistance and bandwidth to the minimum needed to reduce noise.
