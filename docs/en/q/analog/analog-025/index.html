Analog and electronic circuits

Which circuit generates a clock at a frequency such as an integer multiple of a reference clock while locking its phase to the reference?

  1. APLL (phase-locked loop)
  2. BRectifier
  3. CSample-and-hold circuit
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. PLL (phase-locked loop)

A PLL consists of a phase detector, a loop filter, a voltage-controlled oscillator (VCO) and a frequency divider, and controls the VCO so that its divided output matches the phase of the reference clock. With a division ratio of N, it produces a clock N times the reference frequency, used for CPU clock generation and wireless communication.
