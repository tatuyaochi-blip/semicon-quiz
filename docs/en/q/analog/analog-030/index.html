Analog and electronic circuits

Compared with a class A amplifier, which is a characteristic of a class B push-pull amplifier?

  1. ALow efficiency but very low distortion
  2. BIt always conducts a large bias current
  3. CHigh power efficiency (theoretical maximum about 78.5%), but prone to crossover distortion
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. High power efficiency (theoretical maximum about 78.5%), but prone to crossover distortion

Class A always draws a large bias current, giving low distortion but a theoretical maximum efficiency of only 25% with a resistive load (50% transformer-coupled), and it heats up even without a signal. Class B draws almost no current at idle, reaching about 78.5% (π/4), but suffers crossover distortion at the handover. In practice, class AB with a small bias current and the even more efficient class D are used.
