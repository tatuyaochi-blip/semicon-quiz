Analog and electronic circuits

For a circuit with first-order low-pass behavior, which approximately relates its bandwidth BW to its rise time tr for a step input? (10% to 90% rise time)

  1. Atr ≈ 0.35 / BW
  2. Btr ≈ BW / 0.35
  3. Ctr ≈ 0.35 × BW
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. tr ≈ 0.35 / BW

For a first-order low-pass circuit, the 10%–90% rise time is about 2.2RC and the bandwidth is 1/(2πRC), so tr ≈ 2.2/(2π·BW) ≈ 0.35/BW; 100 MHz of bandwidth gives about 3.5 ns. When choosing an oscilloscope, the rule of thumb is a system rise time at most 1/3–1/5 of the signal's, and 0.35/tr also estimates the significant frequency content of digital edges.

  • A. Correct. tr ≈ 0.35/BW.
  • B. Time and frequency are reciprocals, so BW should not be in the numerator.
  • C. A wider bandwidth gives a faster rise, so tr is not proportional to BW.
