Analog and electronic circuits

What is a voltage gain of 100 expressed in decibels?

  1. A100 dB
  2. B20 dB
  3. C40 dB
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. 40 dB

Voltage gain in decibels is 20 log₁₀(gain): 20 × log₁₀100 = 40 dB. Handy values: 10× is 20 dB, 2× is about 6 dB, and √2× about 3 dB. Power is proportional to voltage squared, so power uses 10 log₁₀, making a 100× power gain 20 dB. Decibels let cascaded gains be added rather than multiplied and make wide ranges of values easier to handle.
