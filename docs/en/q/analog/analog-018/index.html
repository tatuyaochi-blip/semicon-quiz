Analog and electronic circuits

What is the characteristic of an RC circuit with a capacitor in series with the signal path and a resistor between the output and ground?

  1. AIt attenuates high frequencies and passes low frequencies
  2. BIt attenuates low frequencies and passes high frequencies
  3. CIt passes all frequencies equally
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. It attenuates low frequencies and passes high frequencies

A capacitor's impedance rises at lower frequencies, so in series it blocks low-frequency signals. This circuit is a high-pass filter that attenuates low frequencies, with the same cutoff frequency as the low-pass filter, 1/(2πRC). It is also used as a coupling capacitor to block DC.
