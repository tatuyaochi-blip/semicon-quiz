Analog and electronic circuits

What does the "−3 dB" point in a filter's frequency response represent?

  1. AThe point where power falls to about 1/2 (voltage to about 0.707 times)
  2. BThe point where the voltage is exactly 1/3
  3. CThe point where the power becomes zero
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. The point where power falls to about 1/2 (voltage to about 0.707 times)

−3 dB corresponds to about 1/2 in power and 1/√2 ≈ 0.707 in voltage. In filters, this point is taken as the cutoff frequency and serves as the boundary of the passband.

  • A. Correct. Power halves and voltage falls to about 0.707.
  • B. A voltage of 1/3 is about −9.5 dB.
  • C. Zero power corresponds to −∞ dB.
