Analog and electronic circuits

What is the combined resistance of two 10 kΩ resistors connected in parallel?

  1. A10 kΩ
  2. B5 kΩ
  3. C20 kΩ
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. 5 kΩ

The combined resistance in parallel is given by 1/R = 1/R1 + 1/R2; two equal resistors give half the value. 10 kΩ × 10 kΩ ÷ (10 kΩ + 10 kΩ) = 5 kΩ. In series they would simply add to 20 kΩ.

  • A. Adding a resistor in parallel always lowers the combined resistance.
  • B. Correct. Two equal resistors in parallel give half, 5 kΩ.
  • C. 20 kΩ is the value for a series connection.
