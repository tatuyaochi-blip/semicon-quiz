Analog and electronic circuits

What is the combined resistance of two 10 kΩ resistors connected in parallel?

  1. A10 kΩ
  2. B5 kΩ
  3. C20 kΩ
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. 5 kΩ

Parallel resistance follows 1/R = 1/R1 + 1/R2, or for two resistors R1R2/(R1 + R2) (product over sum). Two equal resistors give half (5 kΩ), and n equal ones give R/n. The combination is always smaller than the smallest resistor, so pairing a large and a small resistor yields roughly the small value. In series they simply add, to 20 kΩ.

  • A. Adding a resistor in parallel always lowers the combined resistance.
  • B. Correct. Two equal resistors in parallel give half, 5 kΩ.
  • C. 20 kΩ is the value for a series connection.
