Analog and electronic circuits

Which expression gives the resonant frequency of a series LC circuit?

  1. A2π√(LC)
  2. B1 / (2π√(LC))
  3. C√(L / C)
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. 1 / (2π√(LC))

An LC circuit resonates where the inductor's reactance 2πfL equals the capacitor's 1/(2πfC), giving f = 1/(2π√(LC)); for L = 10 µH and C = 100 pF, about 5 MHz. Series resonance gives minimum impedance and parallel resonance maximum. LC resonance is used in radio tuning, LC oscillators, filters, and antenna circuits for wireless power and RFID.
