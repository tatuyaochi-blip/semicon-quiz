Analog and electronic circuits

What condition must a feedback oscillator satisfy to sustain oscillation?

  1. ALoop gain of 0 and a 180° phase shift
  2. BLoop gain of 1 or more and a total phase shift of 0° (360°) around the loop
  3. CThe input signal is always 0
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. Loop gain of 1 or more and a total phase shift of 0° (360°) around the loop

For sustained oscillation, a signal traveling around the amplifier and feedback network must return in phase and at least as large (the Barkhausen criterion: loop gain ≥ 1, phase 0°). In practice the loop gain starts above 1 so oscillation builds up from noise, then settles to 1 as amplitude grows, through device saturation or automatic gain control. Classic examples are RC phase-shift, Wien-bridge, Colpitts and crystal oscillators.
