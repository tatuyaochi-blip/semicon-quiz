Computer Arithmetic

Which gives the resolution and range of the 16-bit signed fixed-point Q15 format (15 fraction bits)?

  1. AResolution 2⁻¹⁶, range 0 to 1
  2. BResolution 2⁻¹⁵, range −1 to 1 − 2⁻¹⁵
  3. CResolution 2⁻¹⁵, range −32768 to 32767
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. Resolution 2⁻¹⁵, range −1 to 1 − 2⁻¹⁵

Q15 uses 1 of 16 bits for sign and 15 for the fraction, representing the integer reading divided by 2¹⁵ = 32768. Dividing the integer range −32768 to 32767 by 32768 gives −1 to 32767/32768 = 1 − 2⁻¹⁵, with resolution 1/32768 = 2⁻¹⁵ ≈ 3.05 × 10⁻⁵. Note that exactly +1 cannot be represented. −32768 to 32767 is the range when read as an integer, ignoring the binary point. Q15 is common in audio and other signal processing.

  • A. Being signed, it covers negatives, and the resolution is 2⁻¹⁵.
  • B. Correct. Resolution 2⁻¹⁵, from −1 to 1 − 2⁻¹⁵.
  • C. −32768 to 32767 is the range when read as an integer.
