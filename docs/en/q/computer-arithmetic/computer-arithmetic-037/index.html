Computer Arithmetic

Why are carry-save adders used to sum the many partial products in a multiplier?

  1. AA carry-save adder reduces the partial products to one from the start
  2. BA carry-save adder performs multiplication without any addition
  3. CIt reduces three numbers to two without carry propagation
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. It reduces three numbers to two without carry propagation

Adding partial products one at a time with ordinary adders means waiting each time for carries to ripple from low to high bits. A carry-save adder is just full adders placed independently per bit, reducing three numbers to two (the per-bit sums and the per-bit carries) without waiting for carry propagation, so its delay is a constant one full-adder level. Stages of these reduce the partial products to two, which are added once at the end by a carry-lookahead adder or similar. It cannot reduce them to one in a single step.

  • A. It reduces three to two, not to one.
  • B. It is used for the very additions that sum the partial products.
  • C. Correct. It reduces three to two without carry propagation, with constant delay.
