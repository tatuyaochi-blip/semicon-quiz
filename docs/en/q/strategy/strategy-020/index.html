Business Strategy and Accounting Basics

Analog semiconductor maker A narrows its focus to a limited market, ICs for high-precision sensors in industrial equipment, and competes by providing products tailored to the detailed requirements of those customers. Among Porter's three generic strategies, which is company A's strategy?

  1. ACost leadership strategy
  2. BDifferentiation strategy
  3. CFocus strategy
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. Focus strategy

Porter's generic strategies are three: cost leadership, which targets the whole industry and wins through lower costs; differentiation, which targets the whole industry and wins through the uniqueness of products or services; and focus, which concentrates resources on a specific market (customer group, application, region and so on). Company A has narrowed its target market to a specific application, so its strategy is a focus strategy, and since it competes on uniqueness within that market it is also called differentiation focus. It is used by companies with limited resources to build an advantage without competing head-on with large rivals.

  • A. This strategy targets the whole industry and wins by achieving lower costs than competitors through economies of scale and the like.
  • B. This strategy targets the whole industry and wins through product uniqueness. Company A differs in that it has narrowed its target market.
  • C. Correct. It narrows the target to a specific market and wins within it.
