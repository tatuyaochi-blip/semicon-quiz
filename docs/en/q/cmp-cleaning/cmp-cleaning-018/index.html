CMP and cleaning

What problems are caused by metal contamination such as iron and copper on the wafer surface?

  1. ADegraded characteristics and reliability, such as reduced carrier lifetime and lower gate oxide breakdown strength
  2. BThe wafer becomes lighter
  3. CThe wafer emits light
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. Degraded characteristics and reliability, such as reduced carrier lifetime and lower gate oxide breakdown strength

Metal impurities act as recombination centers in silicon, reducing carrier lifetime and increasing junction leakage. When incorporated into oxides, they reduce breakdown strength and reliability. Metal contamination must therefore be kept extremely low by cleaning.
