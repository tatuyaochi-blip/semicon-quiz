CMP and cleaning

What is the main reason wafer cleaning is considered important in semiconductor manufacturing?

  1. ABecause even slight contamination by particles or metals can cause shorts, opens and characteristic shifts that lower yield
  2. BTo make wafer colors uniform
  3. CTo make wafers lighter
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. Because even slight contamination by particles or metals can cause shorts, opens and characteristic shifts that lower yield

In scaled ICs a single particle tens of nm across—comparable to wiring spacing—can short or open a circuit, and trace metal or organic contamination raises leakage or shifts threshold voltage. With billions of transistors per chip, one defect can kill it. Cleaning before and after each step is therefore essential and underpins yield.

  • A. Correct. Even slight contamination causes defects.
  • B. It is not done to make colors uniform.
  • C. It is not done to change weight.
