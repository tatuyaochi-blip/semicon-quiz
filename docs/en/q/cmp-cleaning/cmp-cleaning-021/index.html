CMP and cleaning

Which combination correctly lists processes in which CMP is used?

  1. APlanarization of STI oxide, tungsten plugs, copper damascene wiring and more
  2. BExposure and resist coating
  3. CIon implantation and annealing
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. Planarization of STI oxide, tungsten plugs, copper damascene wiring and more

CMP planarizes STI fill oxide and interlayer dielectrics, removes excess metal from tungsten plugs and copper wiring, exposes dummy gates in gate-last flows and prepares surfaces for wafer bonding—dozens of steps in advanced logic. Slurry, pad, polish conditions and endpoint method are tailored to each material.
