CMP and cleaning

In processes such as multilevel wiring, what is the main reason the wafer surface must be planarized by CMP?

  1. ATo polish the surface for appearance
  2. BBecause remaining unevenness causes areas to be out of focus in the next exposure and makes film processing difficult
  3. CTo make the wafer heavier
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. Because remaining unevenness causes areas to be out of focus in the next exposure and makes film processing difficult

Each added layer increases surface unevenness, and once it exceeds the range the exposure tool can keep in focus (depth of focus), patterns can no longer be transferred accurately. Steps also make film deposition and processing harder. Planarizing with CMP made it possible to stack many wiring layers.

  • A. It is not a step for appearance.
  • B. Correct. Planarization is needed for exposure and processing.
  • C. It is not meant to make the wafer heavier.
