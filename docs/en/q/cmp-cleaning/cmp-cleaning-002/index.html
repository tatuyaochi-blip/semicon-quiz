CMP and cleaning

What is the main purpose of SC-1 (a mixture of ammonia and hydrogen peroxide) in RCA cleaning?

  1. ARemoving metal contamination
  2. BRemoving particles and organics
  3. CRemoving native oxide
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. Removing particles and organics

SC-1 (NH4OH:H2O2:H2O, e.g., 1:1:5 at about 70 °C) oxidizes the surface with peroxide while ammonia slowly dissolves that oxide at roughly 0.1–1 nm per minute, undercutting and lifting particles away. In the alkaline solution, particles and surface both charge negatively and repel, preventing redeposition, and organics are removed as well. SC-2 removes metal contamination, and dilute HF removes native oxide.
