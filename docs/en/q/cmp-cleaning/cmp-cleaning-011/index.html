CMP and cleaning

In Preston's equation for CMP removal rate, which quantity is the removal rate proportional to?

  1. AThe color of the slurry
  2. BThe wafer diameter
  3. CThe product of polishing pressure and the relative velocity between pad and wafer
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. The product of polishing pressure and the relative velocity between pad and wafer

Preston's equation is the empirical relation removal rate = k × pressure × relative velocity, stating that the removal rate is proportional to the product of pressure and velocity. The constant k depends on the slurry, pad and film being polished.
