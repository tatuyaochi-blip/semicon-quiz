CMP and cleaning

What is the main purpose of using IPA (isopropyl alcohol) when drying wafers after cleaning?

  1. ATo prevent watermarks (drying stains)
  2. BTo etch the wafer surface
  3. CTo oxidize the wafer surface
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. To prevent watermarks (drying stains)

Water left to evaporate on a surface deposits dissolved silica and contaminants as watermarks, especially on hydrophobic silicon. IPA mixes readily with water and has about one third of water's surface tension, so displacing water with IPA dries the wafer quickly, and IPA vapor at the meniscus creates a surface-tension gradient (Marangoni effect) that pulls water off the wafer, preventing watermarks and collapse of fine patterns.
