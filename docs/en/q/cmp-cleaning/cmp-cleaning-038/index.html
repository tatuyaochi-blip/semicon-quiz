CMP and cleaning

What is the state of a silicon surface whose last cleaning step was dilute hydrofluoric acid?

  1. AThe surface is hydrogen-terminated and becomes hydrophobic
  2. BThe surface is covered with metal
  3. CIt is covered with a thick oxide and becomes hydrophilic
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. The surface is hydrogen-terminated and becomes hydrophobic

After dilute HF strips the oxide, the silicon surface is hydrogen-terminated with Si-H bonds and becomes hydrophobic. It resists reoxidation in air for tens of minutes to hours, but being hydrophobic it also attracts particles and forms watermarks easily. Fabs therefore control the queue time (Q-time) before the next step, such as gate oxidation or epitaxy.

  • A. Correct. It becomes hydrogen-terminated and hydrophobic.
  • B. HF cleaning does not coat the surface with metal.
  • C. HF removes oxide, so it does not leave an oxide coating.
