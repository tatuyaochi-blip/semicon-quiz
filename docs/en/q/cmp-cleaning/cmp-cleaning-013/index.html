CMP and cleaning

Which cleaning method applies MHz-range ultrasound to the cleaning solution to remove tiny particles from the wafer surface?

  1. AAshing
  2. BMegasonic cleaning
  3. CScribing
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. Megasonic cleaning

Megasonic cleaning applies ultrasound around 1 MHz to the cleaning liquid, using fast acoustic streaming and a thin boundary layer to detach tiny particles. At tens of kHz, collapsing bubbles (cavitation) deliver violent shocks that damage fine patterns, whereas higher frequencies produce gentler action, removing particles below 0.1 µm with less damage. It is often combined with SC-1.
