CMP and cleaning

Which set correctly lists the kinds of surface contamination removed from wafers by cleaning?

  1. ALight, sound and heat
  2. BElectrons, holes and implanted dopants
  3. CParticles, metal contamination, organic contamination, native oxide and so on
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. Particles, metal contamination, organic contamination, native oxide and so on

Contamination removed by cleaning includes tiny particles, metals such as iron and copper, organics from resist residue or the air, and unwanted native oxide. Solutions suited to each are combined to remove them.

  • A. Light, sound and heat are not contamination.
  • B. Carriers and intentionally introduced dopants are not targets of cleaning.
  • C. Correct. Particles, metals, organics, native oxide and so on.
