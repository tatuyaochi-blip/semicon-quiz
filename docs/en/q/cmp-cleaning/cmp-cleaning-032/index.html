CMP and cleaning

Which correctly describes how many times cleaning is performed across the whole IC manufacturing flow?

  1. AOnly once at the end
  2. BIt is repeated before and after many steps and is one of the most frequent steps in the whole flow
  3. COnly once at the beginning
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. It is repeated before and after many steps and is one of the most frequent steps in the whole flow

Cleaning is repeated before and after deposition, etching, implantation, CMP and ashing—over 200 times in advanced ICs, reportedly 20–30% of all process steps. As one of the most frequent operations and a major consumer of chemicals and ultrapure water, it strongly affects both yield and cost.

  • A. Cleaning only at the end cannot remove contamination from intermediate steps.
  • B. Correct. It is repeated many times before and after steps.
  • C. A single cleaning cannot remove contamination created in each step.
