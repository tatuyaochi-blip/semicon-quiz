CMP and cleaning

What is the operation called in which the surface of a CMP pad is dressed with a diamond tool to stabilize the removal rate?

  1. AAshing
  2. BPad conditioning (dressing)
  3. CDicing
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. Pad conditioning (dressing)

With use, the fine asperities on a pad flatten (glazing) and pores clog with debris, so the pad holds less slurry and removal rate drops. A conditioner studded with diamond grit lightly abrades the surface to regenerate texture and stabilize the rate. In-situ conditioning during polishing is standard, and over-conditioning shortens pad life.
