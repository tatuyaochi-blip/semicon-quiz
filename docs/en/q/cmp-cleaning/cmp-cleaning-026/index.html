CMP and cleaning

Why are oxidizers such as hydrogen peroxide added to the slurry in tungsten CMP?

  1. ATo convert the tungsten surface into an oxide that is easy to polish
  2. BTo make the tungsten shine
  3. CTo cool the slurry
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. To convert the tungsten surface into an oxide that is easy to polish

Tungsten is too hard to polish efficiently by mechanical action alone. Oxidizers such as hydrogen peroxide or iron salts convert its surface to softer tungsten oxide, which abrasive particles remove, and the cycle repeats. Removal rate depends strongly on oxidizer concentration—a clear illustration of the "chemical" in CMP—while measures are needed to keep recessed areas from oxidizing and corroding.
