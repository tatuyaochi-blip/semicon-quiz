CMP and cleaning

Why are oxidizers such as hydrogen peroxide added to the slurry in tungsten CMP?

  1. ATo convert the tungsten surface into an oxide that is easy to polish
  2. BTo make the tungsten shine
  3. CTo cool the slurry
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. To convert the tungsten surface into an oxide that is easy to polish

Tungsten is a hard metal that is difficult to polish mechanically. The oxidizer converts the surface into a soft oxide, which the abrasive particles then remove, and this is repeated as polishing proceeds. It is a clear example of the role of the 'chemical' part of CMP.
