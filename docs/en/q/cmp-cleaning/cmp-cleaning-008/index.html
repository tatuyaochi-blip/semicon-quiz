CMP and cleaning

What is an advantage of single-wafer cleaning tools, which process one wafer at a time?

  1. AThey can process large numbers of wafers at once
  2. BThey use no chemicals at all
  3. CCross-contamination between wafers is unlikely, and conditions are easy to control for each wafer
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. Cross-contamination between wafers is unlikely, and conditions are easy to control for each wafer

Single-wafer cleaning sprays chemicals from nozzles onto one spinning wafer at a time, minimizing cross-contamination and allowing per-wafer control of chemical temperature, flow and time, with front, back and edge cleaned separately. Batch tools that immerse many wafers have higher throughput and use less chemical but risk cross-contamination. Single-wafer cleaning is growing at advanced nodes, with SCREEN and Tokyo Electron among the leading suppliers.
