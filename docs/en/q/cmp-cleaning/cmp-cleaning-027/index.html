CMP and cleaning

What is a correct design countermeasure to the CMP removal amount varying with pattern density within a chip?

  1. AChanging the chip's color
  2. BPlacing dummy patterns to make pattern density uniform
  3. CReducing the number of patterns
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. Placing dummy patterns to make pattern density uniform

Dense areas present more film area and polish slowly, while sparse areas see higher local pressure and polish faster, so post-CMP thickness varies. Inserting electrically inactive dummy fill into empty regions at design time evens out density and thickness. Density checks and automatic fill are standard in design tools—an example of design for manufacturability (DFM), where design and fabrication cooperate to raise yield.
