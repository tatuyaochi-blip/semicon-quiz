CMP and cleaning

Which CMP method fixes the abrasive particles in the polishing pad instead of putting them in the slurry?

  1. ASpin coating
  2. BFixed-abrasive CMP
  3. CElectroplating
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. Fixed-abrasive CMP

Fixed-abrasive CMP uses pads with abrasive particles such as ceria embedded in resin, supplying only particle-free chemistry. Only raised areas touch the abrasive and are removed while recesses are barely polished, giving strong planarization and a self-stopping tendency as the surface flattens. It also reduces slurry use and waste and has been used for STI planarization, though controlling pad-induced defects is a challenge.

  • A. Spin coating spreads liquid by rotation to form a film.
  • B. Correct. The abrasive particles are fixed in the pad.
  • C. Electroplating deposits metal electrically.
