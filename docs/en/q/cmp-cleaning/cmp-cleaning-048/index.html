CMP and cleaning

Which CMP method fixes the abrasive particles in the polishing pad instead of putting them in the slurry?

  1. ASpin coating
  2. BFixed-abrasive CMP
  3. CElectroplating
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. Fixed-abrasive CMP

Fixed-abrasive CMP uses a pad with abrasive particles embedded in resin and supplies only chemicals. Because mainly the high points contact the abrasive and get removed, it planarizes well, and it reduces slurry consumption and waste, so it has been used for STI planarization and similar.

  • A. Spin coating spreads liquid by rotation to form a film.
  • B. Correct. The abrasive particles are fixed in the pad.
  • C. Electroplating deposits metal electrically.
