CMP and cleaning

Which method is commonly used to count particles on a wafer surface after cleaning?

  1. AWeighing the wafer
  2. BScanning a laser across the wafer surface and detecting light scattered by particles
  3. CBreaking the wafer and examining the cross section
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. Scanning a laser across the wafer surface and detecting light scattered by particles

Particle inspection (surface inspection) tools scan a laser across a spinning wafer and detect light scattered by particles and defects, reporting their positions and sizes (as scattering intensity equivalent to reference spheres). Modern tools detect particles below 20 nm. They evaluate cleaning and tool cleanliness on unpatterned wafers, whereas patterned-wafer defects are inspected with image-comparison tools.

  • A. Particles weigh far too little to count by weight.
  • B. Correct. Particles are detected by scattered laser light.
  • C. Cross sections cannot count particles over the whole surface.
