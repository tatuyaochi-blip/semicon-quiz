CMP and cleaning

What is the main purpose of installing filters in the lines that supply CMP slurry to the polisher?

  1. ATo remove all abrasive particles
  2. BTo remove large agglomerated particles and the like, preventing scratches on wafers
  3. CTo warm the slurry
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. To remove large agglomerated particles and the like, preventing scratches on wafers

Slurry particles can agglomerate into large clusters through temperature swings in storage, shear in piping or drying, and these clusters scratch wafers. Filters with pore sizes of a few µm are installed after pumps and tanks and just before the tool to remove only oversized particles while passing the working abrasive. Clogged filters alter flow, so replacement schedules must be managed.

  • A. Without abrasive particles, polishing is impossible.
  • B. Correct. They remove coarse agglomerates to prevent scratches.
  • C. Filters are not for warming the slurry.
