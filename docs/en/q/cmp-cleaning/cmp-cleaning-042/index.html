CMP and cleaning

In SC-1 (a mixture of ammonia and hydrogen peroxide), what role does the hydrogen peroxide play?

  1. AIt glues particles to the surface
  2. BIt hydrogen-terminates the surface
  3. CIt oxidizes the silicon surface to cover it with a thin oxide, preventing direct roughening by ammonia while the surface continues to be etched slightly
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. It oxidizes the silicon surface to cover it with a thin oxide, preventing direct roughening by ammonia while the surface continues to be etched slightly

In SC-1, hydrogen peroxide oxidizes the silicon surface to form a thin oxide, and ammonia slowly dissolves that oxide. With oxidation and etching proceeding together, the surface is removed little by little, lifting particles off for removal. If there is too little peroxide, ammonia etches the silicon directly and roughens the surface.

  • A. The solution is for removing particles.
  • B. Hydrogen termination results from dilute HF treatment.
  • C. Correct. It oxidizes and protects the surface while slight etching continues.
