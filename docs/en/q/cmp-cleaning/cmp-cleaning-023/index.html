CMP and cleaning

Compared with planarization by thermal reflow or etch-back, what is an advantage of CMP?

  1. AIt is always cheaper
  2. BIt produces no polishing debris at all
  3. CIt achieves planarization over wide areas across the whole wafer (global planarization)
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. It achieves planarization over wide areas across the whole wafer (global planarization)

BPSG reflow and resist etch-back can smooth local steps but cannot planarize over millimeters to centimeters. CMP's stiff pad polishes high areas preferentially, achieving global planarity across chips and wafers. This keeps surfaces within lithographic depth of focus and enabled interconnect stacks of more than ten levels and copper wiring.
