CMP and cleaning

What is the role of the retaining ring that surrounds the wafer in a CMP polishing head?

  1. ATo cool the wafer
  2. BTo color the slurry
  3. CTo hold the wafer so it does not slip out during polishing and to reduce polishing variation at the wafer edge
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. To hold the wafer so it does not slip out during polishing and to reduce polishing variation at the wafer edge

A retaining ring is a plastic ring surrounding the wafer in the polishing head. It keeps the wafer from slipping out sideways and presses on the pad just beyond the wafer edge, suppressing pad rebound and deformation at the edge and reducing removal variation in the outer few mm. The ring wears against the pad and is replaced periodically.
