CMP and cleaning

Which correctly describes CMP (chemical mechanical polishing)?

  1. AIt planarizes the surface by combining the chemical action of a slurry with mechanical polishing by a pad
  2. BIt planarizes the surface by etching it with ions from a plasma
  3. CIt planarizes the surface by melting it at high temperature
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. It planarizes the surface by combining the chemical action of a slurry with mechanical polishing by a pad

CMP presses a rotating wafer against a polishing pad while feeding slurry containing abrasive particles and chemicals. Chemistry converts the surface into a softer layer that particles and pad remove mechanically, achieving rates and flatness neither chemical nor mechanical action could alone. Developed at IBM in the 1980s, it keeps topography across the entire wafer to tens of nm or less and is indispensable for multilevel and copper interconnects.
