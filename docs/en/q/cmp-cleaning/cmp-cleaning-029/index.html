CMP and cleaning

What is the main purpose of diluting chemicals or using functional water in recent cleaning processes?

  1. ATo reduce chemical use and environmental impact
  2. BTo always strengthen cleaning power
  3. CTo change the color of the wafer
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. To reduce chemical use and environmental impact

Cleaning used to consume large amounts of concentrated chemicals, but diluting chemicals and using functional waters such as ozonated or hydrogenated water reduce the use of chemicals and ultrapure water and the amount of waste while achieving the needed cleaning effect.
