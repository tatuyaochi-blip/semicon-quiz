CMP and cleaning

What is the main purpose of diluting chemicals or using functional water in recent cleaning processes?

  1. ATo reduce chemical use and environmental impact
  2. BTo always strengthen cleaning power
  3. CTo change the color of the wafer
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. To reduce chemical use and environmental impact

Traditional cleans used large volumes of concentrated chemicals, but diluting chemicals tens to hundreds of times and using functional waters such as ozonated or hydrogenated water, or electrolyzed water, now achieve the needed cleaning while reducing chemical, ultrapure-water and waste volumes. Because fabs consume huge amounts of water and chemicals, this matters for cost and environmental impact alike.
