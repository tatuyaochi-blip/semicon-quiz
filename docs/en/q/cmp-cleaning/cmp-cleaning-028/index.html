CMP and cleaning

What is the approximate size of the abrasive particles in CMP slurries?

  1. AA few mm
  2. BA few cm
  3. CTens to over a hundred nm
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. Tens to over a hundred nm

CMP abrasive particles are very fine, tens to over a hundred nm across. Even a few particles larger than 1 µm per milliliter can cause scratches, so not only size distribution but also large-particle counts are tightly controlled. Particle shape (spherical or irregular) and hardness also affect removal rate and scratch propensity, and softer organic particles are being developed.
