CMP and cleaning

Which correctly describes the basic configuration of a CMP tool?

  1. AThe wafer is milled with an ion beam in vacuum
  2. BThe wafer is simply immersed in a solution to dissolve material
  3. CA wafer held by a polishing head is pressed against a polishing pad on a rotating platen while slurry is supplied
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. A wafer held by a polishing head is pressed against a polishing pad on a rotating platen while slurry is supplied

A CMP tool rotates a platen about 70–80 cm in diameter carrying a polishing pad, while a rotating head presses the wafer face-down onto it. Slurry is supplied, chemical and mechanical action remove material, and a conditioner dresses the pad. Each tool has several platens plus cleaning and drying modules, running staged polishing through to dry wafer output in one continuous sequence.

  • A. Milling with an ion beam is ion milling or similar.
  • B. Simple immersion is wet etching.
  • C. Correct. The wafer is pressed against a rotating pad and polished with slurry.
