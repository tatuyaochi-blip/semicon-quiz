AD/DA conversion

Which AD converter architecture is classified as oversampling?

  1. AFlash
  2. BDelta-sigma
  3. CSuccessive approximation
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. Delta-sigma

ADCs are divided into Nyquist-rate types that sample at about twice the Nyquist frequency (flash, successive approximation, pipeline and others) and oversampling types that sample at much higher rates. The delta-sigma converter is the representative oversampling type.
