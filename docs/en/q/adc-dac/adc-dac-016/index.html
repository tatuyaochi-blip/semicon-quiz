AD/DA conversion

Which metric converts the performance of a real AD converter, including noise and distortion, into the bit count of an ideal ADC?

  1. AENOB (effective number of bits)
  2. BLSB
  3. CDNL
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. ENOB (effective number of bits)

ENOB (effective number of bits) is calculated from the measured SINAD (signal-to-noise-and-distortion ratio) as ENOB = (SINAD − 1.76) / 6.02. Even a 12-bit ADC may have an ENOB of about 10 bits because of noise and distortion, so ENOB is used to compare real-world performance.
