AD/DA conversion

Which metric converts the performance of a real AD converter, including noise and distortion, into the bit count of an ideal ADC?

  1. AENOB (effective number of bits)
  2. BLSB
  3. CDNL
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. ENOB (effective number of bits)

ENOB is calculated from measured SINAD (signal-to-noise-and-distortion ratio) as ENOB = (SINAD − 1.76)/6.02, expressing how many bits of an ideal ADC the real one equals. A 12-bit ADC with 62 dB SINAD has an ENOB of about 10 bits. ENOB falls at higher input frequencies due to jitter and bandwidth limits, so check the frequency at which it is specified. This is why nominal resolution alone does not reveal real performance.
