AD/DA conversion

Which is the most appropriate example of where a digital-to-analog converter (DAC) is used?

  1. AReading a temperature sensor's voltage into a microcontroller
  2. BTurning digital audio data into an analog signal to drive a speaker
  3. CConverting the pixel voltages of a camera's image sensor into numbers
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. Turning digital audio data into an analog signal to drive a speaker

A DAC converts digital values into analog voltages or currents. Turning digital audio into a signal that an amplifier and speaker can play is the classic example; DACs also generate radio transmit signals, display gray-scale voltages, motor-control commands, and comparison voltages inside ADCs. The output is staircase-shaped, so a following low-pass (reconstruction) filter smooths it. Converting sensor voltages to numbers is the ADC's job.

  • A. Turning a sensor voltage into a number is the role of an ADC.
  • B. Correct. Converting digital audio into an analog signal is a DAC's job.
  • C. Turning pixel voltages into numbers is the role of an ADC.
