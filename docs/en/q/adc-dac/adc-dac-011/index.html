A/D and D/A conversion

Which is the approximate formula for the signal-to-noise ratio (SNR) of an ideal N-bit ADC, set by quantization noise? (For a full-scale sine-wave input)

  1. A6.02N + 1.76 dB
  2. B3N dB
  3. C20N dB
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. 6.02N + 1.76 dB

An ideal ADC's SNR is 6.02N + 1.76 dB, improving about 6 dB per bit: roughly 74 dB at 12 bits and 98 dB at 16 bits. It follows from the ratio of a full-scale sine's rms value to the quantization noise rms, LSB/√12. Real ADCs fall short due to thermal noise, distortion and clock jitter, so actual performance is compared by the effective number of bits (ENOB) calculated from measurements.
