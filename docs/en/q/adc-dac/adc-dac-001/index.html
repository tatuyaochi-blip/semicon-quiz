A/D and D/A conversion

How many output levels can an 8-bit ADC represent?

  1. A8 levels
  2. B256 levels
  3. C1024 levels
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. 256 levels

An N-bit converter represents 2ᴺ levels; 8 bits give 2⁸ = 256. Each extra bit doubles the count: 1024 at 10 bits, 4096 at 12, 65,536 at 16. Converting 3.3 V with 8 bits gives steps of about 13 mV, but 12 bits gives about 0.8 mV. Typical resolutions are 10–12 bits in microcontrollers, 16–24 bits in audio and 24 bits for precision measurement.
