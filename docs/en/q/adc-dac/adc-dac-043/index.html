A/D and D/A conversion

Which correctly describes the SFDR (spurious-free dynamic range) of an ADC?

  1. AThe ratio of the maximum to minimum input voltage
  2. BThe ratio between the signal amplitude and the largest unwanted frequency component (spur)
  3. CThe ratio of the sampling frequency to the Nyquist frequency
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. The ratio between the signal amplitude and the largest unwanted frequency component (spur)

SFDR is the difference in dB between the fundamental and the largest spur (a harmonic or other unwanted component) in the output spectrum for a sine-wave input. In radio receivers it indicates whether a weak signal can be detected without being buried by distortion from a strong interferer. High-speed communications ADCs typically achieve about 70–90 dB. Whereas SNR considers total noise, SFDR looks only at the single worst spur.

  • A. A ratio of input range limits is not the definition of SFDR.
  • B. Correct. It is the ratio of the signal to the largest spur.
  • C. A ratio of frequencies is not SFDR.
