AD/DA conversion

What is the main purpose of deliberately adding a small noise (dither) before AD conversion?

  1. ATo make quantization error resemble noise uncorrelated with the signal, reducing distortion and allowing averaging to improve resolution
  2. BTo lower the sampling frequency
  3. CTo increase the input signal amplitude
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. To make quantization error resemble noise uncorrelated with the signal, reducing distortion and allowing averaging to improve resolution

When the input is small or changes slowly, the quantization error is correlated with the signal and appears as distortion (harmonics). Adding suitable dither makes the error behave like noise uncorrelated with the signal, reducing distortion. With averaging, values finer than 1 LSB can also be estimated.

  • A. Correct. It decorrelates the quantization error and reduces distortion.
  • B. Dither does not change the sampling frequency.
  • C. Dither is a very small noise, not a means of increasing amplitude.
