A/D and D/A conversion

Which architecture runs several ADCs alternately with slightly staggered timing to achieve a high overall sampling rate?

  1. ATime-interleaved
  2. BDual-slope
  3. CDelta-sigma
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. Time-interleaved

Time interleaving runs, for example, four ADCs offset by a quarter period each, achieving four times the sampling rate of one ADC. It is used in oscilloscopes running from tens to over 100 GS/s and in optical-communication receivers. Offset, gain and timing mismatches among the ADCs create spurs tied to the switching period, so continuously running background digital calibration is essential.
