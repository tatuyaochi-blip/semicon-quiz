AD/DA conversion

In a time-interleaved ADC that alternates between several ADCs, what problem is caused by mismatches in offset, gain and timing between the individual ADCs?

  1. AUnwanted spurs appear at specific frequencies related to the sampling frequency
  2. BThe input range automatically widens
  3. CMissing codes are always eliminated
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. Unwanted spurs appear at specific frequencies related to the sampling frequency

If the interleaved ADCs differ slightly, the differences become an error that repeats with the switching period, producing spurs at positions related to the sampling frequency divided by the number of ADCs. High-performance interleaved ADCs remove these mismatches with digital calibration.

  • A. Correct. Spurs appear related to the switching period.
  • B. Mismatches do not widen the input range.
  • C. Mismatches do not eliminate missing codes.
