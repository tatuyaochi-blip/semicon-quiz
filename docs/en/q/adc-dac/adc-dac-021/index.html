A/D and D/A conversion

What does 'MS/s (MSPS)', used to express the conversion speed of an ADC, mean?

  1. AMegasiemens per second
  2. BHow many million times per second it can sample (convert)
  3. CThe number of milliseconds per conversion
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. How many million times per second it can sample (convert)

MS/s stands for mega samples per second—how many million conversions per second; 100 MS/s means 100 million per second. By the sampling theorem the usable bandwidth is at most half (50 MHz), though about 40% is typical after filter margin. kS/s and GS/s are also used. Data rate is sampling rate × bits, so 100 MS/s at 12 bits produces 1.2 Gb/s.
