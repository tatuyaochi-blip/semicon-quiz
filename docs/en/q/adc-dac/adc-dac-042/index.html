AD/DA conversion

If a 10 kHz sine wave is sampled at 12 kHz without an anti-aliasing filter, at what frequency does it appear?

  1. A10 kHz
  2. B22 kHz
  3. C2 kHz
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. 2 kHz

The Nyquist frequency (half the sampling frequency) is 6 kHz, and 10 kHz exceeds it. Such a signal folds back and appears at |fs − f| = |12 − 10| = 2 kHz (aliasing). Once aliased, it cannot be distinguished from a real signal, so it must be removed by a filter before sampling.

  • A. 10 kHz is above the Nyquist frequency (6 kHz) and cannot be represented as is.
  • B. 22 kHz is above the Nyquist frequency and does not appear in the sampled signal.
  • C. Correct. It folds back to 12 kHz − 10 kHz = 2 kHz.
