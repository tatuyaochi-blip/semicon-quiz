AD/DA conversion

What is the effect of raising the order of the modulator in a ΔΣ AD converter (for example, from first to second order)?

  1. AThe sampling frequency can be lowered
  2. BQuantization noise is pushed more strongly to high frequencies, reducing in-band noise at the same oversampling ratio
  3. CThe decimation filter becomes unnecessary
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. Quantization noise is pushed more strongly to high frequencies, reducing in-band noise at the same oversampling ratio

A ΔΣ modulator pushes quantization noise to higher frequencies (noise shaping), and a higher order suppresses the low-frequency noise more strongly. The in-band SNR therefore improves at the same oversampling ratio. However, higher-order modulators tend to become unstable and need careful design.

  • A. Lowering the sampling frequency is not the direct effect of raising the order.
  • B. Correct. Noise shaping becomes stronger and in-band noise decreases.
  • C. A decimation filter is still needed to remove the high-frequency noise.
