AD/DA conversion

What is the brief spike-like error that appears at the output of a DA converter at the moment the input code changes?

  1. AJitter
  2. BOffset
  3. CGlitch
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. Glitch

A glitch is a temporary disturbance of the output caused, for example, by slight timing differences between switches when several bits change at once. It tends to be largest near mid-scale codes where many bits change together (for example, from 0111… to 1000…), and it affects the performance of high-speed DACs.
