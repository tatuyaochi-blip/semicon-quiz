A/D and D/A conversion

What sampling frequency is needed to sample an analog signal containing components up to 20 kHz without aliasing?

  1. A10 kHz or more
  2. BExactly 20 kHz
  3. CA frequency higher than 40 kHz
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. A frequency higher than 40 kHz

The sampling theorem says a signal can be reconstructed if sampled faster than twice its highest frequency, so 20 kHz content requires more than 40 kHz. Since filters cannot cut off instantly, practical systems add margin: audio CDs sample at 44.1 kHz, using 20–22.05 kHz as the filter's transition band. Half the sampling frequency is called the Nyquist frequency.
