AD/DA conversion

Which correctly describes "sampling" in AD conversion?

  1. ARounding the signal amplitude to discrete values
  2. BTaking the value of a continuous signal at regular time intervals
  3. CRemoving noise from the signal
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. Taking the value of a continuous signal at regular time intervals

Sampling means taking values from a time-continuous signal at regular intervals. Rounding those values to discrete steps is called quantization. AD conversion consists of these two processes: sampling and quantization.

  • A. Rounding values to discrete steps is quantization.
  • B. Correct. It takes values at regular time intervals.
  • C. Removing noise is the job of a filter.
