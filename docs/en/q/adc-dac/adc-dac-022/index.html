AD/DA conversion

In the transfer characteristic of an ADC, what is the error called when the output deviates from 0 at zero input?

  1. AOffset error
  2. BGain error
  3. CDifferential nonlinearity
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. Offset error

Offset error is a parallel shift of the entire transfer curve, seen as nonzero output at zero input, mainly caused by comparator or amplifier offset voltage; a slope error is gain error. Both are linear errors, so a two-point calibration—measuring zero and a known voltage to derive correction factors—removes them. Nonlinear errors such as INL and DNL cannot be removed by simple calibration.
