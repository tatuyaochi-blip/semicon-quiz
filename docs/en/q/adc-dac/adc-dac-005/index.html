AD/DA conversion

What is the maximum quantization error of an ideal AD converter? (LSB: the voltage corresponding to one least significant bit)

  1. A±2 LSB
  2. B±1 LSB
  3. C±1/2 LSB
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. ±1/2 LSB

Because a continuous analog value is rounded to steps of a fixed width (1 LSB), conversion always introduces error. This quantization error is at most ±1/2 LSB in an ideal ADC. More bits make 1 LSB smaller and reduce quantization error.
