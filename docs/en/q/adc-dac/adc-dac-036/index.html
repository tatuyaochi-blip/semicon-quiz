AD/DA conversion

What is the sampling frequency of a music CD?

  1. A44.1 kHz
  2. B8 kHz
  3. C1 MHz
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. 44.1 kHz

Audio CDs store 44.1 kHz, 16-bit, two-channel audio at about 1.4 Mb/s. The rate slightly exceeds twice the ~20 kHz upper limit of hearing and is said to derive from figures convenient for recording on professional video equipment of the time. Sixteen bits give about 96 dB of dynamic range. Broadcasting and video use 48 kHz, high-resolution audio uses 96 or 192 kHz at 24 bits, and telephone speech uses 8 kHz.

  • A. Correct. CDs sample at 44.1 kHz.
  • B. 8 kHz is used for telephone speech and is too low for music.
  • C. 1 MHz is far higher than the CD standard.
