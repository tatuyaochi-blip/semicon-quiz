AD/DA conversion

What is the sampling frequency of a music CD?

  1. A44.1 kHz
  2. B8 kHz
  3. C1 MHz
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. 44.1 kHz

Music CDs record audio at a sampling frequency of 44.1 kHz with 16 bits. Because this is more than twice the roughly 20 kHz upper limit of human hearing, the sampling theorem allows audible sound to be reproduced. 8 kHz has been used for telephone speech.

  • A. Correct. CDs sample at 44.1 kHz.
  • B. 8 kHz is used for telephone speech and is too low for music.
  • C. 1 MHz is far higher than the CD standard.
