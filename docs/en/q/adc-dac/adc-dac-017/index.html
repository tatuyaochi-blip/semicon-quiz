AD/DA conversion

Which AD converter splits conversion into several stages processed like an assembly line, combining high speed with medium to high resolution?

  1. ADual-slope
  2. BDelta-sigma
  3. CPipeline
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. Pipeline

A pipeline ADC converts a few bits in each stage, amplifies the remaining error (residue) and passes it to the next stage. Because each stage processes a different sample in parallel, it combines conversion rates of tens to hundreds of MS/s with resolutions of about 10 to 14 bits, and is used in communications and video.
