AD/DA conversion

What does an AD converter (ADC) do?

  1. AConverts an analog signal into digital values
  2. BConverts digital values into an analog signal
  3. CConverts a DC voltage into an AC voltage
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. Converts an analog signal into digital values

An ADC (analog-to-digital converter) samples a continuous analog voltage, such as from a sensor, at regular intervals and converts it into discrete numbers (digital values). Converting digital values back to an analog signal is the job of a DA converter (DAC).

  • A. Correct. It converts analog to digital.
  • B. Converting digital to analog is the job of a DA converter.
  • C. Converting DC to AC is the job of an inverter.
