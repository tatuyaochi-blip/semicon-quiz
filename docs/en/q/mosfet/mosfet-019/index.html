MOSFET

Which expression roughly represents the delay of a CMOS logic gate? (C: load capacitance, V: supply voltage, I: drive current)

  1. AC × V ÷ I
  2. BI ÷ (C × V)
  3. CC × V × I
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. C × V ÷ I

Gate delay can be viewed as the time for current I to charge or discharge load capacitance C to the supply voltage V, roughly CV/I. Scaling made circuits faster by shrinking C, but lowering V reduces I even more, so delay increases. The expression guides speed-up strategies—cutting wire capacitance, raising drive current, limiting fan-out—and is used to estimate ring-oscillator frequency.
