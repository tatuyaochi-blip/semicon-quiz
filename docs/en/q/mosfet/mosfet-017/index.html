MOSFET

What is the main reason the channel mobility of a MOSFET is lower than the mobility inside bulk silicon?

  1. ABecause the temperature in the channel is extremely low
  2. BBecause carriers in the channel flow near the Si/SiO2 interface and are easily scattered by interface roughness and charges
  3. CBecause the channel contains no impurities at all
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. Because carriers in the channel flow near the Si/SiO2 interface and are easily scattered by interface roughness and charges

A MOSFET channel forms within a few nm of the gate-dielectric interface. Carriers are scattered by interface roughness, charges at the interface and in the dielectric, and phonons, and the stronger the gate field pulls them toward the interface, the more scattering they suffer, so mobility falls to about half of the bulk value or less. High-k films add remote-phonon scattering, so a thin SiO2 interlayer is kept at the interface to limit the mobility loss.
