MOSFET

Which phenomenon increases off-state leakage because band-to-band tunneling current flows due to the strong field where the gate overlaps the drain?

  1. ADIBL
  2. BNBTI
  3. CGIDL (gate-induced drain leakage)
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. GIDL (gate-induced drain leakage)

Even when a transistor is off, a large gate-to-drain voltage bends the bands sharply near the drain edge of the gate, and band-to-band tunneling causes current to flow—GIDL, which notably grows as the gate is driven more negative. It is a problem in devices requiring ultra-low leakage such as DRAM cell transistors and is controlled by engineering the drain-edge doping and field. DIBL is threshold lowering by drain voltage, and NBTI is a pMOS degradation mechanism.
