MOSFET

Which correctly describes punch-through in a MOSFET?

  1. ADielectric breakdown of the gate insulator
  2. BA drop in channel mobility with temperature
  3. CThe source and drain depletion layers merge and current flows that the gate cannot control
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. The source and drain depletion layers merge and current flows that the gate cannot control

With a short channel and lightly doped substrate, raising the drain voltage can make the drain depletion layer merge with the source depletion layer. Current then flows deep in the substrate regardless of the gate, and the transistor cannot be turned off. This is called punch-through.

  • A. Insulator breakdown is gate oxide breakdown, a different phenomenon.
  • B. The temperature dependence of mobility is not punch-through.
  • C. Correct. The depletion layers merge and uncontrollable current flows.
