MOSFET

Besides the gate, source and drain, what is the fourth terminal of a MOSFET?

  1. AEmitter
  2. BBody (substrate)
  3. CCollector
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. Body (substrate)

A MOSFET is a four-terminal device: gate, source, drain, and the body (substrate or back gate) where the channel forms. Discrete parts tie the body to the source internally, so they look like three-terminal devices—which creates a parasitic body diode between source and drain. In ICs, the nMOS body is tied to the lowest potential and the pMOS body (n-well) to the highest so the junctions are never forward-biased.

  • A. The emitter is a bipolar transistor terminal.
  • B. Correct. The fourth terminal is the body (substrate).
  • C. The collector is a bipolar transistor terminal.
