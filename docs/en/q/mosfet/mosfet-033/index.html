MOSFET

Which carriers carry the current in the channel of an n-channel MOSFET?

  1. AHoles
  2. BIons
  3. CElectrons
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. Electrons

In an n-channel MOSFET, the gate voltage attracts electrons to the surface of the p-type substrate to form an inversion layer, and these electrons flow from source to drain to carry the current. In a p-channel MOSFET, holes carry the current.

  • A. Holes carry the current in a p-channel MOSFET.
  • B. Ions do not carry current in a MOSFET channel.
  • C. Correct. Electrons carry the current in an n-channel device.
