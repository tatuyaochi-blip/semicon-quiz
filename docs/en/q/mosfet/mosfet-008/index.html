MOSFET

Which value is closest to the theoretical lower limit of a MOSFET's subthreshold swing at room temperature (the gate voltage needed to change the drain current by one decade below threshold)?

  1. AAbout 600 mV/dec
  2. BAbout 6 mV/dec
  3. CAbout 60 mV/dec
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. About 60 mV/dec

Even below the threshold voltage, drain current flows and depends exponentially on gate voltage. At room temperature, the theoretical lower limit set by thermal energy is about 60 mV/dec. Because switching cannot be steeper than this, lowering the threshold voltage increases off-state leakage, which is a barrier to lowering supply voltages.
