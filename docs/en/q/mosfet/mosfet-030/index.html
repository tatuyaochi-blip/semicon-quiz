MOSFET

Which combination lists the main components of leakage current in a MOSFET in the off state?

  1. ASubthreshold leakage, gate leakage and junction leakage
  2. BOnly photocurrent
  3. CNo leakage current flows at all
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. Subthreshold leakage, gate leakage and junction leakage

Even an off MOSFET leaks: subthreshold leakage below threshold, gate leakage through the dielectric, and junction leakage at the source/drain junctions (including GIDL). Each device leaks less than a nanoampere, but billions of them add up to watts in standby. Countermeasures combine high-k dielectrics, multiple threshold voltages, power gating of idle blocks, and body-bias control.
