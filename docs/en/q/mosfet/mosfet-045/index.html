MOSFET

Under Dennard (constant-field) scaling, which statement is correct when dimensions and voltages are each scaled by 1/k?

  1. APower density increases by a factor of k²
  2. BCircuit delay becomes 1/k, and power density stays constant
  3. CCircuit delay increases by a factor of k
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. Circuit delay becomes 1/k, and power density stays constant

Under Dennard scaling, with dimensions and voltages scaled by 1/k and doping by k, the internal field stays constant, delay becomes 1/k and power per device 1/k²; since area also shrinks by 1/k², power density stays constant. Since the mid-2000s, when threshold and supply voltages became hard to lower, this relationship has broken down.

  • A. Because voltage is also reduced, power density ideally stays constant.
  • B. Correct. Circuits get faster and power density stays constant.
  • C. Smaller dimensions reduce delay (make circuits faster).
