MOSFET

Under Dennard (constant-field) scaling, which statement is correct when dimensions and voltages are each scaled by 1/k?

  1. ACircuit delay becomes 1/k, and power density stays constant
  2. BCircuit delay increases by a factor of k
  3. CPower density increases by a factor of k²
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. Circuit delay becomes 1/k, and power density stays constant

Under Dennard (constant-field) scaling, shrinking dimensions and voltage by 1/k and raising doping by k keeps the field constant: delay falls to 1/k, per-device power to 1/k², and area to 1/k², so power density stays constant. Published in 1974, it guided scaling for nearly 30 years. From the mid-2000s, subthreshold leakage prevented further cuts in Vth and supply voltage, scaling broke down, clock frequencies stalled and the industry turned to multicore.

  • A. Correct. Circuits get faster and power density stays constant.
  • B. Smaller dimensions reduce delay (make circuits faster).
  • C. Because voltage is also reduced, power density ideally stays constant.
