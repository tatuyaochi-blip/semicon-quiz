MOSFET

Which is a characteristic of a MOSFET source follower (common-drain) circuit?

  1. AIts voltage gain is slightly less than 1 and its output impedance is low
  2. BIts voltage gain is very large and its output is inverted
  3. CIts current gain is zero
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. Its voltage gain is slightly less than 1 and its output impedance is low

In a source follower the source tracks the gate minus VGS, so the voltage gain is slightly below 1 (gmRs/(1 + gmRs)) and non-inverting; the body effect lowers it further. The input impedance is essentially infinite (it is a gate), and the output impedance is low, about 1/gm, so it serves as a buffer. It is the counterpart of the emitter follower, but its level shift (VGS) is larger than VBE and varies more.
