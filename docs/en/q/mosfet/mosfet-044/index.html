MOSFET

Power MOSFETs are easy to use in parallel. Which statement about the temperature dependence of on-resistance explains why?

  1. AOn-resistance is independent of temperature
  2. BOn-resistance falls as temperature rises, so current concentrates further in the hottest device
  3. COn-resistance rises with temperature, so a device that heats up naturally carries less current and the current spreads out
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. On-resistance rises with temperature, so a device that heats up naturally carries less current and the current spreads out

A MOSFET's on-resistance increases with temperature because mobility decreases (a positive temperature coefficient). If one of several parallel devices heats up, its resistance rises and its current falls, shifting current to the others. Thermal runaway is therefore unlikely, and parallel connection is easy.

  • A. On-resistance changes considerably with temperature.
  • B. Resistance falling with temperature and concentrating current is the property behind thermal runaway in bipolar transistors.
  • C. Correct. The positive temperature coefficient naturally spreads the current.
