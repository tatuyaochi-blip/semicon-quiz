MOSFET

Which correctly describes latch-up in CMOS circuits?

  1. ACharge gradually builds up in the gate insulator and the threshold voltage shifts
  2. BMetal atoms in the wiring move with the current and cause an open circuit
  3. CA parasitic thyristor formed by the wells and substrate turns on and a large current keeps flowing between supply and ground
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. A parasitic thyristor formed by the wells and substrate turns on and a large current keeps flowing between supply and ground

In CMOS, the pMOS and nMOS diffusions, wells and substrate form a parasitic PNPN structure (a thyristor). If it is triggered by noise or similar, a large current keeps flowing between supply and ground until the power is removed. It is prevented with contacts that firmly tie the well potentials and with guard rings.

  • A. Charge buildup in the insulator is a degradation mechanism such as BTI.
  • B. Metal atoms moving with current is electromigration.
  • C. Correct. A parasitic thyristor turns on and conducts a large current.
