MOSFET

Which is a characteristic of a depletion-mode MOSFET?

  1. AIt has no gate
  2. BA channel exists even at a gate voltage of 0 V, so current flows
  3. CNo current flows even when the gate voltage is above the threshold voltage
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. A channel exists even at a gate voltage of 0 V, so current flows

A depletion-mode MOSFET has a channel built in, so current flows even at a gate voltage of 0 V (normally on). Applying gate voltage of the opposite polarity removes the channel and stops the current. A MOSFET that does not conduct at 0 V gate voltage (normally off) is called enhancement mode.
