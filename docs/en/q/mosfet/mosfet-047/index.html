MOSFET

In a high-frequency C-V measurement of a MOS capacitor on a p-type substrate, what happens to the capacitance when a large positive gate voltage drives the surface into strong inversion?

  1. AIt stays nearly constant at its minimum value
  2. BIt becomes infinite
  3. CIt reaches a maximum equal to the oxide capacitance Cox
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. It stays nearly constant at its minimum value

At high frequency, generation of minority carriers (electrons) in the inversion layer cannot keep up with the signal, so the inversion layer does not contribute to the capacitance. The capacitance therefore stays nearly constant at its minimum, set by Cox in series with the depletion capacitance at maximum depletion width. At low frequency the inversion layer responds and the capacitance returns to Cox.

  • A. Correct. At high frequency the inversion layer cannot follow, so the capacitance stays at its minimum.
  • B. The capacitance never becomes infinite.
  • C. It equals Cox in accumulation, or in inversion at low frequency.
