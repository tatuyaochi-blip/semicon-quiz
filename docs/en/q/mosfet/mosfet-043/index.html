MOSFET

In a CMOS inverter, what is the current that flows from the supply to ground while the input is switching called?

  1. AGate leakage current
  2. BShort-circuit (crowbar) current
  3. CSubthreshold current
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. Short-circuit (crowbar) current

While the input passes through intermediate voltages, the nMOS and pMOS are both partly on and current flows directly from supply to ground—short-circuit (crowbar) current. Slower input transitions prolong it, so rise and fall times are kept short, and design tools insert buffers to meet maximum-transition limits. With fast transitions it stays at roughly a tenth of dynamic power or less.

  • A. Gate leakage current is current through the gate insulator.
  • B. Correct. It flows while both transistors are on at the same time.
  • C. Subthreshold current is leakage in a transistor that is off.
