MOSFET

What is the condition for a long-channel nMOSFET to operate in the saturation region? (Assume VGS > Vth.)

  1. AVDS = 0
  2. BVDS < VGS − Vth
  3. CVDS ≥ VGS − Vth
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. VDS ≥ VGS − Vth

With VGS > Vth and a channel present, once VDS ≥ VGS − Vth the channel pinches off at the drain end and the drain current becomes nearly independent of VDS. This is the saturation region. When VDS < VGS − Vth, the device is in the linear (triode) region.

  • A. With VDS = 0, no current flows.
  • B. VDS < VGS − Vth is the condition for the linear region.
  • C. Correct. This is the condition for pinch-off at the drain end.
