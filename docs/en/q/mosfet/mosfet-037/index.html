MOSFET

What is the condition for a long-channel nMOSFET to operate in the saturation region? (Assume VGS > Vth.)

  1. AVDS = 0
  2. BVDS < VGS − Vth
  3. CVDS ≥ VGS − Vth
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. VDS ≥ VGS − Vth

With VGS > Vth and a channel present, once VDS ≥ VGS − Vth the gate-to-channel voltage at the drain end falls below Vth, the channel pinches off, and drain current becomes nearly independent of VDS—the saturation region. Amplifiers operate here, and the minimum VDS (= VGS − Vth) sets the lower limit of output swing. In short-channel devices velocity saturation makes current saturate at a lower VDS.

  • A. With VDS = 0, no current flows.
  • B. VDS < VGS − Vth is the condition for the linear region.
  • C. Correct. This is the condition for pinch-off at the drain end.
