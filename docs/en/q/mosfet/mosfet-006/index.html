MOSFET

In an n-channel MOSFET, what happens to the threshold voltage when a negative voltage is applied to the substrate (body) relative to the source (body bias)?

  1. AIt rises
  2. BIt falls
  3. CIt does not change
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. It rises

Reverse-biasing the source-substrate junction widens the depletion layer under the gate, and a larger gate voltage is needed to offset its charge. The threshold voltage therefore rises. This is called the body bias effect (body effect) and must be considered in circuit design.
