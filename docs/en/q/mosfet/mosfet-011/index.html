MOSFET

In a MOSFET in saturation, what is the phenomenon in which raising the drain voltage shortens the effective channel length and slightly increases the drain current?

  1. ALatch-up
  2. BBody bias effect
  3. CChannel length modulation
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. Channel length modulation

In saturation, a pinch-off point forms where the channel vanishes at the drain end. Raising the drain voltage moves it toward the source, shortening the effective channel and slightly increasing drain current—channel-length modulation. It is modeled as ID = (1/2)μCox(W/L)(VGS − Vth)²(1 + λVDS) and sets the output resistance ro ≈ 1/(λID). Because shorter channels suffer more, analog circuits use longer L.
