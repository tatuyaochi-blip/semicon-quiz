MOSFET

What failure can occur when a voltage far above the rating is applied between the gate and source of a MOSFET?

  1. ADielectric breakdown of the gate insulator
  2. BElectromigration
  3. CSoft error
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. Dielectric breakdown of the gate insulator

Gate dielectrics in advanced devices are only a few nm thick, and SiO2 breaks down at about 10 MV/cm, so a 2 nm film would fail at only a few volts. Breakdown shorts gate to channel and destroys the transistor. Static electricity (kilovolts) can easily cause it, so IC I/Os include ESD protection, and even power MOSFETs are rated around ±20 V gate-to-source. Time-dependent dielectric breakdown (TDDB) under prolonged voltage within ratings is also a reliability concern.
