MOSFET

What does the "C" (complementary) in CMOS mean?

  1. ACircuits are built only from nMOS transistors connected in series
  2. BnMOS and pMOS transistors are combined and operate in a complementary way
  3. CBipolar transistors and MOSFETs are combined
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. nMOS and pMOS transistors are combined and operate in a complementary way

CMOS is a circuit style that uses n-channel and p-channel MOSFETs in pairs. When the input changes, one turns on and the other off, so in steady state there is no current path from the supply to ground and power consumption can be kept low.

  • A. Using only nMOS transistors is NMOS logic.
  • B. Correct. nMOS and pMOS are combined in a complementary way.
  • C. Combining bipolar transistors with CMOS is called BiCMOS.
