MOSFET

Which technique locally adds extra impurities of the same type as the substrate under the channel near the source and drain edges to suppress short-channel effects?

  1. ALDD
  2. BSilicidation
  3. CHalo (pocket) implant
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. Halo (pocket) implant

A halo (pocket) implant adds impurities of the same type as the substrate around the source and drain junctions by angled ion implantation, locally raising the concentration. It limits depletion spreading and suppresses punch-through, DIBL and threshold voltage roll-off.

  • A. LDD lightly dopes part of the drain to relax the field at the drain edge.
  • B. Silicidation lowers the resistance of the source, drain and gate.
  • C. Correct. It locally raises the doping around the junctions.
