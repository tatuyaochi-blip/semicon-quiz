MOSFET

Which design makes the logic threshold of a CMOS inverter about half the supply voltage?

  1. ARemove the nMOS
  2. BSet the supply voltage to 0 V
  3. CMake the pMOS gate wider than the nMOS so that their drive strengths are balanced
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. Make the pMOS gate wider than the nMOS so that their drive strengths are balanced

Hole mobility is lower than electron mobility, so a pMOS of the same size is weaker and the switching threshold shifts toward ground. Making the pMOS 2–3 times wider than the nMOS balances drive strength, putting the threshold near half the supply, equalizing high and low noise margins and matching rise and fall delays. Wider pMOS adds area and input capacitance, so speed-critical designs sometimes use a smaller ratio deliberately.
