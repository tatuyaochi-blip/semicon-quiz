MOSFET

How does the threshold voltage change when the impurity concentration of the channel region (p-type substrate) of an nMOSFET is increased?

  1. AIt becomes higher
  2. BIt becomes lower
  3. CIt does not change
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. It becomes higher

With a higher substrate concentration, more acceptor charge in the depletion layer must be balanced before the surface inverts, so a larger gate voltage is needed. The threshold voltage therefore rises. Adjusting the threshold voltage with channel implants uses this property.

  • A. Correct. More depletion charge must be overcome before inversion.
  • B. Lowering the concentration lowers the threshold; raising it does the opposite.
  • C. Substrate doping is one of the main factors that set the threshold voltage.
