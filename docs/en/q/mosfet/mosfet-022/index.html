MOSFET

What problem results from depletion of the polysilicon on the gate electrode side of a polysilicon gate?

  1. AThe gate resistance becomes zero
  2. BThe transistor emits light
  3. CIt is equivalent to a thicker effective gate insulator, so drive current decreases
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. It is equivalent to a thicker effective gate insulator, so drive current decreases

Polysilicon is a semiconductor, so when a gate voltage is applied, a thin depletion layer also forms on the gate electrode side. This depletion layer acts as a series capacitance, making the effective gate insulator thicker and reducing drive current. The introduction of metal gates solved this problem.
