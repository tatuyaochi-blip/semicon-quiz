MOSFET

What problem results from depletion of the polysilicon on the gate electrode side of a polysilicon gate?

  1. AThe gate resistance becomes zero
  2. BThe transistor emits light
  3. CIt is equivalent to a thicker effective gate insulator, so drive current decreases
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. It is equivalent to a thicker effective gate insulator, so drive current decreases

Polysilicon is a semiconductor, so gate bias forms a depletion layer of about 0.3–0.5 nm on the gate-electrode side. It acts as a series capacitance, as if the dielectric were thicker, reducing drive current. As oxides thinned to around 1 nm this became significant. High-k/metal gate (HKMG) technology, from the 45 nm generation on, eliminated the problem by using metal electrodes.
