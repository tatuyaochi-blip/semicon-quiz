MOSFET

If only the thickness of the gate insulator is reduced, without changing its material or area, what happens to the gate capacitance per unit area?

  1. AIt increases
  2. BIt decreases
  3. CIt does not change
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. It increases

The gate behaves like a parallel-plate capacitor with Cox = ε/t per unit area. Thinning the dielectric raises capacitance, so the same gate voltage induces more channel charge, increasing drive current and suppressing short-channel effects. But once SiO2 reached about 1.2 nm, tunneling leakage hit its limit, and from the 45 nm generation HfO2-based high-k dielectrics, which can be physically thicker, have been used.
