Bipolar transistors

Which bias condition is correct when an NPN transistor is used for amplification (operated in the active region)?

  1. ABase-emitter forward-biased, base-collector reverse-biased
  2. BBase-emitter reverse-biased, base-collector forward-biased
  3. CBoth base-emitter and base-collector forward-biased
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. Base-emitter forward-biased, base-collector reverse-biased

In the active region, the base-emitter junction is forward-biased to inject carriers from the emitter into the base, and the base-collector junction is reverse-biased to sweep those carriers into the collector. When both junctions are forward-biased, the transistor is in the saturation region, which corresponds to a switch in the on state.
