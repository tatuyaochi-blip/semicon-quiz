Bipolar transistors

Which is a characteristic of a common-collector amplifier (emitter follower)?

  1. AIts voltage gain is very large
  2. BIts output phase is inverted from the input
  3. CIts voltage gain is about 1 and its output impedance is low, so it is used as a buffer
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. Its voltage gain is about 1 and its output impedance is low, so it is used as a buffer

In an emitter follower, the emitter voltage follows the base voltage at a level lower by VBE, so the voltage gain is about 1 and the phase is the same. With high input impedance and low output impedance, it is used as a buffer for impedance conversion.
