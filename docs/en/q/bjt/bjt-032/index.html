Bipolar transistors

In the circuit symbol of an NPN transistor, which way does the arrow on the emitter point?

  1. AFrom the collector toward the emitter
  2. BOutward, from the base out through the emitter
  3. CInward, from outside the emitter toward the base
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. Outward, from the base out through the emitter

The arrow in the symbol shows the direction of forward current through the base-emitter PN junction. In an NPN transistor, current flows from the base (p) to the emitter (n), so the arrow points outward on the emitter. In a PNP transistor it points inward.

  • A. The arrow shows the base-emitter forward current; it is unrelated to the collector.
  • B. Correct. In an NPN transistor the arrow points out of the emitter.
  • C. An arrow pointing inward toward the base is the PNP symbol.
