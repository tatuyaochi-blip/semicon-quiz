Bipolar transistors

What does the transition frequency fT of a bipolar transistor mean?

  1. AThe frequency of the power supply
  2. BThe frequency at which the common-emitter current gain falls to 1
  3. CThe frequency at which a circuit oscillates
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. The frequency at which the common-emitter current gain falls to 1

A transistor's current gain decreases at higher frequencies. The frequency at which the common-emitter current gain becomes 1 is called the transition frequency fT, and it is an indicator of the transistor's high-frequency performance.
