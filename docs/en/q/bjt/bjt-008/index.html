Bipolar transistors

Which correctly describes the Early effect in a bipolar transistor?

  1. AThe current gain falls as temperature rises
  2. BAs the collector-base reverse bias increases, the effective base width narrows and the collector current increases
  3. CBase current flows when light shines on the device
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. As the collector-base reverse bias increases, the effective base width narrows and the collector current increases

Raising the collector voltage widens the collector-base depletion region into the base, shrinking the effective base width. The minority-carrier gradient steepens and collector current rises slightly—the Early effect. Extending the output curves to the left, they meet the voltage axis near one point (−VA, the Early voltage). A larger VA means a higher output resistance ro = VA/IC and a higher maximum amplifier gain gm·ro = VA/VT.
