Bipolar transistors

When a switching transistor is turned off from deep saturation, what is the main reason it takes time to turn off?

  1. AThe transistor's temperature drops
  2. BThe junction capacitance becomes zero
  3. CIt takes time to remove the excess minority carriers stored in the base region
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. It takes time to remove the excess minority carriers stored in the base region

In saturation the base-collector junction is also forward-biased, and excess minority carriers build up in the base and collector. When turning off, collector current continues until they are removed or recombine (storage time ts). Remedies include Schottky clamping to prevent deep saturation, pulling charge out quickly with reverse base current (e.g., a speed-up capacitor), and avoiding excessive base overdrive.
