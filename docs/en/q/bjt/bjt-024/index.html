Bipolar transistors

What is the role of a coupling capacitor placed between amplifier stages?

  1. ATo amplify the signal
  2. BTo isolate the DC bias of each stage and pass only the AC signal
  3. CTo convert AC to DC
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. To isolate the DC bias of each stage and pass only the AC signal

A capacitor blocks DC and passes AC. A coupling capacitor between stages keeps the DC bias voltages of the preceding and following stages from affecting each other while passing only the AC signal. Its impedance rises at low frequencies, which also reduces low-frequency gain.
