Bipolar transistors

What is the approximate common-emitter current gain β of a bipolar transistor whose common-base current gain α is 0.99?

  1. A0.99
  2. B9.9
  3. C99
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. 99

α and β are related by β = α/(1 − α), so α = 0.99 gives β = 0.99 ÷ 0.01 = 99; conversely α = β/(1 + β). With α = 0.995, β becomes 199—a tiny change in α makes a big change in β. If 1% of the emitter current (1 − α) is lost in the base, the base current is about 1/99 of the collector current, which is why minimizing base losses is central to transistor design.
