Bipolar transistors

Which model represents the large-signal behavior of a bipolar transistor with two diodes and current sources?

  1. AShockley's depletion approximation
  2. BThe Poisson model
  3. CThe Ebers-Moll model
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. The Ebers-Moll model

The Ebers-Moll model represents the base-emitter and base-collector junctions as diodes, each paired with a dependent current source proportional to the other junction's current. One set of equations covers the active, saturation, cutoff and reverse-active regions. Adding the Early effect, high injection and charge storage gives the Gummel-Poon model, the basis of SPICE bipolar models.

  • A. The depletion approximation is used to analyze the depletion layer of a PN junction, not to model a whole transistor.
  • B. The Poisson model is used for calculations such as yield.
  • C. Correct. It uses two diodes and dependent current sources.
