Bipolar transistors

Which correctly relates BVCEO, the collector-emitter breakdown voltage with the base open, and BVCBO, the collector-base breakdown voltage with the emitter open?

  1. ABVCEO and BVCBO are always equal
  2. BBVCEO is lower than BVCBO
  3. CBVCEO is higher than BVCBO
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. BVCEO is lower than BVCBO

With the base open, holes generated by avalanche at the collector junction accumulate in the base and act like base current, which the transistor then amplifies. Current therefore runs away once the multiplication factor M reaches only about 1 + 1/hFE, giving BVCEO ≈ BVCBO/hFE^(1/n) (n about 3–6)—sometimes a fraction of BVCBO. Designers use the lower BVCEO as the voltage rating, with margin.

  • A. The two breakdown voltages generally differ.
  • B. Correct. Because of the gain, BVCEO is lower than BVCBO.
  • C. Transistor action makes BVCEO the lower of the two.
