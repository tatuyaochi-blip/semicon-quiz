Bipolar transistors

In a PNP transistor, which carriers are injected from the emitter into the base and mainly carry the current?

  1. AElectrons
  2. BHoles
  3. CIons
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. Holes

In a PNP transistor, holes are injected from the p-type emitter into the n-type base, cross the base, and are collected by the p-type collector. In an NPN transistor, electrons play the same role. Because electrons have higher mobility, NPN transistors are often used in high-speed applications.
