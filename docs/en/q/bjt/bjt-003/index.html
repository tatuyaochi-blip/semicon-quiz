Bipolar transistors

What is the main reason for making the base of a bipolar transistor thin?

  1. ATo raise the collector breakdown voltage
  2. BTo lower the base resistance
  3. CTo reduce recombination in the base and increase the current gain
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. To reduce recombination in the base and increase the current gain

Carriers injected from the emitter partly recombine while crossing the base, and that loss forms part of the base current. Making the base much thinner than the diffusion length shortens the transit time so nearly all carriers reach the collector, raising current gain and improving high-frequency performance. The trade-off is higher base resistance and a greater risk of punch-through, where the collector depletion region reaches the emitter, which limits breakdown voltage.
