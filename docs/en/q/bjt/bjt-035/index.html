Bipolar transistors

What is the state of the junctions when a bipolar transistor is in the cutoff region (off)?

  1. ABoth the base-emitter and base-collector junctions are reverse biased
  2. BThe base-emitter junction is forward biased and the base-collector junction is reverse biased
  3. CBoth junctions are forward biased
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. Both the base-emitter and base-collector junctions are reverse biased

In the cutoff region, both the base-emitter and base-collector junctions are reverse biased (or the base-emitter voltage does not reach the forward voltage), and almost no collector current flows. This is the off state when the transistor is used as a switch.

  • A. Correct. Cutoff is when both junctions are reverse biased.
  • B. This combination is the active region, used for amplification.
  • C. Both junctions forward biased is the saturation region.
