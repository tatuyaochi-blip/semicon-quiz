Bipolar transistors

What is the difference between the DC current gain hFE and the small-signal current gain hfe?

  1. AhFE is the DC ratio IC/IB, while hfe is the ratio of small changes around the operating point, ΔIC/ΔIB
  2. BhFE is the ratio of small-signal changes, while hfe is the DC ratio
  3. CThey are just different names for the same quantity
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. hFE is the DC ratio IC/IB, while hfe is the ratio of small changes around the operating point, ΔIC/ΔIB

hFE is the DC ratio IC/IB, while hfe is the small-signal ratio ΔIC/ΔIB around the operating point. In h-parameter notation, uppercase subscripts denote DC and lowercase denote small-signal. Because hFE varies with current, the two generally differ, though they are often close and treated as equal in rough estimates. Use hFE for DC bias calculations and hfe for AC gain and input-resistance calculations.

  • A. Correct. It is the difference between the DC ratio and the small-signal ratio.
  • B. Uppercase F denotes DC and lowercase f denotes small-signal, so this is reversed.
  • C. They are defined differently and are generally not equal.
