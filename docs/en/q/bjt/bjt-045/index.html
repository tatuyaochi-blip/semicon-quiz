Bipolar transistors

What is the difference between the DC current gain hFE and the small-signal current gain hfe?

  1. AhFE is the ratio of small-signal changes, while hfe is the DC ratio
  2. BThey are just different names for the same quantity
  3. ChFE is the DC ratio IC/IB, while hfe is the ratio of small changes around the operating point, ΔIC/ΔIB
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. hFE is the DC ratio IC/IB, while hfe is the ratio of small changes around the operating point, ΔIC/ΔIB

hFE is the ratio of DC collector current to base current, IC/IB. hfe is the ratio ΔIC/ΔIB when the signal changes slightly around a given operating point. The two are often similar but generally not equal, because hFE varies with current.

  • A. Uppercase F denotes DC and lowercase f denotes small-signal, so this is reversed.
  • B. They are defined differently and are generally not equal.
  • C. Correct. It is the difference between the DC ratio and the small-signal ratio.
