Bipolar transistors

What does the load line drawn on the output characteristics of a transistor amplifier represent?

  1. AThe transistor's maximum ratings
  2. BThe relationship between temperature and collector current
  3. CThe possible combinations of VCE and IC set by the load resistor and supply voltage
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. The possible combinations of VCE and IC set by the load resistor and supply voltage

With a collector resistor RC and supply VCC, VCE = VCC − IC × RC, and drawing this line on the output characteristics gives the load line. Its endpoints are VCE = VCC at IC = 0 (cutoff) and IC = VCC/RC at VCE = 0 (saturation). Where it meets an IB curve is the operating point, and the signal moves the operating point along the line. When there is a separate AC load, two load lines with different slopes—DC and AC—are considered.

  • A. Maximum ratings are shown by curves such as the dissipation limit, which are separate from the load line.
  • B. The load line does not express a relationship with temperature.
  • C. Correct. It shows the combinations of VCE and IC that the external circuit allows.
