Bipolar transistors

Which correctly relates the emitter current IE, base current IB and collector current IC of a bipolar transistor?

  1. AIE = IC − IB
  2. BIE = IB × IC
  3. CIE = IB + IC
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. IE = IB + IC

The currents into and out of a transistor must balance (Kirchhoff's current law), so IE = IB + IC. In an NPN, current entering at the base and collector leaves together through the emitter. Since IB is about 1/100 of IC, IE ≈ IC is often a good approximation—for instance, collector current can be estimated from the voltage across the emitter resistor. The relation β = α/(1 − α) also follows from this equation.

  • A. In an NPN transistor, both the base and collector currents flow into the emitter, so they add rather than subtract.
  • B. The currents add; they do not multiply.
  • C. Correct. By Kirchhoff's current law, IE = IB + IC.
