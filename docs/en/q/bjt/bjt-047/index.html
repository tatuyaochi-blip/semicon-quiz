Bipolar transistors

How does the current gain hFE of a bipolar transistor depend on collector current?

  1. AIt peaks at moderate currents and falls at both low and high currents
  2. BIt is constant regardless of collector current
  3. CIt increases monotonically with collector current
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. It peaks at moderate currents and falls at both low and high currents

At low currents, recombination current in the base-emitter depletion layer makes up a large part of the base current, lowering hFE. At high currents, high-injection effects and the Kirk effect (effective widening of the base) lower hFE. hFE is therefore highest at intermediate currents.

  • A. Correct. It falls on both the low-current and high-current sides.
  • B. hFE clearly changes with current.
  • C. hFE drops at high currents.
