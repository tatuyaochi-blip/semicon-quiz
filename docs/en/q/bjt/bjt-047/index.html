Bipolar transistors

How does the current gain hFE of a bipolar transistor depend on collector current?

  1. AIt peaks at moderate currents and falls at both low and high currents
  2. BIt is constant regardless of collector current
  3. CIt increases monotonically with collector current
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. It peaks at moderate currents and falls at both low and high currents

At low current, recombination current in the depletion region (proportional to exp(qV/2kT)) becomes a large share of base current and hFE falls. At high current, high injection—injected carriers exceeding base doping—and the Kirk effect, where the base effectively widens into the collector, also reduce hFE. hFE therefore peaks at moderate current, so designers check the datasheet's hFE-IC curve and operate where hFE is high.

  • A. Correct. It falls on both the low-current and high-current sides.
  • B. hFE clearly changes with current.
  • C. hFE drops at high currents.
