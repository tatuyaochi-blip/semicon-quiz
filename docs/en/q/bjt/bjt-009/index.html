Bipolar transistors

Which is a characteristic of a Darlington connection of two transistors?

  1. AThe two are paralleled to double the breakdown voltage
  2. BIt is a connection used to build oscillators
  3. CThe overall current gain is roughly the product of the individual current gains
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. The overall current gain is roughly the product of the individual current gains

In a Darlington connection, the emitter current of the first transistor drives the base of the second. The overall current gain is about hFE1 × hFE2, giving a very large current gain. In return, the base-emitter voltage roughly doubles and switching becomes slower.
