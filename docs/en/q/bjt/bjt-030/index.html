Bipolar transistors

Which pairing of a technology that integrates bipolar transistors and CMOS on the same chip with its purpose is correct?

  1. ASOI: isolating the substrate with an insulating layer
  2. BBiCMOS: combining the high density and low power of CMOS with the speed and drive capability of bipolar transistors
  3. CFinFET: making the channel three-dimensional
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. BiCMOS: combining the high density and low power of CMOS with the speed and drive capability of bipolar transistors

BiCMOS lets designers use high-density, low-power CMOS for logic and bipolar transistors for high-speed circuits, driving heavy loads, and analog and RF circuits. SiGe BiCMOS, which adds SiGe HBTs, is used in communication ICs.
