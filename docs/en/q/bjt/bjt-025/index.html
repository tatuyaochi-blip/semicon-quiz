Bipolar transistors

How does the current gain hFE of a silicon bipolar transistor generally change as temperature rises?

  1. AIt increases
  2. BIt decreases
  3. CIt does not change
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. It increases

A silicon transistor's hFE generally increases with temperature, by roughly 0.5–1% per °C. In addition, VBE drops about 2 mV/°C and the collector-base leakage ICBO roughly doubles every 10 °C. Together these raise collector current as temperature climbs, so the operating point is stabilized with emitter-resistor feedback and similar techniques. Conversely, hFE falls at low temperature, so beware of insufficient drive.
