Bipolar transistors

How does the current gain hFE of a silicon bipolar transistor generally change as temperature rises?

  1. AIt increases
  2. BIt decreases
  3. CIt does not change
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. It increases

The hFE of silicon transistors generally increases with temperature. This is one reason the operating point shifts with temperature, so bias circuits are designed to stabilize it. The decrease in base-emitter voltage with rising temperature also increases the collector current.
