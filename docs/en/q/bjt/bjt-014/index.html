Bipolar transistors

Which correctly describes thermal runaway in a bipolar transistor?

  1. AOperation stops because the temperature drops too much
  2. BThe current suddenly becomes zero
  3. CRising temperature increases the collector current, which generates more heat, and this positive feedback leads to destruction
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. Rising temperature increases the collector current, which generates more heat, and this positive feedback leads to destruction

In a bipolar transistor, the collector current increases with temperature even at the same base-emitter voltage. If more current produces more heat, raising the temperature further, positive feedback drives the current up without limit until the device is destroyed. It is prevented by negative feedback from an emitter resistor and adequate heat sinking.
