Bipolar transistors

Which is a characteristic of a SiGe HBT (heterojunction bipolar transistor)?

  1. AIt is a transistor with a metal base
  2. BThanks to a heterojunction with a SiGe base, it can operate up to high frequencies
  3. CIts current gain is fixed at 1 or less
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. Thanks to a heterojunction with a SiGe base, it can operate up to high frequencies

A SiGe HBT is a heterojunction bipolar transistor with a narrow-bandgap SiGe base. The bandgap difference between emitter and base suppresses hole back-injection, so the base can be heavily doped and thin without losing injection efficiency, and grading the Ge content creates a field in the base that accelerates electrons. With fT reaching hundreds of GHz on a silicon process, it is used in smartphone RF circuits, optical communications and automotive radar.
