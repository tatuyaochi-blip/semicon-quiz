Bipolar transistors

When a bipolar transistor is used as a switch, which operating region corresponds to the on state?

  1. ACutoff region
  2. BActive region
  3. CSaturation region
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. Saturation region

As a switch, the off state corresponds to the cutoff region, where both junctions are reverse-biased, and the on state to the saturation region, where both are forward-biased. In saturation, the collector-emitter voltage is small, close to a closed switch. The active region is used for amplification.
