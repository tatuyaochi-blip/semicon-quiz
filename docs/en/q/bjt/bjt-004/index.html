Bipolar transistors

When a bipolar transistor is used as a switch, which operating region corresponds to the on state?

  1. ACutoff region
  2. BActive region
  3. CSaturation region
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. Saturation region

As a switch, off corresponds to cutoff (both junctions reverse-biased) and on to saturation (both forward-biased). In saturation VCE(sat) is only about 0.1–0.3 V, close to a closed switch. To guarantee saturation, the base is typically driven with 2–10 times IC ÷ hFE (overdrive). The deeper the saturation, however, the more charge is stored and the slower the transistor turns off.
