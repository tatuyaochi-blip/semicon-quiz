Bipolar transistors

What is the collector dissipation of a transistor operating at a collector-emitter voltage of 5 V and a collector current of 100 mA?

  1. A50 W
  2. B0.05 W
  3. C0.5 W
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. 0.5 W

The power dissipated as heat (collector dissipation) is PC ≈ VCE × IC = 5 V × 0.1 A = 0.5 W. Temperature rise is dissipation × thermal resistance; in a TO-92 package at about 200 °C/W, 0.5 W raises the junction by about 100 °C, which can exceed the junction limit (around 150 °C) depending on ambient. Datasheet maximum dissipation is specified at 25 °C ambient and must be derated at higher temperatures.

  • A. 50 W results from mistaking the current for 100 A.
  • B. 0.05 W is an order of magnitude too small.
  • C. Correct. 5 V × 0.1 A = 0.5 W.
