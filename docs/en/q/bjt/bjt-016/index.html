Bipolar transistors

What is the typical collector-emitter voltage of a silicon bipolar transistor in the saturation region?

  1. AAbout 0.1–0.3 V
  2. BAbout 5 V
  3. CAbout 20 V
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. About 0.1–0.3 V

In saturation both junctions are forward-biased and their forward voltages largely cancel, so VCE(sat) is small, about 0.1–0.3 V. This is the on-state drop of the switch, and the loss is VCE(sat) × IC. At high current the collector's resistance adds to it. Compared with the drop across a MOSFET's on-resistance, MOSFETs often win at low voltage and high current.
