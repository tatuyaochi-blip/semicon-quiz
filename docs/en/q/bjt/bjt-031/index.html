Bipolar transistors

Which set correctly names the three terminals of a bipolar transistor?

  1. AEmitter, base, collector
  2. BAnode, cathode, gate
  3. CSource, gate, drain
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. Emitter, base, collector

A bipolar transistor's terminals are the emitter, which injects (emits) carriers; the base, which controls the current; and the collector, which collects carriers. Their roles are fixed by structure—the emitter is heavily doped and the collector lightly doped and larger—so swapping emitter and collector greatly reduces current gain. The name "base" comes from the first point-contact transistor, where it was the supporting base.

  • A. Correct. Emitter, base and collector.
  • B. Anode, cathode and gate are thyristor terminals.
  • C. Source, gate and drain are MOSFET terminals.
