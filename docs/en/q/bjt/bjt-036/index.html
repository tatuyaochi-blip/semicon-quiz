Bipolar transistors

What is the typical base-emitter voltage VBE of a silicon bipolar transistor operating in the active region?

  1. AAbout 0.6 to 0.7 V
  2. BAbout 0.1 V
  3. CAbout 3 V
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. About 0.6 to 0.7 V

The base-emitter junction is a silicon PN junction, so the voltage across it when conducting forward current is about 0.6 to 0.7 V. In circuit design, VBE ≈ 0.7 V is often used for calculations.

  • A. Correct. For silicon it is about 0.6 to 0.7 V.
  • B. At about 0.1 V almost no current flows through a silicon junction.
  • C. About 3 V is far too large for the forward voltage of a silicon PN junction.
