Bipolar transistors

What is the typical base-emitter voltage VBE of a silicon bipolar transistor operating in the active region?

  1. AAbout 0.6 to 0.7 V
  2. BAbout 0.1 V
  3. CAbout 3 V
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. About 0.6 to 0.7 V

The base-emitter junction is a silicon PN junction, so VBE is about 0.6–0.7 V in the active region, and designers often use VBE ≈ 0.7 V. More precisely IC ∝ exp(VBE/VT): VBE rises about 60 mV per tenfold increase in current and falls about 2 mV per °C. These properties make VBE useful in temperature sensors and bandgap voltage references.

  • A. Correct. For silicon it is about 0.6 to 0.7 V.
  • B. At about 0.1 V almost no current flows through a silicon junction.
  • C. About 3 V is far too large for the forward voltage of a silicon PN junction.
