Bipolar transistors

Which value is closest to the maximum power efficiency of an ideal class B push-pull amplifier?

  1. AAbout 100%
  2. BAbout 78.5%
  3. CAbout 25%
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. About 78.5%

In class B amplification, each of the two transistors operates for half of the cycle, and almost no current flows when there is no signal. The ideal maximum efficiency is π/4 ≈ 78.5%, higher than class A, which always carries bias current (maximum 25% with a resistive load).

  • A. Voltage drops across the transistors mean linear amplification cannot reach 100%.
  • B. Correct. The theoretical maximum efficiency is π/4 ≈ 78.5%.
  • C. About 25% is the maximum efficiency of a class A amplifier with a resistive load.
