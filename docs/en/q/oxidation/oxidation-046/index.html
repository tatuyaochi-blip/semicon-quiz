Oxidation and diffusion

In LOCOS, what is the phenomenon called in which a thin nitride forms on the silicon surface under the nitride mask, later causing the gate oxide there to be locally thin or defective?

  1. ADishing
  2. BBird's beak
  3. CKooi effect (white ribbon)
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. Kooi effect (white ribbon)

During field oxidation, the nitride reacts with steam to form ammonia and the like, which can nitride the silicon surface under the edge of the nitride mask. This nitride makes the oxide thinner there during later gate oxidation, causing breakdown failures. It is called the Kooi effect and is countered by growing and removing a sacrificial oxide.

  • A. Dishing is the recessing of a line's center during CMP.
  • B. Bird's beak is the oxide encroaching under the edge of the nitride mask.
  • C. Correct. A nitride locally degrades the gate oxide.
