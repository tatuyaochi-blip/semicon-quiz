Oxidation and diffusion

What is the heat treatment called that heats wafers one at a time to high temperature for a few seconds using lamps or similar sources?

  1. ABatch furnace annealing
  2. BRTP (rapid thermal processing)
  3. CCMP
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. RTP (rapid thermal processing)

RTP (rapid thermal processing) heats and cools wafers rapidly with halogen lamps or similar sources. Because the time at high temperature is short, activation annealing and thin oxide formation can be done while suppressing dopant diffusion.
