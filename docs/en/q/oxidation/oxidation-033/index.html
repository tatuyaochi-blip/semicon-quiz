Oxidation and diffusion

Which correctly describes "diffusion" of impurities in semiconductor manufacturing?

  1. AA method that accelerates impurities in one direction with an electric field and implants them
  2. BDissolving impurities in a liquid and washing them away
  3. CThe thermally driven spreading of impurity atoms from regions of high concentration to regions of low concentration
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. The thermally driven spreading of impurity atoms from regions of high concentration to regions of low concentration

Diffusion is the spreading of impurities from high-concentration regions to low-concentration regions by the thermal motion of atoms; it proceeds faster at higher temperatures. It was once used to introduce impurities from the surface, but today diffusion mostly occurs during the heat treatment after impurities are introduced by ion implantation.

  • A. Accelerating and implanting with an electric field is ion implantation.
  • B. Washing away is cleaning, not diffusion.
  • C. Correct. Impurities spread thermally from high to low concentration.
