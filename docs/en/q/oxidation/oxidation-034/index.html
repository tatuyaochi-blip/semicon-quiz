Oxidation and diffusion

Which is a typical use of thermally grown silicon oxide?

  1. AAs the conductor in wiring
  2. BAs gate insulators, isolation between devices, and masks that define where impurities are introduced
  3. CAs a light-emitting layer
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. As gate insulators, isolation between devices, and masks that define where impurities are introduced

Thermally grown SiO2 is highly insulating and forms a high-quality interface with silicon, so it has been used as the gate insulator of MOSFETs, for electrically isolating devices from each other, and as a mask for ion implantation and diffusion.

  • A. SiO2 is an insulator and cannot be used as a wiring conductor.
  • B. Correct. It is used for insulators, isolation and masks.
  • C. SiO2 is an insulator and is not used as a light-emitting layer.
