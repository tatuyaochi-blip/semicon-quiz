Oxidation and diffusion

In thermal diffusion, what is the step called that drives dopants deeper after a fixed amount has been introduced at the surface?

  1. APredeposition
  2. BEpitaxy
  3. CDrive-in
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. Drive-in

Thermal diffusion is often done in two steps: predeposition introduces a fixed amount of dopant at the surface, then drive-in stops the supply and heats the wafer to diffuse the dopant inward to the desired depth and profile. Today, ion implantation is generally used to introduce dopants, and subsequent heat treatment adjusts the profile.
