Oxidation and diffusion

What problem occurs when mobile ions such as sodium are present in the gate oxide?

  1. AThe oxide emits light
  2. BThe ions move under electric fields and temperature, shifting the threshold voltage
  3. CThe mechanical strength of the oxide increases
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. The ions move under electric fields and temperature, shifting the threshold voltage

Alkali metal ions such as sodium move easily in oxide, and when electric fields or temperature drive them toward the interface, they shift the threshold voltage. This was a major problem in early MOS devices, addressed by contamination control in cleanrooms, chlorine oxidation and trapping with phosphosilicate glass.
