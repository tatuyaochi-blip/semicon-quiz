Oxidation and diffusion

Which equipment is commonly used to thermally oxidize many wafers at once?

  1. AA vertical furnace (diffusion furnace)
  2. BAn ion implanter
  3. CA CMP tool
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. A vertical furnace (diffusion furnace)

Thermal oxidation, diffusion and annealing often use vertical furnaces, which heat about 100 wafers together in a quartz tube. They offer high uniformity and process many wafers at once. When short heating is needed, RTP equipment that heats wafers one at a time with lamps is used.
