Oxidation and diffusion

When thin oxides of a few tens of nanometers or less are grown by dry oxidation, which known deviation from the Deal-Grove model occurs?

  1. AInitial oxidation proceeds faster than the model predicts
  2. BOxidation does not proceed at all
  3. CThe oxide becomes a conductor
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. Initial oxidation proceeds faster than the model predicts

The Deal-Grove model describes thick-oxide growth well, but dry oxidation in the initial regime below a few tens of nm proceeds faster than predicted. Proposed causes include alternative oxidant transport through thin films and interfacial stress effects. The model compensates with an effective initial thickness (τ, equivalent to about 20 nm), but setting gate oxides of a few nm still relies on empirical calibration.

  • A. Correct. Oxidation is faster than the model in the initial thin regime.
  • B. Oxidation does proceed.
  • C. SiO2 is an insulator and does not become a conductor.
