Oxidation and diffusion

How does the diffusion coefficient of dopants in silicon depend on temperature?

  1. AIt does not depend on temperature
  2. BIt decreases as temperature rises
  3. CIt increases exponentially as temperature rises
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. It increases exponentially as temperature rises

The diffusion coefficient follows the Arrhenius form D = D0 exp(−Ea / kT) and increases exponentially with temperature. Because small temperature differences greatly change diffusion depth, temperature must be controlled precisely in diffusion and heat-treatment steps.
