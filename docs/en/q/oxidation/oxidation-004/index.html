Oxidation and diffusion

Which method is mainly used for device isolation in scaled LSIs?

  1. AWire bonding
  2. BLOCOS (local oxidation)
  3. CSTI (shallow trench isolation)
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. STI (shallow trench isolation)

Today's scaled LSIs mainly use STI, which etches shallow trenches in silicon, fills them with insulator, and planarizes them by CMP. LOCOS, once mainstream, produces a 'bird's beak' where oxide encroaches sideways, making it hard to shrink isolation width, so it was replaced by STI as scaling progressed.
