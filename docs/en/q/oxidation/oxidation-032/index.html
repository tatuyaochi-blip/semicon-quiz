Oxidation and diffusion

Which is closest to the temperature range at which silicon thermal oxidation is generally carried out?

  1. AAbout 100 to 200 °C
  2. BAbout 800 to 1,200 °C
  3. CAbout 2,000 to 3,000 °C
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. About 800 to 1,200 °C

Thermal oxidation of silicon is generally carried out at high temperatures of about 800 to 1,200 °C; the higher the temperature, the faster the oxidation. Since silicon melts at about 1,414 °C, the process is run well below that.

  • A. At about 100 to 200 °C, silicon hardly oxidizes.
  • B. Correct. It is done at about 800 to 1,200 °C.
  • C. Above about 2,000 °C exceeds silicon's melting point.
