Oxidation and diffusion

What is the main purpose of introducing nitrogen into the gate oxide to make an oxynitride (SiON)?

  1. ATo suppress boron penetration from the gate electrode and improve reliability
  2. BTo make the film conductive
  3. CTo increase the etch rate
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. To suppress boron penetration from the gate electrode and improve reliability

Boron in p+ polysilicon gates can penetrate thin gate oxide into the channel and shift the threshold voltage. Adding nitrogen suppresses boron diffusion and slightly raises the dielectric constant. It also improves reliability, such as resistance to hot carriers.
