Oxidation and diffusion

Which compound has been used as a liquid diffusion source for introducing phosphorus into silicon by thermal diffusion?

  1. AArsine (AsH3)
  2. BPhosphorus oxychloride (POCl3)
  3. CDiborane (B2H6)
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. Phosphorus oxychloride (POCl3)

Phosphorus oxychloride (POCl3) is vaporized by bubbling nitrogen through it and fed into the furnace with oxygen, forming a phosphorus-containing glass layer on the silicon surface from which phosphorus diffuses. It is still used, for example, to form the n-type layer of solar cells.

  • A. Arsine is an arsenic-containing gas.
  • B. Correct. It is a liquid phosphorus diffusion source.
  • C. Diborane is a boron-containing gas.
