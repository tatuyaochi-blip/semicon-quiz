Oxidation and diffusion

Which compound has been used as a liquid diffusion source for introducing phosphorus into silicon by thermal diffusion?

  1. AArsine (AsH3)
  2. BPhosphorus oxychloride (POCl3)
  3. CDiborane (B2H6)
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. Phosphorus oxychloride (POCl3)

Phosphorus oxychloride (POCl3), a liquid at room temperature, is vaporized by bubbling nitrogen through it and fed with oxygen into a furnace, forming phosphorus-rich glass (PSG) on the silicon from which phosphorus diffuses. It processes hundreds of wafers at once at low cost and is still widely used for the n-type emitters of crystalline-silicon solar cells. BBr3 is the liquid boron source, and phosphine and diborane are gas sources.

  • A. Arsine is an arsenic-containing gas.
  • B. Correct. It is a liquid phosphorus diffusion source.
  • C. Diborane is a boron-containing gas.
