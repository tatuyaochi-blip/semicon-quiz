Oxidation and diffusion

Which correctly describes pyrogenic oxidation (hydrogen combustion oxidation)?

  1. AA wet oxidation method using high-purity water vapor produced by burning hydrogen and oxygen
  2. BA dry oxidation method using only dry oxygen
  3. CA method of depositing oxide by CVD
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. A wet oxidation method using high-purity water vapor produced by burning hydrogen and oxygen

Pyrogenic oxidation burns hydrogen and oxygen in a torch at the furnace inlet to create steam, which oxidizes the silicon. Compared with boiling water to supply steam, it introduces less contamination and controls water vapor precisely through gas flow. Because leftover hydrogen would be explosive, systems run slightly oxygen-rich for safety. It has been widely used for thick field and masking oxides.
