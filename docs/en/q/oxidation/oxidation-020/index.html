Oxidation and diffusion

Which correctly describes pyrogenic oxidation (hydrogen combustion oxidation)?

  1. AA wet oxidation method using high-purity water vapor produced by burning hydrogen and oxygen
  2. BA dry oxidation method using only dry oxygen
  3. CA method of depositing oxide by CVD
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. A wet oxidation method using high-purity water vapor produced by burning hydrogen and oxygen

In pyrogenic oxidation, hydrogen and oxygen are burned at the furnace inlet to produce water vapor, which oxidizes the silicon. It causes less contamination than boiling deionized water and makes the amount of steam easier to control, so it is widely used for wet oxidation.
