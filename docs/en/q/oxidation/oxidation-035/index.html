Oxidation and diffusion

What is the correct unit of the diffusion coefficient D?

  1. Acm/s
  2. Bcm²/s
  3. Ccm⁻³
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. cm²/s

The diffusion coefficient expresses how much area the spread covers per unit time, so its unit is cm²/s (or m²/s). The characteristic spreading distance is √(Dt); multiplying cm²/s by time in seconds and taking the square root gives a length.

  • A. cm/s is a unit of velocity.
  • B. Correct. The diffusion coefficient has units of cm²/s.
  • C. cm⁻³ is a unit of concentration.
