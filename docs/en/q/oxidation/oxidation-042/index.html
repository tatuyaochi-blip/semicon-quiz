Oxidation and diffusion

Which generally describes the thickness relationship between the field oxide used for isolation such as LOCOS and the gate oxide of a MOSFET?

  1. AThe gate oxide is much thicker
  2. BBoth have the same thickness
  3. CThe field oxide is much thicker
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. The field oxide is much thicker

Field oxide is made thick, around hundreds of nanometers, so that parasitic transistors under wiring and similar do not turn on. Gate oxide, by contrast, is made very thin, a few nanometers, so the gate field couples strongly to the channel.

  • A. Gate oxide is made thin to transmit the field strongly.
  • B. Their roles differ, so their thicknesses differ greatly.
  • C. Correct. Isolation oxide is made much thicker.
