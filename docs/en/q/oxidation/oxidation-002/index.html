Oxidation and diffusion

When an SiO2 film of thickness x is formed by thermally oxidizing silicon, approximately how much silicon is consumed?

  1. AAbout 0.44x
  2. BAbout 1.0x
  3. CAbout 2.2x
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. About 0.44x

In thermal oxidation silicon itself reacts with oxygen to form SiO2. From the ratio of silicon's atomic density (about 5.0×10²² cm⁻³) to SiO2's molecular density (about 2.3×10²² cm⁻³), forming an oxide of thickness x consumes about 0.44x of silicon: 44% of the oxide lies below the original surface and 56% above, a volume expansion of about 2.2×. Oxidized regions therefore form steps, and the interface is always freshly formed and clean.
