Oxidation and diffusion

When an SiO2 film of thickness x is formed by thermally oxidizing silicon, approximately how much silicon is consumed?

  1. AAbout 0.44x
  2. BAbout 1.0x
  3. CAbout 2.2x
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. About 0.44x

In thermal oxidation, silicon itself reacts with oxygen to become SiO2. From the densities and molecular weights of Si and SiO2, forming oxide of thickness x consumes about 0.44x of silicon. About 44% of the oxide therefore lies below the original silicon surface and the rest above it.
