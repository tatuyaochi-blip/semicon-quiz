Oxidation and diffusion

Which is one of the major reasons silicon has been so widely used as a material for MOS devices?

  1. AIt has a direct band gap and emits light efficiently
  2. BIts electron mobility is the highest of all semiconductors
  3. CSimple thermal oxidation forms a high-quality insulator (SiO2) with few interface states
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. Simple thermal oxidation forms a high-quality insulator (SiO2) with few interface states

Silicon forms an SiO2 film with high insulating quality and few defects (interface states) at the silicon interface simply by thermal oxidation. This excellent MOS interface is one of the major reasons silicon has been widely used for MOSFETs and LSIs.
