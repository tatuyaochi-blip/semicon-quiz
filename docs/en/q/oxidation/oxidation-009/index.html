Oxidation and diffusion

How does the thermal oxidation rate of silicon depend on crystal orientation?

  1. A(100) oxidizes faster than (111)
  2. BOxidation rate is the same regardless of orientation
  3. C(111) oxidizes faster than (100)
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. (111) oxidizes faster than (100)

Under the same conditions, (111) surfaces oxidize faster than (100) surfaces because they have a higher surface atom density and more bonds available for oxidation. In structures such as FinFETs, where the sides and top have different orientations, this difference must be considered.
