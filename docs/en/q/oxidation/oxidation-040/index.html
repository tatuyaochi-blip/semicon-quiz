Oxidation and diffusion

Which set represents the commonly used classification of charges in and at the interface of the oxide in a MOS structure?

  1. AFixed charge, mobile ionic charge, oxide trapped charge and interface trapped charge
  2. BDC charge, AC charge and static charge
  3. CHole charge, electron charge and neutral charge
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. Fixed charge, mobile ionic charge, oxide trapped charge and interface trapped charge

Charges around the oxide are classified into four types: fixed charge near the interface, mobile ionic charge such as sodium, oxide trapped charge captured by defects in the film, and interface trapped charge due to interface states. All of them shift the flat-band and threshold voltages.

  • A. Correct. They are classified into these four types.
  • B. This classification is not used.
  • C. This classification is not used.
