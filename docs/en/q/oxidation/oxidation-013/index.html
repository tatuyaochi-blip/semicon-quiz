Oxidation and diffusion

What is the main purpose of adding a small amount of chlorine-containing gas such as hydrogen chloride (HCl) during thermal oxidation?

  1. ATo make the oxide conductive
  2. BTo reduce the oxidation rate to zero
  3. CTo suppress the effects of mobile ions such as sodium and of metal contamination
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. To suppress the effects of mobile ions such as sodium and of metal contamination

Oxidizing in a chlorine-containing atmosphere removes mobile ions such as sodium and metal contaminants as chlorides or neutralizes them in the oxide. As a result, the oxide's electrical characteristics and reliability improve.
