Oxidation and diffusion

Which chemical reaction occurs in the dry thermal oxidation of silicon?

  1. ASi + O2 → SiO2
  2. BSi + N2 → SiN
  3. CSiO2 + H2 → Si + H2O
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. Si + O2 → SiO2

In dry oxidation, silicon reacts with oxygen at high temperature to form silicon dioxide (Si + O2 → SiO2). Wet oxidation uses steam, with the reaction Si + 2H2O → SiO2 + 2H2.

  • A. Correct. Silicon and oxygen form SiO2.
  • B. Reaction with nitrogen does not form an oxide.
  • C. This is the reverse reaction that reduces the oxide, not oxidation.
