Oxidation and diffusion

If the thermal oxidation temperature is raised, how does the oxide thickness obtained in the same time change?

  1. AIt becomes thicker
  2. BIt becomes thinner
  3. CIt does not change
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. It becomes thicker

Both the interface reaction rate and oxidant diffusion through the oxide accelerate exponentially with temperature. One hour of wet oxidation grows about 200 nm at 900 °C but about 700 nm at 1100 °C. Thick films are grown quickly with high-temperature steam, whereas thin gate oxides are grown slowly at lower temperature or in dry oxygen for precise thickness control.
