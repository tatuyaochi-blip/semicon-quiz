Oxidation and diffusion

Which correctly expresses Fick's first law? (J: diffusion flux, D: diffusion coefficient, C: concentration, x: position)

  1. AJ = D × C
  2. BJ = −D × dC/dx
  3. CJ = −D × dx/dC
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. J = −D × dC/dx

Fick's first law states that the amount of atoms flowing by diffusion (the flux J) is proportional to the concentration gradient and flows from high to low concentration. The equation is J = −D (dC/dx); the minus sign shows flow toward decreasing concentration.

  • A. The flux is proportional to the concentration gradient, not the concentration itself.
  • B. Correct. It is proportional to the gradient and flows toward lower concentration.
  • C. The gradient is taken the wrong way around.
