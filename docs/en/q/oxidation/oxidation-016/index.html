Oxidation and diffusion

Which value is closest to the relative permittivity of SiO2 formed by thermal oxidation?

  1. AAbout 1
  2. BAbout 3.9
  3. CAbout 25
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. About 3.9

Thermal SiO2 has a relative permittivity of about 3.9, the reference for equivalent oxide thickness (EOT) when comparing gate dielectrics. SiON is about 5–6, silicon nitride (Si3N4) about 7 and high-k hafnium oxide about 20–25. Low-k interconnect dielectrics are about 3 or less, including porous films and air gaps that incorporate air (about 1). High and low permittivities are chosen according to purpose.
