Oxidation and diffusion

Which n-type dopant diffuses relatively slowly in silicon during heat treatment, making shallow junctions easier to form?

  1. ABoron (B)
  2. BPhosphorus (P)
  3. CArsenic (As)
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. Arsenic (As)

Among n-type dopants, arsenic is larger than phosphorus with a smaller diffusion coefficient, and at mass 75—more than twice phosphorus's 31—it implants shallower. Its high solubility also allows heavy doping, so it has been used for the shallow, abrupt, low-resistance source/drain regions and extensions of nMOS transistors. Faster-diffusing phosphorus is used for deep wells and, more recently, in epitaxial SiP. Boron is p-type.
