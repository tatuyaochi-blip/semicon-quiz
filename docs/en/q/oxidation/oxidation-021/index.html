Oxidation and diffusion

What is the approximate breakdown field of high-quality thermal oxide (SiO2)?

  1. AAbout 10 MV/cm
  2. BAbout 10 V/cm
  3. CAbout 10 kV/cm
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. About 10 MV/cm

The breakdown field of high-quality thermal oxide is about 10 MV/cm. For example, a 10 nm oxide would break down at about 10 V. Real devices operate at much lower fields to ensure long-term reliability.
