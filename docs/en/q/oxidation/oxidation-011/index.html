Oxidation and diffusion

Why can thermal oxide be used as a mask against dopants during diffusion?

  1. ABecause many dopants, such as boron and phosphorus, diffuse more slowly in SiO2 than in silicon
  2. BBecause SiO2 conducts electricity well
  3. CBecause SiO2 does not transmit light
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. Because many dopants, such as boron and phosphorus, diffuse more slowly in SiO2 than in silicon

Major dopants such as boron, phosphorus and arsenic diffuse much more slowly in SiO2 than in silicon, so a sufficiently thick oxide prevents them from reaching the silicon below. This property has been used for selective diffusion, introducing dopants only through windows opened in the oxide.
