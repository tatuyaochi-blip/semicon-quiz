Wireless Communication Basics

Taking the speed of light as 3×10⁸ m/s, which is closest to the wavelength in vacuum of a 2.4 GHz radio wave?

  1. A1.25 cm
  2. B12.5 cm
  3. C125 cm
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. 12.5 cm

Wavelength is found from λ = c/f. λ = 3×10⁸ ÷ (2.4×10⁹) = 0.125 m, or 12.5 cm. A frequency ten times higher gives one tenth the wavelength, so 24 GHz gives 1.25 cm and 240 MHz gives 125 cm. Antenna size scales with wavelength, so a half-wave dipole antenna at 2.4 GHz is about 6 cm long.

  • A. This is the wavelength at 24 GHz, the value obtained by taking the frequency ten times too high.
  • B. Correct. 3×10⁸ ÷ (2.4×10⁹) = 0.125 m = 12.5 cm.
  • C. This is the wavelength at 240 MHz, the value obtained by taking the frequency ten times too low.
