If a loop with 100 iterations is unrolled by replicating its body four times, how many counter checks and branches remain? (Assume 100 is divisible by 4.)

  1. A100
  2. B25
  3. C400
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. 25

With four copies of the body, each iteration does the work of four original iterations, so there are 100 ÷ 4 = 25 iterations and only 25 counter checks and branches, a quarter of the original overhead. When the count is not divisible by 4, a short loop is appended to handle the remainder. 100 is the count without unrolling, and 400 mistakenly multiplies.

  • A. 100 is the count without unrolling.
  • B. Correct. 100 ÷ 4 = 25.
  • C. 400 is a mistaken multiplication.
