IT Service Management and Systems Audit

Which of the following describes the approach of IT service management appropriately?

  1. AThe quality of an IT service is judged only from the provider's technical point of view, without considering its effect on the users' business
  2. BIT services that support the outcomes users want are delivered stably at the agreed quality and improved continually
  3. CThe role of the IT service provider ends when a new system has been developed and put into operation
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. IT services that support the outcomes users want are delivered stably at the agreed quality and improved continually

IT service management treats IT as a "service" and is the management approach for delivering services that help the outcomes of users' business stably, at the agreed quality and cost, and for continuing to improve them. The value of a service is determined by the balance between the outcomes users obtain and the costs and risks involved. ITIL is a widely referenced guide to putting this into practice around the world. Putting a system into operation is a beginning, and quality must be maintained and improved during operation.

  • A. The value of a service is determined by the outcomes users obtain, so the effect on the users' business must be considered.
  • B. Correct. Services that support users' outcomes are delivered at the agreed quality and continually improved.
  • C. Putting a system into operation is a beginning; the subsequent operation and improvement are also the role of the IT service provider.
