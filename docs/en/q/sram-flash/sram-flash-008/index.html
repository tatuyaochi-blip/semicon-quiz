SRAM and flash memory

What is the unit in which data is erased in NAND flash memory?

  1. ABit
  2. BBlock
  3. CByte
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. Block

NAND reads and programs by page (around 16 KB), but erases only by block—hundreds to thousands of pages, several to tens of MB—because erase applies high voltage to an entire well to remove electrons in bulk. Even partial updates cannot overwrite in place: new data goes to a free page, the old page is marked invalid, and garbage collection later erases blocks in bulk.
