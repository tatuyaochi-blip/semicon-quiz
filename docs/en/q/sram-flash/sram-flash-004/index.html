SRAM and flash memory

Which correctly describes how data is written to and erased from flash memory?

  1. ACharge is stored in a capacitor
  2. BThe magnetization direction of a magnetic layer is changed
  3. CElectrons are moved into or out of a floating gate or charge-trap layer, changing the transistor's threshold voltage
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. Electrons are moved into or out of a floating gate or charge-trap layer, changing the transistor's threshold voltage

A flash cell is a transistor with a floating gate (or charge-trap layer) beneath its control gate that stores electrons. Moving electrons in or out with high voltage—by tunneling, for example—shifts the threshold voltage, which distinguishes 0 from 1. Surrounded by insulating films a few nm thick, the electrons are retained for about ten years without power. Reading applies a set gate voltage and checks whether current flows.
