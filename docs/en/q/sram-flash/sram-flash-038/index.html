SRAM and flash memory

Which correctly describes the static noise margin (SNM) that represents the stability of an SRAM cell?

  1. AThe amount of noise voltage the cell can withstand without flipping its stored state, obtained from the butterfly curve
  2. BThe current consumption of the cell
  3. CThe time taken to read the cell
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. The amount of noise voltage the cell can withstand without flipping its stored state, obtained from the butterfly curve

SNM is the side of the largest square that fits inside the butterfly curve formed by overlaying the transfer characteristics of a cell's two inverters; larger means more noise-tolerant. Read SNM, with the access transistor on, is smaller than hold SNM and is the binding stability constraint. It shrinks with lower supply voltage or greater variation, making it a key metric in low-voltage design.

  • A. Correct. It is the noise margin obtained from the butterfly curve.
  • B. Current consumption is a separate characteristic from SNM.
  • C. Read time is the access time, not the SNM.
