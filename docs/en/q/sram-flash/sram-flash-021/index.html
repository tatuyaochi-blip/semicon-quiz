SRAM and flash memory

What problem tends to occur as the supply voltage of SRAM is lowered?

  1. AThe margin for stable reading and writing (noise margin) shrinks, making malfunctions more likely
  2. BAccess becomes too fast
  3. CStorage capacity increases
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. The margin for stable reading and writing (noise margin) shrinks, making malfunctions more likely

An SRAM cell works by balancing latch stability against access-transistor drive. Lowering the supply voltage shrinks noise margins and magnifies transistor variation, making read upsets and write failures more likely. As a result, SRAM's minimum operating voltage (Vmin) often sets the floor for the whole chip's supply, a barrier to voltage scaling.
