SRAM and flash memory

In a 6T SRAM cell, which design is needed so that the cell contents are not flipped during a read (to prevent read upset)?

  1. AMake the access transistor stronger than the pull-down transistor
  2. BRemove the pull-up transistor
  3. CMake the pull-down (driver) transistor stronger than the access transistor
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. Make the pull-down (driver) transistor stronger than the access transistor

During a read, current flows into the node holding 0 from the precharged bit line through the access transistor, raising its voltage slightly. If the pull-down transistor is sufficiently stronger than the access transistor (cell ratio β > 1), this rise stays small and the opposite inverter does not flip.

  • A. A too-strong access transistor makes the cell more likely to flip during reads.
  • B. Without pull-up transistors, the cell cannot hold a 1.
  • C. Correct. A stronger pull-down limits the rise at the node holding 0.
