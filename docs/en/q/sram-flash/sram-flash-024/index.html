SRAM and flash memory

Which is closest to the number of layers in recent 3D NAND?

  1. AA few layers
  2. BTens of layers
  3. C200 or more layers
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. 200 or more layers

3D NAND started at 24 layers around 2013 and has added layers every generation; products above 200 layers are in mass production and 300+ layers have appeared. Taller stacks make it harder to etch holes several µm deep with uniform diameter top to bottom, so the stack is built in two or more decks whose holes are joined (string stacking). Thinner layers are also used to limit total height.
