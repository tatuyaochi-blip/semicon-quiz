SRAM and flash memory

Which is closest to the number of layers in recent 3D NAND?

  1. AA few layers
  2. BTens of layers
  3. C200 or more layers
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. 200 or more layers

3D NAND started at about 24 layers and has increased the layer count with each generation. Products with more than 200 layers are now in mass production, and further layering is under way. The more layers, the harder it becomes to etch deep holes uniformly.
