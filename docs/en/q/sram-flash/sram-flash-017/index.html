SRAM and flash memory

Which electron injection mechanism is mainly used for writing (programming) NAND flash memory?

  1. AFowler-Nordheim (FN) tunneling current
  2. BExcitation of electrons by light
  3. CMovement of electrons by a magnetic field
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. Fowler-Nordheim (FN) tunneling current

NAND programs by applying around 20 V to the control gate so electrons tunnel from the channel through the thin tunnel oxide into the storage layer—Fowler-Nordheim (FN) tunneling. The current per cell is tiny, so the more than one hundred thousand cells in a page can be programmed at once. NOR flash often uses channel hot-electron injection, accelerating electrons with drain voltage, which is faster but draws more current.
