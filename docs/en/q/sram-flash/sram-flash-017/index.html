SRAM and flash memory

Which electron injection mechanism is mainly used for writing (programming) NAND flash memory?

  1. AFowler-Nordheim (FN) tunneling current
  2. BExcitation of electrons by light
  3. CMovement of electrons by a magnetic field
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. Fowler-Nordheim (FN) tunneling current

In NAND flash, a high voltage is applied to the control gate to inject electrons from the channel into the charge-storage layer through a thin tunnel oxide. This quantum-mechanical tunneling current is called FN tunneling current. Because the current is small, many cells can be programmed at once. NOR flash also often uses channel hot-electron injection.
