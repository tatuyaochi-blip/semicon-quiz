SRAM and flash memory

What is the main reason flash memory has a limited number of rewrite cycles?

  1. ARepeated rewriting degrades the tunnel oxide that electrons pass through
  2. BThe capacitor's capacitance increases
  3. CThe wiring stretches
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. Repeated rewriting degrades the tunnel oxide that electrons pass through

In flash memory, electrons pass through the tunnel oxide under a high electric field at every program and erase. Repeating this creates defects in the oxide, making it harder to retain electrons. The number of rewrites is therefore limited, and the limit tends to decrease as more bits are stored per cell.
