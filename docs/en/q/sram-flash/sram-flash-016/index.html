SRAM and flash memory

In an SRAM read, what is the operation called that charges the bit lines in advance to the supply voltage or similar?

  1. ARefresh
  2. BErase
  3. CPrecharge
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. Precharge

To read SRAM, the bit-line pair is first precharged to the supply voltage; when the word line selects a cell, the bit line on the side storing 0 discharges slightly through the access transistor. The sense amplifier fires once a difference of tens to about a hundred mV develops, without waiting for a full swing, which makes reads fast. The bit lines are then precharged again for the next access.
