SRAM and flash memory

In 3D NAND, what is the main advantage of placing peripheral circuits such as sense amplifiers beneath the memory array (CMOS under Array and similar)?

  1. AIt makes erasing unnecessary
  2. BIt reduces chip area, increasing the capacity obtained from one wafer
  3. CIt reduces the number of stacked layers
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. It reduces chip area, increasing the capacity obtained from one wafer

Peripheral circuits traditionally sat beside the cell array; placing them directly beneath it (CMOS under Array, Periphery under Cell, etc.) cuts die area by about 20–30%, raising bit density and lowering cost. Since the periphery is built first, it must withstand the thermal steps of array formation. Approaches that build the periphery and array on separate wafers and bond them (such as CBA) are also in production.

  • A. Erasing is still required regardless of the structure.
  • B. Correct. Placing peripherals under the array reduces area.
  • C. It is not directly related to the number of layers.
