SRAM and flash memory

Which correctly describes 3D NAND flash memory?

  1. AMemory cells are stacked vertically in many layers to increase capacity
  2. BIt operates on three different supply voltages
  3. CIt stores 3 bits per cell
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. Memory cells are stacked vertically in many layers to increase capacity

As planar scaling neared its limit around 15 nm, 3D NAND, which stacks cells vertically, entered mass production around 2013 and became mainstream. Deep cylindrical channel holes are etched through a thick stack of alternating conductive and insulating layers, and cells are formed on the hole walls. Capacity grows without shrinking cells aggressively, and interference drops. Three bits per cell is TLC, commonly combined with 3D NAND.
