System Architecture and Reliability

In a system with 500 GB of data in total, a full backup is taken every Sunday, and a differential backup is taken every night from Monday to Saturday. Each day, 20 GB of other data that had not been changed until then is changed. Which is the total size of the six differential backups from Monday to Saturday?

  1. A120 GB
  2. B420 GB
  3. C3,000 GB
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. 420 GB

A differential backup contains all changes since Sunday's full backup, so each one grows: 20 GB on Monday, 40 GB on Tuesday, and so on, reaching 120 GB on Saturday. The total is 20 + 40 + 60 + 80 + 100 + 120 = 420 GB. With incremental backups each would be 20 GB, for a total of 120 GB, but a restore would need many backups applied in order. Taking a full backup every day would give 6 × 500 = 3,000 GB.

  • A. This is the total for incremental backups (20 GB × 6).
  • B. Correct. 20 + 40 + … + 120 = 420 GB.
  • C. This is the value for taking a full backup every day (500 GB × 6).
