Image sensors and optical devices

Which evaluation method obtains an image sensor's conversion gain and similar parameters from the relationship between the mean and variance of the signal in images taken at different brightness levels?

  1. AC-V measurement
  2. BThe photon transfer curve (PTC) method
  3. CThe four-terminal method
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. The photon transfer curve (PTC) method

With photon shot noise, the variance of the electron count equals its mean (Poisson statistics). Plotting the mean and variance of the output (in DN) from flat-field images at various light levels, the relation variance = mean × (1/K) gives the conversion gain K from the slope. Read noise, full-well capacity and dynamic range can all be derived from this one measurement, making the photon transfer curve a standard sensor-characterization method.

  • A. C-V measurement evaluates MOS structures and the like from capacitance versus voltage.
  • B. Correct. The conversion gain is obtained from the mean-variance relationship.
  • C. The four-terminal method measures resistance precisely.
