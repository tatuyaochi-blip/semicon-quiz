Image sensors and optical devices

Which autofocus method uses some pixels of the image sensor to receive light from the left and right sides of the lens separately?

  1. AContrast AF
  2. BToF
  3. COn-sensor phase-detection autofocus
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. On-sensor phase-detection autofocus

On-sensor phase-detection AF uses pixels that separately receive light from the left and right (or top and bottom) of the lens; the offset between the two images gives both the amount and direction of defocus at once. Unlike contrast AF, it need not hunt by moving the lens to find peak sharpness, so it is fast and tracks smoothly in video. Some sensors now split every pixel into two photodiodes for phase detection across the whole frame.
