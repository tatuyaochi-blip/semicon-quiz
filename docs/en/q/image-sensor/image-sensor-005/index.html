Image sensors and optical devices

In a Bayer color filter array, which color appears most often in each 2×2 block of pixels?

  1. ARed
  2. BBlue
  3. CGreen
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. Green

A Bayer pattern places one red, one blue and two green filters in each 2×2 block. The eye is most sensitive to green and derives most luminance information from it, so doubling green improves perceived resolution. Each pixel's two missing colors are interpolated from neighbors (demosaicing). Invented by Bryce Bayer at Kodak in 1976, it is still used in nearly all color sensors.
