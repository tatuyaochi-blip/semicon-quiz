Image sensors and optical devices

In the buried (pinned) photodiode of a CMOS image sensor, what is the main purpose of the heavily doped p+ layer at the surface?

  1. ATo act as a color filter
  2. BTo make the pixel larger
  3. CTo suppress dark current originating from surface interface states
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. To suppress dark current originating from surface interface states

The silicon/oxide interface has many interface states that generate dark current. Burying the photodiode's n-region away from the surface and covering the surface with a heavily doped p+ layer fills those states with holes, suppressing electron generation and greatly reducing dark current. The n-region also fully depletes, allowing complete charge transfer that reduces image lag and reset noise. Nearly all modern pixels use this pinned-photodiode structure.
