Image sensors and optical devices

In image sensors that capture color images, why must color filters be placed over the pixels?

  1. ATo increase the number of pixels
  2. BBecause the photodiode itself cannot distinguish the color (wavelength) of light, only its intensity
  3. CTo protect the photodiodes from heat
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. Because the photodiode itself cannot distinguish the color (wavelength) of light, only its intensity

A silicon photodiode generates charge from any photon above the band gap regardless of color, so it senses only brightness. Each pixel therefore gets a red, green or blue organic-pigment filter, and measuring the light that passes yields color. Filters discard the other colors, losing about two-thirds of the light, so alternatives such as separating colors by absorption depth have been explored.

  • A. Filters do not increase the number of pixels.
  • B. Correct. Photodiodes cannot distinguish color.
  • C. Color filters are not there to protect against heat.
