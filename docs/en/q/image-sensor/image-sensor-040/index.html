Image sensors and optical devices

Which component is sometimes placed in front of the sensor to suppress moiré, false patterns caused by interference between a fine striped subject and the pixel array?

  1. AOptical low-pass filter
  2. BMicrolens
  3. CPolarizer
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. Optical low-pass filter

Patterns finer than the pixel pitch cannot be represented correctly and alias into moiré and false color. An optical low-pass filter—typically birefringent quartz plates that split light slightly in two directions—blurs the image just enough to remove these too-fine components. It also softens detail, so cameras with very high pixel counts or with false-color correction in processing increasingly omit it.

  • A. Correct. It slightly blurs light to remove overly fine detail.
  • B. Microlenses focus light onto each pixel's photosensitive area.
  • C. A polarizer passes light of a particular polarization and is not a moiré countermeasure.
