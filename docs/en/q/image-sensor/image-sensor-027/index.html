Image sensors and optical devices

Which is closest to the pixel size of high-resolution image sensors for smartphones?

  1. AAbout 1 mm
  2. BAround 1 µm
  3. CAbout 100 µm
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. Around 1 µm

High-resolution smartphone sensors use pixels around 1 µm, now down to about 0.6 µm—close to visible wavelengths (0.4–0.7 µm), so diffraction makes focusing light difficult. To keep sensitivity, they combine back-side illumination, DTI and stacking with arrangements such as Quad Bayer, which groups four (or nine or sixteen) same-color pixels for binning. Pixels in interchangeable-lens cameras are about 4–6 µm.
