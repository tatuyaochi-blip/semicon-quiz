Image sensors and optical devices

Which is closest to the pixel size of high-resolution image sensors for smartphones?

  1. AAbout 1 mm
  2. BAround 1 µm
  3. CAbout 100 µm
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. Around 1 µm

In high-resolution smartphone sensors, pixels have shrunk to around 1 µm and even smaller. To maintain sensitivity with small pixels, technologies such as backside illumination, DTI and binning multiple pixels into one are combined.
