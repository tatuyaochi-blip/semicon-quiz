Image sensors and optical devices

Which correctly describes "pixel binning" in image sensors?

  1. ACombining the signals of several neighboring pixels into one, trading resolution for sensitivity and SNR
  2. BRearranging pixels by color
  3. CRemoving defective pixels
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. Combining the signals of several neighboring pixels into one, trading resolution for sensitivity and SNR

Pixel binning sums the signals of, for example, a 2×2 group of four pixels and reads them as one pixel. Resolution drops, but the signal per pixel increases and the SNR improves, which helps in dark scenes. High-resolution smartphone sensors switch it on and off depending on the light.

  • A. Correct. It combines pixels to raise sensitivity.
  • B. Signals are summed, not rearranged.
  • C. Correcting defective pixels is a separate process.
