Image sensors and optical devices

Which correctly describes the "three-chip" imaging method used in broadcast cameras and similar?

  1. AThree lenses capture stereoscopic video
  2. BThree exposures are combined for brightness
  3. CA prism splits light into red, green and blue, each received by a separate image sensor
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. A prism splits light into red, green and blue, each received by a separate image sensor

In a three-chip camera, a dichroic prism splits the incoming light into red, green and blue, and three image sensors each receive one color. Every pixel has full color information, so no color interpolation is needed, giving excellent color reproduction and sharpness. In exchange, the optics are larger and more expensive.

  • A. It is not a stereoscopic capture method.
  • B. It is not an HDR method combining exposures.
  • C. Correct. Light is split into three colors received by separate sensors.
