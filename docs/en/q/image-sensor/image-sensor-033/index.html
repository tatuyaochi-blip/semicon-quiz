Image sensors and optical devices

Which image sensor is mainly used in current smartphone cameras?

  1. ACamera tube
  2. BCCD image sensor
  3. CCMOS image sensor
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. CMOS image sensor

Smartphone cameras use CMOS image sensors, which can be made in the same CMOS process as logic, consume little power and integrate readout circuits and AD converters on the same chip. CCDs were once mainstream in digital cameras but are now limited to special applications.

  • A. Camera tubes were vacuum-tube imaging devices used in old television cameras.
  • B. CCDs are now limited to special applications.
  • C. Correct. CMOS image sensors are mainstream.
