Image sensors and optical devices

Which light detector is operated with a reverse bias above its breakdown voltage so that even a single photon triggers a pulse through avalanche multiplication?

  1. ASPAD (single-photon avalanche diode)
  2. BSolar cell
  3. CPIN photodiode
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. SPAD (single-photon avalanche diode)

A SPAD operates above breakdown (Geiger mode), so even one photon-generated electron triggers an avalanche producing a large current pulse. The pulse size does not depend on photon number, making it a photon-counting device. A quench circuit stops the avalanche to prepare for the next photon. With picosecond-level timing precision, SPADs are used in LiDAR, smartphone ranging sensors and fluorescence-lifetime measurement.

  • A. Correct. It is an avalanche diode that can detect single photons.
  • B. A solar cell generates power and is not used for single-photon detection.
  • C. A PIN photodiode has no multiplication.
