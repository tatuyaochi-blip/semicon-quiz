Image sensors and optical devices

Which material is suitable for photodiodes that receive light in the 1.55 µm band used in optical fiber communication?

  1. ASilicon (Si)
  2. BGallium nitride (GaN)
  3. CIndium gallium arsenide (InGaAs)
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. Indium gallium arsenide (InGaAs)

Silicon's ~1.12 eV band gap means it barely absorbs light beyond about 1.1 µm. InGaAs lattice-matched to InP (band gap about 0.75 eV) absorbs out to about 1.7 µm, so it is used in photodetectors for the 1.3 µm and 1.55 µm bands, where optical fiber loss is low. GaN, with its wide gap, suits UV to blue. Silicon photonics grows germanium for its photodetectors.
