Image sensors and optical devices

Which infrared wavelength band is mainly detected in thermography, which images the temperature of people and objects?

  1. AAbout 0.4 to 0.7 µm (visible light)
  2. BAbout 8 to 14 µm (long-wave infrared)
  3. CAbout 0.01 µm (extreme ultraviolet)
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. About 8 to 14 µm (long-wave infrared)

By Wien's displacement law (λmax ≈ 2898/T µm), objects near room temperature (about 300 K) radiate most strongly around 10 µm. The 8–14 µm band is also an atmospheric window with little absorption, so thermal cameras detect this long-wave infrared. Silicon cannot absorb it, so thermal detectors such as microbolometers, or cooled photon detectors such as HgCdTe, are used.

  • A. Visible light is not used to measure object temperature.
  • B. Correct. The 8–14 µm band, where room-temperature thermal radiation is strong, is used.
  • C. Extreme ultraviolet is the wavelength used for EUV lithography.
