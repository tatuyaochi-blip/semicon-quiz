Image sensors and optical devices

Why is it difficult to increase the near-infrared sensitivity of silicon image sensors?

  1. ABecause silicon absorbs near-infrared light weakly, so it penetrates deep into the silicon
  2. BBecause near-infrared light does not pass through lenses
  3. CBecause near-infrared light does not generate electrons
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. Because silicon absorbs near-infrared light weakly, so it penetrates deep into the silicon

Silicon absorbs longer wavelengths more weakly: the absorption depth is about 1.5 µm at 550 nm, about 20 µm at 850 nm and over 50 µm at 940 nm. A photodiode only a few µm thick cannot absorb enough, so sensitivity is low. To improve near-infrared response for face authentication and ToF, structures that texture the surface to scatter light obliquely and lengthen its path within the pixel are used.
