Probability, Statistics and Queueing

Which of the following correctly describes the correlation coefficient of two quantities?

  1. AEven if the correlation coefficient is close to 0, there may be a curved relationship between the two quantities
  2. BWhen the correlation coefficient is −0.9, there is almost no relationship between the two quantities
  3. CThe larger the correlation coefficient, the larger the slope of the regression line predicting one quantity from the other, without exception
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. Even if the correlation coefficient is close to 0, there may be a curved relationship between the two quantities

The correlation coefficient expresses the strength and direction of the linear relationship between two quantities as a value from −1 to 1. Close to 1, the points lie along a line rising to the right; close to −1, along a line falling to the right; close to 0, the linear relationship is weak. For example, for y = x² with x from −1 to 1, there is a strong valley-shaped relationship, yet the correlation coefficient can be close to 0. The slope of the regression line depends not only on the correlation coefficient but also on the ratio of the standard deviations of the two quantities.

  • A. Correct. The correlation coefficient expresses only linear relationships, so it can miss curved ones.
  • B. −0.9 is a strong negative correlation, indicating a strong linear relationship in which one quantity decreases as the other increases.
  • C. The slope of the regression line is the correlation coefficient multiplied by the ratio of the standard deviations of the two quantities, so it is not determined by the correlation coefficient alone.
