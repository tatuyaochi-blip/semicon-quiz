Compound semiconductors

What is the main reason GaAs has been used in RF amplifiers for mobile phones and similar applications?

  1. AIts wafers are cheap and easy to make in large diameters
  2. BIt readily forms a high-quality thermal oxide
  3. CIts electron mobility is higher than Si's
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. Its electron mobility is higher than Si's

GaAs has an electron mobility of about 8500 cm²/V·s—roughly six times silicon's—and a small effective mass, so it operates well at high frequencies and has served in phone power amplifiers and switches and satellite receivers. Its semi-insulating substrate also minimizes parasitic capacitance. But GaAs wafers are costlier and harder to scale up, and it lacks a good native oxide, making it unsuitable for large digital circuits.
