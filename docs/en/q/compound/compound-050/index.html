Compound semiconductors

What does LED stand for?

  1. ALow Energy Device
  2. BLight Emitting Diode
  3. CLiquid Electric Display
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. Light Emitting Diode

LED stands for light-emitting diode. Forward current through a PN junction makes electrons and holes recombine, emitting light whose color corresponds to the band gap. The red GaAsP LED was invented in 1962, and the blue LED of the 1990s completed the three primary colors. A liquid crystal display is an LCD, which uses LEDs as its backlight.

  • A. LED is not an abbreviation for this.
  • B. Correct. It stands for light emitting diode.
  • C. This confuses LED with LCD, the abbreviation for liquid crystal display.
