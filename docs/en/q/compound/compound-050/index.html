Compound semiconductors

What does LED stand for?

  1. ALow Energy Device
  2. BLight Emitting Diode
  3. CLiquid Electric Display
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. Light Emitting Diode

LED stands for light emitting diode. It is a diode that emits light when forward current flows through its PN junction and electrons and holes recombine. A liquid crystal display is an LCD, which is different from an LED.

  • A. LED is not an abbreviation for this.
  • B. Correct. It stands for light emitting diode.
  • C. This confuses LED with LCD, the abbreviation for liquid crystal display.
