Compound semiconductors

Which impurity is typically used to make p-type layers in GaN?

  1. APhosphorus (P)
  2. BArsenic (As)
  3. CMagnesium (Mg)
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. Magnesium (Mg)

GaN is made p-type with magnesium, but its acceptor level lies about 0.2 eV above the valence band, so only a few percent ionize at room temperature; doping above 10¹⁹ cm⁻³ yields hole concentrations of only 10¹⁷–10¹⁸ cm⁻³. As grown, Mg is also passivated by hydrogen and must be activated, for example by annealing—the breakthrough that enabled bright blue LEDs.
