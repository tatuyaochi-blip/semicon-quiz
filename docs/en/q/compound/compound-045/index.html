Compound semiconductors

When a strong field is applied to GaAs, electrons transfer to another valley with lower mobility, and negative differential resistance appears in which current decreases as the field rises. Which oscillator device uses this?

  1. AGunn diode
  2. BZener diode
  3. CPhotodiode
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. Gunn diode

In GaAs, electrons accelerated by strong fields (above about 3 kV/cm) transfer to the L valley, about 0.3 eV higher with a larger effective mass, so drift velocity falls (the transferred-electron effect). This negative differential resistance repeatedly forms and propagates charge domains, so a Gunn diode oscillates at microwave frequencies from a DC bias alone. Discovered by J. B. Gunn in 1963, it has been used in automotive radar and speed guns.

  • A. Correct. It is a microwave oscillator using the transferred-electron effect.
  • B. A Zener diode is a voltage regulator using reverse breakdown.
  • C. A photodiode detects light.
