Compound semiconductors

Which compound semiconductor, with very high electron mobility, is used in magnetic sensors (Hall elements) and the like?

  1. ASiC
  2. BInSb (indium antimonide)
  3. CAlN
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. InSb (indium antimonide)

InSb is a compound semiconductor with very high electron mobility, tens of thousands of cm²/(V·s) at room temperature. Because the Hall output voltage relates to mobility, it is used for highly sensitive Hall elements, for example to detect motor rotation. Its small band gap also makes it useful for infrared detection.

  • A. SiC is used for high-voltage power devices, but its mobility is not high.
  • B. Correct. It has very high electron mobility.
  • C. AlN has a very wide band gap and relates to deep-UV emission and the like.
