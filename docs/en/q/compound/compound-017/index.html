Compound semiconductors

What is one main reason SiC is used as the substrate for GaN HEMTs?

  1. AIt is cheaper than silicon
  2. BIt does not transmit light
  3. CIts high thermal conductivity makes it easy to remove heat from the device
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. Its high thermal conductivity makes it easy to remove heat from the device

High-power GaN HEMTs generate a lot of heat, and performance and reliability fall as temperature rises. SiC has high thermal conductivity and a relatively small lattice mismatch with GaN, so it is used as the substrate for high-frequency, high-power GaN HEMTs. Si substrates are also used where cost is a priority.
