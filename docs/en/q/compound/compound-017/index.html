Compound semiconductors

What is one main reason SiC is used as the substrate for GaN HEMTs?

  1. AIt is cheaper than silicon
  2. BIt does not transmit light
  3. CIts high thermal conductivity makes it easy to remove heat from the device
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. Its high thermal conductivity makes it easy to remove heat from the device

High-power GaN HEMTs concentrate heat near the gate, and rising temperature degrades performance and lifetime. SiC conducts heat at about 400 W/(m·K), far better than silicon (about 150) or sapphire (about 35), and its lattice mismatch with GaN is a relatively small 3.5%, so it serves as the substrate for RF and high-power GaN HEMTs in base stations and radar (GaN-on-SiC). Cost-sensitive power devices use silicon substrates.
