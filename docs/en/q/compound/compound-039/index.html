Compound semiconductors

Which material, whose band gap can be tuned by composition, has been used for high-performance mid- to long-wave infrared detectors?

  1. AAl2O3
  2. BHgCdTe (mercury cadmium telluride)
  3. CSiO2
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. HgCdTe (mercury cadmium telluride)

HgCdTe's band gap can be tuned from nearly zero (HgTe) to about 1.5 eV (CdTe) by varying the Hg:Cd ratio, matching the target infrared wavelength. Its lattice constant barely changes with composition, easing growth of high-quality crystals. Cooled to around liquid-nitrogen temperature (77 K), it forms highly sensitive infrared detectors for astronomy, Earth-observation satellites and military applications.

  • A. Al2O3 (sapphire) is an insulator, not a light-absorbing material.
  • B. Correct. It is an infrared detector material with composition-tunable band gap.
  • C. SiO2 is an insulator, not an infrared detector material.
