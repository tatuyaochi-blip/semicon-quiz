Compound semiconductors

Which is an example of an application of HBTs (heterojunction bipolar transistors) made from InP or GaAs?

  1. ADRAM memory cells
  2. BHigh-speed, high-frequency circuits for optical and wireless communication
  3. CGenerating power from solar heat
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. High-speed, high-frequency circuits for optical and wireless communication

Compound-semiconductor HBTs use a wide-gap emitter to suppress hole back-injection, allowing a thin, heavily doped base, and their high electron mobility makes them fast. GaAs-based (InGaP/GaAs) HBTs run from a single supply with high efficiency and dominate smartphone transmit power amplifiers, while InP HBTs, with fT of several hundred GHz, drive electronics for optical links beyond 100 Gb/s and terahertz research.
