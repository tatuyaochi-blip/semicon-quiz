Compound semiconductors

In GaN devices, which substrates are commonly used for growing the GaN layers?

  1. AOnly quartz glass
  2. BForeign substrates such as sapphire, SiC and Si
  3. CCopper plates
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. Foreign substrates such as sapphire, SiC and Si

GaN decomposes before melting and cannot be pulled from a melt, so large, cheap GaN substrates are hard to make, and GaN is grown epitaxially on foreign substrates: inexpensive, transparent sapphire for LEDs, thermally conductive SiC for RF devices, and large, cheap silicon for power devices. Buffer layers that control defects and wafer bow from lattice and thermal-expansion mismatch are the key technology.
