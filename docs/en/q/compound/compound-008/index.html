Compound semiconductors

In GaN devices, which substrates are commonly used for growing the GaN layers?

  1. AOnly quartz glass
  2. BForeign substrates such as sapphire, SiC and Si
  3. CCopper plates
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. Foreign substrates such as sapphire, SiC and Si

Large, inexpensive GaN single-crystal substrates are hard to make, so GaN is often grown epitaxially on substrates of other materials such as sapphire, SiC and Si. Sapphire is common for LEDs, SiC for RF devices, and large, cheap Si substrates for power devices.
