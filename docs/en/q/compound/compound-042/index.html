Compound semiconductors

What is a high-frequency IC called that integrates transistors together with passive components such as inductors and capacitors on a substrate such as GaAs?

  1. ADRAM
  2. BMEMS
  3. CMMIC (monolithic microwave integrated circuit)
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. MMIC (monolithic microwave integrated circuit)

An MMIC (monolithic microwave integrated circuit) integrates high-frequency transistors with inductors, capacitors and transmission lines for matching networks on a single substrate. Semi-insulating GaAs substrates have low high-frequency loss and are used for MMICs such as mobile phone power amplifiers and radar.

  • A. DRAM is a memory IC.
  • B. MEMS are devices with tiny mechanical structures.
  • C. Correct. It is an integrated circuit for high frequencies.
