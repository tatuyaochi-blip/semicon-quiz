Compound semiconductors

Why is LED lighting more energy-efficient than incandescent bulbs?

  1. ABecause it lights by heating a filament to a high temperature
  2. BBecause LEDs emit no light at all, only heat
  3. CBecause it converts electrical energy directly into light inside the semiconductor, losing a smaller share as heat
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. Because it converts electrical energy directly into light inside the semiconductor, losing a smaller share as heat

Incandescent bulbs turn over 90% of their input into infrared heat, achieving only about 15 lm/W. LEDs convert electricity directly into light through electron-hole recombination, reaching 100–200 lm/W in lighting—about ten times better—and lasting about 40,000 hours, dozens of times longer. Converting lighting, which once consumed roughly 15–20% of the world's electricity, to LEDs has delivered major energy and emissions savings.

  • A. Heating a filament is how incandescent bulbs work.
  • B. LEDs are devices that emit light.
  • C. Correct. Converting electricity directly into light makes them efficient.
