Compound semiconductors

Which is a characteristic of multi-junction solar cells, which stack several compound semiconductor layers with different band gaps?

  1. AThey make efficient use of a wide range of sunlight wavelengths and achieve high conversion efficiency
  2. BThey have the lowest material cost
  3. CThey can generate electricity even where there is no light
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. They make efficient use of a wide range of sunlight wavelengths and achieve high conversion efficiency

With a single material, light below the band gap cannot be absorbed, and excess energy from higher-energy light is lost as heat. In a multi-junction solar cell, layers with different band gaps each absorb the wavelengths they handle best, achieving high conversion efficiency. Because they are expensive, they are used in satellites and similar applications.
