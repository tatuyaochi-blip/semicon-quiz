Compound semiconductors

Which material, named for its crystal structure, has attracted intense research as a high-efficiency solar cell material that can be made by low-temperature processes such as coating?

  1. APerovskite
  2. BDiamond-structure silicon
  3. CWurtzite GaN
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. Perovskite

Lead-halide materials with the perovskite crystal structure can be coated from solution at low temperatures (below 150 °C), yet single-junction cells exceed 26% efficiency, approaching crystalline silicon. Research exploded after Tsutomu Miyasaka and colleagues reported perovskite solar cells in 2009. Their light weight and flexibility suit building walls, and tandems with silicon are promising, but durability against moisture and heat and the handling of lead remain challenges.

  • A. Correct. Perovskite solar cells are actively researched.
  • B. Crystalline silicon solar cells are made with high-temperature processes.
  • C. GaN is used mainly for LEDs and power and RF devices.
