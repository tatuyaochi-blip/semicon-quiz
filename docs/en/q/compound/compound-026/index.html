Compound semiconductors

What structure do most of today's widely used GaN power devices adopt?

  1. AA lateral HEMT structure built on a Si or similar substrate
  2. BA vertical thyristor structure
  3. CA bipolar transistor structure
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. A lateral HEMT structure built on a Si or similar substrate

Most current GaN power devices are lateral HEMTs grown epitaxially on silicon substrates. Large, cheap silicon wafers and existing silicon fabs keep costs down, and their fast switching has made them popular in 100–650 V chargers, data-center power supplies and automotive equipment. For higher voltages and currents, vertical devices on GaN substrates, conducting current through the thickness, are under research.
