Compound semiconductors

What is the typical crystal structure of GaN?

  1. AWurtzite (hexagonal)
  2. BDiamond structure
  3. CBody-centered cubic
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. Wurtzite (hexagonal)

GaN normally adopts the hexagonal wurtzite structure. Lacking symmetry along the c-axis, with alternating Ga and N planes, it is polar and exhibits spontaneous polarization plus strain-induced piezoelectric polarization. This creates the dense 2DEG at AlGaN/GaN interfaces but also, in LED quantum wells, separates electrons and holes and lowers efficiency. GaAs and similar compounds have the cubic zincblende structure.
