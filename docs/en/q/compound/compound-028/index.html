Compound semiconductors

How does the thermal conductivity of GaAs compare with that of silicon?

  1. ALower (about one third of silicon's)
  2. BMuch higher than silicon's
  3. CThe same as silicon's
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. Lower (about one third of silicon's)

GaAs conducts heat at about 46 W/(m·K), roughly a third of silicon's (about 150). High-power GaAs devices therefore thin the substrate to about 50–100 µm and extract heat through backside metal and through-substrate vias. SiC's high conductivity (about 400) is one reason it serves as the substrate for high-power GaN devices; InP is also low, at about 68.
