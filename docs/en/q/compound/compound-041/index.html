Compound semiconductors

How is silicon germanium (SiGe) classified?

  1. AA III-V compound semiconductor
  2. BAn alloy semiconductor of two group IV elements
  3. CA II-VI compound semiconductor
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. An alloy semiconductor of two group IV elements

SiGe is an alloy semiconductor mixing silicon and germanium, both group IV elements, in any ratio. It is compatible with silicon processes and is used for heterojunction bipolar transistors (HBTs), channel strain engineering and pMOS channel materials.

  • A. III-V compounds consist of group III and group V elements.
  • B. Correct. It is an alloy of group IV elements.
  • C. II-VI compounds consist of group II and group VI elements.
