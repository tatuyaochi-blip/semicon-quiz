Compound semiconductors

Why is GaN well suited to high-frequency, high-power transistors?

  1. AIts large band gap gives a high breakdown field, and its electron saturation velocity is also high
  2. BIt generates no heat at all
  3. CIt is cheaper than silicon
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. Its large band gap gives a high breakdown field, and its electron saturation velocity is also high

GaN's breakdown field is about ten times silicon's, allowing high-voltage operation, and its electron saturation velocity of about 2.5×10⁷ cm/s supports high-frequency, high-power operation. In HEMT structures its dense two-dimensional electron gas (about 10¹³ cm⁻²) also yields high current density, giving several times the power density of GaAs. It serves power amplifiers in cellular base stations, radar and satellite communications.
