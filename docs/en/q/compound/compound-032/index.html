Compound semiconductors

Which elements make up gallium arsenide (GaAs)?

  1. AGallium (group III) and sulfur (group VI)
  2. BGallium (group III) and arsenic (group V)
  3. CGermanium (group IV) and arsenic (group V)
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. Gallium (group III) and arsenic (group V)

GaAs is a III-V compound of group-III gallium and group-V arsenic bonded 1:1. In its zincblende crystal each Ga atom is surrounded by four As atoms and vice versa, averaging four valence electrons per atom, like silicon. Gallium is obtained as a byproduct of aluminum refining, and arsenic's toxicity demands strict controls in manufacturing and disposal.

  • A. As is arsenic, not sulfur (S).
  • B. Correct. Ga from group III and As from group V.
  • C. Germanium's symbol is Ge; it is not in GaAs.
