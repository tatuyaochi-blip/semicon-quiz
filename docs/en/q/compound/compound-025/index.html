Compound semiconductors

Which is a characteristic of micro-LED displays?

  1. AThey display images with liquid crystals and a backlight
  2. BThey aim for bright, self-emissive displays by placing very small inorganic LEDs at each pixel
  3. CThey emit light from coated organic materials
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. They aim for bright, self-emissive displays by placing very small inorganic LEDs at each pixel

A micro-LED display places tiny inorganic LED chips, tens of µm or smaller, at each pixel and makes them emit light themselves. They are expected to be bright, efficient and long-lived, but transferring huge numbers of tiny LEDs onto the substrate with high yield remains a challenge.
