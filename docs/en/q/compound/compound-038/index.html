Compound semiconductors

What material is used for the blue-violet semiconductor laser of about 405 nm that reads and writes Blu-ray discs?

  1. AGaN-based
  2. BGaAs-based
  3. CInP-based
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. GaN-based

Blu-ray uses a blue-violet laser of about 405 nm, and the laser diode is made of GaN-based materials. Shorter wavelengths can be focused to smaller spots, allowing higher recording density than DVDs, which use red lasers. GaAs-based materials are used for red to infrared lasers and InP-based for infrared lasers for optical communication.

  • A. Correct. Blue-violet lasers are made from GaN-based materials.
  • B. GaAs-based materials are used for red to infrared lasers.
  • C. InP-based materials are used for 1.3–1.55 µm optical communication lasers.
