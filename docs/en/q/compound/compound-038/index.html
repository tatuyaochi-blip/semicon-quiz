Compound semiconductors

What material is used for the blue-violet semiconductor laser of about 405 nm that reads and writes Blu-ray discs?

  1. AGaN-based
  2. BGaAs-based
  3. CInP-based
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. GaN-based

Blu-ray uses a blue-violet laser at about 405 nm made from GaN-based materials. The focused spot size scales with wavelength and inversely with numerical aperture, so it focuses tighter than DVD's 650 nm red laser, storing 25 GB per layer—about five times DVD's 4.7 GB. GaN lasers now also serve projectors, automotive headlights and metal processing.

  • A. Correct. Blue-violet lasers are made from GaN-based materials.
  • B. GaAs-based materials are used for red to infrared lasers.
  • C. InP-based materials are used for 1.3–1.55 µm optical communication lasers.
