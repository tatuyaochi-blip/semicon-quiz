Compound semiconductors

Which material is mainly used in the light-emitting layer of deep-ultraviolet LEDs, such as those for sterilization?

  1. AGaAs
  2. BAlGaN (aluminum gallium nitride)
  3. CInP
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. AlGaN (aluminum gallium nitride)

The band gap of AlGaN increases as the aluminum fraction increases, so it can emit short deep-ultraviolet wavelengths. Deep-UV LEDs are used to sterilize water and air, but improving their light-emission efficiency remains a challenge. GaAs and InP are used for infrared emission.
