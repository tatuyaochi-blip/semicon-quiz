Compound semiconductors

Which correctly describes a "type I" band alignment at a heterojunction?

  1. AThe conduction band minimum and valence band maximum of the narrower-gap material both lie within the band gap of the wider-gap material
  2. BThe band gaps of the two materials do not overlap
  3. CThe conduction and valence bands of the two materials are offset in a staggered way
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. The conduction band minimum and valence band maximum of the narrower-gap material both lie within the band gap of the wider-gap material

In type I alignment, the bands of the narrower-gap material lie inside those of the wider-gap material, so both electrons and holes are confined in the narrow-gap layer. This is used in LED and laser quantum wells for efficient recombination. A staggered alignment is type II.

  • A. Correct. Both electrons and holes are confined in the same layer.
  • B. A completely non-overlapping alignment is called type III.
  • C. A staggered alignment is type II.
