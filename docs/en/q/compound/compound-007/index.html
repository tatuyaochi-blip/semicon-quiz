Compound semiconductors

What is a typical application of HEMTs (high electron mobility transistors)?

  1. AHigh-frequency, low-noise amplifiers in satellite TV receivers and mobile base stations
  2. BDRAM memory cells
  3. CGenerating power from solar heat
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. High-frequency, low-noise amplifiers in satellite TV receivers and mobile base stations

A HEMT uses the two-dimensional electron gas at a heterojunction interface as its channel; with little impurity scattering, electron mobility is high, enabling low-noise amplification at high frequencies. GaAs HEMTs are used in satellite TV receivers and similar devices, and GaN HEMTs in base station power amplifiers.
