Compound semiconductors

What is a typical application of HEMTs (high electron mobility transistors)?

  1. AHigh-frequency, low-noise amplifiers in satellite TV receivers and mobile base stations
  2. BDRAM memory cells
  3. CGenerating power from solar heat
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. High-frequency, low-noise amplifiers in satellite TV receivers and mobile base stations

A HEMT uses the two-dimensional electron gas at a heterojunction as its channel; because the dopants supplying electrons are separated from the layer where electrons travel (modulation doping), impurity scattering is low, enabling low-noise, high-frequency amplification. Takashi Mimura of Fujitsu invented it in 1980. GaAs HEMTs serve satellite receivers and radio telescopes, and GaN HEMTs serve base-station and radar power amplifiers.
