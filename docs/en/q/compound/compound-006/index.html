Compound semiconductors

What is an advantage of alloy semiconductors such as AlGaAs?

  1. AThey always become n-type without doping
  2. BThey always have an indirect band gap
  3. CTheir band gap and lattice constant can be adjusted by changing the composition
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. Their band gap and lattice constant can be adjusted by changing the composition

Alloy semiconductors let composition tune band gap and lattice constant continuously. Increasing aluminum in AlGaAs raises the gap from 1.42 eV, while its lattice constant stays nearly identical to GaAs, so layers stack on GaAs without strain. This enables heterostructures that confine electrons and light, giving rise to semiconductor lasers and HEMTs.
