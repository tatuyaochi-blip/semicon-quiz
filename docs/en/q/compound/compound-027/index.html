Compound semiconductors

Which is a characteristic of diamond, sometimes called the ultimate semiconductor material?

  1. AIts band gap is small and it can only be used at low voltage
  2. BIt is a metal that conducts electricity
  3. CIt has a large band gap of about 5.5 eV and very high thermal conductivity
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. It has a large band gap of about 5.5 eV and very high thermal conductivity

Diamond has a large band gap of about 5.5 eV, a high breakdown field and one of the highest thermal conductivities of any material, so it is expected to be a device material for high voltage, high temperature and high frequency. Challenges include making large, high-quality substrates and n-type doping.
