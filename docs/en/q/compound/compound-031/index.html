Compound semiconductors

Which trio of researchers received the 2014 Nobel Prize in Physics for inventing the high-brightness blue LED?

  1. ALeo Esaki, Ivar Giaever and Brian Josephson
  2. BIsamu Akasaki, Hiroshi Amano and Shuji Nakamura
  3. CWilliam Shockley, John Bardeen and Walter Brattain
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. Isamu Akasaki, Hiroshi Amano and Shuji Nakamura

Isamu Akasaki, Hiroshi Amano and Shuji Nakamura made high-brightness blue LEDs possible through achievements such as growing high-quality GaN crystals and realizing p-type GaN, and received the 2014 Nobel Prize in Physics. Blue LEDs enabled the spread of white LED lighting and full-color displays.

  • A. Leo Esaki and colleagues received the 1973 prize for work on tunneling.
  • B. Correct. They received the 2014 prize for the blue LED.
  • C. Shockley and colleagues received the 1956 prize for inventing the transistor.
