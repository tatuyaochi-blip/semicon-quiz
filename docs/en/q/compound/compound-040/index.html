Compound semiconductors

Which is one reason it is difficult to build silicon-like CMOS circuits in GaAs?

  1. ABecause GaAs hole mobility is very low compared with electron mobility, making high-performance p-channel transistors hard to build
  2. BBecause GaAs electron mobility is too low
  3. CBecause GaAs does not absorb light
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. Because GaAs hole mobility is very low compared with electron mobility, making high-performance p-channel transistors hard to build

GaAs has higher electron mobility than silicon but low hole mobility, so the gap between electrons and holes is very large. CMOS needs n-channel and p-channel transistors of comparable performance, so high-performance CMOS is hard to build in GaAs. The difficulty of forming a good gate insulator is another reason.

  • A. Correct. Low hole mobility makes p-channel devices weak.
  • B. GaAs is notable for electron mobility higher than silicon's.
  • C. GaAs is a direct-gap semiconductor that absorbs light well.
