Packaging and assembly

What material is most widely used as the base material of printed circuit boards in electronics?

  1. AGlass-fiber cloth impregnated with epoxy resin (such as FR-4)
  2. BSingle-crystal silicon
  3. CA pure copper plate
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. Glass-fiber cloth impregnated with epoxy resin (such as FR-4)

Typical circuit boards use glass-fiber cloth impregnated with epoxy and cured (such as FR-4) as the insulating base, with copper-foil wiring on the surfaces and inner layers. FR stands for flame retardant; the material is cheap, strong and a good insulator. Its permittivity is about 4.5 and its losses grow at high frequencies, so special low-loss resins are used for RF boards.

  • A. Correct. Glass-epoxy (such as FR-4) is widely used.
  • B. Single-crystal silicon is a chip material and is not used for PCBs.
  • C. Copper is the wiring material; the base must be an insulator.
