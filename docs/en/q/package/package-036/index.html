Packaging and assembly

In leadframe packages, what is the main purpose of plating the exposed leads with tin (Sn)-based plating?

  1. ATo improve solder wetting for reliable soldering to the board and to prevent lead oxidation
  2. BTo make the leads shiny for appearance
  3. CTo make the leads insulating
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. To improve solder wetting for reliable soldering to the board and to prevent lead oxidation

Copper-alloy leads oxidize easily, and solder does not wet them well as is. Tin-based plating lets solder wet readily and ensures reliable joints during assembly. Lead-free regulations replaced tin–lead plating with pure tin, but pure tin can grow needle-like whiskers that cause shorts, so annealing or nickel underlayers are used as countermeasures.

  • A. Correct. It improves solderability and prevents oxidation.
  • B. It is not for appearance.
  • C. Plating is metal and does not make the leads insulating.
