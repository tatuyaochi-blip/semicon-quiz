Packaging and assembly

In high-pin-count BGA packages, why are many of the balls assigned to power and ground?

  1. ATo make the package heavier
  2. BTo reduce the number of signals
  3. CTo spread large currents, lowering supply resistance and inductance and suppressing power noise and voltage drop
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. To spread large currents, lowering supply resistance and inductance and suppressing power noise and voltage drop

High-performance chips draw large currents, and rapid current changes make the voltage drop or fluctuate because of the resistance and inductance of the supply path. Placing many power and ground balls in parallel spreads the current and lowers path impedance, stabilizing the supply.

  • A. It is not about weight.
  • B. It is not intended to reduce the number of signals.
  • C. Correct. Lowering supply path impedance stabilizes the supply.
