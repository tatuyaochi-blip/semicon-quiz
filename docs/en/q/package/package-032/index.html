Packaging and assembly

What is the common step for displaying the part name, lot number and similar on the package surface of a finished IC?

  1. AEpitaxial growth
  2. BMarking, such as by laser
  3. CIon implantation
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. Marking, such as by laser

Product name, lot number, date code and similar information are marked on the package surface, either by laser-etching the mold compound or by printing with ink. This marking enables traceability back through manufacturing history when defects are found. Laser marking is dominant because it is durable and fast, and 2D codes may also be engraved. Very small parts may carry only a short code instead of full text.

  • A. Epitaxial growth grows crystal on the wafer.
  • B. Correct. It is marked by laser or similar.
  • C. Ion implantation introduces impurities into the wafer.
