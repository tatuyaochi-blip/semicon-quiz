Packaging and assembly

What is the common step for displaying the part name, lot number and similar on the package surface of a finished IC?

  1. AEpitaxial growth
  2. BMarking, such as by laser
  3. CIon implantation
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. Marking, such as by laser

Product name, lot number, date code and the like are displayed on the package surface by laser-burning the resin surface or printing with ink. This is called marking and is used for product identification and traceability.

  • A. Epitaxial growth grows crystal on the wafer.
  • B. Correct. It is marked by laser or similar.
  • C. Ion implantation introduces impurities into the wafer.
