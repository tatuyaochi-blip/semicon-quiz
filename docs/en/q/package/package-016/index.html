Packaging and assembly

Which symbol denotes the thermal resistance from the chip junction to the surrounding air, indicating a package's heat-dissipation performance?

  1. AθJA (Rth(j-a))
  2. BCpk
  3. CFIT
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. θJA (Rth(j-a))

θJA is the thermal resistance from the chip junction to the ambient air, in °C/W. Junction temperature is Tj = Ta + P×θJA; a chip dissipating 2 W in a package with θJA = 40 °C/W runs 80 °C above ambient. Because θJA depends on the board and airflow, the junction-to-case value θJC is often given too. Cpk is a process capability index and FIT is a unit of failure rate.
