Packaging and assembly

What change occurs when the temperature of a molding compound exceeds its glass transition temperature (Tg)?

  1. AThe resin becomes a metal
  2. BThe resin becomes transparent
  3. CThe thermal expansion coefficient increases and the elastic modulus drops
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. The thermal expansion coefficient increases and the elastic modulus drops

Above its glass transition temperature, the resin changes from a hard glassy state to a soft rubbery state, its thermal expansion coefficient rises sharply and its modulus drops. Because this changes the stress from thermal expansion mismatch with the chip and leadframe, it is an important property in package reliability design.

  • A. The resin does not become a metal.
  • B. It does not become transparent.
  • C. Correct. The expansion coefficient rises and the modulus falls.
