Packaging and assembly

For surface-mount packages, which moisture-related rating specifies, among other things, how long they may be left after opening the moisture-proof packing before reflow?

  1. AMSL (moisture sensitivity level)
  2. BFIT
  3. CCpk
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. MSL (moisture sensitivity level)

MSL rates how readily a package absorbs moisture, specifying how long it may sit in factory conditions (for example ≤30 °C/60% RH) after the dry bag is opened, on a scale from 1 (unlimited) to 6. MSL 3, for instance, allows 168 hours. Parts that exceed their floor life are baked before reflow to drive out moisture and prevent popcorning. FIT is a failure-rate unit and Cpk a process capability index.
