Packaging and assembly

What is the main purpose of placing decoupling capacitors (bypass capacitors) on packages and printed circuit boards?

  1. ATo amplify signals
  2. BTo suppress supply voltage fluctuation (noise) by supplying charge when current changes suddenly
  3. CTo make the chip emit light
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. To suppress supply voltage fluctuation (noise) by supplying charge when current changes suddenly

An LSI's supply current changes suddenly as it operates, and the inductance of the power wiring makes the supply voltage fluctuate. A decoupling capacitor placed near the chip supplies charge in response to sudden current changes, suppressing supply voltage fluctuation.
