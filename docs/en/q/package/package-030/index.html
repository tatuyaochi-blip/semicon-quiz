Packaging and assembly

What is the main purpose of placing decoupling capacitors (bypass capacitors) on packages and printed circuit boards?

  1. ATo amplify signals
  2. BTo suppress supply voltage fluctuation (noise) by supplying charge when current changes suddenly
  3. CTo make the chip emit light
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. To suppress supply voltage fluctuation (noise) by supplying charge when current changes suddenly

An IC's supply current changes abruptly with activity, and the inductance L of the power path produces L·di/dt voltage swings. Decoupling capacitors placed near the chip supply charge during sudden current changes, suppressing supply fluctuations. Because each location is effective in a different frequency range, capacitors of different sizes are distributed on the die, in the package and on the board. Amplifying signals is not their role.
