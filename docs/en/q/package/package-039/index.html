Packaging and assembly

Which package has flat electrodes (lands) arranged in a grid on its bottom instead of solder balls?

  1. ASOP
  2. BLGA (land grid array)
  3. CDIP
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. LGA (land grid array)

An LGA has flat lands arranged in a grid on its underside. With no solder balls, mounted height is lower, and in PC processors spring pins in the socket press against the lands, allowing replacement. When soldered directly, it is joined with solder printed on the board. Its grid arrangement is the same as a BGA's, while SOP and DIP place terminals around the edges.

  • A. An SOP is a surface-mount package with leads on two sides.
  • B. Correct. It has flat lands arranged in a grid.
  • C. A DIP has pins on two sides inserted into board holes.
