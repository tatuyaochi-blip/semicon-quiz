Packaging and assembly

In wire bonding that forms a ball, where is the first bond (ball bond) generally made?

  1. AOn the surface of the molding compound
  2. BOn the chip's bond pad
  3. COn the leadframe or package substrate
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. On the chip's bond pad

In ball bonding, the ball formed at the wire tip is first bonded to the chip pad (first bond), then the wire is looped to the lead or substrate pad and pressed flat there (second bond). The ball bond is small and suits narrow chip pads. When low wire loops are needed, as in stacked dies, the order is sometimes reversed.

  • A. Nothing is bonded to the molding compound surface.
  • B. Correct. The ball is first bonded to the chip pad.
  • C. The lead or substrate side is the second bond.
