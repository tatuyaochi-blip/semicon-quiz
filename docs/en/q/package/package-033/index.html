Packaging and assembly

What is the typical diameter of wires used in wire bonding?

  1. AAbout 1 to 2 mm
  2. BAbout 1 nm
  3. CAbout 15 to 50 µm
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. About 15 to 50 µm

Bonding wires are very fine metal wires about 15–50 µm in diameter, mostly thinner than a human hair (about 70 µm). Chip pads are only tens of micrometers across, so the wire must be correspondingly fine. Power devices carrying large currents, on the other hand, use thick aluminum wires about 100–500 µm in diameter, flat ribbons or copper clips.

  • A. 1 mm or more is far too thick to bond to chip electrodes.
  • B. 1 nm is the width of a few atoms and cannot form a wire.
  • C. Correct. They are wires of a few tens of micrometers, thinner than a hair.
