Packaging and assembly

Which correctly describes wire bonding?

  1. AA method that connects the chip's bond pads to a lead frame or substrate with fine metal wires
  2. BA method that flips the chip and connects it directly to the substrate with bumps
  3. CA method that encapsulates the chip in resin
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. A method that connects the chip's bond pads to a lead frame or substrate with fine metal wires

Wire bonding connects the chip's bond pads to the terminals of a lead frame or package substrate with fine wires of gold, copper, aluminum or similar metals. The bonds are made using heat, pressure and ultrasonic energy. It has a long history and is still used in many packages today.
