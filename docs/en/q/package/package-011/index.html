Packaging and assembly

Which correctly describes a feature of QFN (Quad Flat No-lead) packages?

  1. ALong leads extend outward from all four sides of the package
  2. BA small package with no protruding leads, with electrodes on the four edges of its bottom
  3. CSolder balls are arranged in a grid over the entire bottom
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. A small package with no protruding leads, with electrodes on the four edges of its bottom

A QFN has no leads sticking out of its sides; instead, electrodes are exposed around the edges of its bottom. It is small and thin, often has a heat-dissipating electrode (thermal pad) in the center of the bottom, and offers excellent electrical and thermal performance.
