Packaging and assembly

Which method bonds chips to substrates one at a time while heating and applying pressure, and is used to connect thin chips with large warpage and fine-pitch bumps?

  1. AReflow soldering
  2. BWire bonding
  3. CThermocompression bonding (TCB)
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. Thermocompression bonding (TCB)

Thermocompression bonding holds each chip with a bond head and joins its bumps under heat and pressure, one chip at a time. Unlike oven reflow, it bonds while flattening chip warpage and aligns precisely, so it is used to stack thin dies such as HBM and connect fine-pitch microbumps. Processing one chip at a time makes throughput lower than reflow.
