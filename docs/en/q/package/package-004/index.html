Packaging and assembly

Which correctly describes dicing?

  1. AThe step of encapsulating chips in resin
  2. BThe step of testing the electrical characteristics of chips
  3. CThe step of cutting a wafer into individual chips
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. The step of cutting a wafer into individual chips

Dicing cuts a finished wafer into individual chips (dies) with a diamond blade tens of micrometers thick or a laser. The wafer is mounted on tape so the chips stay in place after cutting. When fragile low-k films are present, a laser first grooves the surface to prevent chipping. Encapsulation and electrical testing are separate steps.
