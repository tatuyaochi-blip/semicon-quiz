Packaging and assembly

Which dicing method, with almost no kerf loss, focuses a laser inside the wafer to create a modified layer and then separates chips by stretching the tape?

  1. AStealth dicing
  2. BBlade dicing only
  3. CWire saw
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. Stealth dicing

Stealth dicing focuses an infrared laser, which passes through silicon, inside the wafer to form a modified layer, then expands the tape to cleave the wafer along it. Since the surface is not cut, no debris or water is involved and the kerf is nearly zero. It suits MEMS sensitive to contamination and vibration, thin wafers, and small dies where maximizing die count matters. Blade dicing needs a kerf of tens of micrometers.

  • A. Correct. A modified layer is formed inside and the wafer is broken along it.
  • B. Blade dicing cuts with a rotating blade, leaving kerf and debris.
  • C. A wire saw slices ingots into wafers.
