Packaging and assembly

Which correctly describes a feature of wafer-level packaging (WLP)?

  1. AChips are cut out one by one before the package is assembled
  2. BChips are shaped by melting them in resin
  3. CRedistribution and external terminals are formed while still in wafer form, and the wafer is then singulated
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. Redistribution and external terminals are formed while still in wafer form, and the wafer is then singulated

In WLP, redistribution layers and solder balls are formed across the whole wafer before dicing, then the wafer is singulated into chips. In fan-in WLP the terminals fit within the die; in fan-out WLP the dies are embedded in resin to form a reconstituted wafer so wiring can extend beyond the die edge. Fan-out needs no substrate and is thin, so it is used even for smartphone processors.
