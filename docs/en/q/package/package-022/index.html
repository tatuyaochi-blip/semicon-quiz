Packaging and assembly

In high-power processor packages, what is the metal plate placed on top of the chip to spread heat called?

  1. ALead frame
  2. BHeat spreader (lid)
  3. CProbe card
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. Heat spreader (lid)

A heat spreader is a metal (mainly copper) plate that spreads heat generated in the chip over a wide area so it transfers more easily to a heat sink above it. It also serves as a lid protecting the chip. A TIM is placed between it and the chip to conduct heat.
