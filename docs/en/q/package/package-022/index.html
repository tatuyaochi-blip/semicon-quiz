Packaging and assembly

In high-power processor packages, what is the metal plate placed on top of the chip to spread heat called?

  1. ALead frame
  2. BHeat spreader (lid)
  3. CProbe card
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. Heat spreader (lid)

A heat spreader is a metal plate that spreads chip heat over a larger area so it passes easily to a heat sink; nickel-plated copper, with its high thermal conductivity, is typical. It also serves as a protective lid. A thermal interface material (TIM) between die and lid fills microscopic air gaps that would otherwise add thermal resistance. A lead frame provides terminals, and a probe card is test hardware.
