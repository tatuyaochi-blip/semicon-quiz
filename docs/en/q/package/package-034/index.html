Packaging and assembly

What is the packing format called in which surface-mount ICs are placed one by one in pockets in a tape and wound up, to be fed to placement machines?

  1. AWafer cassette
  2. BTape and reel
  3. CFOUP
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. Tape and reel

Tape and reel places parts in pockets of a carrier tape, seals them with cover tape, and winds the tape onto a reel. Mounted on a placement machine, the tape is indexed while a nozzle picks up parts one after another and places them on the board. Large BGAs may be supplied in trays and through-hole parts in stick-shaped tubes.

  • A. A wafer cassette holds wafers in a row.
  • B. Correct. Parts are packed in tape wound on a reel.
  • C. A FOUP is a sealed container for moving wafers within the fab.
