Packaging and assembly

Which organic package substrate, used in flip-chip BGAs and similar packages, is made by stacking insulating layers and fine wiring layers?

  1. ALead frame
  2. BSingle-layer ceramic board
  3. CBuild-up substrate
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. Build-up substrate

A build-up substrate stacks insulating resin films and fine copper wiring layers one at a time on both sides of a glass-epoxy core. Layers are linked by small laser-drilled vias, fanning out wiring from thousands of bumps to the coarser pitch of the circuit board. It serves as the substrate for flip-chip BGAs such as processors. Lead frames or single-layer ceramic plates cannot achieve this wiring density.
