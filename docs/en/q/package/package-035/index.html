Packaging and assembly

What is the main advantage of surface mounting compared with inserting leads into holes in the PCB?

  1. AParts always become larger
  2. BSoldering becomes unnecessary
  3. CParts can be smaller, mounted densely on both sides of the board, and assembly is easy to automate
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. Parts can be smaller, mounted densely on both sides of the board, and assembly is easy to automate

Surface mounting solders component terminals to pads on the board surface, so no holes are needed, parts can be smaller, and both sides of the board can be densely populated. Placement machines and reflow ovens automate assembly in one pass, and the short terminals improve high-frequency performance. Through-hole mounting is still used for connectors and large parts that must withstand mechanical force.

  • A. Surface mounting actually allows smaller parts.
  • B. Surface mounting still uses soldering.
  • C. Correct. Small size, high density and easy automation are its advantages.
