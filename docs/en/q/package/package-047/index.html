Packaging and assembly

Which method makes very thin chips by first cutting grooves partway into the wafer from the front and then grinding the back to separate the chips?

  1. ADicing before grinding (DBG)
  2. BStepper exposure
  3. CThe CZ method
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. Dicing before grinding (DBG)

In DBG, grooves slightly deeper than the final thickness are first cut from the front of the wafer; grinding the back then separates the chips once the grooves are reached. Compared with cutting an already-thinned wafer, this reduces cracking and chipping, so it is used for thin chips such as those stacked in memory packages.

  • A. Correct. Grooves are cut first, then the back is ground to separate chips.
  • B. Stepper exposure prints patterns.
  • C. The CZ method pulls single crystals.
