Packaging and assembly

Which wire bonding method melts the tip of a gold wire with an electrical discharge to form a ball, then bonds it to the electrode with heat and ultrasonic energy?

  1. ABall bonding (thermosonic bonding)
  2. BWedge bonding
  3. CReflow soldering
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. Ball bonding (thermosonic bonding)

In ball bonding, the wire tip protruding from a fine tube called a capillary is melted by an electric spark into a ball about twice the wire diameter, which is bonded to the chip pad with heat, pressure and ultrasound. Since a ball has no orientation, the wire can be routed in any direction. Wedge bonding presses the wire flat from the side and is common with aluminum wire. Reflow uses no wire.
