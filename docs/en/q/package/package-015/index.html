Packaging and assembly

In wire bonding, what is the main reason gold (Au) wire has been increasingly replaced by copper (Cu) wire?

  1. ABecause copper oxidizes less readily than gold
  2. BBecause copper is softer than gold and easier to bond
  3. CBecause material cost can be cut greatly, and its electrical conductivity is also good
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. Because material cost can be cut greatly, and its electrical conductivity is also good

Soaring gold prices drove the switch to cheaper copper wire, which also conducts electricity and heat better than gold. However, copper oxidizes more readily and is harder, so balls are formed under nitrogen–hydrogen gas to prevent oxidation, palladium-coated wire is used, and bonding conditions are tuned to avoid cracking pads. The options claiming copper resists oxidation or is softer have it backwards.
