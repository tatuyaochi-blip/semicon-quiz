Packaging and assembly

What is the phenomenon called in which, when a moisture-laden resin package is heated in solder reflow, the moisture inside suddenly vaporizes and expands, cracking the package?

  1. AWhisker
  2. BPopcorn effect
  3. CStiction
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. Popcorn effect

Plastic packages absorb moisture from the air during storage. When exposed to reflow at about 260 °C, the moisture flashes to steam and expands, causing delamination and cracks. Because the package can crack audibly, this is called the popcorn effect; it is prevented by dry packing, limiting exposure time after opening, and baking before assembly if needed. Whiskers are needle-like crystals growing from tin plating, and stiction is sticking in MEMS.
