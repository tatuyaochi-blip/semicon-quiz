Packaging and assembly

Which composition is widely used as lead-free solder to meet environmental regulations?

  1. ASn-Pb eutectic
  2. BSn-Ag-Cu system
  3. CHg-Au system
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. Sn-Ag-Cu system

Since regulations such as the RoHS Directive restricted lead, Sn-Ag-Cu (SAC) solders based on tin, silver and copper have come into wide use. Their melting point is higher than that of conventional Sn-Pb eutectic solder, so reflow temperatures are also higher.
