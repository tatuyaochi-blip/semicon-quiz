Packaging and assembly

Which correctly describes flip-chip mounting?

  1. AA method that ships chips without encapsulating them in resin
  2. BA method that places the chip circuit side down and connects it directly to the substrate with bumps
  3. CA method that mounts the chip standing vertically on the substrate
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. A method that places the chip circuit side down and connects it directly to the substrate with bumps

In flip-chip packaging, the chip is turned face down and connected to the substrate through solder or copper-pillar bumps formed on its circuit side. The short connections have low inductance, benefiting high-speed signals and power delivery, and bumps can cover the whole die, enabling thousands of terminals. It originated with IBM's C4 technology in the 1960s. It has nothing to do with shipping unencapsulated chips or mounting them vertically.
