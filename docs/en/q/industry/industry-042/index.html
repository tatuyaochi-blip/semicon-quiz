Semiconductor industry and market

Which correctly describes chips made on so-called mature (legacy) processes older than the 28 nm generation?

  1. AThey are no longer used at all
  2. BThey are used only in the latest smartphone CPUs
  3. CThey are still used in huge volumes in microcontrollers, power devices, analog chips, sensors and the like, and are essential to cars and industrial equipment
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. They are still used in huge volumes in microcontrollers, power devices, analog chips, sensors and the like, and are essential to cars and industrial equipment

Only some products, such as CPUs and GPUs, need leading-edge scaling; many microcontrollers, power-management ICs, analog chips, power devices and sensors are made on mature processes for reasons of cost, reliability and high-voltage capability. Mature processes run on depreciated equipment, making them cheap, and long-term supply is easier to commit to. They are used in vast quantities in cars, industrial equipment and appliances, and shortages of them became a problem during the chip crisis.

  • A. Mature-process products are still used in huge volumes.
  • B. The latest smartphone CPUs are made on leading-edge processes.
  • C. Correct. They are an important product group supporting many kinds of equipment.
