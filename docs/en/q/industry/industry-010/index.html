Semiconductor industry and market

Which appropriately describes investment in advanced semiconductor fabs?

  1. AA cutting-edge fab can be built with a small investment (a few hundred million yen)
  2. BManufacturing equipment such as exposure tools is expensive, and building an advanced fab can require investment on the scale of trillions of yen
  3. CProduction relies mainly on manual labor with almost no manufacturing equipment
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. Manufacturing equipment such as exposure tools is expensive, and building an advanced fab can require investment on the scale of trillions of yen

Fabrication equipment is extremely expensive, with a single leading-edge EUV scanner costing hundreds of millions of dollars, and tools account for most of a fab's construction cost. Building an advanced fab can require investment on the scale of tens of billions of dollars, driving concentration of manufacturing at foundries and government subsidy programs. A few million dollars would not buy even one leading-edge tool, and nanometer-scale processing cannot be done by hand.
