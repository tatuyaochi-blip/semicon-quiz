Semiconductor industry and market

When a chipmaker announces the end of life (EOL) of a product, what is it called when equipment makers using it buy, one last time, the quantity they will need for future maintenance and production?

  1. AMPW
  2. BJust-in-time
  3. CLast-time buy
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. Last-time buy

When chipmakers discontinue old products, they accept final orders during a set period. Makers of equipment that needs long-term supply and maintenance, such as cars and industrial equipment, take this opportunity to buy the quantity they need all at once (a last-time buy), and afterward respond by redesigning around substitutes and the like.

  • A. MPW is a scheme for prototyping multiple designs together on one wafer.
  • B. Just-in-time is the idea of procuring and producing only what is needed when it is needed.
  • C. Correct. It is the final bulk purchase before the end of production.
