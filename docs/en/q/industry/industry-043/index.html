Semiconductor industry and market

Which set correctly names major semiconductor manufacturing equipment makers?

  1. AApplied Materials, ASML, Lam Research, Tokyo Electron and KLA
  2. BTSMC, Samsung, Intel, SMIC and GlobalFoundries
  3. CQualcomm, NVIDIA, AMD, MediaTek and Broadcom
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. Applied Materials, ASML, Lam Research, Tokyo Electron and KLA

The equipment market is led by Applied Materials (US) in deposition and more, ASML (Netherlands) in lithography, Lam Research (US) in etch, Tokyo Electron (Japan) in coater/developers, deposition and cleaning, and KLA (US) in inspection and metrology. Japan also has many other globally strong equipment makers in areas such as cleaning, dicing, testers and mask inspection. TSMC and peers are chip manufacturers, and Qualcomm and peers are fabless companies.

  • A. Correct. These are the major equipment makers.
  • B. These are chip manufacturers (foundries and IDMs).
  • C. These are fabless companies.
