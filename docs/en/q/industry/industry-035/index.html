Semiconductor industry and market

Which small computer, embedded in appliances, cars and other equipment to control them, integrates a CPU, memory and I/O circuits on one chip?

  1. AMicrocontroller (MCU)
  2. BDRAM
  3. CImage sensor
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. Microcontroller (MCU)

A microcontroller integrates a CPU, nonvolatile program memory, RAM, timers, A/D converters and other I/O on one chip, controlling everyday devices from rice cookers and air conditioners to car engines and brakes. A car carries dozens of microcontrollers, and luxury models more than a hundred. DRAM is for storage and an image sensor detects light.

  • A. Correct. It is a small computer for controlling equipment.
  • B. DRAM is memory that stores data temporarily.
  • C. An image sensor converts light into electrical signals.
