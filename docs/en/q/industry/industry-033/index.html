Semiconductor industry and market

Which company, headquartered in Taiwan, holds the largest share of the world's foundry (contract chip manufacturing) market?

  1. AASML
  2. BSynopsys
  3. CTSMC
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. TSMC

TSMC (Taiwan Semiconductor Manufacturing Company), founded in 1987, is a foundry dedicated to contract manufacturing and holds the largest share of the foundry market. It makes leading-edge logic chips for many fabless companies and is also building fabs in Japan (Kumamoto), the US and elsewhere.

  • A. ASML is a Dutch maker of lithography systems.
  • B. Synopsys is a US EDA tool company.
  • C. Correct. It is the world's largest foundry.
