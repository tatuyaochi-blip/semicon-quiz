Semiconductor industry and market

Which company, headquartered in Taiwan, holds the largest share of the world's foundry (contract chip manufacturing) market?

  1. AASML
  2. BSynopsys
  3. CTSMC
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. TSMC

TSMC (Taiwan Semiconductor Manufacturing Company), founded in 1987, is a pure-play foundry and holds the largest share of the foundry market. Having no products of its own and never competing with customers earns their trust, and it manufactures leading-edge logic for many fabless companies. It has also built fabs in Japan (Kumamoto) and the US. ASML makes lithography tools and Synopsys EDA software.

  • A. ASML is a Dutch maker of lithography systems.
  • B. Synopsys is a US EDA tool company.
  • C. Correct. It is the world's largest foundry.
