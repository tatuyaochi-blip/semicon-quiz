Semiconductor industry and market

Which correctly describes Dennard scaling?

  1. AThe number of transistors doubles about every two years
  2. BShrinking transistor dimensions and supply voltage by the same ratio allows higher speed and integration while keeping power density constant
  3. CThe semiconductor market repeats booms and busts on a cycle of about four years
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. Shrinking transistor dimensions and supply voltage by the same ratio allows higher speed and integration while keeping power density constant

Dennard scaling is the rule that shrinking transistor dimensions and voltage by the same ratio makes transistors faster and smaller while keeping power per unit area constant. In the mid-2000s, leakage current made voltage hard to lower, and scaling no longer followed this rule.
