Semiconductor industry and market

What is a company called whose business model is licensing design assets (IP) such as CPUs to other companies for fees?

  1. AFoundry
  2. BIP vendor
  3. COSAT
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. IP vendor

IP vendors develop design assets such as CPU cores and interface circuits and license them to chip companies, earning upfront license fees and per-unit royalties. Most smartphone processors use CPU cores licensed from IP vendors. Using IP lets chip companies cut development time and cost. Foundries provide manufacturing and OSATs back-end services.
