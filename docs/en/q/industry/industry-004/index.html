Semiconductor industry and market

Which correctly describes companies called OSATs?

  1. ACompanies that take on back-end processes such as semiconductor assembly and test on contract
  2. BCompanies that only design semiconductors
  3. CCompanies that make exposure tools
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. Companies that take on back-end processes such as semiconductor assembly and test on contract

An OSAT (outsourced semiconductor assembly and test) company provides back-end services, assembling (packaging) and testing chips. Major OSATs are concentrated in Taiwan, China and Southeast Asia, with strengths in labor and geographically dispersed sites. Together with foundries for front-end work and fabless design houses, they form the industry's division of labor. As advanced packaging grows in importance, foundries themselves increasingly do back-end work.
