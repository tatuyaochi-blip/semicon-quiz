Semiconductor industry and market

Which is an appropriate reason the amount of semiconductors in each car is increasing?

  1. ABecause electrification, driver assistance and information and communications functions have increased, using many more microcontrollers, power semiconductors, sensors and so on
  2. BBecause car production has fallen
  3. CBecause electronics have disappeared from cars
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. Because electrification, driver assistance and information and communications functions have increased, using many more microcontrollers, power semiconductors, sensors and so on

Electric vehicles use power semiconductors in inverters that drive the motors, and driver-assistance systems need camera and radar sensors plus high-performance processors. Infotainment, connectivity and numerous electronic control units (ECUs) add more, so semiconductor content per vehicle has risen sharply. Automotive parts require long-term supply and high reliability, and many are made on mature processes.
