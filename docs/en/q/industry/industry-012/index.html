Semiconductor industry and market

What is an IC called that is custom-designed for a specific application or customer?

  1. ACommodity memory
  2. BASIC (application-specific integrated circuit)
  3. CFPGA
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. ASIC (application-specific integrated circuit)

An ASIC (application specific integrated circuit) is designed specifically for a particular product or application. Optimized for just the needed functions, it excels in performance and power, but high development costs including masks and months of development time mean it is used where sufficient volume is expected. Reprogrammable FPGAs suit low volumes and prototypes, and commodity memory serves many applications alike.
