Semiconductor industry and market

Fabs place particular emphasis on keeping tool utilization high. Which is an appropriate reason?

  1. ABecause stopping tools makes the cleanroom dirty
  2. BBecause high utilization makes tools last longer
  3. CBecause fixed costs such as depreciation on huge investments in tools and buildings are large, so lower output sharply raises the cost per chip
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. Because fixed costs such as depreciation on huge investments in tools and buildings are large, so lower output sharply raises the cost per chip

Fab costs are dominated by fixed costs incurred regardless of output, such as depreciation of expensive equipment and buildings. When utilization drops, the same fixed costs are spread over fewer chips, raising cost per unit. Fabs therefore try to keep running even in downturns, sometimes by cutting prices, which can deepen price declines. Idling tools does not by itself contaminate the cleanroom.

  • A. Stopping tools does not in itself make the cleanroom dirty.
  • B. Utilization and tool life are not the reason here.
  • C. Correct. It is because fixed costs make up a large share.
