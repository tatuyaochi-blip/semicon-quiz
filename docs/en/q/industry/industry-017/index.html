Semiconductor industry and market

Which appropriately describes cutting-edge EUV exposure tools?

  1. AMany companies supply them cheaply, and they are easy to obtain
  2. BVery few companies can supply them, and they are central to manufacturing advanced semiconductors
  3. CThey are not used to manufacture advanced semiconductors
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. Very few companies can supply them, and they are central to manufacturing advanced semiconductors

EUV scanners combine a plasma light source created by firing lasers at tin droplets, multilayer mirrors with about 70% reflectivity, and high-vacuum technology; in practice only ASML in the Netherlands supplies production tools. Each costs hundreds of millions of dollars and is indispensable for leading-edge logic and memory. Their components come from specialist suppliers worldwide, and the tools are subject to export controls.
