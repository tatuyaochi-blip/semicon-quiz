Semiconductor industry and market

Which correctly describes a 'fabless' company?

  1. AA company that handles everything from design to manufacturing and sales in-house
  2. BA company that has no manufacturing plants of its own and specializes in design and sales
  3. CA company that takes on only back-end semiconductor processes on contract
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. A company that has no manufacturing plants of its own and specializes in design and sales

Fabless means a company without a manufacturing plant (fab). It concentrates its resources on design and sales and outsources manufacturing to foundries. A company that does everything from design to manufacturing in-house is called an IDM (integrated device manufacturer), and a company that does back-end work on contract is called an OSAT.
