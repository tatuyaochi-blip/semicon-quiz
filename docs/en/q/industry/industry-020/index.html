Semiconductor industry and market

Which industry body develops standards for DRAM such as DDR?

  1. AISO
  2. BJEDEC
  3. CSEMI
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. JEDEC

JEDEC, dating from 1958, is the semiconductor standards body that defines DRAM standards such as DDR, LPDDR, GDDR and HBM, as well as package and reliability test standards. Common standards let memory and processors from different vendors work together and foster market competition. ISO is the International Organization for Standardization, and SEMI is the industry association handling standards for equipment and materials.
