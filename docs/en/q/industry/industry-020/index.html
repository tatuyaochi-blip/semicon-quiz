Semiconductor industry and market

Which industry body develops standards for DRAM such as DDR?

  1. AISO
  2. BJEDEC
  3. CSEMI
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. JEDEC

JEDEC is an industry body that develops standards for semiconductor memory and other devices, defining DRAM standards such as DDR, LPDDR, GDDR and HBM. Common standards allow memory and processors from different makers to be used together. SEMI is an industry body dealing with standards for manufacturing equipment and materials.
