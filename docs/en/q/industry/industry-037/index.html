Semiconductor industry and market

Which law, enacted in the US in 2022, provides large subsidies and more for domestic semiconductor manufacturing and research and development?

  1. ACHIPS Act (CHIPS and Science Act)
  2. BRoHS Directive
  3. CREACH Regulation
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. CHIPS Act (CHIPS and Science Act)

The CHIPS and Science Act provided over 50 billion dollars, centered on manufacturing subsidies, to support semiconductor fab construction and R&D in the United States. In response, many companies are building new US fabs. Japan, Europe and South Korea have launched similar support programs. The RoHS directive and REACH regulation are EU rules on hazardous substances and chemicals.

  • A. Correct. It is a law supporting the US semiconductor industry.
  • B. The RoHS Directive is the EU restriction on hazardous substances.
  • C. The REACH Regulation is the EU chemicals regulation.
