Semiconductor industry and market

Which business model keeps fab investment down by making some products in its own fabs while outsourcing leading-edge products and the like to foundries?

  1. AFabless
  2. BFab-lite
  3. COSAT
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. Fab-lite

Fab-lite companies make mature-process products and those where they have unique strengths in their own fabs, while outsourcing leading-edge products that require huge investment to foundries. Sitting between fabless (no fabs) and IDM (everything in-house), the model limits capital burden while preserving manufacturing know-how. It is common among major microcontroller, analog and power semiconductor makers. OSATs provide back-end services.

  • A. Fabless means owning no manufacturing fabs.
  • B. Correct. It is a model that keeps fabs light.
  • C. An OSAT is a company that provides back-end services under contract.
