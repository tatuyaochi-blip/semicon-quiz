Semiconductor industry and market

Which business model keeps fab investment down by making some products in its own fabs while outsourcing leading-edge products and the like to foundries?

  1. AFabless
  2. BFab-lite
  3. COSAT
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. Fab-lite

In the fab-lite model, products on mature processes or where the company has strengths are made in its own fabs, while products on leading-edge processes requiring huge investment are outsourced to foundries. It sits between fabless, which owns no fabs, and IDM, which does everything in-house, retaining manufacturing know-how while limiting the investment burden.

  • A. Fabless means owning no manufacturing fabs.
  • B. Correct. It is a model that keeps fabs light.
  • C. An OSAT is a company that provides back-end services under contract.
