Semiconductor industry and market

Which is an appropriate reason the division of labor between foundries and fabless companies spread?

  1. ABecause manufacturing technology became simple
  2. BBecause investment in advanced fabs became huge, making it more efficient to separate design and manufacturing
  3. CBecause demand for semiconductors fell
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. Because investment in advanced fabs became huge, making it more efficient to separate design and manufacturing

Building a leading-edge fab requires enormous investment, and it became hard for one company's products to fill a fab. Consolidating manufacturing at specialized foundries lets many customers share equipment and keeps utilization high, while design companies focus on product development without owning fabs. This spread the division of labor, and some IDMs gave up their fabs to become fabless or fab-lite. Manufacturing has become harder, not easier.
