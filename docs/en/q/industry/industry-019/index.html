Semiconductor industry and market

Which appropriately describes the role scaling has played in semiconductor cost?

  1. AIt has fit more transistors in the same area, lowering the cost per transistor
  2. BIt has lowered manufacturing cost per wafer
  3. CIt has reduced the number of transistors per chip
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. It has fit more transistors in the same area, lowering the cost per transistor

Scaling has raised manufacturing cost per wafer, but even more transistors fit on each wafer, so cost per transistor has fallen. That is why chips at the same price have gained functionality each generation. Recently, steep wafer price increases at advanced nodes are said to have slowed the decline in cost per transistor. Transistors per chip have increased, not decreased.
