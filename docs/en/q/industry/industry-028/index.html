Semiconductor industry and market

Which is an appropriate reason the back end (packaging) has become more important in recent years?

  1. ABecause the back end is no longer needed
  2. BBecause package cost has fallen to almost zero
  3. CBecause chiplets and advanced packaging have become important means of raising system performance
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. Because chiplets and advanced packaging have become important means of raising system performance

As scaling alone struggles to raise performance, advanced packaging that densely interconnects multiple chips has become a way to boost performance and power efficiency. For AI chips, capacity for 2.5D packaging, which places HBM beside the processor and links them through a silicon interposer, now constrains product supply. The back end has neither become unnecessary nor free.
