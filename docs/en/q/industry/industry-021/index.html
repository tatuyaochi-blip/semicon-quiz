Semiconductor industry and market

Which region is known for an especially heavy concentration of contract (foundry) manufacturing of advanced logic semiconductors?

  1. ATaiwan
  2. BNorthern Europe
  3. CSouth America
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. Taiwan

Contract manufacturing of leading-edge logic is heavily concentrated in Taiwanese companies, which are said to produce most of the world's output at the most advanced nodes. This concentration is viewed as a supply risk from earthquakes and geopolitical tension, motivating efforts in the US, Japan and Europe to attract fabs. Northern Europe and South America have no large leading-edge foundries.
