Semiconductor industry and market

Because semiconductors are basic components essential to all kinds of products, what metaphor has been used for them in Japan?

  1. AThe rice of industry
  2. BThe salt of industry
  3. CThe water of industry
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. The rice of industry

Because semiconductors are basic components used in every kind of product, including home appliances, cars, communications equipment and computers, Japan has likened them to its staple food, calling them "the rice of industry." In recent years they have also been treated as strategic goods underpinning the economy and national security.

  • A. Correct. It likens them to the staple food, rice.
  • B. This expression is not commonly used.
  • C. This expression is not commonly used.
