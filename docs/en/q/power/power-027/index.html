Power semiconductors

Which metric indicates how long a power device can withstand a short circuit, such as both upper and lower switches of an inverter turning on at once, without being destroyed?

  1. AFIT
  2. BMTBF
  3. CShort-circuit withstand time
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. Short-circuit withstand time

Short-circuit withstand time is how long a device survives the large current of a shoot-through or load short—typically about 10 µs for silicon IGBTs. Protection must detect the fault and turn the device off within that time; IGBT drivers often use desaturation (DESAT) detection, sensing the rise in collector voltage. SiC MOSFETs, with small chips and low thermal capacity, often survive only a few µs and need faster protection.
