Power semiconductors

What is the main role of a gate driver for power semiconductors?

  1. ATo receive signals from the control circuit and drive the power device's gate with the right voltage and enough current
  2. BTo convert AC to DC
  3. CTo measure and display current
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. To receive signals from the control circuit and drive the power device's gate with the right voltage and enough current

The gate of a power device has a large capacitance, and fast switching requires charging and discharging it with large currents in a short time. A gate driver does this in response to small signals from a microcontroller or similar. Many gate drivers also include isolation for driving high-side devices.
