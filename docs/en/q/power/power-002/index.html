Power semiconductors

Which correctly describes an IGBT?

  1. AA two-terminal device with only two PN junctions
  2. BA device driven by a MOS gate that achieves a low on-state voltage through conductivity modulation from bipolar operation
  3. CA thyristor turned on by light
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. A device driven by a MOS gate that achieves a low on-state voltage through conductivity modulation from bipolar operation

An IGBT combines a MOSFET input with a bipolar output, adding a p+ layer on the drain side of a MOSFET-like structure. It is voltage-driven, simplifying gate drive, and when on, holes injected from the p+ layer sharply reduce drift-layer resistance (conductivity modulation), keeping on-voltage low even at 600 V to 6.5 kV ratings. It is the workhorse of EV, rail and industrial inverters, though tail current makes it unsuitable for high frequencies.
