Power semiconductors

Which correctly describes the typical division of use between silicon MOSFETs and IGBTs?

  1. AMOSFETs suit fast switching at relatively low voltages, while IGBTs suit high voltages of several hundred volts or more and large currents
  2. BIGBTs suit low voltage and small current, while MOSFETs suit high voltage and large current
  3. CBoth can only be used for the same applications
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. MOSFETs suit fast switching at relatively low voltages, while IGBTs suit high voltages of several hundred volts or more and large currents

Silicon MOSFETs use only majority carriers and switch fast, but on-resistance soars at high voltage, so they serve mostly below a few hundred volts, especially in low-voltage supplies. IGBTs keep on-voltage low at high voltage through conductivity modulation, suiting high-current uses above 600 V (EVs, industrial inverters, rail), though tail current slows them. SiC MOSFETs are now displacing both in the mid-to-high-voltage range.

  • A. Correct. MOSFETs suit fast, low-to-medium voltage use; IGBTs suit high voltage and current.
  • B. The applications are reversed.
  • C. They are chosen according to voltage and frequency.
