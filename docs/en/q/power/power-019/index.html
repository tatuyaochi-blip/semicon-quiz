Power semiconductors

What is the main advantage of using SiC power devices in electric vehicle inverters?

  1. AThey are always cheaper than silicon
  2. BLower losses lead to longer driving range and smaller cooling systems
  3. CThe motor becomes unnecessary
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. Lower losses lead to longer driving range and smaller cooling systems

SiC devices have low on-resistance and switching loss and operate well at high temperatures, so inverter losses decrease. As a result, the vehicle can travel farther on the same battery, and the cooling system and the inverter itself can be made smaller and lighter.
