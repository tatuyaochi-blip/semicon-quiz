Power semiconductors

What maximum junction temperature (Tj max) is commonly specified for typical silicon power devices?

  1. AAbout 500 °C
  2. BAbout 50 °C
  3. CAbout 150 to 175 °C
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. About 150 to 175 °C

Silicon power devices commonly have a maximum junction temperature of 150 °C or 175 °C. Above this, increased leakage, thermal runaway and shortened lifetime become likely. SiC as a material withstands higher temperatures, but the heat resistance of packages and bonding materials becomes the limit.

  • A. About 500 °C is far too high for silicon device ratings.
  • B. About 50 °C would be exceeded even in normal operation.
  • C. Correct. 150 to 175 °C is typical.
