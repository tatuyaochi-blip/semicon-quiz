Power semiconductors

Which circuit uses a diode and capacitor to create a gate voltage above the supply to drive the upper (high-side) n-channel MOSFET of a half bridge?

  1. ABootstrap circuit
  2. BCurrent mirror
  3. CSample-and-hold circuit
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. Bootstrap circuit

Turning on a high-side nMOSFET needs a gate about 10 V or more above its source (the output node), which rises to the supply voltage when on. A bootstrap circuit charges a capacitor through a diode while the low-side switch is on and the output sits near ground, then stacks that voltage on top of the output node to drive the gate. It is cheap and simple, but the high-side switch cannot be held on indefinitely.

  • A. Correct. It drives the high side with the voltage stored on a capacitor.
  • B. A current mirror produces copies of a current.
  • C. A sample-and-hold temporarily holds a voltage.
