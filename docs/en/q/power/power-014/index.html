Power semiconductors

Which is a characteristic of a thyristor?

  1. AOnce turned on by a gate signal, it stays on until the current falls nearly to zero
  2. BIt can be turned off freely at any time by a gate signal
  3. CIt is a two-terminal device
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. Once turned on by a gate signal, it stays on until the current falls nearly to zero

A thyristor is a four-layer PNPN switch; a gate pulse triggers positive feedback between its PNP and NPN transistor sections, turning it on and keeping it on without further gate signal (latching). Turning it off requires dropping the current below the holding current, which happens naturally at each AC zero crossing. Handling several kV and kA, thyristors serve in HVDC transmission and high-power rectification; GTOs can also be turned off by the gate.
