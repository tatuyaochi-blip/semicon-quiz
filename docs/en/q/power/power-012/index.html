Power semiconductors

Why are power devices required to be 'normally off', with no current flowing at 0 V gate voltage?

  1. ATo slow down switching
  2. BTo avoid the danger of current flowing unintentionally when, for example, the drive circuit fails
  3. CTo make the device cheaper
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. To avoid the danger of current flowing unintentionally when, for example, the drive circuit fails

With a normally-on device, if the gate drive power is lost or the drive circuit fails, the device turns on and a large current may flow. For safety, power supply circuits generally require normally-off devices. GaN HEMTs use specially designed structures to achieve normally-off operation.
