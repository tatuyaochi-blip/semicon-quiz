Power semiconductors

What is the main advantage of the trench gate structure in power MOSFETs, in which the gate is buried in a groove etched into the silicon?

  1. AThe gate capacitance becomes zero
  2. BThe breakdown voltage always doubles
  3. CCells can be packed densely to increase channel width and lower on-resistance
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. Cells can be packed densely to increase channel width and lower on-resistance

In a trench-gate structure the channel forms vertically on trench sidewalls, so more cells fit per unit area than in planar designs, increasing total channel width. It also eliminates the JFET resistance between adjacent planar cells, lowering on-resistance. Trench gates dominate low-voltage silicon MOSFETs, IGBTs and recent SiC MOSFETs, but the field concentrates at the trench-bottom corners, so protective structures there are critical.

  • A. The gate capacitance does not disappear; it actually tends to increase.
  • B. The breakdown voltage is set mainly by the drift layer and does not double from the structure alone.
  • C. Correct. Denser cells lower the on-resistance.
