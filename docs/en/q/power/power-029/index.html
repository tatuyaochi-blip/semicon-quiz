Power semiconductors

Which is a known cause of degradation when current flows continuously through the body diode of a SiC MOSFET?

  1. ABasal plane dislocations in the crystal expand into stacking faults
  2. BThe gate electrode melts
  3. CThe package emits light
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. Basal plane dislocations in the crystal expand into stacking faults

When forward current flows through a SiC body diode, the energy from electron-hole recombination can expand basal plane dislocations in the crystal into stacking faults, raising the on-state voltage (bipolar degradation). Countermeasures include improved epitaxial layers and circuit designs that avoid conducting current through the body diode.
