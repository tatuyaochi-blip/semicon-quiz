Power semiconductors

Which is an example of an everyday product in which GaN power devices are increasingly used?

  1. ADesks and chairs
  2. BThe mechanical contacts of light switches
  3. CCompact, high-power chargers (AC adapters) for smartphones and similar devices
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. Compact, high-power chargers (AC adapters) for smartphones and similar devices

GaN power devices switch at hundreds of kHz to MHz, allowing much smaller transformers and capacitors in chargers; units of the same output can be about half the size. Low losses and heat also aid miniaturization. Starting with chargers for phones and laptops, GaN is expanding into data-center power supplies and EV on-board chargers.
