Power semiconductors

What is the parasitic diode that forms structurally inside a power MOSFET called?

  1. ALight-emitting diode
  2. BBody diode (built-in diode)
  3. CPhotodiode
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. Body diode (built-in diode)

In a power MOSFET, a PN junction forms between the p-type body region and the n-type drift layer, creating a parasitic diode that conducts from source to drain. This is called the body diode, and in inverter circuits it is sometimes used as the freewheeling diode.
