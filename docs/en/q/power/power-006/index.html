Power semiconductors

In power devices, which loss mainly increases when the switching frequency is raised?

  1. AConduction loss
  2. BSwitching loss
  3. COnly standby leakage loss
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. Switching loss

Switching loss arises because voltage and current coexist at the moment the device turns on or off; it equals the loss per switching event times the number of switching events (frequency). Raising the frequency allows smaller inductors and capacitors but increases switching loss.
