Power semiconductors

When does the avalanche ruggedness of a power MOSFET become important?

  1. AWhen the device is overcooled
  2. BWhen no voltage is applied to the gate
  3. CWhen the current in an inductive load is interrupted and its energy drives the drain voltage up to breakdown
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. When the current in an inductive load is interrupted and its energy drives the drain voltage up to breakdown

Suddenly interrupting the current flowing in an inductor makes its stored energy drive the drain voltage up, possibly reaching the breakdown voltage and putting the device into avalanche. Avalanche ruggedness is the amount of energy the device can absorb in this state without failing, evaluated by tests such as UIS.

  • A. Cooling is not directly related to avalanche ruggedness.
  • B. Simply having no gate voltage does not cause avalanche.
  • C. Correct. Interrupting an inductive load can drive the device into avalanche.
