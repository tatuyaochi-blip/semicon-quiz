Power semiconductors

What is the main advantage of using SiC Schottky barrier diodes as freewheeling diodes in inverters and similar circuits?

  1. AThey are much cheaper than silicon diodes
  2. BThey emit light so their state can be seen
  3. CThey have almost no reverse recovery current, reducing switching loss
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. They have almost no reverse recovery current, reducing switching loss

A Schottky barrier diode operates with majority carriers only, so it has almost no reverse recovery current caused by stored minority carriers, unlike a PN junction diode. SiC makes high-voltage Schottky diodes possible, reducing losses in high-voltage inverters and power supplies.
