Power semiconductors

In SiC power modules operating at high temperatures, which material is sometimes used instead of solder to bond the chips?

  1. ASintered silver (a bonding material made by sintering silver nanoparticles and the like)
  2. BEpoxy resin
  3. CRubber
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. Sintered silver (a bonding material made by sintering silver nanoparticles and the like)

Conventional solder has a low melting point and fatigues easily under repeated use at high temperatures, so it cannot take full advantage of SiC's high-temperature operation. Bonding materials made by heating and pressing fine silver particles to sinter them have a high melting point and good thermal conductivity, so they are used in power modules requiring high temperature and high reliability.
