Power semiconductors

What is the main purpose of a snubber circuit in a power device switching circuit?

  1. ATo lower the device's on-resistance
  2. BTo suppress voltage spikes (surges) and ringing that occur during switching
  3. CTo raise the switching frequency
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. To suppress voltage spikes (surges) and ringing that occur during switching

In fast switching, parasitic wiring inductance resonates with device capacitance, causing voltage overshoot (surge) and oscillation (ringing). Snubbers—RC networks or combinations with diodes—absorb this energy, protecting the device from overvoltage and reducing noise. The absorbed energy is dissipated as heat in the resistor, so component values are chosen to balance protection against loss.

  • A. On-resistance is a device property and is not lowered by a snubber.
  • B. Correct. It suppresses surges and ringing.
  • C. A snubber is not meant to raise the switching frequency.
