Power semiconductors

In power circuits and the like, what is the small resistor placed in series in the current path to measure current called?

  1. AGate resistor
  2. BPull-up resistor
  3. CShunt resistor
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. Shunt resistor

A shunt resistor is a small resistor in series with the current path; the current is found from the voltage across it using V = IR. To keep losses low, resistors of a few milliohms with small temperature dependence are used.

  • A. A gate resistor adjusts the switching speed of power devices.
  • B. A pull-up resistor pulls a signal line up to a high potential.
  • C. Correct. It is a small resistor for current sensing.
