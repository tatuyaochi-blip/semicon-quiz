Power semiconductors

How many switching devices does a typical three-phase inverter for driving a three-phase motor use?

  1. A3
  2. B6
  3. C2
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. 6

A three-phase inverter has three legs—U, V and W—each with an upper and lower switch in series, totaling six switching devices (each with an antiparallel freewheeling diode). Each leg alternates between upper and lower switches with PWM to vary its average voltage, producing three-phase AC with 120° phase offsets. Six-in-one power modules integrating all six switches are widely used.

  • A. Three would give only one switch per phase, unable to switch between positive and negative.
  • B. Correct. 3 phases × 2 switches = 6.
  • C. Two is the count for a single-phase half bridge.
