Power semiconductors

What is the main role of power semiconductors?

  1. ASwitching or converting high voltages and currents to control power efficiently
  2. BStoring tiny signals
  3. CConverting light into electrical signals
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. Switching or converting high voltages and currents to control power efficiently

Power semiconductors—diodes, MOSFETs, IGBTs, thyristors and more—efficiently control and convert large amounts of power by switching, as in motor drives and voltage conversion. Fine control of power flow underpins energy savings in air conditioners, EVs, data centers and renewable energy. Japanese companies are relatively strong in this field. Their role differs from memory, which stores data, and photodetectors, which handle light.

  • A. Correct. They control and convert large amounts of power efficiently.
  • B. Storing data is the role of memory.
  • C. Converting light into electrical signals is the role of photodetectors.
