Power semiconductors

When SiC or GaN power devices switch at high speed, which problem particularly needs countermeasures?

  1. ANoise (EMI) and surge voltages caused by rapid changes in voltage and current
  2. BThe device emitting light
  3. CSwitching becoming too slow
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. Noise (EMI) and surge voltages caused by rapid changes in voltage and current

In high-speed switching, voltage and current change very rapidly, so surge voltages due to the parasitic inductance of wiring and electromagnetic noise (EMI) tend to grow. Countermeasures include shortening wiring to reduce parasitic inductance and adjusting the gate drive.
