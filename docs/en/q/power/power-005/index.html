Power semiconductors

What is the main role of an inverter in power electronics?

  1. ATo convert DC to AC
  2. BTo convert AC to DC
  3. CTo convert light to electricity
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. To convert DC to AC

An inverter switches power semiconductors at several kHz to tens of kHz and varies the pulse widths (PWM) to synthesize AC of any frequency and voltage from DC. It drives variable-speed motors in air conditioners and washing machines, EVs, solar power conditioners and trains, saving considerable energy by running motors only as fast as needed. Converting AC to DC is done by a converter (rectifier).
