Power semiconductors

Which correctly describes the relationship between breakdown voltage and on-resistance in silicon power MOSFETs?

  1. ABreakdown voltage and on-resistance are unrelated
  2. BRaising the breakdown voltage tends to lower the on-resistance
  3. CRaising the breakdown voltage tends to raise the on-resistance
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. Raising the breakdown voltage tends to raise the on-resistance

Raising breakdown voltage requires a thicker, more lightly doped drift layer, which increases its resistance. Theoretically, the on-resistance of silicon unipolar devices scales with about the 2.5th power of the voltage rating, so doubling the rating multiplies on-resistance by about 5.7 (the silicon limit). Superjunction structures and high-breakdown-field SiC and GaN are used to break this trade-off.
