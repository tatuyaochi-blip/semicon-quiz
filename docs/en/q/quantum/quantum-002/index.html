Quantum Computing Hardware

A qubit is in the state (√3/2)|0⟩ + (1/2)|1⟩. What is the probability of getting 1 when it is measured?

  1. A25%
  2. B50%
  3. C75%
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. 25%

The probability of getting 1 is the squared magnitude of the amplitude of |1⟩: (1/2)² = 1/4 = 25%. The probability of getting 0 is (√3/2)² = 3/4 = 75%, and the two add up to 100%. Taking the amplitude 1/2 itself as the probability leads to the wrong answer of 50%.

  • A. Correct. (1/2)² = 0.25.
  • B. This takes the amplitude 1/2 itself as the probability. The probability is the squared magnitude of the amplitude.
  • C. This is the probability of getting 0, (√3/2)² = 0.75.
