Cyber Attacks and Defense in Practice

Which of the following correctly describes the differences between vulnerability scanning, penetration testing and red team exercises as ways of checking an organization's defenses?

  1. AAll three are different names for the same method, meaning using tools to find known vulnerabilities automatically and produce a list in a report
  2. BScanning finds known vulnerabilities broadly and automatically, penetration testing actually attempts intrusion, and red team exercises evaluate even the ability to detect and respond
  3. CA red team exercise is just the work of finding known vulnerabilities automatically with tools, and it is narrower in both scope and purpose than penetration testing
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. Scanning finds known vulnerabilities broadly and automatically, penetration testing actually attempts intrusion, and red team exercises evaluate even the ability to detect and respond

Vulnerability scanning uses tools to examine many devices and pieces of software and broadly find known vulnerabilities and configuration flaws, and it is repeated regularly. Penetration testing checks, for targets within an agreed scope, whether specialists can actually break in by chaining vulnerabilities and how far they can get. A red team exercise sets an objective such as "exfiltrate customer data", imitates the methods of real attackers (such as ATT&CK techniques) and evaluates even whether the defending side (the blue team) notices and responds. All of them are carried out with the organization's prior permission on the scope and methods.

  • A. Their purposes and methods differ. Finding known vulnerabilities with tools is vulnerability scanning.
  • B. Correct. The purpose widens from broad discovery by scanning, to checking whether intrusion is possible, to testing detection and response.
  • C. A red team exercise imitates attackers to evaluate even the organization's detection and response; it is different from automated scanning.
