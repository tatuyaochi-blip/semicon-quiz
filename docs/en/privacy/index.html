Privacy policy

Semiconductor Room ("this site") handles information about its users as described below.

Personal information

This site has no user registration and does not collect personal information such as names or email addresses for browsing or answering questions.

Data stored in your browser

This site stores your answer records (correct or incorrect, time answered, and session history) and settings such as your chosen language in your browser's localStorage. This data stays on your device and is never sent to our servers. You can delete it with the reset button on the progress page or by clearing site data in your browser.

Cookies

Cookies are small pieces of data that a website stores in your browser. This site itself does not use cookies, but the advertising service and analytics tool described below do. You can disable or delete cookies in your browser settings. You can still answer the questions with cookies disabled, although the ads you see may change.

Advertising and analytics

This site uses Google AdSense, a third-party advertising service. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your previous visits to this site and other websites. You can opt out of personalized advertising in Google Ads Settings. For details, see how Google uses cookies in advertising. No ads are shown on the quiz screen.

This site may use affiliate programs (performance-based advertising) when introducing books or services. If you buy a product or sign up for a service through one of these links, this site may receive a referral fee from the seller. Referral fees never affect the content of our questions or explanations. Links and sections that contain advertising are labeled "PR" or "Ad".

This site uses Google Analytics to understand how visitors use it. Google Analytics uses cookies to collect information that does not identify you personally, and the data is handled under Google's privacy policy. You can opt out with the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on. For details, see how Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

As an Amazon Associate, this site earns from qualifying purchases.

Information sent to third parties

When you open a page on this site, information about you may be sent to the following companies.

  • Google AdSense (Google LLC): the URL of the page you view, your IP address, browser type, cookie identifiers and similar data, used to serve ads and measure their performance (Google Privacy Policy).
  • Google Analytics (Google LLC): the URL of the page you view, the referring page, browser and device type, approximate region, cookie identifiers and similar data, used to understand and improve how this site is used.

In addition, when you open the contact form or a book link, information is sent to Google Forms or Amazon under that service's own terms.

Contact form

We use an external service (Google Forms) for inquiries. Information you enter is used only to reply and to improve this site, and is not provided to third parties except as required by law.

Hosting

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Information such as IP addresses at the time of access is handled by GitHub in accordance with its own policies.

Changes

This policy may be changed when necessary. The revised policy takes effect when it is posted on this page. If this English version differs from the Japanese version, the Japanese version prevails. Questions about this policy can be sent through the contact page.
