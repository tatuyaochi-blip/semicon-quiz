PCIe 3.0 vs 4.0 vs 5.0 vs 6.0: Speed and Differences

PCI Express doubles its per-lane transfer rate with each generation, so a link of the same width gets twice the bandwidth: 3.0 runs at 8 GT/s, 4.0 at 16 GT/s, 5.0 at 32 GT/s and 6.0 at 64 GT/s. Generations 3 to 5 use simple two-level signaling, while 6.0 switches to four-level PAM4 with forward error correction. Every generation is backward and forward compatible, so a device simply runs at the highest speed that both sides support.

When to use which

PCIe 3.0
Still adequate for most graphics cards and for everyday SSD use.
PCIe 4.0
The mainstream today for fast NVMe SSDs and graphics cards.
PCIe 5.0
For the fastest SSDs and the most demanding devices, where bandwidth is the limit.
PCIe 6.0
For servers and AI infrastructure, where very high bandwidth per link is needed.
