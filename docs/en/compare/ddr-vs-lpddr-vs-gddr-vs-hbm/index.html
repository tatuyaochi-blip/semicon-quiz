DDR vs LPDDR vs GDDR vs HBM: DRAM Types Compared

All four are DRAM, but they are tuned for different jobs. DDR is the standard main memory for PCs and servers. LPDDR cuts power for phones and thin laptops. GDDR pushes per-pin speed for graphics cards. HBM stacks dies and uses a very wide bus, giving the highest bandwidth for AI accelerators at the highest cost.

When to use which

DDR
General-purpose main memory where large capacity, low cost and upgradability matter.
LPDDR
Battery-powered devices, where energy per bit and size matter more than capacity.
GDDR
Graphics and gaming, where high bandwidth is needed at a reasonable price.
HBM
AI training and high-performance computing, where bandwidth is the bottleneck and cost is secondary.
