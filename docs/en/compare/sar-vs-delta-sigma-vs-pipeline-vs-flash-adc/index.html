SAR vs Delta-Sigma vs Pipeline vs Flash ADC: Architectures Compared

An analog-to-digital converter (ADC) can be built in several ways, and each architecture trades speed against resolution. Flash ADCs are the fastest but only a few bits. SAR ADCs are small, low-power and the general-purpose choice for medium speed. Pipeline ADCs reach high speed with medium-to-high resolution. Delta-sigma ADCs trade speed for the highest resolution, which is ideal for audio and precision sensors.

When to use which

Flash
When you need extreme speed and only a few bits, or a fast building block inside another architecture.
SAR
The default for medium speed and medium-to-high resolution, especially when power and size matter.
Pipeline
When you need both high speed and good resolution, as in video and radio receivers.
Delta-sigma
When resolution matters more than speed: audio, scales and low-frequency sensors.
