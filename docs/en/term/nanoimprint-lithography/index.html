What is Nanoimprint lithography?

Transferring patterns by pressing a mold with fine features into resist on the wafer. It can make fine patterns without expensive optics.

Japanese name: ナノインプリントリソグラフィ

Answer: C. Nanoimprint lithography

Nanoimprint lithography presses a template bearing a relief pattern into liquid resist on the wafer, cures it with UV light and separates the template, copying the shape. Free of diffraction limits, it can transfer features in the 10 nm range, and without expensive optics it reduces tool cost and power. Canon has developed production tools aimed at applications such as 3D NAND, though defect control and overlay remain challenges.
