What is Nanoimprint lithography?

Transferring patterns by pressing a mold with fine features into resist on the wafer. It can make fine patterns without expensive optics.

Field: Lithography

Nanoimprint lithography in the explanations

  • Nanoimprint lithography presses a template bearing a relief pattern into liquid resist on the wafer, cures it with UV light and separates the template, copying the shape.
