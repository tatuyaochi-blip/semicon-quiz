What is Phase-shift mask?

A photomask that shifts the phase of light passing through neighboring openings, using interference to improve image contrast and resolution.

Japanese name: 位相シフトマスク

Answer: A. To change the phase of light passing through adjacent openings and use interference to improve resolution and contrast

A phase-shift mask shifts the phase of light through certain regions by 180°, so amplitudes from neighboring features cancel at their boundaries, darkening dark regions and sharpening edge contrast. Types include alternating (Levenson) masks, which invert the phase of adjacent openings, and attenuated (halftone) masks, whose absorber is a slightly transmissive phase-shifting film such as MoSi; the latter are widely used in ArF lithography.
