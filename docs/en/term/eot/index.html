What is EOT (equivalent oxide thickness)?

The thickness of SiO2 that would give the same gate capacitance as a gate insulator such as a high-k film. A physically thick film can have a small EOT, giving high capacitance while limiting tunneling current.

Japanese name: EOT(等価酸化膜厚)

Answer: A. The thickness of an SiO2 film that would give the same gate capacitance as a gate insulator such as a high-k film

EOT expresses a gate dielectric's capacitance as the thickness of SiO2 (relative permittivity 3.9) that would give the same value: EOT = physical thickness × 3.9/ε. A high-k film with ε = 20 needs about 5 nm physically for 1 nm EOT. Advanced devices reach about 0.8–1 nm EOT including the interfacial layer. Measured electrical thickness (CET) is somewhat larger because of inversion-layer quantum effects and similar contributions.
