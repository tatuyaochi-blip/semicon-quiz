What is HBM?

High Bandwidth Memory. Multiple DRAM dies stacked with TSVs, achieving very high bandwidth through an extremely wide bus.

Field: Advanced packaging (3D and chiplets)

HBM in the explanations

  • HBM stacks multiple DRAM dies with TSVs and connects to the processor over an extremely wide 1,024-bit data bus to achieve high bandwidth.
