What is Lockout/tagout (LOTO)?

A procedure that isolates energy sources such as power and gas before maintenance and fits locks and tags so no one can accidentally restore them during the work.

Japanese name: ロックアウト・タグアウト(LOTO)

Which procedure, before tool maintenance, shuts off energy sources such as electricity and gas and attaches locks and tags so that no one can mistakenly turn them back on during the work?

  2. BLockout/tagout (LOTO)

Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. Lockout/tagout (LOTO)

Lockout/tagout de-energizes equipment before maintenance and places each worker's own lock and a tag indicating work in progress on breakers and valves. Since each worker holds their own key, no one else can accidentally restore power or gas during the work. When several people work together, each attaches a personal lock. After isolation, workers also verify that no stored energy remains.
