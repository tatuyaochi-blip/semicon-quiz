What is Passivation layer?

A film formed as the topmost layer of a chip to protect the circuitry from moisture, contamination and mechanical damage. Silicon nitride and polyimide are typical materials.

Japanese name: パッシベーション膜

Which correctly describes silicon nitride deposited by plasma CVD?

  1. AIt contains relatively much hydrogen and tends to be less dense than thermal CVD film
  2. BIt is perfectly stoichiometric (Si3N4) and contains no hydrogen
  3. CIt conducts electricity like a metal
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. It contains relatively much hydrogen and tends to be less dense than thermal CVD film

PECVD silicon nitride forms from silane and ammonia at 300–400 °C, so it contains about 10–30% hydrogen as Si-H and N-H bonds and deviates from stoichiometry (SiNx). It is therefore less dense and etches faster than thermal CVD nitride. Still, it blocks moisture and sodium and deposits at low temperature, making it the final passivation layer on chips and a diffusion barrier over copper wiring.
