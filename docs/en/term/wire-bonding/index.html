What is Wire bonding?

A method of connecting the chip's bond pads to a lead frame or substrate with thin wires of gold, copper or similar metals.

Field: Packaging and assembly

Wire bonding in the explanations

  • Wire bonding connects the chip's bond pads to the terminals of a lead frame or package substrate with fine wires of gold, copper, aluminum or similar metals.
