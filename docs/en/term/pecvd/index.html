What is PECVD (plasma-enhanced CVD)?

CVD that decomposes source gases with plasma to deposit films. It can deposit at lower temperatures than thermal CVD and is used for insulating films in interconnect steps and the like.

Field: Deposition (CVD and PVD)

PECVD (plasma-enhanced CVD) in the explanations

  • Plasma-enhanced CVD uses energetic plasma electrons to decompose and activate precursors, so it deposits at about 300–400 °C rather than the 600–800 °C typical of thermal CVD.
