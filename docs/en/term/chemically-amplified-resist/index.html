What is Chemically amplified resist?

A resist in which acid generated by exposure catalyzes a chain of reactions during the post-exposure bake (PEB). It needs little exposure dose and is widely used from KrF onward, but is sensitive to basic contamination.

Japanese name: 化学増幅型レジスト

Answer: C. Negative-tone development (NTD)

Negative-tone development uses a positive chemically amplified resist but develops it in an organic solvent (such as butyl acetate) that dissolves the unexposed areas, leaving the exposed ones. For narrow trenches and small holes, keeping mask bright areas small (dark-field) gives better image contrast, so NTD is used with ArF immersion for contact holes and wiring trenches. Reduced resist swelling is another advantage.
