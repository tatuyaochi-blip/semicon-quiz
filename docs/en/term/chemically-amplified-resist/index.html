What is Chemically amplified resist?

A resist in which acid generated by exposure catalyzes a chain of reactions during the post-exposure bake (PEB). It needs little exposure dose and is widely used from KrF onward, but is sensitive to basic contamination.

Field: Lithography

Chemically amplified resist in the explanations

  • Negative-tone development uses a positive chemically amplified resist but develops it in an organic solvent (such as butyl acetate) that dissolves the unexposed areas, leaving the exposed ones.
