What is EFEM?

Short for equipment front end module: the unit at the front of a tool that opens FOUPs and transfers wafers into the tool in a clean environment. It includes load ports and a transfer robot.

Japanese name: EFEM

Which unit at the front of process equipment opens FOUP doors and transfers wafers into the equipment while keeping them in a clean environment?

  1. AOHT
  2. BULPA filter
  3. CEFEM (equipment front-end module, including load ports and transfer robots)
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. EFEM (equipment front-end module, including load ports and transfer robots)

An EFEM (equipment front end module) houses the load ports for FOUPs, the door-opening mechanism and the robot that transfers wafers into the tool, all in a space kept clean by an FFU. Wafers move between FOUP and tool without exposure to room air, making it the heart of the minienvironment. OHTs move FOUPs between tools, and ULPA filters clean the air.

Which device, arrayed on the ceiling, integrates a filter and fan to deliver clean air?

  1. AFFU (fan filter unit)
  2. BEFEM
  3. COHT
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. FFU (fan filter unit)

An FFU combines a fan with a HEPA or ULPA filter in one unit; many are installed across a cleanroom ceiling to blow clean air downward. Airflow is adjustable per unit, accommodating layout changes and local cleanliness requirements, so FFUs have largely replaced systems that push air through large ducts. An EFEM is a tool front-end module and an OHT an overhead transport vehicle.
