What is Change management?

The process for preventing failures caused by changes, in which changes to IT services and their components are recorded, assessed for impact, risk and cost, and approved before being carried out. Change requests are received and recorded, and depending on their content they are reviewed at a meeting of the people concerned. An emergency change procedure, in which a small group approves quickly, is also defined for urgent changes. After implementation, the result is checked and the configuration management information is updated.

A serious security vulnerability has been found, and a patch must be applied to the production servers immediately. The regular meeting that reviews changes is two weeks away. Which of the following is the most appropriate response in change management?

  1. ABecause it is urgent, skip both approval and records, apply the patch immediately at the discretion of the person in charge, and report only if a problem occurs
  2. BFollow the normal procedure: review the impact and risk at the regular meeting two weeks from now, and apply the patch after it is approved
  3. CFollow the emergency change procedure defined in advance: a small group of authorized approvers quickly assesses and approves the change, it is applied, and the records are completed and reviewed afterward
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. Follow the emergency change procedure defined in advance: a small group of authorized approvers quickly assesses and approves the change, it is applied, and the records are completed and reviewed afterward

Change management defines an emergency change procedure for changes that must be carried out urgently. Even for an emergency change, a small group of approvers with the authority to approve quickly assesses the impact and risk and approves it, and the change is applied after testing and a back-out procedure have been prepared as far as possible. If records and documents are completed after implementation, they are completed and reviewed afterward. If both approval and records are skipped, the cause cannot be traced when the change causes a failure.
