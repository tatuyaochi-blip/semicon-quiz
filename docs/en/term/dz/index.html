What is DZ (denuded zone)?

A layer near the wafer surface with few defects such as oxygen precipitates, made by out-diffusing oxygen during heat treatment. Devices are built in this layer while precipitates deeper inside provide gettering.

Japanese name: DZ(無欠陥層)

Heat treatment of a wafer diffuses oxygen out from near the surface, creating a low-defect layer at the surface where devices are made. What is this layer called?

  1. ABuried oxide (BOX) layer
  2. BDZ (denuded zone)

Answer: B. DZ (denuded zone)

Annealing above 1100 °C drives oxygen out of the near-surface region, creating a denuded zone (DZ) tens of µm deep free of precipitates. Subsequent low- and medium-temperature anneals nucleate precipitates in the bulk, forming a gettering layer. Devices sit in the defect-free surface while metals are trapped in the bulk. Today such profiles are also produced by hydrogen or argon anneals and rapid thermal processing.
