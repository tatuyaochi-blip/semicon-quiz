What is DZ (denuded zone)?

A layer near the wafer surface with few defects such as oxygen precipitates, made by out-diffusing oxygen during heat treatment. Devices are built in this layer while precipitates deeper inside provide gettering.

Field: Silicon wafers

DZ (denuded zone) in the explanations

  • High-temperature heat treatment drives oxygen out from near the surface, forming a denuded zone (DZ) free of oxygen precipitates at the surface and a gettering layer of precipitates inside.
