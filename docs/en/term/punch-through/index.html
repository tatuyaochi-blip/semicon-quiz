What is Punch-through?

A condition in which the source and drain depletion regions merge and current flows that the gate cannot control. It occurs when the channel is too short or the substrate doping is too low.

Field: MOSFET

Punch-through in the explanations

  • With a short channel and light substrate doping, the drain's depletion region can merge with the source's under drain bias, so current flows deep in the substrate independent of the gate and the device cannot turn off—punch-through.
