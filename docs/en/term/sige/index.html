What is SiGe (silicon-germanium)?

An alloy of silicon and germanium with a larger lattice constant than silicon. Grown epitaxially, it is used to strain channels, as the base of HBTs and as the sacrificial layer in GAA transistors.

Japanese name: SiGe(シリコンゲルマニウム)

Which is a characteristic of a SiGe HBT (heterojunction bipolar transistor)?

  1. AIt is a transistor with a metal base
  2. BThanks to a heterojunction with a SiGe base, it can operate up to high frequencies
  3. CIts current gain is fixed at 1 or less
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. Thanks to a heterojunction with a SiGe base, it can operate up to high frequencies

A SiGe HBT is a heterojunction bipolar transistor with a narrow-bandgap SiGe base. The band gap difference between emitter and base suppresses hole back-injection, so the base can be heavily doped and thin without losing injection efficiency, and grading the Ge content creates a field in the base that accelerates electrons. With fT reaching hundreds of GHz on a silicon process, it is used in smartphone RF circuits, optical communications and automotive radar.

How is silicon germanium (SiGe) classified?

  1. AA III-V compound semiconductor
  2. BAn alloy semiconductor of two group IV elements
  3. CA II-VI compound semiconductor
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. An alloy semiconductor of two group IV elements

SiGe is an alloy of group-IV silicon and germanium mixed in any proportion; both have the diamond structure and are fully miscible, and more germanium means a smaller band gap and larger lattice constant. Compatible with silicon processing, it is used in HBT bases, in pMOS source/drain regions that apply compressive strain, and as sacrificial layers when forming GAA transistors.
