What is Necking?

Growing a thin neck just after the seed at the start of CZ pulling. It lets dislocations created by thermal shock when the seed touches the melt escape, giving a dislocation-free crystal.

Field: Silicon wafers

Necking in the explanations

  • Narrowing the growth to about 3–5 mm in diameter lets them, propagating at an angle to the growth axis, run out to the surface, after which a dislocation-free crystal can be expanded to full diameter (Dash necking, 1958).
