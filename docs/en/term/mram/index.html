What is MRAM?

Non-volatile memory that stores data as the difference in resistance caused by the magnetization direction of a magnetic tunnel junction (MTJ).

Field: Emerging memories

MRAM in the explanations

  • MRAM uses a magnetic tunnel junction (MTJ): two magnetic layers sandwiching an insulating layer about 1 nm thick.

  • MRAM stores data in magnetization, so unlike flash—whose oxide wears—it endures far more cycles (over 10¹⁰), writes in a few to tens of ns, and needs no erase step.
