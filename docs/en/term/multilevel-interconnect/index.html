What is Multilevel interconnect?

A structure that stacks many wiring layers separated by insulating films and connects them vertically with vias. Leading-edge ICs have more than ten layers.

Japanese name: 多層配線

Field: Interconnect processes

How the stack is organized

Above the transistors, the front-end-of-line (FEOL), sit the middle-of-line (MOL) contacts and local interconnects, then the back-end-of-line (BEOL) metal stack. Metal layers are separated by interlayer dielectrics and connected by vias. The lowest levels have the tightest pitch for local connections between nearby cells; intermediate levels route signals between blocks; the top levels are thick and wide, carrying power, ground, clocks and long global signals with low resistance. Leading-edge logic chips use more than ten metal layers.

Copper dual damascene

Copper is hard to pattern by dry etching, so copper wiring is built by damascene processing. Trenches and via holes are etched into the dielectric, typically both at once in the dual-damascene flow, then lined with a thin barrier such as TaN/Ta that stops copper diffusing into the dielectric. A copper seed layer is deposited, the features are filled by electroplating, and chemical mechanical polishing (CMP) removes the excess, leaving inlaid lines. The sequence repeats for each level.

RC delay and materials

Wire delay scales roughly with resistance times capacitance. As lines shrink, resistance rises faster than the cross-section alone suggests, because the barrier takes up a larger share of the line and electrons scatter more at surfaces and grain boundaries. Capacitance is reduced with low-k dielectrics, carbon-doped oxides with k around 2.5 to 3 compared with about 3.9 for SiO2, though they are mechanically weaker. Current density in thin lines is limited by electromigration, the gradual movement of metal atoms under electron flow.

Contacts, vias and TSVs

A contact connects the first wiring level or local interconnect to a transistor's source, drain or gate, whereas a via connects two metal layers. Contacts only about ten-odd nanometers across now contribute significant resistance, which has driven materials such as cobalt and ruthenium into the MOL. A through-silicon via (TSV) is different again: a much larger conductor that passes through the entire silicon substrate to connect stacked dies, as in high-bandwidth memory (HBM), rather than linking layers within one die.

Answer: B. MOL (middle of line)

Middle-of-line (MOL) refers to the processes and structures between transistor fabrication (FEOL) and the multilevel interconnect (BEOL): contacts to sources, drains and gates and local interconnects. Resistance of contacts only ten-odd nm across has risen sharply with scaling and now strongly affects transistor performance, driving the adoption of cobalt and ruthenium and innovations such as contacts over active gates.

Which correctly describes a TSV (through-silicon via)?

  1. ALateral wiring formed only on the chip surface
  2. BAn electrode that passes through the silicon substrate in the thickness direction, connecting stacked chips vertically
  3. CA solder ball used as an external package terminal
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. An electrode that passes through the silicon substrate in the thickness direction, connecting stacked chips vertically

A TSV (through-silicon via) is a conductor filling a hole a few to over ten micrometers in diameter drilled through the silicon substrate, electrically linking its front and back. It enables thousands of connections between stacked dies over far shorter distances than wire bonds, so it is used in 3D stacking, HBM and silicon interposers. Lateral wiring on the surface is ordinary multilevel interconnect, and solder balls are external terminals.

FAQ

Why is copper used instead of aluminum for chip wiring?

Copper has lower resistivity (about 1.7 versus 2.7 µΩ·cm in bulk) and better electromigration resistance. Because it is hard to dry-etch, it is patterned by the damascene process.

How many metal layers does a modern chip have?

Leading-edge logic chips use more than ten metal layers, while simpler analog or mature-node chips may use only a few.

What is the difference between a contact and a via?

A contact connects wiring to the transistor's source, drain or gate; a via connects one metal layer to another.
