What is IR drop?

The drop in supply voltage reaching a circuit because current (I) flows through the resistance (R) of the power wiring. It increases delay and can cause malfunctions.

Japanese name: IRドロップ

IR drop appears in 4 practice questions across 4 topics, including Interconnect processes, Packaging and assembly, IC design flow and EDA.

Field: Interconnect processes

Try it: questions about IR drop

What is the phenomenon called in which current flowing through the resistance of power wiring lowers the supply voltage that reaches circuits?

  1. AIR drop
  2. BCrosstalk
  3. CDIBL
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. IR drop

Power wiring has resistance, so current I through it drops I × R, lowering the voltage reaching circuits—IR drop. Even a 5% drop (35 mV on a 0.7 V supply) can slow circuits by several percent, causing timing failures or malfunctions. Together with inductive drop (L·di/dt) from rapidly changing current, it is controlled with wide, meshed power grids and decoupling capacitance.

In high-pin-count BGA packages, why are many of the balls assigned to power and ground?

  1. ATo make the package heavier
  2. BTo reduce the number of signals
  3. CTo spread large currents, lowering supply resistance and inductance and suppressing power noise and voltage drop
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. To spread large currents, lowering supply resistance and inductance and suppressing power noise and voltage drop

High-performance chips can draw over 100 A, and abrupt current changes cause voltage drop from path resistance (IR drop) and fluctuations from inductance. Paralleling many power and ground balls spreads the current and lowers path resistance and inductance, stabilizing the supply. It is not unusual for more than half the balls to be assigned to power and ground.
