What is Three-phase AC?

A system that transmits three sinusoidal AC voltages shifted in phase by 120° from one another. It carries the same power with fewer wires than single-phase AC and can create a rotating magnetic field, so it is used for power transmission and distribution and for factory motors. In a Y connection the line voltage is √3 ≈ 1.73 times the phase voltage (a 200 V line voltage means a phase voltage of about 115 V), and in a Δ connection the line current is √3 times the phase current. The power of a balanced load is P = √3 × line voltage × line current × power factor.

Japanese name: 三相交流

Try it: questions about Three-phase AC

In a balanced three-phase system with a Y (star) connection, the phase voltage (between each phase and the neutral) is 100 V RMS. Which is closest to the line-to-line voltage (RMS)?

  1. AAbout 141 V
  2. B200 V
  3. CAbout 173 V
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. About 173 V

In a balanced three-phase system the phase voltages are 120° apart, and a line-to-line voltage is the (vector) difference of two phase voltages. Its magnitude is √3 times the phase voltage: 100 V × √3 ≈ 173 V. Conversely, a Y connection with 400 V line-to-line has a phase voltage of 400/√3 ≈ 231 V. In a delta connection the line and phase voltages are equal, and the line current is √3 times the phase current.

For a balanced three-phase load with a line voltage of 200 V, a line current of 10 A and a power factor of 0.8, which is closest to the load's active power (total of all three phases)?

  1. AAbout 1.6 kW
  2. BAbout 4.8 kW
  3. CAbout 2.8 kW
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. About 2.8 kW

The active power of a balanced three-phase load is P = √3 × VL × IL × cos φ, whether it is Y- or delta-connected: √3 × 200 V × 10 A × 0.8 ≈ 2771 W ≈ 2.8 kW. Viewed as a Y connection, the phase voltage is 200/√3 ≈ 115.5 V and the phase current is 10 A, so each phase takes 115.5 × 10 × 0.8 ≈ 924 W, and three times that is the total.
