What is Diffusion capacitance?

Capacitance in a forward-biased PN junction caused by injected minority carriers stored in the neutral regions. It grows in proportion to forward current and limits high-speed operation.

Field: PN junctions and diodes

Diffusion capacitance in the explanations

  • Under forward bias, minority carriers are injected and stored on both sides of the junction, and because their amount changes with voltage they behave as a capacitance—the diffusion capacitance.
