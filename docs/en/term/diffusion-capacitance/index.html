What is Diffusion capacitance?

Capacitance in a forward-biased PN junction caused by injected minority carriers stored in the neutral regions. It grows in proportion to forward current and limits high-speed operation.

Japanese name: 拡散容量

Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. Diffusion capacitance

Under forward bias, minority carriers are injected and stored on both sides of the junction, and because their amount changes with voltage they behave as a capacitance—the diffusion capacitance. It grows roughly in proportion to current (Cd ≈ τI/(nkT/q)) and can reach several nF at high current. Under reverse bias, the depletion-layer junction capacitance dominates instead. Diffusion capacitance limits the speed of diodes and bipolar transistors.
