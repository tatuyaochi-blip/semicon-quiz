What is Carry-save adder?

An adder that reduces three numbers to two, a sum and a carry, without propagating carries. Made of independent full adders per bit, its delay is constant, so multipliers use it to add many partial products.

Field: Computer Arithmetic

Carry-save adder in the explanations

  • A carry-save adder is just full adders placed independently per bit, reducing three numbers to two (the per-bit sums and the per-bit carries) without waiting for carry propagation, so its delay is a constant one full-adder level.
