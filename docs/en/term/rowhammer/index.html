What is Rowhammer?

A DRAM phenomenon in which repeatedly activating the same row in a short time drains charge from cells in neighboring rows and flips their data. Because it can be exploited in attacks, countermeasures are built into DRAMs and memory controllers.

Japanese name: ロウハンマー

Answer: B. Refreshing rows adjacent to frequently activated rows early (such as TRR), or increasing the refresh rate

Rowhammer is the phenomenon in which activating a row many times in a short period causes electrical interference that drains charge from cells in neighboring rows, flipping bits. It could let unprivileged programs alter other processes' or kernel data. Countermeasures combine mechanisms that additionally refresh neighbors of heavily activated rows, higher refresh rates and ECC to detect and correct flips.
