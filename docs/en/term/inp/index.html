What is InP (indium phosphide)?

A compound semiconductor of indium and phosphorus. As a substrate for lattice-matched layers such as InGaAs, it is used for fiber-optic lasers and photodetectors and for high-speed transistors.

Field: Compound semiconductors

InP (indium phosphide) in the explanations

  • Compound semiconductors such as InGaAsP grown on InP substrates can emit and detect light in the 1.3 µm and 1.55 µm bands, where optical fiber loss is low, so they are used in semiconductor lasers and photodiodes for optical communication.
