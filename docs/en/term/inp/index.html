What is InP (indium phosphide)?

A compound semiconductor of indium and phosphorus. As a substrate for lattice-matched layers such as InGaAs, it is used for fiber-optic lasers and photodetectors and for high-speed transistors.

Japanese name: InP(リン化インジウム)

What are InP (indium phosphide)-based compound semiconductors mainly used for?

  1. ALasers and photodetectors for optical fiber communication
  2. BPolycrystalline substrates for solar cells
  3. CDRAM capacitors
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. Lasers and photodetectors for optical fiber communication

InGaAsP and related alloys grown on InP can be tuned in composition to emit and detect light in the 1.3 µm band (low dispersion) and the 1.55 µm band (minimum loss, about 0.2 dB/km) of optical fiber. They are used in communication lasers, modulators and photodiodes, growing in importance with demand for data-center optical transceivers. InP also hosts ultra-high-speed HBTs and HEMTs.

Which is an example of an application of HBTs (heterojunction bipolar transistors) made from InP or GaAs?

  1. ADRAM memory cells
  2. BHigh-speed, high-frequency circuits for optical and wireless communication
  3. CGenerating power from solar heat
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. High-speed, high-frequency circuits for optical and wireless communication

Compound-semiconductor HBTs use a wide-gap emitter to suppress hole back-injection, allowing a thin, heavily doped base, and their high electron mobility makes them fast. GaAs-based (InGaP/GaAs) HBTs run from a single supply with high efficiency and dominate smartphone transmit power amplifiers, while InP HBTs, with fT of several hundred GHz, drive electronics for optical links beyond 100 Gb/s and terahertz research.
