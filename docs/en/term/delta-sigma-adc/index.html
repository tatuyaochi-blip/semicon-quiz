What is Delta-sigma ADC?

An ADC that oversamples at a high rate, pushes quantization noise to high frequencies and then filters it out. Used in high-resolution applications such as audio and measurement.

Japanese name: ΔΣ型ADC

Answer: B. Decimation filter

A ΔΣ modulator outputs a coarse (e.g., 1-bit) stream at a high sampling rate. The decimation filter averages it with a digital low-pass filter, removing high-frequency quantization noise, and lowers the sample rate while producing high-resolution data. It often combines CIC and FIR filters, and its delay is the main source of ΔΣ ADC latency.
