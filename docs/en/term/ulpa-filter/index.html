What is ULPA filter?

A high-performance air filter that captures even finer particles than a HEPA filter. It is used in the ceilings of high-cleanliness cleanrooms.

Japanese name: ULPAフィルタ

Answer: A. FFU (fan filter unit)

An FFU combines a fan with a HEPA or ULPA filter in one unit; many are installed across a cleanroom ceiling to blow clean air downward. Airflow is adjustable per unit, accommodating layout changes and local cleanliness requirements, so FFUs have largely replaced systems that push air through large ducts. An EFEM is a tool front-end module and an OHT an overhead transport vehicle.
