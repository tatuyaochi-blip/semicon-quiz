What is Correlation coefficient?

A measure expressing the strength and direction of the linear relationship between two quantities as a value from −1 to 1. It is calculated by dividing the covariance of x and y by the product of the standard deviation of x and the standard deviation of y. The closer it is to 1, the stronger the positive correlation; the closer to −1, the stronger the negative correlation; close to 0, the linear relationship is weak. Curved relationships hardly show up in the correlation coefficient, and correlation does not mean that one quantity causes the other. The slope of the regression line is the correlation coefficient multiplied by the ratio of the standard deviations of the two quantities.

  2. BWhen the correlation coefficient is −0.9, there is almost no relationship between the two quantities
  3. CThe larger the correlation coefficient, the larger the slope of the regression line predicting one quantity from the other, without exception
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. Even if the correlation coefficient is close to 0, there may be a curved relationship between the two quantities

The correlation coefficient expresses the strength and direction of the linear relationship between two quantities as a value from −1 to 1. Close to 1, the points lie along a line rising to the right; close to −1, along a line falling to the right; close to 0, the linear relationship is weak. For example, for y = x² with x from −1 to 1, there is a strong valley-shaped relationship, yet the correlation coefficient can be close to 0. The slope of the regression line depends not only on the correlation coefficient but also on the ratio of the standard deviations of the two quantities.

Which of the following is closest to the correlation coefficient of the five data pairs (x, y) = (1, 2), (2, 4), (3, 5), (4, 4), (5, 5)?

  1. A0.50
  2. B0.60
  3. C0.77
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. 0.77

The mean of x is 3 and the mean of y is 4. The sum of the products of the differences from the means is (−2) × (−2) + (−1) × 0 + 0 × 1 + 1 × 0 + 2 × 1 = 6, the sum of the squared differences of x from its mean is 10, and that of y is 6. The correlation coefficient is 6 ÷ √(10 × 6) = 6 ÷ √60 ≈ 0.77, a fairly strong positive correlation.
