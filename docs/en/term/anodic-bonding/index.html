What is Anodic bonding?

Joining silicon to sodium-containing glass by heating them together while applying a voltage. It is used, for example, to seal MEMS devices.

Japanese name: 陽極接合

Answer: A. Anodic bonding

Anodic bonding stacks sodium-containing borosilicate glass (such as Pyrex) on silicon, heats them to 300–450 °C, and applies several hundred to about 1000 V with the glass negative. Sodium ions migrate, creating strong electrostatic attraction at the interface, and oxygen bonds form a strong hermetic seal. Glass with thermal expansion close to silicon's limits warpage, and the technique is used for pressure sensors and packaging.
