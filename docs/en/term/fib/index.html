What is FIB (focused ion beam)?

An instrument that mills or deposits material with a finely focused ion beam. It is used for cross-sectioning tiny areas, preparing thin TEM samples and editing circuits.

Field: Metrology and analysis

FIB (focused ion beam) in the explanations

  • An FIB focuses ions such as gallium onto the sample to mill chosen spots with nanometer precision, or to deposit platinum or tungsten when combined with precursor gases.

    What are the main uses of focused ion beam (FIB) systems?

  • For semiconductor devices, the lift-out method is common: an FIB mills around the region of interest, extracts a thin lamella and finishes it.
