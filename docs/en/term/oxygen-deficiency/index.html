What is Oxygen deficiency?

A condition in which the oxygen concentration in air falls below 18%. It tends to occur where large amounts of nitrogen are used or in confined spaces, and can cause unconsciousness within a few breaths.

Japanese name: 酸素欠乏(酸欠)

Which gas, one of the most heavily used in semiconductor fabs, is supplied by pipeline for purging and as an atmosphere gas?

  1. APhosphine
  2. BNitrogen (N2)
  3. CNitrogen trifluoride (NF3)
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. Nitrogen (N2)

Nitrogen is inert and cheap, so it is used in huge volumes for purging tools and lines, replacing the atmosphere in FOUPs and tools, and protecting vacuum pumps. Large fabs place air separation plants on or next to the site and pipe it in. A leak into a confined space can drop oxygen below 18%, risking oxygen deficiency. Phosphine and NF3 are specialty gases used only in specific processes.

Which occupational hazard requires particular attention where large amounts of nitrogen gas are used?

  1. AOxygen deficiency
  2. BElectric shock
  3. CNoise-induced hearing loss
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. Oxygen deficiency

Nitrogen is colorless, odorless and nontoxic, but a leak that lowers oxygen concentration causes oxygen deficiency without warning. Below 18% oxygen is considered hazardous, and a single breath of air very low in oxygen can cause loss of consciousness. Before entering confined spaces or tools using nitrogen, measure oxygen, ventilate thoroughly and post an attendant. Electric shock and noise are not hazards specific to nitrogen.
