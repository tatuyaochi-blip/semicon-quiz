What is USB Power Delivery?

A specification in which source and sink negotiate voltage and current over USB, allowing up to 48 V and 240 W.

Japanese name: USB PD

Which describes USB Power Delivery (USB PD)?

  1. AThe source and sink negotiate voltage and current so that higher voltages than 5 V can deliver more power
  2. BIt carries USB data over power lines
  3. CIt always applies 20 V to every device to charge faster
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. The source and sink negotiate voltage and current so that higher voltages than 5 V can deliver more power

Basic USB power is 5 V with limited power. With USB PD, after connection the two sides communicate over the CC wire and agree on a voltage and current the sink can accept before the voltage is raised. It is used to charge laptops and similar, and extended versions can deliver up to 48 V at 5 A (240 W). Until the agreement is made, the supply stays at a safe 5 V.
