What is Schottky barrier diode?

A diode that uses a metal-semiconductor junction (Schottky junction). It has a low forward voltage and switches fast because there is no minority-carrier storage.

Field: PN junctions and diodes

Schottky barrier diode in the explanations

  • A Schottky barrier diode uses a metal-semiconductor (Schottky) junction and operates with majority carriers only.

    Which is a characteristic of a Schottky barrier diode?

  • A Schottky barrier diode operates with majority carriers only, so it has almost no reverse recovery current caused by stored minority carriers, unlike a PN junction diode.
