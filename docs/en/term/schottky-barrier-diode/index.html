What is Schottky barrier diode?

A diode that uses a metal-semiconductor junction (Schottky junction). It has a low forward voltage and switches fast because there is no minority-carrier storage.

Japanese name: ショットキーバリアダイオード

Which is a characteristic of a Schottky barrier diode?

  1. AIt is a diode made by joining two p-type semiconductors
  2. BIt uses a metal-semiconductor junction, with a low forward voltage and fast switching
  3. CIt emits strong light when forward current flows
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. It uses a metal-semiconductor junction, with a low forward voltage and fast switching

A Schottky barrier diode uses a metal-semiconductor (Schottky) junction and operates with majority carriers only. With no minority-carrier storage it has almost no reverse recovery and switches fast, and its forward voltage of about 0.2–0.4 V is lower than a PN junction's, so it is used for rectification in switching power supplies. Its weakness is larger reverse leakage that rises with temperature; silicon parts reach only about 200 V, while SiC versions above 1 kV are in practical use.

What is the aim of a transistor with a Schottky barrier diode connected across its base-collector junction (a Schottky clamp)?

  1. ATo increase the current gain
  2. BTo keep the transistor out of deep saturation, reducing stored charge and speeding up switching
  3. CTo increase the breakdown voltage
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. To keep the transistor out of deep saturation, reducing stored charge and speeding up switching

A Schottky diode conducts at about 0.3 V, lower than a PN junction (about 0.7 V). It therefore diverts excess base current before the base-collector junction becomes deeply forward-biased, keeping the transistor out of deep saturation. Less minority charge is stored in the base, so turn-off is faster; this was used in the 74S and 74LS (Schottky TTL) families for speed and lower power. The trade-off is a slightly higher VCE(sat), about 0.4 V.
