What is Ashing?

Removing photoresist that is no longer needed after etching or implantation by oxidizing it to ash, typically with an oxygen plasma.

Japanese name: アッシング

Ashing appears in 7 practice questions across 4 topics, including Ion implantation, Etching, CMP and cleaning.

What problem must be watched for when etching or ashing low-k films?

  1. AThe film becomes far too hard
  2. BPlasma damages the film, for example by removing carbon, raising its dielectric constant
  3. CThe film emits light
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. Plasma damages the film, for example by removing carbon, raising its dielectric constant

SiOC low-k films lower permittivity with Si-CH3 (methyl) groups, but exposure to oxygen plasma strips them into Si-OH, making the film absorb moisture and raising its permittivity from about 3.0 to above 4. In porous films damage penetrates through the pores. Low-damage ashing (e.g., hydrogen-based), silylation treatments that restore damaged surfaces and post-process dehydration are therefore required.
