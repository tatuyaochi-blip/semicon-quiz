What is X-optimism?

In HDL simulation, the tendency for conditions containing unknown X to resolve to definite values instead of propagating X, hiding bugs.

Japanese name: X楽観主義

X-optimism appears in 2 practice questions across 1 topic, including HDL & Verification.

What is the main purpose of simulating the post-synthesis or post-layout gate-level netlist with delay information?

  1. ATo run many tests faster than RTL simulation
  2. BTo check issues invisible in RTL simulation, such as initialization or reset bugs, synthesis differences and delay-related problems
  3. CTo reduce circuit area
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. To check issues invisible in RTL simulation, such as initialization or reset bugs, synthesis differences and delay-related problems

RTL simulation is fast but cannot reveal unknown-value problems hidden by X-optimism, or cases where synthesis produced a circuit different from the description. Gate-level simulation exercises the actual gates and flip-flops, and with each gate's and wire's delay supplied in SDF format, delay-related problems also show up. It is very slow, so it is generally run on a limited set of tests.
