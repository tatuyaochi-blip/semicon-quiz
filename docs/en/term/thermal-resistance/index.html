What is Thermal resistance?

A measure of how hard it is for heat to flow, in °C/W. Multiplying dissipated power by thermal resistance gives the temperature rise. Junction-to-ambient thermal resistance is written θJA.

Field: Packaging and assembly

Thermal resistance in the explanations

  • Temperature rise is thermal resistance × power: 2 °C/W × 10 W = 20 °C, so at 50 °C ambient the junction reaches 70 °C.
