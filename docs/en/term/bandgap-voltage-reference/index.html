What is Bandgap voltage reference?

A circuit that adds a diode voltage that falls with temperature to a voltage that rises with temperature, producing a reference of about 1.2 V that barely depends on temperature. It is widely used in ADCs and power ICs.

Japanese name: バンドギャップ基準電圧回路

Answer: A. It decreases by about 2 mV per °C as temperature rises

At constant current, a silicon PN junction's forward voltage drops about 2 mV per °C. The voltage difference between two junctions at different current densities is proportional to absolute temperature (PTAT), enabling on-chip temperature sensors with about ±1 °C accuracy. They protect CPUs from overheating and monitor batteries, and combining the two temperature dependencies to cancel yields a bandgap voltage reference.
