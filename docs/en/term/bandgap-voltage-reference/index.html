What is Bandgap voltage reference?

A circuit that adds a diode voltage that falls with temperature to a voltage that rises with temperature, producing a reference of about 1.2 V that barely depends on temperature. It is widely used in ADCs and power ICs.

Field: Analog and electronic circuits
