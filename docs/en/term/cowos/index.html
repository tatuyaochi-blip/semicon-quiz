What is CoWoS?

TSMC's 2.5D packaging technology, which places logic chips and HBM side by side on a silicon interposer. It is widely used for AI GPUs and similar products.

Field: Advanced packaging (3D and chiplets)

  • CoWoS (chip on wafer on substrate) is a 2.5D packaging technology in which dies are mounted on a wafer-form interposer (chip on wafer), which is then mounted on a package substrate (on substrate).
