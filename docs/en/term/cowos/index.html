What is CoWoS?

TSMC's 2.5D packaging technology, which places logic chips and HBM side by side on a silicon interposer. It is widely used for AI GPUs and similar products.

Japanese name: CoWoS

Which TSMC 2.5D packaging technology is widely used to place AI GPUs and HBM side by side on a silicon interposer and connect them?

  1. AInFO
  2. BEMIB
  3. CCoWoS
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. CoWoS

CoWoS (chip on wafer on substrate) is a 2.5D packaging technology in which dies are mounted on a wafer-form interposer (chip on wafer), which is then mounted on a package substrate (on substrate). Interposers can now be several times the lithography field size, and CoWoS is widely used to connect AI GPUs with multiple HBM stacks. Surging AI demand made expanding its capacity a major challenge. InFO is a fan-out technology and EMIB is Intel's bridge approach.

Which TSMC fan-out wafer-level packaging technology became widely known when it was adopted for a smartphone processor in 2016?

  1. AInFO (Integrated Fan-Out)
  2. BCoWoS
  3. CHBM
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. InFO (Integrated Fan-Out)

InFO is a fan-out packaging technology that forms redistribution layers on a reconstituted wafer with dies embedded in mold compound, making packages thin and small without a substrate. Its adoption in 2016 for Apple's smartphone processors, in a PoP configuration with memory stacked on top, triggered the widespread adoption of fan-out advanced packaging. CoWoS is 2.5D packaging with an interposer, and HBM is stacked memory.
