What is PCM (phase-change memory)?

Nonvolatile memory that stores information by the difference between the crystalline (low-resistance) and amorphous (high-resistance) states of a chalcogenide material.

Field: Emerging memories

PCM (phase-change memory) in the explanations

  • PCM heats chalcogenide materials such as GeSbTe with Joule heating from current and quenches them, switching between a low-resistance crystalline state and a high-resistance amorphous state.
