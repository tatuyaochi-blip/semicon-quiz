What is Random dopant fluctuation (RDF)?

Threshold-voltage variation in small transistors caused by statistical variation in the number and position of dopant atoms in the channel. FinFETs and FD-SOI, which allow lightly doped channels, reduce it.

Japanese name: ランダムドーパントゆらぎ(RDF)

Try it: questions about Random dopant fluctuation (RDF)

How does the threshold voltage change when the impurity concentration of the channel region (p-type substrate) of an nMOSFET is increased?

  1. AIt becomes higher
  2. BIt becomes lower
  3. CIt does not change
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. It becomes higher

Higher substrate doping means more acceptor charge in the depletion region before the surface inverts, and a larger Fermi potential φF, so more gate voltage is needed and Vth rises. Channel implants adjust Vth this way. But heavy doping lowers mobility and increases variability from random dopant fluctuation, so FinFETs and GAA devices use nearly undoped channels and set Vth with the gate work function.

In recent advanced processes, what is the most appropriate reason SRAM cell area has become harder to shrink than logic?

  1. ASRAM is sensitive to variations in transistor characteristics, making it hard to secure margins for stable reading and writing
  2. BSRAM does not use transistors
  3. CSRAM needs a large capacitor
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. SRAM is sensitive to variations in transistor characteristics, making it hard to secure margins for stable reading and writing

An SRAM cell's stability depends on balancing the drive strengths of six near-minimum-size transistors. With scaling, variation from effects such as random dopant fluctuation grows relative to device size, making it hard to keep read and write margins even for the worst of hundreds of millions of cells. In recent nodes SRAM cell area has shrunk much less than logic, so cache area has become a growing share of the die.
