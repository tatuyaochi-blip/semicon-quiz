What is Gustafson's law?

The observation that when processors are added and the problem is enlarged, the parallel fraction grows, giving gains nearly proportional to processor count.

Japanese name: グスタフソンの法則

Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. When adding processors, also enlarging the problem increases the parallel fraction, yielding large speedups

Amdahl's law considers adding processors to a fixed-size problem, so the serial part caps the speedup. Gustafson's law instead assumes that with more processors one solves a larger problem in the same time. Enlarging the problem, for example with a finer simulation grid, grows the parallel part and shrinks the serial fraction, giving gains nearly proportional to processor count.
