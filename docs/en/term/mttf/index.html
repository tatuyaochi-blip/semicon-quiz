What is MTTF (mean time to failure)?

The average time until a non-repairable product fails. It is used as an indicator of product lifetime.

Japanese name: MTTF(平均故障時間)

What is the mean time to failure (MTTF) of a part with a constant failure rate of 1×10⁻⁵ per hour?

  1. A1×10³ hours
  2. B1×10⁵ hours
  3. C1×10⁷ hours
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. 1×10⁵ hours

With a constant failure rate, MTTF is the reciprocal of the failure rate: 1 ÷ (1×10⁻⁵) = 1×10⁵ hours, about 11 years. 1×10³ or 1×10⁷ hours result from exponent errors. However, this value derives from the random-failure rate and does not indicate when wear-out begins. MTTF can even exceed the actual wear-out life, so the two must not be confused.
