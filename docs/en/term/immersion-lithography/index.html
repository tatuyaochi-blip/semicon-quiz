What is Immersion lithography?

Exposure with the space between the lens and the wafer filled with water. It allows a numerical aperture above 1, so ArF excimer lasers can form finer patterns.

Field: Lithography

Immersion lithography in the explanations

  • In immersion lithography, the space between the projection lens and the wafer is filled with water (refractive index about 1.44), which has a higher refractive index than air (about 1).
