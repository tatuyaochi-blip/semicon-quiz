What is 3D XPoint?

Cross-point non-volatile memory jointly developed by Intel and Micron. It stacked phase-change material with OTS selectors and was sold as Optane, but the business was wound down in 2022.

Japanese name: 3D XPoint

Answer: B. 3D XPoint

3D XPoint stacked resistive storage elements (believed to be phase-change based) with OTS selectors at word-line/bit-line crossings. Announced in 2015 and productized as Optane, it claimed roughly 1000× NAND's speed with more capacity and lower cost than DRAM, appearing in SSDs and memory modules. Squeezed by steadily improving NAND and DRAM, the market never grew, and the business was wound down in 2022.
