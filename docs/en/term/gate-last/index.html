What is Gate-last (replacement metal gate)?

Building the transistor first with a dummy gate, then removing it after high-temperature steps such as source/drain activation and replacing it with a high-k dielectric and metal gate.

Field: Advanced transistors (FinFET and GAA)

Gate-last (replacement metal gate) in the explanations

  • In a gate-last flow, polysilicon dummy gates go through the hot steps first; then the dielectric is planarized to expose the dummies, which are removed, and high-k and metal gates fill the resulting trenches.
