What is Gate-last (replacement metal gate)?

Building the transistor first with a dummy gate, then removing it after high-temperature steps such as source/drain activation and replacing it with a high-k dielectric and metal gate.

Japanese name: ゲートラスト(リプレースメントメタルゲート)

  1. AThe metal gate is made first, and then all high-temperature steps are performed
  2. BThe transistor is first formed with a dummy gate, and after high-temperature steps the dummy is removed and replaced with a metal gate
  3. CA transistor without a gate is made
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. The transistor is first formed with a dummy gate, and after high-temperature steps the dummy is removed and replaced with a metal gate

High-temperature steps such as source/drain activation anneals (around 1000 °C) can shift the work function of high-k films and metal gates. In a gate-last flow, polysilicon dummy gates go through the hot steps first; then the dielectric is planarized to expose the dummies, which are removed, and high-k and metal gates fill the resulting trenches. Intel adopted it at 45 nm, and it is now mainstream.
