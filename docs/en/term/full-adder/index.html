What is Full adder?

A circuit that adds two bits plus a carry-in from the lower digit and outputs a sum and a carry-out. A version without carry-in is a half adder. Chaining full adders makes a multi-bit adder.

Japanese name: 全加算器

  2. BOnly the two bits to be added
  3. COnly a clock signal
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. The two bits to be added and the carry from the lower digit

A full adder takes three inputs—bits A and B plus a carry-in from the lower position—and produces a sum and a carry-out. Chaining n full adders adds n-bit numbers (a ripple-carry adder). Setting the lowest carry-in to 1 and inverting B computes A − B = A + (¬B + 1), so the same circuit subtracts. This is how a CPU's ALU uses one adder for both addition and subtraction.
