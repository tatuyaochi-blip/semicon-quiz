What is Interface states?

Energy levels within the band gap at a semiconductor-insulator interface, created by broken bonds and similar defects. They trap and release carriers, causing threshold shifts, lower mobility and noise.

What is the heat treatment called, performed in a hydrogen-containing atmosphere to reduce interface states at the Si/SiO2 interface?

  1. AOxygen plasma treatment
  2. BForming gas anneal
  3. CSputtering
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. Forming gas anneal

The Si/SiO2 interface retains interface states from dangling silicon bonds at about 10¹¹–10¹² cm⁻²eV⁻¹. Annealing at about 400–450 °C in forming gas (a few percent hydrogen in nitrogen) lets hydrogen passivate those bonds, reducing interface states to about 10¹⁰ cm⁻²eV⁻¹ and improving threshold stability, subthreshold slope and mobility. It is often performed at the end of interconnect processing.
