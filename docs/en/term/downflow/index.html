What is Downflow?

A cleanroom airflow method that sends clean air in one direction from the entire ceiling to the floor, quickly carrying generated particles away under the floor.

Japanese name: ダウンフロー

Try it: questions about Downflow

What is the main purpose of the downflow method, in which air flows in one direction from ceiling to floor in a cleanroom?

  1. ATo quickly carry generated particles below the floor so they do not linger around wafers
  2. BTo raise the room temperature
  3. CTo lower noise in the room
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. To quickly carry generated particles below the floor so they do not linger around wafers

Clean air from high-efficiency ceiling filters flowing uniformly downward carries particles from people and tools to the underfloor before they can rise, keeping them from lingering around wafers. It achieves higher cleanliness than mixing and diluting room air, but moving such large air volumes consumes much energy. Adjusting temperature or noise is not the purpose.

In downflow cleanrooms, why are perforated floor panels (gratings) used?

  1. ATo make the floor non-slip
  2. BTo make the floor lighter
  3. CTo pass air blown from the ceiling into the space below the floor for recirculation
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. To pass air blown from the ceiling into the space below the floor for recirculation

In downflow cleanrooms, air from ceiling filters is drawn through perforated floor panels into the underfloor space, then returns through side chases to the ceiling. If the floor did not pass air, the flow would deflect sideways at the floor and stir particles up. Because air keeps moving in one direction, particles are swept to the underfloor immediately. Slip resistance or lighter weight is not the purpose.
