What is Audit trail?

Records that make it possible to trace and verify afterward the flow of a transaction or data from input through processing, output and storage, and who did what and when. Access and operation logs, processing records and approval records are examples. Auditors follow them to verify that processing was legitimate and accurate. Mechanisms that protect them from tampering or deletion and keep them for the required period are considered from the system design stage.

Japanese name: 監査証跡

An audit trail is a set of records that makes it possible to trace and verify afterward the flow of data from input through processing, output and storage, together with who operated on it and when. Access and operation logs, processing records and approval records are examples, and auditors follow the audit trail to verify that processing was legitimate and accurate. It is important to plan from the system design stage so that an audit trail is kept.
