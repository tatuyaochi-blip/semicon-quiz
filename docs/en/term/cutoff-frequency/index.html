What is Cutoff frequency?

The frequency at which the output of a filter or amplifier falls 3 dB (to about 0.707 times) below the passband. For an RC circuit it is 1/(2πRC).

Field: Analog and electronic circuits

Cutoff frequency in the explanations

  • −3 dB corresponds to about half the power, or 1/√2 ≈ 0.707 of the voltage, and filters and amplifiers use this point as the cutoff frequency (the band edge).
