What is GaN (gallium nitride)?

A III-V compound semiconductor with a band gap of about 3.4 eV. Used in blue LEDs and in high-frequency and power devices.

Japanese name: GaN(窒化ガリウム)

Which is the channel through which current flows in a GaN HEMT?

  1. AA p-type inversion layer formed under the gate
  2. BA thick, heavily doped n-type layer
  3. CA two-dimensional electron gas (2DEG) formed by polarization at the AlGaN/GaN interface
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. A two-dimensional electron gas (2DEG) formed by polarization at the AlGaN/GaN interface

In a GaN HEMT, the difference in spontaneous and piezoelectric polarization at the AlGaN/GaN interface creates a dense two-dimensional electron gas (2DEG) of about 10¹³ cm⁻². Because no doping is needed to supply the electrons, scattering is low and mobility reaches about 2000 cm²/V·s, combining low on-resistance with fast switching. The 2DEG exists even at 0 V gate bias, so the basic device is normally on.

What is the general term for semiconductors with a larger band gap than silicon, such as SiC and GaN?

  1. AWide-band-gap semiconductors
  2. BNarrow-gap semiconductors
  3. CAmorphous semiconductors
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. Wide-band-gap semiconductors

Semiconductors with bandgaps of roughly 3 eV or more are called wide-bandgap semiconductors; SiC (about 3.3 eV) and GaN (about 3.4 eV) are the main examples. High breakdown fields and few intrinsic carriers let them operate at high temperature, suiting high-voltage, low-loss power and RF devices. Even wider-gap Ga2O3 (about 4.8 eV) and diamond (about 5.5 eV) are called ultra-wide-bandgap semiconductors.
