What is FOUP?

A sealed container for transporting and storing 300 mm wafers.

Japanese name: FOUP

Which correctly describes a FOUP?

  1. AThe light source of an exposure tool
  2. BA machine that polishes wafers
  3. CA container that transports and stores 300 mm wafers sealed inside
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. A container that transports and stores 300 mm wafers sealed inside

A FOUP (front opening unified pod) is a front-opening container that seals and carries up to 25 300 mm wafers, weighing about 8 kg when loaded. Keeping wafers clean only inside FOUPs and tools prevents contamination without making the whole fab ultra-clean. Standardized dimensions and door mechanisms let it fit any vendor's tool, and it is moved automatically by overhead transport.

Which unit at the front of process equipment opens FOUP doors and transfers wafers into the equipment while keeping them in a clean environment?

  1. AOHT
  2. BULPA filter
  3. CEFEM (equipment front-end module, including load ports and transfer robots)
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. EFEM (equipment front-end module, including load ports and transfer robots)

An EFEM (equipment front end module) houses the load ports for FOUPs, the door-opening mechanism and the robot that transfers wafers into the tool, all in a space kept clean by an FFU. Wafers move between FOUP and tool without exposure to room air, making it the heart of the minienvironment. OHTs move FOUPs between tools, and ULPA filters clean the air.
