What is FOUP?

A sealed container for transporting and storing 300 mm wafers.

Field: Cleanrooms and equipment

FOUP in the explanations

  • A FOUP (front opening unified pod) is a front-opening container that seals and carries up to 25 300 mm wafers, weighing about 8 kg when loaded.
