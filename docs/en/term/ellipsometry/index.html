What is Ellipsometry?

A measurement method that shines polarized light on a sample and determines thin-film thickness and refractive index from the change in polarization on reflection. It is nondestructive.

Field: Metrology and analysis

Ellipsometry in the explanations

  • Ellipsometry shines polarized light at an angle and measures the amplitude ratio (Ψ) and phase difference (Δ) between p- and s-polarized reflections, fitting them to an optical model to extract film thickness and refractive index.
