What is Gettering?

A technique that collects and traps metal impurities in a wafer at locations away from the surface where devices are built, using oxygen precipitates and the like.

Field: Silicon wafers

Gettering in the explanations

  • Gettering collects and traps metal impurities that degrade device characteristics at defects inside the wafer or on its back, away from the device region at the surface.
