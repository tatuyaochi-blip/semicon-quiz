What is SPM (sulfuric acid-hydrogen peroxide mixture)?

A mixture of sulfuric acid and hydrogen peroxide. Its strong oxidizing power removes organics such as resist.

Japanese name: SPM(硫酸過水)

What is the main purpose of cleaning with a mixture of sulfuric acid and hydrogen peroxide (SPM)?

  1. ARemoving organics such as resist
  2. BRemoving native oxide
  3. CDepositing copper
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. Removing organics such as resist

SPM (H2SO4:H2O2, e.g., 4:1 at about 120–150 °C) forms peroxymonosulfuric acid (Caro's acid), whose powerful oxidizing action decomposes organic residues such as resist. Used after ashing and for post-implant resist strip, it is so reactive it is nicknamed "piranha solution" and demands careful handling. Because it consumes large amounts of sulfuric acid, alternatives such as ozonated water are being adopted.

Which known problem tends to occur if rinsing after SPM (sulfuric acid and hydrogen peroxide) cleaning is insufficient?

  1. ASulfate ions and the like remain on the surface and later cause haze and defects
  2. BThe wafer becomes thicker
  3. CThe surface becomes n-type
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. Sulfate ions and the like remain on the surface and later cause haze and defects

Viscous sulfuric acid adsorbs readily, so inadequate rinsing leaves sulfate ions that react with airborne ammonia to precipitate ammonium sulfate microcrystals, causing haze and defects. On photomasks this produced progressive haze defects that reduced transmission. Thorough hot-water rinsing and sulfuric-acid-free cleaning are the countermeasures.
