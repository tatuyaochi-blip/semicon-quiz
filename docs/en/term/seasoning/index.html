What is Seasoning?

Running dummy wafers before product to stabilize the condition of an etch (or similar) chamber's walls, since wall changes alter process results.

Field: Etching

Seasoning in the explanations

  • Seasoning runs dummy wafers after cleaning or part replacement to build a consistent wall coating and stabilize performance.
