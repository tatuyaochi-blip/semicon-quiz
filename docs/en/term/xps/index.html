What is XPS (X-ray photoelectron spectroscopy)?

An analysis method that determines the elements in the top few nanometers of a surface and their chemical bonding states from the energies of photoelectrons emitted under X-ray irradiation.

Japanese name: XPS(X線光電子分光法)

Answer: B. XPS (X-ray photoelectron spectroscopy)

XPS irradiates the surface with X-rays and measures the kinetic energy of emitted photoelectrons, from which binding energies reveal elements and chemical states. Silicon shifts from about 99 eV as the element to about 103 eV in SiO2, indicating oxidation state. Photoelectrons escape only from the top few nanometers, making XPS suited to thin films, interfaces and surface treatments. AFM measures shape and the four-point probe measures resistance.
