What is STT-MRAM?

MRAM that writes by flipping the magnetization of the MTJ's free layer using the spin-transfer torque of a spin-polarized current. It is in volume production as embedded non-volatile memory.

Field: Emerging memories

STT-MRAM in the explanations

  • In STT-MRAM, current flows through the MTJ, and electrons spin-polarized by the reference layer transfer angular momentum to the free layer (spin-transfer torque), flipping its magnetization; the current direction determines 0 or 1.
