What is SC-1 (APM)?

A mixture of ammonia, hydrogen peroxide and water used first in RCA cleaning. It removes particles and organics while lightly etching the surface.

Field: CMP and cleaning

SC-1 (APM) in the explanations

  • SC-1 (NH4OH:H2O2:H2O, e.g., 1:1:5 at about 70 °C) oxidizes the surface with peroxide while ammonia slowly dissolves that oxide at roughly 0.1–1 nm per minute, undercutting and lifting particles away.
