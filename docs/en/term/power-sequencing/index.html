What is Power sequencing?

The specified order for applying and removing multiple supply voltages to a chip; violating it can cause malfunction or latch-up.

Japanese name: パワーシーケンス

In chips using multiple supply voltages, why must the power-up order (power sequencing) be observed?

  1. AA wrong order can drive signals into unpowered circuits or push large currents through protection devices, causing malfunction, latch-up or damage
  2. BFollowing the order makes the chip run twice as fast
  3. CThe order is cosmetic and does not affect operation
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. A wrong order can drive signals into unpowered circuits or push large currents through protection devices, causing malfunction, latch-up or damage

For example, if the I/O supply comes up before the core supply, current can flow from the I/O side into the unpowered core, or circuits in an undefined state may drive signals externally. Large currents through protection diodes can trigger latch-up or damage. So chip datasheets specify the order for powering up and down, and PMICs or similar control the supplies accordingly.
