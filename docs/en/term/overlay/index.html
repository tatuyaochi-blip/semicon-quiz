What is Overlay?

How accurately a new pattern is placed relative to the patterns made in earlier steps. Misalignment causes defects such as missed contacts, so it is controlled at the nanometer level.

Field: Lithography

Overlay in the explanations

  • An IC stacks dozens of patterned layers, so each must align precisely to the previous ones; overlay accuracy measures how small that misregistration is.

    What is overlay accuracy in lithography?

  • To measure overlay, dedicated targets combining patterns from the previous and current layers are placed in scribe lines and similar areas.

    What is placed on the wafer to measure overlay error?

  • Self-aligned contacts remove the margin otherwise reserved for overlay error, tightening gate-to-contact spacing, and lead to techniques such as contact-over-active-gate (COAG) that further shrink cells.
