What is Short-circuit current?

Current that flows from supply to ground in a CMOS gate during the brief time while the input switches and both the pMOS and nMOS are on. Also called crowbar current, it adds to power consumption.

Japanese name: 貫通電流

Which is the correct definition of the fill factor (FF) of a solar cell?

  1. AShort-circuit current ÷ open-circuit voltage
  2. BOutput power ÷ incident light energy
  3. CMaximum power ÷ (open-circuit voltage × short-circuit current)
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. Maximum power ÷ (open-circuit voltage × short-circuit current)

The fill factor is the power at the maximum power point divided by the product of open-circuit voltage and short-circuit current, indicating how close the I-V curve is to a rectangle. Crystalline silicon cells typically reach about 0.75–0.85. It drops with high series resistance, low shunt resistance (leakage paths) or heavy recombination. Efficiency is η = Voc × Isc × FF ÷ incident light power, so FF is one of the three factors that determine it.
