What is Photodiode?

A diode that generates charge (current) when it receives light. Used as the light-receiving element in image sensor pixels.

Field: Image sensors and optical devices

Photodiode in the explanations

  • Reverse-biasing a photodiode widens the depletion region, where light-generated electrons and holes are quickly separated by the field, producing a photocurrent proportional to light intensity.

  • An APD is a photodiode biased near breakdown so that light-generated carriers are multiplied tens to hundreds of times by avalanche multiplication.
