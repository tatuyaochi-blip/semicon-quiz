What is High-level synthesis (HLS)?

Automatically generating RTL from an algorithm written in C, C++ or similar. It is used in developing FPGAs and accelerators.

Field: IC design flow and EDA

High-level synthesis (HLS) in the explanations

  • High-level synthesis automatically allocates functional units and registers and schedules which clock cycle each operation occurs in, generating RTL from behavioral descriptions in C/C++ and similar languages.
