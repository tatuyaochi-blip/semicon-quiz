What is Selectivity?

In etching, the ratio of the etch rate of the target film to that of the mask or underlying film. Higher selectivity means less damage to the underlayer.

Field: Etching

Selectivity in the explanations

  • Selectivity is the ratio of the etch rate of the film being removed to that of films that should not be removed (the mask or underlayer).
