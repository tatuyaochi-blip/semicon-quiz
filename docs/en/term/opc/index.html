What is OPC (optical proximity correction)?

A technique that corrects mask patterns in advance, anticipating distortion of the printed pattern caused by diffraction of light and similar effects.

Japanese name: OPC(光学近接効果補正)

Answer: C. Correcting the mask pattern in advance to reduce shape distortion after transfer

Near the resolution limit, diffraction and interference shorten line ends, round corners and make linewidth depend on surrounding pattern density. OPC uses simulation to anticipate these distortions, adding assist features such as hammerheads and serifs and finely adjusting dimensions on the mask. Computing OPC for advanced masks requires large-scale computing resources.
