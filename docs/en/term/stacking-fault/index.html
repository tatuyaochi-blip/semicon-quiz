What is Stacking fault?

A planar defect in which the stacking order of atomic planes is disrupted. It is a concern in epitaxial layers, thermal oxidation (OSF) and SiC crystals.

Japanese name: 積層欠陥

Answer: C. Stacking fault

A stacking fault is a local disruption of the stacking sequence of atomic planes (ABCABC… on silicon {111} planes). In epitaxy it nucleates at surface contamination, particles or scratches and appears at the surface as triangular or square features that optical inspection can count. Because stacking faults cause device leakage, pre-growth cleaning and hydrogen-bake surface preparation are critical.
