What is Seed layer?

A thin copper film deposited by sputtering or similar before copper electroplating. It provides the conductive path for the plating current.

Field: Interconnect processes

Seed layer in the explanations

  • In the damascene process, a barrier (e.g., Ta/TaN) and a Cu seed layer a few to tens of nm thick are sputtered into the trenches, and copper is then electroplated to fill them.
