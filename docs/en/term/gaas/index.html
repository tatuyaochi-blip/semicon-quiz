What is GaAs (gallium arsenide)?

A III-V compound semiconductor of gallium and arsenic with a direct band gap of about 1.42 eV. Its high electron mobility makes it useful for high-frequency transistors and infrared LEDs and lasers.

Field: Compound semiconductors

GaAs (gallium arsenide) in the explanations

  • GaAs is a direct-gap semiconductor: its conduction-band minimum and valence-band maximum coincide in momentum space, so electrons and holes recombine radiatively without phonons and emit light efficiently.
