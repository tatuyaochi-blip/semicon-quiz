What is Fault coverage?

The fraction of modeled faults that a set of test patterns can detect. The higher it is, the less likely defective chips are to escape.

Japanese name: 故障検出率(フォールトカバレッジ)

  2. BThe fraction of shipped products that are defective
  3. CThe fraction of good chips on a wafer
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. The fraction of modeled faults that the test can detect

Fault coverage is the fraction of modeled faults that the test patterns detect. The escaping defect level is estimated as DL = 1 − Y^(1−T) for yield Y and coverage T; even with Y = 0.5 and T = 99%, DL is about 0.7% (about 7,000 DPPM). Automotive products therefore demand coverage well above 99%. It is distinct from the shipped defect rate or yield itself.
