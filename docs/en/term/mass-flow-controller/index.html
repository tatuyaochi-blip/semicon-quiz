What is Mass flow controller (MFC)?

A device in deposition and etch tools that precisely measures the mass flow of a gas and holds it at a set value.

Field: Process materials (gases and chemicals)

Mass flow controller (MFC) in the explanations

  • A mass flow controller measures the mass flow of a gas with a sensor and adjusts an internal valve to hold the setpoint.
