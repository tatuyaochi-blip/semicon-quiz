What is SOI wafer?

A wafer with a thin silicon layer on a buried oxide (BOX) layer over a silicon substrate. It is made, for example, by bonding a hydrogen-implanted wafer and splitting it. Its low parasitic capacitance suits FD-SOI devices and MEMS.

Japanese name: SOIウエハ

Which correctly describes a typical method of manufacturing SOI wafers?

  1. AThe buried oxide is made just by thermally oxidizing silicon
  2. BThe wafer is melted and recrystallized
  3. CA wafer implanted with hydrogen ions is bonded to another wafer and split at the implant depth, leaving a thin silicon layer
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. A wafer implanted with hydrogen ions is bonded to another wafer and split at the implant depth, leaving a thin silicon layer

In the Smart Cut process, a wafer is thermally oxidized and implanted with hydrogen ions to a depth of a few hundred nm, then bonded to a handle wafer. Annealing at a few hundred °C causes the hydrogen to form microcavities that link up, splitting the wafer at that depth and leaving a thin single-crystal silicon layer on oxide—an SOI wafer. The surface is finished by CMP to nanometer-level thickness control, and the donor wafer's remainder can be repolished and reused.
