What is Power MOSFET?

A MOSFET built to handle large currents and voltages. It switches quickly and is widely used in power supply circuits and the like.

Japanese name: パワーMOSFET

Try it: questions about Power MOSFET

What is the parasitic diode that forms structurally inside a power MOSFET called?

  1. ALight-emitting diode
  2. BBody diode (built-in diode)
  3. CPhotodiode
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. Body diode (built-in diode)

In a power MOSFET, the p-type body tied to the source forms a PN junction with the n-type drift region, creating a parasitic diode that conducts from source to drain—the body diode. It can serve as the freewheeling path in inverters, but in silicon MOSFETs its slow reverse recovery causes losses, so fast applications add external diodes or rely on synchronous rectification.

What is the conduction loss of a power MOSFET with an on-resistance of 10 mΩ carrying 10 A?

  1. A0.1 W
  2. B1 W
  3. C100 W
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. 1 W

Conduction loss is P = I²R: (10 A)² × 0.01 Ω = 1 W. Because it scales with current squared, doubling the current quadruples the loss, so at high currents small differences in on-resistance matter a lot. On-resistance also rises with temperature (about 1.5× at 100 °C), so real heating exceeds room-temperature estimates. Devices are often paralleled to reduce effective resistance.
