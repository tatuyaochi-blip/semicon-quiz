What is CMP?

Chemical mechanical polishing. Planarizes the wafer surface by combining the chemical action of a slurry with mechanical polishing by a pad.

Japanese name: CMP

In the mirror polishing (final CMP polishing) of silicon wafers, what is commonly used as the abrasive in the slurry?

  1. AIron powder
  2. BColloidal silica (fine silica particles)
  3. CLarge diamond grains
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. Colloidal silica (fine silica particles)

Final wafer polishing uses colloidal silica—silica particles tens of nm across dispersed in an alkaline solution. The alkali chemically converts the silicon surface into a softer layer that the particles mechanically remove, and this combined chemical-mechanical action yields a damage-free mirror finish with roughness around 0.1 nm. The soft particles avoid deep scratches and metal contamination.

Which correctly describes CMP (chemical mechanical polishing)?

  1. AIt planarizes the surface by combining the chemical action of a slurry with mechanical polishing by a pad
  2. BIt planarizes the surface by etching it with ions from a plasma
  3. CIt planarizes the surface by melting it at high temperature
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. It planarizes the surface by combining the chemical action of a slurry with mechanical polishing by a pad

CMP presses a rotating wafer against a polishing pad while feeding slurry containing abrasive particles and chemicals. Chemistry converts the surface into a softer layer that particles and pad remove mechanically, achieving rates and flatness neither chemical nor mechanical action could alone. Developed at IBM in the 1980s, it removes topography across the entire wafer to within tens of nm and is indispensable for multilevel and copper interconnects.
