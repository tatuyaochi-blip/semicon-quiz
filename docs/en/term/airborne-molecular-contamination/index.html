What is Airborne molecular contamination (AMC)?

Gaseous contaminants in the air such as acids, alkalis and organics. Particle filters cannot remove them, so chemical filters are used.

Japanese name: 分子状汚染(AMC)

Answer: B. Chemical filter (using activated carbon or ion exchangers)

ULPA and HEPA filters capture particles but let gas molecules through. Chemical filters use activated carbon, media impregnated with reagents that react with acids or bases, or ion-exchange resins to adsorb or react away molecular contaminants. Types are chosen for the target species, and because their capacity is finite they are replaced periodically. They are used at outside-air intakes and in critical tools such as scanners.
