What is Quantization-aware training?

Training that simulates quantization rounding so weights adapt to its error, preserving accuracy even at low bit widths.

Field: AI Hardware & Software Stack

Quantization-aware training in the explanations

  • Quantization-aware training simulates quantization rounding in the forward pass during training and adjusts weights with that error included, preserving accuracy even at precisions as low as 4 bits.
