What is Exposed pad?

A large metal area on the underside of packages such as QFN, connected to the die pad. Soldered to the board, it is the main path for removing heat and is usually tied to inner-layer planes with thermal vias.

Japanese name: 露出パッド(エクスポーズドパッド)

Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. Provide a land on the board, solder the pad, connect it to the specified potential (often GND) and conduct heat away through thermal vias

The exposed pad is the underside of the die pad, on which the chip is mounted, exposed outside the package; it is the main path for heat into the board. The datasheet θJA value is typically measured with this pad soldered to the board and connected to inner-layer planes through thermal vias, and leaving it unsoldered greatly degrades heat dissipation. The recommended land pattern may also show the stencil aperture divided into segments to adjust the solder volume and prevent voids and component lifting. Follow the datasheet for the potential to connect it to.
