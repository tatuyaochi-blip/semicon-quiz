What is Skin effect?

The concentration of high-frequency current near the surface of a conductor, which raises its effective resistance. The higher the frequency, the thinner the surface layer carrying the current.

Japanese name: 表皮効果

Answer: A. Trace loss grows with frequency, so faster signals are attenuated more over the same distance

In traces, conductor loss from the skin effect and dielectric loss from the board material both grow with frequency. Doubling the speed weakens the signal much more over the same length, beyond what the receiver's equalizer can recover. So designers use low-loss board materials or insert retimers that receive the signal and retransmit a clean copy.
