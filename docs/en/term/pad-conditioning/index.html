What is Pad conditioning?

Abrading the surface of a CMP polishing pad with a diamond tool to restore its texture and slurry-holding ability, stabilizing the removal rate. Also called dressing.

Japanese name: パッドコンディショニング

Answer: C. Large agglomerated particles in the slurry, or foreign matter left on the pad

When slurry particles agglomerate into micron-sized clusters through drying or changes in temperature or pH, or when hard debris or fragments of conditioner diamonds lodge in the pad, they scratch the wafer surface. Scratches cause shorts or opens between copper lines, and low-k films are especially vulnerable. Prevention includes slurry filtration, circulation and temperature control, pad conditioning and optimized polish pressure.
