What is Interconnect via?

A conductor that electrically connects the wiring layers above and below it in a multilevel interconnect. It is made by filling a hole in the insulator with metal.

Japanese name: ビア

What is the method called that fills wiring trenches and vias with copper at the same time and finishes them with a single CMP step?

  1. ASingle damascene process
  2. BEtch-back process
  3. CDual damascene process
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. Dual damascene process

Dual damascene patterns both via holes and wiring trenches into the dielectric, then fills them together with barrier, seed and electroplated copper and finishes with a single CMP. Compared with single damascene, which builds vias and lines separately, it saves one round each of deposition, plating and CMP, and avoids a barrier interface between via and line, benefiting resistance and reliability. Variants differ in whether vias or trenches are opened first.

Which technique forms vias self-aligned to wiring trenches to tolerate misalignment between upper and lower wiring layers?

  1. ATSV
  2. BSelf-aligned via
  3. CLDD
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. Self-aligned via

At wiring pitches in the 20 nm range, a via misplaced by just a few nm can short to the adjacent line or narrow the spacing enough to cause dielectric breakdown. Self-aligned vias retain the trench-patterning hard mask or use materially distinct layers atop the lines so the via etch is confined within the target line's width, preventing connection to neighbors despite misalignment.
