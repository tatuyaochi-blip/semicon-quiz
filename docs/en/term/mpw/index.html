What is MPW (multi-project wafer)?

Prototyping several customers' designs together on one wafer (one mask set). Sharing the expensive mask cost makes it popular with universities and startups.

Japanese name: MPW(マルチプロジェクトウエハ)

What is the scheme called in which a foundry puts designs from several customers on one wafer (one mask set) for prototyping?

  1. AMPW (multi-project wafer)
  2. BOSAT
  3. CIDM
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. MPW (multi-project wafer)

An MPW (multi-project wafer) combines several designs on one mask set and wafer run, sharing mask and fabrication costs among participants; it is also called a shuttle, like a shared bus. Since advanced-node mask sets can cost tens of millions of dollars, universities and startups use MPWs for low-cost prototyping. Each participant gets only a few chips, so it serves prototyping and evaluation, not volume production. OSAT and IDM are business models.

When a chipmaker announces the end of life (EOL) of a product, what is it called when equipment makers using it buy, one last time, the quantity they will need for future maintenance and production?

  1. AMPW
  2. BJust-in-time
  3. CLast-time buy
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. Last-time buy

When a chipmaker discontinues an old product, it accepts final orders for a set period. Makers of equipment needing ten or more years of supply and maintenance, such as automotive, industrial and medical equipment, use this chance to buy all the quantity they will need (a last-time buy) and store it long-term. Afterward they redesign around substitutes. An MPW is a prototyping service and just-in-time is a low-inventory philosophy.
