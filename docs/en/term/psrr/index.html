What is PSRR (power supply rejection ratio)?

A measure of how little supply-voltage variation reaches a circuit's output. The larger it is, the more immune the circuit is to supply noise.

Japanese name: PSRR(電源電圧変動除去比)

Answer: B. PSRR (power supply rejection ratio)

PSRR expresses how much supply-voltage variation appears at the output, in dB; higher means better immunity to supply noise. Op-amps typically offer 80–100 dB at low frequencies, but it usually drops sharply as frequency rises. Switching-regulator ripple (hundreds of kHz and up) tends to fall where PSRR is poor, so supplies are cleaned with LC filters, LDOs and bypass capacitors before feeding analog circuits.
