What is INL (integral nonlinearity)?

The maximum deviation of an ADC's or DAC's actual transfer characteristic from the ideal straight line. It corresponds to the accumulated DNL.

Japanese name: INL(積分非直線性)

  1. AThe output deviation when the input is 0

Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. The maximum deviation of the actual transfer characteristic from an ideal straight line

INL is the maximum distance of the actual transfer curve from an ideal straight line, defined either through the endpoints or as a best-fit line. It corresponds to the accumulation of DNL. INL causes harmonic distortion of large signals, which matters in audio and measurement, and unlike offset and gain errors it is hard to remove by calibration.
