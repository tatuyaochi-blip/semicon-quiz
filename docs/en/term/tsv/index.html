What is TSV (through-silicon via)?

An electrode that passes through the silicon substrate. It connects stacked chips vertically over a short distance.

Field: Advanced packaging (3D and chiplets)

TSV (through-silicon via) in the explanations

  • A TSV (Through-Silicon Via) is an electrode made by drilling a hole in the silicon substrate and filling it with conductor, electrically connecting the front and back of the substrate.
