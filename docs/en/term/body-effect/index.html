What is Body effect?

An increase in threshold voltage when reverse bias is applied between source and substrate (body), because the depletion charge grows. Also called the substrate bias effect; it is sometimes used to tune the threshold.

Field: MOSFET

Body effect in the explanations

  • Reverse-biasing the source-to-body junction widens the depletion region under the gate and increases its charge, so a larger gate voltage is needed to compensate—the threshold voltage rises (the body effect).
