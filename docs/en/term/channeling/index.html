What is Channeling?

A phenomenon in which ions pass through gaps between rows of crystal atoms and penetrate deeper than expected. It is prevented by tilting the wafer during implantation and similar measures.

Japanese name: チャネリング

Which is a common way to suppress channeling (ions traveling too deep through gaps in the crystal) in ion implantation?

  1. AUsing the maximum acceleration energy
  2. BCleaning the wafer after implantation
  3. CTilting the wafer a few degrees from the crystal axis during implantation
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. Tilting the wafer a few degrees from the crystal axis during implantation

Ions entering along crystal rows travel through open channels between atomic columns with few collisions and penetrate deeper than intended—channeling. It is suppressed by tilting the wafer about 7° from the crystal axis with a twist offset, implanting through a thin screen oxide that randomizes directions, or pre-amorphizing the surface. This trims the deep tail of the profile, giving shallower junctions.
