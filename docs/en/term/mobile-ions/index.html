What is Mobile ions?

Ions such as sodium and potassium that can move within the gate oxide. They drift with field and temperature and shift the threshold voltage. Because they can come from sources such as human sweat, they are tightly controlled.

Japanese name: 可動イオン

Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. The ions move under electric fields and temperature, shifting the threshold voltage

Alkali ions such as sodium move readily through oxide, and under electric fields or heat (around 200 °C) they drift toward the interface as positive charge, shifting threshold voltage. They were the main cause of 1960s MOS instability and can come from human sweat or tools. Cleanroom contamination control, chlorine oxidation and gettering by phosphosilicate glass (PSG) brought them under control and made MOS technology practical.
