What is FMEA (failure mode and effects analysis)?

A method that lists the possible failure modes of a product or process, evaluates their effects and severity, and plans countermeasures in advance.

Japanese name: FMEA(故障モード影響解析)

Try it: questions about FMEA (failure mode and effects analysis)

Which technique evaluates whether a measuring instrument's repeatability and reproducibility are small enough compared with the process variation to be controlled?

  1. APareto analysis
  2. BGage R&R (measurement system analysis)
  3. CFMEA
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. Gage R&R (measurement system analysis)

Measured variation includes not only process variation but also measurement-system variation. Gauge R&R evaluates repeatability (variation when the same sample is measured repeatedly) and reproducibility (variation across operators or tools). As a guideline, measurement variation below 10% of the tolerance is good, while above 30% the gauge is unfit for process control. Pareto analysis and FMEA serve other purposes.

Which type of control automatically adjusts equipment processing conditions for the next lot or wafer based on, for example, measurement results from earlier steps?

  1. AFMEA
  2. BPareto analysis
  3. CAPC (run-to-run control, etc.)
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. APC (run-to-run control, etc.)

APC (advanced process control) uses metrology data to adjust tool settings automatically and reduce process variation. In run-to-run control, for example, if the incoming film measured thick, the next CMP time is extended. Whereas SPC monitors and stops on abnormalities, APC actively corrects toward target. FMEA is proactive risk assessment and Pareto analysis finds priorities.
