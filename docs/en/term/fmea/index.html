What is FMEA (failure mode and effects analysis)?

A method that lists the possible failure modes of a product or process, evaluates their effects and severity, and plans countermeasures in advance.

Field: Yield and quality control

FMEA (failure mode and effects analysis) in the explanations

  • FMEA lists possible failure modes for each part or process step, evaluates the severity of their effects, frequency of occurrence and ease of detection, and decides which items to address first.
