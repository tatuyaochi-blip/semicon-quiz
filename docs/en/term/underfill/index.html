What is Underfill?

Resin filled into the gap between the chip and the substrate in flip-chip assembly. It spreads out stress from thermal expansion mismatch and protects the bump joints.

Japanese name: アンダーフィル

Underfill appears in 4 practice questions across 3 topics, including Advanced packaging (3D and chiplets), Packaging and assembly, Reliability.

In flip-chip mounting, what is the main purpose of filling the gap between chip and substrate with underfill resin?

  1. ATo keep stress from thermal expansion mismatch between chip and substrate from concentrating on the bumps, improving connection reliability
  2. BTo make the chip emit light
  3. CTo block heat dissipation from the chip
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. To keep stress from thermal expansion mismatch between chip and substrate from concentrating on the bumps, improving connection reliability

Silicon expands at about 3 ppm/K while organic substrates expand at about 15–17 ppm/K, so each temperature cycle repeatedly strains the small bumps, risking fatigue cracks. Filling the chip–substrate gap with underfill bonds them together, spreading stress across all bumps and greatly extending joint life. Underfill is usually dispensed at the edge and drawn in by capillary action.
