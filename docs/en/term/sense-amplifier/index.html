What is Sense amplifier?

A circuit that amplifies the tiny voltage difference read from a memory cell and decides whether it is 0 or 1.

Field: DRAM

Sense amplifier in the explanations

  • The sense amplifier therefore amplifies a difference of tens to about a hundred mV to decide 0 or 1 and simultaneously writes the amplified level back into the cell.
