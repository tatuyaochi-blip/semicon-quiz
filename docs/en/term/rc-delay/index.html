What is RC delay?

Signal delay determined by the product of wire resistance (R) and capacitance (C). It grows as scaling makes wires thinner, limiting performance.

Japanese name: RC遅延

RC delay appears in 4 practice questions across 1 topic, including Interconnect processes.

Try it: questions about RC delay

If a wire's length is doubled without changing its cross-section or spacing, approximately how many times larger does its RC delay become?

  1. A2 times
  2. B4 times
  3. C8 times
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. 4 times

Both wire resistance R and capacitance C scale with length, so RC delay scales with length squared—doubling length roughly quadruples delay. Inserting buffers (repeaters) to segment long wires makes delay nearly linear in length. Advanced chips contain hundreds of thousands to millions of repeaters, whose power is not negligible; routing long nets on thick upper-level wires serves the same goal.
