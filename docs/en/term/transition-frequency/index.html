What is Transition frequency?

The frequency at which the common-emitter current gain falls to 1, written fT. It is a key indicator of a transistor's high-frequency performance.

Field: Bipolar transistors

Transition frequency in the explanations

  • The frequency at which common-emitter current gain drops to 1 is the transition frequency fT, roughly fT ≈ gm/(2π(Cπ + Cμ)).
