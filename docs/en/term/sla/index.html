What is SLA (service level agreement)?

An agreement between the provider and the user of an IT service that sets the quality levels, such as availability, response time, time to recover from failures and support hours, together with how they are measured and what happens if they are not met.

Japanese name: SLA(サービスレベル合意)

The SLA for a service specifies a monthly availability of at least 99.95%. A month is taken as 30 days, and planned downtime is included in the calculation. This month the service has already been down for a total of 15 minutes due to failures. How much more downtime is allowed for the rest of this month while still meeting the SLA?

  1. A6.6 minutes
  2. B21.6 minutes
  3. C36.6 minutes
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. 6.6 minutes

A month is 30 × 24 × 60 = 43,200 minutes, and at 99.95% availability the allowed downtime is 43,200 × 0.0005 = 21.6 minutes. Since 15 minutes have already been used, 21.6 − 15 = 6.6 minutes remain. 21.6 minutes forgets to subtract the downtime that has already occurred, and 36.6 minutes adds it instead of subtracting it. An SLA should specify, as agreed between the service provider and the user, matters such as whether planned downtime counts toward availability and the measurement period.

To achieve the service levels promised to users in an SLA, an IT service provider agrees with the network operations department and other departments within the same organization on the content and level of the work each is responsible for. Which of the following is this agreement?

  1. ASLA (service level agreement)
  2. BA contract with an external supplier
  3. COLA (operational level agreement)
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. OLA (operational level agreement)

IT services are delivered through the cooperation of several departments within the organization, such as the service desk, server operations and network operations, and of outside companies. To keep the SLA with users, OLAs (operational level agreements) are made with the internal departments that support it and contracts with external suppliers, fixing the level of the work each one is responsible for. The targets of the OLAs and contracts are set so that the SLA targets can be achieved.
