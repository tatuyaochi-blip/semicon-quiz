What is WLP (wafer-level package)?

A package in which redistribution wiring and external terminals are formed while the chips are still in wafer form, before dicing. The result is a small package about the size of the chip.

Field: Packaging and assembly

WLP (wafer-level package) in the explanations

  • In WLP, redistribution layers and external terminals such as solder balls are formed all at once on the wafer before dicing, and the wafer is finally cut into individual chips.
