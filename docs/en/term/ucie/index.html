What is UCIe?

Short for Universal Chiplet Interconnect Express: an industry-standard die-to-die interface intended to let chiplets from different companies connect to each other.

Japanese name: UCIe

Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. UCIe

UCIe (Universal Chiplet Interconnect Express) is a standard for connecting chiplets within a package, announced in 2022 by a group of major semiconductor companies. It specifies everything from physical-layer dimensions and signaling to upper-layer protocols, aiming to make it easier to combine chiplets from different companies and processes. HDMI connects video equipment and Bluetooth is a short-range wireless standard.
