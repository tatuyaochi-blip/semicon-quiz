What is Decoder?

A circuit that activates exactly one of 2ⁿ outputs according to an n-bit input. It is used to select the row addressed in a memory. The reverse function is an encoder.

Japanese name: デコーダ

Answer: C. Circuits specialized for a task use far less energy than doing it on the CPU, extending battery life

A general-purpose CPU, designed to handle anything, spends much energy beyond the computation itself on fetching and decoding instructions and reading and writing registers. A dedicated circuit such as a video decoder hard-wires a fixed processing flow, doing the same work with a fraction, even a few percent, of the energy. SoCs carry many dedicated blocks for video, images, audio, AI and more.
