What is Baud rate?

The number of symbols sent per second in serial communication; for a UART it equals bits per second and must match on both sides.

Field: Embedded Systems & Firmware

  • The receiver aligns its timing at the start of the start bit and then counts at its own baud rate, sampling each bit at its center.
