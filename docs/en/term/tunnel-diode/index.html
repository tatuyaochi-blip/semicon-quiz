What is Tunnel diode (Esaki diode)?

A diode with very heavily doped p and n sides that shows negative resistance in forward bias due to tunneling. It was discovered by Leo Esaki in 1957.

Japanese name: トンネルダイオード(エサキダイオード)

Answer: C. Tunnel diode (Esaki diode)

A tunnel diode is a PN junction doped so heavily on both sides that it is degenerate; its depletion region is so thin that tunneling current flows at low forward voltage. As voltage rises, the electron energies on the n side and the empty states on the p side move out of alignment, tunneling current falls, and a negative-resistance region appears. Leo Esaki discovered it in 1957 and received the 1973 Nobel Prize in Physics; it was the first electronic device to demonstrate tunneling.
