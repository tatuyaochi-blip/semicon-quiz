What is Coriolis force?

An apparent force acting perpendicular to the motion of an object moving in a rotating frame. Vibratory MEMS gyroscopes detect angular rate from the displacement it causes.

Japanese name: コリオリ力

Which force does a vibratory MEMS gyroscope use to detect angular velocity?

  1. ACoriolis force
  2. BOnly the Coulomb force
  3. CBuoyancy
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. Coriolis force

When a vibrating mass rotates, a Coriolis force proportional to its velocity and the angular rate acts perpendicular to both the vibration and the rotation axis. MEMS gyroscopes resonate a proof mass at several to tens of kHz and detect the tiny orthogonal displacement induced by rotation—typically capacitively—to measure angular rate. Uses include camera stabilization, phone orientation, vehicle stability control and drone flight control.
