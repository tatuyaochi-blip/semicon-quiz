What is Ion trap?

A device that holds ions in a vacuum with electric fields or similar means, or the quantum computing approach that uses one. Electronic energy levels of the ions serve as qubits and are controlled with lasers or microwaves. All the qubits are identical, with long coherence times and high fidelities, but gates are slower than in superconducting platforms.

Japanese name: イオントラップ

  3. CIt uses electronic energy levels of ions held by electric fields in a vacuum as qubits, and controls them with lasers or microwaves
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. It uses electronic energy levels of ions held by electric fields in a vacuum as qubits, and controls them with lasers or microwaves

In a trapped-ion system, ions such as ytterbium or calcium are levitated and lined up in a vacuum, for example by radio-frequency electric fields produced by electrodes, and electronic energy levels of the ions serve as qubits. All ions of the same species have identical properties, so there is no fabrication variation, and long coherence times and high gate fidelities are strengths of this approach. The ions share vibrational motion through the Coulomb force, so two-qubit gates can be performed even between ions that are not neighbors. On the other hand, gates are slower than in superconducting systems, and the number of ions that can be lined up in one trap is limited, so handling many ions is a challenge.

  1. AIn superconducting platforms each qubit is an atom, so there is no fabrication variation at all
  2. BSuperconducting platforms have fast gates but connect qubits mainly to their neighbors; trapped-ion platforms have slower gates but can easily connect any ions in the same trap directly
  3. CTrapped-ion platforms require a dilution refrigerator at about 10 mK, while superconducting platforms operate at room temperature
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. Superconducting platforms have fast gates but connect qubits mainly to their neighbors; trapped-ion platforms have slower gates but can easily connect any ions in the same trap directly

Two-qubit gates in superconducting platforms are fast, about tens to hundreds of ns, but the qubits are coupled through circuits on the chip, so they connect mainly to neighboring qubits. Gates in trapped-ion platforms are generally about a few µs to hundreds of µs, often two to three orders of magnitude slower than superconducting gates, but ions in the same trap can perform gates in any combination through their shared vibrational motion. Superconducting qubits are fabricated circuits, so their frequencies vary with tiny differences in the junctions, whereas ions of the same species are all identical. Superconducting platforms need a dilution refrigerator, and trapped-ion platforms need an ultra-high vacuum and lasers.
