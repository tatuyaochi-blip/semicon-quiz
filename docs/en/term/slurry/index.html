What is Slurry?

The liquid used in CMP, with fine abrasive particles dispersed in chemicals. It flattens surfaces by combining chemical action with mechanical polishing.

Field: CMP and cleaning

Slurry in the explanations

  • Slurry consists of fine abrasive particles such as silica (SiO2), ceria (CeO2) or alumina dispersed in a solution containing oxidizers, pH adjusters and other chemicals.
