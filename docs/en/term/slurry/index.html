What is Slurry?

The liquid used in CMP, with fine abrasive particles dispersed in chemicals. It flattens surfaces by combining chemical action with mechanical polishing.

Japanese name: スラリー

In the mirror polishing (final CMP polishing) of silicon wafers, what is commonly used as the abrasive in the slurry?

  1. AIron powder
  2. BColloidal silica (fine silica particles)
  3. CLarge diamond grains
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. Colloidal silica (fine silica particles)

Final wafer polishing uses colloidal silica—silica particles tens of nm across dispersed in an alkaline solution. The alkali chemically converts the silicon surface into a softer layer that the particles mechanically remove, and this combined chemical-mechanical action yields a damage-free mirror finish with roughness around 0.1 nm. The soft particles avoid deep scratches and metal contamination.

Which correctly describes the slurry used in CMP?

  1. AA polishing liquid with abrasive particles such as silica or ceria dispersed in a chemical solution
  2. BA light-sensitive resin applied to the wafer
  3. CA gas for generating plasma
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. A polishing liquid with abrasive particles such as silica or ceria dispersed in a chemical solution

Slurry disperses abrasive particles tens to over a hundred nm in size—silica (SiO2), ceria (CeO2), alumina—in chemistry containing oxidizers, pH adjusters, corrosion inhibitors and dispersants. Particles and chemistry are chosen per film: silica or ceria for oxide, oxidizer-containing silica slurries for tungsten and copper. Slurry is a major CMP consumable cost, and Japanese companies are strong suppliers.
