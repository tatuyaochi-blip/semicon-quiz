What is Scanner?

An exposure tool that moves the mask and wafer in sync under a slit of light. It is the mainstream production lithography tool and generally projects the mask pattern at 1/4 size.

Field: Lithography

Scanner in the explanations

  • A scanner illuminates only a slit a few mm wide and moves the mask and wafer synchronously in opposite directions at a 4:1 speed ratio to expose each 26 × 33 mm field.
