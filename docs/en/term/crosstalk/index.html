What is Crosstalk?

Noise that appears on one wire when the signal on a neighboring wire changes, mainly through capacitive coupling. Wider spacing and shield wires reduce it.

Japanese name: クロストーク

Which correctly describes the acquisition time of a sample-and-hold circuit?

  1. AThe time, in track mode, for the hold capacitor voltage to settle to the input within the required accuracy
  2. BHow fast the held voltage gradually decreases
  3. CThe time until the conversion result is output digitally
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. The time, in track mode, for the hold capacitor voltage to settle to the input within the required accuracy

Acquisition time is the time needed in track mode for the hold capacitor's voltage to approach the input within a specified error. It is RC settling, so reaching 1/2 LSB takes (N+1)·ln2 time constants—about nine for 12 bits. If it is too short, the previous sample's influence remains, causing crosstalk when multiplexing channels. Higher source resistance requires longer acquisition time.

What is the main purpose of deep trench isolation (DTI), in which deep trenches filled with insulators are formed between fine pixels?

  1. ATo prevent light or charge from leaking into neighboring pixels (crosstalk)
  2. BTo make pixels larger
  3. CTo eliminate the need for color filters
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. To prevent light or charge from leaking into neighboring pixels (crosstalk)

As pixels shrink, oblique light and charge generated deep in the silicon spill into neighbors, mixing colors and reducing resolution. Deep trench isolation (DTI) etches trenches several µm deep between pixels and fills them with oxide or metal to block light and charge, suppressing this crosstalk. Trenches are usually etched from the back side, and passivating the trench walls to limit dark current is essential.
