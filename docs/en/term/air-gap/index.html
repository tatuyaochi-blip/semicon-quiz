What is Air gap?

Leaving part of the insulator between wires as empty space. Since air has a relative permittivity of about 1, it greatly reduces capacitance between wires.

Japanese name: エアギャップ

Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. To reduce capacitance between wires (air has a relative permittivity of about 1)

Air (vacuum) has a relative permittivity of about 1, lower than any dielectric, so air gaps between wires cut inter-wire capacitance by about 20–30%. They are formed by removing dielectric between finished lines and pinching off the top, or by deliberately leaving voids during deposition. Concerns about mechanical strength, heat dissipation and vias from above landing in gaps limit their use to dense levels where capacitance reduction matters most.
