What is Air gap?

Leaving part of the insulator between wires as empty space. Since air has a relative permittivity of about 1, it greatly reduces capacitance between wires.

Field: Interconnect processes

Air gap in the explanations

  • Because solids and air differ greatly in acoustic impedance, an air gap at a delamination reflects nearly all the sound, so delamination between mold compound and die or lead frame, cracks and voids are found non-destructively.
