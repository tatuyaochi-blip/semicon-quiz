What is Darlington connection?

A connection that feeds the emitter current of one transistor into the base of a second. The overall current gain is roughly the product of the two, so a small base current can control a large current.

Japanese name: ダーリントン接続

Answer: C. The overall current gain is roughly the product of the individual current gains

A Darlington pair feeds the first transistor's emitter current into the second's base, giving an overall current gain of about hFE1 × hFE2 (e.g., 100 × 100 = 10,000). It lets tiny currents drive motors or relays directly, so single-chip Darlington transistors and arrays (such as the ULN2003) are common. The costs are a VBE of about 1.4 V, a higher saturation voltage and slower switching.
