What is HDP-CVD (high-density plasma CVD)?

CVD using a high-density plasma that deposits while ion sputtering trims film at the trench openings. It has been used to fill narrow trenches with insulator without voids.

Japanese name: HDP-CVD(高密度プラズマCVD)

Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. Because ion sputtering during deposition trims film at the openings, allowing filling with few voids

Filling a narrow trench by ordinary CVD can let film build up at the opening corners and pinch off first, trapping a void. HDP-CVD deposits from a dense plasma (above 10¹¹ cm⁻³) while ions such as argon, drawn in by substrate bias, sputter the corners at an angle, so the trench fills from the bottom up. It was used for STI and inter-metal gap fill from about the 0.25 µm to 65 nm generations; narrower trenches use flowable CVD and similar methods.
