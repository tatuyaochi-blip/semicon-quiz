What is Piezoresistive effect?

The change in a material's electrical resistivity under mechanical stress. It is especially large in silicon and is used in semiconductor pressure sensors and similar devices.

Field: MEMS and sensors

Piezoresistive effect in the explanations

  • Stress alters silicon's band structure, changing its resistivity tens of times more than a metal strain gauge (the piezoresistive effect).
