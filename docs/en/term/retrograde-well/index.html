What is Retrograde well?

A well whose doping is higher deeper below the surface, made by high-energy implantation. It suppresses punch-through and latch-up while keeping the channel doping at the surface low.

Japanese name: レトログレードウェル

Answer: A. Retrograde well

A retrograde well uses high-energy implants of hundreds of keV to MeV to place its peak doping deep below the surface. Surface doping stays low to suit transistor characteristics, while the heavily doped depths suppress punch-through and latch-up (parasitic thyristor action). It also eliminates the hours of diffusion above 1000 °C needed for conventional diffused wells, greatly reducing thermal budget and cycle time.
