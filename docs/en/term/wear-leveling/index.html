What is Wear leveling?

A technique that spreads writes so rewrites do not concentrate on particular flash blocks, extending the memory's life.

Japanese name: ウェアレベリング

  1. ATo compress data and increase capacity

  3. CTo spread writes across the whole device instead of concentrating them on certain blocks, extending its life
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. To spread writes across the whole device instead of concentrating them on certain blocks, extending its life

Each block tolerates a limited number of erase cycles, so writing the same logical address repeatedly would wear one spot out early. With wear leveling, the controller maintains a logical-to-physical mapping (the FTL) and steers writes to blocks with fewer erases. It also occasionally relocates rarely changed static data, evening out wear across all blocks and extending product life.
