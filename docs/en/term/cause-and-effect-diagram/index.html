What is Cause-and-effect diagram?

A diagram that organizes the causes affecting a problem (the effect) in the shape of a fish skeleton. Also called a fishbone diagram.

Japanese name: 特性要因図

Try it: questions about Cause-and-effect diagram

Which diagram organizes the relationship between a problem (effect) and the factors that influence it in a shape like a fish skeleton?

  1. APareto chart
  2. BHistogram
  3. CCause-and-effect diagram
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. Cause-and-effect diagram

A cause-and-effect diagram places the problem (effect) at the right and branches influencing factors into major bones such as man, machine, material and method (the 4Ms), with finer causes as smaller bones; its shape gives it the name fishbone diagram. It is also called an Ishikawa diagram after its originator. One of the seven basic QC tools, it helps list candidate causes exhaustively. Pareto charts rank counts and histograms show distributions.

Which chart divides data into intervals and shows the frequencies as bars to see the shape of the distribution of variation?

  1. AScatter diagram
  2. BCause-and-effect diagram
  3. CHistogram
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. Histogram

A histogram divides measurements into intervals and shows the count in each as bar height. It reveals the center, spread, skew and relation to spec limits at a glance. Two peaks often mean data from two tools or two materials are mixed, prompting stratified analysis. A scatter plot shows correlation between two quantities, and a cause-and-effect diagram organizes causes.
