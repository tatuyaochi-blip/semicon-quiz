What is TOPS?

Trillions of operations per second, a performance figure for AI accelerators, usually a theoretical peak counted at low precision.

Answer: A. It is usually a theoretical peak counted at a low precision such as INT8, and real model speed varies greatly with memory bandwidth and utilization

TOPS (trillions of operations per second) is the theoretical figure with every unit busy all the time, usually counted at INT8 or lower and sometimes assuming sparsity. Real models may be limited by memory bandwidth, leave units idle or fall back to other circuits for unsupported operations, so effective performance can be much lower. Comparing products requires measured results under the same conditions.
