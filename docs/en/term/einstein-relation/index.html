What is Einstein relation?

The relation D = μkT/q that links the diffusion coefficient D and mobility μ of carriers. It shows that materials with higher mobility also allow faster diffusion.

Japanese name: アインシュタインの関係式

Answer: C. D = μkT / q

The Einstein relation is D = μkT/q. Both diffusion and drift are governed by how freely carriers move between scattering events, so the two are proportional. At room temperature kT/q ≈ 26 mV, so electrons (μ ≈ 1400 cm²/V·s) have D ≈ 36 cm²/s and holes (about 450) about 12 cm²/s. With a mobility table you can immediately find diffusion coefficients for diffusion-length and device calculations.
