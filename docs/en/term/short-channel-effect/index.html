What is Short-channel effect?

Phenomena in which, as gate length shrinks, the drain's electric field lowers the threshold voltage and increases off-state leakage current.

Japanese name: 短チャネル効果

Which describes a short-channel effect in MOSFETs?

  1. AShortening the gate makes the gate capacitance infinite
  2. BShortening the gate always doubles the channel mobility
  3. CShortening the gate lowers the threshold voltage and increases off-state leakage
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. Shortening the gate lowers the threshold voltage and increases off-state leakage

As gate length shrinks, the source and drain depletion regions become relatively large and control part of the channel charge instead of the gate. Consequences include threshold roll-off, drain-induced barrier lowering (DIBL), degraded subthreshold swing and rising off-state leakage. Device characteristics also become highly sensitive to small process variations in gate length.
