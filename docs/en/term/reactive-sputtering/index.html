What is Reactive sputtering?

Sputtering a metal target in an atmosphere containing a reactive gas such as nitrogen or oxygen to form nitride or oxide films. It is used for films such as titanium nitride (TiN).

Japanese name: 反応性スパッタリング

Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. Target poisoning

In reactive sputtering, increasing reactive gas eventually coats the target surface itself with nitride or oxide. Compounds have lower sputter yields than metals, so deposition rate abruptly drops to a fraction—target poisoning. Because the compound layer is not removed immediately when gas is reduced, rate versus gas flow shows hysteresis. Control schemes that monitor plasma emission to regulate gas flow stabilize operation in the transition region.
