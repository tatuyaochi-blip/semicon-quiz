What is Photoresist?

A light-sensitive material whose solubility in developer changes when exposed. In positive resist the exposed areas dissolve; in negative resist they remain.

Field: Lithography

Photoresist in the explanations

  • Photoresist is a photosensitive polymer, coated tens of nm to several µm thick, whose chemistry changes on exposure: in positive resists exposed areas dissolve in developer, and in negative resists they remain.
