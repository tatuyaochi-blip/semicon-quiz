What is Weibull distribution?

A probability distribution widely used to analyze lifetime data. Its shape parameter can represent early-failure, random-failure and wear-out behavior.

Field: Reliability

Weibull distribution in the explanations

  • In the Weibull distribution, a shape parameter m less than 1 represents the early-failure type, with failure rate decreasing over time; m = 1 represents the constant random-failure type; and m greater than 1 represents the wear-out type, with failure rate increasing.
