What is Weibull distribution?

A probability distribution widely used to analyze lifetime data. Its shape parameter can represent early-failure, random-failure and wear-out behavior.

Field: Reliability

Weibull distribution in the explanations

  • In the Weibull distribution, a shape parameter m below 1 describes infant mortality with decreasing failure rate, m = 1 a constant random-failure rate, and m above 1 wear-out with increasing rate.
