What is Weibull distribution?

A probability distribution widely used to analyze lifetime data. Its shape parameter can represent early-failure, random-failure and wear-out behavior.

Japanese name: ワイブル分布

Answer: A. Weibull distribution

In the Weibull distribution, a shape parameter m below 1 describes infant mortality with decreasing failure rate, m = 1 a constant random-failure rate, and m above 1 wear-out with increasing rate. Its strength is representing all three bathtub periods with one distribution. Failure data plotted on Weibull probability paper form a straight line whose slope is m. A uniform distribution gives equally likely values, and a binomial distribution counts successes.
