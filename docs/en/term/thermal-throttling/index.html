What is Thermal throttling?

Control that lowers frequency and voltage when a chip gets too hot, reducing heat to protect it.

Japanese name: サーマルスロットリング

  1. AStopping the fan to cool the chip

Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. Lowering frequency and voltage when the chip gets too hot, reducing heat to protect it from damage

When on-chip temperature sensors exceed a set threshold, the power-management circuitry or OS lowers frequency and voltage to bring heat within the cooling capacity. Performance drops, but chip failure and shortened lifetime are prevented. Thin laptops and smartphones slow down partway through sustained heavy work because of this throttling. If temperature keeps rising anyway, the system finally shuts down for protection.
