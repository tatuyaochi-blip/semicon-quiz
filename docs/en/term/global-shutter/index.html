What is Global shutter?

An electronic shutter method that exposes all pixels at the same time. Unlike a rolling shutter, which reads row by row, it does not distort images of moving objects.

Field: Image sensors and optical devices

Global shutter in the explanations

  • A global shutter exposes all pixels simultaneously, avoiding distortion, and is used in industrial inspection and automotive cameras, but it needs in-pixel charge storage, making pixels larger.
