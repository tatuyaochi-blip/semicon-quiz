What is MMIC?

Short for monolithic microwave integrated circuit: a high-frequency IC that integrates transistors together with passive components such as inductors and capacitors on a substrate such as GaAs.

Answer: C. MMIC (monolithic microwave integrated circuit)

An MMIC (monolithic microwave integrated circuit) integrates RF transistors with matching-network inductors, capacitors and transmission lines on one substrate. It is smaller and more reproducible than assembling discrete parts on a board and suits mass production. Low-loss semi-insulating GaAs substrates host MMICs for smartphone power amplifiers, radar and satellite communications, and GaN-on-SiC MMICs are increasingly common.
