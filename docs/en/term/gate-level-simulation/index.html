What is Gate-level simulation?

Simulation of the post-synthesis or post-layout netlist, optionally with delay information, to catch problems invisible at RTL.

Field: HDL & Verification

Gate-level simulation in the explanations

  • Gate-level simulation exercises the actual gates and flip-flops, and with each gate's and wire's delay supplied in SDF format, delay-related problems also show up.
