What is Power gating?

A low-power technique that switches off the supply to unused blocks, eliminating nearly all their leakage. State saving and wake-up time must be considered.

Field: Power Management

Power gating in the explanations

  • Power gating turns off a large transistor switch placed between the block and the supply, cutting power itself, so leakage nearly disappears too.
