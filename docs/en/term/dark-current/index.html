What is Dark current?

Current that flows in a photodiode even without light, caused for example by thermal generation of carriers. It rises with temperature and appears as noise in dark images.

Japanese name: 暗電流

  1. AA signal current that increases in proportion to light intensity
  2. BCurrent that flows even without light, and increases with temperature

Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. Current that flows even without light, and increases with temperature

Dark current is charge generated thermally in the photodiode without light, arising mainly from interface states, crystal defects and metal-contamination levels. It causes noise and white spots ("hot pixels") in low-light shots and long exposures, and roughly doubles every 6–8 °C. This is why astronomical cameras cool their sensors.
