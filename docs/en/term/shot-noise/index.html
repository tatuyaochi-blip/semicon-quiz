What is Shot noise?

Noise from statistical fluctuation in the number of photons or electrons. Its size is proportional to √N for a signal of N electrons, so it is relatively larger when light is scarce.

Japanese name: ショットノイズ

  1. AProportional to N
  2. BProportional to √N
  3. CConstant regardless of N
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. Proportional to √N

Photons arrive randomly, so a signal averaging N electrons fluctuates by about √N (Poisson statistics). This shot noise gives S/N = N/√N = √N, improving with more light: 10 for 100 electrons, 100 for 10,000. Being inherent to light itself, it cannot be eliminated by better circuits; gathering more light with larger pixels or lenses is the fundamental remedy.
