What is Word line?

The wire connected to the gates of memory-cell transistors that selects the row to read or write.

Japanese name: ワード線

Word line appears in 7 practice questions across 2 topics, including DRAM, SRAM and flash memory.

Field: DRAM

Try it: questions about Word line

What is the main purpose of burying the gate electrode in a trench in the silicon substrate (buried word line) in recent DRAM cell transistors?

  1. ATo make the cell emit light
  2. BTo lengthen the effective channel and suppress leakage while shrinking the cell
  3. CTo eliminate the need for a capacitor
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. To lengthen the effective channel and suppress leakage while shrinking the cell

A DRAM cell transistor must leak extraordinarily little—femtoamperes—to hold the capacitor's charge for 64 ms or more. Burying the gate in a trench lengthens the channel along the trench while keeping the footprint small, suppressing short-channel leakage. Buried-channel array transistors (BCAT) became mainstream around 2010 and also reduce capacitance between the word line and overlying wiring.

In 3D NAND, what is the structure called in which the edge of the stack is shaped into steps so that each layer's word line can be connected?

  1. AStaircase structure
  2. BDamascene structure
  3. CTrench structure
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. Staircase structure

In 3D NAND, hundreds of conductive word-line layers are stacked, so the array edge is etched into a staircase that exposes each layer's top surface for contacts. The steps are formed by repeatedly etching while trimming back the resist, so more layers mean longer processing and more staircase area. Designs split the staircase into multiple rows or add steps in another direction to save area.
