What is Switching loss?

Loss that occurs while a power device switches between on and off, when voltage and current are present at the same time. It grows with switching frequency.

Field: Power semiconductors

Switching loss in the explanations

  • Switching loss arises because voltage and current coexist during each transition; it equals energy per switching event × switching frequency, so ten times the frequency means ten times the switching loss.
