What is STA (static timing analysis)?

A method that calculates the delay of every path in a circuit without test patterns and checks that timing constraints are met.

Field: IC design flow and EDA

STA (static timing analysis) in the explanations

  • STA sums cell and wire delays along every signal path and exhaustively checks timing constraints such as setup and hold times.
