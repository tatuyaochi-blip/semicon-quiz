What is 3D NAND?

NAND flash in which memory cells are stacked vertically in many layers to increase capacity. It was introduced to get past the limits of planar scaling, and layer counts now exceed 200.

Field: SRAM and flash memory

3D NAND in the explanations

  • As planar scaling neared its limit around 15 nm, 3D NAND, which stacks cells vertically, entered mass production around 2013 and became mainstream.
