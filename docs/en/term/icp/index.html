What is ICP (inductively coupled plasma)?

A method that generates a high-density plasma with RF current in a coil. It makes it easy to control plasma density and the energy of ions reaching the wafer independently.

Field: Etching

ICP (inductively coupled plasma) in the explanations

  • An ICP etcher drives RF current through a coil above or around the chamber to induce an electric field that sustains a dense plasma of 10¹¹–10¹² cm⁻³, while a separate RF supply biases the wafer.
