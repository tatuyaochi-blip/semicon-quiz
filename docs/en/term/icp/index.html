What is ICP (inductively coupled plasma)?

A method that generates a high-density plasma with RF current in a coil. It makes it easy to control plasma density and the energy of ions reaching the wafer independently.

Japanese name: ICP(誘導結合プラズマ)

Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. It generates high-density plasma with a coil, and plasma density and ion energy are easy to control separately

An ICP etcher drives RF current through a coil above or around the chamber to induce an electric field that sustains a dense plasma of 10¹¹–10¹² cm⁻³, while a separate RF supply biases the wafer. Plasma density (radical and ion flux) and ion energy at the wafer can be set independently, enabling fast, highly anisotropic etching at pressures of a few Pa or less; ICP tools are widely used for silicon and metal etching.
