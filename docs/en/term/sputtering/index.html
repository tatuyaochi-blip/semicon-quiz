What is Sputtering?

A type of PVD in which ions in a plasma bombard a target and the ejected atoms are deposited on the wafer.

Field: Deposition (CVD and PVD)

Sputtering in the explanations

  • Sputtering bombards a target with ions from a plasma and deposits the ejected atoms on the wafer; because it relies on a physical phenomenon, it is classified as PVD.
