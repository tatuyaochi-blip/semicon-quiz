What is GIDL (gate-induced drain leakage)?

Off-state leakage caused by band-to-band tunneling in the strong field where the gate overlaps the drain. It is a concern for data retention in DRAM, among others.

Japanese name: GIDL(ゲート誘起ドレインリーク)

Which phenomenon increases off-state leakage because band-to-band tunneling current flows due to the strong field where the gate overlaps the drain?

  1. ADIBL
  2. BNBTI
  3. CGIDL (gate-induced drain leakage)
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. GIDL (gate-induced drain leakage)

Even when a transistor is off, a large gate-to-drain voltage bends the bands sharply near the drain edge of the gate, and band-to-band tunneling causes current to flow—GIDL, which notably grows as the gate is driven more negative. It is a problem in devices requiring ultra-low leakage such as DRAM cell transistors and is controlled by engineering the drain-edge doping and field. DIBL is threshold lowering by drain voltage, and NBTI is a pMOS degradation mechanism.

Which combination lists the main components of leakage current in a MOSFET in the off state?

  1. ASubthreshold leakage, gate leakage and junction leakage
  2. BOnly photocurrent
  3. CNo leakage current flows at all
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. Subthreshold leakage, gate leakage and junction leakage

Even an off MOSFET leaks: subthreshold leakage below threshold, gate leakage through the dielectric, and junction leakage at the source/drain junctions (including GIDL). Each device leaks less than a nanoampere, but billions of them add up to watts in standby. Countermeasures combine high-k dielectrics, multiple threshold voltages, power gating of idle blocks, and body-bias control.
