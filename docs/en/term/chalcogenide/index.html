What is Chalcogenide?

A compound containing group 16 elements such as sulfur, selenium or tellurium. It is used as the recording material of phase-change memory (such as GST) and in selectors (OTS) that switch on abruptly above a threshold voltage.

Japanese name: カルコゲナイド

Answer: A. OTS (ovonic threshold switch)

An OTS (ovonic threshold switch) is a chalcogenide device that snaps to low resistance within nanoseconds once its threshold voltage is exceeded and returns to high resistance when the current drops. Unlike PCM it does not crystallize or store a state, so it works as a selector. Its strong nonlinearity—tiny off-current, large on-current—suppresses sneak currents through unselected cells, enabling stacked crosspoint memories.
