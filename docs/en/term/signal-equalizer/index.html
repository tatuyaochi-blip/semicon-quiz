What is Signal equalizer?

A circuit that compensates for a channel attenuating high frequencies more, which smears the signal. Examples include receiver CTLE and DFE and transmitter de-emphasis.

Japanese name: イコライザ

  1. ATo adjust treble and bass to taste in music

  3. CTo compensate for the channel attenuating high frequencies more, which smears the signal, and reduce inter-symbol interference
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. To compensate for the channel attenuating high frequencies more, which smears the signal, and reduce inter-symbol interference

Board traces and cables attenuate higher frequencies more, so a fast bit stream loses its sharp edges and remnants of earlier bits overlap later ones (inter-symbol interference). A CTLE boosts high frequencies to offset the loss, and a DFE estimates the overlap from bits already decided and subtracts it. The transmitter adds emphasis right after transitions (de-emphasis) as well.
