What is Plasma?

A state in which a gas is ionized into a mixture of electrons, ions and radicals (reactive neutrals). It is used in many processes, including dry etching, plasma CVD and ashing.

Japanese name: プラズマ

What is the treatment called that introduces plasma-generated nitrogen into the surface of a thin gate oxide?

  1. ADrive-in
  2. BCMP
  3. CPlasma nitridation
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. Plasma nitridation

Plasma nitridation introduces active nitrogen from a nitrogen plasma into the upper part of an oxide at relatively low temperatures (a few hundred °C), forming SiON with about 10–20% nitrogen. Because nitrogen concentrates near the top surface, it blocks boron penetration and raises permittivity to thin the EOT without degrading Si/SiO2 interface properties such as mobility. Thermal nitridation (NO or N2O anneals) instead piles nitrogen at the interface.

What is an advantage of plasma doping, in which the wafer is exposed directly to a dopant-containing plasma and implanted with low-energy ions?

  1. AMass analysis can accurately select only the desired ions
  2. BHigh doses at low energy are easy, and 3D structures can be doped relatively uniformly
  3. CIt is suited to deep, high-energy implants
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. High doses at low energy are easy, and 3D structures can be doped relatively uniformly

Plasma doping exposes the whole wafer at once rather than scanning a beam, delivering high doses of 10¹⁵–10¹⁶ cm⁻² quickly even at energies below 1 keV. Ions and radicals arrive from many angles, making it easier to dope the sidewalls of 3D structures such as FinFET fins. Lacking mass analysis, however, it admits unwanted species, and dose measurement and control are more difficult.
