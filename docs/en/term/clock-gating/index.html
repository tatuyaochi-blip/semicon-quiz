What is Clock gating?

A low-power design technique that stops the clock to idle circuit blocks, avoiding wasted switching power.

Field: LSI design flow and EDA

Clock gating in the explanations

  • Clock gating only stops the clock, so state is retained and operation resumes on the next clock, but leakage continues.
