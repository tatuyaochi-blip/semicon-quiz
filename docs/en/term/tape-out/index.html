What is Tape-out?

Handing over the finished, verified layout data to manufacturing for mask making. The name comes from the days when data was delivered on magnetic tape.

Japanese name: テープアウト

Tape-out appears in 4 practice questions across 1 topic, including IC design flow and EDA.

Field: IC design flow and EDA

What is handed over

At tape-out the design team delivers the final layout as GDSII or OASIS files, which list polygons layer by layer. The manufacturing side then performs mask data preparation: optical proximity correction (OPC) and other resolution enhancements are applied, the shapes are fractured into a format the mask writer accepts, and masks are written and inspected. Only then can wafers start. Logic descriptions such as Verilog or SPICE netlists are not what mask making uses.

Sign-off checks before tape-out

Because errors are so costly afterward, tape-out is gated by sign-off. Static timing analysis must pass across process, voltage and temperature corners. Design rule checking (DRC) confirms the layout obeys manufacturing rules, and layout versus schematic (LVS) confirms it implements the intended circuit. Power-grid analysis covers IR drop and electromigration, and further checks address antenna effects, electrical rules and manufacturability. The foundry may run its own checks on the received data as well.

Respins and metal fixes

A bug found after tape-out means new masks. A full mask set for an advanced process can cost tens of millions of dollars and the fix delays the product by months, so teams try to limit the damage. Spare cells scattered through the layout allow many logic fixes to be made by changing only metal and via layers, an engineering change order (ECO) that needs far fewer new masks than a full respin.

Where it sits in the schedule

A typical digital flow runs from specification to RTL design, logic synthesis, place and route, sign-off and tape-out. For leading-edge chips this can take one to two years, and wafer processing after tape-out adds months before first silicon returns for testing. For prototypes, small designs often share a reticle with other projects on a multi-project wafer (MPW), splitting mask cost among them.

Answer: B. IR drop and electromigration analysis

Sign-off covers timing, DRC and LVS, plus analysis of power-grid voltage drop (IR drop) and electromigration from current density. When many circuits switch simultaneously, current surges and the local supply voltage sags, increasing delay, so IR-drop results may be fed into timing analysis. Checking color or price is not part of sign-off.

FAQ

Why is it called tape-out?

Layout data was once delivered to the mask shop on magnetic tape. The name stuck even though files are now transferred electronically.

What happens after tape-out?

The layout goes through mask data preparation, including OPC, then masks are written and wafers are processed, followed by packaging, testing and silicon bring-up.

What is a respin in chip design?

A respin is a second (or later) tape-out to fix problems found in silicon. It requires new masks for at least the changed layers and delays the product.
