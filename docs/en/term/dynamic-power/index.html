What is Dynamic power?

Power consumed in CMOS circuits charging and discharging capacitance when signals switch, expressed as αCV²f with activity factor α, capacitance C, voltage V and frequency f.

Japanese name: 動的消費電力

Dynamic power appears in 12 practice questions across 4 topics, including Power Management, CPUs and architecture, MOSFET.

Field: Power Management

Try it: questions about Dynamic power

In a CMOS circuit, if only the supply voltage is halved with everything else unchanged, what happens to dynamic power consumption?

  1. AIt does not change
  2. BIt halves
  3. CIt becomes one quarter
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. It becomes one quarter

CMOS dynamic power P = αCV²f scales with the square of supply voltage, so halving the voltage cuts it to a quarter. Lower voltage also reduces transistor current and speed, so the same frequency can no longer be sustained. Still, lowering frequency together with voltage reduces power nearly with the cube, which is why DVFS—scaling both down under light load—is the most effective power-saving technique.

In the CMOS dynamic power formula P = αCV²f, what does α represent?

  1. AThe number of transistors
  2. BThe fraction of signals switching per clock cycle (the activity factor)
  3. CThe chip temperature
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. The fraction of signals switching per clock cycle (the activity factor)

Dynamic power is consumed charging and discharging the capacitance of wires and gates. C is the switched capacitance, V the supply voltage, f the clock frequency and α the activity factor, the fraction of signals that switch per clock. Parts whose data does not change, or whose clocks are stopped, do not switch, so reducing α is also an important power-saving lever.
