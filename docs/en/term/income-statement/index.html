What is Income statement (P/L)?

A financial statement that compares revenues and expenses over a period (the accounting period) to show a company's operating results. Subtracting expenses from sales step by step, it shows gross profit, operating profit, ordinary profit (in Japanese statements), profit before income taxes and net income.

Japanese name: 損益計算書(P/L)

In a certain period, the income statement shows sales of ¥80 million, cost of sales of ¥50 million, selling, general and administrative expenses of ¥18 million, non-operating income of ¥1 million, non-operating expenses of ¥3 million, extraordinary gains of ¥2 million and extraordinary losses of ¥4 million. Which is the ordinary profit for this period?

  1. A¥8 million
  2. B¥10 million
  3. C¥12 million
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. ¥10 million

Gross profit is 80 − 50 = ¥30 million, and operating profit is 30 − 18 = ¥12 million. Ordinary profit (keijō rieki, used in Japanese financial statements) adds non-operating income such as interest received to operating profit and subtracts non-operating expenses such as interest paid: 12 + 1 − 3 = ¥10 million. Extraordinary gains and losses are one-off items such as gains on the sale of fixed assets or losses from disasters, and they are added and subtracted after ordinary profit to get profit before income taxes (10 + 2 − 4 = ¥8 million).
