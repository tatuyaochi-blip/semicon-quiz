What is De Morgan's theorem?

The rules for transforming logic expressions: NOT (A AND B) equals (NOT A) OR (NOT B), and NOT (A OR B) equals (NOT A) AND (NOT B).

Japanese name: ド・モルガンの定理

According to De Morgan's laws, which expression is equal to NOT(A AND B)?

  1. A(NOT A) AND (NOT B)
  2. B(NOT A) OR (NOT B)
  3. CA OR B
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. (NOT A) OR (NOT B)

De Morgan's theorem states NOT(A AND B) = (NOT A) OR (NOT B) and NOT(A OR B) = (NOT A) AND (NOT B): distributing a negation swaps AND and OR. In words, "not both true" equals "at least one false." In schematics a NAND can be redrawn as an OR with bubbled inputs, which helps when handling active-low signals or building circuits from NAND gates alone.
