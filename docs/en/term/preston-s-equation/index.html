What is Preston's equation?

The empirical rule that the CMP removal rate is proportional to the product of polishing pressure and the relative speed between pad and wafer.

Japanese name: プレストンの式

Answer: C. The product of polishing pressure and the relative velocity between pad and wafer

Preston's equation, removal rate = k × pressure × relative velocity, is an empirical law introduced for glass polishing in 1927. Doubling pressure or rotation speed roughly doubles removal rate, making it the basis of process design, with k depending on slurry, pad and film. Higher local pressure on raised areas makes them polish faster—the reason CMP planarizes.
