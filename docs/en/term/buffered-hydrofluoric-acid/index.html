What is Buffered hydrofluoric acid (BHF)?

Hydrofluoric acid buffered with ammonium fluoride. It is used for wet etching of SiO2 with a stable etch rate and little damage to resist.

Field: Etching

Buffered hydrofluoric acid (BHF) in the explanations

  • Buffered hydrofluoric acid (BHF) adds ammonium fluoride to HF to stabilize pH, so the concentration of the main etching species (HF2⁻) changes little as the bath is consumed, keeping etch rates steady and resist intact.
