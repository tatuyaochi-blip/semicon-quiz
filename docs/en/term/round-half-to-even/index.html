What is Round half to even?

Rounding to the nearest representable value and, on an exact tie, to the one whose last bit is even (0). It is the IEEE 754 default and keeps rounding errors from accumulating in one direction.

Japanese name: 最近接偶数丸め

Rounding to integers with round half to even, what do 2.5 and 3.5 become?

  1. A3 and 4
  2. B2 and 4
  3. C2 and 3
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. 2 and 4

Both are exact ties, so each rounds to the even neighbor: 2.5, halfway between 2 and 3, goes to 2; 3.5, halfway between 3 and 4, goes to 4. Always rounding ties up would give 3 and 4, summing to 7, one more than the original sum of 6, whereas rounding to even gives 2 + 4 = 6, preserving the sum. 2 and 3 result from always rounding ties down. Avoiding biased rounding error is the method's strength.
