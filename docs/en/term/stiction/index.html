What is Stiction?

The sticking of tiny moving MEMS parts to the substrate through surface tension or surface adhesion, so they no longer move. It often happens during drying after sacrificial-layer removal.

Japanese name: スティクション

When removing an SiO2 sacrificial layer to free a MEMS structure, which method is suitable for preventing stiction?

  1. ARinsing with large amounts of water and letting it dry naturally
  2. BRemoving it by polishing
  3. CVapor-phase etching with hydrogen fluoride (HF) vapor
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. Vapor-phase etching with hydrogen fluoride (HF) vapor

Removing a sacrificial oxide with liquid HF can leave liquid trapped under the structure, whose surface tension pulls it down to stick during drying. Vapor-phase HF etching uses no liquid, so no such force arises; small amounts of water or alcohol vapor control the reaction, and temperature is managed so the water produced does not condense. Supercritical CO2 drying solves the same problem by eliminating the liquid-gas interface.
