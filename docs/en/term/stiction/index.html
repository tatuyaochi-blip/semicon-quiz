What is Stiction?

The sticking of tiny moving MEMS parts to the substrate through surface tension or surface adhesion, so they no longer move. It often happens during drying after sacrificial-layer removal.

Field: MEMS and sensors

Stiction in the explanations

  • MEMS moving parts are tiny with large surface area relative to mass, so capillary forces during drying, electrostatic and van der Waals forces, and humidity can pin them to the substrate—stiction.
