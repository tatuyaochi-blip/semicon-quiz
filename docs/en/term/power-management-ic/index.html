What is Power management IC (PMIC)?

An IC that generates multiple voltages from a battery or similar and manages power-up sequencing and voltage changes.

Japanese name: PMIC

Power management IC (PMIC) appears in 2 practice questions across 2 topics, including Power Management, DRAM.

What is the role of a PMIC (power management IC) used in smartphones and similar devices?

  1. AGenerating the multiple voltages the SoC, memory and peripherals need from the battery and managing power-up sequencing and voltage changes
  2. BGenerating images for the display
  3. CTransmitting and receiving radio signals
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. Generating the multiple voltages the SoC, memory and peripherals need from the battery and managing power-up sequencing and voltage changes

SoC cores, memory, I/O, cameras and sensors each need different voltages. A PMIC integrates many switching regulators and LDOs, efficiently generating the required voltages from the battery and, as directed by the SoC, changing voltages for DVFS, switching power to blocks on and off and powering up in the required order. Some also manage battery charging and fuel gauging.
