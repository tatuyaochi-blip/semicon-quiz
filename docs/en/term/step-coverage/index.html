What is Step coverage?

A measure of how uniformly a film covers the sidewalls and bottoms of steps and holes compared with flat areas. ALD gives excellent step coverage.

Field: Deposition (CVD and PVD)

Step coverage in the explanations

  • Step coverage expresses how much film lands on the sidewalls and bottoms of trenches and holes relative to flat areas, e.g., bottom thickness ÷ top thickness as a percentage.

    Which correctly describes step coverage in deposition?

  • Faster gas diffusion makes deposition surface-reaction-limited, so more than 100 wafers stood a few mm apart can be coated uniformly, with good step coverage.

    Which is a characteristic of low-pressure CVD (LPCVD)?

  • The equipment is relatively simple and films are pure, but line-of-sight travel gives poor step coverage and alloy compositions drift, so sputtering dominates IC wiring.
