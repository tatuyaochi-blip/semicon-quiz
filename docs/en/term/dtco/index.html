What is DTCO (design-technology co-optimization)?

Optimizing cell area and performance by considering circuit design and process technology together, as scaling becomes harder.

Japanese name: DTCO(設計・技術協調最適化)

Answer: C. DTCO (design-technology co-optimization)

DTCO (design-technology co-optimization) develops transistor and interconnect process technology together with standard-cell and SRAM design rules to improve area, performance and power—for example, removing fins to shorten cells, or burying power rails or moving them to the backside to free routing. With dimensional shrinking alone no longer enough, it has become a major pillar of density scaling at advanced nodes.
