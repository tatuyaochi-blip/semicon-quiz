What is Dual damascene?

Forming both the trench for a wire and the hole for a via in the insulator, filling them with copper together and finishing with a single CMP step. It reduces process steps.

Japanese name: デュアルダマシン法

Which correctly describes the damascene process commonly used to form copper wiring?

  1. ACopper film is patterned directly by dry etching
  2. BTrenches are formed in the insulator, filled with copper, and the excess copper is removed by CMP
  3. CCopper is thermally oxidized to form the wiring shape
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. Trenches are formed in the insulator, filled with copper, and the excess copper is removed by CMP

In damascene processing, trenches shaped like the wiring are etched into the dielectric, barrier and copper seed layers are sputtered, and copper is electroplated to fill them. CMP then removes excess copper and barrier from the surface, leaving metal only in the trenches. The name comes from the ancient inlay craft of Damascus, in which gold or silver was set into grooves cut in metal. Filling trenches and vias together is called dual damascene.

Which correctly describes a via in multilevel interconnects?

  1. AA conductor that electrically connects upper and lower wiring layers
  2. BA film that insulates adjacent wires in the same layer
  3. CA metal wire that connects the chip to the package
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. A conductor that electrically connects upper and lower wiring layers

A via is a vertical conductor formed by filling a hole in the interlayer dielectric with metal, linking wiring on adjacent levels. At advanced nodes vias are only about 20 nm across, and a chip contains billions of them. Connections from transistors to the first wiring level are called contacts. Dual damascene fills trenches and vias with copper together. Via resistance and open failures strongly affect performance and yield.
