What is Ultrapure water?

Water from which ions, particles and organic matter have been removed to the extreme. Used in large amounts for wafer cleaning, with purity controlled by resistivity and other measures.

Japanese name: 超純水

What is the main purpose of cleaning with ozone dissolved in ultrapure water (ozonated water)?

  1. ATo deposit metal films
  2. BTo polish the wafer
  3. CTo oxidize, decompose and remove organics
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. To oxidize, decompose and remove organics

Ozonated water with a few to tens of ppm dissolved ozone is a strong oxidizer that decomposes organics and grows thin oxide on silicon at room temperature. It reverts to oxygen after use, simplifying waste treatment, and compared with SPM, which consumes large amounts of sulfuric acid and peroxide, it reduces chemical use and CO2 emissions. It is also used in cleans that alternate with dilute HF.

Which is a typical indicator used to control the purity of ultrapure water in semiconductor manufacturing?

  1. ADepth of color
  2. BResistivity
  3. CViscosity
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. Resistivity

The fewer dissolved ions, the less water conducts, so ultrapure water is controlled by resistivity. The theoretical limit, set only by water's own dissociation, is about 18.2 MΩ·cm at 25 °C, and semiconductor UPW is kept close to it. Because resistivity does not respond to non-ionic contamination, TOC (total organic carbon), particles, dissolved oxygen and silica are monitored separately at ppb levels. Color or viscosity cannot reveal purity differences.
