What is Efficiency droop?

The drop in luminous efficiency of InGaN LEDs as current increases. Auger recombination is thought to be one cause.

Field: Compound semiconductors

Efficiency droop in the explanations

  • InGaN LED efficiency peaks at relatively low current densities of a few A/cm² and declines as current increases—efficiency droop.
