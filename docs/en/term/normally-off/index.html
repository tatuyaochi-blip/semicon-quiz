What is Normally-off?

The property of passing no current when the gate voltage is 0 V. It is required of power devices for safety, since current stops even if the drive circuit fails.

Japanese name: ノーマリーオフ

Answer: C. Cascode configuration

In a cascode, a low-voltage (about 30 V) silicon MOSFET is placed in series on the source side of a GaN HEMT, with the HEMT's gate tied to the MOSFET's source. Turning the MOSFET off negatively biases the HEMT gate, switching it off automatically, so the pair behaves as a normally-off device driven by a standard silicon MOSFET gate driver. Drawbacks are the extra component and the MOSFET's parasitics at high speed.
