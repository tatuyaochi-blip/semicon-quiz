What is ARDE (aspect-ratio-dependent etching)?

The slowdown of etch rate in narrower, deeper features because ions and radicals reach the bottom less easily. Also called the microloading effect.

Japanese name: ARDE(アスペクト比依存エッチング)

Answer: B. Aspect-ratio-dependent etching (ARDE, microloading)

At the bottom of narrow, deep features, radicals are lost to wall collisions, ions are deflected by charging, and reaction products escape slowly, so etch rate falls as aspect ratio rises. This aspect-ratio-dependent etching (ARDE, or microloading) leaves narrow holes shallower than wide ones on the same wafer, so depths vary with size. Etch-stop layers and process tuning compensate.
