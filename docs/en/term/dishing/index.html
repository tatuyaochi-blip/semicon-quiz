What is Dishing?

A CMP phenomenon in which the center of a wide metal line is over-polished into a dish-like depression.

Japanese name: ディッシング

Which correctly describes 'dishing' in CMP?

  1. AThe polishing pad wears down and becomes flat
  2. BSlurry particles stick to and remain on the wafer
  3. CThe center of wide wiring is over-polished relative to its surroundings, becoming dish-shaped
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. The center of wide wiring is over-polished relative to its surroundings, becoming dish-shaped

Dishing occurs in wide metal lines and pads, where the softer metal at the center is polished below the surrounding dielectric, forming a dish-shaped recess that deepens with width. Lines thin and their resistance rises, and topography carries into upper layers. Over-polishing of dielectric and metal together across dense arrays is erosion. Designs cap linewidth or slot wide lines to limit dishing.
