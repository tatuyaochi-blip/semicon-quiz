What is Dishing?

A CMP phenomenon in which the center of a wide metal line is over-polished into a dish-like depression.

Field: CMP and cleaning

Dishing in the explanations

  • Dishing occurs in wide metal features, where the soft metal in the center is removed more than the surrounding insulator, creating a dish-like recess.
