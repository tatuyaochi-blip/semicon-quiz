What is Die bonding?

Attaching and fixing a diced chip at its position on a lead frame or package substrate, using adhesive, solder or sintered material.

Japanese name: ダイボンディング

What is the step called that attaches and fixes the chip at a set position on a lead frame or substrate?

  1. AWire bonding
  2. BDicing
  3. CDie bonding
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. Die bonding

Die bonding fixes a singulated chip to a lead frame or substrate using adhesives such as silver paste, solder or die-attach film. Power devices with high current and heat use solder or sintered silver for thermal and electrical conductivity, while stacked memory uses film for uniform thickness. Wire bonding then makes the electrical connections. Dicing is the step that separates the chips.
