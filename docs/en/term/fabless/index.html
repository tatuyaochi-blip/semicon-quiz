What is Fabless?

A company model that owns no fabrication plant and focuses on designing and selling semiconductors.

Japanese name: ファブレス

Fabless appears in 9 practice questions across 1 topic, including Semiconductor industry and market.

Field: Semiconductor industry and market

How a fabless company builds chips

A fabless company owns no wafer fabrication plant. It specifies the product, writes and verifies the register-transfer-level (RTL) design, and completes physical design using the foundry's process design kit (PDK), commercial electronic design automation (EDA) tools and often licensed IP blocks. The finished layout is taped out to a foundry, which manufactures the wafers; an outsourced semiconductor assembly and test (OSAT) company, or increasingly the foundry, packages and tests the chips. The fabless company then sells the product, so its value lies in architecture, circuit design, software and customer relationships rather than in manufacturing.

Why the model spread

A leading-edge fab requires enormous investment and keeps getting more expensive each generation, and one company's products often cannot keep such a fab fully loaded. Consolidating manufacturing at specialized foundries lets many customers share the equipment and keep utilization high, while design companies avoid the capital burden. This lowered the barrier to entry, so even young companies can bring leading-edge chips to market. Typical fabless products include smartphone systems-on-chip (SoCs), GPUs, communication and networking chips. Some integrated device manufacturers moved toward this model by selling or closing fabs.

Fabless, IDM and fab-lite

An integrated device manufacturer (IDM) designs, fabricates, packages and sells its own chips. Having design and process teams in one company makes it easier to co-optimize the two, which matters in memory, power and analog devices where the manufacturing technology itself differentiates the product. Fab-lite companies sit in between: they keep fabs for mature processes and specialty technologies where they have unique strengths, while outsourcing leading-edge products to foundries. The model is common among microcontroller, analog and power semiconductor makers that want to limit capital spending without losing manufacturing know-how.

What fabless companies give up

Without their own fabs, fabless companies depend on foundry capacity, pricing and schedules, and during shortages they compete with other customers for wafer starts. They design within the foundry's rules and device options, with limited ability to customize the process, although large customers work closely with foundries on design-technology co-optimization. Concentration of advanced foundry capacity in a few locations also concentrates supply risk. In exchange they gain flexibility to choose processes per product and can put capital into engineering rather than plants.

Answer: B. A company that has no manufacturing plants of its own and specializes in design and sales

Fabless means having no fab (manufacturing plant). These companies concentrate on design and sales, outsourcing manufacturing to foundries and assembly and test to OSATs. With no need for huge plant investments, even young companies can bring leading-edge products to market, and many major GPU and smartphone SoC vendors operate this way. Companies doing everything from design to manufacturing are IDMs, and those taking on back-end work are OSATs.

Answer: C. Smartphone SoCs and GPUs

Fabless companies design smartphone SoCs, GPUs, communication chips, networking chips and the like, outsourcing manufacturing to foundries and assembly and test to OSATs. Their value comes from circuit design and software, and many enjoy high profit margins. Silicon wafers are made by materials companies and scanners by equipment makers; they are not fabless products.

FAQ

What does fabless mean in the semiconductor industry?

Fabless means a company has no fab, or wafer manufacturing plant, of its own. It designs and sells chips and outsources manufacturing to foundries and packaging and test to OSATs.

What is the difference between fabless and IDM?

A fabless company only designs and sells chips, relying on outside manufacturers. An IDM, or integrated device manufacturer, does design, manufacturing and sales in-house.

What is a fab-lite company?

A fab-lite company keeps some fabs for mature or specialty processes while outsourcing its most advanced products to foundries. It is a middle path between fabless and a full IDM.
