What is Mobility?

How easily carriers move when an electric field is applied. In silicon, electron mobility is higher than hole mobility.

Field: Semiconductor physics

Mobility in the explanations

  • In silicon at room temperature and low doping, electron mobility is about 1400 cm²/V·s and hole mobility about 450 cm²/V·s, so electrons are about three times faster, mainly because holes have a larger effective mass.
