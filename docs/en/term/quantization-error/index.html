What is Quantization error?

The error that arises when rounding a continuous analog value to a digital value with a finite number of bits. At most half of one LSB.

Japanese name: 量子化誤差

What is the maximum quantization error of an ideal ADC? (LSB: the voltage corresponding to one least significant bit)

  1. A±2 LSB
  2. B±1 LSB
  3. C±1/2 LSB
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. ±1/2 LSB

Rounding a continuous value to steps 1 LSB wide always introduces error, at most ±1/2 LSB in an ideal ADC. When the input varies enough, the error behaves like noise uniformly distributed over ±1/2 LSB with an rms value of LSB/√12—the basis of the ideal SNR of 6.02N + 1.76 dB. More bits mean a smaller LSB and smaller quantization error.
