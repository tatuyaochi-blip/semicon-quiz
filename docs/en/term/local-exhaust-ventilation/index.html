What is Local exhaust ventilation?

Equipment that draws in gases and mists at their source, such as tools handling chemicals, and exhausts them. It keeps workers from inhaling hazardous substances.

Japanese name: 局所排気装置

  1. ABecause it contains radioactive substances
  2. BBecause it contains arsenic and must be properly managed and treated as a hazardous substance
  3. CBecause it explodes on contact with air
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. Because it contains arsenic and must be properly managed and treated as a hazardous substance

GaAs is about half arsenic by weight (about 52%), so grinding and polishing dust, waste liquids and scrapped wafers must be handled as hazardous industrial waste under regulations, with arsenic recovery. Local exhaust ventilation and protective equipment prevent worker exposure. Arsine (AsH3) gas used in manufacturing is even more toxic, requiring rigorous gas detection and abatement systems.

Which facility, used on equipment that handles chemicals and the like, captures generated gases and mists near their source and exhausts them?

  1. ALocal exhaust ventilation
  2. BHumidifier
  3. CLighting fixture
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. Local exhaust ventilation

Local exhaust ventilation captures hazardous gases and mists near their source and removes them before workers can inhale them. Because contaminants are removed before spreading through the room, it is more efficient than general ventilation and needs less airflow. Exhausted gas is treated by scrubbers if needed before release. Its performance is checked periodically, for example by capture velocity at the hood.
