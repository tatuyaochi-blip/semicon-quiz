What is LPDDR?

Short for low-power DDR: a DRAM standard for smartphones and laptops with lower supply voltages and enhanced power-saving features.

Japanese name: LPDDR

Answer: C. Devices that require low power, such as smartphones

LPDDR (Low Power DDR) is mobile DRAM that cuts power with low supply voltages, deep power-down modes and temperature-aware refresh. Used in smartphones, tablets and thin laptops, it is stacked on the processor package (PoP) or placed right beside it to keep wiring short. Because it is soldered down rather than socketed, it usually cannot be upgraded later; LPDDR5X reaches 8533 MT/s and beyond.
