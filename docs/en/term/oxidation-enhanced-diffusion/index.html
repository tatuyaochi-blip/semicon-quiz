What is Oxidation-enhanced diffusion (OED)?

Faster diffusion of boron and phosphorus during oxidation than without it, caused by silicon interstitials injected into the silicon by the oxidation.

Japanese name: 酸化増速拡散(OED)

Answer: C. Oxidation-enhanced diffusion (OED)

As oxidation proceeds, excess self-interstitials from the interface are injected into the silicon. Boron and phosphorus diffuse largely via interstitials, so their diffusion speeds up several-fold during oxidation (oxidation-enhanced diffusion, OED). Antimony diffuses mainly via vacancies, which the interstitial excess depletes, so it slows (oxidation-retarded diffusion). This contrast helped reveal the mechanisms of dopant diffusion.
