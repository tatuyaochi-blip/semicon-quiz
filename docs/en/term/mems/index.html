What is MEMS?

Micro-electro-mechanical systems. Devices that integrate tiny mechanical structures and electronic circuits using semiconductor microfabrication.

Field: MEMS and sensors

MEMS in the explanations

  • MEMS (micro-electro-mechanical systems) uses semiconductor deposition, lithography and etching to build tiny mechanical structures with moving parts—springs, proof masses, membranes, mirrors—that act as sensors or actuators.
