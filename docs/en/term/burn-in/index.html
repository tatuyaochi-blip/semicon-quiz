What is Burn-in?

A test that operates products under stress such as high temperature and high voltage to remove units prone to early failure before shipment.

Field: Testing and inspection

Burn-in in the explanations

  • Burn-in operates products for a set time under conditions such as high temperature and high voltage, causing products with latent defects to fail early so they can be removed (screened).
