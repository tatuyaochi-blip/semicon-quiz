What is Backside power delivery?

Placing the power wiring on the back of the wafer so the front-side wiring can be used for signals. It reduces voltage drop and routing congestion.

Japanese name: 裏面電源供給

Answer: C. A method that places power wiring on the back side of the wafer, freeing the front-side wiring for signals and reducing voltage drop and routing congestion

Traditionally, signal and power wiring were all built above the transistors, so power descended through more than ten metal levels and vias. Backside power delivery thins the wafer to hundreds of nm or a few µm and places thick power wiring on its back, feeding transistors from below. Voltage drop falls, front-side wiring is freed for signals and cells can shrink. It is being introduced in Intel's PowerVia (18A) and TSMC's A16, among others.
