What is Lower explosive limit (LEL)?

The lowest concentration in air at which a flammable gas can burn or explode. Gas detectors are often set to alarm at a percentage of this level.

Japanese name: 爆発下限界(LEL)

What does the lower explosive limit (LEL) of a flammable gas mean?

  1. AThe maximum temperature at which the gas burns
  2. BThe lowest concentration in air at which the gas can burn or explode
  3. CThe maximum pressure of the gas container
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. The lowest concentration in air at which the gas can burn or explode

Flammable gases burn or explode only within a concentration range in air, the flammable range. Its lower bound is the lower explosive limit (LEL), for example about 4% for hydrogen and about 1.4% for silane. Combustible gas detectors read concentration as a percentage of LEL (%LEL) and alarm at a safely low level, typically 10–25% of the LEL. It is unrelated to flame temperature or cylinder pressure.
