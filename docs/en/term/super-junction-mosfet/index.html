What is Super-junction MOSFET?

A power MOSFET whose drift layer has alternating p-type and n-type pillars. It allows higher drift-layer doping without losing breakdown voltage, achieving on-resistance below the conventional silicon limit.

Japanese name: スーパージャンクションMOSFET

  2. BIt has two gates
  3. CThe gate oxide is made very thick to raise breakdown voltage
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. Alternating p-type and n-type pillars in the drift layer lower on-resistance while maintaining breakdown voltage

A superjunction structure fills the drift region with alternating p- and n-type pillars. When off, they deplete laterally so the entire region becomes depleted, sustaining high voltage even with heavily doped n-pillars; when on, current flows through those n-pillars. This brings the on-resistance of 600 V-class silicon MOSFETs to a fraction of the classical silicon limit, and such devices are widely used in power supplies and PFC circuits. Precisely balancing the p and n charge is the manufacturing key.
