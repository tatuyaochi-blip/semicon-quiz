What is Place and route?

The design step that arranges cells on the chip (placement) from the netlist produced by logic synthesis and determines the wiring between them (routing).

Field: LSI design flow and EDA

Questions that mention Place and route (2)

  1. What is the step called that extracts wire resistance and capacitance from the layout after place and route?
  2. Which step at the start of place and route decides the rough placement of large circuit blocks, I/O pins, power wiring and the like?
