What is Place and route?

The design step that arranges cells on the chip (placement) from the netlist produced by logic synthesis and determines the wiring between them (routing).

Japanese name: 配置配線

Place and route appears in 2 practice questions across 1 topics, including IC design flow and EDA.

Try it: questions about Place and route

What is the step called that extracts wire resistance and capacitance from the layout after place and route?

  1. ALogic synthesis
  2. BATPG
  3. CParasitic extraction (RC extraction)
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. Parasitic extraction (RC extraction)

Parasitic extraction calculates wire resistance and capacitance from the geometry, spacing and overlap of wires with layers above and below. These values enable accurate static timing, power and crosstalk analysis. Scaling has made wire delay large relative to cell delay, increasing its importance. Logic synthesis generates gates and ATPG generates test patterns.

Which step at the start of place and route decides the rough placement of large circuit blocks, I/O pins, power wiring and the like?

  1. AFloorplanning
  2. BClock tree synthesis
  3. CDRC
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. Floorplanning

Floorplanning determines die size, the positions of large blocks such as memories, I/O placement and the backbone of the power grid. Placing blocks that exchange data near each other shortens wires and eases congestion. Decisions here strongly affect congestion, timing and power and are hard to change later, making it a critical step. CTS and DRC come later in the flow.
