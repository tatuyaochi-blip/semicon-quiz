What is Czochralski (CZ) method?

A method of making single-crystal silicon by dipping a seed crystal into molten silicon in a crucible and slowly pulling it up while rotating.

Field: Silicon wafers

Czochralski (CZ) method in the explanations

  • CZ silicon is melted in a quartz (SiO2) crucible, and oxygen dissolving from it enters the crystal at about 10¹⁸ cm⁻³.
