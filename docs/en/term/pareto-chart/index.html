What is Pareto chart?

A chart that lists items such as defect types as bars in descending order of count, with a line for the cumulative share. It shows which items to tackle first.

Japanese name: パレート図

  3. CTo arrange defect categories in descending order of count and find the items to address first
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. To arrange defect categories in descending order of count and find the items to address first

A Pareto chart arranges defect categories or causes as bars in descending order of count (or cost), overlaid with a cumulative-percentage line. Often the top two or three items account for 70–80% of the total (the Pareto principle), showing at a glance where limited resources should go first. Correlation between two variables is shown by a scatter plot, and time-series monitoring by a control chart.
