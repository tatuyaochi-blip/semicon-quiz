What is Nyquist frequency?

Half the sampling frequency. Components above it are folded back to lower frequencies when sampled (aliasing).

Japanese name: ナイキスト周波数

Try it: questions about Nyquist frequency

What sampling frequency is needed to sample an analog signal containing components up to 20 kHz without aliasing?

  1. A10 kHz or more
  2. BExactly 20 kHz
  3. CA frequency higher than 40 kHz
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. A frequency higher than 40 kHz

The sampling theorem says a signal can be reconstructed if sampled faster than twice its highest frequency, so 20 kHz content requires more than 40 kHz. Since filters cannot cut off instantly, practical systems add margin: audio CDs sample at 44.1 kHz, using 20–22.05 kHz as the filter's transition band. Half the sampling frequency is called the Nyquist frequency.

What type of filter is used as the anti-aliasing filter placed before an ADC?

  1. AHigh-pass filter
  2. BBand-stop filter
  3. CLow-pass filter
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. Low-pass filter

Components above the Nyquist frequency (half the sampling rate) that reach an ADC fold back as false lower-frequency signals that can never be distinguished or removed afterward. A low-pass filter is therefore placed before the ADC to remove content above Nyquist. Because steep analog filters are hard to build, many designs oversample to widen the margin and get by with a simple first- or second-order RC filter.
