What is Slip?

A defect in which crystal planes shift and dislocations form under stress from temperature differences or the wafer's own weight during high-temperature processing of large wafers. It can cause problems such as overlay errors.

Field: Silicon wafers

Slip in the explanations

  • At high temperature silicon weakens greatly, so stress from temperature gradients during rapid heating or cooling, or from the wafer's own weight at support points, can make crystal planes ({111}) slip, creating dislocations (slip).
