What is 1T1C cell?

The DRAM memory cell that stores one bit with one transistor and one capacitor. The presence or absence of charge on the capacitor represents 0 or 1.

Field: DRAM

1T1C cell in the explanations

  • DRAM's small 1T1C cell (about 6F²) packs roughly 20 times denser than a 6T SRAM cell (about 120–150F²), lowering cost per bit.
