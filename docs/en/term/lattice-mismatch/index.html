What is Lattice mismatch?

A difference in the atomic spacing (lattice constant) between a grown film and its substrate. A large mismatch causes strain and defects such as dislocations.

Field: Epitaxial growth

Lattice mismatch in the explanations

  • SiC conducts heat at about 400 W/(m·K), far better than silicon (about 150) or sapphire (about 35), and its lattice mismatch with GaN is a relatively small 3.5%, so it serves as the substrate for RF and high-power GaN HEMTs in base stations and radar (GaN-on-SiC).
