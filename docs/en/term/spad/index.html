What is SPAD (single-photon avalanche diode)?

A photodetector biased above breakdown so that even a single photon triggers a large avalanche pulse. It is used for ranging, such as in LiDAR.

Field: Image sensors and optical devices

SPAD (single-photon avalanche diode) in the explanations

  • APDs are used in optical-communication receivers and LiDAR, and in Geiger mode (SPAD), biased above breakdown, even a single photon produces a large pulse, used in ranging and quantum optics.

    Which is a characteristic of an avalanche photodiode (APD)?

  • A SPAD operates above breakdown (Geiger mode), so even one photon-generated electron triggers an avalanche producing a large current pulse.
