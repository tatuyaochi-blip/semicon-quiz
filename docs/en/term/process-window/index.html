What is Process window?

The range of exposure dose and focus over which pattern dimensions stay within tolerance. The wider it is, the more robust manufacturing is to variation.

Japanese name: プロセスウィンドウ

Which correctly describes the factor k1 in the resolution formula R = k1 × λ / NA?

  1. AIt reflects the difficulty of the process, with a theoretical minimum of 0.25 for a single exposure
  2. BThe larger it is, the higher the resolution
  3. CIt is always 1 or more
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. It reflects the difficulty of the process, with a theoretical minimum of 0.25 for a single exposure

k1 summarizes the combined capability of resist, mask tricks and illumination; smaller values resolve finer patterns but narrow the process window. The theoretical single-exposure limit for periodic lines and spaces is 0.25. In the 1990s k1 was around 0.6, but resolution-enhancement techniques—off-axis illumination, phase-shift masks, OPC—have brought production values down to about 0.27–0.3.

What is the range of exposure dose and focus over which pattern dimensions stay within tolerance called?

  1. AProcess window
  2. BOverlay
  3. CLine edge roughness
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. Process window

The process window is the range of exposure dose and focus over which feature dimensions stay within tolerance (e.g., ±10% of target), plotted as dose versus focus (an exposure-defocus window). In production, dose and focus drift with tool variation, wafer topography and resist-thickness changes, so a wider window means more stable manufacturing. OPC and illumination optimization largely aim to widen it.
