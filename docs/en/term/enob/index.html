What is ENOB (effective number of bits)?

The real performance of an ADC, including noise and distortion (SINAD), expressed as the bit count of an ideal ADC. A 12-bit ADC with an ENOB of around 10 bits is not unusual.

Field: AD/DA conversion

ENOB (effective number of bits) in the explanations

  • Real ADCs fall short due to thermal noise, distortion and clock jitter, so actual performance is compared by the effective number of bits (ENOB) calculated from measurements.
