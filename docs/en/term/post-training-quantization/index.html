What is Post-training quantization?

Converting a trained model's weights and activations to lower precision after examining value ranges with representative data.

Japanese name: 学習後量子化

What is the difference between post-training quantization (PTQ) and quantization-aware training (QAT)?

  1. APTQ quantizes before training, QAT after
  2. BPTQ quantizes a trained model as is; QAT simulates quantization during training so the weights adapt and errors stay small at low precision
  3. CNeither PTQ nor QAT actually quantizes
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. PTQ quantizes a trained model as is; QAT simulates quantization during training so the weights adapt and errors stay small at low precision

Post-training quantization converts a trained model's weights (and activations) to lower precision after examining value ranges with a small representative dataset. It is easy but loses accuracy as bit width shrinks. Quantization-aware training simulates quantization rounding in the forward pass during training and adjusts weights with that error included, preserving accuracy even at precisions as low as 4 bits.

In post-training quantization of activations to INT8, what is the purpose of calibration?

  1. ARunning representative input data to observe each layer's activation range and set the quantization scales
  2. BPrecisely adjusting the GPU clock
  3. CAdjusting how colors appear on a screen
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. Running representative input data to observe each layer's activation range and set the quantization scales

Weights are fixed once training ends, but activation values vary with the input. So a few hundred inputs resembling real usage are run through the model to observe each layer's activation range and distribution, and the range mapped onto INT8's -127 to 127 (the scale) is chosen. Matching the maximum lets outliers waste resolution, so the range is sometimes narrowed to cover most of the distribution.
