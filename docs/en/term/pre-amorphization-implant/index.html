What is Pre-amorphization implant (PAI)?

Implanting germanium or similar first to make the surface amorphous, so that the following implant does not channel and a shallow junction results.

Japanese name: プリアモルファス化注入(PAI)

Answer: B. To make the surface amorphous by implanting Ge or similar species, suppressing channeling in subsequent implants and giving shallow junctions

Pre-implanting heavy ions such as germanium or silicon amorphizes the surface to a depth of tens of nm, eliminating the ordered rows so that subsequent boron implants do not channel, yielding shallow junctions. During annealing the amorphous layer regrows by solid-phase epitaxy from the crystal below, incorporating dopants onto lattice sites with high activation. End-of-range defects may remain at the former interface.
