What is High-k / metal gate?

The combination of a high-dielectric-constant gate insulator and a metal gate electrode. It keeps gate capacitance high while suppressing gate leakage.

Japanese name: High-k/メタルゲート

In high-k/metal gate transistors, which is one of the main ways to adjust the threshold voltage?

  1. AChoosing the work function of the metal used for the gate electrode
  2. BChanging the wafer diameter
  3. CChanging the package material
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. Choosing the work function of the metal used for the gate electrode

In metal-gate transistors, the gate metal's work function sets the flat-band voltage and thus the threshold. nMOS needs a work function near the conduction band (about 4.1 eV, e.g., TiAl) and pMOS one near the valence band (about 5.1 eV, e.g., TiN). Combining thin metal layers a few nm thick in different stacks and thicknesses produces multiple thresholds for high-speed and low-leakage devices.

In high-k/metal gate transistors, why is a very thin layer such as SiO2 often placed between the silicon and the high-k film (such as hafnium oxide)?

  1. ATo preserve the quality of the silicon interface and limit effects such as mobility degradation
  2. BTo increase the gate capacitance
  3. CTo lower the resistance of the metal gate
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. To preserve the quality of the silicon interface and limit effects such as mobility degradation

High-k films placed directly on silicon degrade channel mobility through interface defects plus charges and lattice vibrations in the high-k layer (remote phonons). A thin interfacial layer (IL) of SiO2 or similar, about 0.5–1 nm, preserves a good interface. Because this low-k layer accounts for much of the EOT, thinning it as much as possible is key to reducing EOT.
