What is OHT (overhead hoist transport)?

Vehicles that run along ceiling rails and automatically carry FOUPs between tools. They are the backbone of automated transport in 300 mm fabs.

Japanese name: OHT(天井走行式無人搬送車)

Field: Cleanrooms and equipment

How an OHT delivers a FOUP

An OHT vehicle travels along rails suspended from the cleanroom ceiling. When it reaches a tool, it stops above the load port and a hoist lowers a gripper that holds the front opening unified pod (FOUP) by its top flange, setting it down on the port. Vehicle and tool exchange handshake signals during the transfer, commonly following the SEMI E84 standard, so a FOUP is placed or lifted only when the port is ready. Pickup works the same way in reverse.

Why 300 mm fabs depend on it

A FOUP holding 25 wafers of 300 mm weighs about 8 kg, since each wafer is roughly 128 g. Carrying such loads all day is strenuous, and a drop can destroy wafers worth a great deal. Overhead transport removes this from operators, keeps traffic off the floor and lets tools be arranged freely in a ballroom-style cleanroom, where sealed FOUPs and tool minienvironments protect the wafers instead of an ultra-clean room.

Part of the AMHS

The OHT fleet is one piece of the automated material handling system (AMHS). Stockers and overhead buffers next to the track hold FOUPs waiting for tools, and a material control system dispatches vehicles in response to requests from the manufacturing execution system. Large fabs run hundreds of vehicles or more on a single network, so routing, congestion and delivery time directly affect tool idle time and cycle time.

OHT vs. other transport

Automated guided vehicles (AGVs) and rail-guided vehicles (RGVs) run on the floor, taking up space that tools and people need. Older 200 mm fabs typically used automated interbay transport between stockers but moved lots within a bay by hand. With OHT, a single overhead system can carry a FOUP directly from one tool to the next, which is why it became the standard backbone of 300 mm fabs.

FAQ

What does OHT stand for in a semiconductor fab?

Overhead hoist transport: ceiling-rail vehicles that hoist FOUPs up from and down to tool load ports.

Why are FOUPs moved automatically instead of by hand?

A full 300 mm FOUP weighs about 8 kg, so manual handling is tiring and risks drops and contamination. Automated transport is safer, cleaner and integrates with fab scheduling.

What is the difference between OHT and AMHS?

AMHS is the whole automated material handling system, including stockers, buffers and control software. OHT is the overhead vehicle system that does most of the actual moving within it.
