What is OHT (overhead hoist transport)?

Vehicles that run along ceiling rails and automatically carry FOUPs between tools. They are the backbone of automated transport in 300 mm fabs.

Japanese name: OHT(天井走行式無人搬送車)

Which is closest to the mass of one silicon wafer 300 mm in diameter and 775 µm thick? (Silicon density is about 2.33 g/cm³.)

  1. AAbout 13 g
  2. BAbout 1,280 g
  3. CAbout 128 g
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. About 128 g

Volume is π × (15 cm)² × 0.0775 cm ≈ 54.8 cm³; multiplying by 2.33 g/cm³ gives about 128 g. A 200 mm wafer (725 µm thick) weighs about 53 g. A FOUP holding 25 300-mm wafers totals around 8 kg, so fabs move them with overhead hoist transport (OHT). Weight and fragility were among the reasons manual handling was abandoned at larger diameters.

In 300 mm wafer fabs, which system automatically transports FOUPs between tools along rails on the ceiling?

  1. AManual cart transport
  2. BTransport by conveyor belts only
  3. COHT (overhead hoist transport)
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. OHT (overhead hoist transport)

A FOUP holding 25 300 mm wafers weighs about 8 kg, so manual handling is strenuous and risks particles and drops. An OHT is a vehicle running on ceiling rails that suspends FOUPs and delivers them automatically to tool load ports, freeing floor space for people and equipment. It is the core of the automated material handling system (AMHS), and large fabs run hundreds of vehicles or more.
