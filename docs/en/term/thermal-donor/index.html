What is Thermal donor?

Oxygen clusters that act as donors when CZ silicon is heated around 450°C. Because they change the wafer's resistivity, they are annihilated by a higher-temperature treatment.

Japanese name: サーマルドナー

Which impurity is incorporated in relatively large amounts from the crucible into silicon crystals grown by the CZ method?

  1. AGold
  2. BOxygen
  3. CHelium
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. Oxygen

CZ silicon is melted in a quartz (SiO2) crucible, and oxygen dissolving from it enters the crystal at about 10¹⁸ cm⁻³. Oxygen pins dislocations, strengthening the wafer, and its precipitates act as gettering sites, but excess oxygen causes defects and thermal donors. Concentration is therefore controlled, for example with magnetic CZ (MCZ) that suppresses melt convection, and specified per application.

Which is closest to the typical concentration of interstitial oxygen in silicon crystals grown by the CZ method?

  1. AAbout 10¹⁸ cm⁻³
  2. BAbout 10²³ cm⁻³
  3. CAbout 10¹⁰ cm⁻³
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. About 10¹⁸ cm⁻³

CZ crystals contain about 10¹⁷–10¹⁸ cm⁻³ (a few to over ten ppma) of oxygen from the quartz crucible, mostly at interstitial sites. It precipitates during anneals to aid gettering and pins dislocations to strengthen wafers, but too much causes defects and thermal donors. Concentration is measured by infrared absorption (near 9 µm) and held within narrow ranges per application.
