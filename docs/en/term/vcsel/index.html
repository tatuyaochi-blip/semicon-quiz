What is VCSEL (vertical-cavity surface-emitting laser)?

A semiconductor laser that emits light perpendicular to the substrate. It can be tested on the wafer and easily arranged in 2D arrays, so it is used in data links and as the light source for face recognition.

Japanese name: VCSEL(垂直共振器面発光レーザー)

Which is a characteristic of a VCSEL (vertical-cavity surface-emitting laser)?

  1. AIt emits light parallel to the substrate and cannot be tested without cleaving
  2. BLike an LED, it emits only spontaneous emission
  3. CIt emits light perpendicular to the substrate, can easily be tested on the wafer, and is easy to arrange in 2D arrays
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. It emits light perpendicular to the substrate, can easily be tested on the wafer, and is easy to arrange in 2D arrays

A VCSEL has a short cavity sandwiched between distributed Bragg reflectors (DBRs) of dozens of layers and emits a circular beam perpendicular to the wafer. It can be tested on the wafer without cleaving, arrayed by the thousands in 2D, and coupled easily to fiber. Proposed in 1977 by Kenichi Iga of Tokyo Institute of Technology, VCSELs are used in face authentication, computer mice and data-center optical links.
