What is Isotropic etching?

Etching that proceeds at the same rate vertically and laterally. It cuts in under the mask (undercut).

Japanese name: 等方性エッチング

When a 1 µm film is etched just down to the underlayer by perfectly isotropic wet etching, roughly how much is etched laterally from the mask edge (undercut)?

  1. AAbout 1 µm
  2. BAbout 10 µm
  3. CAlmost 0
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. About 1 µm

Isotropic etching proceeds equally downward and sideways, so by the time 1 µm of film clears to the underlayer, the mask edge is undercut by about 1 µm, and overetch increases this further. Dimensional changes on the order of the film thickness make it unsuitable for features narrower than the film is thick. Conversely, MEMS exploits this behavior to hollow out material beneath structures.
