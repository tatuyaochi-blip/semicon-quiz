What is Area-selective deposition?

Growing a film only in certain places by exploiting differences in the underlying materials. Because it can save lithography and etch steps, it is being researched and introduced for advanced interconnects.

Japanese name: 領域選択成膜

Answer: C. Area-selective deposition

Area-selective deposition grows film only where needed—on metal but not on dielectric, for instance—by exploiting differences in reactivity between surface materials; surfaces to be protected can also be blocked with self-assembled monolayers (SAMs). It removes lithography and etch steps and is inherently self-aligned, so it is being researched for fine interconnects and other applications.
