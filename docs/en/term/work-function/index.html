What is Work function?

The energy difference between the vacuum level and the Fermi level: the minimum energy needed to remove an electron from a material. It affects the threshold voltage of metal gates and the height of Schottky barriers.

Japanese name: 仕事関数

Field: Semiconductor physics

Work function vs. electron affinity

The work function is measured from the Fermi level to the vacuum level, whereas the electron affinity is measured from the conduction-band edge to the vacuum level. In a metal the Fermi level lies inside a band, so the work function is a single material property, typically about 4 to 6 eV and somewhat dependent on crystal face and surface cleanliness. In a semiconductor the work function equals the electron affinity plus the gap between the conduction band and the Fermi level. Silicon's electron affinity is about 4.05 eV, so its work function ranges from about 4.05 eV (heavily n-type) to 5.17 eV (heavily p-type).

Schottky barriers and contacts

When a metal touches an n-type semiconductor, the ideal (Schottky-Mott) barrier height for electrons is the metal work function minus the semiconductor's electron affinity. A high-work-function metal on n-type silicon should therefore make a rectifying Schottky contact. In practice, interface states pin the Fermi level, so barrier heights on silicon vary much less with the metal than the simple rule predicts. Ohmic contacts are therefore made mainly by heavy doping under the contact, which thins the barrier so carriers tunnel through it.

Setting threshold voltage

In a MOS structure, the difference between the gate and channel work functions sets the flat-band voltage, which adds directly to the threshold voltage. Polysilicon gates gave the right values automatically: n+ poly near the conduction band for nMOS and p+ poly near the valence band for pMOS. With high-k dielectrics, metal gates replaced poly, so the metals must provide effective work functions near 4.1 eV for nMOS (aluminum-containing stacks such as TiAl) and near 5.1 eV for pMOS (TiN-based stacks). FinFETs and gate-all-around devices use lightly doped channels, so stacking thin layers to tune the effective work function is the main way to offer multiple threshold options.

Why gate-last processing helps

The effective work function of a metal gate depends on reactions at its interfaces with the high-k film, and the roughly 1000 °C anneals used to activate source and drain can shift it unpredictably. A gate-last, or replacement metal gate, flow therefore builds the transistor around a sacrificial polysilicon gate, removes it after the hot steps, and only then deposits the high-k and work-function metals. Work function also governs thermionic and photoelectric emission, and it is measured by photoemission spectroscopy or Kelvin probe techniques.

Answer: C. The energy difference between the vacuum level and the Fermi level

The work function is the energy needed to remove an electron at the Fermi level into vacuum, i.e. the difference between the vacuum level and the Fermi level. In a semiconductor the Fermi level moves with doping, so the work function changes too (about 4.05–5.17 eV in silicon). The work-function difference between metal and semiconductor affects Schottky barriers and MOS threshold voltage, and metal-gate materials (TiN, TiAl, etc.) are chosen for the work function that gives the target threshold.

FAQ

What is the difference between work function and electron affinity?

Work function is the energy from the Fermi level to the vacuum level; electron affinity is the energy from the conduction-band edge to the vacuum level. In a semiconductor the electron affinity is fixed by the material, while the work function changes with doping.

Does doping change the work function of silicon?

Yes. Doping moves the Fermi level, so silicon's work function varies from about 4.05 eV when heavily n-type to about 5.17 eV when heavily p-type.

Why does the gate metal work function affect threshold voltage?

The gate-to-channel work-function difference sets the flat-band voltage, which shifts the threshold voltage one-for-one. Choosing or tuning the gate metal therefore sets the threshold without heavy channel doping.
