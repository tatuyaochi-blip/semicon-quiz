What is Process capability index (Cpk)?

An index of how much margin a process's variation and mean offset leave relative to the specification limits. A common benchmark is 1.33 or higher.

Field: Yield and quality control

Process capability index (Cpk) in the explanations

  • Cp assumes the mean is centered in the spec, whereas Cpk divides the distance from the mean to the nearer spec limit by 3σ, so it reflects off-centering.
