What is Process capability index (Cpk)?

An index of how much margin a process's variation and mean offset leave relative to the specification limits. A common benchmark is 1.33 or higher.

Japanese name: 工程能力指数(Cpk)

Field: Yield and quality control

Cp and Cpk formulas

The process capability index Cp compares the specification width with the natural process spread: Cp = (USL − LSL)/6σ, where USL and LSL are the upper and lower specification limits and σ is the process standard deviation. Cp assumes the process mean sits at the center of the specification. Cpk removes that assumption by measuring the distance from the mean to the nearer limit in units of 3σ: Cpk = min(USL − μ, μ − LSL)/3σ. When the mean is centered, Cpk equals Cp; any offset makes Cpk smaller.

A worked example and typical targets

Suppose the specification is ±6 around a target and σ = 1. Cp = 12/6 = 2. If the mean drifts 3 units from center, the nearer limit is only 3 units away, so Cpk = 3/3 = 1, even though Cp is unchanged. Cpk = 1 means the nearest limit is 3σ from the mean, about 0.135% of a normal distribution beyond it. A common minimum is Cpk ≥ 1.33, which puts the nearest limit at 4σ and leaves only a few tens of ppm beyond it. Critical parameters may require 1.67 or more.

Control limits vs. specification limits

Specification limits come from customer or design requirements and define acceptable product. Control limits on a control chart come from the process's own mean and variation and signal when something unusual has happened. A process can be in statistical control yet produce out-of-spec parts if it is too variable, or meet spec while being out of control after a sudden shift. Cp and Cpk link the two worlds, and they are meaningful only when the process is stable; computing Cpk from unstable data predicts nothing about future output.

Practical cautions

The ppm interpretations assume a roughly normal distribution, which does not always hold for quantities such as particle counts or leakage current. For one-sided specifications, only the relevant half is used, for example (USL − μ)/3σ when only an upper limit exists. Many quality systems distinguish Cpk, computed from short-term within-subgroup variation, from Ppk, computed from the overall long-term standard deviation; Ppk is usually lower. In fabs these indices are tracked for parameters such as critical dimension, film thickness, overlay and electrical test results.

Which formula gives the process capability index Cp (when the process mean coincides with the center of the specification)? (σ: process standard deviation)

  1. A(Upper spec limit − lower spec limit) ÷ 6σ
  2. BMean ÷ σ
  3. Cσ ÷ mean
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. (Upper spec limit − lower spec limit) ÷ 6σ

Cp divides the specification width by the process spread (6σ), showing how comfortably the process meets spec. Cp = 1 means the spec width equals exactly ±3σ, with about 0.27% out of spec. Generally Cp ≥ 1.33 (±4σ to the limits) is considered adequate. Mean ÷ σ and its inverse are different measures of relative variation.

Which process capability index is used when the process mean is offset from the center of the specification?

  1. ACp
  2. BCpk
  3. CMTTR
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. Cpk

Cp assumes the mean is centered in the spec, whereas Cpk divides the distance from the mean to the nearer spec limit by 3σ, so it reflects off-centering. For example, with spec ±6 and σ = 1, Cp = 2, but if the mean shifts 3 from center, Cpk = (6−3)/3 = 1. When the mean is centered, Cp equals Cpk. MTTR is mean time to repair, a maintenance metric.

FAQ

What is the difference between Cp and Cpk?

Cp compares specification width with the 6σ process spread and assumes a centered mean. Cpk uses the distance from the actual mean to the nearer specification limit, so it also penalizes an off-center process.

What Cpk value is considered good?

A Cpk of 1.33 or higher, meaning the nearest specification limit is at least 4σ from the mean, is a widely used minimum; 1.67 or higher is often required for critical characteristics.

Can Cpk be higher than Cp?

No. Cpk equals Cp when the mean is exactly centered and is smaller whenever the mean is offset toward either limit.
