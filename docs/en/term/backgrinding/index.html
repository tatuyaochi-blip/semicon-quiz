What is Backgrinding?

Grinding the back of a wafer before dicing to thin it for packaging. Chips for stacking are thinned to a few tens of micrometers.

Japanese name: バックグラインド

What is the step called that grinds the back side of a wafer before dicing to thin it and make the package thinner?

  1. ABack grinding
  2. BCMP
  3. CEtch-back
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. Back grinding

In backgrinding, the wafer face is protected with tape while a rotating grinding wheel thins the back from about 750 µm to the target thickness. For stacked memory it may go down to tens of micrometers. The damaged layer left by grinding can initiate cracks, so it is removed by polishing or etching. CMP planarizes the front surface, and etch-back removes a film uniformly.

Which method makes very thin chips by first cutting grooves partway into the wafer from the front and then grinding the back to separate the chips?

  1. ADicing before grinding (DBG)
  2. BStepper exposure
  3. CThe CZ method
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. Dicing before grinding (DBG)

In DBG (dicing before grinding), grooves slightly deeper than the final thickness are cut into the wafer front first; backgrinding then separates the chips when it reaches the grooves. This reduces breakage and backside chipping compared with dicing an already thinned wafer, so it is used for chips tens of micrometers thick for stacked memory. A variant forms a laser-modified layer instead of grooves.
