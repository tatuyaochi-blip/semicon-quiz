What is Risk assessment?

Identifying the hazards of tasks or chemicals, estimating the size of the risks and deciding countermeasures in order of priority.

Japanese name: リスクアセスメント

Risk assessment appears in 2 practice questions across 2 topics, including Yield and quality control, Environment, health and safety.

Try it: questions about Risk assessment

Which type of control automatically adjusts equipment processing conditions for the next lot or wafer based on, for example, measurement results from earlier steps?

  1. AFMEA
  2. BPareto analysis
  3. CAPC (run-to-run control, etc.)
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. APC (run-to-run control, etc.)

APC (advanced process control) uses metrology data to adjust tool settings automatically and reduce process variation. In run-to-run control, for example, if the incoming film measured thick, the next CMP time is extended. Whereas SPC monitors and stops on abnormalities, APC actively corrects toward target. FMEA is proactive risk assessment and Pareto analysis finds priorities.

For work using chemicals, what is the activity called that identifies hazards, estimates the magnitude of risk and decides countermeasures?

  1. ARisk assessment
  2. BStatistical process control
  3. CBinning
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. Risk assessment

Risk assessment checks chemical hazards via SDSs and the like, estimates the likelihood of exposure or accidents from quantities and work methods, and decides countermeasures. Measures are prioritized in order: substitution with less hazardous substances, engineering controls such as enclosure and exhaust, procedural changes, then personal protective equipment. In Japan it is mandatory for certain chemicals. SPC and binning are quality and product-management methods.
