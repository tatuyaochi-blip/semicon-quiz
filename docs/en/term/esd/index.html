What is ESD (electrostatic discharge)?

A sudden discharge of static electricity from a charged person or object. Applied to an IC pin it can destroy gate oxides and other structures, so I/O pins have protection circuits.

Japanese name: ESD(静電気放電)

What is the main role of ESD protection diodes connected to the input/output pins of an IC?

  1. ATo divert excessive voltages such as static discharge to the supply or ground and protect internal circuits
  2. BTo increase pin capacitance and speed up signals
  3. CTo amplify signals
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. To divert excessive voltages such as static discharge to the supply or ground and protect internal circuits

Electrostatic discharge (ESD) from people or equipment can reach thousands of volts, while gate oxides a few nm thick fail at a few volts. Diodes between each I/O pin and the supply and ground rails conduct when an excessive voltage appears, diverting the current and protecting the internal circuitry. About 2 kV under the human body model (HBM) is a common robustness target for ICs. Because protection capacitance degrades fast signals, it is kept small on high-speed I/O.

What is provided at an IC's I/O pins as a countermeasure against ESD (electrostatic discharge)?

  1. ALight-emitting diodes
  2. BCooling fans
  3. CESD protection circuits (such as protection diodes)
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. ESD protection circuits (such as protection diodes)

Static charge on people or equipment can reach thousands of volts, and discharging into an IC pin can instantly destroy thin gate oxides and junctions. I/O pins therefore have ESD protection circuits built from protection diodes and power-to-ground clamps that shunt the discharge current to supply or ground. Factories also use wrist straps, conductive floors and containers, and ionizers.
