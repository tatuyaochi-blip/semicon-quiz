What is Probe card?

A fixture that presses many needles (probes) onto chip electrodes on the wafer to connect them electrically to a tester.

Japanese name: プローブカード

Probe card appears in 3 practice questions across 2 topics, including Testing and inspection, Packaging and assembly.

Try it: questions about Probe card

Which correctly describes a probe card?

  1. AA mold for encapsulating chips
  2. BA container for transporting wafers
  3. CA fixture that contacts needles to the chip electrodes on a wafer and connects them electrically to the tester
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. A fixture that contacts needles to the chip electrodes on a wafer and connects them electrically to the tester

A probe card is a product-specific fixture with many needles arranged to match the chip's pad layout. The prober aligns the wafer by camera and raises the chuck to bring pads into contact with the needles, electrically connecting tester and chip. Needle types include angled cantilever, vertical and MEMS-fabricated probes. It is unrelated to molds for encapsulation or wafer carriers.

In wafer testing, where on the chip do the probe card needles make contact?

  1. AOn top of transistor gates
  2. BOn the chip's electrode pads
  3. COnly at the center of the wafer's back
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. On the chip's electrode pads

In wafer test, the many needles of a probe card contact pads arranged around each chip, and the tester applies power and signals and measures the responses. Pads, tens of micrometers square, also serve later for wire bonding or bumps. Probe-mark size and position are inspected so they do not impair bonding. Transistors lie beneath the wiring layers and cannot be touched directly.
