What is Probe card?

A fixture that presses many needles (probes) onto chip electrodes on the wafer to connect them electrically to a tester.

Field: Testing and inspection

Probe card in the explanations

  • A probe card is a fixture used in wafer test, with many needles (probes) arranged to match the layout of the chip's bond pads.

  • In wafer testing, the many needles of a probe card contact electrode pads arranged around each chip, and the tester applies power and signals and measures the responses.
