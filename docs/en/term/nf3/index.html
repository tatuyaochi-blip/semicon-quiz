What is NF3 (nitrogen trifluoride)?

A gas used for cleaning CVD chambers and the like. Its global warming potential is high, so emission reduction is required.

Japanese name: NF3(三フッ化窒素)

Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. Cleaning films deposited inside CVD chambers

Repeated CVD deposits film on chamber walls, which flakes off and causes particles. NF3 is broken down in a plasma to produce fluorine radicals that convert these deposits into volatile fluorides. Remote plasma sources, which generate the plasma outside the chamber and deliver only radicals, are now standard and reduce damage to chamber parts. NF3's global warming potential is about 16,000 times that of CO2, so exhaust is abated before release.
