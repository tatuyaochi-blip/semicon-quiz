What is ATE (automatic test equipment)?

Equipment that applies test patterns to chips, measures their outputs and automatically judges pass or fail. Also called a tester.

Japanese name: ATE(自動試験装置)

  1. AEquipment that applies test patterns and compares outputs with expected values to judge pass or fail
  2. BEquipment that exposes circuit patterns onto wafers
  3. CEquipment that cleans wafers
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. Equipment that applies test patterns and compares outputs with expected values to judge pass or fail

ATE (automatic test equipment) applies power and signals to the device under test, measures its outputs, compares them with expected values and judges pass or fail. It has hundreds to thousands of channels, each able to drive signals, measure voltage and current, and check timing per pin. It is paired with a prober for wafer test and a handler for final test. Exposure and cleaning are jobs for front-end tools.
