What is ATE (automatic test equipment)?

Equipment that applies test patterns to chips, measures their outputs and automatically judges pass or fail. Also called a tester.

Field: Testing and inspection

ATE (automatic test equipment) in the explanations

  • ATE (automatic test equipment) applies power and signals to the device under test, measures its outputs, compares them with expected values and judges pass or fail.
