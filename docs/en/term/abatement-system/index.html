What is Abatement system?

Equipment that removes hazardous substances from tool exhaust gas by combustion, adsorption, water scrubbing and the like.

Japanese name: 除害装置

Abatement system appears in 3 practice questions across 2 topics, including Process materials (gases and chemicals), Compound semiconductors.

Why is care needed when handling GaAs wafer processing debris and waste?

  1. ABecause it contains radioactive substances
  2. BBecause it contains arsenic and must be properly managed and treated as a hazardous substance
  3. CBecause it explodes on contact with air
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. Because it contains arsenic and must be properly managed and treated as a hazardous substance

GaAs is about half arsenic by weight (about 52%), so grinding and polishing dust, waste liquids and scrapped wafers must be handled as hazardous industrial waste under regulations, with arsenic recovery. Local exhaust ventilation and protective equipment prevent worker exposure. Arsine (AsH3) gas used in manufacturing is even more toxic, requiring rigorous gas detection and abatement systems.

Which gases are mainly used for dry etching of silicon oxide (SiO2)?

  1. ANitrogen only
  2. BHelium only
  3. CFluorocarbon gases such as CF4 and C4F8
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. Fluorocarbon gases such as CF4 and C4F8

SiO2 is dry-etched with plasmas of fluorocarbon gases such as CF4 and C4F8. Fluorine combines with silicon to form volatile SiF4, while carbon leaves with the film's oxygen as CO or CO2. Nitrogen or helium alone causes no chemical reaction and cannot etch the film. These gases have global warming potentials thousands of times that of CO2 or more, so exhaust is treated in abatement systems before release.
