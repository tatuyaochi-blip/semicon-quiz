What is Halo implant (pocket implant)?

Angled implantation of dopant of the same type as the channel near the source and drain edges. It limits the spread of the drain depletion region and suppresses short-channel effects.

Japanese name: ハロー注入(ポケット注入)

  1. ATo lower the gate electrode resistance
  2. BTo suppress short-channel effects
  3. CTo make the transistor emit light
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. To suppress short-channel effects

Halo implants tilt the wafer about 20–45° so that dopant of the channel's type is placed locally near the source/drain edges, tucking under the gate. This restrains the drain depletion region, curbing threshold roll-off and punch-through as gates shrink, with less mobility loss than doping the entire channel. Halos were standard in scaled planar transistors.
