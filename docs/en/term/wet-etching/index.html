What is Wet etching?

Etching that dissolves films by immersing them in chemicals. Selectivity is high and damage low, but it generally proceeds isotropically and does not suit fine patterning.

Japanese name: ウェットエッチング

Which is a general characteristic of wet etching?

  1. AIt is highly anisotropic because it uses plasma
  2. BIt generally proceeds isotropically and readily undercuts the mask
  3. CIt requires vacuum equipment
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. It generally proceeds isotropically and readily undercuts the mask

Wet etching dissolves films by chemical reaction in liquid, so it generally proceeds equally in all directions (isotropic) and undercuts the mask by about the film thickness. It needs simple equipment, processes many wafers at once and easily achieves high selectivity. Dry etching is used for fine patterning, but wet etching remains important for blanket film removal and cleaning.

Which solution is commonly used for wet etching of silicon nitride?

  1. ADeionized water
  2. BHot phosphoric acid
  3. CAmmonia water
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. Hot phosphoric acid

Silicon nitride is etched in concentrated phosphoric acid heated to about 160 °C with selectivity of tens to over 100 to oxide. It removes nitride masks after LOCOS or STI formation and, in 3D NAND, strips the nitride layers from alternating oxide/nitride stacks so they can be replaced with word-line metal. Selectivity depends on dissolved silicon in the bath, requiring tight control.
