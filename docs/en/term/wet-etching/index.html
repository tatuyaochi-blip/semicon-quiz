What is Wet etching?

Etching that dissolves films by immersing them in chemicals. Selectivity is high and damage low, but it generally proceeds isotropically and does not suit fine patterning.

Field: Etching

Wet etching in the explanations

  • Wet etching dissolves films by chemical reaction in liquid, so it generally proceeds equally in all directions (isotropic) and undercuts the mask by about the film thickness.
