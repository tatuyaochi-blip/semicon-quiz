What is HEPA filter?

A high-efficiency air filter that captures at least 99.97% of 0.3 µm particles. Filters that capture even finer particles are ULPA filters.

Japanese name: HEPAフィルタ

Which is the common definition of HEPA filter performance?

  1. ACapturing at least 99.97% of particles 0.3 µm in size
  2. BCapturing at least 50% of particles 10 µm in size
  3. CRemoving at least 99.99% of gaseous molecules
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. Capturing at least 99.97% of particles 0.3 µm in size

A HEPA filter is defined (in JIS and elsewhere) as capturing at least 99.97% of particles around 0.3 µm, close to the hardest size to filter. Larger particles are caught by inertia and smaller ones by Brownian diffusion, so intermediate sizes penetrate most easily. Higher-performance ULPA filters are rated at 99.9995% or better at 0.15 µm. Neither removes gases.
