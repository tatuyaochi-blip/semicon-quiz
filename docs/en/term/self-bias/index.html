What is Self-bias?

The negative DC potential that the wafer electrode naturally acquires in a capacitively coupled plasma. It accelerates positive ions toward the wafer, aiding anisotropic etching.

Field: Etching

Self-bias in the explanations

  • On an electrode driven with RF power, the light, mobile electrons flow onto it more readily than ions, so the electrode acquires a negative average DC potential (self-bias).
