What is Vegard's law?

The empirical rule that the lattice constant of an alloy varies almost linearly with composition. It is used to find the composition that lattice-matches a given substrate.

Japanese name: ベガード則

Answer: B. Vegard's law

Vegard's law is the empirical rule that an alloy's lattice constant interpolates linearly between those of its constituents by composition. For InxGa1-xAs, a straight line between GaAs (0.565 nm) and InAs (0.606 nm) gives x ≈ 0.53 to match InP (0.587 nm). Bandgaps usually sag below the straight line (bowing), so a quadratic correction term is added.
