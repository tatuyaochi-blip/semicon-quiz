What is Ceria (cerium oxide)?

An abrasive used in CMP slurries. It reacts chemically with oxide and gives high selectivity to nitride, so it is used for STI CMP.

Japanese name: セリア(酸化セリウム)

Which correctly describes the slurry used in CMP?

  1. AA polishing liquid with abrasive particles such as silica or ceria dispersed in a chemical solution
  2. BA light-sensitive resin applied to the wafer
  3. CA gas for generating plasma
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. A polishing liquid with abrasive particles such as silica or ceria dispersed in a chemical solution

Slurry disperses abrasive particles tens to over a hundred nm in size—silica (SiO2), ceria (CeO2), alumina—in chemistry containing oxidizers, pH adjusters, corrosion inhibitors and dispersants. Particles and chemistry are chosen per film: silica or ceria for oxide, oxidizer-containing silica slurries for tungsten and copper. Slurry is a major CMP consumable cost, and Japanese companies are strong suppliers.

Which abrasive particles are often used in STI CMP to achieve high polishing selectivity of oxide to silicon nitride?

  1. ACeria (cerium oxide)
  2. BIron powder
  3. CCoarse diamond grit
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. Ceria (cerium oxide)

Ceria (cerium oxide) bonds chemically with oxide and removes it efficiently, while additives such as amino-acid surfactants adsorb on nitride and suppress its polishing, giving oxide-to-nitride selectivity of tens to over 100. It is used in STI CMP, where nitride serves as the stop, to combine planarity with a reliable stop. Ceria also polishes quickly at low particle concentrations.
