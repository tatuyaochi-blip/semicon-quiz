What is Perfluorocarbons?

Also called PFCs. Fluorinated gases such as CF4 and C2F6 used in etching and chamber cleaning. Their greenhouse effect is very large.

Japanese name: パーフルオロカーボン

What PFCs are

Perfluorocarbons (PFCs) are compounds of carbon and fluorine only, in which every hydrogen of a hydrocarbon has been replaced by fluorine; examples are CF4 (carbon tetrafluoride), C2F6, C3F8 and c-C4F8. The very strong C-F bond makes them chemically stable and hard to burn. Semiconductor fabs use them as gases that a plasma breaks down into fluorine-containing radicals and ions. In the semiconductor industry, the term PFCs is sometimes used loosely to also cover other potent greenhouse gases such as HFCs like CHF3, NF3 and SF6, and reduction targets and emission reports commonly treat them together.

Use in etching and chamber cleaning

Dry etching of insulating films such as SiO2 uses plasmas of fluorocarbons such as CF4 and C4F8. Fluorine combines with silicon to form volatile SiF4, and carbon leaves with the oxygen in the film as CO or CO2. Carbon-rich gases such as C4F8 deposit a protective fluorocarbon film on sidewalls and underlying layers, helping achieve selectivity and vertical profiles. Cleaning films off the chamber walls of CVD tools once also relied on gases such as C2F6 turned into plasma inside the chamber, but the large fraction released without being broken down was a problem.

Global-warming impact

PFCs absorb infrared radiation strongly and are hardly broken down in the atmosphere, so their global warming potential (GWP) reaches thousands of times that of CO2 or more. CF4 in particular has an atmospheric lifetime of about 50,000 years and C2F6 about 10,000 years, so once released they do not disappear on any human timescale. Because GWP values are revised between editions of the IPCC assessment reports, emission accounting must specify which edition's values are used. PFC emissions from a fab are counted in its greenhouse-gas inventory as direct emissions (Scope 1).

Cutting emissions

Emissions are reduced by combining several measures. Process optimization revisits gas flows and discharge conditions to reduce the amount that escapes unreacted. Chamber cleaning has moved to remote-plasma systems that break down NF3 outside the tool with an efficiency of 95% or more. In abatement, PFCs barely dissolve in water, so wet scrubbers alone cannot remove them; combustion or plasma abatement systems heat them to around 1000 ℃ or higher to decompose them, and a downstream scrubber removes the resulting hydrogen fluoride. CF4 is especially hard to decompose and needs even higher temperatures.

Answer: C. Because they are greenhouse gases with high global warming potential and long atmospheric lifetimes

PFCs such as CF4 and C2F6 have global warming potentials thousands of times that of CO2, and CF4 persists in the atmosphere for about 50,000 years. Once emitted, they effectively never disappear on human timescales, so the semiconductor industry reduces emissions through abatement, switching to lower-GWP alternatives and optimizing usage. They are not major causes of water pollution or acid rain.

FAQ

What are PFCs?

Short for perfluorocarbons, they are compounds of carbon and fluorine only, such as CF4 and C2F6. Semiconductor fabs use them for dry etching and for cleaning CVD chambers, and as greenhouse gases with very high global warming potential they are targeted for emission reduction.

How high is the global warming potential of PFCs?

For CF4 and C2F6 it is thousands of times that of CO2 or more, with exact values differing slightly between editions of the IPCC assessment reports. Their atmospheric lifetimes are also very long, about 50,000 years for CF4.

How are PFC emissions reduced?

Measures are combined: optimizing processes to use less gas, switching to methods with high decomposition efficiency such as remote-plasma NF3 or to alternative gases with lower GWP, and decomposing PFCs at high temperature in combustion or plasma abatement systems. Because PFCs barely dissolve in water, wet scrubbers alone cannot remove them.
