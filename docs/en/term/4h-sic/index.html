What is 4H-SiC?

The SiC polytype mainly used for power devices. It has a wide band gap of about 3.3 eV and relatively high electron mobility.

Field: Epitaxial growth

4H-SiC in the explanations

  • Substrates cut about 4° off-axis present many atomic steps; adatoms incorporate at the steps and growth proceeds laterally (step-flow growth), so the crystal inherits the substrate's 4H-SiC stacking sequence.
