What is Bathtub curve?

A curve showing how the failure rate changes over time. It consists of the early-failure, random-failure and wear-out periods and is shaped like a bathtub.

Field: Reliability

Bathtub curve in the explanations

  • The bathtub curve consists of the early failure period, when the failure rate is high at the start of use; the random failure period, when the failure rate is low and nearly constant; and the wear-out failure period, when the failure rate rises due to degradation.
