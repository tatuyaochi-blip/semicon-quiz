What is Magnetron sputtering?

Sputtering with magnets behind the target that trap electrons near it and raise plasma density. It increases deposition rate and is the mainstream sputtering method today.

Japanese name: マグネトロンスパッタリング

What is the main purpose of placing magnets behind the target in magnetron sputtering?

  1. ATo cool the target
  2. BTo hold the wafer in place
  3. CTo confine electrons near the target with a magnetic field, raising plasma density and deposition rate
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. To confine electrons near the target with a magnetic field, raising plasma density and deposition rate

Magnets behind the target create a magnetic field crossing the electric field near its surface, trapping electrons in spiraling paths that ionize more gas and produce a dense plasma. This sustains high deposition rates at low pressures of about 0.1–1 Pa and reduces wafer heating from electron bombardment. Erosion concentrates where the field is strongest, carving a groove, so magnets are rotated to improve target utilization.
