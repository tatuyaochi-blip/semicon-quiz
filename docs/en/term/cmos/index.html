What is CMOS?

A circuit style that combines nMOS and pMOS transistors in a complementary way. Almost no current flows through it in steady state, so power consumption is low.

Japanese name: CMOS

Which pairing of a technology that integrates bipolar transistors and CMOS on the same chip with its purpose is correct?

  1. ASOI: isolating the substrate with an insulating layer
  2. BBiCMOS: combining the high density and low power of CMOS with the speed and drive capability of bipolar transistors
  3. CFinFET: making the channel three-dimensional
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. BiCMOS: combining the high density and low power of CMOS with the speed and drive capability of bipolar transistors

BiCMOS combines dense, low-power CMOS for logic and memory with bipolar transistors for high-speed circuits, heavy loads, and analog/RF functions. In the 1990s it was used even in CPUs and SRAMs, but as supply voltages dropped, the bipolar VBE (about 0.7 V) became a handicap and it disappeared from digital chips. Today SiGe BiCMOS, incorporating SiGe HBTs, is used in communications ICs and automotive radar.

Why is the static (steady-state) power consumption of a CMOS inverter small?

  1. ABecause the supply voltage is high
  2. BBecause current always flows into the gate
  3. CBecause whether the input is 0 or 1, either the pMOS or the nMOS is off, so almost no current flows from supply to ground
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. Because whether the input is 0 or 1, either the pMOS or the nMOS is off, so almost no current flows from supply to ground

In a CMOS inverter, input 0 turns the pMOS on and nMOS off, and input 1 does the reverse, so in steady state there is always an off transistor between supply and ground and almost no through current flows. Power is consumed mainly by charging and discharging load capacitance during switching: P = α·C·V²·f. With scaling, however, off-state leakage has grown, and static power is no longer negligible in advanced chips.
