What is Specialty gases?

High-purity gases with toxic or flammable properties used in semiconductor manufacturing, such as silane, arsine and phosphine. They are supplied under strict control.

Japanese name: 特殊材料ガス

Which correctly describes the typical handling of cylinders of toxic or flammable specialty gases?

  1. AKeep them near the worker's desk so they can be used right away
  2. BHouse them in exhausted cylinder cabinets and monitor for leaks with gas detectors
  3. CLeave them outdoors and use them without connecting piping
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. House them in exhausted cylinder cabinets and monitor for leaks with gas detectors

Specialty gas cylinders are kept in continuously exhausted cylinder cabinets, with gas detectors monitoring for leaks. When a leak is detected, shutoff valves close automatically and alarms sound. Cylinder changes are done by trained personnel following procedures, with line purging before and after. Cylinders are never kept by workers' desks or used without proper piping.
