What is Drive-in?

In thermal diffusion, the high-temperature step after a fixed amount of dopant has been introduced at the surface (predeposition), which pushes the dopant deeper.

Field: Oxidation and diffusion

Drive-in in the explanations

  • Thermal diffusion was often done in two steps: predeposition introduces a set dose shallowly while holding surface concentration constant, then drive-in stops the supply and heats the wafer to spread that fixed dose to the desired depth and profile.
