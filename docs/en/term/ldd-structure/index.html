What is LDD structure?

Short for lightly doped drain. The drain is made lightly doped only at its edge next to the gate, relaxing the electric field and suppressing hot-carrier degradation.

Japanese name: LDD構造

What is the purpose of the LDD (lightly doped drain) structure in a MOSFET?

  1. ATo lower the resistance of the gate electrode
  2. BTo lower the doping at the drain edge, relax the electric field, and suppress degradation by hot carriers
  3. CTo double the channel mobility
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. To lower the doping at the drain edge, relax the electric field, and suppress degradation by hot carriers

The strong field near the drain creates hot carriers that are injected into the gate dielectric or create interface states, shifting threshold voltage and reducing current. An LDD structure uses the gate sidewall spacer to form a shallow, lightly doped region on the channel side of the drain, lowering the peak field and suppressing degradation. Today, more heavily doped shallow "extensions," which also help control short-channel effects, are the mainstream.
