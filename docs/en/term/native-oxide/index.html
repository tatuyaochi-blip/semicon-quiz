What is Native oxide?

A thin oxide about 1 nm thick that forms naturally when silicon is exposed to air or water. Because it raises contact resistance and disturbs epitaxial growth, it is removed right before such steps.

Japanese name: 自然酸化膜

Answer: B. Dry cleaning using vapor-phase gases or radicals

Tall, narrow patterns can be pulled over by surface tension as cleaning liquids dry. Vapor-phase (dry) cleans using HF vapor or radicals from NF3/NH3 plasmas (as in SiCoNi processing) involve no liquid, eliminating collapse risk, and can be vacuum-linked to deposition chambers so wafers proceed without reoxidizing. They are used, for example, to remove oxide at the bottoms of contacts.
