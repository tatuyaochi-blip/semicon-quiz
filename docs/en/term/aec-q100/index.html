What is AEC-Q100?

The qualification standard for reliability testing of automotive integrated circuits, defined by the Automotive Electronics Council (AEC). It has operating temperature grades: Grade 1 is −40 to 125 °C and Grade 0 is −40 to 150 °C. Discrete semiconductors are covered by AEC-Q101 and passive components by AEC-Q200.

Japanese name: AEC-Q100

Try it: questions about AEC-Q100

In AEC-Q100, the reliability qualification standard for automotive ICs, which ambient temperature range is specified for operating temperature Grade 1?

  1. A−40 to 85 °C
  2. B−40 to 125 °C
  3. C−40 to 150 °C
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. −40 to 125 °C

AEC-Q100 grades denote ambient temperature ranges; the smaller the number, the higher the upper limit. Grade 0 is −40 to 150 °C, Grade 1 is −40 to 125 °C, Grade 2 is −40 to 105 °C and Grade 3 is −40 to 85 °C. −40 to 150 °C is the most stringent, Grade 0, and −40 to 85 °C is the Grade 3 range. Because self-heating makes the chip's junction temperature higher than the ambient temperature, thermal design must also account for that difference.

Which correctly pairs AEC-Q100, AEC-Q101 and AEC-Q200, the qualification standards for automotive electronic components, with the components they mainly cover?

  1. AQ100: integrated circuits (ICs); Q101: discrete semiconductors such as diodes and transistors; Q200: passive components such as resistors and capacitors
  2. BQ100: passive components; Q101: integrated circuits (ICs); Q200: discrete semiconductors such as diodes and transistors
  3. CQ100: discrete semiconductors; Q101: passive components; Q200: integrated circuits (ICs)
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. Q100: integrated circuits (ICs); Q101: discrete semiconductors such as diodes and transistors; Q200: passive components such as resistors and capacitors

The AEC standards are divided by component type. Integrated circuits such as microcontrollers and power ICs fall under AEC-Q100; discrete semiconductors such as diodes, power MOSFETs and IGBTs fall under AEC-Q101; and passive components such as resistors, capacitors, inductors and crystal resonators fall under AEC-Q200. Because the components differ in structure and failure behavior, the test items and conditions also differ. For example, automotive multilayer ceramic capacitors use AEC-Q200-compliant types.
