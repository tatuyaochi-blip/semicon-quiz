Editorial policy

The questions, explanations, glossary, comparisons, diagrams and calculators on Semiconductor Room are written and reviewed according to the following policy.

How questions are written

  • Exactly one correct answer: only one of the three choices is correct. When a question asks for the "most appropriate" choice, the explanation must be able to say clearly why the second-best choice falls short.
  • State the conditions: numbers come with their conditions, such as temperature, voltage, material, generation or standard revision. Values that vary widely with conditions are given as ranges.
  • Avoid absolutes: "always" and "never" are used only when a definition or physical law makes them true. Rules of thumb and industry practice are described as "generally" or "as a guideline".
  • Date time-sensitive facts: market sizes, product generations, prices and similar information say when they were true.
  • Show the calculation: explanations of calculation questions show the working, and the correct value appears in the explanation.

Sources

When checking content, we give priority to sources in this order.

  1. Standards (ISO, IEC, JEDEC, AEC, IEEE and others)
  2. Peer-reviewed papers and standard textbooks
  3. Technical documents from semiconductor manufacturers
  4. Materials from universities and research institutions

Where we have not been able to read the source itself, such as a paid standard, we avoid definitive statements and record the item as unverified.

Review and corrections

We use AI to help write and review questions and explanations, and check the content against the standards above. In October 2026 we reviewed all 2,750 questions and corrected the text of 71 of them (no question had a wrong answer key).

Answers and numbers are also checked automatically by a program (for example, that the correct value appears in the explanation and that numbers are written correctly in every language). Errors we find, or that readers report and we confirm, are corrected, and each correction is recorded with its reason. If you notice an error, please let us know through the contact page.

Translations

The original content is written in Japanese. Pages in other languages are translated from the Japanese original, and when the original is corrected the translations are corrected with it. Questions without a translation are not shown in that language.

Advertising

This site is funded by advertising and affiliate income, but advertising and referral fees never affect the content of the questions or explanations.
